## Adolf asks to test Vikir

The chapter resumes directly from Vikir's deadpan response to Adolf. Hugo visibly suppresses a smile; Camus is surprised; Adolf is the only person losing composure.

Adolf asks Hugo for permission to speak with/test Vikir more seriously. Hugo returns Adolf's earlier political jab by asking whether the Empire's `Magic Star` intends to bully an eight-year-old boy.

Adolf frames the request as the concern of Camus's uncle rather than as official Morgue diplomacy. Supporting Fandom material independently identifies Adolf as Camus's uncle/teacher and a **Sixth Circle Master**.

A scene break then separates the political exchange from the physical test.

## Vikir's assessment of Adolf

Adolf is an elite Morgue combatant and political representative whom pre-regression Vikir considered extremely dangerous. Vikir estimates that at his own former peak he might have had roughly a 50% chance through ambush/assassination but only around 10% in a direct frontal fight.

Current Vikir has not recovered all of that former power and has no reason to reveal his true Low Graduator/Fifth Fang capability. His goal is to satisfy Adolf's curiosity while gathering information about a top Morgue mage.

## Water-jar handicap

Adolf demonstrates effortless multi-element control by creating and firing a clay jar, filling it with water, levitating it, and balancing it on his head.

The victory condition is simple: **if Vikir can make even one drop of water spill from the jar, Vikir wins.**

Adolf's shield magic is the central obstacle. He can create shields of different sizes, shapes and thicknesses with minimal mana waste, including small barriers placed exactly where an incoming sword or projectile will strike.

## Vikir's probing attacks

Vikir begins with a direct charge, then uses the rebound to change direction and kicks stones toward the jar. Adolf blocks both Vikir and the stones without difficulty.

Adolf then begins attacking with fire spears strong enough to gouge the ground. Camus protests that Adolf never used such serious magic against her, revealing her growing concern for Vikir despite her uncle's annoyance.

Vikir recognizes Adolf's fighting style from his first-life experience. The danger is familiar enough that Vikir quickly develops a route to satisfy the handicap without revealing his hidden maximum.

## Repeated shield strike is a feint

Vikir repeatedly strikes **the exact same point** on Adolf's shield. Adolf correctly identifies the local magical fatigue building at that point but believes Vikir is foolishly trying to break a superior shield with an inferior short sword.

That interpretation is deliberately incomplete.

After the repeated impacts, Vikir's sword finally shatters. Adolf begins lecturing him about stubbornness and inadequacy as a prospective match for Camus.

Then water begins running down Adolf's head.

A fragment of Vikir's shattered sword, launched by the final collision, has embedded itself in the side of the water jar and opened a leak. Vikir was never trying to break through Adolf's shield; he was engineering the sword fragment's trajectory.

This is the family endpoint and the tactical payoff. Preserve Vikir's final dry remark about the jar having been full to the brim. Do not import Chapter 26's reaction or Graduator-family material backward.

## Power concealment

Nothing in this test justifies exposing Vikir's actual Low Graduator aura, unstable Fifth Fang, Lurking Fang scripture knowledge, or Beelzebub. The win is tactical and positional. That distinction is important because the next title family is explicitly *The Graduate*.
