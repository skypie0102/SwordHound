## The special law escalates

Vikir adds clauses to the same stake law when nobody volunteers:
- Article 1, Section 1: **100,000,000 gold**;
- Section 2 adds **900,000,000**, bringing the total to **1,000,000,000 gold**;
- Section 3 adds **9,000,000,000**, bringing the total to **10,000,000,000 gold**.

The wooden stake remains ordinary and easy to remove. The trap is psychological and political: the crowd assumes such an absurd offer from a Baskerville official must conceal punishment or humiliation.

## Judy

Judy is a malnourished **girl** who sells flowers to support her sick mother. Several recovered MTL pronouns incorrectly masculinize her and are corrected.

She volunteers because she needs money, not because she understands Vikir's plan. Even after pulling the stake easily, both Judy and the crowd expect Vikir to invent some offense and execute her.

Her mother rushes forward and begs for mercy, prompting the crowd to become openly angry at what they assume is abuse of power.

## Literal enforcement

Vikir reaches past his sword and hands Judy a heavy leather pouch containing the promised reward. He pays the full **10 billion gold**.

This is the point of the scene: the first public demonstration of the new administration is that a law will be enforced **exactly as written regardless of the claimant's poverty or status**.

Exact endpoint:

> “This is how the law works.”
>
> “It will be upheld.”
