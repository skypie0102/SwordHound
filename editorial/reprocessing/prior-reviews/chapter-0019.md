## Why Vikir asks for a stronger opponent

The chapter opens by restating the public conclusion from Chapter 18: Baskerville believes an eight-year-old Vikir has reached a complete **Low Sword Expert** level. Vikir's request for something stronger immediately excites the combat-obsessed family.

Vikir's private goal remains controlled disclosure. He believes showing an extraordinary result while young can eventually give him a legitimate reason to leave Hugo's direct supervision—for a provincial assignment, study in the Imperial Capital, or possibly admission to the Academy. He does not particularly welcome the Academy option at this point, so it remains only one possibility.

Do not confuse the publicly displayed rank with Vikir's real rank. His actual state remains **Low Sword Graduator** with an unstable Fifth Fang; the entire family is seeing a deliberately reduced performance.

## Troll selection

Hugo decides that the ordinary orc was beneath his son's level and asks whether the family has a stronger captive. Barrymore reluctantly identifies one remaining specimen captured during an earlier campaign against barbarians.

The troll entry is corrected as follows:
- **Troll**
- Danger Level: **C+**
- Size: **4 m**
- Discovery Location: **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain, Second Ridge** (recovered MTL reading; the supplied Korean location is machine-corrupted)
- enormous cannibalistic humanoid with a vicious temperament and extraordinary regeneration

The recovered English incorrectly labels the troll `C`; the supplied Korean gives **C+**, and Fandom's `Demonic Creatures` page independently lists trolls as C+ monsters with extreme/superspeed regeneration and tough hides. The supporting reference confirms rank/mechanics, not the exact location wording.

A troll's regeneration is so extreme that ordinary flank/abdominal wounds are almost meaningless. The reliable solution is catastrophic damage to the neck, ultimately complete severance.

## Vikir exhausts the troll instead of wasting attacks

The troll is heavily restrained and has been starved. Vikir immediately refuses an apparently open flank strike because he knows the wound would simply regenerate.

Rather than spending stamina on meaningless damage, he repeatedly dodges while allowing the furious troll to exhaust itself fighting both Vikir and its restraints. Experienced Baskervilles correctly recognize that he is deliberately draining the creature's strength.

The recovered `Zagoro troll` wording is discarded; nothing in the Korean establishes a separate troll subtype here.

## First attack: one fang is deliberately insufficient

When the troll finally commits its full body to a charge, Vikir uses its momentum against it. The collision helps his blade penetrate the thick hide/muscle and reach the neck.

Vikir again reveals only the same faint gaseous Sword Expert aura shown during the orc test. He forms **one Stabbing Fang / Baskerville First Form** and tears a large hole in the troll's throat.

The troll immediately begins regenerating and survives. The spectators conclude that one fang and gaseous aura are insufficient. They are correct about the performance Vikir chose to show, but not about his hidden maximum.

Beelzebub's Bleeding could eventually kill the troll through continuing blood loss, but Vikir explicitly rejects that route because a delayed Beelzebub-assisted death would not demonstrate the level he intends to reveal.

The recovered English `Baskerville 1 meal` is a machine-translation error for the **First Form / one fang**.

## “Release the restraints”

Hugo tries to stop the fight, judging that Vikir has already proven himself and that pushing further would be needlessly dangerous.

Vikir instead asks the Guardian Knights to remove the troll's restraints. Everyone, including Hugo, is stunned.

Hugo tells his son that knowing when to be satisfied and step back is part of a swordsman's discipline. This unexpectedly paternal/admonishing tone is itself surprising to the family.

Vikir answers that he is nearly finished and promises to stop if the **next strike** fails to kill the troll. Hugo's silence functions as permission to continue.

## Second Form and Intermediate Sword Expert reveal

Once fully unrestrained, the troll charges faster and harder. Vikir has intentionally created this condition because the creature's greater momentum will increase the reaction force when it runs onto his blade.

Vikir allows his aura to become **slightly denser and brighter**, but still does not reveal the liquid/materialized aura of a Graduator. Several experienced observers notice the change.

This time two fang trajectories appear:
- the first tears open the throat;
- a second, smaller/fainter but unmistakable fang bites into the side of the neck.

Normalize the corrupted recovered wording `Baskerville Two-Thirds` to **Baskerville Second Form** and `Sword Expert Intermediate` to **Intermediate Sword Expert**.

This is the ceiling Vikir chooses to reveal publicly in the Bared Teeth family. His real Low Graduator/Fifth Fang capability remains concealed.

The combined momentum and two-fang strike sever the troll's neck. The troll collapses decapitated while Hugo and the gathered Baskervilles stand frozen in disbelief.

Exact endpoint: the decapitated troll falls to its knees. Do not import Chapter 20's reaction or Camus Morgue material into this family.
