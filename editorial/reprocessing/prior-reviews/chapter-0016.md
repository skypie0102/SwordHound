## First-timeline discovery

The chapter explains why Vikir recognizes **Lurking Embedded Teeth**. In his first life, while his Hound unit was clearing a dungeon near the frontier/Black Mountains, he found a single torn page from an unfamiliar sword scripture. Sensing that it was extraordinary, he memorized the page before turning it in.

Hugo immediately recognized that the fragment belonged to something important and searched the Baskerville library for the source volume. The ancient, neglected miscellaneous book he found was the scripture now standardized in this project as **Lurking Embedded Teeth**.

The Korean/MTL witnesses call the book `Hidden Eagle`, `Hidden Blade`, and `Lurking Ambush` in different passages. Those are treated as machine-translation variants of the same scripture, not separate manuals.

## True purpose of the scripture

Once a missing page was restored, the seemingly crude/basic text changed meaning. Its swordsmanship hides aggression, receives/evades an enemy's attack, then suddenly bites into a fatal opening. The scripture describes the path toward **ten fangs** and contains the true essence of Baskerville swordsmanship.

Use **Tenth Fang / Tenth Form** for the ultimate stage while preserving the separate scripture title **Lurking Embedded Teeth**. Supporting Fandom material independently identifies the book by that title and confirms that it is the scripture connected to the Tenth Fang.

Hugo had reached Sword Master while using seven fangs at that time and therefore became obsessed with completing the text.

## Seven missing pages and the recovery operation

The scripture was still incomplete. There were **seven torn pages in total**. Vikir had recovered one; the remaining six were missing.

After years of investigation and enormous expenditure, Hugo traced the missing pages to territories/possessions connected to the Seven Great Families. He ordered the Baskerville hounds to retrieve them. The operations are retained as the source's paired names:
- **Operation Reclaim the Torn Pages**
- **Operation Restore the Missing Teeth**

The exact wording is less important than their identity as the same long-running recovery campaign.

Other families naturally resisted armed Baskerville hounds entering their territory. The campaign became close to open war and consumed large numbers of Baskerville operatives.

## Cost to Vikir

Preserve the tooth metaphor running through the casualties: familiar siblings/companions vanished from the dining hall; gaps were filled by new hounds who in turn disappeared. Vikir was the `permanent tooth` that survived the grinding campaign.

The recovered MTL calls him a `right-handed black dog`, while the Korean describes him as a scarred/maimed black hound with wording damaged by machine translation. Avoid inventing a new formal title here. The important point is that he survived repeated missions while being physically mutilated and scarred.

By the end, Vikir had recovered all seven pages and memorized their contents. The cost included fingers/toes, severe burns and cuts, and the deaths of his companions. His reward from Hugo was simply: **“Well done.”**

Vikir now recognizes how completely he once accepted that treatment.

## Hugo's Ninth Fang and Vikir's old limitation

In the first timeline Hugo used the recovered knowledge to advance from seven fangs to nine, raising Baskerville's prestige on the blood of its hounds. This aligns with the accepted continuity in Chapters 13–15 and supporting Fandom material.

Vikir had secretly memorized all seven missing pages, but could not use them to surpass the Fourth Fang because fragments alone were useless without the original scripture's framework.

That limitation is gone in the current timeline: he now has the original **Lurking Embedded Teeth** volume in his hands and all seven missing pages in memory.
