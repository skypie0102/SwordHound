## Territorial inspection / ruby mine

Hugo also orders Vikir to inspect the agricultural districts before entering the Academy. The farms produce sugar cane, tobacco, cotton, and other crops and rely heavily on barbarian captives.

The inspection is also a **joint operation** connected to Vikir's childhood ruby-mine strategy. Baskerville leased the mining area to Morgue and encouraged pressure from the barbarians so the two outside forces would wear one another down—the classic strategy of killing with a borrowed blade.

The plan has worked well enough that Morgue itself proposes a **joint punitive expedition** against the barbarians.
