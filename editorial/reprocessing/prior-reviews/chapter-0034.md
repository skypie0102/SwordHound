## No political compromise

Underdog expects the seven heirs' families to settle the confrontation through apologies, tribute and political pressure. Houses Montblanc, Pierre, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Ferragamo, Hermès and Prada do in fact send apologies and expensive tribute to City Hall.

Vikir rejects the expected compromise. The existing laws contain no exemption for wealth, family status or political usefulness.

The seven heirs are implicated in illegal brothels, lobbying, human trafficking, drug use/distribution, illicit profits, tax evasion, violence and sexual assault. Human trafficking already carries death under the old Baskerville code.

## Public enforcement

The next morning, the seven executed heirs' **salt-preserved heads** are displayed in the central square. The recovered MTL's `salted and disembowelled heads` is rejected as nonsense.

Additional statutory punishments are displayed with them. Preserve the recovered source structure and its intentionally extreme legal regime:
- illegal brothels — facial branding; the character `色` / lust is branded across the face;
- illegal gambling — wrist punishment, with repeated violations applied retroactively and one offender recorded as receiving seventy-two cuts;
- illegal usury — removal of an eye;
- illegal human trafficking — death;
- illegal lobbying — exile; because the offenders are dead, their bodies other than the displayed heads are removed beyond the city walls.

The narrative point is not that Vikir creates new punishments here. He applies already existing statutes to people previously assumed to be untouchable.

## War on crime

The scene rewinds to before execution. The seven heirs are battered in the City Hall dungeon. Chihuahua worries both about the severity of Vikir's actions and about retaliation from the local magnates.

Normalize the likely consequences as political/economic retaliation: boycotts, refusal to cooperate with official functions, disruption of intercity trade and declining tax revenue.

Vikir is unconcerned because the seven families themselves are connected to criminal organizations throughout Underdog.

The Korean explicitly supports Vikir's morally compromised evidence policy:

**Evidence can be found; if it cannot be found, it can be fabricated.**

Do not sanitize this characterization. At the same time, keep it distinct from the actual crimes already established in the source.

## Vikir's short horizon

The MTL incorrectly shifts one sentence's subject to Hugo. The intended continuity is that **Vikir does not expect to remain Deputy Magistrate for long**. He anticipates Academy admission or reassignment to real combat as the wider world changes.

Therefore he favors an extremely strong short-term crackdown even if it creates side effects for his successor.

The `thousand miles` line is normalized to the proverb: **a journey of a thousand miles begins with one step.** The immediate step is forcing the seven heirs to reveal their criminal partners and locations.

## Charges and interrogation

Vikir lists accusations including illegal kidnapping/imprisonment, sexual assault, intimidation, forced drugging, human trafficking, forced prostitution, bribery, extortion, blackmail, commissioned violence and murder.

The heirs decide to remain silent because a confession would expose their families and could invite wider collective punishment.

Vikir has a veteran City Hall interrogator prepare more than one hundred specialized knives/tools based on interrogation methods remembered from the Age of Destruction. Even the thirty-year interrogator is disturbed by them.

The final lines are darkly nostalgic from Vikir's perspective: he remembers future wartime interrogation methods breaking even demon prisoners and privately hopes the heirs will not break too quickly.
