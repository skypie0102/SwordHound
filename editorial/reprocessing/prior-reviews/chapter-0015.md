## Inner Library atmosphere and hierarchy

The chapter opens inside the **Inner Library**, a vast restricted library carved deep into the Baskerville stronghold. Vikir enters using Hugo's personally signed permission. Knights recognize the authorization and do not obstruct him.

The library should feel less cozy than oppressive: old books, ink and dust, but shelves full of killing theory, weapon technique, lethal calculations and generations of Baskerville swordsmen arguing silently through their manuals. Preserve the prison/cold-blooded-killers imagery.

The deeper Vikir goes, the older and more restricted the manuals become. The recovered MTL mangles several technique names, but the governing hierarchy is clear: branch/illegitimate members are normally limited around the Fourth Fang; direct-line members can learn higher forms; elite direct-line swordsmen reach the Sixth; Hugo has mastered seven fangs in the present timeline and stands at Sword Master level.

The comparison with the Morgue family head supports the established sword/magic rank equivalence rather than introducing a new rank system.

## Hugo's Sixth Form manual

Vikir reaches the locked section Hugo authorized and opens the high-level manual. Treat it as **Baskerville Sixth Form** in project-facing prose. The recovered MTL/Korean subtitle is unstable and tooth-specific; do not let that noisy subtitle override the stable Sixth Form identification.

The book explains how successive fang trajectories change cutting, stabbing, blocking, crushing and related attack patterns. Its real importance to Vikir is that it fills the theoretical gap between his perfectly honed Fourth Fang and the Fifth/Sixth Fang progression.

Vikir spent nearly twenty years in his first life trapped around the Fourth Fang while accumulating enormous practical experience. Because of that foundation, he needs only about three hours to understand and memorize the Sixth Form theory. The key realization is that the mana flow must be broken/reoriented after the Fourth Fang; that missing principle had prevented him from producing the Fifth.

Do not overstate this as full practical mastery of the Sixth Fang. At this point he has understood and memorized the theory and solved the path forward.

## Returning to the shallow shelves

The Sixth Form is not Vikir's real objective. After closing the book he returns to the shallowest, publicly accessible portion of the Inner Library. Servants and Guardian Knights are surprised that he appears to be finished after only a few hours and assume he is too young to understand the value of the deeper manuals.

Vikir deliberately lets them underestimate him. He says he still intends to look through other books, then searches the neglected miscellaneous shelves.

## Canonical scripture: Lurking Embedded Teeth

The recovered witnesses give unstable English/Korean titles such as `Lurking Ambush`, `Hidden Eagle`, `Hidden Blade`, and `Lurking Fang`. Supporting Fandom story/synopsis/martial-skill pages consistently identify the **scripture/book** as **Lurking Embedded Teeth**, which contains Baskerville's true essence and the path toward the Tenth Fang.

Use the terminology as follows:
- **Lurking Embedded Teeth** = the ancient damaged scripture/manual Vikir finds and later burns.
- **Lurking Fang** = the Tenth Fang/form itself when the narrative reaches that specific technique.

This distinction prevents the source witnesses' shifting machine-translations from collapsing book title and technique name into one term.

The book is physically old, dusty and unprotected because everyone believes it is a useless basic manual. Its important pages have been torn out, leaving the surviving text fragmented and apparently nonsensical. This explains why it was discarded among miscellaneous books despite its true value.

Vikir alone understands its significance because he remembers the first timeline's operations to recover the missing pages. Chapter 15 should reveal that he remembers those missing contents, but the full history and number of pages belong to Chapter 16.

Exact endpoint: Vikir remembers that in his first life **he was the hound who crossed countless battle lines to recover the pages torn from this scripture**.
