## Seven-year time skip and title wordplay

Seven years have passed since the Camus–Vikir duel. Vikir and the children of his generation are now about fifteen.

The chapter title `The Graduate` is deliberately ambiguous in English: the trainees are literally **graduating** from seven years of Baskerville education, while several characters are also discussed in the established **Sword Graduator** combat rank. Keep those concepts distinct in prose.

## Staffordshire Baskerville

Canonical name: **Staffordshire Baskerville**. Supporting Fandom material identifies him as an experienced Baskerville Guide Dog/instructor.

The Korean describes him as thirty-five and at the **Intermediate Sword Graduator** level. His sword curriculum, however, is a perfected **Baskerville Fourth Form / four fangs**. Do not force the simplified one-rank/one-form age table onto him: aura realm and number of learned Baskerville Forms can diverge, especially for someone who earned the family surname rather than being raised in the direct-line curriculum.

Staffordshire is an outsider/common-born retainer who earned the Baskerville surname through talent and service. Normalize the MTL's confused `outsider/commoner, not even given Van/La` sentence to that meaning without inventing a bloodline.

## Oxbear

Reconstruct the creature window as:
- **Oxbear**
- Danger Level: **A**
- Adult Size: **5 m**
- Discovery Location: **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain, Seventh Ridge**
- a massive horned bear-like predator

The specific captive used in training is a **sub-adult**, described narratively as roughly 800 kg and about 3 m tall. This reconciles the apparent 3 m versus 5 m discrepancy rather than treating one witness as wrong.

Supporting Fandom material independently lists Oxbear as A-rank. Later equipment/reference material also treats Oxbear as the source of a skill, confirming the creature identity.

## Graduation exercise

The fifteen- and sixteen-year-old trainees have reached at least **Low Sword Expert / First Form / one fang**. Staffordshire deliberately does not kill the captive oxbear himself. He teaches the group to:
- evade a stronger frontal charge without fleeing too far;
- take small attacks while circling;
- attack balance/mobility first rather than greedily seeking an immediate killing blow;
- repeatedly damage the right hind leg until the horn charge is neutralized.

More than thirty First-Form fangs then tear down the weakened oxbear. Highbro, Middlebro and Lowbro are the most aggressive members of the group.

Staffordshire opens the corpse and distributes the meat/organs as lunch. He then formally ends **3.5 years basic training + 3.5 years practical training = 7 years total** and tells the children that captured-monster exercises were only a tutorial. Their next roughly **21 months** will be real frontier experience.

## Seven Knight Orders

The graduates discuss future assignments among Baskerville's Seven Knight Orders. Keep named orders such as Wolfhound, Doberman, Pit Bull, Rottweiler, Shepherd and Bull Mastiff where supplied; do not invent a missing seventh name merely to make the list numerically complete.

The Wolfhounds are treated as the prestigious pinnacle; assignment is selective rather than simply chosen by preference.

## Vikir's public and real strength at fifteen

Public rumor:
- **Advanced Sword Expert**
- **three fangs / Third Form**
- solo oxbear kill

Actual hidden state:
- **Intermediate Sword Graduator**
- **five Baskerville Forms learned/usable in principle**
- River Styx blessing
- Beelzebub

This is a major progression from the Camus family. The recovered line `beyond that of a Graduator Intermediate` is normalized to Vikir **being at Intermediate Sword Graduator**, not vaguely beyond an undefined grade. He remains in the process of fully stabilizing/mastering that level and Fifth-Form output.

The solo oxbear at his feet is a larger wild specimen than the captive sub-adult killed by the group. Vikir used hidden strength to kill it; the other youths only know the resulting corpse and rumors.

## Academy lead

The conversation about the **Imperial Academy, Colosseo** finally interests Vikir. He does not care about prestige or ordinary education. He sees the Academy as:
- a chance to move away from Hugo's immediate supervision;
- a way to improve independently;
- an opportunity to settle first-life debts and seize future resources/artifacts.

Butler **John Barrymore** then summons him to Hugo. Barrymore says Hugo has decided Vikir's post/position.

Exact endpoint: Vikir learns that Hugo has decided his position. Do not import the Deputy Magistrate appointment from Chapter 27 backward.
