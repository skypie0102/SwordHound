## Baskerville triplet reversal

Vikir is genuinely surprised that **Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro** are now protecting him rather than harassing him. In the old timeline they contributed to his capture and eventual execution.

Their new loyalty is tied to Baskerville's strength-oriented culture and to Vikir's Chapter 40 recommendation that they accompany him to the Academy. Vikir recognizes that their judgment is limited but that they may become useful, highly obedient combat assets.

## Six-way youth confrontation

The Morgue and Baskerville triplets renew their long-running rivalry.

The individual Morgue elemental assignments are taken from the Korean/source pair:
- **Lowsis — water**
- **Midsis — earth**
- **Highsis — plant/grass**

A secondary fan wiki reverses Highsis/Lowsis, but the primary Korean witness and recovered source agree with the assignments above, so the reconstruction follows the primary evidence.

The Baskerville brothers are still junior Sword Experts using the First Form; the Morgue sisters are similarly young mages using first-circle magic. Vikir therefore finds the contest trivial despite the excitement of the surrounding knights.

## Camus intervention

The youth fight is abruptly stopped by a vastly stronger combination of fire and iron magic. Both sides are nearly caught in it, and the Morgue triplets are frightened despite belonging to the same family as the caster.

The caster is **Camus Morgue**, now fifteen, reunited with Vikir after seven years.

- established future title: **Ironblood Empress**
- scene-specific epithets: **Queen of Fire and Skewers** / **Queen of Red and Black**
- signature imagery: fire plus iron spikes
