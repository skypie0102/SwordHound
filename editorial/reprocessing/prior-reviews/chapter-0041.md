## Baskerville inspection party

Vikir leaves Underdog with Guardian Knight **Staffordshire Baskerville**, who is again acting as escort. Staffordshire reports that Count Boston Terrier continues trying to recruit Vikir into the Pit Bull Knights and specifically wants to keep the Wolfhound Knights from taking him first.

The party travels through the agricultural basin near Red Awl Mountain and the leased ruby-mining district. Farms, slave labor, mines, forts, and watchtowers are observed as part of the nominal estate inspection.

## Hidden joint operation

The visit is only partly diplomatic. Baskerville and Morgue are preparing a coordinated strike against the barbarians beyond the mountains.

Morgue has concealed armed personnel among the miners. The Korean witness adds an important force detail omitted by the MTL summary: beyond the ranked Light- and Dark-Faction personnel, approximately **100 additional mages** are present.

Baskerville appears to have brought only Vikir and a few dozen ordinary knights, but its real elite force is concealed beyond the mountains in accordance with the joint plan.

## Camus foreshadowing

The iron spikes holding burned human and demonic corpses are a deliberate warning to monsters and barbarians. Vikir recognizes the style before Camus herself appears, establishing continuity with her fire-and-iron magic.

## Gate confrontation

The Morgue triplets try to delay the Baskerville delegation despite the visit having been arranged in advance. Vikir invokes his authority as Deputy Magistrate and notes that he has already amended the tenant law they attempt to use against him.

The sisters respond with magic. Staffordshire concludes that words will not resolve the confrontation.

Then **Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro** step forward. Their final declaration identifies Vikir—not themselves—as the figure representing Baskerville's standing in the confrontation.
