## Auction infiltration

The illegal slave auction operates at night on the outskirts of Underdog beneath black tents and is deliberately hidden from Baskerville's official reporting system.

**Chihuahua Montblanc** is Chihuahua Baskerville's auction disguise. His forged Montblanc signature and paperwork exploit the calligraphy/imitation skill established in Chapter 31. He enters as a seller with one young male captive waiting backstage.

The auction's own host openly confirms that captives include displaced barbarians, kidnapped commoners and isolated nobles who are conditioned through restraint, drugs and abuse until resistance collapses.

## Human auction

The auction makes no meaningful moral distinction between art, weapons, beasts and human beings. Captives include displaced barbarians, kidnapped nobles and commoners.

Descriptions of trafficking and exploitation are kept clinically faithful rather than sensationalized. The source establishes coercion, imprisonment and sale; it does not need erotic amplification.

## The barbarian girl

A separate captive is a roughly **seventeen-year-old barbarian girl** with:
- black hair streaked with silver;
- pointed ears;
- ash deliberately smeared over a naturally light-brown face;
- a thorned collar and torn animal hides;
- a lean, trained body covered in scars;
- a language the auction audience cannot understand.

Even the traffickers cannot control her. She is offered at successively lower prices from five million to one million gold and receives no bids. While being removed, she bites off a trader's finger.

Keep this girl distinct from Vikir and from Chihuahua's forged seller identity. Her later relevance is not revealed in Chapter 35.

## Premium lot cliffhanger

To recover the auction's atmosphere after the failed sale, the host unveils the night's supposed premium item: a calm, chained boy of roughly fifteen.

The chapter ends the moment the audience sees his face and falls silent. The reader can identify him as Vikir, but Chapter 36 supplies the explicit confirmation and the bidding war. Do not import the breakout early.
