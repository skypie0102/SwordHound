## End of the Camus–Vikir match

Vikir maneuvers Camus into collateral fire from the neighboring fifteen-year-old bout. The accident is minor in combat terms but burns much of Camus's outer clothing. Keep the scene matter-of-fact and childlike: Camus is embarrassed and frightened that the spectators may see her in her underclothes; do not sexualize the eight-year-old characters.

## Ruby-mine solution

Hugo asks Vikir directly what should be done about the ruby mine. Vikir recommends granting Morgue the practical mining rights.

This is **not** capitulation. Vikir recognizes the strategic value of putting Morgue personnel at the downstream/frontier side of the Red Fang Mountain dispute while Baskerville continues expanding against monsters and barbarians farther up the mountain system.

The intended strategy is:
- Morgue receives the ruby access it desperately wants;
- Baskerville avoids wasting effort on a mineral it values less;
- Morgue personnel become a convenient downstream barrier / pressure sink while Baskerville advances its frontier;
- fleeing hostile creatures and barbarians are naturally driven toward the Morgue mining zone instead of deeper into newly claimed Baskerville territory.

Vikir knows this plan because Hugo used essentially the same strategy in the first timeline. Hugo is delighted because Vikir has independently given the answer Hugo himself prefers.

Use the established **Red Fang Mountain / Le Rouge et Le Noir mountain-system** terminology and document the Korean/MTL Red Peak/Red Spire variants as witness corruption rather than inventing additional locations.

## Marriage proposal

Adolf then proposes a marriage link to make the territorial/mining arrangement politically respectable rather than look like simple paid access.

His first proposal is **Camus Morgue and Osiris Le Baskerville**, i.e. Morgue's eldest/direct daughter with Baskerville's eldest son. The Korean explicitly gives Camus as eight and Osiris as twenty, a **twelve-year** difference. Correct the recovered MTL's impossible `seventeen-year difference` arithmetic.

Hugo rejects the match partly because Osiris is already bound by a betrothal arranged before birth.

Camus then states that she will not marry anyone weaker than her mother, Raspane. She also says she prefers someone her age or younger rather than a much older man, and her attention shifts toward Vikir.

Adolf immediately objects because Vikir bears the `Van` middle name rather than direct-line `Le`. Hugo, who has repeatedly said he values talent over pedigree, is irritated by the slight and openly pushes back on the idea that birthline alone decides worth.

When Hugo asks Vikir what he thinks about marriage, Vikir answers that he will obey if Hugo orders it. This is hound-like loyalty, not a romantic declaration.

## Adolf pressures Vikir

Adolf is a **Sixth Circle Master**, an important Morgue political/diplomatic representative, and an overprotective uncle to Camus. He leans close and warns Vikir that becoming Camus's husband would require far more than his current standing.

The chapter ends on Vikir's deadpan response: **“You said it wasn't for me to hear, so I stopped listening.”** Do not pull Adolf's direct test from Chapter 25 backward.
