Vikir deliberately answers like an eight-year-old, describing the sensations of aura in childish physical terms rather than explaining the Sixth Form or Lurking Embedded Teeth.

## Pure physical skill before aura

Vikir initially uses **no aura at all**. He exploits the size difference, ducks beneath the orc's reach, severs one wrist, passes beneath it, and takes an ankle before the creature understands what happened.

The surrounding family reads this as impossible physical talent. The important truth is different: Vikir's decades of battlefield experience, River-Styx-tempered body, and current strength make veteran technique look like childhood genius.

The Korean explicitly describes him as moving like a battlefield veteran; preserve that rather than the MTL's awkward `looks like an old man` line.

## Deliberately revealed Low Sword Expert aura

Hugo remains silent because Vikir still has not shown the result he supposedly obtained in the library. Vikir then reveals a **faint gaseous aura** at the sword tip—the hallmark of a Sword Expert.

This is a controlled deception. Vikir's actual aura has already crossed into Low Graduator territory, but he reveals only enough density to look like a prodigious **Low Sword Expert**.

The short sword is about four kilograms in the Korean witness. Preserve the sense that this would be heavy for an ordinary child but is effortless in Vikir's hands.
