## Fifth Fang breakthrough

Vikir finishes memorizing the reconstructed scripture word for word, to the point that he believes he could recite it forward or backward.

He tests the motions in the air with his hand as a substitute for a sword. The first four fang trajectories are familiar. He then produces a **fifth trajectory** for the first time. It is smaller, blunter and unstable because of his eight-year-old body, but it is unquestionably the Fifth Fang.

Do not overstate this as perfect Fifth-Fang mastery. The breakthrough proves the path works; the fifth fang is still immature.

Vikir briefly wishes for a real sword to test aura flow, but refuses to expose **Beelzebub** merely for experimentation inside the library.

## Rank advancement: Low Sword Graduator

The conceptual breakthrough also raises Vikir's aura state. Keep the rank system consistent with Chapters 4 and 11:
- Advanced Sword Expert = gaseous aura / approximately Third-Circle Mage.
- **Low Sword Graduator = liquid/materialized aura / approximately Fourth-Circle Mage.**

Vikir has crossed into the **beginning/low Sword Graduator** stage. A Graduator can envelop the blade with denser, liquid-like aura whose shape is more controllable than the gaseous aura of a Sword Expert. The source's water-vapor-versus-water comparison expresses the qualitative difference.

In his first life Vikir did not reach this level until roughly age thirty. In the current timeline he is only eight.

The source says this may be unprecedented or nearly unprecedented for someone his age in Baskerville history; preserve the cautious framing rather than asserting an absolute historical record without evidence.

## Old hound swordsmanship versus the restored scripture

Before regression Vikir's working style was the **Stabbing Fang** school taught to expendable Baskerville hounds. It let him become useful quickly and kill reliably, but imposed clear growth limits and demanded large personal risk.

The swordsmanship encoded in **Lurking Embedded Teeth** is fundamentally different. It conceals power and aggression, appears slow/elegant or unthreatening, protects the user, then bites into an opening and follows with relentless attacks designed to maximize damage and pain.

Do not rename the scripture itself `Lurking Ambush` or `Hidden Eagle`. In project terminology:
- **Lurking Embedded Teeth** = scripture/manual and the complete system it records.
- **Lurking Fang** = the Tenth Fang when specifically referring to the ultimate form.
- **Stabbing Fang** = the ordinary hound-oriented style Vikir previously used.

The important tactical benefit in this chapter is concealment: the restored style looks similar enough to familiar Baskerville fang techniques that Vikir can hide how far he has really advanced.

## Current combat estimate

Vikir assesses himself at age eight:
- four fangs can be produced reliably;
- the Fifth Fang can be produced, but unsteadily;
- his aura has crossed into low Graduator territory;
- he retains decades of first-life combat experience;
- Beelzebub supplies additional monster-derived abilities.

Against an **Intermediate Sword Graduator**, Vikir estimates that a prepared assassination would be effectively certain while a direct confrontation would be roughly a fifty-fifty proposition. Preserve this as Vikir's own tactical estimate, not an objective system guarantee.

## How much strength to reveal

Vikir deliberately chooses not to display everything. His rule of thumb is to keep roughly thirty percent of his power hidden.

However, hiding too much would also trap him under Hugo's direct supervision. He wants to show enough talent to increase Hugo's expectations and earn opportunities away from the main family—examples in the source include a **provincial posting** or **Academy placement**—without showing enough to make enemies panic or Hugo scrutinize him too closely.

This calculation directly sets up the next title family, where Hugo tests Vikir's claimed awakening.

## Burning Lurking Embedded Teeth

After memorizing the book, Vikir uses a reading magnifier and sunlight at the window to ignite **Lurking Embedded Teeth**. The physical scripture containing Baskerville's route toward ten fangs is thereby destroyed; its complete contents remain only in Vikir's memory.

Servants rush in with water. Vikir apologizes and offers to report the incident if necessary. He then exploits their desire to avoid paperwork and Barrymore's current preoccupation with a **ruby-mine dispute involving the Morgue family**. Everyone agrees the destroyed book looked like a worthless miscellaneous volume and would rather treat the matter as trivial.

Vikir says he remembers the contents and can provide an appropriate replacement/transcription. Do not infer more than the source says about what that replacement would contain.

His improved hearing lets him hear the servants praising him afterward, but he ignores it. His attention is already on how he will answer Hugo when summoned after leaving the library.

Exact endpoint: Vikir rehearses how he will handle Hugo's expected questioning/test once he exits the Inner Library.
