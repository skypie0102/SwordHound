## Editorial decisions

- Dolores initially communicates through a speaking stone statue rather than meeting the masked visitor directly; Vikir considers that caution reasonable.
- Vikir presents contaminated water only as evidence of the fictional Red Death outbreak and attempts to leave after reporting it.
- The Quovadis residence is deliberately defensible: doors seal and the lobby converts into an enclosed combat area with paladins above.
- Introduce **Mozgus Quovadis** as an inquisitor/archbishop figure whom Vikir remembers as a trusted pre-regression battlefield comrade. Mozgus does not know Vikir in the current timeline.
- Normalize rank language conservatively: Mozgus is an upper-level Graduator combatant, while Vikir's current aura is dense liquid Graduator aura. Do not create a new parallel rank system from unstable MTL labels.
- Mozgus's massive holy book is both weapon and shield; his page-edge aura attacks remain intentionally unusual.
- Vikir uses Ballak movement/assassination footwork, Beelzebub, and his stored Oxbear physical-stability ability. The staging avoids locking the MTL's unstable `Thousand Muscles` label as a new canonical Beelzebub slot name.
- Preserve the roughly **600 kg** effective-weight effect already associated with Vikir's Oxbear-derived stability.
- Vikir uses six concealed Baskerville fangs but judges the form visually unrecognizable enough that the display will not immediately expose his identity.
- Cerberus Burn wounds Mozgus; ordinary suppression is ineffective, but Mozgus converts statue water into **holy water** and extinguishes it through Quovadis power.
- Do not overstate Mozgus's defeat. Vikir clearly wins the one-on-one exchanges, but Mozgus remains capable of standing and fighting after healing/support from holy power.
- Mozgus's tactical assessment is preserved as his own: one-on-one defeat appears virtually certain, while a many-against-one fight is still risky and unresolved.
- Vikir did not come to kill Mozgus or Quovadis paladins. The confrontation stops when **Saint Dolores** personally appears.
