# Hounds of Hell (1–4) title-family review — Chapters 8–11

This batch is defined by the contiguous base title **Hounds of Hell** in the chapter tracker: Chapter 8 `(1)` through Chapter 11 `(4)`. Chapter 12 changes base title to **The Gluttonous Flies (1)** and is therefore outside this batch.

All four chapters use the `korean_plus_mtl` review basis. Their recovered MTL and supplied Korean sources remain unchanged. Chapter-level source conflicts, audit triage, structural mappings and endpoints are documented in `chapter-0008.md` through `chapter-0011.md`.

## Cross-chapter continuity locked for this family

- **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain** is the working proper-name form. Literal/secondary `Red and Black Mountains` wording is recorded as a variant, not a separate location.
- **Guide Hounds** are the Guardian Knights supervising the practical examination boundary.
- The practical examination begins in Chapter 8; Chapter 9 shows Vikir leaving the ordinary score contest and preparing the hidden pit; Chapters 10–11 pay off the Bloody Beans, Hellhound mechanics and trap preparation in reveal order.
- **Bloody Beans / cacao** remain consistent from Chapter 7 onward. Hellhounds are vulnerable to chocolate; Chapter 10 establishes the systemic poisoning sequence and Chapter 11 applies Bloody-Bean liquid through prepared stakes.
- The **Cradle** inside the secured ridge area, the **Forbidden Zone** outside it, the **Cradle of Swords** ritual, and Chapter 11's **Cradle of Needles** trap are distinct concepts and must not be flattened into one term.
- Sword ranks follow the accepted Chapter 4 register: Sword Beginner / Sword Expert / Sword Graduator / Sword Master, with low/intermediate/advanced subdivisions where the source supplies them.
- River Styx protection is strong but finite. Chapter 10 uses high resistance rather than universal invulnerability; Chapter 11 confirms that Cerberus can still break several of Vikir's ribs.
- Highbro, Middlebro and Lowbro retain established order/names and their broken fighting spirit from Chapters 5–7. Chapter 11 explains that their three-person tactics draw on Cerberus-style three-headed attack patterns.
- Cerberus is A+, about 7 m, associated with the Seventh Ridge, and presented with the AKA `Hell’s Guard Dog`. The malformed Chapter 10 window and repeated Chapter 11 window are normalized without deleting recovered source slots.
- Chapter 11 ends on the reveal that **Bloody Mamba venom** still lingers on the hidden thorns. No Chapter 12 aftermath is imported into this title family.
