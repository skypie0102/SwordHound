## Family continuity

### Post-crackdown political settlement
Burning Suspension is destroyed, the seven criminal heirs have been executed, and Vikir publicly establishes that legal status does not change liability before the law.

The seven houses initially protest, but the seizure of black ledgers, financial records, and evidence of illegal slave trafficking exposes them to an Imperial-level treason investigation. Their political resistance collapses.

Their assets are seized, implicated members receive enhanced sentences under Noblesse Oblige, and the houses cease to function as meaningful competing powers in Underdog.

### Administrative support and reform
Formerly marginalized honest officials, respected intellectuals, teachers, and other civic figures rally behind Vikir. Reinvestigation of older cases frees wrongfully imprisoned people and drives new public-sector hiring, funded by recovered underground-economy revenue.

Citizens begin daily demonstrations asking Vikir to remain as Deputy Magistrate.

Vikir also learns handwriting imitation from Chihuahua and uses memories of successful future policy to accelerate legal and administrative reform.

### Cindywendy
Chapter 38's final prison reference leads directly into Chapter 39, where the only remaining prisoner is **Sen Rose Cindywendy**, Baron Gambino's former secretary and a highly capable financier.

Vikir deliberately spared her during the auction raid and questions her not merely for information, but for an outside assessment of his rule.

### Political advice
Cindywendy argues that Vikir's rule is highly effective but dangerously dependent on fear and harsh law. She advises him to withdraw from the Deputy Magistrate post while his reputation is strong, return formal authority to Baskerville, let senior institutions absorb the political consequences, then hide his strength and develop himself through training or the Academy.

Her advice closely matches Vikir's existing plan.

### House Messinadnaro reveal
The seven native houses were once eight. **House Messinadnaro**, the richest merchant house, was framed for stealing Baskerville swordsmanship after its eight-year-old heir publicly demonstrated the Baskerville Style.

The other seven houses engineered the framing. Baskerville then annihilated Messinadnaro as punishment for what it believed was theft of a military secret.

The sole survivor is Cindywendy:
- alias: **Sen Rose Cindywendy**
- real name: **Messinadnaro Sen Cindywendy**

The seven heirs tortured and executed by Vikir confessed their role and left blood-written apologies. Vikir also explicitly acknowledges House Baskerville's responsibility for carrying out the massacre.

### Sponsor endpoint
Vikir bows to Cindywendy on behalf of Baskerville and apologizes. She recognizes that his own hostility toward the family runs deep.

Cindywendy leaves the dungeon promising that Vikir will never lack money, establishing her as his new financial sponsor.
