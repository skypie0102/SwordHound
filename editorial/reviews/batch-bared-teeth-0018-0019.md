## Central continuity rule: controlled disclosure

The entire family is built around the gap between Vikir's real strength and the strength he deliberately shows House Baskerville.

After *Solitary (1–4)*, Vikir's actual state is **Low Sword Graduator** with an unstable **Fifth Fang** and the concealed Beelzebub artifact. He does not reveal that state here.

Public progression in this family is intentionally limited:
1. Chapter 18: Vikir first defeats the orc through physical skill, then reveals only a **faint gaseous aura**, **Low Sword Expert**, and **Baskerville First Form / one Stabbing Fang**.
2. Chapter 19: against the troll, he initially repeats that same one-fang performance, then permits a modest increase to **Intermediate Sword Expert** and **Baskerville Second Form / two fangs**.
3. He never exposes a Graduator's liquid/materialized aura, the Fifth Fang, Lurking Fang, or Beelzebub's existence to the observers.

This concealment gradient is deliberate. Vikir wants Hugo to see enough extraordinary promise that Vikir may later gain freedom through a provincial posting, Imperial Capital study, or possibly Academy admission, without provoking the scrutiny that his true power would cause.

## Chapter 18 — orc test

Hugo summons Vikir immediately after the Inner Library visit and asks what he learned. Vikir gives deliberately childlike, vague sensory descriptions. Hugo suspects aura awakening and asks for a demonstration.

The training-ground rumors must remain consistent with the earlier chapters: Styx survival, killing venomous snakes in the Cradle, and the Cerberus feat.

The orc is treated as a C-rank humanoid demonic creature, roughly two meters tall and physically equivalent to several unarmed adult men. The recovered English location wording is too unstable to elevate into a new canonical place name without stronger evidence.

Vikir severs a wrist and ankle through pure physical technique before showing aura. Beelzebub's stored Hellhound **Bleeding** effect suppresses the orc's rapid regeneration, but Vikir does not reveal Beelzebub itself. He then displays one Stabbing Fang and kills the orc.

Hugo is visibly pleased but immediately rationalizes the feat by suggesting the orc must have been weakened. Vikir then asks for a stronger opponent.

## Chapter 19 — troll test

The troll entry is reconstructed as:
- **Troll**
- Danger Level: **C+**
- Size: **4 m**
- Discovery Location: **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain, Second Ridge**
- a massive cannibalistic humanoid with extreme regeneration

The recovered English `C` rank is corrected to **C+** from the Korean witness and supporting creature reference. The recovered `Zagoro troll` phrase is discarded; no supplied witness establishes a separate subtype.

Vikir refuses a seemingly easy flank strike because noncritical wounds would simply regenerate. Instead he lets the restrained, starving troll exhaust itself, then uses its own body weight and momentum to drive a one-fang gaseous-aura strike through the neck. The troll survives by regenerating.

Although Beelzebub's Bleeding could eventually kill it, Vikir rejects a delayed artifact-assisted victory because it would not demonstrate the public level he wants to show.

When Hugo tries to stop the fight, Vikir asks for the troll's restraints to be removed and promises to stop if his next strike fails. Hugo's silence grants permission.

The unrestricted troll's faster charge supplies greater opposing force. Vikir slightly densifies his still-gaseous aura and reveals **Baskerville Second Form / two fangs**, publicly establishing **Intermediate Sword Expert**. The second strike severs the troll's neck.

Exact family endpoint: the decapitated troll falls to its knees while Hugo and the other Baskervilles stand frozen in disbelief. Do not import Chapter 20's reaction or Morgue material backward.
