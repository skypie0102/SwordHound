## Family continuity

- Dolores interrupts the Night Hound/Mozgus confrontation and becomes the first Quovadis authority willing to hear Vikir's warning directly.
- Vikir's obscure theological quotation and his prior-life familiarity with Dolores cause her to misread him as an unusually learned and wounded religious seeker.
- Vikir's political diagnosis identifies **Leviathan** and **Bourgeois** as likely beneficiaries of the epidemic, but the family preserves the difference between prior-life inference, current suspicion, and provable evidence.
- Quovadis mobilizes quickly, contains the St. Mecca outbreak before fatalities, and demonstrates that Dolores's holy power can cure the Red Death.
- Vikir helps inside the quarantine wards using future-war supernatural healing knowledge and Ballak survival experience, which further deepens Dolores's trust in him.
- Quovadis distills three concentrated drops of **Saintess's Tears**, each powerful enough to cleanse a major infected water source.
- Vikir's St. Mecca operation is deliberately manipulative: he creates a limited local outbreak, warns children away from the contaminated well, alerts Quovadis before deaths occur, and plants Leviathan/Bourgeois evidence to redirect Quovadis hostility toward rival great houses.
- Cardinal Humbert treats the planted dagger and button as genuine causal evidence and declares hostile action. The review therefore does not present his conclusion as independently verified truth.
- Dolores openly defends the Night Hound against her father's order to detain him, a rare break from her usual obedience.
- Vikir leaves before the arrest party reaches him and takes exactly **one drop of Saintess's Tears**, fulfilling the cure-acquisition objective that brought him to Quovadis territory.
- The family ends with Dolores understanding his immediate purpose only after he is gone and feeling the emotional impact of his disappearance.
