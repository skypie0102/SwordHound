# Chapter 5 source comparison — 2026-09-14

Korean lines 1–4 are repeated headings; paragraph 148 owns two consecutive Korean lines because the Korean witness places Highbro’s short-sword detail on a separate physical line. No source scene break occurs in this chapter. The recovered MTL and supplied Korean remain preserved unchanged and are not authenticated publisher witnesses.

## Triplets, ages, and action attribution

Keep **Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro**. Vikir is eight and the triplets are nine, continuing Chapter 4. Search-retrieved Fandom character pages identify Highbro as the eldest and Lowbro as the youngest, and the Middlebro synopsis gives the same childhood encounter: Lowbro restrains Vikir and covers his mouth, Vikir holds his breath for ten minutes and bites off one of Lowbro’s fingers, Highbro then tries to stab him, and Vikir retaliates against Highbro and Middlebro before throwing the knife.

This matters because the supplied Korean witness contains two localized actor corruptions. Its line 43 calls Lowbro the eldest even though the surrounding scene and character references identify him as the youngest. Later, line 96 momentarily says Highbro is releasing Vikir even though Lowbro has continuously been the brother covering Vikir’s nose and mouth. The reconstruction keeps **Lowbro** in both places rather than inventing an off-page handoff.

## Location and obvious MTL corruption

Retain **Fang Castle**, already established in Chapter 4. The Korean witness calls the setting the “fortress of young wolves”; that variant is treated as a description/alternate witness, not evidence for a second location. Paragraph 26’s MTL line about a hallway where the triplets “didn’t wear their bikinis” is a clear corruption. Korean instead places the group in a secluded room at the end of an unused corridor, which is restored.

The route through a half-flooded cellar entrance, abandoned food storeroom, cracks, and spiral stairs is retained without adding architectural details absent from the witnesses.

## Breath-holding, finger wager, and Styx protection

Chapter 4 already established the triplets’ disbelief about Vikir’s seven-minute Styx immersion. Here they deliberately block his breathing. The chapter counts from one through ten minutes. Korean line 88 explicitly describes ten minutes as **600 seconds**, so paragraph 84 corrects the MTL’s nonsensical “counting from 1 to 60.” Vikir does not raise mana during the breath-holding test.

Highbro wagers a finger if Vikir can last three minutes. Lowbro is the one restraining him and is the one whose index finger Vikir bites off when the restraint loosens. Korean later calls the finger “broken,” but that conflicts with the MTL’s severing, the repeated plan to have a priest reattach it, and the supporting synopsis. The reconstruction therefore keeps the severed-finger reading and makes the wager callback explicit.

When Highbro attacks again, Korean supplies a separate detail that he is holding a short sword; it is folded into paragraph 148 so the 183-paragraph MTL structure is conserved. The blade strikes Vikir, the contact point flashes black, and no blood is drawn. “Impervious to blades” is used only for the physical protection demonstrated in this scene. It is not a claim that Vikir is universally invulnerable to magic, aura, poison, or later threats.

## Hugo’s child-rearing maxim

MTL paragraphs 144–146 are severely garbled. The Korean witness gives a coherent two-part maxim: after a sufficient beating, nobody dares talk back; if someone still talks back, the question is whether the beating was severe enough. The next line attributes that philosophy to Hugo and says it functions as the Baskerville family’s code of conduct. The reconstruction restores that meaning without modernizing it into approval or moral commentary.

## Violence, sounds, and ending

Pronoun and subject errors in the fight are corrected without changing the event order: Vikir breaks Highbro’s nose and teeth, dislocates Middlebro’s jaw, blocks the door, warns that delayed treatment may leave lasting damage, withstands Highbro’s blade, then knocks out the rest of Highbro’s teeth. Opaque MTL sound tokens are rendered contextually as short impact sounds rather than preserved as meaningless romanizations.

The chapter ends when Vikir says only one of the three may leave alive, throws/leaves a dagger between them, and forces the brothers to decide. The final line remains: **the Trident of Baskerville begins to split from within**. The subsequent fight, healing, Hugo meeting, or any Chapter 6 consequence is deliberately excluded.
