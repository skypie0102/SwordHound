## Rubber dagger reveal

Hugo reprimands Vikir only mildly and then takes visible satisfaction in seeing the normally composed Adolf flustered. Keep Hugo's paternal `Son` / Vikir's `Father` exchange intact.

## Annual friendly exercise

The Baskerville–Morgue event follows the old imperial maxim that sword and magic oppose one another in ordinary times but complement one another in crisis.

Traditionally:
- ages roughly eight through fifteen participate;
- eight-year-olds focus on theory/mana sensitivity rather than serious combat;
- fifteen-year-olds provide the main sword-versus-magic bouts.

This year the eight-year-old match is deliberately elevated because both houses have a once-in-a-century prodigy: **Vikir Van Baskerville** and **Camus Morgue**.

## Camus's magic

Camus begins by revealing extraordinary multi-casting talent. Normalize `Quadra casting` to **Quadruple Casting** and preserve the point that she simultaneously maintains four First-Circle spells, a feat difficult even for fifteen-year-old Morgue mages.

The four spells are reconstructed from the Korean/MTL witnesses as:
- Fireball
- Ice Spear (the MTL's `Ice Sphere` is a variant/mistranslation)
- Lightning Ring
- Mud Wall

Her mistake is tactical rather than magical: she reveals too many cards immediately and divides concentration across multiple spells.

## Vikir refuses to expose his real strength

Vikir is not threatened by Camus's First-Circle offense. He avoids the attacks while closing distance and deliberately fights as an eight-year-old prodigy rather than as a hidden Low Graduator.

He breaks through her Mud Wall physically, reaches striking distance, and merely flicks her forehead instead of injuring her. Camus interprets the restraint as mockery and becomes progressively angrier.

When she layers Mud Wall, Vikir repeatedly breaks through the barriers and flicks the exact same point on her forehead. The comedy should remain sharp but not cruel: Vikir is intentionally using minimal force while demonstrating that her defensive choices are self-defeating.

## Four-wall trap and neighboring arena

Camus eventually encloses herself completely behind four Mud Walls, sacrificing vision for defense. Vikir waits for her to create a small viewing hole and lands another forehead flick through it.

She then abandons defense and launches four offensive spells at once. Vikir decides that dealing directly with an enraged child is troublesome and instead maneuvers her toward the boundary of the neighboring fifteen-year-old match.

The neighboring Morgue/Baskerville teenagers are already producing dangerous sword-and-magic collateral. Vikir's goal is to let the surrounding situation resolve the match without revealing additional power.

Exact endpoint: an explosion from the neighboring bout reaches their area; alarmed observers cry out Camus's name, and Vikir recognizes that the situation he engineered has begun. Do not import the clothing/fire aftermath from Chapter 24 backward.
