The chapter begins immediately after Pavlov rings the bell in Chapter 8. Baskerville children are accustomed to practical bouts beginning with a bell, so the familiar sound cuts through their unease and their trained fighting instincts take over.

Clever children may feign knowledge of a valuable location to lure rivals into pits or swamps. ## Four Hellhound weaknesses and chapter endpoint

Vikir exploits knowledge unavailable to most people of the current era:

1. Hellhounds charge in straight lines and handle abrupt direction changes poorly.
2. They refuse to cross even very shallow, thin water.
3. Canine demonic creatures run with their mouths open.
4. Chocolate is poisonous to canine demonic creatures.

He sidesteps the initial charge, then pours the drinking water collected from two days of dew into a thin line that forces the Hellhound to detour. He notes that this behavior will become common knowledge once the Demon Realm Gate opens and Hellhounds spread across the continent.

Even intermediate Expert-rank knights struggle against one one-on-one; before regression, Vikir himself only became reliably capable of soloing one around age eighteen.
