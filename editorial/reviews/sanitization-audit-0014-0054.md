# Sanitation audit — canonical Chapters 14–54

Date: 2026-09-16

Status: **sanitation-focused review complete for canonical Chapters 14–54**. This is not a general mistranslation audit; it specifically checks whether the prior staged reconstructions softened, euphemized, generalized, or omitted source-supported harsh / sexual / bodily / degrading material.

## Method

Evidence priority for this audit:

1. canonical Korean raw `source/korean/chapters/NNN.txt`;
2. the actual staged reconstruction on the applicable historical production branch (`editorial/staging/chapter-NNNN-final-text.txt`);
3. recovered English MTL only as a secondary witness;
4. prior production review notes as evidence, never as automatic acceptance.

The broad and strict diagnostic scans were used only to locate passages. Every item below was manually checked against Korean and staging. Heuristic hits that were already faithfully represented in staging were rejected rather than counted as findings.

**Result:** confirmed sanitation/source-intensity loss in **14 chapters**: **22, 24, 28, 34, 35, 39, 43, 45, 46, 48, 49, 52, 53, 54**.

No comparable confirmed sanitation was found in the remaining Chapters 14–54 during this sanitation-focused pass. That does not mean those chapters are free of ordinary translation, grammar, continuity, or terminology errors; those remain part of the broader reprocessing review.

## Confirmed findings

### Chapter 22 — future-adult Camus description softened

**Korean evidence** (`source/korean/chapters/022.txt`):

- `미인계 전술에도 능숙했다` — she was skilled in seduction / beauty-trap tactics.
- `그녀의 글래머러스한 몸매는 ... 황실 가족 조차도 매료시킬 만큼` — her glamorous / voluptuous figure was alluring enough to captivate even the Imperial Family.

**Staged reconstruction** (`production/batch-camus-morgue-0020-0025`):

- reduces the first point to using “charm and intelligence in politics”;
- reduces the second to “drawing even royalty into her orbit.”

**Finding:** confirmed euphemistic generalization. The source deliberately identifies seduction tactics and her physical figure as part of her future political influence.

**Required restoration:** retain both the seduction-tactics statement and the physical-description statement without adding erotic detail beyond the Korean.

### Chapter 24 — repeated nudity / bodily-detail sanitization

**Korean evidence** (`source/korean/chapters/024.txt`) explicitly states that:

- Camus’s face is covered in **tears and snot** while she faces being seen publicly in her underwear;
- a prior childhood punishment involved her being **stripped and driven out of the house** after talking back;
- Vikir gives her clothing while standing before her **wearing only his underwear**;
- Vikir’s thought explicitly uses **naked**: being able to be naked without losing dignity is a child’s privilege;
- children sometimes run around **naked**;
- after the smoke clears, Camus is wrapped in Baskerville training clothes while Vikir is standing **naked and bleeding**;
- other Baskervilles explicitly react to the still-naked Vikir.

**Staged reconstruction** replaces these with:

- generic humiliation, omitting the tears-and-snot detail;
- merely being “driven outside,” omitting that she had been stripped;
- Vikir giving up an “outer garment,” without stating he is left in underwear;
- “half-dressed” / “barely dressed” instead of the source’s repeated naked wording;
- a final reveal that avoids stating Vikir is naked.

**Finding:** confirmed multi-line sanitation block.

**Required restoration:** restore the source’s direct non-euphemized state descriptions while keeping the prose neutral and non-erotic.

### Chapter 35 — slave-auction degradation and sexual exploitation sanitized

This chapter contains several separate but related losses.

**A. Brutal conditioning softened**

Korean says the slaves were secretly abducted and **brutally tamed / broken in** until absolutely obedient and no longer inclined to flee or resist.

Staging uses `thoroughly conditioned`, removing `잔혹하게` / brutally.

**B. Naked captive omitted**

Korean explicitly identifies a **naked barbarian slave** on the auction stage. Staging changes this to merely `a barbarian captive on stage`.

**C. Sexual exploitation replaced with nonsexual labor**

Korean auction pitch:

`그녀를 침대에서 시중들게 할지, 아니면 검투사로 만들지!`

Meaning: **have her serve you in bed, or make her a gladiator.**

Staging instead advertises `hard labor, guard duty, or gladiatorial combat`.

**D. Dehumanizing “meat value” replaced**

When bidding collapses, Korean says in substance that at one million she is worthwhile **even just for the meat / if you only consider the meat value**.

Staging replaces this with `at that price you could buy her for labor alone`.

**Finding:** confirmed multi-line sanitation block affecting nudity, coercive sexual exploitation, brutality, and dehumanization.

**Required restoration:** reconstruct these lines directly from Korean. Preserve the bed-service wording and meat-value degradation without expanding beyond the source.

## Important non-findings

The broad diagnostics surfaced many raw-MTL problems that the staged reconstruction had already repaired. These are **not** counted as sanitation in our reconstructed work. Examples:

- Chapter 19: the troll’s roar is already restored with the entrails image.
- Chapter 39: the earlier `불구` passage is already restored as `crippled`; later vomiting, bloody tear, foul dungeon smell, spitting, and torture are retained.
- Chapter 36: Baron Gambino’s groping / sexual threat, Vikir tearing him apart, blood spray, severed heads, and Beelzebub drinking blood are retained.
- Chapter 38: severed heads and the source’s intentionally crude `You. Are. Fucked.` summary are retained.
- Chapter 52: the thorn-tree execution’s blood, burning, stabbing, wolves, and deaths are retained; the sanitation is specifically the omission of nakedness.

This distinction matters: the audit corrects only actual source-intensity loss and does not make already-faithful passages more graphic than the Korean.

## Reprocessing rule

These findings are mandatory inputs to canonical reconstruction. Historical staging on the production branches is evidence only and must not be reused verbatim where this audit identifies sanitation. During canonical reprocessing, each affected passage must be rebuilt from the Korean raw, then checked against the recovered MTL and surrounding continuity.
