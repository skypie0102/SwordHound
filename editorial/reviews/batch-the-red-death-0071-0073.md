## Family continuity

- Vikir remembers that the historical Red Death devastated the frontier, reached Imperial territory, and indirectly strengthened Baskerville by destroying tribal populations and destabilizing the mountain ecosystem.
- In that prior timeline, Camus Morgue helped contain spread toward the Empire with large-scale fire magic while Dolores of Quovadis treated Imperial victims. The tribes still suffered catastrophic losses.
- Vikir confirms contaminated water as the primary vector through controlled tests and teaches Ballak simple prevention: boiling water, reducing wetland exposure, and avoiding contaminated waste/corpses.
- Akwilla chooses to share the warning with neighboring tribes instead of treating disease knowledge as a Ballak monopoly.
- Ahmen correctly identifies one practical weakness in Vikir's first plan: record rainy-season flooding makes water avoidance impossible even though the shaman's curse explanation is wrong.
- Vikir's civil works raise Ballak into the canopy, secure bridges/housing, and redirect floodwater, allowing the settlement to survive record rainfall.
- During the flood, Vikir and Aiyen repel an A+ **Monsieur Hushu**. Vikir uses Beelzebub and the Sixth Fang to maim and drive it off, but does not kill it.
- Pomeranian remains closely attached to Vikir after rescue; her gender is normalized consistently to female across MTL pronoun drift.
- Aheul becomes infected despite the preventive system, proving that Ballak still lacks effective treatment once symptoms begin.
- Vikir concludes that an actual cure requires outside specialist knowledge/resources and asks to leave the depths.
- Aiyen deliberately bypasses Akwilla's likely refusal, taking personal responsibility for granting Vikir passage.
- Vikir leaves with Pomeranian, promising Aiyen that he will return. This is the handoff from the Ballak-centered epidemic defense into the next external phase.
