## The opening banquet is a trap

Vikir's first spoken order is effectively **“Bring me wine and women.”** It is not evidence that he has immediately become corrupt or debauched. He intentionally permits an extravagant welcome banquet to form around his arrival because the gathering will draw exactly the kind of people he wants to identify and target.

The chapter endpoint confirms the logic: if one wants to catch flies, one first puts out something that attracts them.

## Chihuahua Baskerville

- Chihuahua Baskerville is male.
- He is a long-serving Underdog City Hall administrator / chief secretary.
- Like Staffordshire, he entered Baskerville from outside the bloodline and earned the Baskerville surname through ability and service.
- He has **no family middle name**, which has historically exposed him to discrimination from bloodline nobles and incoming Deputy Magistrates.
- His accomplishments were often appropriated by superiors.
- Vikir remembers him from the first timeline as capable, conscientious and trustworthy despite his somewhat sly appearance.

Vikir explicitly prefers proven outsiders to blood relatives he does not trust. His positive reaction to Chihuahua's lack of a middle name is therefore intentional, not sarcasm.

## Underdog's real problem

Vikir quickly identifies the underground economy as the city's core administrative disease:
- illegal gambling;
- brothels / entertainment houses;
- slave auctions;
- private or illegal finance and moneylending;
- bribed officials.

His first-life intelligence and enforcement experience gives him prior knowledge of criminal organizations, hiding places, corrupt officials and black ledgers. That knowledge explains why he can identify the pattern much faster than an ordinary new official.

Vikir does **not** intend to rewrite the entire legal code. He tells Chihuahua he will preserve the existing laws and add only **one short new law**.

## Warning and justification

Vikir says the underground economy can be destroyed within **one week**, but he wants a justification for the new law and its enforcement. Because he is young and officially begins office the following day, he frames the banquet as advance warning / professional courtesy before the crackdown.

The recovered MTL's line about physically `floating off to the Academy` is not treated literally; the declaration instead marks another step toward producing the results that can earn Academy admission.
