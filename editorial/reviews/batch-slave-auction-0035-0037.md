## Family continuity

### Infiltration
Chihuahua Baskerville enters the illegal auction as **Chihuahua Montblanc**, using the signature-imitation skill established earlier. He poses as a seller with Vikir as the single young captive.

Vikir is deliberately offered as the night's premium lot so the operation can expose itself around him. The separate seventeen-year-old barbarian girl is an unrelated trafficking victim and must remain distinct from the infiltration team.

### Auction economy
The auction traffics displaced barbarians, kidnapped commoners and nobles, stolen demonic-creature carcasses, art, weapons, and other black-market goods. House Baskerville is not officially informed of the slave auction.

The stolen **Infernal Buffalo ‘Murciélago’** carcass is a recurring operational target: Danger Level A, 3 m, Second Ridge of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain, nineteen hearts.

### Breakout and military reveal
Baron Gambino wins Vikir for six billion gold and immediately abuses him. The passage is preserved clinically without erotic amplification.

Vikir breaks his restraints, reveals Beelzebub and the Baskerville Fifth Form, and demonstrates liquid crimson aura consistent with the **Sword Graduator** rank.

Chihuahua is briefly used as a hostage. Vikir then uses Hugo's red-fang whistle/horn to summon the Pit Bull Knights.

### Hugo's authorization
The hidden payoff to Vikir's Chapter 27 request is explicit in Chapter 37: Hugo granted him command of one knight order for half a day—**six hours**.

The summoned force is the **Pit Bull Knights**, roughly one hundred Sword Graduators led by **Count Boston Terrier Le Baskerville**.

### Cleanup and evidence
The Pit Bull Knights destroy or capture the auction's major criminal participants. Ledgers, receipts, contact records, cash, and valuables are seized for follow-up investigations and city revenue.

### Barbarian girl / Cerberus thread
Vikir finds the barbarian girl injured after the auction, heals her, and notices scars suggesting contact with Cerberus. He suspects her tribe may be connected to the unknown archers who previously wounded the A+ demonic creature.

He uses rudimentary barbarian speech to tell her to leave, gives her access to the remaining potion, and lets her escape. Her identity is not advanced beyond the source.

### End state
Vikir finally locates Murciélago's carcass for Beelzebub. The family ends with Beelzebub reacting hungrily while the Pit Bull Knights' fighting draws closer.
