## Family continuity

- The oxbear hunt's payoff is both social and mechanical: Vikir gains public hunting honor, accelerated physical recovery, and **Sacral Spine — Oxbear (A)** in Beelzebub.
- Beelzebub remains constrained to three stored abilities at this point: **Burn — Cerberus (A+)**, **Sacral Spine — Oxbear (A)**, **Tight Throat — Infernal Buffalo (A)**.
- Ballak's hunting economy is deliberately non-Imperial: the greatest contributor gives up ordinary meat share in exchange for honor, while carcasses are fully processed into food, hides, tools, fuel, and ritual offerings.
- Madam Eight-Legs remains an active territorial power. Ballak sacrifices part of major hunts to her, and Vikir confirms that the tribute is actually collected.
- Vikir's practical survival knowledge continues to earn social value beyond swordsmanship: butchery, preservation, fishing, and resource use all matter to his acceptance.
- The title family closes Vikir's **slave** phase. Akwilla formally recognizes him as Ballak after the oxbear hunt.
- Vikir rejects material reward and a partner arrangement and instead asks for the **Fountain of Valor**, because restoring his damaged body is strategically more valuable than wealth or status.
- The request exposes a political split between Akwilla/Aiyen and Ahmen/Ahun over how fully an Imperial-born newcomer should be allowed into Ballak's sacred institutions.
- The unresolved Fountain of Valor debate is interrupted by an outsider alarm, handing directly into **Unfair Trade (1)** at Chapter 63.
