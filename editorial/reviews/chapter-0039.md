## House Messinadnaro

Underdog once had eight major native houses. The eighth, **House Messinadnaro**, was the wealthiest merchant family.

The house was annihilated after its eight-year-old son publicly demonstrated the Baskerville Style, creating the appearance that the family had stolen a top-secret Baskerville sword manual.

The later truth was that heirs of the other seven houses engineered the incident. They arranged for a young Baskerville to obtain the material and for the Messinadnaro child to learn enough of it to demonstrate the technique before adults, thereby creating the evidence used to destroy the family.

One infant girl survived. She later became Sen Rose Cindywendy.
