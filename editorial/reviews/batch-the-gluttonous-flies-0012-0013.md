## Continuity anchors from Hounds of Hell

Chapter 12 begins immediately after Chapter 11's Bloody Mamba-venom reveal. Cerberus is dead, Vikir has already used Bloody Beans/chocolate and the hidden venom thorns, and the practical assessment is still underway. The corpse must remain sufficiently intact to serve as assessment evidence later.

Do not import later Beelzebub skills or later demon-sword lore into these two chapters.

## Shared terminology decisions

Use **Beelzebub** and the epithet **the Gluttonous Fly**. Supporting Fandom identifies Beelzebub as one of the Seven Calamities/Demon Constellations and confirms its skill-stealing blood-drinking mechanic.

Use the established early skill labels consistently:
- **Hemorrhage** (or Bleeding where a literal window label is required) — Hellhound, B+
- **Incinerate** (Burn in the compact window label) — Cerberus, A+
- **Rapid Regeneration** — Brown Rat Norvegicus, F

The weapon stores at most three abilities at this point in the story. Later replacement skills are not evidence for these chapters.

For the dungeon geography, keep **Red Fang Mountain** as the previously adjudicated branch of **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain**. The supplied Korean's `Red Peak` wording is recorded as a witness variant, not introduced as a new mountain.
