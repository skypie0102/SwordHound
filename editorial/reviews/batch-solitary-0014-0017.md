## Family continuity

### Chapter 14 — reward and access
- Practical evaluation ends; Baskerville's post-exam calm remains brutal rather than sentimental.
- Hugo learns that eight-year-old Vikir entered the Forbidden Zone and killed A+ Cerberus.
- Vikir deliberately concealed the chocolate/Bloody Bean weakness and Cerberus corpse until the exam ended.
- Hugo approves of information control and merit over bloodline.
- Vikir reveals chocolate as poison to canine demonic creatures only to Hugo, whom he identifies as his master because Hugo is family head.
- Hugo grants Vikir the entire Cerberus corpse and, as an additional reward, access to the **Inner Library**.
- Hugo recommends the **Baskerville Sixth Form**, while Vikir secretly seeks a far more valuable scripture.

### Chapter 15 — locating the scripture
- The Inner Library is presented as an archive of generations of lethal Baskerville theory rather than a comforting scholarly space.
- The Sixth Form gives Vikir the missing theoretical principle for progressing beyond the Fourth Fang; he understands/memorizes its theory in about three hours.
- The real target is found among neglected miscellaneous books.
- Standardize the scripture/manual as **Lurking Embedded Teeth**, supported by Fandom's Story Arcs, Vikir synopsis, and Martial Skills pages.
- Do not use the recovered variants `Lurking Ambush`, `Hidden Eagle`, or `Hidden Blade` as separate books.

### Chapter 16 — first-life cost and complete reconstruction
- In Vikir's first life he found and memorized one torn page of Lurking Embedded Teeth, then turned it over to Hugo.
- Seven pages were missing in total. Hugo's recovery campaign—`Operation Reclaim the Torn Pages` / `Operation Restore the Missing Teeth`—cost the lives and bodies of many Baskerville hounds.
- Vikir recovered all seven pages and memorized them, but could not surpass the Fourth Fang because he lacked the original scripture's framework.
- Hugo used the recovered material to reach nine fangs in the first timeline.
- In the current timeline Vikir has both the original book and the seven missing pages in memory, allowing him to reconstruct the full system.
- Vikir resolves to memorize the scripture and destroy it so Hugo cannot repeat the same progression.
- The recovered English duplicates the six-paragraph fire epilogue; the Korean contains it once. The second English occurrence is a provenance-preserved duplicate to be suppressed from reader-facing prose.
- Source conflict: MTL says the fire occurs after 11h50m; Korean says approximately 10h50m. Current editorial staging follows the Korean figure.

### Chapter 17 — Fifth Fang and Graduator breakthrough
- Vikir successfully produces an unstable but genuine **Fifth Fang**.
- He crosses from Advanced Sword Expert into **Low Sword Graduator**, consistent with the existing rank table: denser/liquid aura and rough equivalence to a Fourth-Circle mage.
- In his first life he did not reach this realm until around age thirty; now he is eight.
- Preserve the distinction between ordinary **Stabbing Fang** hound swordsmanship and the concealed, self-protective principles encoded in **Lurking Embedded Teeth**.
- **Lurking Fang** is reserved for the Tenth Fang/form, not used as a replacement title for the scripture.
- Vikir estimates that he could assassinate an Intermediate Graduator with near certainty or face one head-on at roughly even odds; this remains his own tactical estimate.
- He chooses to reveal enough ability to earn independence/opportunity (for example, a provincial posting or Academy placement) while keeping roughly thirty percent hidden.
- Vikir burns Lurking Embedded Teeth with a magnifier, manipulates the servants into treating the loss as trivial, and prepares for Hugo's expected test.
