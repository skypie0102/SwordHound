## Family continuity

### Underdog exit / Beelzebub
Vikir is now a target of repeated assassination attempts. Beelzebub's Murciélago-derived **Tough Life** has replaced the Troll's Super Regeneration and combines with the River Styx protection to make his body extremely difficult to injure.

Vikir privately plans to leave the Deputy Magistrate post, consistent with Cindywendy's advice.

### Academy reward
Hugo summons Vikir back to the main house, praises the Underdog crackdown, and rewards him with early entry to **Colosseo, the Imperial Academy**.

When asked which brothers he would like to take, Vikir names **Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro**. Their awareness of that recommendation explains the sudden loyalty they show in Chapters 41–42.

### Ruby-mine plan becomes a joint expedition
The territory inspection doubles as the next phase of Vikir's childhood ruby-mine strategy. Baskerville's lease to Morgue and the pressure of barbarian raids have developed into a proposed **joint punitive expedition**.

Morgue has concentrated Light- and Dark-Faction personnel plus roughly one hundred additional mages in the mining district, while Baskerville conceals its real elite force beyond the mountains.

### Morgue triplets
The Morgue sisters are normalized as:
- Highsis Morgue
- Midsis Morgue
- Lowsis Morgue

Collective titles: **Three Flowers of Morgue** / **Samhwa, the Three Plagues**.

Chapter 42 primary-source element assignments:
- Lowsis — water
- Midsis — earth
- Highsis — plant/grass

They clash with the Baskerville triplets at the fortress gate after attempting to obstruct the delegation.

### Camus reunion
The escalating youth fight is stopped by much stronger fire-and-iron magic. The caster is **Camus Morgue**, now fifteen, whom Vikir last saw at eight.

Established continuity:
- future title: **Ironblood Empress**
- fire/iron epithets: **Queen of Fire and Skewers**, **Queen of Red and Black**
- she is dangerous enough that the Morgue triplets fear her intervention even though they are her own sisters.

The family ends at the moment Camus and Vikir see one another again after seven years.
