## Family continuity

- Vikir leaves Ballak under the new covert identity **Hound of the Night**, inspired by Akwilla's Night Fox title and deliberately separate from the old Hound of Baskerville reputation.
- His first Imperial stop is Underdog, where Pomeranian is entrusted to Chihuahua for medical care, food, safety, and basic education.
- Chihuahua has preserved two years of political/current-events material and becomes Vikir's hidden administrative foothold in Underdog.
- Vikir points Chihuahua toward Messinadnaro Sen Cindywendy and offers legal frontier-trade monopoly as the incentive for helping stabilize the city.
- Vikir's immediate mission remains obtaining a Quovadis cure for Aheul.
- In St. Mecca, Vikir deliberately uses the fictional Red Death and planted Leviathan evidence to force Quovadis attention. The tactic is morally dangerous and politically manipulative; the reconstruction preserves Vikir warning children away and trying to trigger intervention before civilian deaths rather than expanding the biological mechanics.
- Dolores's character is established through refusal of wealth/status petitioners and Vikir's memory of her treating wounded people on future battlefields.
- Vikir gains entry by presenting himself simply as a **lost lamb**.
- Quovadis initially responds defensively. Mozgus confronts the masked stranger, leading to a fight that showcases Vikir's two years of growth: Ballak movement, Beelzebub, dense Graduator aura, Oxbear stability, Six Fangs, and Cerberus Burn.
- Mozgus's holy power can extinguish Cerberus Burn through consecrated water, preventing the scene from implying that Vikir's fire is universally unstoppable.
- Vikir wins the direct exchanges but does not come to kill Quovadis personnel. The family ends the instant Dolores appears and orders the confrontation stopped.
