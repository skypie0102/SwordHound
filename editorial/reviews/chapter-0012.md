## Red Fang Mountain dungeon

The dungeon is old and concealed between earthen mounds. Ruby veins connect it to the same mountain system already established in the preceding chapters. Use **Red Fang Mountain**, a branch of **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain**. The supplied Korean's `Red Peak` wording and other recovered mountain variants are recorded as alternate witnesses, not separate locations.

Vikir remembers the site being discovered roughly ten years later in his first life and remembers it as empty by then. In the present timeline, however, he detects demonic energy and enters.

## The shadow riddle

The wall inscription is reconstructed by meaning as a three-stage riddle: one enters, two are present inside, and one leaves. Cain and Abel interpret this through Baskerville's sibling-rivalry culture and conclude that two twins must become one by killing one another.

Vikir immediately sees the flaw: he is not a twin, yet the riddle still applies to him. The second presence is his **shadow**. In the dark approach passage he is effectively one; ruby light inside the chamber produces a second silhouette; when he destroys the ruby and returns the room to complete darkness, body and shadow become one again.

The recovered MTL actor `Baskerville looked at the cave behind him` is corrected to **Vikir**. The solution is the shadow, not a bloodline/twin sacrifice.

## Hidden chamber and cliffhanger

Destroying the ruby removes its faint magical light and opens the enormous concealed wall. Cain and Abel had already cleared the dungeon's monsters, so Vikir proceeds directly to the reward chamber and finds a sword embedded vertically in stone.

The inscription states that only Baskerville blood can draw the weapon. The supplied Korean and recovered MTL both indicate that Vikir reads the sword's name and is shocked, but Chapter 12 does **not** print that name explicitly in the final reveal. Preserve the cliffhanger: Vikir recognizes what artifact this is, while Chapter 13 opens by giving the full inscription and naming **Beelzebub**.

Exact endpoint: Vikir, shocked by the sword's identity, recognizes that this is the artifact that had been found here. Do not pull Chapter 13's Beelzebub name or absorption mechanics backward into this chapter.
