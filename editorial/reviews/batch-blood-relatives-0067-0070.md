## Family continuity

- A two-year time skip moves Vikir to age seventeen. His body is fully restored, his Ballak training is mature, and he has mastered the **Sixth Fang** while remaining below the Sword Master barrier.
- Camus Morgue has continued personally leading search parties for Vikir throughout those two years.
- Unknown Imperials contaminate Ballak's headwaters with **red liquid** and leave behind a single-serpent dagger that Vikir identifies as the emblem of **House Leviathan**.
- Simultaneously, Rokoko activity collapses despite its large population. Investigation reveals hunters weakened badly enough to be killed by normally unimpressive prey.
- The trail leads to an abandoned Rokoko settlement where the same red-spotted syndrome appears among the dead. Vikir recognizes the outbreak from his previous life as the **Red Death**.
- Rokoko survivors appear to have fled while potentially still infected, creating a regional epidemic risk just as the rainy season begins.
- The abandoned village contains a surviving Imperial child, **Pomeranian La Baskerville**, formerly used as a kitchen slave.
- Pomeranian's old Baskerville pendant depicts young Hugo. From her identification of the figures and remembered Baskerville history, Vikir infers that she may be Hugo's granddaughter and therefore his own niece. The family review preserves that genealogy as Vikir's current inference rather than external confirmation.
- Vikir brings Pomeranian back to Ballak and begins planning a future return toward Imperial territory, potentially including Morgue, both for the child and to assess the current Baskerville–Morgue alliance.
- Red Death signs are also reported among other jungle species, showing that the epidemic is not confined to Rokoko.
- Ahmen argues for ritual sacrifice and calls the outbreak a curse; younger Ballak argue for disease-control measures and relocation. Vikir closes the family by stating that he knows how to stop the epidemic.
