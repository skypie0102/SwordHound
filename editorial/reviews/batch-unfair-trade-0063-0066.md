## Family continuity

- The outsider alarm from Chapter 62 introduces an unauthorized Bourgeois caravan exploiting Ballak through extreme information asymmetry.
- Vikir recognizes both the true Imperial value of Ballak's goods and the caravan's smuggling route through Underdog/Baskerville territory.
- The merchants' scheme goes beyond unfair prices: seed stock is deliberately heat-killed to prevent Ballak farming, and free trinkets/cosmetics conceal narcotics that weaken the tribe and maintain dependency.
- Vikir first cancels the trade, then reprices it fairly, then assesses separate punitive damages for fraud and illegal drugs.
- Aheul's declining health connects the narcotic scheme directly to Ahun's family and explains his abrupt gratitude toward Vikir.
- The merchants' armed retaliation fails because Vikir combines Ballak's elite night archery with deliberate operational planning: survivors are allowed to flee to expose the reserve camp; rope-slung oil barrels bypass shields; the resulting fire/noise draws Madam Eight-Legs.
- Lord Smuggler's genuine Underdog permit becomes useless once Vikir reveals himself and revokes the authority personally.
- These events elevate Vikir from newly accepted outsider to a genuine Ballak hero and break much of Ahmen's credibility as a shaman.
- Ahmen is now under suspicion because the merchants claimed shamanic blessing, but the family deliberately ends without treating complicity as proven.
- Akwilla grants Vikir access to the **Fountain of Valor**, significantly advancing his recovery.
- The Fountain is associated with legendary archer **Adonai**, who is said to have been capable of directly confronting Madam Eight-Legs.
- The family closes with Vikir's incorporation into Ballak made socially explicit rather than merely legal: Aiyen welcomes him as one of them.
