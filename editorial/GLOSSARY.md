## Chapter 1 accepted decisions

- Use **Vikir Van Baskerville** and **Hugo Le Baskerville** as the established English names.
- Use **Marquis** for Hugo while preserving the documented `伯爵` rank conflict in provenance instead of pretending the Chinese localization says the same thing.
- Use **Cradle of Swords** for `刀刃摇篮`.
- Use **River Styx** for `斯蒂克斯` and retain the Chinese-described mechanics without MTL additions.
- Use **Demon Realm** for `魔界`; translate `魔族` contextually as demons / the demon race rather than carrying over the MTL phrase “internal communication.”

Add new accepted terms with chapter number, Chinese source location, any conflicting secondary evidence, reasoning, and review status. Do not use the glossary to override the Chinese raw’s plot meaning, explicitness, sequence, or reveal chronology.
