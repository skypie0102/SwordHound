# Terminology register

This register follows the restarted 2026-09-16 workflow:

- **Chinese raws control semantic identity and narrative meaning.**
- **The user-designated English Fandom wiki controls canonical English renderings** for identified names, terms, locations, ranks, skills, monsters, organizations, titles, and other proper nouns where an established form exists.
- MTL, historical edits, and recovered production notes are supporting leads only unless revalidated.
- Reveal chronology must always be protected.

“Accepted” below means the term has passed the current Chinese-source + Fandom-canonical + title-family workflow for the stated scope. It does not authorize backfilling later revelations into earlier chapters.

## Canonical-reference access note

Direct Fandom page access can be intermittently blocked by robots rules. Current indexed Fandom results may be used for canonical terminology when clearly documented as indexed retrieval. Do not represent a blocked direct refresh as successful access.

## Rules for adding or promoting entries

An entry may be marked **accepted** only when its current chapter/title-family has passed the restarted workflow. Record:

1. chapter number and title family;
2. Chinese source evidence/location;
3. the relevant English Fandom page/entry or a documented access limitation;
4. competing MTL/historical forms where consequential;
5. the final English form and reasoning;
6. reveal-chronology scope.

The glossary never overrides Chinese plot meaning, explicitness, dialogue, sequence, or omissions/additions. Canonical English naming and semantic translation authority are separate concerns.
