# Terminology register

This register follows the restarted 2026-09-16 workflow:

- **Chinese raws control semantic identity and narrative meaning.**
- **The user-designated English Fandom wiki controls canonical English renderings** for identified names, terms, locations, ranks, skills, monsters, organizations, titles, and other proper nouns where an established form exists.
- MTL, historical edits, and recovered production notes are supporting leads only unless revalidated.
- Reveal chronology must always be protected.

“Accepted” means the term has passed the current Chinese-source + Fandom-canonical + title-family workflow for the stated scope. It does not authorize importing later revelations early.

## Camus Morgue family additions — Chapters 20–25

- **Camus Morgue** — accepted current-era name; female; age eight at introduction. Chinese Chapter 22 itself invokes her future identity/title **Ironblood Empress**; later Fandom-only biography/faction/spouse spoilers remain excluded.
- **Adolf Morgue** — accepted; Camus's uncle, Raspane's brother, senior Morgue political/diplomatic figure and **Sixth-Circle** mage.
- **Raspane Morgue** — accepted where referenced; current Morgue Clan head/matriarch, Camus's mother, Adolf's sister.
- **Osiris Le Baskerville** — accepted; Hugo's eldest son / **Young Patriarch**.
- **Morgue Clan** — accepted canonical clan name.
- **Quadra Casting** — accepted Camus technique: simultaneous four-spell control.
- **Fireball / Ice Sphere / Thunder Ring / Mud Wall** — accepted Chapter 23 spell names.
- **Superspeed Regeneration — Troll (C+)** — from Chapter 20 onward, Beelzebub Slot 3 replaces Brown Rat Rapid Regeneration with this ability. Do not backfill the slot replacement into Chapter 19.
- **Camus–Osiris proposed engagement:** Camus is 8 and Osiris 20, therefore **12 years** apart. Recovered-English 17-year wording is rejected. Chinese also contains a separate awkward Hugo `40 / 57` rhetorical jab; do not use that to redefine the engagement gap.
- Chapter 24's accidental clothing damage involving the two eight-year-old characters is an event detail, not terminology; render it neutrally/non-sexually without added body description.
