The appearance of the slave boy onstage silenced the entire auction hall in an instant.
There was still a trace of baby fat in his cheeks, yet his jawline was already sharp.
He had a straight nose, firmly set red lips, dark eyebrows, and long eyelashes.
On top of that, he possessed rare black hair and red eyes.
Because he was only half-dressed, most of his body had tanned to a healthy brown, but the pale marks left beneath his clothing showed that his natural skin was originally clean and fair.
Even with nothing more than the basic stage makeup used on slaves, the boy's looks stood out.
Vikir. Vikir van Baskerville.
He stood upon the stage.
The auctioneer spoke.
“Now then, what do you think? This is the final lot of the day! Given the quality of the merchandise, I expect a fierce bidding war!”
As expected, the hall fell silent.
Even the barbarian girl in the cage on the opposite side of the stage, who had been thrashing wildly after failing to sell, lost her momentum and went quiet when Vikir appeared.
Just then,
someone raised a bidding paddle.
“Six hundred million!”
The noblewoman in the butterfly mask cried out in excitement.
“Six hundred million! No—six hundred and fifty million!”
A middle-aged man seated opposite her rose as though unwilling to concede.
“Eight hundred million!”
“What?! You're a man!”
“What does being a man have to do with bidding eight hundred million?!”
“That's not what I mean! What would a man even buy this boy for?!”
“Hmph! Who knows? Maybe I want to train him as a gladiator!”
“Nonsense! Does that delicate-looking kid even look capable of holding a sword?!”
“All right, both of you. If you want to fight, go home and do it. Nine hundred million.”
“One billion!”
“One billion, one hundred fifty million!”
“One billion, three hundred million!”
“One billion, five hundred million!”
A fierce bidding war broke out, with the price now climbing by hundreds of millions at a time.
Just as the auctioneer was delighting in the commission he would earn,
“Six billion.”
The figure landed like a bomb.
Everyone turned toward a fat man noisily chewing his food.
“…Him.”
“Baron Gambino of the Granary. Now that's a rare sight.”
“That's one hell of a bid.”
“I hear he's been swallowing up underground funds lately. No wonder his pockets are so deep.”
The people nearby turned away with bitter expressions.
Several noblewomen gritted their teeth and tried to scrape together enough money to compete, but challenging Baron Gambino—now notorious as a rising force in the underworld—looked impossible.
Just then,
“…Baron, isn't that far too much?”
Baron Gambino's secretary spoke from beside him.
With short greenish-blond hair and a monocle, she gave off a cold, domineering impression.
Baron Gambino frowned slightly at her remark, then cleared his throat.
“A beautiful slave like that would fetch far more in the Imperial Capital. Buy him here and I can resell him later for a profit.”
“He is certainly handsome, but six billion is excessive. Besides, reselling him for a profit assumes you'll actually leave him in a condition to be resold.”
“Hmph. Ahem!”
Apparently conscious of the looks around him, Baron Gambino glanced about and suddenly roared.
“Shut up, wench! I show you a little favor and now you dare climb over your master?”
“….”
“I like him! I'll buy him with my own money…!”
Baron Gambino shouted, while his secretary sighed and shook her head.
Soon afterward,
having won the bid for an enormous sum, Baron Gambino grinned and had Vikir brought before him.
He reached out one thick hand and groped Vikir's backside.
“….”
Vikir stared in disbelief, looking from Baron Gambino to the hand on him.
Seeing Vikir's expression—as if he had never experienced anything like this—Baron Gambino smiled in satisfaction.
“What? Not used to it? You'd better get used to it. This will happen often from now on.”
“….”
“Heh heh—don't worry. To be fair, I'll let you touch mine too. Tonight….”
Baron Gambino spoke while leering over Vikir's body.
At that moment,
Vikir raised both hands.
Thinking he was asking to have the cuffs removed, Baron Gambino smiled and reached for the key, when—
Crack! Snap!
The restraints in front of him burst apart in the blink of an eye.
Steel cuffs and chains tore like paper.
Vikir had shattered the restraints around his wrists with nothing but the strength of his hands.
Then he extended a hand toward the speechless Baron Gambino.
“Turn around.”
Baron Gambino had no time to resist.
Vikir easily flipped the baron's obese body over and seized him.
Rip, rip, rip!
A sickening sound rang out, followed by Baron Gambino screaming like a slaughtered pig.
“Aaaagh!”
Blood sprayed out and stained the surroundings red.
The onlookers screamed as they watched a man being torn apart alive.
The mercenary guards rushed over in alarm.
But.
Slash—slash—swish!
The charging guards' heads were severed from their bodies in an instant and rolled across the floor.
At some point, a black blade had extended from Vikir's wrist.
Bzzzz—
The magic sword Beelzebub hummed as it began drinking blood.
“Get him! Kill him!”
The nobles called for their private guards.
Mercenaries and knights drew their weapons and charged, auras flaring.
Sssssss—
Vikir released his aura as well.
What followed was the Fifth Form of the Baskerville Style.
Five hidden fangs sprang out at once and tore into the enemies' throats.
Thud, thud, thud.
Blood rained down.
Headless bodies dropped to their knees and collapsed.
The swordsmen behind them who had narrowly escaped death could only stare in horror.
An aura red and viscous as blood. A Sword Graduator.
The ultimate weapon symbolizing a nation's military strength—a killing machine made solely for slaughter.
Every step Vikir took seemed to end with another throat cut.
One step, one person. With every step, someone died.
Vikir had advanced only a dozen or so paces, yet blood was already raining around him.
Everyone shared the same thought.
“We don't stand a chance against a Sword Graduator!”
The moment the swordsmen saw Vikir's aura flowing like liquid, they abandoned resistance and began to flee.
But,
“Idiots! Even a Sword Graduator can be overwhelmed by numbers!”
“If you run now, you'll all end up as slaves!”
“You won't get paid if you run!”
“Think of your families!”
The shouts of nobles and wealthy patrons made several swordsmen stop in their tracks.
Before long, the chaos in the auction hall settled somewhat.
Hundreds of fully armed mercenaries poured in and surrounded Vikir.
“W-what the hell is that kid?”
“How can someone that young already be a Sword Graduator?”
“And that swordsmanship looks familiar… isn't that the Baskerville Style? Are you even human? What are you?!”
Everyone looked bewildered, but their hostility was unmistakable.
Countless swords, spears, arrows, and spells hemmed Vikir in from every direction.
No matter how powerful a Sword Graduator was, surviving such a concentrated attack would be difficult.
And that was not all.
“You bastard! Drop your weapon and surrender right now, or your companion dies!”
The clown standing on the stage threatened Vikir with a hostage.
The person held at knifepoint was Chihuahua Baskerville.
The goateed Chihuahua trembled as he looked toward Vikir.
“….”
Vikir stopped moving and stood still.
The armed mercenaries around him gradually tightened the encirclement.
Just then,
“Deputy Magistrate! I'm fine! Please look after yourself!”
Chihuahua shouted with a determined expression.
He immediately seized the clown's arm and pulled it toward himself.
When Chihuahua tried to drive the other man's blade into his own neck, the clown panicked even more.
“You lunatic! What are you doing?!”
“Let go! The word compromise does not exist in my dictionary! I'd rather die than become a burden to my superior!”
“How can a face that treacherous deliver a line like that? Let go!”
The clown and Chihuahua began grappling with each other.
Vikir gave a low chuckle at the sight.
A moment later,
Thwip!
Vikir released a droplet of aura that pierced the clown between the brows, caught the falling Chihuahua, and dropped down from the stage.
Killing intent filled the surroundings.
Countless mercenaries and knights belonging to the local noble families advanced on Vikir and Chihuahua with swords, bows, and magic.
“Deputy Magistrate, are you sure about this? No matter how strong you are, fighting this many people is… and I'm only extra baggage.”
Chihuahua's concern was reasonable.
But Vikir remained calm.
“Don't worry. When we left the Baskerville estate…”
He tore open a small slit in the flesh of his thigh and pulled out an object hidden inside.
It was a small horn shaped like a red fang.
“…I received a trump card from the Patriarch.”
A moment later,
Vikir raised the blood-red whistle to his lips and blew.
Piiii—
A sharp, ear-splitting note rang out.
The people surrounding him stared anxiously toward the center, unable to tell what Vikir was doing.
But.
The real change began above them, on the roof of the tent where no one was looking.
Riiip!
The canopy stretched like a night sky tore open, and shadows poured through it.
Countless black-clad figures descended from above.
Then,
Thud! Thud! Thud! Thud!
Wherever they landed, blood erupted and heads flew.
“Aaaagh! What are those things?!”
“There are more than a hundred of them!”
“And every one of them is a Sword Graduator!”
One hundred Sword Graduators, killing machines whose blades were wrapped in blood-red aura.
They slaughtered everyone before them in an instant, then lined up respectfully behind Vikir.
Only then did those fortunate enough to survive at the rear understand what Vikir's group of one hundred Graduators was.
“No way… they can't be….”
“No. No, please!”
“Ah—right. Who else would wear that insignia?!”
The red fang-shaped badge on their chests made their identity obvious.
House Baskerville's Pit Bull Knights.
The Empire's most ferocious suppression force—not merely House Baskerville's.
The black-cloaked, silent hounds stood waiting as Vikir gave them one short order: “Bite them all to death.”
