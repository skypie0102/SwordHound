“Where there are females, males are never far away.”
Vikir finally understood what Aiyen had meant.
He and Aiyen had spent the last several days concealed near a den deep in the jungle.
Inside were the enormous female oxbear they had first found and the smaller male that had approached her.
The two oxbears had remained together for three days.
The female’s stamina and determination were extraordinary.
She blocked the den entrance with her massive body whenever the exhausted male tried to leave.
The male had endured remarkably well for the first two days, but by the third he was visibly running out of strength.
Female oxbears were larger, stronger, and especially voracious.
With hibernation approaching, the female seemed determined to ensure conception before letting the male go.
Whenever he collapsed from exhaustion, she groomed him, waited for him to recover, and kept him inside the den.
Then the cycle began again.
Vikir let out a quiet sigh.
“I cannot believe this is part of the hunt.”
His arms were full of fish, berries, mushrooms, and other food gathered nearby.
Aiyen had collected more and arranged it near the den as well.
None of it was meant for the hunters.
It was bait and support for the oxbears they had been monitoring.
Whenever the female left the den to forage, Aiyen and Vikir placed small amounts of food where she could find them quickly.
The cautious female checked each offering before carrying it back and sharing it with the exhausted male.
Aiyen gave a knowing smile.
“Those berries and mushrooms provide a powerful short-term boost, but they are terrible over time. They heat the body quickly, then leave it drained.”
Vikir knew the same plants.
They could temporarily increase strength and alertness, but they consumed stamina and life force in return.
The old female appeared to understand the cost and use them anyway.
The male seemed to understand too, but in his condition he had little choice except to eat.
So he ate whatever she brought him.
Whenever Vikir accidentally left too much scent or disturbed the ground, the female emerged from the den on high alert.
Age had weakened her eyesight, but her hearing more than compensated.
GRRRR!
She raked through thick logs with her claws and tore stones loose from the earth.
Ancient trees snapped and rocks supporting the hillside shattered beneath her strength.
Only after thoroughly checking the area did she return to the den and settle beside the exhausted male again.
From behind cover, Vikir murmured under his breath.
“I cannot tell whether that display is meant to threaten intruders or the male.”
Either way, the message was effective: intruders would retreat, and the male would think twice about attempting escape.
Vikir now understood why Aiyen had deliberately selected such an enormous female.
The larger and stronger the female, the longer she could keep a suitable male occupied and weakened.
Meanwhile, the vigil dragged on.
Hunters lying in wait still had to eat.
Aiyen checked the wind and prepared a meal whose smell would not drift toward the den.
She boiled clear water in a coconut shell with crayfish, adding only salt and a little dried seaweed.
Aiyen passed some of the broth to Vikir.
She also pushed most of the plump crayfish toward him.
Once again, it was difficult to tell which of them was supposed to be master and which slave.
“Eat. You need strength if you want to recover.”
Aiyen piled still more food in front of him.
For a moment, her insistence reminded Vikir of Camus.
Crack!
He split the shell of a red crayfish.
White meat showed beneath the hard shell.
Steam rose from the rich contents inside.
Vikir ate methodically.
“They have certainly been at this for a long time.”
For three days, the oxbears had barely left the den.
Even without looking inside, the constant movement made it obvious how relentlessly the female was keeping the male occupied.
Aiyen looked at Vikir with a mischievous glint.
“Think you could endure three days of that?”
“…What?”
“Ballak hunters are supposed to have impressive stamina. What about Imperials?”
Vikir frowned.
After the latrine prank, he had learned not to trust Aiyen’s claims about Ballak customs.
She had already tricked him once into embarrassing himself in the middle of the village.
Aiyen kept needling him anyway.
“No answer?”
“….”
“Maybe Imperials are softer than I thought.”
“Who are you calling soft?”
“Then you could keep up?”
“….”
“Could you? Could you?”
Aiyen was becoming irritating.
Knowing that answering would only encourage her, Vikir ignored her completely.
Aiyen muttered about her cheeky slave under her breath.
Just then, a new sound tore through the den.
CRACK!
It was unlike anything they had heard during the previous three days.
“…!”
All playfulness vanished from Aiyen’s face.
She sprang up, bow already in hand, and peered over the brush.
The teasing seventeen-year-old from moments earlier was gone.
In her place stood Ballak’s young hunting captain.
GRRRR…
Bakira bared her teeth as well.
Vikir set down his food and rose into a crouch beside Aiyen.
The disturbance was coming from the oxbears’ den.
GROOOAR!
The female’s behavior had changed abruptly.
She bared her teeth at the male and displayed her claws.
Startled, the male remained where he was at first, apparently trusting the bond that had kept them together for days.
He seemed reluctant to leave the safety of the den.
The female had other ideas.
BOOM!
A heavy impact shook the ground.
Her front paw came down with several tons of force.
Teeth broke and blood sprayed.
The blow finally sent the male scrambling upright.
He staggered out of the den on unsteady legs.
Aiyen’s eyes lit with triumph.
“She must be certain she has conceived.”
With the mating period over, the female no longer had any reason to tolerate the male.
Oxbears were solitary predators.
Once mating ended, the opposite sex immediately became a rival trespassing in their territory.
ROOOAR!
The female roared after the retreating male, who bolted in panic.
Half-dazed, he fled from the den.
If he wanted to avoid being killed and eaten, he had to escape before the female changed her mind.
His legs trembled and his movements were clumsy with exhaustion.
His coat was matted and dull.
His eyes and nose were dry, and most of the fat that had once padded his body was gone.
One side of his face was already swollen from the female’s blow.
Aiyen watched the weakened male with the focused gaze of a hunter.
“Follow me, slave.”
“….”
Aiyen had finally chosen her prey, and Vikir followed his nominal master.
The real hunt was beginning.
