Adonai was the Ballak hero who had once fought the Empire almost single-handedly.
He was a mythical archer who terrified even Imperial Sword Masters—the Bowmaster remembered as the strongest archer in Ballak history.
This was Adonai.
The legendary warrior who stood at the summit of Ballak’s recorded history now faced Akwilla and the new generation.
Short gray hair clung to a dried face, and withered lips exposed the teeth beneath.
A black burial shroud shifted around the body, occasionally revealing the enormous bow on his back.
The bow had sharply pointed ends and radiated darkness.
It looked almost like two giant insect legs bent together into a weapon.
“….”
Vikir stared silently at Adonai.
It had been a long time since he had stood this close to a being who touched the Master realm.
The power around Adonai was as violent and oppressive as Hugo Le Baskerville’s.
Death Knight corruption made that presence feel even harsher.
“…So this is Adonai the Archer.”
Terror of the Empire, hero of the tribes.
Seeing a figure Vikir had known only through jungle legend felt unreal.
The wonder lasted only a moment.
Adonai raised the huge black bow.
There was no physical string and no arrow.
When Adonai reached toward empty air, cold aura stretched from one end of the bow to the other like a thread.
It resembled a spider spinning silk.
Sssss…
Black energy connected the bow’s tips.
Adonai pinched the invisible string and drew.
An arrow of aura formed at the same time.
Quack!
He released.
“…!”
Vikir narrowed his eyes.
The shot was so fast that even his advanced Graduator senses could barely follow it.
Half instinct and half luck let him extend Beelzebub far enough to deflect the arrow.
BOOM!
The altered shot punched straight through a massive log.
Crunch! Crack!
The rocks behind the tree shattered as well.
The devastation looked as though countless blades had struck the cliff at once.
A single Adonai arrow destroyed everything along its path.
Taking one head-on was unthinkable.
“…Hm.”
Vikir frowned.
Even after successfully deflecting the shot, his wrist was stiff and his whole arm tingled.
Muscle had torn and the wrist bone itself might have cracked.
The liquid aura coating Beelzebub had been ripped apart.
Adonai’s arrow possessed the hard, solid density associated with the Master realm.
Graduator-class liquid aura simply could not meet it evenly.
Worse—
Tsutsutsutsu…
The log struck by the aura began to wither.
The poison in the attack was strong enough to kill a huge old tree almost immediately.
Vikir examined Adonai’s black bow again.
The weapon was enormous yet looked strangely light.
Its pointed ends and horn-like projections resembled joined insect limbs.
Vikir recognized what it was made from.
“…Remains of Madam Eight-Legs.”
When Vikir encountered Madam Eight-Legs, all eight current legs had been intact, so the bow likely came from an older molt or body fragment.
That reminded him of something Aiyen had once said.
‘Adonai was the only archer said to have stood against Madam Eight-Legs. As Ballak, you should honor him and take pride in that inheritance.’
The legend said Adonai had once fought the great spider and won.
His bow had been fashioned from her remains and retained part of her poisonous power.
Even after Adonai’s death, the black weapon still carried that venomous aura.
“This is bad.”
Vikir clicked his tongue.
His opponent had reached the equivalent of a Sword Master—or worse for this situation, a Bowmaster.
Death Knights normally attacked with little judgment, which could create openings at close range.
Poof! Pow! Crack!
At long distance, that weakness mattered far less.
Adonai unleashed arrow after arrow.
Solid black aura, each shot carrying poison.
The barrage shredded Vikir’s liquid aura.
Vikir poured power into Beelzebub and swung.
Baskerville’s carnivorous swordsmanship.
Six fangs traced bloody arcs through the air, but the arrows still pushed them back.
Even a powerful Graduator could not contest a Master head-on.
Liquid could not overpower solid.
Vikir remained alive only because of his Styx-hardened body, exceptional recovery, Beelzebub, and the monster abilities sealed inside it.
“…! …!”
Sacral Spine increased Vikir’s effective mass and stability enough that Adonai’s arrows could not simply throw him away.
Even so, the Sixth Fang could not completely stop the rain of arrows.
Each arrow was harder and more numerous than Vikir’s individual fangs.
And Adonai’s bowstring and arrows never seemed to run out.
As a Death Knight, he could pour out negative energy in a relentless stream.
Boom! Crack! Puck!
Vikir abandoned the idea of meeting the attacks directly and focused on deflection, but he was still driven backward.
Even a Graduator’s deep mana reserves were draining fast.
Whenever Beelzebub’s aura touched Adonai’s, it burst apart like thick honey struck by a hammer.
His trembling wrists warned that he was reaching his limit.
Every part of his body was reporting damage.
‘At this rate I’ll end up full of arrows.’
Vikir tried to change position, but Adonai tracked him faster than expected.
Closing the distance meant entering an impossible storm of arrows, while retreating farther from a Bowmaster was suicide.
An ambush might have worked, but there was no clear path to victory in a frontal exchange.
At this point, the question was survival rather than winning.
‘At minimum, I have to kill Ahmen.’
That remained impossible while Adonai stood between them.
Ballak’s warriors fired together, but their arrows were blocked by Adonai’s shroud, bow, and counterfire.
While Vikir searched for an opening—
Puck!
A foot struck his cheek.
“Slave, move!”
Aiyen kicked Vikir’s head sideways.
Pow!
A black streak passed through the exact place his head had occupied.
Pit!
A thin cut opened along Aiyen’s calf.
Vikir wiped blood from his forehead.
“Thanks. A concussion is preferable to an arrow through the skull.”
“No time for jokes. More are coming!”
Aiyen grabbed the back of Vikir’s neck and pulled him again.
Another black arrow tore a deep hole in the ground where he had been standing.
“…He’s targeting vulnerable spots without hesitation.”
Vikir glanced at Aiyen.
“Don’t get careless just because he’s the great Adonai.”
“Right now he’s Ahmen’s puppet. We can honor him after we survive.”
Aiyen rapidly loaded her bow.
A spread of arrows flew toward Adonai.
Their trajectories separated in flight and converged on different points across his body.
But—
Puff-puff-puff-puff!
Adonai caught every arrow with one hand.
Woof!
His fist closed and snapped the shafts apart.
“…That hand is absurd.”
Aiyen ground her teeth as broken arrows fell.
Adonai raised the black bow once more.
This time the target was unmistakably Vikir and Aiyen.
BOOM!
A shot like a cannon blast erupted from the bow.
A spiraling blade of dark aura tore through the air toward them.
Its speed and destructive force left almost no room to evade, and both prepared to meet it.
Then—
BOOM!
Someone struck the attack aside with bare hands.
Kwagigik! Boom!
The spinning aura broke under raw force.
Adonai’s arrow dissolved into black currents.
“…!”
Vikir and Aiyen stared at the figure who had stepped in front of them.
Blood poured from a torn hand, but the warrior did not move back.
Chieftain Akwilla—the Night Fox.
She stood as the representative of Ballak’s new generation against the greatest representative of the old.
