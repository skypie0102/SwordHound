# Chapter 2 continuity handoff

- Opening overlaps Chapter 1: Vikir realizes he is an infant in the nursery, hears Hugo, and enters the same Cradle of Swords trial. Age remains about one hundred days.
- He remembers serving for thirty years, receiving little reward, and being executed after taking blame for Hugo's deeds. This memory motivates his resolve; it is not an independent narrator's new verification of later revelations.
- In his former life he reached the river late and received little power; this time he uses his wounds and early arrival to absorb more. Bones, flesh and mana channels improve; he drinks to strengthen internal organs.
- The poisoned-heel legend concerns an unnamed earlier swordsman. Vikir tries to avoid an untouched spot by moving his whole body and shifting his grip.
- The river grants no further blessing to a part once surfaced; the chapter does not say earlier protection is erased. He risks blood loss and suffocation while holding on below.
- Teeth grow during immersion, after an earlier explicitly toothless jaw clench. He surfaces himself in this account. Hugo's voice is heard, but no physical rescue by Hugo is stated.
- Exact endpoint: Vikir opens his mouth for air; Hugo smiles and notices his teeth; the hound/fangs closing metaphor follows. Chapter 3 must begin after this endpoint unless its own text intentionally overlaps.
- Do not backfill wiki immersion duration, named butler, snakes, or later abilities. “Tower of Swords” at paragraph 76 was normalized to this same Cradle trial only; no global location equivalence is established.
