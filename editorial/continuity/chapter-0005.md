# Chapter 5 continuity handoff

- Vikir is eight; Highbro, Middlebro and Lowbro are nine. Highbro is the eldest, Middlebro the middle brother and Lowbro the youngest.
- The triplets bring Vikir from the public area of Fang Castle to a secluded room through old service passages. Do not preserve the MTL’s corrupted “bikinis” wording.
- Lowbro covers Vikir’s nose and mouth. Vikir holds his breath for ten minutes without raising mana, then bites off Lowbro’s index finger as Lowbro begins to release him.
- Highbro attacks Vikir. Vikir breaks Highbro’s nose/teeth and dislocates Middlebro’s jaw. Middlebro carries Lowbro when they first try to leave.
- Vikir’s Styx protection is visibly demonstrated against Highbro’s short sword: the contact point flashes black and the blade draws no blood. Treat this as blade protection demonstrated here, not universal invulnerability.
- Hugo’s child-rearing maxim is restored from Korean: if someone still talks back after a beating, the family logic is that they were not beaten hard enough. This is characterization, not narrator endorsement.
- Vikir blocks the door and later leaves a dagger between the three brothers, saying only one may leave alive and they must decide who.
- Exact endpoint: the Trident of Baskerville begins to split from within. Do not import the triplets’ ensuing fight, treatment, forgiveness, Hugo meeting, or other Chapter 6 events.
