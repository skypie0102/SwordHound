# Chapter 6 continuity handoff

- John Barrymore is the Baskerville head butler and has served the family for four generations. Hugo Le Baskerville remains patriarch, Sword Master/Sword Star and one of the Empire's seven pillars.
- Use **Morgue Family** and **Red Fang Mountain** for the western ruby-mine dispute. Red Cane/Red Peak are recorded source variants, not separate locations.
- Fang Castle's practical exam begins in five days; most guardian knights are away preparing. The two-suns report directly resolves Chapter 4's ending. Servants interpret the second sun as a small sun born to aid the heir.
- Barrymore reports the Chapter 5 injuries as treated: Highbro's teeth, Middlebro's jaw and Lowbro's severed right index finger. The triplets' physical health is restored, but their teamwork and fighting spirit have collapsed.
- Restore `◆◆◆` after Barrymore identifies Vikir as the single younger attacker and written-exam first place. Vikir is eight.
- Vikir can conceal mana through experience gained in the war against demons/Age of Destruction, but recognizes the concealment will fail if his mana becomes too strong.
- Hugo's attitude toward Vikir is noticeably warmer than Vikir remembers. Vikir formally calls him **Patriarch**; Hugo's response is reconstructed as dissatisfaction that Vikir does not call him **Father**.
- The triplets now eat, sleep and live separately and are terrified of Vikir. Vikir considers a hound without fighting spirit useless on the battlefield.
- Vikir tells Hugo that carrying three dull blades is worse than one finely forged blade, then invokes the family creed: strength is justice and weakness is sin. Exact endpoint: Hugo's mouth twitches because the eight-year-old before him is unexpectedly endearing. Do not import later reconciliation or Chapter 7 events.
