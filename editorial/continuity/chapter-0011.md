# Chapter 11 continuity handoff

- This is the final chapter in the contiguous **Hounds of Hell (1–4)** title family. It begins immediately from Chapter 10's Cerberus reveal and ends on the Bloody Mamba venom payoff.
- Cerberus remains A+, 7 m, native to Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain's Seventh Ridge, AKA `Hell’s Guard Dog`. The `Red and Black Mountains` wording is a source/secondary English variant of the same place.
- In Vikir's first-life future, Camus Morgue later became known as the **Ironblood Empress** and personally helped stop catastrophic Cerberus losses. Do not import later relationship or academy information here.
- Vikir is currently **Advanced Sword Expert**, emitting dense gaseous aura. The sword/mage equivalence window runs Low/Intermediate/Advanced Sword Expert = 1st/2nd/3rd Circle Mage, Low/Intermediate/Advanced Sword Graduator = 4th/5th/6th Circle Mage, Sword Master = 7th Circle Mage.
- Vikir's present child body can form only three Baskerville fangs, though in his previous life he had mastered through the Fourth Form/four fangs and later reached Advanced Sword Graduator.
- Cerberus is already badly injured by unknown mountain barbarians before fighting Vikir. Several ribs are broken and arrow wounds mark its flank. Do not identify a specific tribe yet.
- Highbro/Middlebro/Lowbro's coordinated trident tactics were inspired by Cerberus's three-headed patterns; Vikir's old experience fighting the triplets helps him read Cerberus.
- The Age of Destruction refined Vikir's swordsmanship into a stripped-down killing style. Preserve the beef/chicken/jerky analogy as intentional source rhetoric.
- River Styx durability is finite. Cerberus's claw does not rupture Vikir's organs, but it breaks several ribs; he says a second such hit would be impossible.
- The Thirty-Six Stratagems passage resolves to retreat being the best policy. Vikir deliberately retreats across the boundary to lure Cerberus into the pit prepared in Chapter 9.
- The trap is the **Cradle of Needles**, not the Cradle of Swords. It uses upward wooden stakes; several hit the preexisting arrow wound.
- Vikir soaked the stakes in liquid made from boiled Bloody Beans, then feeds Cerberus the remaining beans directly. Cerberus is more resistant than a Hellhound and does not collapse immediately.
- The final thrown spear carries two hidden thorns contaminated with **Bloody Mamba venom**. Cerberus manages seven steps before dying; the exact endpoint reveals that the venom still lingered on the thorns.
- Do not carry Chapter 12's aftermath into this chapter. Chapter 12 begins a new title family, **The Gluttonous Flies**.
