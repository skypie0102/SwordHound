# Chapter 12 continuity handoff

## Inherited from Chapter 11

- Cerberus is dead after Vikir's trap, Bloody Bean/chocolate poisoning and hidden Bloody Mamba venom.
- The practical assessment is still in progress; the Cerberus corpse remains important evidence for Vikir's result.
- Guide Hounds supervise the assessment area, but Vikir had crossed beyond their ordinary observation range.
- Red Fang Mountain / Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain terminology and the finite River Styx durability decisions remain in force.

## Chapter 12 decisions to carry forward

- Cerberus's corpse is left largely intact; Vikir removes only select useful parts/organs.
- The nearby hidden dungeon is associated with Red Fang Mountain, a branch of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.
- There is **one skeleton** in the ruby-lit chamber: Abel's remains. Cain wrote the journal, killed Abel after misreading the riddle, and later left alive.
- The dungeon riddle is solved by **Vikir's shadow**, not by sibling sacrifice.
- Destroying the ruby removes the light/shadow and opens the hidden final chamber.
- Vikir finds a sword embedded in rock. He recognizes its identity, but Chapter 12 deliberately withholds the name.

## Exact handoff to Chapter 13

Chapter 12 ends when Vikir realizes that the sword is the artifact he knew from his first life. Chapter 13 immediately repeats/completes the inscription and names the weapon **Beelzebub**. Do not introduce its name, fusion mechanics, hunger, or skill absorption before that handoff.
