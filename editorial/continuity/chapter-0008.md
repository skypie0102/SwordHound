# Chapter 8 continuity handoff

- Chapter 8 begins the practical/survival examination foreshadowed in Chapters 6–7. Vikir is still eight years old.
- Baskerville children take midterm and final evaluations every year until fifteen. This chapter focuses on the practical survival exam.
- At eight, formal training intensifies and the children carry hardened, salted haggis/jerky as field rations.
- Standardize the location as **Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain**. Do not revert to `Le Rouge`, `Le Rogue`, `Red Mountain and Black Mountain`, or similar MTL variants except when discussing source corruption.
- Guardian Knights assigned to supervise the survival-test boundary are **Guide Hounds**. Pavlov Van Baskerville is one of them and rings the starting bell at the chapter endpoint.
- Survival objectives: survive; if possible, hunt large powerful demonic creatures. Score ladder is 10/30/50/70/90, with no 100. Death is always zero.
- Geography: the children's secured survival area is comparatively safe; the **Forbidden Zone** lies beyond the guarded boundary; the **Cradle** is a named ridge/safer area within this geography. Keep these concepts distinct.
- Vikir has prior-life familiarity with the region as examinee, instructor and hunting hound. Instructors searched beyond the safe zone.
- The badge mechanic is active: eight-year-olds try to protect their badges, while nine-year-olds can steal them for bonus points.
- Preserve rumors already known to the children: Vikir's seven-minute Styx immersion, two venomous snakes killed in the Cradle, and perfect written-exam score.
- Highbro, Middlebro and Lowbro remain psychologically broken from Chapters 5–7 and are conspicuously silent instead of threatening Vikir.
- Vikir's response to the other nine-year-olds is controlled: he first denies being special, then calls them pathetic. Their anger contrasts with his old-hound indifference.
- Exact endpoint: Pavlov rings the bell and the practical examination begins. Do not reveal Chapter 9's actual combat or the purpose of the Bloody Beans yet.
