BOOM.

Vikir landed.

Aura still rose from the tip of Beelzebub.

At its peak—

a black sun.

A dense mass of solid aura.

It illuminated the abyss below—

and every face around it.

Students.

Parents.

Professors.

Everyone at Colosseo Academy—

stared.

Silence.

Professor Banshee Morgue finally found his voice.

“V-Vikir?”

“Is that really you?”

“How did you gain that kind of—”

He pointed at the solid Sword Master aura.

His hand trembled.

He never finished the question.

BOOM!

Something enormous crashed down from above.

A dark shadow swallowed the crowd.

**Amdusias.**

A gigantic unicorn.

Eyes burning red.

Mane blazing like fire.

Winston Smith’s host body—

was gone.

Amdusias had abandoned it completely.

Vikir looked up.

“You’re manifesting your true body without a host.”

“That strain will destroy you.”

[It does not matter!]

Amdusias roared.

[As long as I kill you!]

His gaze locked on Vikir.

[You cannot be allowed to continue existing.]

Vikir’s eyes narrowed.

“You mean because of the Gate?”

Amdusias stiffened.

[…You know too much.]

[All the more reason you must die.]

Power gathered at the tip of his horn.

A huge blast of demonic aura formed.

Its trajectory included—

not only Vikir—

but everyone behind him.

“Everyone back!”

“Get down!”

Cervantes and Roderick reacted first.

Both moved to intercept.

But another attack arrived faster.

FLASH.

Vikir extended a massive line of aura from Beelzebub.

A crimson-black slash rose like a tidal wave.

The two attacks collided.

BOOM!

Amdusias reeled.

[…!?]

Vikir was stronger than before.

Far stronger.

That was not surprising.

Inside the Hell Tree—

Vikir had reached Level 100.

His stats had exploded.

His talent had been corrected and elevated.

He had trained through endless hostile environments.

Then—

outside the tower—

his original power returned on top of everything accumulated within.

The result—

was monstrous.

Vikir moved his sword.

One fang.

Two.

Three.

Four.

Five.

Six.

Seven.

Then—

the eighth.

The final fang was larger than all the others.

A complete **Baskerville Eighth Form**.

Everyone who understood swordsmanship froze.

Cervantes.

Roderick.

Osiris.

Even the strongest veterans in the crowd.

“…The Eighth?”

“That’s impossible.”

“A Baskerville technique beyond the Seventh?”

The shock did not last long.

Amdusias attacked again.

Vikir blocked.

Then—

a black-red cage of magic suddenly rose around the demon.

Camus Morgue.

She stood with one hand raised.

Black flames and bars twisted around Amdusias.

Her eyes—

however—

were not fixed on the demon.

They were on Vikir.

“Hey.”

“You.”

“Why do you keep hanging around **my husband**?”

Dolores turned.

“What?”

Camus pointed at her.

“You heard me.”

“Stop clinging to my fiancé.”

Dolores’s face hardened instantly.

“Who are you?”

“And what do you mean—fiancé?”

Camus clicked her tongue.

“I don’t need to introduce myself.”

“Get away from him.”

“You have no right to talk like that!”

Dolores snapped back.

“When have we even met before?”

Camus answered without thinking.

“At the festival.”

Dolores froze.

“…Festival?”

The voice.

The cadence.

A memory surfaced.

Her expression changed.

“You…”

“The black mage who invaded the festival?”

“The Corpse King!?”

Camus’s face went blank.

“…Ah.”

“Damn it.”

She had said too much.

Dolores immediately looked at Vikir.

Demanding an explanation.

Vikir ignored both of them.

“Not now.”

A shadow dropped between them.

Osiris.

Black cloak.

Red sword.

He landed beside Vikir and faced Amdusias.

Then—

to everyone’s disbelief—

he bowed.

“…Acting Head.”

“Give the order.”

Camus stared.

Dolores stared.

So did the crowd.

Osiris Le Baskerville—

young patriarch of the Iron-Blooded Sword House—

was bowing to a supposedly common-born Academy student.

And calling him **Acting Head**.

Vikir accepted the gesture naturally.

He stepped forward.

Camus’s cage groaned around Amdusias.

The demon’s true body pushed against it—

ready to explode free.

Vikir spoke one sentence.

“Hounds.”

“Assemble.”

He took something from inside his clothes.

A dark crimson horn—

shaped like a fang.

The military whistle Hugo had entrusted to him.

Vikir blew.

The sound was low.

Heavy.

Everyone present felt a cold shiver.

Then—

flutter.

A black cloak appeared atop the Academy wall.

Then another.

Then another.

And another.

Hundreds.

Each figure carried a long sword.

Crimson aura hung from the blade tips—

thick as honey.

One hundred Graduators in a unit.

Seven units.

Seven hundred Baskerville hounds.

Pit Bull.

Mastiff.

Doberman.

Shepherd.

Rottweiler.

Wolfhound.

The great knight orders.

And six of the Seven Counts who led them.

Only Cane Corso—

guardian of the Grave of Swords—

was absent.

The dark red military horn represented the full armed authority of House Baskerville.

Hugo had granted it to Vikir during the Wraith Tree mission.

And every hound within reach—

had answered.

The Iron-Blooded Sword House—

had assembled.
