BOOM!

Vikir landed on the ground.

A pillar of solid aura still rose from his blade—

and at its peak—

a massive black sun burned.

Every student.

Every parent.

Every professor.

All of them stared.

The light illuminated the abyss left behind by the collapsing Hell Tree.

Silence.

The first person to force words out—

was Professor Banshee Morgue.

“V-Vikir?”

“Is that really you?”

“How can you possibly have that kind of—”

Her hand trembled as she pointed toward the physical aura of a Sword Master.

She never finished.

RUMBLE!

The fragments of the collapsing abyss that had been thrown into the sky began raining back down.

Then—

something even larger appeared above everyone.

**Amdusias.**

The giant unicorn roared.

Its eyes burned red.

Its mane writhed like flame.

Winston Smith’s host body was already gone.

Amdusias had forced its true demonic body into the world.

Vikir looked up.

“Manifesting without a host puts an enormous strain on you.”

“You planning to destroy yourself?”

[I don’t care!]

[Not if it lets me eliminate you!]

Amdusias glared down.

[You are something that cannot be allowed to remain alive.]

[Not now.]

Vikir’s eyes narrowed.

“You mean because of the Gate?”

Amdusias stiffened.

[…If you know even that much—]

[then there is absolutely no reason to let you survive!]

Mana concentrated at the tip of its horn.

BOOM!

A massive horn-shaped blast fired downward.

Not merely at Vikir.

At everyone behind him.

“Move!”

“Everyone down!”

Spear King Cervantes and Roderick Usher reacted first.

Two heroes at the highest realm gathered their own power.

But someone else was faster.

Flash!

Vikir drew out Beelzebub.

A vast crimson slash rose like a tidal wave.

It collided with Amdusias’s attack.

BOOM!

[…!?]

Amdusias recoiled.

Vikir’s strength—

had changed.

The tower had raised him to Level 100.

Awakened stats he had never possessed.

Then forced those talents through decades of accelerated training.

And now—

outside the tower—

all of that sat on top of the power Vikir had possessed before entering.

His sword moved.

One fang.

Two.

Three.

Four.

Five.

Six.

Seven.

Then—

the eighth.

RUMBLE!

The eighth fang dwarfed every earlier one.

The complete extreme form of Baskerville’s Eighth Fang.

Everyone watching forgot to breathe.

Cervantes.

Roderick.

Osiris.

Especially Osiris.

“The Eighth Fang…”

Cervantes stared.

“Not merely the Eighth.”

“The perfected extreme.”

Osiris said nothing.

Officially—

Hugo Le Baskerville had mastered the Seventh Fang.

Osiris had only recently stepped into that realm.

Yet here stood a nineteen-year-old—

someone most of the world barely knew—

wielding the Eighth.

The eight slashes spiraled around Amdusias’s horn.

Grinding.

Cutting.

Then—

CRACK!

The horn snapped in half.

The impact shook the world.

Clouds above the Academy scattered from the pressure.

The ground convulsed.

Vikir—

alone—

had stopped the charge of a Demon King specialized in direct one-on-one combat.

[ROOOOAAAR!]

Amdusias screamed.

Pain.

Humiliation.

Rage.

Its hooves tore through rock.

Its body began burning as it pushed itself beyond its limits.

[I will not lose to a mere human!]

[Not one-on-one!]

Amdusias—

among the Ten Corpses—

had always taken pride in direct combat.

That pride would not let it accept this defeat.

The unicorn gathered everything remaining in its body.

Life.

Mana.

Flesh.

It began consuming itself to charge.

Vikir took one step backward.

“I know.”

“You’re strong in a duel.”

Amdusias accelerated.

Vikir did not meet it head-on.

“There’s no reason to fight an enemy where it’s strongest.”

If Amdusias specialized in one-on-one combat—

then Vikir had no obligation to give it one.

“Exactly!”

A bright voice rang out.

Vikir turned.

A familiar girl—

smiling brilliantly despite obvious exhaustion.

**Camus Morgue.**

“We met again fast this time.”

“I thought I’d have to wait another few years!”

Camus entered the battlefield.

Fire.

Iron.

A wall of burning skewers erupted—

blocking Amdusias’s charge.

BOOM!

Amdusias ground its teeth.

[Seere!]

[You betrayed me!?]

Camus scowled.

“Look properly.”

“Who exactly are you calling Seere?”

[…But I can clearly feel Seere’s presence—]

“If water is 99.99 percent water and 0.01 percent beer—”

“do you call it beer?”

Camus snorted.

The enormous magical power around her had once belonged to Seere.

But the demon itself—

was now tiny.

Smaller than a fairy.

Hanging from Camus’s shoulder like a miniature servant.

Decarabia laughed from Vikir’s chest.

[HAHAHAHA!]

[The mighty Seere reduced to that!]

Seere snapped back.

[You’re one to talk.]

[You look like a cheap roadside necklace.]

[I’m still better off than you!]

[My contractor and I are partners!]

[Not master and servant!]

Camus’s flaming iron wall held.

Then—

“Vikir!”

Another voice.

Dolores landed beside him.

Holy power spread from her.

Vikir’s wounds closed.

Mana began recovering.

Camus’s eyes narrowed instantly.

“Hey.”

“Why are you always hanging around my husband?”

Dolores stared.

“…What?”

“Who are **you**?”

“No name for you.”

“Move.”

“And stop touching my fiancé.”

“What kind of rude—”

“When have I ever even seen you?!”

“At the festival.”

Dolores froze.

“…Festival?”

That voice.

Familiar.

Her face changed.

“Wait.”

“You’re that black mage who broke into the festival.”

“The Corpse King—”

Camus’s expression stiffened.

“…Ah.”

“Oops.”

Dolores turned toward Vikir.

Clearly demanding an explanation.

Then—

“This isn’t the time.”

A calm voice landed beside them.

**Osiris Le Baskerville.**

Black cloak.

Red blade.

Solid aura.

A Sword Master.

Osiris lowered his head toward Vikir.

“…Acting Head.”

“Your orders.”

Camus froze.

Dolores froze.

So did everyone watching.

The young head of Baskerville—

bowing to an Academy first-year?

Calling him Acting Head?

Vikir accepted the salute as though it were normal.

Then stepped toward Amdusias.

Camus’s barrier creaked.

The unicorn was about to break free.

Vikir spoke.

“Hounds.”

“Gather.”

He drew an object from his clothing.

A dark-red—

tooth-shaped whistle.

The **Black Whistle**.

It pulsed once.

Low.

Heavy.

Every person nearby felt a chill.

Flutter.

A cloak moved somewhere.

Then another.

And another.

Across the Academy walls—

black-cloaked figures appeared one by one.

Long swords.

Crimson aura hanging from their blades like thick blood.

One hundred per unit.

Seven knight orders.

Roughly seven hundred Sword Graduators.

And six Counts to command them.

The Seven Counts of Baskerville—

all except Cane Corso, who remained tied to the Grave of Swords.

The Black Whistle represented the military authority Hugo had personally entrusted to Vikir.

And its call—

had summoned the hounds of Baskerville.
