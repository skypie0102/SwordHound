The Sack of Love opened.

It was supposed to produce—

the face Vikir loved.

Dolores stared.

“…!”

Then blinked.

*There's no one inside.*

The sack was empty.

Nothing.

No face.

No figure.

Only darkness.

Dantalian's thirty-six faces twisted.

[Impossible!]

[Humans live through love!]

[Not only humans—every animal possesses attachment!]

[What are you!?]

Vikir answered by kicking the sack aside.

Stab!

Beelzebub plunged into Dantalian's chest.

[GAH!?]

Black blood burst out.

The demon staggered.

For an instant—

Dantalian wondered whether the spell had failed.

It had not.

The mana had been spent.

The backlash from the broken spell struck him normally.

The Sack of Love had found exactly what was there.

Nothing.

Vikir stepped into the opening.

Stab.

Stab-stab-stab!

Aura—

so dense it felt solid—

drove through flesh.

Fangs of force crushed bone.

Tore organs.

Gnawed even at the demon's soul.

Dantalian backed away—

holding an abdomen reduced almost to rags.

[Impossible!]

[A human who has never loved anyone in his entire life!?]

[There is no such human!]

“There is.”

Vikir's answer was short.

“Here.”

From childhood—

he had been trained to suppress emotion.

Not bend it.

Not manage it.

Kill it.

And Vikir had obeyed so completely—

that the result was its own kind of distortion.

A killing machine.

A hound that moved only when commanded.

That—

was **Vikir Van Baskerville** in his previous life.

The Age of Destruction had only made it worse.

Hundreds of battles.

Countless brushes with death.

A world in which everything disappeared eventually.

Who had taught the hound to love?

When would he have found time to learn?

Dolores stood behind him.

Through the holy power she had poured into the Night Hound—

she could sense more than before.

Life.

Soul.

The resonance created when a priest prayed for—

healed—

or blessed another person.

Priests touched other souls through their work.

Sometimes deeply enough to be influenced.

Sometimes—

to partially synchronize.

A sentence returned to Dolores.

*Theology is the study of understanding people.*

The Night Hound had said that once.

She had not understood it fully at the time.

Now—

she did.

She could feel something of the life inside him.

*What kind of life did he survive?*

*How long has he carried this alone?*

*How long has he fought by himself?*

Not long ago—

Dolores had thought the Night Hound a terrorist.

Her own newspaper club had helped create the public villain-name used against him.

But the person she had now seen—

was fighting evil before anyone else even recognized it.

Misunderstood.

Persecuted.

Unloved.

Standing alone at a distance no one else could yet see.

Tears warmed Dolores's eyes.

She wanted to stand behind him.

No.

Beside him.

Not merely follow his path—

but walk it with him.

Support him.

Like the old stories of a priest traveling beside a hero on a Demon King subjugation.

She wanted to tell him—

*you are not alone.*

But Dolores understood something else too.

The Night Hound was not someone who leaned on others.

He did not offer his side.

Did not depend.

Did not ask.

He simply stood—

and advanced.

Even when the road was thorns.

Blood.

Isolation.

That knowledge hurt more than ignorance.

Dolores knew she might wait forever for someone who would never turn toward her.

Meanwhile—

Dantalian had a much more immediate kind of pain.

[AAAAAGH!]

Vikir had seized him by the hair.

The sword kept entering.

Again.

Again.

Again.

A hound did not release prey after biting.

Even when Dantalian's waves of demonic power tore at Vikir's body—

Vikir remained at close range.

[Damn you!]

[Then take this!]

Dantalian reached into Vikir's memories.

The demon recoiled.

The memories were full of cold, sharp fragments.

Like reaching into a sack of broken glass and knives.

*What kind of human lived this life!?*

Dantalian's hand—

metaphorically and spiritually—

came away torn.

At last—

he found one memory with a trace of warmth.

A family connection.

Dantalian shaped it into a face.

[Look!]

[Someone who once cared for you!]

[Can you still stab me!?]

The face—

was **Seth Le Baskerville**.

Dolores did not recognize him.

Seth had been secluded for so long that even many Baskervilles had forgotten his face.

Pale skin.

Heavy brows.

A handsome, distant expression.

Handsome enough to be striking—

bloodless enough to be eerie.

*Someone connected to the Night Hound?*

Dolores had no time to study him.

Vikir moved.

“Thanks.”

“This helps.”

One intended stab—

became two.

Then many.

Stab-stab!

Slash!

Dantalian's Seth-face exploded beneath the barrage.

The demon's actual body tore with it.

[AAAAAGH!]

Then—

one face changed.

A young girl.

Blond hair.

Fair skin.

Eyes that drooped slightly—

giving her a sad look.

An old golden necklace hung around her neck.

Engraved on it—

**Peri**.

Vikir stopped.

“…!”

Dantalian did not understand why.

Only that Vikir had stopped.

An opening.

[DIE!]

Purple tongues launched.

Then—

BOOM!

White fire swallowed them.

Dolores.

The moment she saw Peri's face—

anger replaced hesitation.

*I told you.*

*Don't touch what matters to someone else.*

Holy flame scorched the tongues.

Dolores landed beside Vikir.

Crisis had made her calmer.

Braver.

She looked directly at him.

“If it becomes too hard—”

“lean on me.”

“?”

“I'll wait.”

“No matter how long.”

“???”

Vikir tilted his head.

He clearly had no idea what she was talking about.

Then—

flash!

The white light around Dolores flowed into him.

Both of them froze.

“…!”

Soul resonance.

Not simply a spell.

Not merely healer and recipient.

For an instant—

their souls aligned.

The connection described in old Saintess traditions—

a **soulmate** resonance.

Dolores's holy light exploded.

Nearly ten times stronger.

The buff of an awakened Saintess.

The phenomenon occurred only when someone reached the deepest level of resonance with the Saintess's soul—

someone capable of drawing out the power sleeping inside her.

The Night Hound.

Consciously or not—

Dolores's soul had recognized him.

Strength drained from her body.

All the divine power she had gathered—

poured into Vikir.

Dolores possessed an enormous natural reserve.

Multiplied through awakening—

the effect was no ordinary blessing.

The instant it entered him—

BOOM!

Vikir felt the wall above him break.

The barrier he had been unable to cross—

collapsed.

A new realm became visible.

**Sword Master.**

The realm of true superhumans.

With Dolores's power—

Vikir stepped across it.
