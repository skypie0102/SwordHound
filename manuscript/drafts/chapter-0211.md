[Be careful.]

The Corpse Queen—

watched Bianca retreat.

Irritation—

still burning.

The girl—

looked young.

Probably—

a first-year.

Maybe—

an early entrant.

And that—

triggered a thought.

*What if?*

History—

had no if.

But—

just once—

the Corpse Queen allowed herself one.

*What if I had never become this?*

Would she—

have entered this Academy?

Worn ridiculous costumes?

Walked festival streets—

like everyone else her age?

*…With him.*

One face—

surfaced.

Time—

could erase almost anything.

Not that face.

Expressionless eyes.

Always looking—

somewhere distant.

Then—

a memory.

“Hey.”

“Want to come to my house and cook?”

“….”

“I'll take care of you.”

“….”

“When are you entering the Academy?”

“Let's coordinate.”

“I'll probably enter a year or two early.”

“If we became first-years together—

that would be fun…”

The memory—

hurt.

Then—

tug.

A small figure beside her—

pulled the cloak.

**Geronto.**

Red hair.

Bandages—

covering face and neck.

A high undead—

brought back by the Queen.

Geronto—

reported without words.

The Corpse Queen nodded.

[Yes, Rosie.]

[The Night Hound's scent continues this way.]

Night Hound.

Last night.

The intruder—

who fought like a barbarian—

and wielded swordsmanship—

strangely familiar.

At the decisive moment—

his blade had erupted—

with a sun-bright aura.

The exact trajectory—

lost inside the light.

But the Corpse Queen—

had seen something like it before.

[…Madam Eight-Legs.]

[That night.]

For the first time in years—

her heart—

had seemed to beat.

She had almost forgotten—

what that felt like.

During closed-door training—

she had pushed her mind—

toward the deepest Abyss of magic.

Mana backflow—

destroyed half her body.

Since then—

her heart had never properly beaten.

Until—

the Night Hound.

*Impossible.*

*Impossible.*

*It can't be.*

*But—*

She did not even know—

what she wanted the answer to be.

That confusion—

brought her here.

To Colosseo.

To find—

the Night Hound.

Geronto led her—

to a dormitory.

[…A student dorm?]

[Surely the villain terrifying the Imperial Capital—

isn't an Academy student.]

She melted the back-door lock.

Then another.

Then another.

One room.

A girl's room.

Simple.

Private.

Empty—

because everyone was at the festival.

On the windowsill—

small pawprints.

Dog.

[…She keeps a dog?]

[Lucky.]

The Queen—

felt something unpleasant—

close to guilt.

In pursuit of black magic—

she had cut apart—

reassembled—

and experimented on—

many animals.

Dogs included.

Pets—

therefore—

made her uneasy.

Geronto—

shook her head.

Scent—

ended here.

The Queen sighed.

[Then we catch the owner.]

[And question her.]

Back—

to the festival street.

Then—

pink.

Thousands—

of magical cherry petals—

falling through the night.

The Corpse Queen—

stopped.

Eyes—

wide behind the skull mask.

Fake flowers.

Still—

beautiful.

“Wow!”

“Your costume is incredible!”

Several boys—

passed.

“Thought the armor was real bone.”

“That mask is ridiculously detailed.”

“Did you see her jawline?”

“She looks amazing.”

The Corpse Queen—

stood—

strangely still.

People—

talking about her.

Casually.

Not—

fear.

Not—

rank.

Not—

death.

Just—

festival chatter.

Unfamiliar.

Not unpleasant.

*Halloween.*

Colosseo's summer festival—

more open—

more chaotic—

than the winter version.

Strangers—

Sometimes—

hugged.

Students—

zombies.

skeletons.

ghouls.

vampires.

death knights.

ghosts.

mummies.

Outside visitors—

copying them.

Then—

“Pretty girls!”

“Buy a skewer!”

“Half price because you're pretty!”

pushed food toward them.

“Wow!”

“You went all out on that costume!”

“You must love the festival!”

The Queen—

said nothing.

The vendor added—

“Here.”

“Free extra.”

[…Is it good?]

Geronto—

nodded.

Soon—

the Corpse Queen—

ate a sugared fruit skewer.

Geronto—

an ice-cream waffle.

For perhaps—

the first time—

slightly good.

Only slightly.

Geronto tracked.

somehow—

a giant teddy bear.

[Rosie.]

[Are we actually going the right way?]

**Ryukeion.**

shouting.

Drums.

Music.

stomping.

dazed.

[…That girl?]

[Still standing around stupidly?]

[She won't live long.]

[…!?]
