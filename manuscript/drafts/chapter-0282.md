# Chapter 282: What Money Can't Buy (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/282.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 280 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Night Hound, Dolores L. Quovadis, Damien Bourgeois, Bartolomeo Bourgeois, Cindywendy, House Bourgeois, Dantalian, Sixth Corpse, Pit Bull Knights, Boston Terrier Le Baskerville, and Sword Graduator.

A few days later—

Night Hound returned to Damien’s estate.

This time—

Dolores came with him.

Vikir intended to formalize Damien’s cooperation—

and introduce the two.

The mansion remained heavily guarded.

But Damien had provided a route.

One thin gap—

left unwatched.

Vikir guided Dolores through it.

Several checkpoints later—

they reached a high wall.

Dolores looked up.

Before she could decide how to cross—

“Come here.”

“…What?”

Vikir picked her up.

Then crossed the wall in one movement.

THUD.

He landed.

Set her down.

Dolores blinked.

“Ah…”

“We’re already over?”

“…?”

“No!”

“That came out wrong!”

Her ears reddened.

She hurriedly covered them with her hair.

A voice came from the garden.

“This place is a little plain for a date.”

Vikir and Dolores turned.

Damien stood there.

“Since you officiated my daughter’s wedding—

I would be willing to officiate yours.”

“You talk more nonsense now.”

“Hahaha.”

“Maybe.”

Dolores stared.

The Damien she remembered from the University League—

had been gloomy.

Tense.

Quiet to the point of discomfort.

This man—

was smiling.

*Officiated his daughter’s wedding?*

*But I thought his daughter died in the carriage accident…*

Dolores had questions.

Many.

Vikir answered none of them.

“This man will help us.”

Damien extended his hand.

“Welcome, Saintess of Quovadis.”

“We’ve met before.”

Dolores shook it politely.

“It is an honor to meet you again, Director Damien.”

“If I’d known we would end up in the same boat—

I would have paid more attention to you back then.”

He laughed again.

Dolores looked even more surprised.

Vikir moved directly to business.

“I’m going to kill Bartolomeo—

head of House Bourgeois.”

Dolores looked at Damien.

The proposed target was his own brother.

His own family head.

Damien showed no hesitation.

“Good.”

“I’ve already arranged the opening.”

Damien explained.

“I had several loyal contacts inside the family spread rumors about Oracle’s recent investment activity.”

“My brother was very interested.”

“Young investment geniuses.”

“Unusual talent.”

“That kind of thing attracts him.”

Vikir asked:

“Did he bite?”

“Yes.”

Dolores still looked lost.

Damien clarified.

“A dinner invitation.”

“Oh.”

“Remember this.”

“Dinner is the ideal opportunity.”

Dolores frowned.

“Why dinner?”

“Why not while he’s traveling?”

“Or sleeping?”

“My brother has a peculiar habit.”

Damien continued.

“Whenever he meets or eats with outsiders—

he brings them into the family vault.”

“At all other times—

elite private guards remain around him.”

“The vault?”

“Yes.”

Dolores imagined a large storage room.

Damien corrected that idea immediately.

“It’s a cube.”

“Roughly one hundred meters in each dimension.”

“Length.”

“Width.”

“Height.”

Dolores stopped writing.

Damien continued.

“The inner walls contain mithril and orichalcon.”

“Ordinary force does nothing.”

“Dozens—

possibly hundreds—

of anti-teleportation layers overlap through the structure.”

“No magical escape.”

“No one even knows exactly who designed it.”

A fortress—

disguised as a vault.

Inside—

mountains of treasure.

Gold.

Currency.

Jewels.

Artwork.

Guests dined for two hours in the middle of it all.

“Why eat there?”

Damien smiled faintly.

“Because too many people visit my brother intending to brag about wealth.”

“He likes to destroy their confidence before negotiations begin.”

Dolores continued writing.

“And why exactly two hours?”

“Because once the vault closes—

it stays closed for two hours.”

“No exception.”

“That limit comes from the original security test.”

Vikir looked over.

“Who tested it?”

“Baskerville.”

Damien nodded.

“My brother borrowed one of their knight orders.”

“The **Pit Bull Knights**, I believe.”

Vikir knew them.

An extermination order commanded by **Boston Terrier Le Baskerville**.

Roughly one hundred Sword Graduators.

Once they bit—

they did not let go.

Damien continued.

“The entire order attacked the vault.”

“It took them two hours to break through.”

Dolores stared.

So—

the vault could endure continuous attacks from around one hundred Sword Graduators—

for two hours.

She summarized:

“Which means…”

“If we enter the vault with Bartolomeo—

we have two hours with no outside interference.”

“Correct.”

“No one outside can see or hear what happens.”

Dolores asked:

“Doesn’t he worry about being attacked by a guest?”

“Strangely—

I’ve never seen him worry.”

Vikir answered.

“Because he believes no guest can threaten him.”

Damien looked over.

Vikir continued.

“He has absolute confidence in his own strength.”

“Because the man occupying that position—

is almost certainly the Sixth Corpse.”

Damien’s expression shifted.

“…A demon?”

He had not known.

Dolores started writing even faster.

Vikir’s certainty came from Cindywendy’s newest investigation.

The Sixth Corpse.

Dantalian’s old ally.

A demon using the wealth of House Bourgeois to construct influence—

and eventually an army.

Vikir remembered the future battlefield.

Humanity advancing across a ruined Imperial Capital.

Then facing—

one of the most radiant beings cast from heaven.

An enemy whose presence suggested courage.

Dignity.

Majesty.

But whose actual battlefield methods were—

vicious.

Cunning.

Dirty.

Brutal.

Lightning from the eyes.

A disgusting stench with every breath.

A mouth split like a crack across polar ice.

Horns and wings vast enough to dominate the battlefield.

*In army-against-army warfare—

he was a nightmare.*

*The longer he has to build forces—

the worse it becomes.*

Now Vikir understood why the Sixth had chosen Bourgeois.

Money—

could buy an army.

And House Bourgeois had spent years building networks of people already tied to demonic influence.

Damien asked:

“Can you kill him in two hours?”

“My brother isn’t just rich.”

“The former head of Baskerville once wanted to adopt him.”

Vikir’s expression sharpened.

Hugo’s father—

had coveted Bartolomeo’s talent.

*Then the body itself is exceptionally gifted.*

And now—

a demon controlled it.

Vikir thought.

Then nodded.

“Two hours is tight.”

“But possible.”

“If you do one thing outside.”

“Me?”

Damien pointed at himself.

“Outside the vault?”

He was a Sword Graduator—

but no match for a true monster at Vikir’s level.

He would likely not enter the vault at all.

So what use could he be outside—

during a period when outside intervention was impossible?

Vikir placed a written plan in front of Damien and Dolores.

“With this—

House Bourgeois is finished.”

Both read it.

Then looked up.

Damien spoke first.

“This is no longer just a House Bourgeois problem…”

Dolores finished:

“…You’re going to break the entire Empire.”
