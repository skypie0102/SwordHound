# Chapter 374: Sucker Shark (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/374.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 372 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Sakkuth de Leviathan / Plague Leper, Garam Nord, Kirko Grimm, Nouvelle Vague, Poseidon, and the escape-plan chronology.

The prisoners’ third way of getting food—

was begging.

Several guards sat on rocks eating lunch.

While prisoners gnawed on salted fish and black bread—

the guards enjoyed far better meals.

Salted pork.

Large chunks of boiled fish roe.

Fresh seaweed.

Bottles of manatee milk.

Sometimes—

carrots.

Spinach.

Potatoes.

Higher-ranking guards occasionally received fruit.

Apples.

Oranges.

Compared with food on the surface—

it was nothing special.

To the starving prisoners—

it was a feast from heaven.

So some guards used food as bait.

“Hey.”

“Sucker Shark~”

A guard waved lazily.

A prisoner waiting nearby sprinted over so quickly his feet almost slipped.

“Down.”

The prisoner dropped to the ground.

Exposed his stomach.

“Up.”

“Sit.”

“Leg.”

“Open your mouth.”

“Hand.”

The prisoner obeyed every command—

like a trained dog.

Only then did the guard chuckle and toss him a leftover piece of pork.

“Keep working hard.”

“Don’t cause trouble.”

“Yes, Guard!”

“It is my honor!”

The prisoner pressed his forehead to the ground.

Drooling.

Like a sucker shark feeding on scraps left by a larger fish.

After making him wait—

the guard finally said:

“Fine.”

“Eat.”

The prisoner picked up the pork with trembling hands.

He smelled it for a long time.

Then carefully licked the hardened white fat coating its surface.

Salt.

Oil.

Meat.

His eyes filled with tears.

He shaved off tiny bites with the tips of his teeth.

Let each piece melt slowly in his mouth.

As though it were candy.

The surrounding prisoners could only swallow.

“If you finish this month’s quota—

you can have leftovers too!”

“Work hard enough and I’ll let my Sucker Shark help you!”

“Highest workload this month gets ice cream!”

The guards laughed and tossed bits of roe—

meat—

milk—

onto the ground.

The prisoners—

already half-insane from rotten fish and stone bread—

erupted.

“OOOOOH!”

“WORK!”

“You heard him!”

“ICE CREAM!”

“It’s mine!”

“I’ll work the hardest!”

Their morale exploded.

At this point—

they hardly knew whether they were people anymore.

Hunger—

regardless of gender—

was terrifying.

It trained people down to the bone.

…But.

Not every prisoner dreamed of becoming a Sucker Shark.

“Hey.”

Sometimes—

the hierarchy between guard and prisoner was reversed.

A Level Eight prisoner sat on a rock.

Whistling.

Picking absently at his skin.

**Sakkuth de Leviathan.**

Known inside Nouvelle Vague as the **Plague Leper**.

Other prisoners kept away—

afraid of infection.

“Hey.”

“Can’t you hear me?”

“Is your liver swollen enough to stick out of your belly?”

The person Sakkuth was calling—

was not another prisoner.

It was a guard.

A junior one.

Burn scars covered much of his face.

**Garam Nord.**

Sakkuth stretched out a hand.

“Give me your meal.”

“….”

Garam looked around.

None of the other guards were paying attention.

That was not because Sakkuth was being subtle.

It was because nobody cared enough about Garam to watch him.

Sakkuth smirked and took the food.

“You knew how to wait until the others weren’t looking.”

“Good boy.”

“….”

“Consider this the price of me leaving you alone.”

“Understand?”

His voice dropped.

“If I want—

that Kirko girl catches the plague tomorrow.”

“And she dies badly.”

“….”

“You like her, don’t you?”

“You don’t want to see her lose control of her body—

dry up—

and rot away.”

“….”

“Then bring me your entire lunch every day.”

Sakkuth chuckled.

He tried to shove the pork and fish roe into his mouth.

Then—

“HEY!”

A sharp shout crossed the entire work site.

Even Sakkuth hiccuped.

Garam looked over.

A young female guard stood there.

**Kirko Grimm.**

An elite junior officer—

soon expected to receive promotion.

Her eyes narrowed.

First at Sakkuth.

Then Garam.

“A guard giving food to a prisoner?”

“Are you insane?”

Garam lowered his eyes.

Prisoners clicked their tongues.

“That annoying pair again.”

“Smart woman.”

“Stupid man.”

“What a waste.”

“Hehehe.”

“Choose the wrong partner and your life gets hard.”

Apparently—

this was not the first time.

Kirko marched over.

Snatched the food out of Sakkuth’s hand.

Then glared.

“If you take food that doesn’t belong to you again—

I’ll cut your head off on the spot.”

“And report it afterward.”

“Hehehe.”

“Our pretty little guard is fierce today—”

SHING.

Sakkuth stopped.

Kirko’s blade flashed.

The tip of his beard fell away.

Sakkuth blinked.

Kirko snorted.

Then looked at Garam.

“Stop embarrassing your partner—”

She stopped herself.

“No.”

“Stop embarrassing the guards.”

“Idiot.”

“S-sorry.”

“Don’t apologize.”

“Are you really that stupid?”

The contempt in her eyes was faint—

but visible.

“…If you can’t do your job properly—

at least grow a spine.”

Then she turned away.

Cold air snapped through her hair.

Garam could not answer.

The prisoners laughed.

“Stupid Garam is stupid again today.”

“How can a pathetic idiot like that be a guard?”

“If you get assigned to his team—

you can steal his food.”

“He doesn’t even make people work.”

“And still feeds them.”

“I want his team next month too.”

The nicknames followed.

**Stupid Garam.**

**Dog Who Eats Shit.**

**Walking Bread.**

**Idiot.**

**Sucker.**

And surprisingly—

many of those names came from other guards.

“Lost your lunch to a prisoner again?”

“You moron.”

“Bribing them to prevent problems is one thing.”

“But this just spoils them.”

“Don’t make more work for us.”

“Kirko.”

“You okay?”

“Forget that loser.”

“Stress will ruin your pretty face.”

“Hey, idiot.”

“See me after roll call tonight.”

The other guards sneered.

Threatened him.

Garam’s scarred head lowered farther.

Kirko watched.

Sighed faintly.

Then left with the others.

Sakkuth leaned close one more time.

“Hehehe…”

“That bitch saved you today.”

“Not tomorrow.”

“Bring me all your lunch.”

“Or I’ll turn Kirko into a second Plague Leper.”

He walked away humming.

Garam stood alone.

Surrounded by mockery.

Yet—

his eyes still followed Kirko.

Silently.

Watching everything—

was Vikir.

“…Hmm.”

He thought for a while.

Then made a plan.

*This may be useful.*

Garam.

Kirko.

Whatever existed between the two of them—

could become leverage.

*When the time comes to escape.*

Vikir was already planning his breakout from Nouvelle Vague.

The moment he found Poseidon—

humanity’s hope—

he would act.

And—

that moment arrived much sooner than expected.
