**Iron-Blooded Sword House Baskerville**  
**Difficulty: ★★★★★**

**—This student has excellently resolved every task presented by the House.**

**Head of House Hugo Le Baskerville — Seal**

The news that Vikir had completed Baskerville’s assignment stunned all four academies.

Basilios of Varangian.

Hippolyte of Temisquira.

Whitebeard Whale of the Mage Tower.

Even Professor Banshee of Colosseo.

All of them stared.

“When was the last time Baskerville issued a passing certificate?”

“If this is genuine…”

“That House is so closed off that suspicion is inevitable. Hehehe.”

Basilios, Hippolyte, and Whitebeard Whale examined the certificate again and again.

Then—

snatch.

A cold hand took it away.

Professor Banshee.

“Are you doubting my student?”

The other representatives laughed awkwardly.

Banshee turned to Vikir.

“You know you were the last of the top ten to return.”

“Yes.”

Vikir nodded.

Strangely—

far more time had passed outside than he expected.

The flow of time inside the Grave of Swords had clearly been distorted.

*I’m not even sure Cane Corso’s chamber was truly the top of the tower.*

*There may have been something above it.*

Banshee’s voice sharpened.

“You answer quickly.”

“Thank you.”

“That was not praise.”

“Sorry.”

Her brow twitched.

“Vikir.”

“Your late return delayed the artifact-awarding ceremony by ten days.”

“Baskerville gave us no updates.”

“We were close to reporting you missing.”

“Did you know that?”

“The mission was urgent enough that I had no opportunity to report along the way.”

“What kind of mission?”

“To retrieve a Wraith Tree.”

The other representatives froze.

“A Wraith Tree?”

“Nonsense. That thing is only a myth.”

“Hehehe… that’s quite a joke.”

Basilios, Hippolyte, and Whitebeard Whale dismissed it.

Professor Banshee did not.

She knew Vikir well enough to recognize when he was being literal.

“…By Wraith Tree—”

“do you mean the tree from the myths surrounding Morgue Tzersi and the legendary Baskerville Ornati?”

“Yes.”

“And House Baskerville ordered you to retrieve it?”

“Yes.”

“…Did you succeed?”

“I can’t answer that.”

Vikir glanced toward the certificate.

So did everyone else.

Banshee eventually shook her head.

“We’ll discuss that later.”

“For now, congratulations on completing the assignment.”

◆◆◆

At last—

all ten students had returned.

The four keys were gathered.

The gate to the shared artifact vault could finally be opened.

Whitebeard Whale smiled.

“You have one day.”

“If you fail to leave within twenty-four hours, the gate will close.”

“It will not open again until the next League.”

“So respect the time limit.”

Then he added lightly—

“And don’t be alarmed by the skeletons inside.”

“They belong to students who failed to choose an artifact before the gate closed.”

The joke was not especially funny.

◆◆◆

All ten entered under the eyes of the four academies.

Roughly twenty-three hours later—

nine students emerged.

Every one of them had been chosen by an artifact.

That alone was unusual.

Normally, one or two top-ten students left empty-handed.

Tudor, Sancho, Pigi, and Bianca waited near the gate.

“There!”

“Sinclaire! Over here!”

“You got one! Congratulations!”

“See? I told you you could do it!”

Sinclaire looked tired—

but she was smiling brightly.

One by one, the returning students reported what had chosen them.

Dolores came first.

She carried a small mirror.

“My artifact is the **Mirror of Truth**.”

“It reveals the true appearance of whatever it reflects—provided the target is within its effective range.”

She immediately angled it toward Lovegood.

The mirror showed Lovegood without makeup.

“Ahhh! What are you doing?! I just finished getting ready!”

“You’re still pretty.”

Dolores laughed as Lovegood hid her face.

Lovegood went next.

“My artifact is the **Shield of Love**!”

She proudly displayed a heart-shaped brooch-hairpin.

“It blocks even powerful mind control or hypnosis—but only once, and only if the person you truly love is nearby!”

It sounded less impressive than the more permanent artifacts.

Lovegood looked delighted anyway.

“What are you planning to use that for?”

“N-none of your business!”

Dolores and Lovegood immediately began bickering.

Then Bakilaga laughed.

“Hahaha!”

He raised a massive metal gauntlet.

“This is the **Magic King’s Hand**.”

“It amplifies physical strength.”

“Supposedly it belonged to a legendary warrior from another dimension.”

Hohenheim nodded.

“Mine is similar.”

“A staff that increases mana.”

“It supposedly contains traces of pink dragon scales.”

“Though whether such a dragon even exists is another question.”

Then came Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro.

“We received a set of three swords.”

“They become stronger when used together.”

“They’re called the **Three Swords**.”

The weapons looked nearly identical.

Yet when brought together—

their power clearly multiplied.

The three brothers known as Baskerville’s Trident looked extremely pleased.

Then—

“…I’m the only one who got something this weird.”

Granola emerged dejectedly.

In his hand was a small brooch shaped like lips.

“It’s called the **Lips of Sweet Talk**.”

“It can answer any question.”

“But only with ‘yes’ or ‘no.’”

“And it can only be used once in a lifetime.”

Sinclaire patted his shoulder.

“That sounds useful.”

“Sometimes I wonder whether I’m living the right way.”

“I’d ask it then.”

Granola scoffed.

“Why would you wonder about that?”

“I’m living perfectly well.”

“You should have more confidence.”

He complained—

but visibly cheered up.

Finally—

Sinclaire showed her own artifact.

“The one that chose me is the **Money Hat**.”

“It gets stronger by eating money.”

A battered black witch’s hat sat in her hands.

Its brim was worn.

Parts were missing.

It looked ominous—

and not remotely wealthy.

Everyone around her winced.

At Colosseo, everyone knew Sinclaire was poor.

Most students had parents paying their tuition and living expenses.

Sinclaire worked to cover both.

And she had been chosen by an artifact that demanded even more money.

Cruel luck.

But Sinclaire smiled anyway.

“Hehe.”

“In a world where money is power, I guess I’ll just have to earn a lot.”

◆◆◆

Time continued passing.

The students registered their artifacts.

The clock made another circuit.

Sinclaire had been the ninth to exit.

Still—

Vikir had not appeared.

Less than a minute remained.

Everyone stared at the gate.

“What is Vikir doing?”

“There’s almost no time!”

“We’re counting seconds now!”

“Don’t worry. He’ll come out at the last moment.”

“He always likes dramatic entrances.”

“He was last during the second test too.”

Then—

the final second passed.

The gate shut.

“…What?”

Silence.

Then Professor Banshee screamed.

“Open it!”

“Open the gate!”

“My student is still inside!”

It was impossible.

The vault only opened once per year.

All four keys were required.

And the gate itself was an ancient artifact that modern mages could maintain—

but not freely control.

The other representatives lowered their heads.

This had happened before.

Students who grew too greedy.

Students unable to choose.

When the gate reopened the next year—

only skeletons remained.

“No…”

Tudor approached the sealed gate.

Sancho, Pigi, and Bianca stood beside him.

“What just happened?”

“He didn’t make it out?”

“Can’t this thing open again?”

Only clothes and other inert personal items could pass through the gate.

Food and water were destroyed in transit.

Someone trapped inside—

would eventually starve.

“Vikir!”

Lovegood and the other fan-club members cried out.

Nothing answered.

Dolores simply stared.

*No.*

*He must have come out already.*

But he had not.

Her heart seemed to fall to the bottom of her chest.

Her vision darkened.

Her legs weakened.

Then—

rip.

A strange sound came from the gate.

Like a blade tearing through thick leather.

Whitebeard Whale’s expression changed.

“…What?”

The barrier was splitting.

A crack widened.

A face appeared through it.

“Sorry.”

“I’m late.”

Vikir stepped through—

as calm as ever.
