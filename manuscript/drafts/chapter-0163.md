Crack-crack-crack—

Quilt's human shell split apart.

The demon emerged.

Vikir watched without surprise.

*Dantalian.*

The Ninth Corpse.

Old.

Young.

Child.

Woman.

Man.

Handsome.

Beautiful.

Ugly.

Fat.

Thin.

Noble.

Slave.

Rich.

Beggar.

Hero.

Villain.

Thirty-six heads.

Thirty-six faces.

Thirty-six identities.

All joined to one thin body in a gloomy suit.

From every face—

a purple tongue.

Each tongue could extend impossibly far.

Each tip—

sharp as a blade.

Vikir remembered the demon exactly.

*A Thousand Faces.*

*The one who killed more of my comrades than almost any other.*

Dantalian stole the faces of the dead.

Then used those faces against the living.

Mother.

Father.

Sibling.

Grandparent.

Friend.

Teacher.

Student.

Childhood companion.

Fiancé.

First love.

Someone secretly loved.

One moment of hesitation—

and the blade-like tongue killed you.

Then Dantalian wore *your* face—

to kill the next person.

Vikir's conclusion from his previous life had never changed.

*Kill this demon early.*

Not because it was the strongest.

Because given enough time—

Dantalian caused catastrophic damage to the Human Alliance.

Sssssssss—

the smell of rotting flesh spread.

A foul aura followed.

Vikir moved back.

Dantalian possessed countless faces—

and disease.

Plague suited a false holy man perfectly.

Cause an epidemic.

Harvest the suffering.

Smile gently while pretending to save people.

This time—

Vikir had one advantage.

Dolores.

Flash!

Divine light blocked the plague mist.

Dolores stared at Dantalian.

“…Why?”

[Why?]

[Why what?]

“Why come to the human realm and do this?”

“Why an orphanage?”

All thirty-six faces smiled.

Dantalian said nothing more about the gate the ten Demon Kings intended to open.

But he answered the second question.

[A farm.]

Dolores went still.

“…What?”

Dantalian lifted a small golden necklace.

Some children in the orphanage wore identical ones.

[This marks breeding stock.]

“…!”

[Demons survive by consuming humans.]

[Humans consume beasts.]

[I decided hunting every meal was inefficient.]

[Why not raise the livestock instead?]

Dolores's face drained of color.

[Let them reproduce here.]

[Increase the population.]

[Manage the quality of the meat.]

[Slaughter them at the proper age.]

[Efficient.]

[A mutually beneficial system.]

“Mutually beneficial!?”

“You're insane!”

[Of course it is.]

[Many of these orphans would never have been born safely.]

[Others would have died after birth.]

[Others would have starved in the streets.]

[Thanks to me, they live comfortably into adolescence.]

Dantalian smiled.

[Of course, once they grow too old, the meat toughens.]

[So they must be eaten before then.]

“You dare do this under Rune's name!?”

Dolores's holy power flared.

“Divine punishment will find you!”

Dantalian's seventy-two eyes widened.

[Divine punishment?]

[But I have no sin.]

The statement was so absurd it sounded like a joke.

Dantalian meant it.

[I have proof.]

He produced stacks of paper.

Smaller than banknotes.

Red lettering.

Red seals.

He scattered them into the air.

Dolores stared.

> **Certificate of Absolution**
>
> All sins of this faithful believer are forgiven.
>
> —Issued and guaranteed by the Old Testament Faction—

Another.

> **Certificate of Remission**
>
> All punishment owed by this faithful believer is remitted.
>
> —Issued and guaranteed by the Old Testament Faction—

Cardinal seals authenticated the documents.

Dantalian possessed piles of them.

[I paid enormous donations for these.]

[By your church's own standards—]

[my sins do not exist.]

Crime demanded punishment.

That was ordinary reason.

Yet—

under the system Quovadis had permitted—

punishment could be bought away.

Even guilt itself could be declared forgiven.

The result was grotesque.

A demon—

the source of atrocity—

could wave church documents declaring itself innocent.

Dolores could not speak.

Dantalian laughed.

[Ah.]

[One more thing.]

[The man who issued these certificates to me—]

[was your father.]

“…!?”

[Cardinal Humbert.]

[He liked the mountain of gold very much.]

[Stamp.]

[Stamp.]

[Stamp.]

Dolores swayed.

**Humbert L. Quovadis.**

Symbolic leader of the Old Testament Faction.

Rigid defender of ancient law.

Her father.

Behind that public principle—

he had accumulated enormous wealth through indulgence sales—

building power inside the family.

And somewhere along that road—

he had granted absolution even to a demon.

Dolores could not simply point at Dantalian and shout *heresy*.

The rot existed inside her own house.

[Heh heh heh!]

[I am innocent!]

[Your own Rune Church absolved—]

Stab!

A crimson point punched through one mouth.

Then another.

Dantalian's mockery stopped.

“When fighting a demon—”

Vikir seized one long purple tongue.

“don't let its tongue control the fight.”

He pulled.

Then cut.

[AAAAAGH!]

The remaining faces opened their mouths.

Purple tongues shot forward.

Beelzebub moved faster.

Slash.

Slash-slash-slash.

Red trails crossed the darkness.

Face after face split apart.

Dantalian panicked.

[You dare cut *me*!?]

A gentle grandmother's face rotated forward.

Vikir cut it apart.

An elderly grandfather.

Cut.

A beloved young actress.

Cut.

Innocent child.

Sexy woman.

Frail old man.

Face after face—

destroyed without hesitation.

Dantalian became confused.

These were real faces.

Peeled from the facial skin of real people.

Perfectly reproducing the expressions and voices they had in life.

Why did this human not hesitate?

[You inhuman bastard!]

[I have more humanity than you!]

Vikir ignored him.

Cut again.

Black-red blood sprayed.

Dantalian stumbled.

The number of tongue-spears decreased.

New faces tried to regenerate.

Vikir destroyed them faster.

*I paid for this lesson in my previous life.*

*The faces are imitations.*

*The voices are imitations.*

*I won't be fooled twice.*

“Stretch your neck a little farther.”

Vikir flicked blood from Beelzebub.

Dantalian's remaining faces smiled strangely.

[Cold to everyone, aren't you?]

[Then let's try this.]

The faces changed.

Boys.

Girls.

All thirteen or younger.

The children of Indulgentia.

Vikir's voice stayed dry.

“I've cut through children's faces before too.”

“You think this changes anything?”

[Of course not.]

Vikir stopped.

Dantalian was not aiming at him.

Behind him—

Dolores.

She had volunteered here for years.

Weekend after weekend.

Children she knew.

Children she thought had eventually left for better homes.

None of them had.

They were here.

Inside Dantalian.

[Sister!]

[I missed you!]

[Sister, save me!]

[It hurts!]

[Please get me out!]

Faces bloomed across Dantalian's body like flowers from a vase.

Children Dolores had known from her first year at the Academy—

through her third.

Crying.

Begging.

In pain.

Vikir glanced back.

He expected Dolores to break.

Instead—

white light erupted.

Dantalian recoiled.

[GAH!?]

Holy light burned black smoke off his body.

Dolores's voice came from inside the radiance.

Very quiet.

Far too quiet.

Rage hotter than molten rock sat beneath every word.

“…You touched something you never should have.”

Vikir felt a chill.

For an instant—

he remembered the end of the world.

The woman who had repeatedly turned despairing battlefields back toward hope.

Humanity's final hero.

The **Saintess of Steel**.

For one brief moment—

that future Dolores seemed to stand here again.
