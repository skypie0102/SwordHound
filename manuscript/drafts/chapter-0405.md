Bzzzz—

Beelzebub vibrated on Vikir’s right wrist.

Like a compass needle—

the magic sword pointed into the darkness.

Then—

“Where are you going in such a hurry—

Major ‘Rotten Dog’ Garam?”

Black Tongue crawled into view.

“No.”

“Should I call you Major **Night Hound Vikir**?”

“Hehehe.”

He was much larger than before.

The muscles writhing across his body were not really muscles at all.

Leeches.

Every fiber squirmed and contracted.

Black Tongue extended arms longer than his legs—

then used palms larger than his face to drag himself across the floor and walls.

“You aren’t Garam.”

“….”

“It never made sense.”

“How does a garbage-tier junior guard suddenly awaken into a senior officer?”

“….”

“The other idiots can call it hidden talent if they want.”

“I’m not that stupid.”

“I’m the only sane person in Nouvelle Vague.”

His mouth split into a grin.

Vikir smiled faintly too.

“If you recognized me from one taste of blood—

stop wasting time talking.”

“Oh?”

“Did you just admit it?”

“Yes.”

He was leaving anyway.

There was no reason to maintain Garam’s face any longer.

Vikir released the disguise.

The burn marks vanished.

His hair and features returned to normal.

His real face emerged.

Black Tongue’s long tongue slid out.

“Escaped prisoners normally aren’t my responsibility.”

“But I suppose I’ll make an exception.”

WHOOSH!

The darkness around him moved.

A giant palm shot toward Vikir’s face.

Vikir leaned back.

Rubbery fingers grazed his cheek.

Blood welled.

Then was drawn toward Black Tongue’s fingertips.

But—

drip.

Drip.

Drip.

The blood lost momentum and scattered before reaching him.

Black Tongue stopped the suction himself.

“Hm.”

“Your blood is strange.”

“Drinking it carelessly might be bad for me.”

He had noticed the toxins.

Vikir ducked the next strike and rushed forward.

Beelzebub sprang from his wrist.

Then—

bzzzzzz—

the sword vibrated harder.

*What is this reaction?*

He had never seen Beelzebub behave this way.

It did not impair the fight.

But it was clearly reacting to something.

That brief hesitation was enough.

“Welcome.”

Every leech-fiber covering Black Tongue’s body rose at once.

Hundreds of fleshy tendrils lunged toward Vikir.

He abandoned the direct approach.

Beelzebub flashed.

POP!

SPLAT!

Leeches burst apart.

Blood and tissue scattered through the air.

Beelzebub kept humming.

Meanwhile—

Black Tongue untied a bag from his waist.

Small black spheres spilled onto the ground.

Eggs.

Larvae wriggled free—

crawling toward Vikir.

*The same type that attached itself to Kirko.*

Ordinary bloodsucking leeches did not interest him.

But parasites that could steal strength—

and copy someone’s appearance—

were different.

Black Tongue noticed where he was looking.

“Hehehe.”

“A rare breed.”

“The result of my entire life’s research.”

“I only recently succeeded in stabilizing and breeding them.”

“Ever heard of a **doppelganger leech**?”

“No.”

“Of course not.”

“Even inside Nouvelle Vague—

they’re top secret.”

The tip of Black Tongue’s purple tongue opened into another mouth.

“I was authorized to use anyone here as a test subject.”

“Prisoner.”

“Guard.”

“Whoever I wanted.”

Vikir’s eyes cooled.

“Authorized by whom?”

“Who else?”

“Orca.”

“That stubborn old man would do anything to strengthen Nouvelle Vague.”

“He’d probably work with demons if it improved security.”

Black Tongue chuckled.

“The funny part is—

the research was finished a long time ago.”

“I just never reported that it was complete.”

“Why stop having fun?”

Vikir thought of Kirko.

Her parents.

The family that had been destroyed because one sadist wanted entertainment.

Kirko’s old words returned.

*At least Father wasn’t evil.*

*Mother wasn’t pitiful.*

*I was just a bad child.*

A child born and raised in the abyss.

A woman with nowhere to belong.

Vikir inhaled.

Then slowly exhaled.

The heat in his chest vanished.

So did Beelzebub’s restless vibration.

The Night Hound made his decision.

“I’ll take those leeches from you.”

“Not yet.”

Beelzebub screamed across the stone.

CRACK!

SCRAPE!

The blade carved floor—

walls—

ceiling.

The crawling larvae exploded one after another.

Black Tongue’s mouth opened.

Even in near-total darkness—

Vikir had killed every tiny parasite without missing one.

“You’re more troublesome than I expected.”

“Then again—

someone who removed BDISSEM restraints was never ordinary.”

Black Tongue still sounded relaxed.

Vikir soon saw why.

Black spheres covered the corridor ahead.

Huge swollen leeches—

large enough to swallow one or two people each.

They clung to the walls.

Ceiling.

Floor.

Blocking his path.

*He prepared this in advance.*

*He knew I would go after BDISSEM.*

“Don’t worry.”

“I didn’t tell anyone else.”

“They would only get in the way.”

Black Tongue gestured proudly.

“My children are much more useful.”

“New variants.”

“**Oil-sucking leeches**.”

“And **body-heat-sucking leeches**.”

“Hard to breed.”

“Not suitable for mass production.”

The swollen creatures opened their mouths.

Then—

BOOM!

One type sprayed stored oil.

The other expelled accumulated heat as flame.

Boiling oil and fire filled the corridor.

Explosions rolled forward.

Moisture vanished instantly.

Stone blackened.

Ash filled the air.

Even Vikir did not want to take that barrage directly.

Black Tongue stared into the smoke.

“What a shame.”

“With a few more—

I could have kept that fire going longer.”

“But this should be enough.”

Then his eyes narrowed.

A red-black inverted pentagram shone through the smoke.

Vikir stood behind Decarabia’s barrier.

“Hm?”

“That’s it?”

“It was finally getting warm.”

Veins bulged across Black Tongue’s forehead.

“You really are irritating.”

He lowered his body.

“All right.”

“You win.”

“I’ll show you my trump card.”

He pulled out a long object wrapped in dirty bandages.

A sword.

Its grip looked less like metal—

and more like a chunk of dried meat.

Vikir stared.

“You use a sword?”

“I’ve never heard that.”

“Of course not.”

“I almost never use this against humans.”

Black Tongue slowly removed the filthy wrapping.

A hideously twisted blade emerged.

“…!”

Beelzebub vibrated violently.

Vikir finally understood.

“…So that sword was here.”

It was resonance.

Recognition—

between two of the same kind.
