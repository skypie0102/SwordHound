“Wait!”

Dolores stepped in front of Vikir.

“Aren't you being too cold to the child?”

“Did you let her kiss you?”

“No.”

“Not ‘no.’”

Dolores frowned.

“You mean you *wouldn't*.”

“She wanted to kiss you.”

“You could have just accepted it.”

“I'm embarrassed.”

Dolores's mouth fell open.

She had never seen anyone admit embarrassment so bluntly.

With a sigh—

she moved past Vikir and crouched in front of Peri.

“Oh dear.”

“That cold-hearted Big Brother doesn't like kisses.”

“Then how about Big Sister?”

“…”

Dolores leaned closer.

“…You don't want to kiss Big Sister either?”

Peri's refusal was absolute.

Dolores looked from the disappointed child—

to Vikir walking away.

*She never opens up to anyone.*

Dolores knew why Peri had accepted him.

The girl had lost her “ball” in the drain.

Vikir had jumped into the sewage without hesitation.

Then climbed back out—

covered in filth—

and returned it.

Who could watch something like that and remain unmoved?

Dolores herself had seen the scene from a short distance away.

She had seen Peri's face.

Seen how devastated the child had been when the ball disappeared.

Then watched Vikir retrieve it.

*…Vikir.*

*I really can't figure him out.*

Dolores watched his back grow smaller.

For the first time—

she wanted to know more about this junior.

Evening fell.

Vikir left the great hall and returned toward the volunteer dormitory.

The men's room held four people.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Vikir.

Staff members stood watch outside the dormitory entrance.

They waved the students inside.

“All right, volunteers.”

“Come in now.”

“You know what happens if you're late.”

“Every building door will be locked.”

“And even we won't be able to open them for you.”

“No one leaves the dormitory after ten!”

Indulgentia's curfew was unusually strict.

At night—

every building door and window was locked.

Movement between buildings was forbidden.

Supervisors patrolled the dormitory corridors.

Officially—

they were there to help the volunteers.

Vikir had already realized the truth.

They were there to monitor them.

*Especially the children's dormitory blocks.*

Even Vikir had not yet entered the children's quarters at night.

Guards patrolled outside.

Inside—

Quilt's four shadows moved constantly.

**Geronto.**

**Hebe.**

**Pedo.**

**Ephebo.**

Vikir had never seen their uncovered faces.

He did not need to.

Their faint stench was enough to reveal their presence.

As long as all four remained in place—

moving carelessly would be impossible.

So Vikir waited.

He waited for the right night.

For their presence to weaken.

For the security pattern to open.

And tonight—

dark clouds swallowed the moon.

The time to hunt had finally come.

Vikir went to the communal bath.

He washed normally.

Then stepped out with a towel wrapped around himself.

Evening roll call would begin soon.

After that—

bedtime.

Vikir dried himself and walked back toward the sleeping area as if nothing were unusual.

Then—

“Hey, Vikir!”

Tudor threw an arm around his shoulders.

Exactly as planned.

Vikir turned.

Tudor grinned.

Then lowered his voice.

“Seriously.”

“Look at those abs.”

“Why are they so defined?”

“Are you training to murder somebody?”

Tudor laughed and prodded Vikir's stomach.

Then his eyes narrowed mischievously.

“With a body like that—”

“you're really just going to sleep tonight?”

“What else would I do?”

Tudor sighed dramatically.

“Vikir.”

“Think about it.”

“After you die, that body turns to dust.”

“We should use our young, healthy bodies more efficiently while we still can.”

He looked Vikir up and down.

“With a build like yours—”

“it's practically a public resource.”

Sancho stared at Tudor with open disappointment.

“So after all that talking—”

“your point is that you want to sneak into the girls' dormitory.”

“And you're supposed to be a future hero?”

Tudor raised one finger.

“There's a saying.”

“A hero shows his true colors.”

He grinned.

“And heroes happen to like beautiful colors.”

Pigi timidly corrected him.

“Isn't the saying that heroes are lustful?”

“Hahaha!”

Tudor waved the objection away.

“Anyway!”

“A knight dreams of meeting a lady willing to risk everything for him!”

“Who can say I'm wrong?”

His eyes practically shone.

“Just imagine it.”

“Cross the dark night.”

“Slip past strict guards.”

“Climb the castle wall.”

“Meet your lady in secret!”

“If there were a dragon trying to stop us, this would be perfect chivalric literature!”

He spread his arms.

“And everyone knows the Academy separates the men's and women's dormitories completely.”

“If we miss this chance—”

“we may never spend a night in the same building as girls for all four years!”

Pigi blinked.

“Um…”

“What do we actually do after meeting them?”

“Yes!”

Tudor pointed at him.

“Good question!”

“What do we do?”

“We drink!”

“We hang out!”

“We play drinking games!”

“We talk honestly!”

Sancho looked genuinely interested.

“Drinking games?”

“Until someone collapses?”

Tudor stared at him.

“That is the most northern answer possible.”

“Why would we do *that* with girls?”

“Not some brutal northern drinking contest.”

“I mean random games.”

“Truth games.”

“Things like that.”

Sancho came from the North.

His experience of drinking at night consisted mostly of loud, sweaty mercenaries trying to outlast one another.

The idea of a different kind of drinking party—

actually caught his interest.

Pigi looked nervous.

He had barely spoken to girls his age before.

“I-I think it sounds fun.”

“But…”

“What if they don't like me?”

“What if they think I'm just some muscle pig blinded by desire?”

Tudor clicked his tongue.

“Sancho.”

“Girls think about this stuff too.”

“I already kissed a girl earlier today.”

He looked smug.

“We agreed that if we go to the right place at the right time—”

“she'll quietly open the back entrance and let us in.”

Pigi hesitated.

“C-Can I come too?”

“I won't be in the way?”

“Of course!”

Tudor slapped him on the back.

“The more people we have, the better!”

“If we get caught, we can spread the punishment around!”

“And Pigi—have some confidence!”

“That round, cute face of yours is popular!”

The plan was coming together.

After convincing Sancho and Pigi—

Tudor finally turned toward Vikir.

He looked nervous.

“Um…”

“Vikir.”

“We are sharing the same four-person room.”

“So I'll try not to cause trouble for you.”

“But…”

“For the sake of one good memory before we leave—”

“maybe you should indulge yourself once too.”

“Let's go.”

“Aww, don't reject it so bluntly—”

Tudor stopped.

“…What?”

Sancho stared.

Pigi stared.

Vikir repeated himself.

“Let's go.”

Then—

with the same deadpan face—

“I like girls.”

“…”

“You do understand we're not going to duel the girls, right?”

“We're socializing.”

“I know.”

Sancho leaned in.

“Vikir.”

“Are you ill?”

“If something is wrong, say so.”

“I have a northern medicinal salve that can heal the mind too—”

Pigi looked even more alarmed.

“V-Vikir.”

“What happened to you?”

“You normally don't show any interest in girls at all.”

They were right.

Vikir had no interest in the meeting itself.

He had another purpose.

*…Tonight is the best chance to kill the demon.*

A moonless night.

Perfect hunting conditions for the Night Hound.

Vikir would leave the dormitory under the cover of a secret meeting between boys and girls.

Then—

he would search for a gap in Indulgentia's security.

If the assassination attempt was discovered too early—

the other boys could serve as cover.

Teenagers sneaking around to meet one another was ordinary.

Adults who caught them might scold them—

but they were also likely to dismiss the behavior as youthful foolishness.

Tudor came from a prestigious noble family.

Sancho and Pigi were legitimate Academy students with reliable identities.

If Vikir was found wandering a corridor—

he could claim he had gotten lost while trying to find the girls' dormitory.

“All right.”

“If Vikir is joining too, every boy in our room is in.”

“There are boys from other rooms joining as well.”

“So we'll have a decent group.”

“After roll call, we wait.”

“When the clock tower rings midnight, we pretend we're going to the bathroom.”

“Follow the ventilation duct in the ceiling.”

“Climb along the water pipes.”

“The lock on the first-floor bathroom window of the girls' dormitory is broken.”

“We get in there.”

“Then we head for the emergency exit.”

“The girls will leave a note under the flowerpot.”

“That note will have tonight's meeting-room number.”

For a secret plan created by teenagers—

it was surprisingly detailed.

And carefully prepared.

Vikir listened with unusual seriousness.

The more boys and girls involved—

the greater the distraction.

Tudor finished with one final timing note.

“The weak point is when the night supervisors change shifts.”

“Meet after roll call.”

“We move when the midnight bell rings.”

The inexperienced young pups practically vibrated with excitement.

Their first real taste of forbidden adventure.

Quiet.

Experienced.

Sharp teeth hidden behind a blank expression.
