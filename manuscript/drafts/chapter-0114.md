# Chapter 114: The Great Banquet (5)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/114.txt` — `1936c0da4f7c14434a37759a362f31895cb14af7`. Recovered English Chapter 113 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls Peak Sword Graduator, Intermediate Sword Graduator, Sixth/Seventh Fang chronology, Styx, Beelzebub, Highbro, Middlebro, Lowbro, Osiris Le Baskerville, and Seth Le Baskerville. No later knowledge about Seth's true identity is imported before the source reveals it.

Boston Terrier lifted the wine that had been boiling moments earlier and emptied the remaining half in one swallow.

Great Dane cut a large piece from his steaming steak and ate it.

“My nephew's wine has a unique flavor!”

“Meat cooked with a Sword Graduator's aura. Excellent.”

Both Counts stared at Vikir with sharpened interest.

If anything, the demonstration had made them want him even more.

Vikir's thoughts went in the opposite direction.

*Showing only Intermediate Sword Graduator was the right choice.*

He had revealed similar control before.

At Club Burning Suspension, when he detonated the champagne tower.

And again when he first confronted Madam Eight-Legs.

The difference was that his real strength had changed enormously since then.

His hidden realm had already returned to **Peak Sword Graduator**.

The power of his previous life.

The blessing of the Styx.

Baskerville's advanced swordsmanship.

The demon sword Beelzebub.

Taken together, Vikir believed he could defeat one of the current Seven Counts in a direct fight.

*More importantly…*

He glanced toward the lower end of the table.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

The triplets ate in silence.

“…”

“…”

“…”

Apparently, none of them had reported what they saw in the Depths two years ago.

At least, Hugo had not learned from them that Vikir had already demonstrated power beyond what he publicly claimed.

Vikir did not know why they had stayed silent.

*It helps me either way.*

He had considered revealing Peak Sword Graduator tonight.

Now there was no need.

The less he showed, the better.

After the banquet, he would make sure the triplets understood that their silence needed to continue.

Vikir picked up his fork again.

Boston and Great Dane were still marveling at him.

“To send mana through the table and make only one piece of meat cook, or only one cup of wine boil…”

Boston laughed.

“That level of control is no ordinary skill. At seventeen, I was only an Advanced Sword Expert.”

Great Dane nodded.

“You need at least Intermediate Sword Graduator control for that. Baskerville has never produced a talent like this.”

He paused.

“I reached that realm at thirty-three.”

Most Baskerville hounds did not reach it before thirty-five.

Vikir had done so at seventeen.

Nearly two decades early.

Boston turned toward Hugo.

“Intermediate Sword Graduator! I was thirty-three before I achieved that, and your son has done it at seventeen!”

Great Dane raised his glass.

“Congratulations, Brother. This is an honor for the entire clan. A toast to our nephew.”

The two men were completely captivated.

Hugo was smiling too.

Not the nearly invisible curve Vikir had been struggling to interpret.

A clear smile.

The corners of his mouth were unmistakably raised.

Soon Hugo and the two Counts were discussing Vikir as though he were not sitting directly in front of them.

More than ninety percent of the conversation consisted of Boston and Great Dane asking that Vikir join their respective knight orders.

Then the adults' attention shifted for a moment.

A new voice reached Vikir.

“Little brother.”

Precise diction.

A cold voice.

Vikir turned.

Long black hair fell like a curtain.

Red eyes watched him from behind it.

**Osiris Le Baskerville.**

The greatest talent of Baskerville's younger generation.

First in the line of succession.

The heir who could act in the Patriarch's place when Hugo was absent.

Osiris sat diagonally across the table.

“…”

Vikir met his gaze.

Before regression, Vikir had barely dared look directly at him.

Osiris had seemed like a small absolute.

Arrogant.

Untouchable.

Far beyond Vikir in both status and power.

Now Vikir possessed enough strength to meet those eyes without lowering his own.

After a moment, Osiris spoke.

“You did well.”

Vikir's pupils shifted.

Osiris had spoken first.

That alone was unusual.

But praise?

Before regression, Vikir would have thought it impossible.

Osiris continued.

“Very well done.”

His expression remained unchanged.

“Keep improving.”

The voice was cold enough that anyone unfamiliar with him might think he was angry.

The younger Vikir had once believed exactly that.

Now, however—

he noticed something else beneath it.

A faint warmth.

Clumsy.

Difficult to express.

Perhaps so unfamiliar that Osiris himself did not know how to put it into his face, voice, or gestures.

*…He isn't as frightening as I remember.*

Vikir gave him a slight nod.

Why had Osiris seemed so terrifying before regression?

Because he was the acting young patriarch?

Because of the proud expression?

The difference in power?

The difference in status?

Or simply because Osiris had been more than ten years older than him?

Now Vikir's true mental age far exceeded Osiris's.

Once that old fear disappeared, awkward little details became visible.

Osiris was not especially graceful with knife and fork.

He quietly pushed aside foods he disliked.

He had almost no tolerance for alcohol and frowned at the smell.

Whenever his napkin wrinkled, he obsessively smoothed it flat again.

*So he has ordinary sides too.*

Once Vikir stopped viewing him as some unreachable creature above the clouds, Osiris Le Baskerville looked much more human.

He was not the bloodless perfect genius the world described.

Nor the one Vikir remembered.

Then another voice came from beside him.

“Well, this is rare.”

Vikir turned.

A handsome man with unnaturally pale skin smiled at him.

Black hair.

Red eyes.

Deep shadows beneath them.

**Seth Le Baskerville.**

Hugo's second son.

“I think that's the first time I've ever seen Brother Osiris praise someone.”

Seth leaned closer.

“He even looks delighted.”

*That's delighted?*

Vikir glanced back.

Osiris was expressionlessly cutting his steak.

He still looked angry.

No wonder everyone had feared him.

“…So that's a smile.”

Seth laughed softly at Vikir's silence.

“You have the same poker face as him. Then again, everyone in this family is cold and quiet.”

His own expression remained warm.

“It's unfortunate.”

Seth was almost Osiris's opposite.

Open.

Gentle.

Expressive.

And in Vikir's memory—

kind.

Seth had never possessed remarkable sword talent.

He was not cruel.

He cared about people the family considered too insignificant to notice.

He would ask even a newly hired maid whether anything inconvenienced her.

Among Hugo's direct descendants, Seth alone had left flowers for dead hounds.

Vikir still remembered him mourning those who died for the clan.

He remembered Seth crying alone in the garden after Hugo or Osiris dismissed him as weak.

Before regression, Vikir had pitied him.

Respected him.

Eventually served him despite expecting no reward.

Seth's kindness toward expendable hounds like Vikir had meant that much.

…But this life was different.

Vikir remembered the report Cindywendy had delivered the night before.

**Seth Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Magistrate of Underdog City; Lower Council member  
**Title:** Viscount  
**Sword Realm:** Intermediate Sword Graduator  
**Other:** Second son of Hugo Le Baskerville; second heir. Gentle and considerate. Sickly. Limited sword talent. Treated coldly by Hugo. Popular among servants. Underdog magistracy currently vacant while he remains in closed training; temporarily emerged for the Great Banquet…

Most of that was information Vikir already knew.

One final line was not.

A single conclusion from Cindywendy's investigation.

**Seth Le Baskerville is suspected of not being human.**
