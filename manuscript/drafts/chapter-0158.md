# Chapter 158: Familiar Face (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/158.txt` — `d0677ff2c2c232ad017287dd0223f04b90a00d3f`. Recovered English Chapter 157 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls Vikir, Dolores L. Quovadis, Ephebo, Pedo, Hebe, Geronto, Quilt Rune Indulgentia, Beelzebub, Quovadis, and the **Seventh Fang**. This chapter source-reveals Quilt's demon status at the level stated here; later personal demon name/title remains excluded.

[Hhh—gurgle—]

The thing hanging from the ceiling—

breathing with a nauseating wet rasp—

was unmistakably **Ephebo**.

One of Quilt's four masked servants.

Ephebo.

Pedo.

Hebe.

Geronto.

Vikir scanned his body.

*Already restored?*

*That regeneration really is demonic.*

Broken bones—

torn flesh—

ruptured organs—

all of it had returned to normal.

But Vikir had spent far too much of his life fighting monsters to panic over regeneration.

He settled into combat stance.

Calculated distance.

Timing.

Next move.

Dolores—

on the other hand—

froze.

“Lord Ephebo…?”

Her expression went blank.

“Why are you here…?”

The guardian knight assigned to House Indulgentia—

a man recognized within Quovadis—

was hanging from a ceiling in the middle of the night like some malformed beast.

Vikir spoke without looking away from the enemy.

“If you stand there dazed—”

“you'll die.”

Boom!

Ephebo dropped from the ceiling.

A massive hammer came down with him.

Vikir caught Dolores around the waist—

then leapt backward.

“That strength is still ridiculous.”

Beelzebub trembled in his hand.

Quovadis blunt-weapon techniques were built for crushing frontal exchanges.

Meeting that hammer head-on would waste too much strength.

Vikir crossed the corridor in a single burst.

The looping demonic labyrinth made ordinary terrain meaningless.

If the hallway repeated forever—

there was no reason to let its geometry dictate the fight.

He set Dolores down.

“This is as far as I can cover you.”

“Beyond this—”

“I can't spare attention for you.”

Dolores opened her mouth.

She never got the chance to answer.

Thud.

Ephebo had reached the floor.

[Grrrgh—krrrk—]

His body twisted.

The shape was even worse than before.

Arms thick as logs—

longer than his legs—

dragged across the floor.

His legs contained too many joints.

Each bent in an impossible direction.

Not human.

Not anymore.

Dolores's gaze hardened.

Ephebo belonged to the Old Testament side.

But he was still Quovadis.

Still someone she had regarded as an ally.

Now—

nothing about him could be mistaken for human.

“…Why is there a monster here?”

“That isn't some unknown monster.”

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

“It's Lord Ephebo.”

Dolores stared.

“Then…”

“Why did Lord Ephebo become like that?”

“Was he always a monster?”

She remembered what people knew of him.

A Quovadis paladin.

Knighted by the family.

Assigned as a guardian knight to House Indulgentia.

Always masked.

Never publicly seen bare-faced.

But known as a man who silently accepted unpleasant or difficult duties.

*What is happening inside my family?*

There was no time to ask.

Clang!

The Night Hound's blade met Ephebo's hammer.

Boom—boom—boom!

Shockwaves ripped through the corridor.

Ceiling.

Walls.

Floor.

Steel reinforcement inside the masonry bent like soft candy.

Water pipes burst—

flooding the hallway.

Dolores stared at the pressure rolling off Ephebo.

*Graduator.*

And not merely someone who had entered the realm.

He stood high within it.

*Lord Ephebo was this strong?*

Then she sensed something even more shocking.

The Night Hound.

Vikir's aura—

deep crimson.

Grrrrr—

Beelzebub extended.

The aura pouring from its point had become so dense—

so liquid—

that it was nearly solid.

Like swinging an enormous sword forged from blood.

Crack!

Crash!

Ephebo's black aura tore apart under Vikir's red.

Steel against stone.

The **Seventh Fang**.

Seven killing teeth.

They descended together.

Rip.

Pierce.

Tear.

Crush.

The technique existed for one purpose—

to kill.

Not to display refinement.

Not to win points.

Not to prove lineage.

Its sole purpose was to make the enemy die painfully.

A legendary practical killing technique from the Baskerville Ten Fangs—

so obscure that even senior Baskervilles would struggle to recognize Vikir's current swordsmanship as their own.

Dolores watched him—

more confused with every exchange.

“You…”

“You've become much stronger since the last time I saw you.”

“I've been steadily raiding Quovadis-affiliated branches.”

Dolores's eyes widened.

“What!?”

“How can you say that so casually!?”

“No—more importantly—”

“why!?”

Vikir jerked his chin toward Ephebo.

“Why?”

“You still need to ask after seeing that?”

[Grrgh…]

Ephebo remained standing.

His body had been torn apart by the seven teeth.

A normal fighter—

even a powerful Graduator—

would already be dead.

Pssht.

Pssht.

Black blood—

thick as tar—

sprayed from the wounds.

Dolores stared at it.

“…Why is something like that inside our family?”

“Why are you asking me about your own house?”

“You know!”

Dolores snapped back.

“That's why you've been attacking the branches all this time!”

Vikir did not answer.

Ephebo moved first.

Hiss—

black aura spread from his body.

Then hardened into chains.

They wrapped around Vikir.

“…Annoying.”

Vikir immediately understood the tactic.

Ephebo was buying time.

Splash!

Vikir planted his foot on the flooded floor.

Stretched Beelzebub's aura as far as he could.

One decisive blow.

Then—

whoom.

Ephebo spun the enormous hammer.

Released it.

The weapon flew like a siege projectile.

Vikir—

bound by the black chains—

could not evade cleanly.

But before the hammer arrived—

Dolores's voice rang through the corridor.

“Light of my life.”

“Flame of my life.”

“My sin.”

“My soul.”

“Break these filthy bonds!”

White light exploded.

Blessing.

Healing.

Purification.

The demonic restraint vanished from Vikir's body.

His limbs became light again.

The Night Hound moved.

Clang!

He stepped directly onto the flying hammer.

Used its momentum.

Leapt.

Then swung Beelzebub sideways.

A red crescent crossed the corridor.

Boom!

Ephebo's head separated from his body.

The Seventh Fang had entered from above—

one tooth driving into the crown—

the remaining six tearing through muscle, tendon, and bone.

Crash!

The corpse collapsed into the flooding corridor.

Its hammer embedded itself in the wall.

Water continued spraying from broken pipes.

For a moment—

everything else seemed still.

Vikir lifted Ephebo's severed head on Beelzebub's point.

Then threw it down.

“If the neck is completely severed—”

“even a demon shouldn't regenerate easily.”

He kicked the head away.

Then pinned it beneath his boot.

He had learned not to underestimate regeneration.

He had already met monsters capable of escaping with little more than a head remaining.

Dolores spoke in a trembling voice.

“I don't understand.”

“What is happening?”

“I thought the problem inside Quovadis was only the conflict between the Old Testament and New Testament factions.”

“But what's really behind it?”

“Do you know?”

Vikir shook his head.

“I don't know everything.”

Then—

finally—

he gave her the part he did know.

“The head of House Indulgentia is a demon.”

Dolores stopped breathing.

Vikir continued.

“And not a weak one.”

“Demon King-class.”

Her eyes widened so far they hurt.

“Y-You're serious?”

“You're certain?”

Vikir pointed toward Ephebo's body.

“Do you still need more proof?”

Even now—

black blood continued pumping from the corpse.

The severed body twitched.

Trying to regenerate.

Dolores looked down.

Then raised one hand.

White divine light spread across Ephebo.

The holy power destroyed what remained of the undead regenerative structure.

At last—

the body went completely limp.

Vikir watched.

*…She's more useful than I expected.*

Demonic regeneration had repeatedly complicated his hunts.

Dolores's holy power—

could solve that problem.

Then Dolores asked another question.

More quietly this time.

“If demons are hiding behind the Old Testament faction…”

“then what about the New Testament faction?”

“Are we safe?”

Vikir answered with a question.

“Among the branches I've attacked—”

“were any New Testament?”

Dolores hesitated.

“…No.”

“As far as I know—”

“not one.”

“Then you have your answer.”

It was not proof that every New Testament figure was clean.

But it was enough to explain the pattern Vikir had been hunting.

Dolores watched his back in silence.

Vikir—

meanwhile—

looked down at Ephebo's severed head.

The black leather mask.

Up close—

it resembled the inner hood of a gas mask.

It also carried faint demonic power.

An artifact.

Possibly something in the same broad category as Beelzebub—

an OOPArt.

And beneath it—

a face.

Vikir was curious.

So was Dolores.

She had personally watched Ephebo use Quovadis blunt-weapon techniques.

“Who was he?”

Dolores whispered.

“Was he really someone from Quovadis?”

Vikir reached for the mask.

“We'll know once we take it off.”

The black leather slowly peeled away.

Then—

both Vikir and Dolores froze.

“…!?”

The face beneath the mask—

was one both of them recognized.
