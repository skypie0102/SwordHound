Who said the first life came from the sea?

In a world where divine creation was the dominant belief—

few agreed with that proposition.

Far more people—

however—

might agree that the last life would disappear into it.

BOOOOM!

The final battle against the demon—

began as a naval war.

Black sea.

Violent storm.

Columns of fire exploding from the water.

Deep-sea monsters baring their teeth.

House Donquixote’s Invincible Armada—

and the Tochka Alliance—

fought through the First Corpse’s forces in the very center of the Empire.

The Imperial Capital.

Once—

the center of culture and fashion.

The richest.

The most refined.

Now—

a battlefield of tsunamis, storms, rain, artillery, and monsters.

And at the center of that chaos—

was something greater than all the rest.

A monster that appeared constantly in sailors’ epics.

Something no ordinary sailor had seen—

yet every sailor feared.

A creature whose tentacles—

according to legend—

could drag entire islands into the deep.

**<Kraken>**

**Danger Rating:** S+  
**Size:** ?  
**Discovery Location:** Deepest Region of the Lower Sea  
**Aliases:** ‘Great King of the Deep Sea’ / ‘Antagonist of Nouvelle Vague’

One of two great monsters said to have been born when light and darkness were divided at the creation of the world.

Among the countless creatures of sailors’ epics—

it stood at the absolute peak.

It normally remained curled in ocean depths so cold and dark that almost nothing else could live.

But when it moved—

the shape of the six seas changed with it.

No one knew why a creature that seemed more appropriate to Hell’s deepest depths—

lived in the human world.

Its huge round head—

covered in countless oysters, barnacles, coral, and seaweed—

looked from a distance like a rocky island.

Eight tentacles extended like branches of a mountain range.

Two massive eyes burned yellow—

feeding on the darkness below.

The king of the abyssal sea.

And the ancient enemy of Nouvelle Vague.

CRACK.

Orca ground his teeth.

BOOM!

His club—

swung with everything he had—

met a tentacle descending toward the fleet.

Even Orca could not fully stop it.

Usher artillery hammered the Kraken.

It barely mattered.

Centuries of accumulated shells—

barnacles—

coral—

layer upon layer upon layer—

formed armor around its body.

BOOM!

One section of the Usher fire net collapsed.

Sea monsters flooded through.

Some tore into ship hulls.

Others climbed rails.

Several vessels of the Invincible Armada began taking water.

Then—

D’Ordume stepped forward.

“Leave it to us.”

“That bastard used to attack Nouvelle Vague.”

“He owes us.”

Souaré smiled grimly.

“We fought it more than once.”

“See the scar near the eye?”

“That one was mine.”

BDISSEM and Flubber joined them.

“Never thought we’d meet it here.”

The old Nouvelle Vague battle resumed.

D’Ordume’s attacks became a cutting vortex.

Shells.

Barnacles.

Coral.

The accumulated armor began grinding away.

Souaré drew on dwarven power.

From the drowned city beneath them—

she pulled entire sections of high-rise ruins upward.

Stone and earth twisted into giant spears.

THOOM!

They pierced into the Kraken’s body.

A deep roar vibrated through the water.

BDISSEM followed.

Countless seaweed chains wrapped around the enormous body.

Flubber expanded—

coated part of the Kraken—

and began dissolving it.

OOOOOOOO—

The sound came from beneath the sea like an enormous ship horn.

Pain.

The water boiled.

The seafloor shook.

SLASH!

Vikir cut apart a tentacle descending toward his ship.

Blue blood sprayed across the waves—

cold and foul-smelling.

Then—

“My friend!”

“Now!”

Tudor shouted from behind him.

Vikir turned.

Tudor himself was steering a smaller attack ship.

Its prow carried an enormous spike.

The vessel accelerated directly into the Kraken.

BOOOOM!

The ram struck.

Tudor’s squadron severed one of the Kraken’s tentacles.

A feat—

not even Orca or Sade had previously accomplished.

Tudor shouted across the water.

“We handle this gatekeeper!”

“You keep going!”

Vikir looked at him.

When they first met at the Academy—

Tudor had been little more than a promising chick.

In only a few years—

he had grown into a hero.

Commanding ships.

Fighting the Kraken.

Vikir no longer needed to worry about whether Tudor could stand on his own.

Given time—

he could become the hero Vikir remembered from before regression.

Perhaps surpass him.

Especially with Sancho and Bianca beside him.

“Vikir!”

“Go!”

“We don’t have much time!”

Then Tudor roared:

“My friend!”

“Go create a myth!”

The Invincible Armada crashed into the Kraken again.

Thunder.

Artillery.

Tsunami.

Earthquake.

Morgue magic.

Quovadis barriers.

Bourgeois supplies.

Baskerville hounds tearing at the giant body.

Vikir moved.

He boarded a small boat.

While the Kraken fought the Alliance—

he would slip past—

and reach the palace.

Then—

SPLASH!

The Kraken rolled.

One enormous eye turned toward Vikir.

A third eye.

Hidden at the rear of its body.

The creature withdrew all eight tentacles from the main battle.

Turned them toward Vikir.

Even the severed and burned limbs lashed in his direction.

“…!”

Vikir drew Beelzebub.

Then—

BOOOOOOM!

The sea behind him exploded.

Eight enormous legs rose from the water.

Eight against eight.

The incoming tentacles stopped.

[ROAAAAAAR!]

**Baby Madam.**

She had followed Vikir alone.

Leaving her children behind—

the enormous adult spider emerged from the flood.

Once—

she and Vikir had glided together past the Imperial Capital’s clock tower.

Now—

she stood in the sea.

Eight massive legs pinning the Kraken’s tentacles.

The Kraken recoiled at the appearance of another creature nearly its own scale.

Poison was already spreading through its body.

While it struggled with Baby Madam—

the Allied bombardment struck again.

OOOOOOOO!

The Kraken turned back.

Vikir—

finally—

fell out of its attention.

“…Thank you.”

The words left Vikir naturally.

A direct acknowledgment that he had been helped.

If his friends had heard him—

they might have celebrated.

None of them did.

His small boat caught the southeast wind.

The gust was nearly strong enough to snap its mast.

The boat flew over the water like an arrow.

Then—

CRASH!

A high wave threw it into a massive staircase.

The hull shattered.

Vikir jumped before the impact—

landed on the golden steps.

They rose toward the sky.

Half submerged—

yet still enormous.

Above them—

the Imperial Palace.

Usually hidden in cloud.

Now exposed above the flood.

Step.

Vikir began climbing.

SCRAAAAAPE.

Something heavy dragged behind him.

A chain.

And at the end—

a coffin.

Large.

Massive.

Vikir pulled it up the golden stairway.

Far behind him—

Kirko watched.

Even with battle raging—

she tilted her head.

“What is that coffin?”

“Who’s inside?”

Sinclaire answered.

“Come to think of it—

Bro’s been carrying it since we left Tochka.”

Dolores frowned.

“I asked him last night.”

Aiyen nodded.

“I did too.”

“He said it contains the souls of all his dead comrades.”

Then Camus narrowed her eyes.

“…I have a bad feeling.”

Everyone looked at her.

Aiyen.

Dolores.

Sinclaire.

Kirko.

Camus crossed her arms.

“…Woman’s intuition.”

The coffin’s contents—

remained unknown.
