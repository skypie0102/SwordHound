The body of a Saintess was called the **Eucharist**.

Traditionally—

that meant white bread and white wine.

So every weekend—

priests of Rune ate white bread and drank white wine.

The custom came from an old myth.

The prophet Rune—

it was said—

made white bread from his own flesh and white wine from his own blood—

then shared them with his followers.

Religions that imitated Rune—

or twisted his teachings—

often copied the custom.

The **New World Church**.

The **Ohm Cult**.

The **Manson Cult**.

And others rising rapidly across the Empire.

Most copied Rune’s ancient doctrine almost word for word—

then filled doctrinal gaps with their own interpretations—

until entirely new religions emerged.

Now—

the leaders of several such cults had gathered in one place.

Near the outskirts of the Imperial Capital.

At an almost deserted New Testament temple.

Their priests talked among themselves.

“It’s been a while.”

“You look healthier.”

“Well, a merchant guild donated its entire fortune recently.”

“My life has become much easier.”

“Impressive.”

“How did you get them to give you everything?”

“The guild master’s daughter had been sick for years.”

“I promised to cure her.”

“Then I slowly persuaded him to donate everything.”

“Her illness only needed a few anesthetic painkillers anyway.”

“Hahaha!”

“I’m the one with problems.”

“I’ve been losing weight lately.”

“Didn’t you take another young concubine?”

“Hahaha.”

“Yes.”

“She already had a husband.”

“But I used doctrine to persuade her little by little.”

“Eventually she became completely devoted to me.”

“Well done.”

“A true believer should offer us everything.”

“Property.”

“Body.”

“Mind.”

“Otherwise salvation is impossible.”

Their doctrines differed.

Their theology differed.

Their excuses differed.

But the priests of the heresies and cults shared one thing.

They fed themselves by hollowing out people who were already tired, poor, sick, or desperate.

Then—

the crowd parted.

Three figures approached from different directions.

The leaders of the New World Church, Ohm Cult, and Manson Cult.

**Elmanny.**

Old—

but greed still shone in his eyes.

**Ohm.**

So fat he was almost spherical.

**Charles.**

Thin.

Hollow-eyed.

Elmanny spoke first.

“…The New Testament Faction invited us to dinner.”

“Doesn’t that mean they finally recognize our New World Church as a legitimate branch?”

Ohm nodded.

“Exactly.”

“The Eucharist means sharing the Saintess’s flesh and blood.”

“Inviting us here means they want an official alliance.”

Charles agreed.

“They gathered us secretly to counter the Old Testament.”

“They know we already support the Old Testament.”

“Now they want their share too.”

Every cult leader present called himself some variation of pope, prophet, saint, or god.

Each had received a secret dinner invitation—

supposedly from the New Testament Faction.

Each came with only a small number of trusted senior followers.

They had already built relationships with the Old Testament.

Now—

they intended to approach the New Testament too.

“The Old and New Testament factions are fighting.”

“If we support both—”

“we survive no matter which side wins.”

“Exactly.”

“A clever rabbit digs more than one burrow.”

“Maybe the best result is if the two sides keep fighting forever.”

“Then our meeting here must remain secret.”

“The Old Testament cannot find out.”

The cult leaders hurried toward the temple under cover of darkness.

Or—

tried to.

BOOM!

An explosion ripped through the grounds.

Buildings collapsed.

Trees shattered.

Debris smashed into carriages and people.

Screams rose everywhere.

“W-what?!”

The cultists formed defensive circles.

Dust filled the air.

Then came footsteps.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Black, skin-tight clothing.

Heels more than thirty centimeters high.

**Ms. Ouroboros.**

She had appeared without warning.

“Hehehe.”

“What is this?”

“So much garbage in one place.”

She looked around.

“I heard Night Hound was here.”

“I came to see.”

“But all I found was a pile of stinking trash.”

The priests screamed.

“You arrogant criminal!”

“What are you waiting for?!”

“Kill her!”

Soldiers charged with spears and shields.

Ms. Ouroboros moved.

Whip.

Crack.

A barbed lash swept through them like a serpent.

Arms.

Legs.

Bodies.

Armor split under purple-black aura.

Every strike against the stone ground sent a tremor through the area.

Heavily armored paladins were smashed aside.

The cult leaders went pale.

“This is impossible!”

“What kind of monster is she?!”

“Where are the Imperial Guards?!”

“Run!”

Elmanny.

Ohm.

Charles.

All three tried.

None made it far.

Ms. Ouroboros appeared in front of them.

Smiling.

“Who are you?”

“Popes?”

The three tried to recover their dignity.

“I am the pope of the New World Church!”

“Do you not fear divine punishment?”

“I lead the Ohm Cult.”

“If I die, hundreds of thousands will mourn.”

“I am the god of the Manson faith.”

“Whoever harms me will be cursed.”

Crack!

Ms. Ouroboros snapped the whip.

Several uprooted trees shifted under her aura—

their trunks and branches forced upright.

The three men stared.

“…?”

Ms. Ouroboros smiled sweetly.

“If you’re popes and gods—”

“prove it.”

“I’ll let you go.”

Her whip lashed around Charles.

“Huh?”

He was yanked through the air and slammed against a tree.

Then—

thud.

Thud.

Thud.

Daggers pierced both shoulders and thighs—

pinning him in place.

“AAAAAGH!”

Charles screamed.

Ms. Ouroboros smiled.

“Now.”

“Let me give you a laurel crown.”

The barbed whip wrapped around his head.

Blood sprayed.

Charles struggled violently.

Then weakened.

Then stopped.

Shock from blood loss killed him.

Elmanny and Ohm curled in terror.

One of them wet himself.

Ms. Ouroboros hummed.

“Contestant number one.”

“Resurrection challenge—failed.”

“Next.”

She walked to Ohm.

Then raised one heel.

Crack.

She crushed his toes.

“AAAAH!”

Then the rest.

Foot.

Ankle.

Shin.

Knee.

Thigh.

By the time she stopped—

his lower body was mangled beyond use.

Ms. Ouroboros smiled.

“Next challenge.”

“Stand up.”

“Use your own two legs.”

“If you can do it, I’ll let you go.”

Ohm could not.

“P-please!”

“I’m not a god!”

“I’m not even a real priest!”

“I’m a heretic!”

“A fraud!”

For perhaps the first time in his life—

he spoke honestly.

His followers stared.

“T-the Pope…?”

“Our savior…”

“He has no divine power!”

“It was all a lie!”

All around them—

faith began breaking.

Elmanny alone tried to hold his posture.

“I’m not afraid of you!”

“I will not submit!”

“I am a god incarnated in this world!”

“Oh?”

Ms. Ouroboros covered her mouth and laughed.

“If you’re truly a god—”

“holy water shouldn’t hurt you, right?”

“Of course it—”

He did not finish.

She pulled out a bottle.

Splashed the liquid across his face.

“This is holy water.”

Sssssss.

Skin melted.

Blisters boiled.

Burned flesh filled the air with a terrible smell.

“AAAAAAAGH!”

Elmanny clawed at his face.

The liquid burned deeper.

Through flesh.

Into bone.

Toward the organs beneath.

Ms. Ouroboros blinked.

“Oh dear.”

“I seem to have mistaken hydrochloric acid for holy water.”

She tapped one fist lightly against her head.

The remaining leaders went white.

“I’m not Rune reborn!”

“Please spare me!”

“I only liked being worshiped!”

“I founded my church so I could sleep with married men and women!”

“I just wanted a business!”

“I wanted money!”

Confessions erupted everywhere.

So did the collapse of belief.

“…This is drifting away from the plan.”

Vikir—

in Night Hound form—

watched from the roof of the New Testament temple.

Dolores stood beside him.

Gather the leaders of the dangerous cults and heresies in one place.

Then eliminate them all at once.

the core idea had come from Sinclaire.

During the University League—

she had been given the Quovadis assignment.

**Faithful Holy House / Difficulty**

**The Empire is suffering from the rapid spread of cults and heresies.**

**From the standpoint of the Quovadis Inquisition, propose a way to eliminate them and raise Rune’s standing.**

Later—

she explained it to Dolores.

Vikir already knew the same strategy from his pre-regression memories.

“…Cults are like weeds.”

“If you don’t remove the roots, they return.”

“But you can’t chase every seed scattered across the Empire.”

“So the efficient method is to gather them in one place—”

“and burn the entire patch.”

Only predatory organizations.

Those that exploited the poor and exhausted.

Stole property.

Abused children.

Coveted other people’s spouses.

Fed on weakness.

Vikir had sent Baby Madam to Martin Luther’s office.

She copied the texture and shape of Luther’s handwriting from existing documents using hardened silk—

then reproduced invitations in the same hand.

Dolores shivered.

“…Isn’t this too radical?”

This was—

in fact—

the same kind of plan the future Dolores would execute after becoming the Ironblood Saintess.

So he answered flatly.

“Look at what the dying ones are vomiting.”

Black blood.

Every senior leader and fanatic at the gathering—

knowingly or not—

*As expected.*

*They’re tied to the Sixth Corpse.*

Fifty-fifty.

Ms. Ouroboros wanted to meet Night Hound.

Two—

Ms. Ouroboros was hostile to demonic collaborators.

Why—

still unknown.

Wherever Night Hound appeared—

she tended to follow.

Vikir had therefore considered imitating **her** this time.

A disguise.

A whip.

It would not have been especially difficult.

But Ms. Ouroboros had appeared in person.

That saved him effort.

Then—

shouts came from beyond the grounds.

“There!”

“We heard screams!”

“Advance!”

“Catch her this time!”

Imperial Guards flooded toward the scene.

Ms. Ouroboros glanced toward them.

“Hehehe.”

“I feel used.”

“I heard Night Hound was here.”

“What a wasted trip.”

as though speaking to someone unseen.

“Mr. Night Hound…”

“Did you use me?”

“Hehehe.”

“How sly.”

“How charming.”

“I like you even more.”

She leapt.

Then vanished into darkness and dust.

Vikir moved immediately.

Grabbed one cult leader who was still barely alive.

Ohm.

His lower body had been destroyed by Ms. Ouroboros.

He could barely think.

Red light flashed from the single eye in the inverted pentagram.

“Ledger…”

“Under the destroyed carriage…”

“Three-layer hidden bottom…”

“Funding source…”

“Top-secret documents…”

“Find the ledger!”

“Here!”

“I found it!”

“Under the bottom!”

*Now I have more than the tail.*

*I have the body.*

The true force behind it all.

The source feeding the Old Testament’s money.
