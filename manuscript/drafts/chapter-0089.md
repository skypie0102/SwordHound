The sword forests of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain were lethal.

Leaves cut like razors.

Bark tore flesh.

Roots hid spikes and blades of their own.

In the middle of that jungle, two men faced each other.

Vikir.

Ah'Heman.

Knife at the waist.

Bow across the back.

Ballak warriors formed a wide circle around them.

Dense leaves blocked ordinary sight.

Ballak eyes and ears were not ordinary.

They could follow the duel from hundreds of meters away.

Ah'Heman grinned.

“You've lived here two years.”

He had lived in the jungle for almost seventy.

He knew every type of ground.

Every plant.

Every useful poison.

Every hidden hazard.

“You'll regret choosing the forest.”

Then he vanished into the vegetation.

◆◆◆

Ah'Heman prepared the jungle.

A large red-bodied, black-legged spider became arrow poison.

Transparent cutting vines became tripwires.

A soft mud pit disappeared beneath sand and leaves.

Bee nests waited to be disturbed.

He crouched behind roots with the poisoned arrow ready.

“Come.”

A voice spoke behind him.

“Who are you waiting for?”

Ah'Heman almost jumped out of his skin.

Vikir stood there.

Expressionless.

Silent.

“How—?”

Vikir cut the prepared vines.

Ah'Heman stumbled backward.

Into his own mud pit.

Then struck the bee nests.

BUZZZZZZ.

The swarm descended.

Vikir backed away.

Ah'Heman flailed.

Eventually he escaped by submerging himself in mud.

By then, his body was swollen with stings.

He crawled free.

Vikir was sitting in a tree.

Yawning.

“You bastard!”

Ah'Heman fired.

The mud had washed the spider venom from the arrowhead.

The arrow itself was still dangerous.

Vikir cut it in half with a red aura slash.

Then disappeared.

Ah'Heman's eyes widened.

*No sound.*

Vikir moved at terrifying speed.

Grass did not rustle.

Mud did not splash.

Branches did not crack.

Stones did not shift.

Only the jungle's natural insects could be heard.

**Silent Heel.**

Ka'ah's ability.

Ah'Heman fired again and again.

He hit only innocent orangutans.

Then Beelzebub appeared.

Dense dark-red aura coated the blade.

High Sword Graduator.

The sight alone frightened Ah'Heman.

*He's this strong?*

He retreated.

Six Fangs followed.

The Baskerville attack ripped across his body.

Then came the burning.

**Incinerate — Cerberus (A+).**

Hellfire clung to wounds only Vikir could properly perceive.

Ah'Heman screamed.

He had been cut.

Shot.

Stabbed.

Beaten.

For almost seventy years.

Nothing had hurt like this.

The watching Ballak did not understand why he screamed so loudly from apparently modest cuts.

They jeered.

Ah'Heman abandoned his dignity.

He ran.

Then tried shooting again.

Vikir answered with a bow.

Two years under Aiyen had made him a skilled archer.

An arrow curved through the jungle.

THUNK.

He collapsed.

Vikir approached.

“You were eager to prescribe Oxbear genitals to other people.”

“Now look at you.”

“You're playing with me!”

“No.”

Vikir looked down at him.

“You aren't worth playing with.”

Ah'Heman staggered upright.

Then threw aside bow and knife.

The air changed.

Dark mana gathered around his hands.

Vikir's eyes narrowed.

*There it is.*

The hidden card.

Black currents spread.

Hands reached from beneath the undergrowth.

Dead orangutans rose.

Rotten flesh.

Exposed bone.

Corpses made to move.

*Rokoko.*

Ah'Heman's lost birth tribe.

Imperials would call it black magic.

“The Illiad has no rules!”

“Stop him!”

Some nearly one hundred kilograms.

Enough, Ah'Heman believed, to delay Vikir while he treated his own wounds.
