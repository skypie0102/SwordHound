**Bianca Usher.**

Once—

the unquestioned heir to House Usher.

She was the only daughter of the current head—

the Bow Demon **Roderick**.

Under ordinary circumstances—

the succession would have been simple.

But reality had never been interested in ordinary circumstances.

Roderick—

a Master feared across the Empire—

suddenly collapsed from a mysterious illness.

Bianca was still young.

Still a student at Colosseo Academy.

And while she was away—

House Usher changed.

Roderick’s only younger sister—

**Madeline**—

took control.

A woman known since childhood for frail health.

Someone who had rarely involved herself in family affairs.

Then—

the moment Roderick was bedridden—

she began managing everything.

Every major order.

Every minor appointment.

Every military movement.

Every political decision.

Almost as if she had been waiting.

Bianca returned home as soon as she learned about her father.

She was treated like an outsider.

She could not even see Roderick.

So Bianca turned toward relatives she believed would support her.

Her maternal family.

Collateral branches.

Old allies.

All useless.

Madeline had begun buying them long before Bianca realized there was a struggle.

One by one—

people Bianca trusted betrayed her.

Only then did she understand.

House Usher was no longer her refuge.

It was her greatest enemy.

So Bianca left.

She went searching for the one person she believed might understand.

Her childhood rival.

Her closest friend.

Tudor.

But House Donquixote was collapsing in exactly the same way.

Spear King Cervantes—

like Roderick—

had fallen to an unexplained illness.

Passamonte took power.

And Tudor—

unlike Bianca—

was already openly fighting his own family.

So Bianca did the only thing left.

She took Tudor and ran.

Behind them came two forces that ordinary fugitives could never hope to escape.

Donquixote’s **Invincible Cavalry**.

Usher’s elite snipers.

Their flight was long.

Brutal.

Almost impossible.

But somehow—

they survived.

And in the process—

the boy Bianca had always treated like an annoying childhood companion changed.

Tudor became someone dependable.

Someone who could protect her back.

Someone she could—

sometimes—

lean on.

Perhaps that was why she was so angry now.

◆◆◆

“Kukuku.”

“You really are vicious.”

**Pedro Donquixote** stood before her.

Passamonte’s adjutant.

One of the men who had escorted Vikir to Nouvelle Vague years earlier.

The last time Bianca knew of him—

Pedro should have been crippled.

Imprisoned.

Buried in a dungeon.

Instead—

he stood perfectly healthy at the head of Donquixote cavalry.

Bianca drew her bow.

“So you really are demons.”

“Again with that?”

Pedro shrugged.

“I hear Isolde Usher is desperate to get you back.”

“She’ll probably pay well.”

“I came for Tudor—

and found a bonus.”

Bianca’s forehead tightened.

“You called Tudor a coward?”

“That word fits someone who crawled between a demon’s legs much better.”

“And perhaps the trash who raised you too.”

Honor mattered deeply inside House Donquixote.

Pedro’s eyes rolled.

“How dare you insult me!”

His hand moved instinctively toward his glove.

Bianca had been waiting for that.

TWANG!

Her arrow carried dense liquid aura.

A Graduator’s shot.

Fast.

Heavy.

Precise.

The arrow punched through Pedro’s hand—

and continued toward his heart.

He laughed.

“Creating an opening when none exists.”

“Usher archery really is unpleasant.”

Darkness clouded his eyes.

The arrow was thrown aside.

New flesh immediately grew across the wound.

Bianca’s suspicion became certainty.

Pedro was no longer human in any ordinary sense.

Then something else bothered her.

*Why are the other knights obeying him?*

The Invincible Cavalry were among the proudest troops in the Empire.

They were not cowards.

They were not cultists.

They would not knowingly kneel to a demon.

Bianca narrowed her eyes.

Beneath the helmets—

behind the iron faceguards—

she finally saw it.

Blank expressions.

Clouded eyes.

Slack mouths.

Some drooling.

“…Brainwashing.”

The knights were being controlled.

Which meant—

Usher’s own forces might be suffering the same fate.

And the sudden loyalty of people who had betrayed Bianca—

might have the same explanation.

Bianca gritted her teeth.

“I see.”

“Then I have no choice.”

She did not want to kill innocent Donquixote knights.

But if she held back—

she would die.

Bianca fired.

Not at the riders.

At the terrain.

BOOM!

The cliff face cracked.

A section of unstable rock collapsed.

Mud.

Stone.

Debris.

A landslide crashed toward the cavalry.

Ordinary mounted troops would have been annihilated.

The Invincible Cavalry—

did not slow down.

RUMBLE!

Aura gathered at every spear tip.

Horses thundered over slopes that should have been impossible to cross.

The cavalry drove straight through falling rock.

Bianca inhaled sharply.

*Monsters.*

There might not be any terrain capable of stopping them.

She drew again—

already knowing she would eventually have to take the charge with her own body.

Then—

“BIANCA!”

A voice exploded above her.

SLASH!

A spear strike cut diagonally across the front line and threw a mounted knight away.

A black cloak landed beside her.

Tudor.

“Get up!”

Bianca stared at the hand he did **not** extend.

Then remembered.

His left arm was gone.

She pushed herself upright.

“Tudor!”

“They’re targeting you!”

“I know.”

“Focus on getting out!”

He swept his spear across the rocks—

breaking more stone loose.

Then voices came from behind.

“Tudor!”

“Bianca!”

Dolores.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Reinforcements.

But Pedro reacted faster.

“Encircle them!”

The cavalry split.

One detachment turned to block Dolores and the others.

The rest raised spears—

then threw.

THUNK!

One pierced Tudor’s thigh and pinned him against the ground.

“…!”

He clenched his teeth.

Then the next volley came.

“Down.”

Tudor shoved Bianca behind him.

Arrows and spears struck.

Arm.

Shoulder.

Leg.

Torso.

THUD.

THUD.

THUD.

“TUDOR!”

Bianca screamed.

He remained upright.

Protecting her.

Pedro smiled.

“What a moving scene.”

“A beautiful ending.”

He raised a bow.

Dark aura gathered.

TWANG!

The shot hit Tudor in the chest.

Something cracked.

Tudor finally collapsed.

Bianca caught him.

“Tudor!”

The wound was terrible.

Without a priest—

he would die.

Dolores was still trapped on the other side of the cavalry line.

Pedro laughed.

“What will you do?”

“Surrender—

and I’ll bring Tudor back for treatment.”

“And you too.”

For some reason—

he still wanted them alive.

Bianca looked down at Tudor.

There was no hesitation.

“…Help him.”

“I’ll surrender.”

Pedro smiled broadly.

“Smart.”

“Isolde will be pleased.”

He turned toward Dolores’s position.

“Enough!”

“Withdraw—”

He stopped.

WHAM!

A massive golden fist erupted from the ground and crushed into Pedro’s face.

More followed.

BOOM!

BOOM!

BOOM!

Golden hands burst upward—

smashing riders from horses.

Rings of earth rose beneath charging mounts—

tripping them.

Pedro staggered.

His nose flattened.

His lips split.

Blood and broken teeth filled his mouth.

Then a figure dropped between Tudor and Bianca.

Black robe.

Wide hat.

White hair beneath it.

Tall now—

but still with a strangely youthful face.

“Sorry.”

“I’m late.”

Tick.

Tick.

Tick.

A pocket watch spun rapidly.

**Sinclaire** held out her hand to Bianca.
