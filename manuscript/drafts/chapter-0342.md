Silence.

The bath became so quiet it seemed endless.

Dolores stared at Choco.

At Vikir.

“…Choco.”

“Did you just talk?”

She grabbed him.

Turned him.

Examined him.

Vikir realized the situation had gone bad.

He closed his mouth.

Looked away.

Too late.

“Choco?”

“You are Choco, right?”

“The mole on your paw.”

“The scar across the bridge of your nose…”

“Did a demon transform into you?”

“No.”

“I don’t feel demonic energy.”

“But you definitely spoke.”

Dolores inspected everything.

Mouth.

Teeth.

Eyes.

Even under the eyelids.

Vikir’s consciousness—

already strained by the Dragon Majin fight—

barely held together.

He needed to reduce suspicion.

So—

“…Woof.”

Careful.

Clear.

Far too deliberate.

Dolores stiffened further.

“Who are you?”

“What are you?”

Fear entered her face.

If this continued—

it would become worse.

Vikir decided to tell the truth.

“Calm down.”

The warm bath might as well have frozen solid.

Dolores stopped moving.

“….”

“….”

Another unbearable silence.

“I think there’s a misunderstanding—”

“AAAAAAH!”

Dolores screamed.

There was no point continuing like this.

Vikir activated the Picaresque Mask.

Dog form disappeared.

His human body returned.

Splash!

The sudden change of size sent water across the bath.

Vikir ended up half-fallen against Dolores.

Her horrified face was visible even through the steam.

Vikir lowered his voice.

“It’s me.”

“Night Hound.”

“Van—”

“GET AWAY FROM ME!”

Dolores punched.

BOOM!

Holy power exploded through her arm.

The bathwater split outward.

The fist hit Vikir’s jaw with enough force to resemble the future Ironblood Saintess at full strength.

CRACK.

The last thread of Vikir’s consciousness snapped.

◆◆◆

For once—

he slept properly.

No nightmare.

No dream at all.

“…!”

Vikir sat up sharply.

Most of his internal injuries had recovered.

His mask—

gone.

His clothes—

also gone.

He pulled the blanket aside.

Dolores sat beside the bed.

Their eyes met.

“….”

“….”

The silence was so complete—

it felt possible to hear cells dividing.

Eventually—

Dolores spoke first.

Her face was crimson.

“H-have you woken up?”

“Night Hou—”

“No.”

“Van—”

She stopped again.

“…Vikir.”

Her head lowered farther.

“I wasn’t trying to remove your mask.”

“But water had gotten inside it.”

“You couldn’t breathe.”

Vikir nodded.

That was reasonable.

“I owe you one.”

He only meant to move the conversation forward.

Dolores became even redder.

Apparently—

that was possible.

Vikir looked elsewhere.

Baby Madam and Decarabia sat awkwardly on the distant sofa.

“…I wondered where you two went.”

[Sorry, human.]

[I tried to hide.]

[She found me immediately.]

“Hack, hack.”

His bare face was exposed.

His mask.

His altered voice.

Baby Madam.

Decarabia.

At this point—

maintaining the performance was meaningless.

Vikir sighed.

“This—”

“I know.”

Dolores raised her head before he could continue.

“I’m sure you had your reasons.”

“….”

“Don’t try to carry everything alone.”

“We’re comrades.”

The exact kind of words Vikir had once given her.

*Don’t agonize alone.*

*We’re comrades.*

Dolores had wanted—

for a very long time—

to return that sentiment to Night Hound.

To Vikir.

*I can finally say it.*

The circumstances were bizarre.

That hardly mattered.

The man whose identity she had wondered about—

the person she had believed to be connected to her soul—

was here.

Dolores looked him over.

At first—

checking for wounds.

Then—

she flushed again.

“Y-you should put something on.”

She handed him a bathrobe.

Her voice nearly disappeared.

She had treated countless male patients.

Seen countless bodies.

So why was her heart beating like this?

Dolores had no answer.

The silence grew awkward again.

She rushed to fill it.

“Then…”

“how does the dog transformation work?”

“And why—”

“It came from the ability I obtained after killing Dantalian.”

“Oh…”

Dolores swallowed.

Then asked even more quietly:

“The last time we bathed together…”

“was that you too?”

“Yes.”

WHOOSH.

Her face turned red enough to look painful.

Memories arrived all at once.

Kissing Choco’s paws.

His forelegs.

Petting his head.

Pressing his cheeks.

Touching his chin.

Rubbing his stomach.

Touching noses.

Then—

*Dogs kiss all the time.*

*…*

*Why doesn’t our Choco do it?*

*So disappointing.*

*…*

*Are you sure you’re really a dog?*

*Suspicious.*

Vikir licking her cheek.

Dolores kissing Choco’s muzzle—

nose—

face.

Dolores pressed her lips together.

Barely kept another scream inside.

Vikir tried to ease the atmosphere.

“…Good thing you never had me neutered.”

“AAAAAAAH!”

It did not help.

◆◆◆

Two more days passed.

Dolores finally worked through the guilt that had been trapping her.

“The more painful reality becomes—

the more deeply we should think about the meaning of life and faith.”

She repeated the lesson.

Then expanded it in her own words.

“The answer itself isn’t the only thing that matters.”

“The process of confronting the question matters too.”

She looked steadier than Vikir had ever seen her in this timeline.

“Thank you, Vikir.”

Vikir knew little about theology.

He simply nodded.

*Before regression—*

*Dolores wasted more time on this floor than almost anywhere else.*

Months.

She had remained here for months.

Eventually—

Tudor had desperately persuaded her to leave and find Bianca.

Only then had she moved on.

This time—

Dolores had escaped the guilt much faster.

Maybe because of the insight.

Maybe because of the shock in the bathroom.

Maybe both.

She looked toward the exit.

“…When we go through that gate—”

“will we separate again?”

“Probably.”

Vikir answered casually.

Even if they did—

the tower’s structure meant they might meet again later.

And Dolores moving forward earlier—

meant she might save many people who otherwise died.

Vikir stepped forward.

Behind him—

Dolores hesitated.

Caught the sleeve of his robe.

Then—

step.

She moved beside him.

A notice hovered nearby.

**<A small gift for everyone who worked so hard to reach this floor! Send a message to someone outside the tower who is waiting anxiously for you!>**

Dolores looked at it.

“I won’t be fooled by fabricated answers.”

**<The letter item normally costs one golden candy. Residents receive one free letter every month!>**

“I don’t have a month to waste.”

“People on other floors are suffering right now.”

**<The probability of entering this room is below 0.0001%. You are a chosen lucky challenger!>**

“I decide whether something in front of me is luck.”

**<From the next floor onward, difficulty increases by at least ten times!>**

“No matter what suffering waits ahead—”

“I’m not afraid.”

**<Rest here, restore body and mind, then challenge the tower again!>**

Vikir looked at her.

“…Ready?”

“Of course.”

They stood beside each other at the threshold.

“The road from here will be genuinely difficult.”

“If you’re beside me—”

“I can endure it.”

“You’re really abandoning this comfortable place?”

“My answer won’t change.”

Vikir nodded.

Then they walked.

Same direction.

Same pace.

Comrades.

Both looked ahead.

Neither wavering.

Toward Basement Floor11—

and whatever darkness waited there.
