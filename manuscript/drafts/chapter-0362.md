# Chapter 362: Voluntary Escort (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/362.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 360 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Nouvelle Vague, BDISSEM, River Styx, Decarabia, Shadowless King of the Black Sea, Isabella Le Baskerville, Banshee Morgue, Mozgus Quovadis, and Lovebad Bourgeois. Maelstrom / Iron Maiden are source-local witness forms pending direct Fandom access.

*Nouvelle Vague?*

*Don’t even mention it.*

*Living hell.*

*I never want to go back.*

*Surviving that place was luck.*

*I still can’t believe that monster of an old man let us out himself.*

Memories from before regression.

During the war against demons—

many of Vikir’s comrades had once been prisoners.

The extermination war had united humanity.

Once someone stood on the human side—

their criminal record mattered very little.

Everyone became a comrade.

Vikir fought beside escapees from Nouvelle Vague.

Ate with them.

Bled with them.

Listened to their stories.

They talked about prison the way veterans talked about military service—

endlessly.

In detail.

Vikir had always listened.

*So I know what kind of place Nouvelle Vague is.*

He entered the Iron Maiden.

The lid closed.

Spikes drove inward.

CLANG.

They bent.

River Styx had hardened his body.

Hell Tree physical-resistance growth had hardened it further.

Aura reinforced everything.

The coffin’s torture mechanism—

could not pierce him properly.

Vikir spoke through the narrow openings near the maiden’s eyes and mouth.

“Push.”

No hesitation.

The escorts tipped the coffin toward the sea.

Every opening had been sealed with special wax.

Heavy ballast was attached beneath it.

Once released—

the coffin would be caught by Maelstrom.

Dragged downward.

Into the abyss.

Isabella rested her sword against the chain connecting the coffin to the ship.

Her secret mission had been to free him—

even at the cost of everyone aboard.

Yet the prisoner himself—

insisted on entering Nouvelle Vague.

Whatever Vikir truly wanted inside that prison—

it was something Isabella could not understand.

She had escorted many people toward Nouvelle Vague.

Never once—

had she expected to see them again.

But with Vikir—

strangely—

she felt otherwise.

*I think we’ll meet again.*

She exhaled.

Then cut.

SLASH.

The chain broke.

SPLASH!

The coffin dropped into the black sea.

White foam marked where Vikir had entered.

Then the current erased even that.

The ship recovered its anchors.

Slowly—

it escaped the whirlpool’s pull.

After midnight—

Maelstrom weakened.

The currents calmed.

Wind caught the sails.

The four surviving escorts remained silent.

Lovebad finally spoke.

“…What am I supposed to tell Merelini?”

“She’s already bedridden from lovesickness.”

Banshee sighed.

“Our Academy is going to explode when this gets out.”

Mozgus covered his face.

“Dolores will be devastated.”

Isabella muttered:

“And I’ll be destroyed by the Head when I return.”

Eventually—

Banshee forced himself back to reality.

“We need to return quickly.”

“Transfer the demonized prisoners to the court.”

“Report the mutiny.”

Mozgus nodded.

“And request a retrial for Vikir.”

Isabella added:

“That’s not enough.”

“We need a full investigation of Passamonte.”

“Madeline.”

“Hobbes.”

Lovebad looked uneasy.

“All major figures.”

“This could trigger conflict between the Seven Families.”

That possibility was real.

Vikir had entered Nouvelle Vague—

but before leaving the surface—

he had planted a seed of chaos large enough to shake the Empire.

◆◆◆

Far below—

Vikir descended inside the coffin.

Innocence.

Retrial.

Public opinion.

None of it mattered now.

Only one thing did.

Reaching Nouvelle Vague safely.

Bubbles streamed past.

The world darkened.

Cold water began creeping through tiny gaps near his feet.

The coffin shook violently inside the whirlpool.

Then stabilized.

Its enormous weight dragged it down quickly.

CREAK.

Pressure increased.

The orichalcon-alloy coffin began deforming.

Slowly—

the Iron Maiden compressed around Vikir’s body.

Most prisoners—

according to stories—

broke here.

Claustrophobia.

Pain.

Fear.

Some killed themselves.

Some lost their minds.

Vikir remained silent.

CREAK.

CRACK.

The coffin tightened.

The pressure crushing his body—

was less serious than the lack of air.

Then—

sssssss—

bubbles escaped somewhere near his feet.

*Oxygen?*

Vikir looked toward the BDISSEM restraints.

The strange material was emitting a faint supply of air.

Enough to help him breathe.

*Mana absorption.*

*Physical restraint.*

*And oxygen production.*

*What exactly is this substance?*

The old escapees had told him something similar.

BDISSEM was torture on land—

but in the deep sea—

it was also part of the survival system.

The coffin descended farther.

No light remained.

Absolute darkness.

An endless void.

A prisoner inside a metal coffin—

falling through black water—

might as well have been buried alive beneath the world.

Still—

Vikir endured.

Huge things moved outside.

Unknown deep-sea creatures.

Decarabia’s voice came from his chest.

[Human.]

[Still alive?]

“Yes.”

[Something enormous passed us.]

[Many legs.]

“Probably something like the Shadowless King of the Black Sea.”

Decarabia paused.

[…You’re surprisingly relaxed.]

“This is nothing.”

“There are harder things ahead.”

Decarabia sounded offended.

[Very well.]

[Then I will demonstrate one of my many great abilities.]

[Something especially useful right now.]

Its single eye emitted strange light.

“…!”

The darkness outside the coffin appeared inside Vikir’s mind.

“Shared vision.”

“Useful.”

[Only useful?]

[After I give you sight in the abyss?]

Decarabia grumbled.

Vikir ignored it.

Through the shared vision—

the deep sea became visible.

A forest.

No—

a jungle.

Gigantic seaweed rose like pillars from a forgotten temple.

Dense colonies stretched across the seabed.

Ancient plants swayed in the black water—

like an army of giants standing watch.

The coffin continued downward.

Toward the highest cliff in the underwater forest.

There—

a faint light appeared.

An ancient fortress.

Huge bricks.

Old walls.

A gloomy castle—

built atop a dormant volcano at the bottom of the sea.

**Nouvelle Vague.**

The worst prison in the world—

waited for Vikir.
