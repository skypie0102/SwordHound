# Chapter 421: Goodbye, Nouvelle Vague (3)

Nouvelle Vague erupted.

A roar—

as if the entire ocean had been lifted.

The red fire pillar that had blown away the Gate of Good and Evil—

turned blue.

Then widened.

Twice.

Three times.

Four.

Then hundreds of times larger.

Around the fire—

heated water rose.

Second current.

Third current.

More.

A water pillar wrapped around the flame.

Fire and sea—

surging upward together.

An oceanic earthquake unlike anything in recorded human history.

The effect would not remain beneath the water.

The eruption would reach the atmosphere.

The land.

Every living thing above.

◆◆◆

Several minutes earlier—

Vikir watched the Gate of Good and Evil shoot away.

A massive alloy disk—

launched like a bottle cap.

That alone showed how much pressure had accumulated.

But it was not the end.

Second shock wave.

Third.

Fourth.

Then—

hundreds.

Once those arrived—

anything exposed here would die.

*I hope Aiyen is safe.*

Vikir looked upward.

The Gulper Eel was already gone.

Orca's currents had accelerated it toward higher water.

Aiyen and Sadi—

hopefully beyond the worst of what was coming.

There was nothing more Vikir could do for them.

He turned back.

RUMBLE.

BOOM.

The seabed looked as though lightning were striking it.

A country-sized earthquake and tsunami unfolded beneath the sea.

Against a force like that—

a human was less than a grain of sand.

Tap.

Someone touched Vikir's shoulder.

Marquis de Sade.

His expression asked the question.

*What now?*

Vikir's mind moved.

Explosion.

Then rising current.

Then—

everything in the area swept upward or destroyed.

He scanned the darkness.

Then noticed—

the **Gate of Good and Evil**.

The prison's symbol.

Poseidon's blast had launched it.

But had not broken it.

The center—

dented.

No cracks.

The chains around it had failed before the Gate itself.

Vikir remembered the Bourgeois vault built by Belial.

A similarly extraordinary metal structure.

Even a prolonged concentrated assault had barely damaged it.

The Gate—

larger.

Thicker.

Built for the deepest prison in the world.

Had survived a volcanic blow.

Vikir moved immediately.

“Come.”

Marquis de Sade swam after him.

They reached the Gate where it had smashed into the terrain.

Vikir climbed onto the disk.

Sade stared.

“…Are you insane?”

There was no better option.

Oxygen would not last.

Swimming ten thousand meters upward—

impossible.

The eruption—

certain.

So Vikir chose the path with a probability greater than zero.

Ride the Gate.

Like a cork—

on the eruption.

Use it as the floor.

Then shield the top.

*Survive somehow.*

One chance in a hundred?

One in a thousand?

Still better than zero.

Vikir sat near the Gate's dented center.

Then poured mana into Decarabia.

[…Human.]

Decarabia understood.

Vikir pointed upward.

“You block the pressure from above.”

The plan was crude.

A sandwich.

Gate below.

Barrier above.

The Gate of Good and Evil would absorb the lower force.

Decarabia's dome—

the upper force.

A sealed pocket between them.

The problem—

mana.

Maintaining a barrier strong enough to survive the ascent—

far beyond what Vikir could sustain alone.

“I can help.”

Marquis de Sade grabbed Vikir's hand.

High-quality mana flowed through the contact.

The Marquis's face—

for once—

serious.

“I want to see what happened to the surface.”

“Take me with you.”

Even combined—

the odds remained poor.

Then—

SLAP.

A hand appeared over the rim of the Gate.

Thick fingers.

A black harpoon.

A bloody old man pulled his upper body into view.

**Orca.**

One eye ruined by the impact.

Blood everywhere.

“…Take me too.”

Marquis de Sade stared.

“You're insane.”

“We were fighting each other a minute ago.”

“I remember.”

Orca ignored him after that.

Looked only at Vikir.

“I can't die here.”

Sade snapped—

“He'll just add weight and trouble.”

Orca answered—

“I am more trustworthy than a criminal like you.”

Bloodied.

Half-dead.

Still arguing.

Vikir—

instead—

remembered the original history.

*Causality changed.*

Before regression—

Marquis de Sade escaped much later.

Near the end of the demonic war.

Sadi built influence outside.

Used agents inside Nouvelle Vague.

Opened the prison.

Freed her grandfather.

By then—

the surface had become an age of fire and war.

Marquis de Sade killed his enemies—

including BDISSEM—

then incited a massive prisoner revolt.

At the same time—

demon forces invaded.

Orca and the guards were overwhelmed.

Sade escaped amid the chaos.

Nouvelle Vague itself fell under demonic influence.

Faced with both the demon occupation and Sade's escape—

Orca chose an extreme answer.

He activated the device discovered and researched during the **Poseidon Project**.

The eruption destroyed huge numbers of demons.

Killed Marquis de Sade.

Then Orca reached the surface—

and joined the Human Alliance.

A major combatant.

That was the history Vikir remembered.

Now—

that future no longer existed.

Orca lay bleeding on the Gate—

asking the Night Hound for mercy.

“The surface is hell.”

“I saw it.”

“If I get out—

I can help fight the monsters.”

Vikir studied him.

The resolve in Orca's remaining eye—

real.

Then—

“Get on.”

Orca climbed onto the Gate.

Marquis de Sade clicked his tongue.

“Already crowded…”

Orca ignored him.

Three men stood together.

**Vikir.**

**Marquis de Sade.**

**Orca.**

Above them—

a red inverted pentagram appeared.

Decarabia unfolded.

Curved.

Formed a hemisphere.

Five points anchored toward the Gate.

The **Wailing Wall** became a dome.

Vikir looked at the two old men.

“If the Gate fails—

we die.”

“If Decarabia runs out of mana—

we die.”

Sade and Orca moved to either side of him.

Hands on Vikir's shoulders.

Mana poured in.

Below—

space itself seemed to scream.

A terrible force accelerated toward them.

From directly beneath.

BOOOOOOM!

The first explosion—

audible.

Then the second.

Third.

Later impacts passed beyond what the human ear could process.

Silence—

with pressure.

Marquis de Sade's face changed.

So did Orca's.

Men who had lived through gambling—

war—

prison—

violence—

now felt something close to pure fear.

Then—

the Gate moved.

At first—

almost gently.

Like a coin lifted by wind.

Then—

UP.

The disk accelerated.

Vikir's body tried to tear apart.

Pressure from below.

Pressure from above.

Flesh.

Bone.

Blood.

Everything compressed.

He poured all of Sade and Orca's supplied mana into Decarabia.

The barrier held.

Barely.

At the same time—

Beelzebub drove **Immortality — Gargoyle (S)** to its limit.

CRACK.

CRACK.

CRACK.

Bones broke.

Reset.

Muscle tore.

Repaired.

Blood vessels burst.

Closed.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Even S-rank monsters would scream under pressure like this.

Decarabia's voice reached Vikir—

faintly—

between impacts too violent to hear.

[…Human!]

[…Up!]

[…We're going!]

Then—

for no obvious reason—

Vikir remembered words he had spoken upon being promoted inside Nouvelle Vague.

*I will stake everything on it.*

*I will make Nouvelle Vague change.*

That had been the plan from the moment he entered.

And now—

the plan had succeeded.

The prison—

changed beyond recognition.

Destroyed.

Its people scattered.

Its secrets exposed.

Its eruption—

about to change the surface world.

Vikir smiled faintly.

*Do everything a person can.*

*Then accept what remains.*

He had done the human part.

Prepared.

Fought.

Calculated.

Risked everything.

Now—

the rest belonged to fate.

And Vikir—

after nearly four years underground—

was finally going to meet the sky.

Ten thousand meters above.

Toward the surface.
