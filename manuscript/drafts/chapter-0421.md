# Chapter 421: Goodbye, Nouvelle Vague (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/421.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 419 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Local Basilisk-regeneration wording is treated as recurring drift; accepted **Immortality — Gargoyle (S)** continuity controls. Established terminology controls Decarabia, Wailing Wall, Orca, Marquis de Sade, Gate of Good and Evil, and Poseidon.

Nouvelle Vague erupted.

The red pillar of fire that had blasted away the Gate of Good and Evil turned blue.

Then doubled.

Tripled.

Expanded hundreds of times.

Heated seawater surged with it.

Fire and water rose together in an unprecedented oceanic cataclysm.

The effects would not remain beneath the sea.

The eruption would alter the world above.

◆◆◆

Minutes earlier—

Vikir watched the Gate of Good and Evil fly away.

He knew the first blast would not be the last.

More shock waves would come.

Hundreds.

*I hope Aiyen made it.*

The Gulper Eel was gone from sight.

Orca’s current had pushed it upward.

Vikir could only trust that Aiyen and Sadi had reached safer water.

Marquis de Sade tapped his shoulder.

What now?

Vikir searched desperately for a route to survival.

Then noticed the Gate of Good and Evil.

Even after absorbing Poseidon’s power—

it had not broken.

Its center was dented.

Nothing more.

The chains had failed before the gate itself.

Vikir remembered Belial’s Bourgeois vault—

also built from mithril and orharcon-class alloy.

It had endured hours of concentrated assault.

The Gate of Good and Evil was even more absurdly durable.

Vikir moved.

“Come.”

Marquis de Sade followed.

They reached the gate just as the blue fire column beneath them expanded.

Vikir climbed onto the great disc.

Marquis de Sade stared at him.

“…You’re insane.”

“Maybe.”

The plan was simple.

Ride the gate upward.

Use it as the floor—

while Decarabia formed a dome above them.

A sealed shell.

A crude survival capsule.

The chance of living through the eruption was tiny.

But tiny was not zero.

Vikir poured mana into Decarabia.

[…Human.]

“You block the pressure from above.”

The problem was mana.

Even Vikir could not maintain a barrier strong enough for long.

Marquis de Sade grabbed his hand.

“If that’s the issue—

I can help.”

Mana flowed into Vikir.

Still—

it might not be enough.

Then a hand appeared over the edge of the gate.

“…Take me too.”

Orca.

Blood covered him.

One eye had burst from the impact.

Marquis de Sade snarled.

“You’re joking.”

“We were just trying to kill each other.”

Orca ignored him.

He looked only at Vikir.

“I cannot die here.”

Marquis de Sade protested.

“He’ll only make this harder.”

Orca answered coldly.

“I am more trustworthy than a criminal like you.”

Even half-dead—

the two old men argued.

Vikir remembered the future he had once known.

Originally—

Marquis de Sade escaped much later, during the final demonic war.

Sadi opened the prison for him.

The Marquis killed BDISSEM and led a massive riot.

Orca—

caught between the demon army and Sade’s escape—

activated Poseidon and deliberately erupted Nouvelle Vague.

The eruption killed vast numbers of demons.

And Marquis de Sade.

Orca then reached the surface and joined the Human Alliance.

That history no longer existed.

Causality had twisted.

Now Orca—

covered in blood—

was asking Vikir for help.

“The surface has become hell.”

“I have seen it.”

“Take me with you.”

“I can help against the monsters.”

Vikir studied him.

Then nodded.

“Get on.”

Orca climbed onto the Gate of Good and Evil.

Three men stood together.

Vikir.

Marquis de Sade.

Orca.

Above them—

Decarabia expanded into a red inverted pentagram.

Then curved into a hemisphere.

The **Wailing Wall** became a dome.

“If the gate fails—

we die.”

“If Decarabia runs out of mana—

we die.”

Marquis de Sade and Orca placed their hands on Vikir’s shoulders.

Mana flowed in.

Below them—

the world screamed.

The first explosion thundered.

The next blasts passed beyond human hearing.

Then the Gate of Good and Evil rose.

The three men were driven upward.

Pressure crushed them from every direction.

Bones groaned.

Flesh tore.

Blood surged.

Vikir forced Decarabia’s defense to hold.

At the same time—

Beelzebub drove **Immortality — Gargoyle (S)** regeneration to its limit.

[…Human!]

[…Up!]

[…We’re going!]

Decarabia’s voice barely reached him through the impossible pressure.

Then Vikir remembered words he had spoken when promoted inside Nouvelle Vague.

*I’ll put everything on the line.*

*I’ll change this place.*

That plan—

had succeeded.

Nouvelle Vague had changed beyond recognition.

Vikir had done everything a human could do.

Now—

the rest belonged to fate.

A faint smile touched his face.

For nearly four years—

he had not seen the sky.

Now he was going to meet it.

Rising ten thousand meters—

toward the surface.
