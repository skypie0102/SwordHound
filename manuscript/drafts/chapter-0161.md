# Chapter 161: Familiar Face (4)

Canonical/project terminology controls Vikir, Dolores L. Quovadis, Geronto, Camus Morgue, Morgue, Baskerville, Donquixote, Quovadis, Beelzebub, Ephebo, Pedo, Hebe, and Quilt Rune Indulgentia. **Geronto.**

A thin female mage.

Long red hair spilled from beneath the black covering over her face.

Dolores stared at her.

“She joined Quilt relatively recently.”

“And judging from her build…”

“she seems young.”

“…”

Vikir did not answer.

He swallowed the sound in his throat.

Geronto stepped forward.

Dark mana erupted around her.

Grrrrr—

crackle!

Complex magic circles appeared in empty air.

Then—

red flame.

Black iron spikes.

The spikes burst from the floor—

heated red-hot by fire.

Touch one—

and the victim would be cut and burned at the same time.

Dolores stared at the amount of mana being released.

“This level…”

“At least fifth circle.”

She corrected herself.

“No.”

“Possibly sixth.”

“She must have been an extraordinary mage while she was alive.”

Flaming iron spikes filled the hall.

Vikir extended Beelzebub.

Clang!

Clang!

He knocked them aside.

Then a wall of fire twisted around him—

blocking movement.

Whenever Vikir slowed—

new spikes erupted from floor—

wall—

ceiling.

Red.

Black.

The combination felt painfully familiar.

Sizzle—

flames clung to shoulder, back, ribs, feet.

Vikir withdrew.

Behind him—

Dormitory One's central hall had already become a furnace.

Molten iron.

Red-hot spikes.

Fire shaped like teeth.

Geronto remained on the far side—

continuing to cast.

The air itself burned the lungs.

Smoke reduced visibility.

The light was so bright it became another kind of blindness.

Dolores—

forced back toward the entrance—

shouted.

“Night Hound!”

“We can't even approach her like this!”

“…”

Vikir stared through the lenses of his mask.

Black smoke.

Red hair.

A female mage beyond the furnace.

Then—

low and rough—

he spoke.

“…I need to see her face.”

Dolores blinked.

“What?”

“I need to see her face.”

Vikir stepped forward.

The ground ahead had become a field of iron thorns and flame.

He ran into it.

CRUNCH!

A spike erupted beneath his foot.

Punched through the instep.

Another pierced the heel.

More drove upward toward the knee.

Spikes from the walls and ceiling glowed red—

then softened—

then melted.

Drip.

Drip.

Drip.

Molten iron began falling from above.

Below—

new spikes continued emerging.

Around him—

fire intensified.

A rain of flame and liquid metal.

Vikir continued forward.

“…”

Spike after spike entered his body.

He did not stop.

“Face.”

The anger in his throat burned hotter than the furnace.

“Let me see your face.”

The smell of scorched flesh spread.

Blood heated against metal.

Dolores stared—

horrified.

*Why is he doing this?*

*Does he know that woman?*

*What is she to him?*

Questions dried her mouth.

Then—

“…Ugh!”

Dolores shut her eyes.

And followed.

Holy power was most effective against darkness.

It offered much less protection against ordinary heat—

metal—

physical flame.

Even so—

she stepped into the path the Night Hound had opened.

Spikes cut her.

Heat burned her.

Molten iron splashed.

She kept moving.

*The path ahead of him is worse.*

*I can't complain when I'm only following.*

Burns and cuts spread across Dolores's body.

Ahead—

Vikir reached the far end.

Geronto's mana finally faltered.

She staggered backward.

No more spikes.

No more walls of flame.

Vikir stood directly in front of her.

Iron still buried in his body.

Fire still crawling across his clothes.

He reached out.

“Take off the mask.”

One hand locked around Geronto's throat.

The other seized the black covering.

Rip.

The instant it came away—

[Grrrrrrr!]

Geronto convulsed.

Mana inside her body surged violently.

Vikir sensed it.

Released her.

Jumped back.

Dolores barely had time to react.

“W-What—!?”

BOOM!

Geronto detonated.

Her internal mana turned the undead body into a bomb.

The blast flattened the immediate area.

When the fire cleared—

Geronto's body lay on its back.

The head had been destroyed.

Only the black face covering remained intact—

untorn—

unburned—

on the floor.

Dolores frowned.

“…They put a bomb in her head.”

Vikir did not answer.

He was staring at the corpse.

Dark-red robes.

Female body.

Head gone.

After a moment—

he moved toward it.

Dolores's eyes widened.

“Night Hound?”

“What are you doing…?”

Vikir tore open the heavy robes.

Not out of curiosity.

For identification.

The corpse beneath was pale—

and visibly reconstructed.

Parts of the body had been stitched or reinforced with iron—

leather—

other materials.

The state of the flesh and bone made one thing clear.

When Geronto had been raised as undead—

her original corpse had not been whole.

Meaning—

before resurrection—

she had died badly enough that even the body could not remain intact.

Vikir examined the remains in silence.

Then—

“No.”

Dolores looked at him.

“You knew her?”

“…I thought I did.”

He looked once more.

“But I was wrong.”

A face rose in his memory.

**Camus Morgue.**

From the beginning—

Vikir had suspected Geronto might be Camus.

Similar height.

Similar build.

Similar apparent age.

Red hair of almost the same shade and length.

Magic built around iron and fire.

And the timing troubled him.

Camus had left the Morgue Light Faction without an explanation Vikir fully understood.

Joined the Dark Faction—

known for black magic.

Then entered closed-door training.

But Geronto was not Camus.

The body did not match closely enough.

Geronto was slightly shorter than Camus had been the last time Vikir saw her.

Her physical development also suggested a different age.

Most importantly—

Vikir remembered a distinctive identifying mark.

During a joint training incident when Vikir was eight—

Camus's clothing had been burned away.

He had seen a mole high on her chest—

just below the collarbone.

Geronto had no such mark.

Red hair.

Iron.

Fire.

Powerful magic.

Those clues still strongly suggested **Morgue**.

But not Camus.

*…Then why is a woman from Morgue here as undead?*

And not only Morgue.

Baskerville.

Donquixote.

Quovadis.

Young members of multiple great houses—

dead—

then rebuilt into demon-controlled guards.

How far had the demons' reach spread?

Vikir's resolve hardened.

He would have to hunt them even more aggressively.

Then Dolores spoke.

“I've heard something recently.”

“Several famous martial houses have reported grave robberies.”

Vikir touched his chin.

“Grave robbing.”

Normally—

grave robbers wanted treasure buried with the dead.

Gold.

Silver.

Artifacts.

But here—

the corpses themselves were the treasure.

Vikir's expression hardened.

“…Disgusting demons.”

He picked up Geronto's black face covering.

The fourth.

Like the others—

it contained dense demonic power.

All four together—

their total presence approached Beelzebub's.

*What kind of artifacts are these?*

*I'll investigate later.*

Vikir stored them inside his cloak.

Now—

the obstacles were gone.

**Ephebo.**

**Hebe.**

**Pedo.**

**Geronto.**

All four masked hounds—

destroyed.

Only one target remained.

**Quilt Rune Indulgentia.**

The reason Vikir had accumulated Academy demerits.

The reason he had spent Golden Week volunteering.

The reason he had spent ten days pretending to be nothing more than a diligent student worker—

while mapping every route into the orphanage.

The final assassination target.

Vikir steadied himself.

Prepared to take the last step.

Clap.

Clap.

Clap.

Applause echoed from the darkness.

“…!”

Vikir and Dolores looked up.

A familiar face watched them from above.

**Quilt Rune Indulgentia.**

He sat casually on the railing—

smiling down at them.
