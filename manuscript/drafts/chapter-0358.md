# Chapter 358: Crime and Punishment (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/358.txt`. The local Chinese heading is offset by one part; recovered English Chapter 356 confirms this target is **Crime and Punishment (3)**. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Decarabia, Nouvelle Vague, Cindywendy, Chihuahua, MiniPin, Aheul, Osiris Le Baskerville, Picaresque Mask, Camus Morgue, Seere, Dantalian, and Baskerville Senator aides. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked. Source-local **Tochka** remains pending canonical confirmation.

Even the most notorious holding cells in the Imperial Capital seemed uneasy about housing Night Hound.

Before his transfer to Nouvelle Vague—

Vikir was placed temporarily in a secure detention facility.

“Tomorrow at dawn.”

He closed his eyes.

3,021 life sentences.

Nouvelle Vague.

And the sentence would begin only six hours after judgment.

*Avoiding immediate execution was expected.*

*Hugo’s plea was the unexpected part.*

Vikir had studied enough precedent to believe a summary execution was unlikely.

Even if the court had chosen it—

he had contingency plans.

“…Everything is proceeding.”

He accepted the sentence without complaint.

Nouvelle Vague had always been part of the route he intended to take.

There had been variables.

None exceeded his margin of error.

Outside—

voices carried faintly through stone and iron.

“Execute him!”

“Kill the criminal!”

“Burn him!”

“Make him apologize!”

The protesters had not stopped since the verdict.

Some had lost family.

Friends.

Lovers.

To people Night Hound had killed.

Their anger was real.

Their grief—

also real.

[…Does that not feel unjust?]

Decarabia whispered.

It had compressed itself into a tiny point between Vikir’s collarbones.

Small enough to escape the pre-incarceration search.

[Everything you did was for humanity.]

[Without you—

the Gate would have opened.]

[Countless people would have died.]

[And those you killed had already made contracts with demons.]

[They should be thanking you.]

Vikir remained still.

“They don’t know any of that.”

“They don’t know what those people would have done in the future.”

“And even if I told them—

they wouldn’t believe me.”

Human beings—

generally—

believed what they could see.

Explaining the truth would not guarantee understanding.

Trying to persuade every grieving person individually—

would be impossible.

“So it’s simpler if I carry the role of executioner alone.”

Decarabia’s eye rolled.

[Human.]

[Are you trying to become some kind of hero?]

[A saint?]

“No.”

Vikir answered immediately.

“I just want to save as many of the people I want to save as possible.”

He had no particular attachment to absolute goodness.

No desire to become a martyr for all humanity.

He only wanted to preserve—

the few scraps of warmth that had made his previous life bearable—

and the precious connections he had formed by accident in this one.

So Vikir meditated.

And while doing so—

studied the restraints on his body.

Handcuffs.

Ankle restraints.

Chains connecting them.

Cold metal.

Extremely heavy.

Far harder than ordinary material.

And more importantly—

they absorbed mana.

Neutralized it.

*Interesting.*

Vikir traced the surface.

*Maybe this can become useful later.*

Then—

CREAK.

The cell door opened.

A guard spoke.

“Visitor.”

Prisoners awaiting execution—

or transfer to Nouvelle Vague—

received three visitation opportunities.

Vikir had already rejected one request from Baskerville.

And two from Colosseo students.

The guard clearly expected another refusal.

He started closing the door.

“Let them in.”

The guard stopped.

Surprised.

Then left.

Soon—

four hooded figures descended to the bars.

They removed their hoods.

Cindywendy.

Chihuahua.

MiniPin.

Aheul.

Cindywendy smiled first.

“Hello.”

“Been a while.”

Chihuahua looked horrified.

“Young Master!”

“What happened to you!?”

MiniPin looked just as worried.

“Boss!”

“You look terrible!”

Aheul said only one thing.

“…Hunter.”

All four now used the Baskerville surname officially.

Vikir had promoted them into formal aides when he became Senator.

He looked at Cindywendy.

“Unexpected.”

“I thought you’d never take Baskerville’s name.”

She shrugged.

“I’m getting older.”

“Sometimes I want somewhere to lean.”

“You don’t lean on a sword when you’re tired.”

Vikir’s reply was immediate.

“Our alliance was temporary.”

“Wasn’t revenge against Baskerville your real goal?”

Cindywendy smiled.

“It was.”

“I changed the method.”

She raised her left hand.

A ring gleamed on her **middle finger**.

A lover’s promise.

Possibly—

a future marriage.

“If I become the mistress of House Baskerville—

isn’t that revenge too?”

“I can swallow the family from the inside.”

“…?”

For perhaps the first time that day—

Vikir visibly reacted.

A memory surfaced.

Hugo—

during Vikir’s visit to Baskerville—

complaining:

*Osiris seems to have a woman.*

*He refuses to tell me who.*

Before regression—

Osiris had been almost mechanical.

Emotionless.

Now—

he had a lover.

Pomeranian’s arrival had changed Hugo.

Apparently—

the changes had spread farther than Vikir expected.

*Is this good or bad?*

Even Vikir could not calculate the answer immediately.

“…Since when?”

“A while.”

“During the western trade work.”

“We met repeatedly because of security arrangements.”

“And we already knew each other as children.”

That made sense.

After Seth—

Andromalius’s host—

died—

Osiris had investigated and cleaned up much of the aftermath.

He later cleared the falsely condemned Messinadnaro family.

Acknowledged Baskerville’s responsibility.

Handled compensation.

Removed old elders.

Reorganized personnel.

Somewhere during that period—

the two had apparently grown closer.

Cindywendy looked around the prison.

“…Funny.”

“When we first talked—

I was the one in prison.”

“And you were outside.”

“Now it’s reversed.”

Vikir’s answer was short.

“I expected this.”

Then he looked at the others.

Chihuahua.

MiniPin.

Aheul.

They were worried about him.

Vikir—

instead—

worried about what would happen outside.

“You all have work to do.”

He called each one closer.

Whispered instructions.

Different details—

but several repeated ideas.

Stockpile food.

Fortify high ground.

Prepare for mass migration.

Build a final refuge.

**Tochka.**

When it was Cindywendy’s turn—

Vikir stood and approached the bars.

Then—

his face shifted.

Skin peeled away.

The **Picaresque Mask**.

The artifact fused to his flesh when worn—

and the most recognizable symbol of Night Hound.

Vikir passed it through the bars.

Cindywendy blinked.

“Why are you giving me this?”

“It isn’t for you.”

“Give it to Camus.”

Dantalian’s relic.

Filled with demonic power.

A mask Vikir had worn through countless hunts.

“She’ll understand.”

Camus had Seere with her.

She would know what receiving the mask meant.

Cindywendy took it.

Then sighed.

“That girl is going to turn the world upside down again.”

“The Morgue family barely recovered from the Wraith Tree incident.”

Vikir’s expression changed.

“The world is going to turn upside down anyway.”

“…What?”

Cindywendy.

Chihuahua.

MiniPin.

Aheul.

All stared.

Vikir spoke seriously.

“A great flood is coming.”

One of the first survival principles from the Age of Destruction.

The only path—

if humanity intended to survive what was coming.

“Prepare the ark.”

Those were the final instructions Vikir left with his people.
