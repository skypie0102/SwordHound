# Chapter 486: The Marquis of Discord (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/486.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 484 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established project terminology controls Baskerville Ninth Form, Vikir, Andras, Beelzebub, Asmodeus, Kirko Grimm, Cane Corso, Gate of Destruction, Flauros, Andrealphus, and Decarabia. The aligned witness supplies the source-local flaming sword form **Kazanbai**, described there as one of the Seven Demon Swords; direct Fandom verification remains blocked.

*The Ninth Form lies beyond the threshold of death…*

Cane Corso’s voice faded.

The supreme realm—

glimpsed for less than an instant—

had left a mark on Vikir’s vision.

Like recreating an ink painting from memory—

his hand traced that impossible image back into reality.

Nine fangs.

A formation no living swordsman was supposed to produce.

A living constellation.

FLASH!

**Baskerville Ninth Form.**

[AAAAH!?]

Blood burst from Andras.

Nine fangs tore through the boundary of life and death—

then ripped the demon’s body apart.

Vikir stood still.

Almost dazed.

But Andras saw something else.

A black hound.

Crimson eyes.

Crimson mouth.

A death-hound wrapped in violent red aura.

[…I see.]

Andras sounded genuinely impressed.

[After dying multiple times—]

[perhaps a living being can cross the boundary.]

[I simply never expected anyone to actually do it.]

Yet the hand reaching for Andras’s sword remained strangely calm.

Flame roared over the hilt.

The weapon looked molded from volcanic ash and water.

**Kazanbai.**

One of the Seven Demon Swords.

The blade fell.

Beelzebub blocked the descending pillar of fire.

Deflected it.

Countered.

CLANG!

CRACK!

SCREEECH!

Aura fragments and sparks exploded everywhere.

*Good.*

Vikir parried again.

*Only one sword.*

Before regression—

Andras had fought with two.

Kazanbai.

And Asmodeus.

Andras was a master of dual blades.

Now—

Asmodeus belonged to Kirko.

That absence mattered.

[Tsk.]

Andras adjusted his footing.

[A divided body really is inconvenient.]

[I need to become whole.]

Vikir gathered all nine fangs.

A Black Sun emerged.

Heavier.

Larger.

A dark sphere of cutting force that dragged everything toward its center—

then shredded it.

Andras laughed.

[A final duel.]

[The hero awakens—]

[then faces the last boss one-on-one.]

[Isn’t that terribly cliché?]

“Stories become bestsellers for a reason.”

Andras paused.

[…Fair.]

Then—

SLASH!

His face split horizontally.

Everything above the mouth vanished.

Vikir’s sword curved through the air at an unnatural angle.

Andras regenerated his head—

spread his wings—

retreated.

BOOM!

BOOM!

BOOM!

Dozens of exchanges passed in moments.

Andras unleashed a thousand-fire blaze.

Golden pillars melted.

Molten metal poured through floor and ceiling.

Vikir cut through it.

Andras answered with the burning blade.

The final collision approached.

Then—

Vikir felt someone behind him.

The same warmth—

the same inexplicable security—

he had felt on Tochka’s battlefield.

A voice.

Close enough to be beside him.

*You did well.*

Hugo.

The memory struck harder than any blade.

For two lifetimes—

Vikir had ignored that emotion.

Suppressed it.

Erased it.

What remained now—

rose violently.

Blood boiling.

Something directed toward the father he still could not fully understand.

Aura erupted through Beelzebub.

BOOOOOOM!

The Black Sun became a straight line of darkness.

Black lightning.

Then split into nine trajectories.

Pierce.

Catch.

Tear.

Slice.

Sever.

Cleave.

Grind.

Hammer.

Pulverize.

Nine fangs.

The completed killing form of Baskerville swordsmanship.

The pinnacle of murderous intent Cane Corso had demonstrated at the Grave of Swords.

A level Vikir had never reached even during the Age of Destruction.

Andras—

apex predator of the Ten Corpses—

had never faced it either.

[How—!?]

Nine directions collapsed on him.

Andras struggled inside the storm.

The result was already decided.

Wind screamed.

Aura fragments burned away.

Then—

SNAP!

Kazanbai broke.

The Demon Sword shattered inside red fire.

Andras’s body followed.

Cracks opened everywhere.

Red magma boiled beneath.

Wings broke.

Eyes shriveled black.

Vikir knew.

The fight was over.

Every reserve of strength had been spent.

But he had obtained what he came for.

The life of the First Corpse.

Andras.

Marquis of Discord.

BOOM.

The final enemy dropped to his knees.

Vikir lengthened Beelzebub for the finishing strike.

“Demons die.”

A rule unchanged by death.

A dogma.

A demon hunter’s absolute belief.

And yet—

something else had never changed either.

[…So this is how it ends.]

Malice.

Even after losing everything—

Andras smiled.

[You said best-selling stories have reasons for their clichés.]

His dark eyes began pouring shadow again.

[…I agree.]

The strange calm—

even on the edge of annihilation—

made Vikir pause.

[The hero awakens in the final battle.]

[Overcomes hardship through love and friendship.]

[Then defeats the final boss.]

[So shouldn’t the villain have one last struggle too?]

[Real despair.]

“An empty bluff won’t work.”

[Hehehe.]

[I don’t use such cheap tricks.]

Darkness thickened.

Andras’s condition reminded Vikir of other final moments.

Andrealphus.

Flauros.

Demons who—

at death—

had tried to open the Gate of Destruction by sacrificing themselves.

Andras spoke.

[I can do the same.]

[My remaining mana.]

[My life.]

[Enough to open the Gate.]

“Stop.”

“The Gate of Destruction can only be opened once.”

Seere and Decarabia had already explained that rule.

Even the strongest demons—

could open it only once in this world.

Flauros had already done so.

The flood had closed it.

“That Gate is finished.”

Andras blinked.

[Ah.]

[So that flood was your work?]

[I thought the world’s last dragon simply woke at an inconvenient moment.]

“Once the Gate closes—”

“it is done.”

“You can sacrifice whatever you want.”

“It will not open again.”

Andras smiled.

[…Are you certain?]

Then—

[I’m afraid you misunderstand.]

[There is another Gate of Destruction.]

[And it is already fully open.]

“…!”

Decarabia shouted from Vikir’s chest.

[Disgusting, Andras.]

[Using lies to extend your life.]

[There is only one Gate of Destruction.]

[Even creating one requires the combined work of ten Demon Kings.]

[There cannot be a second.]

Andras laughed.

[This is not a lie.]

[Another Gate is open.]

He paused.

[Actually—]

[one is not enough.]

[I can open hundreds.]

Black formations appeared around the hall.

Dozens of small portals.

None required anywhere near the mana of the true Gate of Destruction.

But Vikir’s face became rigid.

*I called you here.*

The first conversation returned to him.

*I made you regress.*

These were not portals opening directly into Hell.

They opened somewhere else.

A world—

where the Gate of Destruction had already opened.

The timeline Vikir had originally lived in.

His world—

before regression.
