The day of the National University League—

had arrived.

Contestants from Colosseo Academy, Varangian Academy, and Temisquira Women’s College stood before Mage Tower.

They knocked.

The main gate opened by itself.

Like the mouth—

of some enormous living thing.

“…Wow.”

The visiting students stepped inside.

From the outside—

Mage Tower looked tall and narrow.

Inside—

it was a world.

One floor held forests.

Another—

lakes.

Oceans.

Deserts.

Canyons full of flowing lava.

Snow-covered mountains buried beneath blizzards.

Stars crowded the ceiling—

forming constellations—

then a vast Milky Way.

Waterfalls ran down walls.

Droplets struck mushrooms with clear musical notes.

Glowing insects drifted through the air.

Then—

as the students climbed—

a stream began running over their feet.

Ankles.

Knees.

Waists.

Shoulders.

At last—

the water rose over their heads.

Panic spread.

Then stopped.

They could breathe.

Their clothes remained dry.

Voices carried clearly.

“Look! I’m not wet at all!”

“I can breathe!”

“It still feels exactly like I’m underwater!”

**Magic Water.**

One of the artifacts a successful contestant might one day earn.

Water that could be drunk or touched normally—

yet did not wet the body when someone submerged their head.

The students swam upward—

through forest—

mountain—

desert—

volcano—

sea—

canyon—

and stars.

Every new floor opened another impossible landscape.

Different places—

joined together by distorted laws of space.

That—

was Mage Tower.

◆◆◆

Before the Survival Contest began—

the four student representatives met.

First—

the map.

A roulette offered terrain after terrain.

Lava Field — Inferno Hell.

Volcano — Charpero Crater.

Desert — Yuuni.

Jungle — Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Snow Mountain — Fort Tochka.

Sea — Harsh Sea.

Abandoned Mine — Forgotten Land.

And more.

Each representative had a preference.

Bakilaga Juragio—

Varangian.

“Snow mountain.”

He grinned.

“Northerners don’t fear the cold. Ice is a weapon. Snow is a blanket.”

Merelini Lovegood—

Temisquira.

“The sea.”

She shrugged.

“Southerners are used to it. No special reason.”

Oraorus Hohenheim—

Mage Tower.

“Lava field.”

A thin smile.

“Fire and lava are my specialties.”

Then—

Dolores L. Quovadis.

Colosseo.

“….”

She was not looking at the roulette.

She was thinking about yesterday.

About Vikir.

*How was he that strong?*

The difference in physique between Vikir and Bakilaga had been enormous.

Yet Vikir had held his own.

Dolores had attended the League three times.

She had never seen another male student match Bakilaga’s strength—

let alone a first-year.

*The midterm results.*

*Knowledge on Professor Banshee’s level.*

*Those strange demerit records.*

A possibility flashed through her mind.

*Could Vikir be the Imperial Family student hiding his identity at the Academy?*

The rumor existed.

If it were true—

some things might make more sense.

But—

*No evidence.*

Dolores immediately checked herself.

*No. I shouldn’t jump to that.*

“Saintess.”

Bakilaga’s voice pulled her back.

“What are you thinking about so seriously?”

“Oh. Sorry.”

Dolores straightened.

“Yesterday’s arm-wrestling match.”

Bakilaga’s grin turned mischievous.

“Your boyfriend was stronger than you expected?”

“…What?”

“You’ve been single all three years. I figured you had no interest in men.”

He nodded approvingly.

“Turns out you’re just picky. That guy passes.”

“No, wait. What are you—”

“Secret relationship?”

Bakilaga laughed.

“Ah. I’m slow. Campus couples. Good for you.”

He turned away.

Dolores finally understood.

She opened her mouth to correct him—

whirrrrr.

The roulette began spinning.

Too late.

Click.

The pointer stopped.

Four faces hardened at once.

Bakilaga.

Lovegood.

Hohenheim.

Dolores.

**Jungle — Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.**

The map—

none of them wanted.

◆◆◆

Elsewhere—

the senior officials of the four academies gathered around a round table.

North—

a short, thickly built man with a sparse beard and a horned helmet.

**Basilios.**

Deputy Director of Varangian Academy.

South—

a woman warrior sitting cross-legged with a bow at her back.

**Hippolite.**

Principal of Temisquira Women’s College.

East—

an enormous old man with long white hair, brows, and beard.

**Whitebeard Whale.**

Mage Tower Master.

West—

a middle-aged man with long black hair and deep-set eyes.

Silent.

**Professor Banshee Morgue.**

Vice Principal of Colosseo Academy.

Whitebeard Whale smiled.

“It is an honor for Mage Tower to host this year’s League.”

Then he looked toward Basilios and Banshee.

“Though I wonder—why have a deputy director and vice principal come instead of the heads of your schools?”

Basilios grimaced.

“Our director is still recovering from the unpleasant incident last time.”

“Ah.”

Whitebeard Whale lifted one finger.

A newspaper article appeared in the air.

A corrected report.

The original headline had blamed **Night Hound** for the assault on Varangian students visiting the Imperial Capital.

The correction named **Ms. Ouroboros** instead.

The later investigation had concluded that the incident was connected to a personal grudge—

an attack by someone acquainted with the victims—

rather than Night Hound.

Varangian’s director had been badly injured.

So Basilios was here in his place.

Whitebeard Whale turned toward Colosseo.

“And your principal?”

Banshee opened his mouth.

“Our principal could not attend because—”

Click.

The map-selection wheel stopped.

The reason went unsaid.

**Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.**

The room quieted.

Of all possible maps—

the jungle was among the most feared.

Snowfields and volcanic zones made food and water scarce—

but anything found could often be preserved through cold, drying, or burial.

The jungle was different.

Hot.

Wet.

Everything spoiled.

Water could be contaminated.

Food could be poisonous.

The environment itself—

was an enemy.

Then—

a huge mirror appeared above the table.

**Extra Eyes.**

A legendary artifact once won by Whitebeard Whale in the League.

Through it—

a viewer could observe a chosen person even from an enormous distance.

Now—

four hundred faces appeared.

Every contestant.

Every school.

All visible.

“Magnificent.”

“You can see all four hundred.”

“Every movement.”

Banshee crossed his arms.

The other officials began discussing favorites.

“Bakilaga Juragio.”

“Merelini Lovegood.”

“Oraorus Hohenheim.”

“Dolores L. Quovadis.”

The obvious third-year aces.

The students most likely—

in their view—

to reach the artifacts.

Banshee said nothing.

He was staring elsewhere.

“Vice Principal?”

Whitebeard Whale followed his gaze.

“Dolores is over there.”

“You’re looking toward the first-years.”

Banshee gave a quiet snort.

“I’m watching someone else.”

His eyes remained fixed—

on the first-year section.
