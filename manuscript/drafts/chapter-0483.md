# Chapter 483: The Marquis of Discord (1)

The Imperial Palace covered roughly five square kilometers.

The largest and tallest building in human history.

Fourteen years of construction.

More than one hundred thousand mages.

More than a million laborers.

Six great palaces formed the complex.

And at its innermost center—

the First Emperor’s Hall stood as the culmination of Imperial architecture.

Splendid.

Enormous.

Solemn.

A place built for the Emperor.

Now—

occupied by the First Crown Prince.

Vikir arrived at its doors soaked by storm and seawater.

A veteran of more battlefields than most people could imagine—

he stared bitterly at the plaque over the hall.

Once—

this had been the seat of his ultimate master.

The sovereign even Hugo Le Baskerville had sworn loyalty to.

Now—

it was a demon’s playground.

BOOM!

Vikir smashed through the enormous doors.

In another age—

that alone would have earned immediate execution.

No one remained to care.

The hall was empty.

Cold.

Abandoned long enough to feel dead.

Vikir crossed the central chamber and moved inward.

At last—

the Imperial throne appeared.

High above the stairs—

someone sat upon it.

Red hair.

Skin almost unnaturally pale.

Cold eyes.

A high, sharp nose.

The Empire’s little sun.

First Crown Prince **Golding Jack Meridieu**.

He smiled brightly.

Then waved.

“Oh my.”

“You came such a long way.”

“You must be exhausted, Mr. Demon Hunter.”

The appearance was princely.

The tone—

almost friendly.

The meaning underneath—

anything but.

Vikir said nothing.

The Crown Prince continued cheerfully.

“I’d offer tea.”

“But everyone is dead.”

“Imagine that.”

“This entire enormous palace and I’m the only one left.”

“How lonely.”

“….”

“This is our first proper meeting, isn’t it?”

“I sent you commendations before.”

“The first time was when you rescued Morgue’s daughter from Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain, wasn’t it?”

“….”

“Camus.”

“Yes.”

“That was her name.”

“Beautiful face.”

“Excellent figure.”

“Remarkably capable.”

“I proposed marriage more than once.”

“Rejected every time.”

“Apparently she already had someone she liked.”

“How jealous I was.”

“….”

“Oh.”

“I wandered off-topic.”

The Crown Prince laughed.

“I thought you were dead back then.”

“Then you returned alive.”

“Next was your Killer Ant paper at Colosseo, wasn’t it?”

“….”

“I wanted to meet you then too.”

“But you were using a false identity.”

“Right?”

“Right?”

“I even visited Colosseo because I thought you might be connected to Baskerville.”

“Then work piled up and I let the matter slip.”

“I should’ve forced a meeting.”

“What a shame.”

Vikir raised one hand.

Enough.

CLANK.

He dragged the chained coffin into the chamber.

Set it down.

The Crown Prince looked at it and laughed.

“What’s that?”

“A coffin for yourself?”

“You came prepared to die?”

“You really are too serious.”

“I came to end the war.”

The Crown Prince pulled a ridiculous face.

“End the war?”

“You mean the civil war?”

“Why is everyone still searching for my missing brother?”

“….”

“Yes, yes.”

“I heard what happened to Leviathan.”

“Terrible.”

“But the Second Crown Prince backed by the other faction disappeared long ago.”

“I’m the only legitimate successor left.”

“So who else should be Emperor?”

“….”

“Why not join me?”

“Wouldn’t becoming a founding hero be better than leading rebels?”

“Being remembered as a usurper is so unfair.”

The words were bizarre.

Detached from reality.

A performance continuing after the stage itself had burned down.

Vikir finally bared his fangs.

“I have no interest in humans fighting humans.”

“Oh?”

“Neutrality?”

“But humans always choose a side eventually.”

“Yes.”

“That is what makes us human.”

Vikir’s eyes hardened.

“So I will never become a demon’s dog.”

[…]

The Crown Prince stopped smiling.

The whites and blacks of his eyes began to shift.

His voice changed.

[Hehehe.]

[Fine.]

[If we both understand each other—]

[we can stop pretending.]

Vikir remained silent.

Then the creature laughed again.

[Before we begin.]

[How did you get past the Kraken?]

[That thing is rare even in the deepest spirit realms.]

[I meant to raise it as a familiar once.]

[Then I released it back into the sea.]

“….”

[Did that spider fight it?]

[What a sight that must have been.]

[Even where I come from, monsters of that size rarely clash.]

[When did that little spider become so enormous?]

[Was it all the poison and demonic energy before the Age of Destruction?]

[Still—]

[it grew larger than even my mother…]

Vikir did not entertain the conversation.

Fourth Form erupted like breathing.

BOOM!

The throne shattered.

[Whoa!]

[You really came to fight?]

The Crown Prince actually looked surprised.

[All right.]

[All right!]

[Let’s talk instead!]

“Demons die.”

[I know.]

[Demons must be exterminated.]

[Right?]

[So calm down.]

[I prepared a topic you will care about.]

The demon continued talking—

until one sentence stopped Vikir.

[I called you here.]

“…!”

Vikir’s eyes narrowed.

The Crown Prince seized the opening.

[I am the one who made you regress.]

For one instant—

Vikir’s thoughts tangled.

He had returned to the past to kill demons.

And a demon claimed responsibility for that return?

The logic sounded impossible.

The Crown Prince looked serious.

[I wanted to return with you.]

[Because I had something I needed to ask.]

“What?”

[My brother.]

The smile disappeared.

[Where is he?]

Vikir’s eyes narrowed further.

The Crown Prince laughed awkwardly.

[I thought you might know.]

[When I looked into the future—]

[I saw only my brother dying.]

[And the final bond connected to him was you.]

“….”

[After your regression, I saw one more scene.]

[Me waiting here.]

[And you arriving with that coffin.]

[You said clearly—]

[‘I brought your other half.’]

[So I waited.]

[Because my foresight told me you would eventually bring information about my brother.]

That—

the Crown Prince claimed—

was how everything began.

The Imperial bloodline inherited a strange prophetic ability from the founding Emperor.

Hohenheim had once mentioned the same thing during the University League.

So at least that part—

could be true.

Then—

*…He really is a good person.*

A strange voice brushed Vikir’s ear.

A voice he had heard before.

A voice tied to memories from before regression.

At the same moment—

the Crown Prince’s expression changed.

[Yes.]

[The blood of the First Emperor is boiling.]

[I can see it again.]

[My brother’s future.]

[His miserable death.]

“….”

[I know you are connected to him.]

[Who is he?]

[Where is he?]

[Tell me.]

Vikir did not answer.

“Why should I?”

“What do you actually want?”

[Idiot.]

[Does an older brother need a reason to find his younger brother?]

“Stop.”

“I already know your body isn’t normal.”

The First Corpse.

A malformed existence even by demonic standards.

The Crown Prince sighed.

[Correct.]

[My body was divided into two.]

[Making us whole again is my highest priority.]

“….”

[And I know you won’t cooperate for free.]

[So I prepared compensation.]

The Crown Prince raised one hand.

A mirror appeared.

“…!”

Vikir’s eyes widened.

Inside—

another world.

A world after destruction.

Shrubs grew again over land once scorched black.

Rain fell.

Rivers and seas returned.

People lived.

Survivors rebuilt homes.

Raised children.

Tried again.

Among them—

faces Vikir knew.

The world he had died in.

His original timeline.

The Crown Prince spoke.

[After your death, the world kept moving.]

[The demons lost the final war.]

[Humanity won.]

[The land was devastated—]

[but survivors are rebuilding.]

“….”

[It is not easy.]

[Too many heroes died.]

[Very few powerful mages remain.]

[They need someone strong.]

[Someone like you.]

If Vikir returned—

he could help them rebuild.

Then came the offer.

[Tell me who my brother is.]

[And where he is.]

[In exchange—]

[I will send you back.]

[You can see the faces you miss.]

[I cannot resurrect everyone.]

[But perhaps I can restore a few people you choose.]

An extraordinary temptation.

Vikir answered immediately.

“No.”

The Crown Prince barely had time to react.

“You cannot do that.”

“Andras.”

“Marquis of Discord.”

[…]

One corner of the Crown Prince’s mouth rose.

Black flame erupted across his body.

Human flesh burned away.

And something ugly—

something ancient—

stepped out from underneath.
