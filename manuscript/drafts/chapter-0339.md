*When I look into it—*

*it looks back.*

*Those who hunt it must take care not to become it themselves.*

*Beyond good and evil—*

*beyond the border of what the human mind can comprehend—*

*they are standing there.*

—**Notes of an Old Nameless Demon Hunter**  
*(The final record found against his chest after his death.)*

◆◆◆

“…!”

Vikir opened his eyes in darkness.

His body was gone.

Only his severed head remained—

floating.

He smiled bitterly.

*I see.*

*So I was dreaming.*

A brief dream—

experienced in the instant after decapitation.

Everything that had happened—

only a dream.

Inside it—

Vikir had reclaimed things stolen from him since childhood.

Uncovered the darkness behind his death.

Taken revenge on brothers of his House.

Entered the Academy.

Built connections with the future heroes.

Hunted powerful demons.

Killed them.

But—

all of it had been an illusion.

Now he was back in reality.

Burning earth.

Mountains of corpses.

Rivers of blood.

Crows circling beneath a ruined sky.

The Age of Destruction.

The Human Realm had technically won the war against the Demon Realm.

Only technically.

Ninety-nine percent of humanity was dead.

Heroic bloodlines had vanished.

And the Gate of Destruction—

once opened—

had never truly closed.

A second demon invasion might come.

A third.

Even death did not let Vikir rest.

Too many comrades had died before him.

Too many still lived.

So even after his head was cut off—

his bloodshot eyes remained open.

Then—

*Vikir.*

A voice.

Vikir tried to turn.

Impossible.

His severed head had been mounted on a post.

*Vikir.*

The voice came closer.

A warm hand touched his cheek.

A woman in military uniform leaned over him.

A long scar had once crossed her face.

Her features were blurred—

yet Vikir knew her immediately.

*Corporal Janet.*

*1st Platoon, 4th Company, 207th Regiment.*

*You’re alive!*

She was not alone.

Faces appeared one after another.

Lieutenant Kirk.

Second Lieutenant Hayar.

Sergeant Kubelin.

Private First Class Saladin.

Private Miriam.

Comrades.

Every one of them.

*You’re alive too?*

*I thought all of you died!*

What could be better—

than discovering dead comrades had survived?

Then Vikir noticed something strange.

They did not look like the people he remembered.

The dried blood around their eyes—

gone.

The injuries—

gone.

Janet’s great facial scar and burn damage had vanished.

Her face was whole.

Healthy.

Almost bridal in its beauty.

Kirk’s missing leg had returned.

Hayar’s ear.

Kubelin’s eye.

All restored.

These were people who had spent their lives poor—

hungry—

sick—

dragged into war as low-ranking officers and soldiers.

Now they wore good clothes.

Their bodies were healthy.

Their expressions—

happy.

A thought surfaced.

In the dream—

Vikir had met Cindywendy.

Through her—

he had quietly supported many of the people who would become his future comrades.

Money.

Education.

Medical care.

Jobs.

A chance to live differently.

*Is that why?*

The comrades before him now seemed to have lived.

Unscarred.

Doing the work they wanted.

Living the lives they should have had.

Then they gathered around Vikir.

Each carried part of his body.

An arm.

A hand.

A leg.

Toes.

An ear.

His nose.

His mouth.

His eyes.

*Vikir!*

They called together.

The body parts rushed toward him.

Emotions spiraled through his mind.

*Sorry.*

*I’m ashamed.*

*We left everything to you.*

*We’re cheering for you.*

*Get up.*

*Keep going.*

Then—

one final message echoed through him.

*Thank you.*

◆◆◆

DING.

[Leaving Basement Floor9 — Dragon Majin’s Laboratory.]

[Entering Basement Floor10 — Lost Paradise.]

“…!”

Vikir’s eyes opened.

An unfamiliar ceiling.

“…So this is reality.”

For once—

his dream had not been a nightmare.

Somehow—

he felt stronger after waking.

*Thinking my comrades gave me strength in a dream…*

*That’s sentimental, even for me.*

He sat up.

White.

Nothing but white.

A vast room.

Floor10.

One level below the Dragon Majin’s Laboratory.

When the Dragon Majin’s fist came down—

Vikir had transformed into dog form again and barely escaped.

Without Sinclaire spending the last of her mana to help—

he would have died.

First—

the reward.

**<Strange Candy> / Level / Golden**

A candy that increases Level by 1.

Sweet.

**Level +1**

Fifty golden candies.

Equivalent to five thousand ordinary candies.

Likely—

the combined reward for escaping the Dragon Majin laboratory—

and surviving the pursuit afterward.

*No item reward.*

Maybe because Vikir had repeatedly refused item rewards.

If so—

the fairies’ attempt to make things harder for him had backfired.

Ordinary challengers might desperately need equipment.

Vikir preferred candy.

He stood.

His uncovered skin brushed something soft.

A bed.

Large.

Warm.

Plush.

Beside it—

a comfortable sofa.

A table and chair.

Silver candlestick.

Fresh fruit.

Bread.

Everything designed for rest.

A sign floated nearby.

**<Floor10 — Lost Paradise>**

Vikir already knew this floor.

There was not much secret about its visible functions.

Most were written directly on the walls.

**<A rest area for challengers exhausted by the tower!>**

**<The largest Candy Shop in the tower is here!>**

**<Exchange items for candy!>**

**<Exchange candy for items!>**

Beside the notice—

a catalog.

**Small hard wooden bed — 1 red candy**

**Large comfortable feather bed — 2 red candies**

**Small cloth sofa — 1 blue candy**

**Large leather sofa — 2 blue candies**

**10 cans of cold beer — 1 blue candy**

**10 crispy fried chicken legs — 1 red candy**

**5 slices of sweet cake — 1 green candy**

**Latest game console — 2 green candies**

**Letter to someone outside the tower — 1 golden candy**

More instructions followed.

**<Items may be exchanged for candy!>**

**<Normally item resale returns only half value. On this floor, items convert at full value!>**

**<Candy already consumed and converted into stats may also be extracted and converted back into candy while residing on this floor!>**

**<For candy consumed before entering this floor, every ten extracted candies grant one additional candy!>**

**<Bonus color is randomized between red, green, and blue!>**

**<Use catalog items to decorate this empty room however you like!>**

At the bottom—

another notice.

**<A small reward for making it this far! Send a message to someone waiting anxiously outside the tower!>**

**<The letter item normally costs one golden candy, but residents of this floor receive one free letter every month!>**

**<The probability of entering this room is below 0.0001%. You are a chosen lucky challenger!>**

**<From the next floor onward, difficulty increases by at least ten times compared with everything before!>**

**<Rest here, recover your body and mind, then challenge the tower again!>**

That was everything.

A spacious room—

meant to be filled with whatever the challenger purchased.

Necessities.

Luxury.

Food.

Entertainment.

No immediate survival threat.

And—

most tempting of all—

contact with people outside the tower.

None of it looked like illusion.

None of it looked like a dream.

Vikir stared at the room.

Then thought:

*This is awful.*

Before regression—

even the greatest heroes had feared this floor.

A trap designed by demons for challengers who had already survived hell.

The most malicious—

cowardly—

and obvious trap in the entire tower.
