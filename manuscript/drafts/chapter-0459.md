A black tent stood atop the dirt mound.

At its center—

a massive cauldron.

Inside—

two giant snakes twisted around each other.

Each bit the other’s tail.

They circled endlessly.

And every time they completed another rotation—

fresh crimson venom gathered in the middle.

Firewood burned beneath the pot.

The poison boiled.

Red vapor filled the tent.

[Grrrrrr…]

[AAAAAAH—]

[Kiiiik!]

Poison Humans entered one after another.

The red steam washed over them.

CRACK.

POP.

SNAP.

Bodies that had been near death suddenly surged with vitality.

Some changed shape entirely—

their restored life force overflowing into new mutations.

Above them—

on a raised seat—

someone watched.

Silently.

A black robe covered his body.

A huge scythe rested behind him.

The mysterious vanguard leader—

the figure the defenders had nicknamed the **Harvester**.

He watched the Poison Humans being reborn in the Red Death vapor.

Then—

BOOM!

One side of the tent tore open.

A stranger rushed inside.

THUD-THUD-THUD!

Heads rolled before the Poison Humans could react.

More than a dozen died immediately.

“…!”

The Harvester rose.

Creating one proper Poison Human soldier consumed tremendous poison—

labor—

and time.

They were not disposable.

He dropped from the platform.

The scythe flashed.

CLANG!

A battered iron sword caught it.

The Harvester’s eyes sharpened.

He recognized the intruder.

Vikir Van Baskerville.

Night Hound.

The infamous criminal once imprisoned in Nouvelle Vague.

Why was he here?

Vikir’s red eyes flashed.

CLANG.

CRACK.

CLANG.

The cheap sword chipped apart with every collision.

Soon—

it looked more like a jagged saw than a blade.

Vikir kept swinging.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Even the Harvester seemed disturbed by the sheer recklessness.

But this was Leviathan territory.

Poison Humans crowded in.

Hands large enough to bend cast-iron pots reached for Vikir.

He cut through limbs.

Bones.

Faces.

Chest cavities.

Chunks of flesh scattered through the tent.

But numbers won.

Eventually—

one enormous palm smashed into Vikir’s face.

THUD!

Another landed on top.

Then another.

And another.

The swarm crushed him.

Tore him apart.

When they finally stopped—

only half of Vikir’s body remained.

The Harvester stared down.

Then reached out.

Almost gently—

he closed Vikir’s empty eyes.

CRUNCH.

The supposedly dead upper body suddenly jerked forward—

and bit down on his hand.

“!?”

The Harvester recoiled.

BOOM!

The wall behind him exploded.

Another Vikir charged in.

Whole.

Uninjured.

A spear in his hands.

He began slaughtering Poison Humans.

Precise thrusts.

Efficient killing.

The Harvester rushed to intercept.

CLANG!

The scythe knocked the spear aside.

Poison Human hands tore into the second Vikir.

Again—

he died.

Then—

BOOM!

A third wall ruptured.

Another Vikir appeared.

Then another.

And another.

Vikir.

Vikir.

Vikir.

The tent filled with him.

Only when moonlight poured through the shredded walls—

did the Harvester notice something wrong.

Black hair.

Red eyes.

But skin as dark as polished obsidian.

The dim tent and crimson vapor had hidden the difference.

The copies fought like animals.

Savage.

Violent.

Almost impossible to regard as living creatures.

Poison Humans fell like wet leaves.

Then—

CRASH!

The central cauldron broke.

The two snakes inside recoiled into a corner.

The red vapor stopped.

The Harvester froze.

So did the Poison Humans outside.

He burst from the tent—

searching for the real Vikir.

Nothing.

Only fire.

Explosions.

And black-skinned Vikirs rampaging through the camp.

Vikir had opened the Andromalius barrier beneath his cloak.

The **Doppelganger leeches**.

The creatures taken from Black Tongue when Vikir left Nouvelle Vague—

now fully grown.

“Oh my.”

“They’re adorable.”

Dolores nodded.

“I kind of want one.”

“…Bro.”

“You didn’t secretly give birth to these somewhere, did you?”

“Friend.”

“Are these really leeches?”

“They look too much like you.”

“I saw them in the Hell Tree once already.”

“I’m still not used to it.”

“They drank Vikir’s blood while growing.”

“So they copied him.”

“And if we dress them properly—

they’ll be almost identical.”

“Eat.”

The creatures swelled rapidly.

Grew.

Black Tongue’s modifications had made them vicious.

Blood.

Death.

Violence.

“Boyfriend!”

“Ignition complete!”

legs—

abdomens.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Sinclaire.

Dolores.

Break open one side of the lake.

Send water toward Tochka.

and flood a large portion of Leviathan’s forces at once.

*Maybe this really is the final operation.*

Toward the line—

where Tochka’s fate would be decided.
