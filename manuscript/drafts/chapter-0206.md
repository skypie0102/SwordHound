Dawn came.

Midterms—

finished.

Weekend.

Public holiday.

A rare stretch of breathing room.

The students of Colosseo used it—

to prepare for the festival.

Classes.

Clubs.

Committees.

Everyone needed—

a concept.

At **Ryukeion**—

the newspaper club—

Dolores nodded over the plan.

“So.”

“Haunted house—

and tavern?”

That was the choice.

Late summer—

meant Halloween season in the Empire.

Exams had just ended.

Everyone was exhausted.

Perfect timing—

for ghosts.

Tudor clapped his hands.

“Then let's decide costumes.”

First-years.

Second-years.

Third-years.

All agreed.

Bianca raised one question.

“What about—

*the tradition*?”

Everyone understood.

Colosseo's festival custom.

Boys—

dress as girls.

Girls—

dress as boys.

Even professors—

could not completely escape it.

Tudor grinned.

“Then the boys become female ghosts.”

“And the girls—

male ghosts.”

Sancho nodded.

“Two traditions at once.”

“Efficient.”

Pigi blinked.

“Do ghosts even have genders?”

Bianca shrugged.

“Doesn't matter.”

“Zombie.”

“Male zombie.”

“Female zombie.”

“Same ghost.”

Sinclaire added—

“And we can serve food in costume.”

Done.

A box appeared.

Lots—

inside.

Each year drew separately—

because numbers and gender ratios differed.

Tudor went first.

His hand entered the box.

“Please.”

“Something normal.”

He opened the paper.

> **Death Knight**

Tudor exhaled.

“Safe.”

Black armor.

Bloodied sword.

Longer blond hair.

Blue eyes.

A costume that worked regardless of gender.

Although—

even with the long hair—

Tudor's face remained too sharply masculine to make the disguise convincing.

Then—

Sancho.

He opened his lot.

> **Snow Maiden**

Silence.

Sancho looked down.

“…Not ideal.”

A northern warrior—

raised on frozen land—

had somehow drawn the coldest possible ghost.

Tudor stared at the muscular body beneath the white costume.

Then laughed.

“That isn't a snow maiden.”

“That's an ice golem.”

Sancho spread his arms.

“Hahaha!”

“Let it go—

friend.”

Everyone broke into laughter.

Sancho—

did not mind much.

He had already decided—

cross-dressing was a uniquely masculine act.

Next—

Pigi.

> **Fairy**

Small frame.

Soft features.

Flowing costume.

Wings on his back.

Pigi looked—

alarmingly convincing.

Then—

Bianca.

> **Frankenstein's Monster**

Painted stitches.

Fake bolts.

A small mustache—

because why not.

Her tall, androgynous frame suited it.

Bianca turned.

“Next—”

Sinclaire already had a bag open.

“I prepared mine.”

Bianca blinked.

“You did?”

Sinclaire pulled out—

a huge homemade spider costume.

Rough stitching.

Tiny crown.

“Spider Queen.”

“How is it?”

Bianca stared.

“I didn't know you liked spiders.”

“Didn't you scream last time one appeared in the bathroom?”

Sinclaire smiled—

too quickly.

“Did I?”

“I don't remember.”

“Anyway—

I love spiders.”

Bianca narrowed her eyes.

Suspicious.

Then—

the room quieted.

One person remained.

Vikir.

“….”

He stood.

Chair—

scraping backward.

Everyone swallowed.

had mana cameras ready.

“I heard first-years through graduating fourth-years lined up just to help with his makeup.”

“The Imperial Palace's makeup artists apparently submitted a formal request.”

“Magazines are offering absurd money for festival photos.”

rejected.

with growing irritation.

Student protection—

came first.

Opened the paper.

> **Witch**

silence.

Black cloak.

Old broom.

Long black false nails.

“…This is unfair.”

“Is he definitely a man?”

“If this gets onto a mana screen—

tomorrow's paper explodes.”

Straight nose.

Long eyelashes.

Dark hair.

Red eyes.
