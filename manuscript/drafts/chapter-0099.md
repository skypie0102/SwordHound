Ancient records described gigantic spiders living in the deepest regions of Hell.

Only hellfire was said to cut them reliably.

Bog Salamanders.

Bone-Sucking Mosquitoes.

Madam.

Expanding.

Always hungry.

A future disaster.

“Good thing I skipped dinner.”

Soft rot below.

Madam's legs above.
