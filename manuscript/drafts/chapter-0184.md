Looking in.

**Baby Madam.**

Crying.

Too dark.

Now—

“Where is Professor Sadi!?”

“Do you not understand the political disaster this could become!?”

meanwhile—

*If they open the barrier and investigate everything—*

*they'll find Baby Madam.*

No.

He needed another route.

And later—

Baby Madam needed discipline.

Black vapor rose.

His body shrank.

A small black dog—

wearing an oversized black hood.

Not from the present.

From the previous timeline.

Seven meters down.

Down.

Through packed earth.

Toward the foundation.

*Small body.*

*Useful.*

Past the barrier.
