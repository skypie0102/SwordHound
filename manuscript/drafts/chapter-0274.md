# Chapter 274: Money War (2)

Late at night—

after the excitement over Oracle’s profits had finally begun to settle—

Dolores carefully opened the rooftop door.

The girls’ dormitory roof was off-limits.

She checked the corridor behind her first.

Then stepped outside.

“…You came.”

The voice sounded like metal scraping metal.

Night Hound stood at the far railing.

At a height that made Dolores dizzy just looking at it—

he seemed perfectly relaxed.

“I’m sorry.”

“I had to avoid the dorm supervisor.”

“Were you waiting long?”

“No.”

Dolores looked at his cloak.

There was no dew on it.

He had arrived recently.

“Did you have trouble finding the building?”

“All the dormitories look similar.”

“There aren’t even clear floor markers.”

“It wasn’t difficult.”

Dolores’s eyebrow moved.

*If this is his first time here—

how did he find it so easily?*

*Is he someone from inside the Academy?*

Then she remembered something else.

The mysterious female mage who had invaded the Academy during the festival with undead soldiers.

She had shouted:

*Where did you hide Night Hound?*

*I found traces of him in your room.*

At the time—

Dolores had dismissed it.

She valued privacy intensely.

She did not let others enter her room.

If furniture or fixtures broke—

she repaired them herself.

*Then why did that woman say Night Hound had been in my room?*

A faint rattle came from inside Dolores’s clothes.

The **Mirror of Truth**.

The artifact she had won at the University League.

A mirror that revealed the true appearance of whatever it reflected.

Dolores hesitated.

*Should I use it?*

She had brought it partly for safety.

But—

no.

She squeezed her eyes shut.

Shook her head.

*I don’t want to learn his identity like that.*

*If he ever wants to tell me—

he should do it himself.*

*That’s basic respect.*

Night Hound stared at her.

“…?”

Dolores opened her eyes.

Took a breath.

“Everything you asked for is finished.”

She reported Oracle’s progress.

The list of ten thousand financial targets Pigi had built.

The prediction letters Sinclaire had managed.

Stocks.

Gold.

Soybeans.

Wheat.

Bronze.

Orichalcon.

Real estate.

Bonds.

Half right.

Half wrong.

Again and again.

Until roughly thirty people had received a seemingly impossible streak of correct predictions.

Then—

the final investment into Cindywendy’s trading company.

And the enormous profit.

“Your investment method was brilliant.”

“I never would have thought of it.”

Dolores meant it.

Vikir did not look impressed.

*Because Cindywendy is the one who uses this trick a few years from now.*

He had simply brought the method forward.

“Now—

we only need to draw out House Bourgeois.”

The money itself was clean.

The method had been underhanded—

but not illegal under current law.

“And thank your friends for helping.”

“I will.”

“But…”

Dolores hesitated.

“Pigi and Sinclaire can’t get dragged into the dangerous part.”

“I already considered that.”

“When the final stage begins—

put them somewhere safe.”

Vikir handed her a tiny bottle.

Dolores stared.

“…Sleeping medicine?”

“Yes.”

Her expression immediately tightened.

Vikir continued.

“The last step before the confrontation is ready.”

“What last step?”

“We turn an insider.”

That was necessary.

House Bourgeois hid almost everything.

Without internal information—

there would be no clean way through.

“We need someone high inside Bourgeois—

but hostile to the current head.”

“Does someone like that exist?”

“Yes.”

“I already checked.”

The head of House Bourgeois.

His enemies.

His political history.

None of it was impossible to uncover.

“Old newspaper reports are enough.”

“Read far enough back in the economic press—

and the answer appears.”

Dolores paused.

*Old economic newspapers…*

That sounded familiar.

Sinclaire had said the same thing about Vikir.

Only recently.

Dolores frowned slightly.

*Where did I hear that?*

Before she could chase the thought—

Night Hound handed her several clippings.

Old economic articles.

**[Economy] Who Will Become Head of House Bourgeois?**

**—The succession contest is intensifying.**

**Leading candidates: eldest son Bartolomeo Bourgeois and second son Damien Bourgeois.**

Another.

**[Economy] Deputy Director Bartolomeo Targeted by Aggressive Tax Investigation**

Accusations.

Embezzlement.

Misappropriation.

Tax evasion.

Then—

**[Economy] Deputy Director Damien Smiles at Daughter’s First-Birthday Celebration**

A warm family article.

Another—

Bartolomeo donating coal in winter.

Another—

Damien accused of abusing authority toward attendants during his daughter’s outing.

The pattern was obvious.

Every negative story about Bartolomeo—

was answered by positive coverage of Bartolomeo.

Every positive story—

was answered by something damaging Damien.

Or vice versa.

Two factions.

Two brothers.

One succession war.

Then—

the final article.

**[Economy] Bloody Shareholders’ Meeting — Winner Decided**

**—Eldest son Bartolomeo selected as next head of House Bourgeois.**

**A broad personnel reorganization follows.**

Among the changes—

the second son Damien was promoted from deputy director to director of the Imperial Money Manufacturing Bureau.

A promotion—

that was also exile.

The younger brother who lost the throne game.

Too capable to discard completely.

Too dangerous to keep close.

House Bourgeois pushed him toward the Imperial Family.

A hostage.

An outsider.

A defeated rival.

Vikir had been watching that “second-place finisher” carefully.

“Damien Bourgeois.”

“Count.”

“Director of the Imperial Money Manufacturing Bureau.”

“He also holds advisory and outside-director positions in several Bourgeois-controlled businesses.”

“But because half his position belongs to the Imperial side—

he cannot interfere freely inside his family.”

Dolores studied the clippings.

“…I know him a little.”

During the University League—

one of the second-round assignments had come from House Bourgeois.

**Tycoon House / Difficulty: ★★★★**

**The Money Manufacturing Bureau has recently issued high-denomination notes, increasing counterfeiting problems.**

**From House Bourgeois’s perspective, select sectors that would benefit from currency reform and propose either a new business or an improvement to an existing one.**

The assignment had been written by Damien himself.

So Dolores had some prior contact with him.

Vikir nodded.

“Good.”

“Now that we have financial visibility—

it’s time to meet Damien.”

“How?”

“He doesn’t auction meal tickets.”

Vikir answered simply.

“We host a party.”

Then he handed Dolores the projected budget.

Her eyes widened.

“…This is for one party?”

“Did you add too many zeros?”

Even the Saintess of one of the Seven Great Houses was stunned.

Vikir intended to spend almost all of Oracle’s astronomical gains—

on a single night.

“It needs to be lavish.”

“Extravagant.”

“So excessive that everyone important in the Imperial Capital talks about it.”

Money—

after all—

was not the goal.

It was only a tool.
