**1,437.**

*Why six?*

*He hit ten every single time before that.*

*Did his concentration suddenly collapse?*

even after her own excellent final sequence.

Vikir's momentum had been too overwhelming.

felt wrong.

“Shame.”

“Back to practice.”

“Still impressive for a commoner.”

“Records don't break that easily.”

“Maybe he was lucky early.”

Bianca waited.

Vikir left.

The remaining spectators disappeared.

Only then—

she walked downrange.

The target looked absurd.

Arrow after arrow had struck almost the same central point.

Some later shots had split earlier shafts.

A thick cluster protruded from the ten-ring—

almost like a single wooden column.

Bianca clicked her tongue.

“…With that little mana.”

“How did he even get into this Academy?”

Not jealousy.

Not hatred.

Competition.

That was what burned.

She remembered seeing Vikir's trained body after an earlier event.

The muscles had not come from casual practice.

*So he really works that hard.*

Archery—

used efficiently—

did not require enormous mana reserves.

A skilled archer could still support a team from range.

*I'm not interested in men.*

*But he is unusual.*

Bianca crouched near the target.

One arrow sat alone.

Six points.

Blue line.

The final shot.

The only miss.

*Why?*

Fatigue?

Loss of concentration?

Possible—

but inconsistent with everything before it.

“Tch.”

“So the last arrow was weak.”

“You might've been a decent rival.”

She turned away.

Then stopped.

“…Hm?”

Bianca's eyes—

trained far beyond ordinary sight—

A tiny shape protruded from the hole made by the six-point arrow.

A thread-thin leg.

An insect.

A mosquito.

Dead.

Pinned precisely by the arrow.

Her gaze moved.

Central cluster.

Six-point arrow.

Mosquito.

Central cluster.

Six-point arrow.

Mosquito.

*That can't be.*

*Training without mana is the real foundation.*

So did his shoulders.

The archery he had learned among the **Ballak**—

continued improving.

He had practiced consistently since leaving the depths.

*I can't let myself be pushed around with a bow.*

Not even by a likely future master of House Usher's archery.

Vikir had lived in the Ballak hunting grounds.

Their pride—

had become partly his.

Then memory softened him.

*How is everyone?*

The Ballak had reportedly abandoned their former settlement—

moving deeper into the dangerous interior.

Cindywendy's latest information was already months old.

the old faces returned.

**Akwilla.**

The Night Fox chieftain.

Argumentative **Ahun**.

His cheerful younger sister **Aheul**.

**Bakilaga**, now a father.

fighting beside—

laughing with—

mourning with.

And one more person.

Rain.

A boat.

A punch.

**Aiyen.**

Still awkward with the Imperial language on the day Vikir left the jungle.

Using hide from the first ox-bear the two of them hunted together.

The memory pulled a faint smile from him.

footsteps.

**Dolores.**

*Annoying.*

Whenever they met—

Tall trees.

Straight road.

Sparse brush.
