Night fell quickly in the jungle.

The heat faded.

Cold crept into the air.

Dolores pushed through the brush.

Exhausted.

Her clothes were torn in several places—

streaked with twigs—

leaves—

mud.

**HP — 38%.**

Then she saw it.

The source of the light.

The smell.

A bed of embers.

And above it—

a coconut pot full of bubbling red stew.

Dolores stared.

“How is this here…?”

Reason told her—

something was wrong.

Her feet—

disagreed.

The smell was overwhelming.

*Someone camped here.*

She searched the darkness.

*No presence waiting close enough for an ambush.*

*A person cooked this—then left in a hurry?*

Under normal circumstances—

Dolores would never touch another person’s food.

But these were not normal circumstances.

*Survive.*

*That is the rule.*

She reached for the pot.

Rustle.

A figure rose from the brush at a distance.

“Wait.”

Vikir.

Far enough away—

that most people would not call it ambush range.

For an archer—

close enough.

Dolores instantly raised a divine shield.

“So there was an ambush!”

Vikir raised his bow.

“….”

“….”

Same academy.

Same uniform.

Still—

competitors.

Eventually—

rankings would make them enemies.

But fighting each other this early—

would benefit neither side.

Silence stretched.

Then—

grrrrrrrr.

Dolores’s stomach betrayed her.

She had eaten nothing all day.

Her face turned red.

Vikir showed no reaction whatsoever.

At last—

Dolores raised both hands.

“…Temporary cooperation?”

She cleared her throat.

“We’re from the same academy. The contest still has a long way to go.”

“Do whatever you want.”

Vikir lowered the bow.

Dolores frowned at the indifference—

but accepted it.

Then came the real problem.

“Um.”

Her eyes returned to the pot.

“Is there any stew left?”

A pause.

“If there is…”

“Could I have some?”

The Saintess.

Colosseo’s student council president.

Reduced—

to asking for leftovers in a jungle.

Dolores rushed to recover some dignity.

“Not for free!”

“I can heal you. Buff you. In an environment like this, having a healer—”

“Don’t need it.”

“…Right, mutually beneficial—what?”

Dolores stared.

Then she saw his suit.

**HP — 100%.**

Perfect.

No visible injury.

No fatigue.

Inside this green hell.

*How?*

“Hey.”

Granola’s head appeared from the bushes.

“Vikir.”

He looked scandalized.

“Do you remember who she is?”

“She is the Saintess of Quovadis.”

Silence.

“The student council president.”

Silence.

“And even if you ignore all of that—how do you refuse a beautiful woman who is asking you for help?”

For once—

Granola had said something most people would find reasonable.

Vikir scratched one ear.

Annoyed.

Three hands emerged behind Granola.

“Secret friend.”

“Let’s be quiet.”

“Quiet.”

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

They dragged Granola backward into the bushes.

The silence returned.

Dolores finally sighed.

Raised her hands higher.

“I am not the Saintess of Quovadis.”

Vikir watched.

“I am not Colosseo’s student council president.”

A breath.

“I’m just a hungry little lamb.”

Vikir paused.

Dolores remembered another night.

Another conversation.

“Who are you?”

“Why have you come here so late?”

And the Night Hound’s answer—

*Just a lost lamb.*

She had remembered those words.

Now—

she borrowed them.

Vikir understood exactly where they came from.

He lowered his bow completely.

Then moved aside.

Dolores sat beside the fire.

“Thank you.”

Vikir filled a bowl himself.

Red stew.

A few small mushrooms added to her serving.

Dolores took one mouthful.

Her expression changed.

“…Delicious.”

Then another.

And another.

She stopped counting—

after thirteen bowls.

Only when she was full did embarrassment return.

“I ended up like this because I met Hohenheim right after the contest began.”

Oraorus Hohenheim.

Mage Tower’s ace.

The man later called—

**Hohenheim of a Thousand Flames.**

Her teammates had protected her.

A healer falling first—

would have destroyed their whole party.

But Hohenheim—

an exceptional flame mage—

fought alone and moved too quickly to pin down.

Dolores healed.

Buffed.

Tried to hold the group together.

It failed.

“My four teammates were eliminated.”

She looked toward the fire.

“They chased him and he picked them off one by one.”

Granola listened carefully.

So did the Baskerville triplets.

Information from a third-year ace—

during a live League—

was valuable.

Vikir merely nodded.

“Is that so?”

Dolores stared at him.

*Why is he so calm?*

*How does he know how to survive here?*

*Where did he get these ingredients?*

Vikir looked as comfortable—

as though Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain were his own yard.

Dolores could not know—

how much time he had spent here before regression.

Or the two years he had actually lived in these mountains after it.

Or the fresh ingredients and seasonings stored through the spatial function of his ring.

Vikir kept cooking.

Kept fanning the pot with a broad leaf.

At night—

heat and scent traveled even more clearly.

Dolores watched him.

“You’re still baiting people?”

“Yes.”

“What if someone strong comes?”

She counted them.

“Hohenheim.”

“Bakilaga.”

“Lovegood.”

If the prey was too strong—

the hunter became prey.

Vikir dropped another mushroom into the pot.

“Doesn’t matter.”

Boom.

The brush ahead vanished beneath a wave of fire.

Ash drifted.

A man walked through the burned clearing.

“What is that smell?”

Mage Tower’s prodigy.

One of the strongest contenders in the League.

A predator who had already eliminated more than fifty opponents.

Oraorus Hohenheim—

had taken the bait.
