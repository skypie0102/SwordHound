BDISSEM revealed her true form in the concentrated saltwater.

“Hehehe.”

“Surprised?”

She winked.

The childlike face was gone.

In its place—

a fully mature woman.

Vikir was genuinely impressed.

“…A seaweed dryad.”

“So they really exist.”

He had heard many stories of forest dryads during his time in Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Naturally—

he had assumed most dryads belonged to forests.

He had been wrong.

Seaweed forests existed in the deep ocean too.

Just as beastfolk descended from many different animal lineages—

dryads could descend from many different plants.

BDISSEM—

kelp.

Wakame.

Seaweed.

“Hm.”

“This is worse than expected.”

Dragging her into the saltwater—

had been a terrible move.

Ordinary plants withered under salt.

Seaweed—

thrived.

THWACK!

Vikir kicked away a seaweed chain.

Hard nodes along its surface struck his foot like bowling balls.

*Annoying.*

He ripped leaves from his arm.

Tried to rise toward the surface.

BDISSEM blocked him.

“Hehehe.”

“Where are you going?”

“You finally came in.”

“Stay and play.”

Tough seaweed stalks moved like sea serpents.

From the bottom of the eel pools—

black growths rose.

Wrapped Vikir’s ankles.

Above—

enormous leaves crossed one another.

Horizontal.

Vertical.

Diagonal.

Layer after layer.

Nets.

Traps.

A cage made of living seaweed.

Then spears formed.

Sharp.

Curved.

They lunged toward the immobilized Vikir.

Bind with nets.

Pierce with spears.

Then—

once contact was made—

suppress mana and strength.

BDISSEM’s perfect combat pattern.

THUD!

THUD!

THUD!

Blood.

Flesh.

Organs.

The water darkened.

BDISSEM narrowed her eyes.

“…Hm?”

The seaweed spears had not struck Vikir.

At the last moment—

he had grabbed a passing Gulper Eel.

Used it as a shield.

THUD!

THUD!

More spears hit the eel instead.

“You—!”

“Those take years to raise!”

“D'Ordume and Souaré will lose their minds!”

BDISSEM bubbled with rage.

Literally.

Bubbles poured from her body.

Oxygen.

Vikir watched.

And understood.

*So that’s why they protect her.*

BDISSEM did more than produce restraints.

She also supplied oxygen to Nouvelle Vague.

Her body could consume sulfur—

steam—

toxic gases—

and convert them into breathable air.

Without BDISSEM—

Nouvelle Vague could not function.

The Flubber-sealed prison had no other meaningful source of oxygen.

“…You’re more useful every minute.”

Vikir dropped the wounded eel.

Jumped onto another.

A seaweed spear rose from below—

piercing through the eel’s upper and lower jaw.

Vikir abandoned that one too.

Dropped to the bottom of the breeding pool.

“Hehehe.”

“Going deeper?”

“That’s suicide.”

Seaweed nets sealed the surface.

Vikir was trapped at the bottom.

Escape—

one issue.

Breathing—

more immediate.

Then—

whoosh.

Something strange happened around Vikir’s right hand.

Water began moving in the wrong direction.

Drawn inward.

BDISSEM frowned.

*A leak?*

No.

There was no reason for current to form there.

Then—

RUMBLE!

The suction intensified.

Water rushed toward Vikir.

Seaweed bent.

BDISSEM herself began sliding.

“What?!”

Instinct screamed at her.

*Do not let that hand touch you.*

**Starvation Drought.**

The Blood Daylily power that drained moisture from anything containing water.

Vikir began drying the pool itself.

Water vanished around his hand.

Fresh water rushed in to replace it.

That water vanished too.

Again.

Again.

A violent current formed.

Then—

Beelzebub flared.

Aura traveled through the spinning water.

Against it.

Toward BDISSEM.

“…!”

A small—

dense—

heavy black sun rose from the floor.

**Black Sun.**

Normally—

Black Sun destroyed.

It did not drag things inward.

But underwater—

combined with Starvation Drought—

the technique changed.

The collapsing current pulled everything toward the center.

BDISSEM finally understood.

*He wanted this environment.*

*He wanted the water.*

The black sphere was not truly one object.

It was countless fangs.

Anything drawn into the center—

would be shredded beyond recovery.

“AAAAH!”

BDISSEM threw every available seaweed growth into the current.

The vegetation vanished.

Torn.

Pulverized.

BDISSEM restraints normally nullified mana and physical force.

Against this combination—

they looked like cheap paper.

Still—

the sacrifice had an effect.

Enough seaweed entered the Black Sun—

and the pull weakened slightly.

BDISSEM escaped.

Shot toward the surface.

SPLASH!

She broke through.

Gasps.

Pale face.

“I have to go.”

“Tell D'Ordume.”

“Tell Souaré—”

Then stumbled.

“…?”

The world spun.

Her legs tangled.

THUD.

*Why?*

*What happened?*

*Why won’t my body move?*

*I need to leave.*

SPLASH.

“You absorbed a lot of water.”
