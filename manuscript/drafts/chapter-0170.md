Newspapers spread through Colosseo Academy.

The Golden Week volunteers—

freshly returned from Indulgentia—

stared at the evening edition.

“Wow.”

“They already published it?”

“We were there when history happened.”

Tudor looked impressed.

Sancho frowned.

“So the club president declared war?”

Pigi shook his head.

“It didn't feel like that at all.”

“This article is exaggerating.”

Bianca nodded.

“That's journalism.”

“If they want views—”

“they make the headline sharper.”

Then Sinclaire turned.

“Vikir!”

“What do you think?”

Everyone looked toward the same place.

Vikir walked several steps behind the group—

reading the paper.

“…”

His expression—

quiet.

Lonely.

Almost bleak.

Bianca glanced back.

“Why is he acting like that?”

“Hey.”

“You coming or not?”

“We're leaving you behind.”

Tudor jabbed her in the ribs.

“Have some consideration.”

“What?”

“Consideration for what?”

“For how Vikir must feel right now.”

Bianca stared.

“What would I know about that?”

Tudor lowered his voice.

“Yesterday.”

“Vikir wet himself in front of everyone.”

Sancho added—

“Right on the club president.”

Pigi's face tightened.

“…There was a lot.”

Bianca grimaced.

“Gross.”

Sinclaire looked back toward Vikir.

“He must feel awful.”

The group exchanged glances.

Tudor nodded gravely.

“He probably wants to be alone.”

“I would too.”

Sancho folded his arms.

“Careless consolation can make embarrassment worse.”

Bianca snorted.

“Or maybe he just needs to pee again.”

Pigi shuddered.

“Please don't say that.”

Sinclaire murmured—

almost wistfully—

“…I wouldn't mind even if Vikir did it to me.”

Everyone turned.

“?”

Tudor blinked.

“…That's not really the point.”

Sinclaire's face flushed.

Tudor clapped his hands.

“Anyway.”

“Let's give him some space tonight.”

“Pigi.”

“Sleep in our room.”

Pigi looked surprised.

“Can I?”

“Sure.”

“We'll finish the rest of the work together.”

“…Thanks, Sancho.”

Over the ten days of volunteer service—

Pigi had clearly grown closer to the others.

The group naturally moved ahead—

leaving Vikir alone.

Vikir watched them go.

*They're leaving first.*

*Perfect.*

He quietly turned down a side path.

◆◆◆

Back in his dormitory—

Vikir sat alone at his desk.

A small wreath lay before him.

Beside it—

a stick of incense burned.

He closed his eyes.

A silent prayer.

For **Peri**.

A conversation from the farewell—

with Dolores—

passed through his memory.

*Weren't you too cold to that child?*

*Did you let her kiss you?*

*No.*

*Not “couldn't.” You didn't! She wanted to kiss you that badly. You could've accepted it once.*

Vikir frowned.

*If I'd known this would happen—*

*I should have waited for the kiss.*

The thought sat badly with him.

“…I need to hunt harder.”

When the Age of Destruction came—

children like Peri would die in numbers too large to count.

To stop that—

Vikir needed to kill demons more aggressively.

Demons.

Traitors.

All of them.

“And for that…”

“I should inspect the spoils.”

He reached inside his clothes.

Then stopped.

The Chinese raw resumes here after a visible omission.

From the aligned witness—

the missing object was clear.

Four black leather coverings.

One from **Ephebo**.

One from **Hebe**.

One from **Pedo**.

One from **Geronto**.

Each piece pulsed faintly—

as if threads moved beneath dead skin.

Vikir brought them together.

The four pieces joined.

“…So that's what these were.”

A single large mask formed.

Part hood.

Part bandanna.

Black.

Oversized.

Two sharp points rose where a dog's cropped ears might have been.

Vikir recognized it.

A demonic artifact—

in the same broad class of impossible relic as **Beelzebub**.

> **Picaresque Mask** / Mask
>
> **Kin-Slaying** +0  
> **Human Face, Beast Heart** — Off

Vikir had once read about an artifact like this in an old Eastern bestiary.

*The more members of its own kind the wearer kills—*

*the stronger the mask becomes.*

If worn by a human—

it rewarded the killing of humans.

It also possessed strange recovery and mana-restoration effects.

*Though I remember there being side effects.*

Vikir put it on.

“…!”

Change spread through his body immediately.

The wounds left by the battle with Dantalian—

and the physical exhaustion underneath them—

rapidly disappeared.

Far beyond what **Bog Salamander** regeneration could do.

*This is absurdly effective.*

Bog Salamander regeneration repaired damaged flesh well.

But it did not truly restore stamina—

or mental fatigue.

The mask did.

Simply wearing it—

felt like replenishing what battle had drained.

Then—

one problem appeared.

“…Woof?”

Vikir's eye twitched.

His body was shrinking.

“Haah—haah—”

He rushed to the mirror.

A black dog stared back.

A ball of glossy dark fur—

panicking in the middle of the room.

*That's me?*

There were sayings about becoming a dog after drinking.

About being worse than a dog.

Less than a dog.

Living like a dog.

Metaphors.

Vikir—

was now literally a dog.

Glossy black coat.

Sharp little teeth.

Round eyes.

Pink paw pads.

A completely ridiculous puppy.

*Is this the side effect!?*

*No.*

*Absolutely not.*

He tried to shout.

“Hrrrng—”

Only a small distressed puppy noise came out.

The artifact status had changed.

> **Picaresque Mask** / Mask
>
> **Kin-Slaying** +0  
> **Human Face, Beast Heart** — On

Vikir pawed frantically at his neck.

The mask was still there—

merged close to the skin.

The moment he consciously tried to remove it—

the artifact slid free as though responding to intent.

> **Picaresque Mask** / Mask
>
> **Kin-Slaying** +0  
> **Human Face, Beast Heart** — Off

Vikir returned to human form.

Naked.

His clothes—

which had not transformed with him—

lay on the floor.

He looked at them.

Then the mirror.

“…Convenient before a bath.”

He put the mask on again.

This time—

he stayed human.

The distinction was clear.

On.

Off.

The transformation responded to intent.

Vikir removed the mask again.

*Turning into a dog whenever I choose.*

*That's less a side effect than an extra ability.*

For Night Hound operations—

it could be extremely useful.

Exceptional recovery.

Escape.

Disguise.

A way to avoid human eyes.

Then another practical question occurred to him.

*Does Colosseo allow pets in the dormitory?*

He checked the rules.

Fortunately—

yes.

Students kept owls.

Pigeons.

Turtles.

Hamsters.

Only large animals likely to injure people or damage buildings—

bears—

elephants—

things of that scale—

were forbidden.

Crack.

Vikir looked up.

“…?”

Something had broken.

The sound came from the coat hanging in the corner.

He searched a pocket.

Then found it.

**Madam Eight-Legs's egg.**

Cracks were spreading across the black sphere.

“Right.”

“This took Dantalian's spear.”

Vikir had always known the egg was bizarrely hard and elastic.

He had not expected it to withstand a Demon King's strike.

Apparently—

it had not done so without damage.

Crack.

Crack-crack!

The shell split farther.

*Even Madam Eight-Legs needed acid to dissolve her own eggs.*

*That's how impact-resistant they are.*

Vikir watched.

Then—

pop!

The shell broke open.

Something inside opened its eyes.

For the first time—

it saw Vikir.

“…?”

Vikir tilted his head.

Inside the broken shell—

a tiny black thing—

like a burnt crumb of bread—

stared back.

A creature he had never seen before.

Vikir tilted his head again.

The hatchling copied him.

Tilt.

Vikir's posture stiffened.

Small or not—

this was the offspring of **Madam Eight-Legs**.

The monster of the flood-haunted Red and Black Mountains—

a creature Vikir had nearly died fighting more than once.

Then—

the little black ball jumped.

Straight into his arms.

It rubbed its face against him.

And made a completely unexpected sound.

[Woof—hah—whine—]

Like a newborn wolf pup.
