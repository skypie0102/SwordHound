# Chapter 409: End game (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/409.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 407 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Nouvelle Vague, BDISSEM, Gate of Good and Evil, Flubber, Gulper Eel, D’Ordume, Souaré, Sucker Shark, Winter Sword Orwell, Winston Smith, and Orca. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

Pandemonium.

No other word described Nouvelle Vague anymore.

Only hours—

minutes—

seconds earlier—

everything had been normal.

Guards held weapons.

Prisoners wore restraints that stripped away mana and strength.

Then—

at a moment almost nobody could identify—

snap.

Handcuffs vanished.

Shackles vanished.

Cell bars withered away.

Across the entire prison.

Guards stared.

Prisoners stared.

Nobody understood.

*What happened?*

*Is this only my cell?*

*Only this floor?*

It was not.

Every BDISSEM restraint in Nouvelle Vague—

gone.

The chains that had crushed wrists.

The shackles heavy enough to tear ankles.

The bars that had made escape impossible.

All disappeared at once.

As though years of suffering had been a dream.

Then prisoners felt it.

Mana.

Strength.

Returning.

Old anger—

old desire—

old hatred—

all reignited.

The first to move were the new arrivals still undergoing induction.

“What is this?”

“My mana is moving!”

“The BDISSEM cuffs are gone!”

“Hahaha!”

“I don’t know why—

but this is perfect!”

“Die!”

Prisoners crossing the blade bridge turned on the guards.

Others rose with them.

“Kill the guards first!”

“Burn the place!”

“Break everything!”

“Open the Gate of Good and Evil!”

“Fifth floor!”

“There are Gulper Eels on the fifth floor!”

“Take them and escape!”

Years of living like insects had dulled them.

Freedom restored the old instincts quickly.

Then—

BOOM!

Bodies exploded across the induction ground.

D’Ordume and Souaré stood in the sulfur haze.

Each had a blood-covered prisoner beneath one foot.

D’Ordume threw away a corpse.

“Wake up.”

“We are ten thousand meters beneath the sea.”

“Even if you overpower us—

where exactly will you go?”

“You have numbers now.”

“Reinforcements will come.”

“And then?”

He was right.

Freedom from restraints did not create a route to the surface.

And if Flubber abandoned the waterproofing—

the entire prison would flood.

Souaré shook gore from her boot.

“Hehehe.”

“I’ll give you an opportunity.”

“Help us suppress this.”

“I’ll make you Sucker Sharks.”

“Less labor.”

“Reduced sentences.”

“Maybe even junior-guard treatment.”

“First come—

first served.”

The two Wardens’ presence crushed the riot’s momentum.

In seconds—

they had turned dozens of prisoners into meat.

And behind them—

the Gate of Good and Evil towered like an impossible wall.

Some prisoners swallowed.

The offer was tempting.

A lighter workload.

Better food.

A sentence reduction.

*The gate cannot be opened anyway.*

*Maybe switching sides is smarter.*

Rage cooled.

Reason returned.

Veteran prisoners understood the reality better than anyone.

The Gate of Good and Evil was not something they could simply cross.

D’Ordume and Souaré saw the hesitation.

Perhaps—

the riot would end more easily than expected.

Then someone shouted:

“The key!”

“The front-gate key is here!”

Something flew into the center of the battlefield.

A broken blade fragment.

White near the base.

Black through the middle.

Red near the top.

A spiral pattern running along it.

THUNK!

It embedded itself high overhead.

Several knowledgeable prisoners stared.

“That’s…!”

“A gate key!”

A fragment of **Winter Sword Orwell**.

The legendary sword once wielded by Colosseo headmaster Winston Smith.

D’Ordume and Souaré went pale.

“…The key?”

“How is that here?!”

Long ago—

Winston and Warden Orca had cooperated to capture a certain prisoner.

Two keys had been divided afterward—

one associated with the surface—

one with the prison below.

And now—

one was here.

“Who threw it?!”

Souaré scanned the riot.

Impossible.

Too many bodies.

Too much chaos.

D’Ordume shouted:

“It changes nothing!”

“Even if you open the Gate of Good and Evil—

there is nowhere to—”

ROOOOAR!

The prisoners drowned him out.

The hesitation vanished.

Collective madness needed only a spark.

“It’s the key!”

“We can open the gate!”

“That gets us back to the surface!”

“Kill them all!”

The fragment became more than metal.

To desperate prisoners—

it became salvation.

D’Ordume shouted again.

“Idiots!”

“That is only half a key!”

“Even if the gate opens—

the ocean will come through!”

“You are at the bottom of the sea!”

Nobody listened.

Souaré stepped forward.

“Stop wasting breath.”

“Do you think these people came here because they were good at rational thought?”

A prisoner charged.

She kicked him dead.

Then pulled the hammer from her back.

The battle began in earnest.

Elite guards slaughtered prisoners.

“No restraint!”

“Kill them!”

“They’ve already crossed the line!”

Most lower-floor prisoners—

even with their strength restored—

had been starved and worked nearly to death for years.

Numbers alone could not overcome trained senior guards.

Especially with D’Ordume and Souaré at the front.

D’Ordume’s axe-blades created tornadoes of slaughter.

Souaré melted ground into boiling magma.

Around them—

a long red boundary formed.

A line no prisoner crossed.

The guards’ morale surged.

“As expected!”

“The Wardens are unstoppable!”

“Nouvelle Vague’s twin pillars!”

“We can hold!”

Then—

a terrified sentry screamed from deeper inside the prison.

“LEVEL NINE!”

“THE LEVEL-NINE PRISONERS ARE COMING UP!”

The cheering stopped.
