# Chapter 410: End game (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/410.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 408 is the verified shifted secondary witness. **Megidio** and **Snow White of the Killing Clan** are source-local aligned-witness forms pending direct Fandom confirmation. Established terminology controls D’Ordume, Souaré, Bastille, BDISSEM, Orca, and Death Roll.

Level Nine.

The prisoners from the deepest established prison floor—

were free.

BDISSEM restraints had been the only reliable means of suppressing them.

Now those restraints were gone.

Every one of these inmates carried more than a thousand life sentences.

Threats not merely to Nouvelle Vague—

but to Imperial security itself.

They possessed overwhelming violence.

And—

in many cases—

minds just as dangerous.

The instant Level Nine joined the uprising—

the battlefield changed.

“These monsters are too strong!”

“The other prisoners are organizing around them!”

“Damn it!”

“They have formations now?!”

“Don’t retreat!”

“If we break here, it’s over!”

“Kill summoners and black mages first!”

The guards struggled even to hold formation.

Several Level-Nine prisoners understood mercenary warfare—

command—

tactics—

and logistics.

The guards’ encirclement collapsed in real time.

Bastille shouted:

“Warden Orca!”

“Where is Warden Orca?!”

“He has not returned yet!”

Bastille bit his lip until it bled.

That left only Nouvelle Vague’s two frontline Wardens.

D’Ordume tore through another prisoner.

“Do not lose your nerve!”

“They’ve been weakened by years of—”

CLAMP.

His axe stopped.

A bare hand had caught the spinning blade.

D’Ordume stared.

“…Megidio.”

“Level Nine.”

A red-haired woman grinned.

Fire burned in her eyes.

Then—

BOOM!

Her fist smashed D’Ordume across the face.

Blood sprayed.

He stumbled backward—

spitting broken teeth.

“Damn it.”

“So the troublesome ones really escaped.”

“What happened to BDISSEM…?”

Souaré laughed from nearby.

“Getting beaten by a prisoner?”

“Embarrassing.”

Then claws raked across her own cheek.

A pale-skinned girl stood before her—

seven huge wolf-shadows swaying behind her.

Souaré smiled despite the blood.

“Well, well.”

“**Snow White of the Killing Clan**.”

“I knew you’d be among the first to move.”

“And among the first to come for me.”

One by one—

Level-Nine monsters gathered.

Every one of them had been infamous on the surface.

In the old Warring States Era—

some could have destroyed small countries alone.

D’Ordume and Souaré stood back-to-back.

“…This may be where I die.”

Souaré glanced at him.

“Not the worst place.”

“Good weather.”

“I even shaved today.”

“I’ll die after you.”

“I want to watch that ugly face get smashed first.”

“Hehehe.”

“Thirty years.”

“We’ve been competing since we were junior guards.”

“And that’s your final line?”

“What?”

“Were you hoping for a confession?”

“…?”

“Just kidding~”

Then both transformed.

CRACK.

CRUNCH.

Black iron scales covered D’Ordume.

His jaws extended.

Blade-like teeth showed.

His massive body expanded further—

into the form of a monstrous saltwater crocodile.

Souaré awakened the dwarf and elf blood in her body.

Her ears sharpened.

Her senses intensified.

Her temperature rose until it could melt metal.

The Level-Nine prisoners answered with laughter.

“D’Ordume!”

“I counted every day until I could kill you!”

“Souaré!”

“I’ll cut off the leg that used to step on me!”

“Where is Orca?!”

“Bring out that old bastard!”

The guards rushed to protect the Wardens.

“Support them!”

Bastille led senior officers forward.

His two-handed greatsword forced Level-Nine prisoners into tighter positions.

“Well done, Bastille!”

“Buy us time!”

D’Ordume decapitated another prisoner.

Blood covered him.

Then he swung his tail.

BOOM!

Stalactites shattered.

Heavy rocks blasted through the crowd.

D’Ordume extended six pairs of axe-blades from his body.

Aura lengthened each edge—

until the weapons resembled guillotines.

“DIE!”

He spun.

**Death Roll.**

A crocodile’s hunting method—

magnified into a battlefield technique.

A black cyclone swept horizontally across the floor.

Anything caught inside—

was shredded.

“Death Roll!”

“Back away!”

“He can’t distinguish allies like that!”

“Push prisoners toward it!”

“Hold formation!”

Even the Level-Nine criminals avoided the vortex.

Stone pillars became dust.

Boulders were shaved flat.

Everyone moved aside.

Everyone—

except one man.

A worn guard uniform.

A silver hibiscus insignia marking Major rank.

Hat pulled low.

The mysterious Major stood directly in Death Roll’s path.

“Major!”

“Are you insane?!”

“D’Ordume!”

“You’ll kill your own officer!”

The crowd split around him.

The man calmly removed the silver rank insignia from his chest.

Clink.

It rolled across the floor.

Then—

one arm moved downward.

One simple motion.

A small blade extending from the wrist.

BOOM!

D’Ordume’s enormous spinning body slammed into the ground.

His head struck stone—

bounced—

then fell still.

Silence.

One attack.

That was all.

Nouvelle Vague froze.

Prisoners.

Guards.

Everyone.

In that terrible quiet—

the man wiped blood from his blade with the collar of his uniform.

His presence swallowed the battlefield.

Then he stepped forward—

placed one foot on D’Ordume’s head—

and spoke.

“…I repay my grudges.”

The Night Hound—

had returned to Nouvelle Vague.
