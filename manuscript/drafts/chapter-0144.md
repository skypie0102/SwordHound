The ten-day holiday began.

A weekend.

The Academy's founding anniversary.

The Rune Religion's Resurrection Festival.

Arrange the class schedule cleverly enough around those dates—

and the students were left with a Golden Week longer than an ordinary week.

Naturally, everyone was delighted to receive such a long break so early in the semester.

First-years used the holiday to deepen new friendships.

Some toured the Imperial Capital together.

Others invited classmates home.

Second- and third-years used the time to catch up on assignments—

prepare for examinations—

or retreat somewhere quiet to recover from the exhaustion of Academy life.

Fourth-years had graduation and employment looming over them.

For them—

weekdays and holidays were equally full of study.

And yet—

there was one first-year who had no choice but to work.

**Vikir.**

The hound of the Ironblood Sword Family.

Squeeze—

twist—

squeeze—

On the first day of volunteer service—

Vikir was wringing out a cleaning rag.

His assigned location stood on the outskirts of the Imperial Capital.

A massive orphanage called **Indulgentia**.

With so many children living there—

the work never ended.

Cleaning.

Laundry.

Cooking.

Portioning meals.

Washing dishes.

Moving supplies.

Repairing facilities.

Playing with the children.

And everything else that came with keeping such a large institution running.

On-campus service was easy by comparison.

There—

professional staff handled the real work and students merely assisted.

Off-campus service was different.

The volunteers did everything themselves.

While the rest of the Academy spent the holiday resting or playing—

Vikir would be living here and doing every unpleasant job the orphanage could give him.

Of course—

he was not alone.

“Hey, Vikir!”

Tudor grinned.

“You'd better be grateful! We gave up our holiday to come help you!”

Sancho shrugged.

“The Academy requires us to complete volunteer hours under its *noblesse oblige* policy anyway.”

“Doing them all at once isn't a bad idea.”

Pigi nodded vigorously.

“I-I like volunteering too! So I don't mind!”

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

The three had followed him out of loyalty.

Vikir gave them a quiet nod.

“Thank you.”

All three froze.

Vikir rarely said things like that.

Then—

“Ahem.”

“A-Ahem.”

“Hehehe.”

Tudor, Sancho, and Pigi grinned as they began wringing out rags beside him.

Vikir watched them.

A faint smile crossed his face.

They tried to act like adults—

but they were still only eighteen.

A simple word of thanks from a friend was enough to improve their entire day.

Vikir himself was different.

His soul had already been weathered by too many years.

The weight he carried made an easy smile difficult.

As he wiped down a corridor filled with running children—

his thoughts never stopped.

*It made the papers after all.*

He remembered the Academy newspaper from yesterday.

Then the stories that spread through the Imperial Capital afterward.

> **WHO IS THE ‘NIGHT HOUND,’ THE VILLAIN DRIVING THE IMPERIAL CAPITAL INTO FEAR!?**
>
> Late last night, near dawn, a mysterious figure appeared and reduced an orphanage to ruins…
>
> Strangely, the attacker left the valuables in the vault untouched…

Another headline followed.

> **THE FAITHFUL HOUSE IN CRISIS — CAN QUOVADIS CONTINUE LIKE THIS!?**
>
> Orphanages, monasteries, hospitals, and other welfare institutions administered by Quovadis have suffered a string of attacks…
>
> At the same time, the Old Testament Faction and New Testament Faction inside Quovadis are engaged in an increasingly bitter struggle…
>
> Critics claim internal political conflict has delayed the response to these incidents…

And another.

> **OLD TESTAMENT VS. NEW TESTAMENT — IS QUOVADIS INFIGHTING EMBOLDENING THE ATTACKER!?**
>
> The conflict began long ago with the dispute between Cardinal Humbert L. Quovadis of the Old Testament Faction and Cardinal Martin Luther of the New Testament Faction…
>
> Among their oldest disputes is whether the order should sell certificates of remission and indulgences…

Then—

> **DOES THE ‘NIGHT HOUND’ REALLY EXIST AS A VILLAIN?**
>
> Citizens across the Imperial Capital have grown anxious over the mysterious attacks…
>
> Yet some continue to question whether a single villain truly lies behind them…
>
> Why have welfare institutions tied specifically to Quovadis been attacked again and again?
>
> Is this really the work of an outside criminal—
>
> or the result of the long political struggle between the Old and New Testament Factions?
>
> Whether the truth is factional conflict or the appearance of a genuine monster remains to be seen…

Vikir had reviewed all of those reports.

Thanks to Pigi—

collecting newspapers from around the Imperial Capital had become easy.

Vikir combined that material with the intelligence Cindywendy sent him.

The conclusion was simple.

Every place the Night Hound had attacked—

by coincidence—

had been connected to Quovadis.

*I've only been following the scent of demons.*

*If that keeps leading me to the same organization—*

*then something is wrong.*

Vikir thought about the people he had killed so far.

Unexplained wealth.

The bodies of young children.

And killers whose faces rotted away after death.

They wore the outer shell of religious institutions.

But something demonic was hidden underneath.

Just as it had been with **Seth Le Baskerville**.

*…And the most suspicious person of all is inside this orphanage.*

Vikir raised his eyes while he cleaned.

A portrait hung on the corridor wall.

A gentle-looking middle-aged man.

Handsome.

Sculpted features.

The kind of face that must have attracted endless attention when he was younger.

He even resembled a famous stage actor who had retired not long ago.

The plaque beneath the portrait read:

> **Quilt Rune Indulgentia**
>
> Head of House Indulgentia.
>
> Director of Indulgentia Orphanage.

House Indulgentia was a collateral family of Quovadis—

and a clerical house within the same Rune faith.

Quilt served both as head of the family and as director of what was likely the largest orphanage in the Imperial Capital.

But none of that mattered to Vikir.

*…Only one thing matters.*

This man—

was his next assassination target.

Golden Week gave Vikir plenty of time.

He had already infiltrated his target's stronghold naturally.

All that remained was to act.

Using cleaning work as an excuse—

Vikir moved from place to place while carefully studying the building.

*This is far too sturdy for an orphanage.*

As he cleaned—

he estimated the number of reinforcing bars inside the stone columns.

The structure was strong enough to serve as a military facility.

Its method of taking in children was unusual too.

Rather than receiving children through smaller regional orphanages—

Indulgentia distributed notices and encouraged unaccompanied children to come directly.

Its facilities were excellent.

Its welfare programs had a good reputation.

Children arrived voluntarily from many places.

That made it extremely difficult to know exactly how many entered—

or how many later disappeared.

A zero could vanish from the records.

Or two.

No ordinary auditor would immediately notice.

*The more orphans an institution reports, the more subsidy money it receives.*

*No one expects an orphanage to deliberately underreport its numbers.*

Ordinary institutions inflated enrollment to receive more public funding.

So audits were designed to catch the opposite problem—

fewer real children than the books claimed.

But demons had different incentives.

For them—

underreporting the number of children was useful.

It created unrecorded victims.

Children who could disappear without anyone outside the building knowing they had ever been there.

Vikir had already noticed something disturbing.

The actual number of children at Indulgentia was far higher than the official records suggested.

People praised Quilt for taking in more children than he was required to support.

Vikir saw something else.

*Runaways.*

*Children with no relatives.*

*Children whose births were poorly registered—or never registered at all.*

*The hardest people in the Empire to trace.*

It had taken only one day for Vikir to find multiple irregularities.

And all of them were surrounded by the same dense demonic stench.

His suspicion hardened.

*Tonight—*

*I'll begin spilling blood.*

Just as he finished forming the plan—

“Um…”

A voice interrupted him.

“?”

Vikir turned.

The person standing there was unexpected.

Short white hair.

Soft and fluffy enough to resemble a puppy's fur.

Even on holiday—

she wore her Academy uniform neatly.

Vikir knew her.

Every first-year at Colosseo knew her.

**Sinclaire.**

Top student of the Hot Department.

For some reason—

she had come to volunteer here too.
