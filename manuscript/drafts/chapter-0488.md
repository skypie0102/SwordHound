[There are more than sixty demons beyond that Gate—]

[stronger than me.]

The words hit like a hammer.

For one instant—

darkness swallowed Vikir’s vision.

He knew Andras was taunting him.

But not lying.

Because the gazes beyond the portals—

were already touching him.

Like sunlight focused through a magnifying glass—

skin began to burn.

Like countless insects crawling through his organs—

his body recoiled.

Sixty-two presences.

Watching.

Each gaze—

a spear driven into body and soul.

An ordinary person might have coughed blood and died from only one.

The shadows beyond the portals grew clearer.

A serpent capable of swallowing nebulae.

A sovereign of insects.

A king of vampires.

A venom-spitting scorpion.

A beautiful woman hiding something unknowable.

A fish vast enough to devour deserts.

A ruler of all that rots.

A spider wrapped around mountains.

A being walking on the ocean floor.

A giant splitting the earth.

A lion drinking fire.

A bird covering the sky.

An executioner of graveyards.

A corpse made from corruption itself.

Tentacles reaching impossibly deep into flesh.

And more.

Far more.

Beings with ominous names—

turning toward this world.

Interested.

Hungry.

A world where the Gate of Destruction had not fully consumed everything.

A world still rich with things to ruin.

Vikir clenched his teeth.

Several shadows had already noticed this timeline.

And some—

especially malignant—

were preparing to cross.

Their destructive intent dwarfed anything Vikir remembered from the Age of Destruction.

Andras chuckled.

[The sixty-two on the other side.]

[Absolute beings I cannot compete with.]

[Among them are nobles so high above me—]

[I would not dare kiss even the shadow they cast.]

“….”

At the largest portal—

behind the monster armies—

stood one shadow deeper than every other darkness.

A banner appeared.

Written in the language of an ancient, dead demonic hegemony.

**Baal.**

The shadow beneath that name felt heavier than the rest.

Decarabia trembled against Vikir’s chest.

[That one…]

[The first among the seventy-two.]

[Something like the mother of demonkind.]

[The Mother of Destruction.]

[If that existence crosses—]

[this world ends.]

[One—]

[and all.]

Vikir finally understood an old exchange between Decarabia and Belial.

*Are you prepared to bear the anger of your own kind?*

*The anger of the existence that is both one and all?*

*…That answer is sealed forever by oath.*

Perhaps Belial had been warning Decarabia about what waited after the Ten Corpses.

Andras spoke.

[Building the Gate of Destruction means building a bridge.]

[The seventy-two demons of Hell recruit forces from their own territories.]

[Then cross.]

“….”

[The Ten Corpses were the vanguard.]

[We completed our role—]

[then withdrew.]

[You died believing the war was over.]

[Only after that did the other sixty-two arrive.]

He laughed.

[You should be grateful you died first.]

Andras looked directly at Vikir.

[It is not too late.]

[Tell me who my brother is.]

[Tell me where he is.]

[I will close these portals now.]

[I would rather solve this within my own authority than involve those above me.]

[Interference across worlds is complicated.]

“….”

[This may not be the world you were originally born in.]

[But surely it feels more like home now.]

[Would you let this one become like the other?]

Dark fog spread from Andras.

Within it—

faces formed.

Camus.

*Stop trying to carry everything alone.*

Dolores.

*Everyone here follows you.*

Aiyen.

*I’ll keep watching. Go rest.*

Sinclaire.

*Stop fighting. Bro has enough to worry about.*

Kirko.

*So that is the Imperial Palace?*

Then more.

People met in Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Friends from Colosseo.

People from Nouvelle Vague.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

The triplets who had once been enemies—

then servants.

Osiris—

once impossibly distant—

now simply an older brother unable to express himself well.

The Seven Counts.

And—

Hugo.

That unresolved feeling again.

Dozens.

Hundreds.

Faces Vikir could almost reach.

Andras knew exactly what he was doing.

[You have no other choice.]

[If you want the portals closed—]

[accept the deal.]

Vikir did not move.

“Kill you.”

“That closes them too.”

[Still dreaming!?]

Andras shouted.

[These are **my** portals.]

[Only I can close them.]

[They are not the Gate of Destruction.]

[If you tear a hole in cloth—]

[does it heal because the one who made it dies?]

[Kill me and these portals remain open forever!]

Vikir’s expression did not change.

“I still won’t make a deal with a demon.”

“I’ll kill you.”

[You’re insane.]

[Do you want these gates open forever!?]

The threat became more concrete.

Sixty-two shadows leaned closer.

Still—

Vikir did not bend.

“No.”

“They can be closed.”

Andras opened his mouth.

[How—]

STAB.

A blade erupted through his chest.

From behind.

Straight through the heart sustaining the portals.

[…?]

Andras’s face snapped back into the form of First Crown Prince Golding Jack Meridieu.

His head turned slowly.

Like rusted clockwork.

Behind him—

the coffin.

The same enormous coffin Vikir had carried from Tochka.

The same one dragged through storm and sea.

Its lid—

heavy with one old demon hunter’s duty, guilt, rage, and hatred—

now stood open.

Someone had risen from inside.

A hand held the sword driven through Andras’s back.

Andras recognized the face.

His eyes widened.

“Pigi.”

No title.

No family name—

at least not the one everyone knew.

A weak Academy student.

Vikir’s timid roommate.

A friend who had followed from behind.

Yet the light in Pigi’s eyes now pressed even Andras backward.

The Crown Prince’s mouth trembled.

[Golding…]

[Golding Ralph…]

[Golding Pigi Ralph…]

[No—]

[Andras!]

The lost Second Prince.

The other branch of the Imperial bloodline.

And at the same time—

the other half of the First Corpse.

The two Princes.

The two halves.

The malformed being called Andras—

finally faced itself.
