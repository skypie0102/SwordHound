# Chapter 216: My Neck Will Be Your Sheath (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/216.txt` — `9875b18d523a76af9badd29fb628ae8c8115ad35`. Recovered English Chapter 214 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls **Camus Morgue**, **Rosie Morgue**, **Adolf Morgue**, **Snake Morgue**, **Light Faction**, **Dark Faction**, **Ironblood Empress**, **Ah'Heman**, **Madam Eight-Legs**, **Seere**, **Eighth Corpse**, **Beelzebub**, **Sixth Fang**, **Seventh Fang**, **Peak Sword Graduator**, and **Sword Master**. This is the first source-explicit identification of the Corpse Queen as Camus Morgue; no earlier chapter is retroactively rewritten to reveal it sooner.

The **Corpse Queen**.

Her skull mask—

came away.

And beneath it—

was a face Vikir knew.

A face that—

despite the years—

had not changed as much as it should have.

Large eyes—

almost deerlike.

Eyes that had always seemed—

a little wet—

whenever they turned toward him.

Dark brows.

Straight nose.

Small lips.

The roundness of childhood—

mostly gone.

Freckles—

gone.

And the whites and blacks of her eyes—

reversed by something no human body should contain.

Vikir stared.

Then—

the name finally arrived.

**Camus Morgue.**

She looked at him—

with the same expression he remembered from the last time.

Tears.

A face unable to accept what was happening.

A voice—

ruined almost beyond recognition.

The girl who had been separated from him—

during the disaster in the Red and Black Mountains.

The girl who had lost **Rosie Morgue**.

The girl who had watched Vikir disappear—

after the battle with **Madam Eight-Legs**.

Time—

seemed to have stopped there for her.

“….”

Vikir said nothing.

For several seconds—

he simply looked.

*Why didn't I see it?*

He had been too focused.

The culprit.

The enemy.

The hidden hand.

The Ten Corpses.

Kill the demons—

prevent the Age of Destruction.

That objective had swallowed—

every smaller thought.

Every suspicion.

Everything inconvenient.

Now—

the clues rearranged themselves.

Camus had searched for him—

for years.

Without stopping.

Then—

suddenly—

she had changed.

She broke with **Adolf Morgue**—

the uncle she had once followed like a parent.

Left the **Light Faction**.

Joined **Snake Morgue's Dark Faction**—

despite having never been close to him.

Then declared closed training—

and cut herself off from the world.

At almost exactly the same point—

history diverged.

Snake Morgue—

who should one day become Seere's **Corpse King**—

remained human.

And an unknown woman—

appeared instead.

The **Corpse Queen**.

A woman who resurrected Rosie—

and kept her close.

A woman who possessed Ah'Heman's remains—

taken from the Red and Black Mountains.

A woman who showed unusual anger—

when she saw the crazed court mage,

perhaps because it stirred memories of losing both Vikir and Rosie.

A woman whose attention shattered—

when she saw Baskerville's Seventh Fang.

A woman who—

even while unconscious—

was taken away by Rosie with a protectiveness that made no sense for an ordinary undead servant.

Vikir's eyes hardened.

The explanation—

had been in front of him.

He had refused to assemble it.

“Why?”

His voice—

low.

“Why did you become one of the Ten Corpses?”

“And what happened to your voice?”

Camus's answer—

came rough.

Broken.

[…After you disappeared…]

[I cried.]

[For years.]

Her voice—

barely resembled the girl Vikir remembered.

Crying.

Screaming.

Searching.

Collapsing from exhaustion.

Waking—

and crying again.

Again.

Again.

Until—

her throat never recovered.

Tears slipped from those inverted eyes.

[…Idiot.]

[I thought you were dead.]

[I wanted to find your body.]

Vikir's brow tightened.

“You searched the Red and Black Mountains—

every day?”

[…Yes.]

“For years?”

[…Yes.]

That—

he could understand.

At least—

partly.

Then Camus continued.

[…Because if I found your body…]

[…I could bring you back.]

Vikir put one hand against his forehead.

“…”

Black magic.

Necromancy.

The art—

of forcing the dead back into the world.

*So that's why she went to the Dark Faction.*

Snake Morgue—

was a black-magic master.

In the history Vikir remembered—

Snake himself would eventually pursue black magic to its extreme—

and contract with **Seere**.

Vikir remembered—

what Highsis.

Middlesis.

Lowsis—

had told him before.

*Camus entered closed training.*

*She broke with Adolf.*

At the time—

it had sounded strange.

Now—

the meaning was obvious.

After Camus stopped searching—

she changed.

The bright, aggressive girl—

quieted.

Spoke less.

Then—

without warning—

left Adolf.

The man she had followed since childhood.

Adolf—

had not publicly stopped her.

He knew—

what she had become after that night.

How long she had searched.

How much guilt she carried.

How completely Vikir's disappearance—

had hollowed her out.

So he watched—

as she walked into the Dark Faction.

Vikir's expression darkened.

Camus had abandoned—

everything.

Because she thought—

he was dead.

And wanted—

to make his corpse move again.

If history had followed its old path—

Camus Morgue would have become—

the **Ironblood Empress**.

A woman who would rise beyond Morgue—

and eventually dominate an era.

Instead—

she stood here.

Half human.

Half Demon King.

Vikir asked—

quietly.

“So you contracted with a demon—

because of me?”

“Why?”

Camus stared at him.

[You're asking because you don't know?]

“I don't know.”

“So I'm asking.”

“You wanted my remains.”

“You wanted to resurrect me.”

“And for that—

you became the Eighth Corpse.”

Camus hesitated.

Then—

gave a small nod.

[…When you say it like that…]

[…it does sound ridiculous.]

Then she looked straight at him.

No evasion.

No ambiguity.

[If death was going to separate us…]

[…then I wanted to be with you—

even beyond death.]

Silence.

The **Night Hound**—

and the **Corpse Queen**—

stood facing each other.

Masks—

gone.

No battlefield aliases left—

between them.

Vikir broke the silence.

“…But demons still have to die.”

Duty—

was duty.

Private feeling—

was private feeling.

No individual bond—

however heavy—

could outweigh humanity.

The situation—

was simple.

A man—

who had to kill the woman in front of him—

to prevent the end of the world.

And a woman—

who had chosen even damnation—

if it meant remaining with that man.

Camus lowered her eyes.

[…Okay.]

Vikir's gaze sharpened.

That answer—

he had not expected.

Camus—

however—

looked strangely calm.

[I knew from the moment I joined hands with a demon—

that I probably wouldn't get a good ending.]

She stepped forward.

Straightened her shoulders.

Raised her chin.

[Come on.]

[Kill me.]

“….”

Vikir did not move.

A Ten Corpse—

was voluntarily offering her neck.

Why—

was his arm still?

Faces flashed through his mind.

Comrades.

The dead.

The Age of Destruction.

Every person he had sworn—

not to let die again.

And yet—

**Beelzebub** remained still on his wrist.

Camus's voice rose.

[What are you doing?]

[Kill me.]

“….”

[Kill me!]

[Hurry!]

Her control cracked.

BOOM!

Black flame—

and iron—

burst around them.

Mana responded—

to emotion.

Tears ran down Camus's cheeks.

[Right!]

[Of course you hate a fallen witch like me!]

[Then kill me and live however you want!]

[Your troublesome ex-girlfriend can die here—

and you can go live happily with your pure little Saintess girlfriend!]

“???”

Vikir stared.

*Ex-girlfriend?*

*Current girlfriend?*

*Why is Dolores involved?*

Something—

had gone catastrophically wrong in Camus's interpretation of events.

Vikir understood that much.

Explaining it—

felt considerably harder.

Worse—

the demonic presence around Camus—

was growing stronger.

Minute by minute.

Midnight—

approached.

After exhausting herself with magic—

Camus sank to the ground.

Then—

began crying again.

[You bastard!]

[We finally meet again—

and all you can say is “demons must die”!]

[Were you born a Baskerville or something!?]

“….”

Vikir—

had no answer.

This was the third time—

he had seen Camus cry.

The first—

when they met as children.

The second—

when they met again years later—

in the Red Fang Castle region.

And now.

All three times—

Vikir had failed completely—

at comforting her.

Camus—

apparently familiar with this deficiency—

eventually stopped on her own.

[…Fine.]

[I know.]

[You're terrible at comforting people.]

That too—

was familiar.

After Rosie's death—

Camus had cried.

And Rosie—

had been the one to hold her.

Camus reached inside her armor.

Threw—

an old book.

Slap.

It landed at Vikir's feet.

[…My diary.]

“….”

[Read it after I'm gone.]

[There are things I can't say—

while looking at your face.]

A book—

made only of paper.

Yet—

when Vikir picked it up—

it felt heavy.

Heavy with years.

A woman's words—

written to a man—

who could never answer.

Camus moved again.

The bone armor around her neck—

opened.

Pale skin—

exposed.

[Now.]

[Do it.]

“….”

[If it's you—

I'm happy.]

[No.]

[Only you have the right.]

She walked to Vikir.

Kneeled.

Then took his right hand—

and placed it against her throat.

[I already gave up on surviving—

the moment I contracted with a demon.]

[If I have to die—]

[then dying by your hand—

is better.]

Her lips touched—

Vikir's right forearm.

A faint trembling breath.

Then—

the title's words.

[**My neck will become your sheath.**]

“….”

Vikir still—

did not move.

Ten Demon Kings.

Ten gates.

The future Age of Destruction.

All ten—

had to die—

before those gates opened.

Vikir's hand—

tightened.

Camus looked up.

Prepared.

Ready.

Still—

the blade did not fall.

Something buried—

deep inside Vikir—

refused.

And suddenly—

he remembered the manuals.

**Sixth Fang.**

> To reach the extreme of the Sixth Fang—
> discard emotion.

**Seventh Fang.**

> To open the gate of the Seventh Fang—
> embrace emotion.

To reach **Peak Sword Graduator**—

become iron.

Eliminate feeling.

Become—

a killing machine.

A hound of death.

That—

had been Vikir's entire life.

No love.

No one loving him.

Dantalian's memory attack—

had proven it.

Beyond comradeship—

nothing connected Vikir—

to the world.

But now—

his hand shook.

A girl—

kneeling before him.

Neck exposed.

Waiting—

for his blade.

Vikir looked at her pale face.

And felt—

something.

Weak.

Small.

But real.

Individual emotion—

could not outweigh humanity.

That remained true.

Yet—

perhaps the existence of such emotion—

was itself the thing he had been missing.

The iron man—

standing before something unbearably heavy—

began to cross toward—

the superhuman.

For an instant—

Vikir saw it.

A wall.

The final wall.

The realm where a sword—

could cut not merely flesh or steel—

but invisible things.

Soul.

Bond.

Concept.

**Sword Master.**

Then—

pain.

“…!”

Something—

pierced his abdomen.

An iron spike—

from Camus's hand.

Camus herself—

looked stunned.

[…What?]

Black mist—

poured from behind her.

Something—

was taking her body.

By force.

A mouth opened—

inside the fog.

[Midnight.]

[My dear little roommate.]

A voice—

wrong.

Ancient.

Amused.

> **Eighth Corpse — Seere**

Half of Camus's consciousness—

went dark.

And **Seere**—

opened her eyes.
