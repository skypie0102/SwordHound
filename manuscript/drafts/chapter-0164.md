[Sister!]

[I'm scared!]

[Big Brother!]

[Save me!]

[Someone help!]

Children's faces cried from Dantalian's body.

Faces taken from children who had tried to escape Indulgentia.

Vikir remembered the night-worker rules.

Now their purpose seemed even clearer.

Fear the children into staying inside.

Teach staff never to help a child alone in the corridor.

Turn every attempted escape into something suspicious.

Something dangerous.

Something to ignore.

Dolores understood too.

And something inside her broke open.

Boom!

Pure white flame swallowed Dantalian.

[AAAAAGH!?]

His flesh smoked.

Burned.

Twisted.

Dolores could barely look.

“Wicked…”

“You…”

“You absolute monster!”

Her vocabulary for curses was limited.

Tears blurred her vision.

She could not meet the eyes of the children crying inside the fire.

Dolores possessed extraordinary divine power.

What she lacked—

was battlefield experience.

Someone beside her had plenty.

Slash!

The Night Hound tore another chunk from Dantalian.

*Opportunity.*

Holy fire had pinned the demon.

Pain interrupted its concentration.

Now Vikir could carve it apart.

Red aura surged from Beelzebub.

The dense liquid aura of a Peak Sword Graduator—

almost solid now.

[Damn human!]

Purple tongues erupted.

Clang!

Clang-clang-clang!

Sword met tongue.

Dozens of exchanges passed in seconds.

Dantalian was fast.

Vikir was experienced.

Neither gave the other room.

A tongue struck hard enough to rattle Vikir's wrist.

*Stronger than Andromalius.*

Andromalius had also been Demon King-class.

But that battle had included Baskerville's knights—

and Hugo had delivered the final blow.

Here—

Vikir had only one ally.

“Night Hound!”

“Back!”

Dolores raised both hands.

A barrier of white flame formed.

Vikir disengaged.

For the first time since the exchange began—

he could breathe.

Sizzle—

holy heat forced Dantalian away.

The demon laughed.

[No human has pushed me this far before.]

[Interesting.]

“Let's see whether you still find it interesting when you're dying.”

[Kill me?]

[Already?]

[Confident.]

One of Dantalian's faces—

a gentleman with a heavy mustache—

moved to the front.

[Humans always think they're special.]

[Chosen.]

[Unique.]

[The hero.]

[The protagonist who will kill the demon and save the world.]

The face changed.

Beautiful woman.

Innocent child.

Stubborn man.

Cruel old man.

A Thousand Faces.

[But everyone is special.]

[Everyone is a hero to someone.]

[A lover.]

[A fool.]

[A villain.]

[Every person thinks their story is the story.]

Black mist poured from thirty-six mouths.

It spread through the hall like smoke from a burning city.

Thump.

Three black leather sacks fell before Vikir and Dolores.

They resembled the face coverings once worn by Ephebo, Hebe, Pedo, and Geronto.

The leftmost one twitched.

Throb.

Black blood pulsed beneath thin leather.

Then all three convulsed.

Thump.

Thump.

Thump.

Like execution hoods writhing around condemned heads.

Dantalian's faces smiled.

[Then shall we hear your stories?]

◆◆◆

Fog swallowed the room.

Dolores searched for Dantalian.

“What is this?”

“My divine power isn't dispersing it.”

“…Be careful.”

Vikir knew this technique.

He had survived it before.

One of the three sacks floated upward.

Opened.

Vikir's voice became dry.

“Dantalian the Face Collector.”

“The first is the **Sack of Shame**.”

Dolores tilted her head.

The sack turned toward her.

Then produced—

another Dolores.

“…What!?”

The duplicate stood expressionless.

Vikir explained.

“It forces the target to confront the face of shame.”

“It materializes the memory they find most humiliating.”

“A mental attack.”

“Useful for breaking trust between allies and destroying composure.”

The false Dolores took one step.

Then—

wet herself.

Silence.

Vikir said nothing.

Dolores said nothing.

Their silence was almost synchronized.

Dolores's face turned scarlet.

“DIE!”

White fire obliterated the duplicate.

The Sack of Shame collapsed with it.

[WHAT!?]

Dantalian's scream came through the fog.

[How did you overcome shame that easily!?]

Destroying the manifestation—

hurt the demon behind it.

The second sack opened.

Vikir's expression tightened.

“**Sack of Fear.**”

“It brings out what the target fears most.”

A man stepped from the black leather.

Middle-aged.

Severe.

Authoritative.

Vikir recognized him.

**Cardinal Humbert L. Quovadis.**

Dolores's father.

Leader of the Old Testament Faction.

Dolores stiffened.

“F-Father…”

[Dolores.]

[Why are you outside this late?]

[Have you finished your work?]

The illusion looked down on her.

A whip rested in its hand.

Dolores's eyes shook.

“I…”

Cold sweat formed.

She could not meet the false Humbert's gaze.

Slash!

The illusion's head flew away.

Vikir lowered his sword.

“An illusion is an illusion.”

“Nothing more.”

Dolores finally breathed.

“S-Sorry.”

“My relationship with my father isn't very good.”

“I forgot for a moment.”

“…I understand.”

Vikir asked nothing else.

One sack remained.

“The **Sack of Love**.”

Dolores's heart skipped.

By now—

she understood the pattern.

It would show the person she loved most.

But who?

Dolores had lived according to the teaching—

love your neighbor.

That was not the kind of love Dantalian meant.

She could not remember ever loving anyone in that sense.

Ssssssss—

a figure formed.

Black clothes.

Black cloak.

Hat.

Long-beaked plague-doctor mask.

The **Night Hound**.

“…”

“…”

An awkward silence fell.

Vikir slowly looked toward Dolores.

She flailed both hands.

“N-No!”

“Something is wrong with that sack!”

“It distorts people!”

Dantalian laughed from the fog.

[My sack is more honest than the person it reads.]

[Even feelings buried so deeply that you haven't recognized them yourself—]

[it finds them.]

Dolores's face turned bright red.

“That's not—!”

“I swear—”

“No, I shouldn't swear, but—”

“I never thought—”

Vikir said nothing.

He simply drew Beelzebub—

and attacked the false Night Hound.

Bang!

The imitation dissolved into mist beneath his sword and Dolores's holy flame.

“Good.”

“It can't reproduce the original's power yet.”

That had not been true in the Age of Destruction.

Back then—

Dantalian could reproduce not only remembered faces—

but remembered strength.

Vikir was fortunate to have found him early.

Dolores remained speechless.

Then—

thump.

The three sacks moved again.

Dantalian chuckled.

[I didn't expect much from the young Saintess.]

[How much history could she possibly have?]

[But you—]

His attention shifted to Vikir.

[I expect much more.]

Shame.

Fear.

Love.

The technique turned toward the Night Hound.

The Sack of Shame opened first.

It searched Vikir's memories.

Sssssssss—

a landscape formed in the fog.

Dolores gasped.

Even Dantalian stopped laughing.

Dark crimson sky.

Mountains of corpses.

Rivers of blood.

A world where everything had already ended.

In the center of that wasteland—

a tall pole.

Dolores looked upward.

Something hung at the top.

A head.

Severed.

Displayed alone against the dead sky.
