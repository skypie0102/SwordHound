# Chapter 198: The Corpse Queen (1)

The Night Hound moved through the sleeping capital.

Roof—

wall—

railing—

air.

Faster than before.

Part of that came from training.

Most of it—

from the tiny creature on his left wrist.

[Chik!]

**Baby Madam** fired a strand of silk.

It caught the wall of the next building.

Vikir swung forward.

Released.

Another strand—

another anchor.

Pendulum movement.

Again.

Again.

The tiny spider produced a ridiculous amount of silk for its size.

The liquid seemed impossibly concentrated—

a minute quantity hardening the instant it touched air.

*If something this small can make this much…*

Vikir remembered **Madam Eight-Legs**.

During the attack on Ballak—

she had already spent enormous amounts of webbing before fighting him.

Had she entered that battle with full reserves—

Vikir might not have escaped intact.

“You're more useful than I expected.”

[Chik-chik.]

He rubbed Baby Madam's head.

Travel time through the city—

had dropped dramatically.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap!

Vikir ran along an outer wall.

Then landed atop—

the half-destroyed **Central Clocktower**.

The old landmark had survived only because its foundations were absurdly solid.

Everything above—

looked as though something enormous had struck it.

Vikir remembered the newspaper.

> **[Exclusive] Night Hound Strikes Again!?**
>
> At approximately 1:00 a.m. yesterday, another terrorist attack attributed to the Night Hound struck the Imperial Capital.
>
> The target was the historic **Central Clocktower**, long regarded as a symbol of Imperial order and peace.
>
> The attack also caused deaths; the exact death toll was still being reported.
>
> The Imperial House has announced that every possible measure will be taken to capture the criminal responsible.

The copycat.

Or—

the person everyone had decided to call one.

Vikir inspected the damage.

Long marks.

Loose curves.

Too irregular—

for sword cuts.

*Whip marks?*

Destroying a clocktower produced little practical gain.

Which meant—

symbolism.

Hatred of order.

Hatred of society.

A desire to attack what people saw every day.

*Not important right now.*

Whatever the motive—

Vikir needed to find the attacker.

And kill them.

Nothing was more important right now.

He stood on the broken tower.

Looked north.

Toward the district where he had volunteered not long ago.

Toward the ruins of the **Indulgentia orphanage**.

Toward the **Royal Martyrs' Cemetery**.

*That's Cindywendy's dead zone.*

Her agents could work almost anywhere in the Imperial Capital.

Not there.

Those who investigated—

disappeared.

Vikir lifted the edge of his mask.

Smelled the night.

“….”

There.

Extremely faint.

But unmistakable.

Demon.

The assassination at Indulgentia was already over.

Dantalian—

dead.

So why did the scent remain?

And why—

was it so diluted?

*Human blood mixed with demon blood.*

A strange odor.

Not fully one.

Not fully the other.

Even Vikir—

a veteran demon hunter—

had never smelled anything quite like it.

He jumped.

Baby Madam's silk caught.

The line ran out just above the street.

Recoil—

flip—

landing.

Then Vikir ran.

Alley to alley.

Roof to railing.

Wall to wall.

Higher—

lower—

lower again.

Until—

the ruins of Indulgentia.

The Royal Martyrs' Cemetery lay barely a hundred meters away.

Inside the ruins—

the demonic smell changed.

Fainter.

Then stronger.

As though something—

were deliberately diluting it.

No investigators remained.

Quovadis had finished its formal search.

Ordinary guards should have watched the site—

but recent copycat incidents had stretched manpower thin.

Only warning signs remained.

Vikir ignored them.

He entered the exact area—

where Cindywendy's agents had vanished.

Then—

“…!”

Understanding came immediately.

Why they vanished.

What had taken them.

What they had become.

And—

where they had gone.

Groan.

A corpse wandered through the rubble.

Lower jaw gone.

Purple tongue hanging down.

Body swollen with gas.

Rotting flesh clotted into greasy masses.

Then another.

And another.

> **Zombie**
>
> **Danger Rating:** C  
> **Size:** 1.7 m  
> **Found In:** All continents  
> **Alias:** ‘Not Completely Rotten’
>
> A common animated corpse found in graveyards, battlefields, ruins, sewers, and other places where death gathers.
>
> In poor rural districts and neglected cities, they can be almost as common as rats.

The dead wandered without purpose.

Vikir could not identify every corpse.

But some—

had almost certainly been Cindywendy's missing informants.

“Go somewhere better.”

Whether such a place existed—

he did not know.

Vikir reached behind him.

Drew—

**Anubis**.

The black bow settled into his hand.

Familiar.

Natural.

“I've let you rest too long.”

Academy bows—

cheap.

ordinary.

safe.

After weeks of using them—

his hands almost felt dull.

People said masters should never blame equipment.

Nonsense.

Equipment mattered.

Had Vikir used Anubis during the Attack test—

the golem would not have been the only thing destroyed.

Neither—

would Sadi have gotten away with only one eye.

*Which is exactly why I couldn't use it.*

Anubis—

could not be explained away.

Baby Madam shifted.

[Chik…]

She retreated from his wrist—

to his shoulder.

Anubis had been made from the remains of **Madam Eight-Legs**.

Perhaps the hatchling still hated the traces of the mother who had nearly eaten her while she was still an egg.

*Spider families aren't necessarily close.*

Vikir thought of Baskerville.

*Neither are hounds.*

Creak.

He drew.

Thump!

One zombie's head burst.

Thump-thump!

Two more.

The undead barely reacted before falling.

Vikir followed the faint demonic odor deeper.

Then—

at the center of the ruins—

he stopped.

Someone he had already seen die—

stood there again.

Red hair.

Twin braids.

Black mask.

**Geronto.**

Dantalian's undead servant.

But Geronto's resurrection—

was not the true shock.

Something else stood behind her.

Bone crown.

Bone armor.

Black cloak.

Long hair—

burning red beneath the skull-like helm.

And an oppressive demonic aura—

the source of the scent that had pulled Vikir here.

One of the **Ten Corpses**.

The **Eighth Corpse**.
