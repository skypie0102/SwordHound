Student Council President—

**Dolores L. Quovadis**.

The recent **Thrifty Bazaar** incident—

had left her with mixed feelings.

For years—

three powers had divided student autonomy.

The student council—

official.

Visible.

Bound by rules—

procedure—

responsibility.

The **noble faction**—

and **power-family faction**—

different.

Connections.

Favors.

Inside information.

Informal pressure.

Influence—

without the same formal obligations.

Dolores disliked that.

Still—

the student council needed to understand campus opinion.

And those factions—

shaped it.

So—

they could not simply be ignored.

Then—

one first-year—

humiliated both.

**Vikir.**

The factions had been growing—

quietly—

almost enough to rival the student council.

After the Bazaar—

members defected.

Support weakened.

Some disappointed students—

even joined the student council instead.

The executives—

were delighted.

“Should we recruit Vikir?”

“Who refuses the student council gold badge?”

“A first-year—

already an executive candidate?”

“Maybe—

youngest student council president?”

“That is going too far.”

“But—

we should move before the other factions recover.”

Some already discussed—

promoting Vikir into an executive track—

as early as his second year.

Dolores—

did not share their confidence.

*He wouldn't accept.*

Probably—

not even ordinary membership.

A normal first-year—

would celebrate.

Vikir—

was not normal.

He brought back—

a huge amount of monster hide.

Burned part of it—

in front of people trying to suppress him.

Raised his bargaining position.

Then donated—

the proceeds he did not need—

to scholarships.

*Gnoll hide isn't priceless.*

*But it is valuable.*

*Burning it in front of opponents—*

*just to change the negotiation—*

*isn't freshman behavior.*

Dolores's curiosity—

had grown slowly—

all semester.

First—

the absurd number of demerits.

Then—

his sincerity at the orphanage.

The drinking-party disaster—

and how he helped afterward.

Then she learned—

he ranked first—

in both written—

and practical midterms.

Smart.

Strong.

Diligent.

If not for his constant harsh articles—

against Night Hound—

perhaps they would already have been much closer.

Then came—

yesterday.

Professor Banshee's office.

The scholarship donation.

And one name.

**Peri Award.**

Banshee—

had not understood.

Dolores—

had.

Peri.

The child—

lost in the Dantalian incident.

Vikir—

still remembered her.

A memory returned.

At the orphanage—

Dolores teasing him.

*You were too cold to her.*

*Did you let her kiss you?*

*No.*

*She wanted to so badly.*

*You could have allowed one.*

*Sorry.*

Vikir had refused—

embarrassed.

Now—

Dolores wondered.

*Maybe he hasn't forgotten her.*

*Maybe that's why he used her name.*

Cold outside.

Warm—

underneath.

Dolores no longer merely suspected that.

She believed it.

Then—

she stood suddenly.

Her chest—

strangely tight.

For some reason—

she needed to see Vikir.

Now.

Perhaps—

responsibility.

Guilt.

She had witnessed Peri's final moments—

during the Dantalian battle.

Or perhaps—

some other feeling—

she had not yet named.

“President?”

“Where are you going?”

“The meeting agenda—”

“Sorry!”

Dolores bowed.

“I'll finish it tonight—

even if I have to stay up.”

Then—

left.

Dormitory district.

Vikir—

nowhere.

“Vikir?”

“He's usually the last one back.”

“Library?”

“He reads constantly.”

“Not there?”

“Training room, then.”

“He works out a lot.”

Tudor.

Sancho.

Sinclaire.

People close to Vikir—

could not tell her—

where he was.

Front gate records—

nothing.

Back gate—

nothing.

So—

he should still be inside the Academy.

“…Where are you?”

It felt—

like the orphanage.

Vikir—

always slipping somewhere else.

Then another memory.

During the festival—

when the unidentified black mage appeared—

Vikir's whereabouts had also been unclear.

Mysteries—

stacked.

Even at close range—

Dolores could rarely read him.

Vikir kept his inner self—

more closed—

than anyone his age.

Then—

she remembered—

his demerits.

*All those wrong turns?*

*For someone that intelligent?*

The explanation—

suddenly felt ridiculous.

She opened—

the Academy record—

Dates.

Positions.

Routes.

emerged.

“Dark places…”

“And high places.”

or approached—

the Academy's high outer wall.

“Trying to climb the wall?”

> **Vikir — Conduct Demerits**
>

“There.”

Full speed.

Up.

Emergency stairs.

Roof.

Empty.

Vikir—

gone.

“If he came down—

his room.”

Pigi—

“Vikir?”

“He came in—

“Do you know where?”

“He said—

Bathhouse.

“He'll come back afterward?”

“Maybe.”

“Vikir walks around a lot at night.”

“No.”

“He always returns.”

late.”

“Vikir doesn't keep much.”

“…Does the student council president—
