Basement Floor 11’s jungle was vicious enough to remind Vikir of the disasters of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Venomous air-sac insects.

Mosquitoes that fed on bone marrow.

Vines covered in needle-like thorns.

Fruit heavy enough to strike like hammers.

Deep ravines hidden beneath rotting leaves.

An ordinary person would not last half a day here.

And now—

night had fallen.

Even the people native to this place would normally avoid moving through the jungle after dark.

Vikir did not.

Rustle.

He moved as though the jungle were his own room.

Cerberus fur—

carried downwind—

kept other beasts away.

Hidden ravines were identified and crossed.

Overgrown Daylily air sacs were cut open to release gas that drove off poisonous insects.

Baby Madam’s silk bridged gaps that would have taken hours to climb around.

Every motion looked practiced.

Natural.

As though Vikir had repeated the same survival routines for decades.

Behind him—

Tudor, Sancho, Pigi, and Bianca could only stare.

“This is more shocking than finding out Vikir was Night Hound.”

“How does he know all this?”

“Even Basilios, the mercenary king of the north, probably couldn’t move through jungle like this.”

“Wait.”

Pigi blinked.

“Vikir is Night Hound?”

Tudor shrugged.

“I saw him wearing the mask earlier.”

“There’s probably a reason.”

“We can ask later.”

Vikir—

meanwhile—

was remembering two different pasts.

The Age of Destruction.

A time when surviving required much more than swordsmanship.

Herb gathering.

Scouting.

Tracking.

Stealth.

Ambush.

Assassination.

Medicine.

Treatment.

Cooking.

Anything useful.

And the other memory—

two years among Ballak in Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Crack!

Vikir split a rotting log blocking the path.

Aura vibrated and rotated at the blade’s edge—

cutting through it instantly.

Again—

the others stared.

“V-Vikir.”

“What level are you?”

“How many stats did you raise?”

“Were you already this strong outside?”

“…You’re full of secrets.”

Then—

Vikir stopped.

He examined footprints.

Checked the wind.

Lowered his body.

Circled behind thick brush—

hiding his scent.

A faint light flickered in the darkness.

A dozen people sat around a small fire.

Tudor clenched his teeth.

“That’s them.”

“The ones stealing our food.”

They were all middle-aged warriors.

Large.

Powerfully built.

Mud covered their faces.

Animal skins and leaves covered the rest of them.

Intricate tattoos covered bronze-colored muscle.

Yet the tattoos were strangely faded.

Tudor whispered.

“They scattered dry cones around the camp.”

“Step on one—

it cracks loudly.”

“I know.”

Vikir advanced.

Slowly.

Only a few meters per hour.

Avoiding every alarm.

Then—

CRUNCH!

He stepped openly into the firelight.

“Return the food.”

The warriors sprang up.

Bows came into their hands.

PING!

Arrows flew.

And with the first volley—

Vikir knew.

*Ballak.*

He had already heard from Cindywendy that Ballak had vanished.

He had wondered where they had gone.

Now—

apparently—

at least some had been swallowed by Amdusias’s abyss.

*They’re alive.*

For a moment—

Vikir thought the problem might be easy.

He pulled aside his clothing.

Revealed the tattoo on his shoulder.

The mark of Ballak’s hunting chief.

“Stop.”

“It’s me.”

Ballak’s warriors—

or any of the western mountain tribes—

should have known him.

Vikir had helped save them from the Red Death.

From catastrophic flooding.

His name had become legend among them.

But—

“…Who is this?”

“Why does he have the hunting-chief tattoo?”

“Anyone recognize him?”

“No.”

“Take him down!”

Vikir frowned.

These were unmistakably Ballak warriors.

Archery.

Language.

Ash markings.

Tattoos.

Everything matched.

And yet—

none recognized him.

PING!

Vikir stepped away from another arrow.

*Warriors this old should remember me.*

He drew his sword.

“Still nothing?”

Baskerville swordsmanship appeared.

Any Ballak warrior who had spent a lifetime fighting Baskervilles should know it.

Instead—

“What kind of weird swordsmanship is that?!”

“Tooth-shaped slashes?”

“Never seen it!”

“Don’t engage!”

“Keep distance!”

They did not recognize even that.

No obvious mind-control symptoms.

No broken memories.

Just—

ignorance.

Vikir sighed.

“Then I’ll show you again.”

“How a foreign slave became your hunting chief.”

At the same time—

he produced an item.

The one hidden piece he had specifically wanted despite usually preferring candy.

**<Scale of Forced Equality> / Scale / S**

**Adjusts other stats according to the holder’s highest stat.**

**All stats slowly rise until matching the highest value.**

**The effect continues until every stat becomes equal.**

Vikir opened his status window.

**<Vikir>**

**LV:** 1

**Titles:**  
‘Gutter Rat Hunter’  
‘Hell Hound’  
‘Daylily Lumberjack’  
‘Majin Executioner’  
‘Black Sea King Slayer’  
‘Dragon Majin’s Retainer’ — NEW

**Stats:**  
Strength: 798  
Agility: 92  
Stamina: 100  
Physical Resistance: 1  
Magic Resistance: 1  
Reflexes: 1

Before Lost Paradise—

his three basic stats had been 300 each.

Nine hundred total.

Now—

Strength, Agility, and Stamina totaled 990.

Almost everything—

concentrated into Strength.

Because in Lost Paradise—

Vikir had converted accumulated stats back into candy.

And then redistributed them.

**<This floor contains the largest Candy Shop in the tower.>**

**<Challengers may exchange held items for candy.>**

**<Stats already absorbed from eaten candy may also be extracted back into candy with local assistance.>**

**<Candy consumed before entering this floor receives one bonus candy for every ten extracted.>**

Vikir had never intended to use the shop as designed.

Instead—

he had extracted his stats.

Collected the ten-percent bonus.

Then eaten the candy again.

*I took the bait out of the demon’s trap.*

Amdusias and the fairies would probably have been furious if they knew.

Probability had given Vikir far more common red Strength candy than green Agility or blue Stamina candy.

That—

normally—

would have been terrible distribution.

For Vikir—

it was perfect.

Because he possessed the **Scale of Forced Equality**.

The scale slowly raised every lower stat toward the highest one.

Then disappeared only after equality was achieved.

*I originally wanted it just to raise the three special stats.*

Its weakness—

was speed.

The balancing effect was absurdly slow.

Most challengers would age into old people before receiving the full benefit.

But here—

on the River of Flow—

time itself raced.

Vikir was aging every moment.

Hair lengthening.

Bones thickening.

Muscle changing.

Voice deepening.

Facial lines maturing.

And with those changes—

Ding!

[The **Scale of Forced Equality** has begun forcibly balancing your stats.]

[Strength is out of balance with the other stats.]

[All lower stats will be adjusted toward Strength: **798**.]

[Agility rises from 92 → 93.]

[This will continue until Agility reaches 798.]

[Stamina rises from 100 → 101.]

[This will continue until Stamina reaches 798.]

[Physical Resistance rises from 1 → 2.]

[This will continue until Physical Resistance reaches 798.]

[Magic Resistance rises from 1 → 2.]

[This will continue until Magic Resistance reaches 798.]

[Reflexes rises from 1 → 2.]

[This will continue until Reflexes reaches 798.]

[Agility 93 → 94…]

[Stamina 101 → 102…]

[Physical Resistance 2 → 3…]

[Magic Resistance 2 → …]

[Reflexes 2 → …]

His highest stat—

Strength—

was 798.

And now all six unlocked stats were slowly climbing toward the same number.

**<Basement Floor 11 — River of Flow>**

Time flowed like a river.

For Vikir—

right now—

that river was carrying him exactly where he wanted to go.
