“……Who are you?”

“What are you?”

“How do you know all of this?”

Very few could describe events that had not happened yet.

“The Saintess asked you a question.”

He cleared his throat.

“Who are you?”

Dolores and Mozgus stared.

Vikir answered with the name he had chosen.

“Hound of the Night.”

“A blasphemous one too.”

“If even part of what he said is possible, we need to verify the plague and report upward.”

Exhausted.

Alone.

A pilgrim.
