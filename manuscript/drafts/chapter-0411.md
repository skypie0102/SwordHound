# Chapter 411: End game (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/411.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 409 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Night Hound, Winter Sword Orwell, Winston Smith, Gate of Good and Evil, Poseidon, Sadi / Ms. Ouroboros, D’Ordume, Souaré, Level Nine, and Aiyen. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

RUMBLE—

D’Ordume’s massive crocodilian body sank into the broken floor.

One strike.

That was all it had taken.

One of Nouvelle Vague’s Five Wardens—

one of the two candidates for the next Warden—

the prison’s great offensive pillar—

had gone down instantly.

Nobody moved.

Prisoner or guard.

The battlefield—

which had been deafening only moments earlier—

fell silent.

Bastille stared at the crater.

“C-Colonel D’Ordume…?”

No answer.

D’Ordume lay with his tongue hanging out.

Unconscious—

or dead.

The watching guards could only hope it was the former.

Then the mysterious Major removed his hat.

“Ladies and gentlemen of Nouvelle Vague.”

“Good evening.”

A man officially recorded as having escaped—

then died miserably somewhere in the deep sea—

showed his real face.

**Night Hound.**

Vikir.

And in his hand—

Winter Sword Orwell.

More precisely—

the fragment that functioned as one of the keys to Nouvelle Vague’s Gate of Good and Evil.

Bastille looked even more horrified.

“Why do you have that?!”

“That should be in Headmaster Winston Smith’s possession!”

Questions piled up.

Vikir answered none of them.

Then—

shadows gathered.

Level Eight.

Level Nine.

Powerful prisoners converged on his position.

“What a waste.”

“D’Ordume was supposed to be mine.”

“Kid’s stronger than he looks.”

“What is with that guard uniform?”

“When is Orca getting here?”

“I could drop D’Ordume with one hit too.”

“If I had eaten properly first.”

Every eye—

settled on the key in Vikir’s hand.

Vikir spoke simply.

“Friends.”

“I have no intention of monopolizing this.”

If he wanted to keep it—

he would never have thrown it into the riot earlier.

Vikir flung the Orwell fragment again.

It spun through the air—

then landed directly between the prisoners and guards.

He did not plan to retrieve it.

The key had already done its job twice.

It had become the greatest possible distraction.

“It’s real!”

“The key!”

“Open the Gate!”

“We can go back to the surface!”

“Wait!”

“Weren’t we supposed to steal the Gulper Eels first?!”

The prisoners surged toward it.

Vikir watched for only a moment.

Then turned the opposite direction.

While everyone looked upward—

toward Level One and the Gate—

he looked deeper.

Level Ten.

**Poseidon.**

The human side’s ultimate hidden weapon—

sleeping within a sphere surrounded by blue volcanic fire.

Ordinary prisoners focused on the front gate.

The clever ones—

on the Gulper Eel kennel at Level Five.

Vikir—

who knew the future—

wanted Level Ten.

He ran.

The key fell behind him.

Another bloody struggle erupted over it.

*Bringing Orwell was absolutely worth it.*

What prison guard—

or prisoner—

could ignore a key to the prison itself?

For an instant—

Vikir remembered Professor Sadi.

During the Academy incident—

she had taken Winter Sword Orwell from Winston.

Without that—

this distraction would not have been possible.

*If she’s alive—

I should thank her if we meet again.*

Sadi—

who had operated as Ms. Ouroboros—

had disappeared after the Winston and Nouvelle Vague-related incidents on the surface.

But Vikir remembered their exchange clearly.

*Give me Orwell.*

*Fine~*

*I need the “front gate key” too, but I can find another way.*

*…*

*Promise me one thing.*

*If you get the chance—

take someone out with you.*

*Understood.*

*Wonderful.*

Keeping that promise—

was another reason Vikir had to reach Level Ten.

Then—

WHIZZ!

A sharpened stone projectile grazed his cheek.

Vikir leaned back.

At his speed—

a direct hit could have shattered his skull.

He looked ahead.

Only one person had ignored the key.

Souaré.

One of Nouvelle Vague’s twin combat pillars.

She stood at the staircase to the lower floors.

Her gaze moved from Vikir—

to the fallen D’Ordume.

“You’re the one I threw into solitary.”

“So you owe me too.”

“Yes.”

Vikir nodded.

“I’ll repay both debts today.”

“If you can.”

Her hammer met Beelzebub.

BOOM!

Shockwaves crawled across the floor like serpents.

Fire.

Steam.

Dust.

Rock.

Hammer and demonic blade clashed again and again.

Souaré’s weapon heated red—

yet she kept losing ground.

*So that’s why D’Ordume went down in one hit.*

Strength.

Speed.

Experience.

No obvious weakness.

Souaré changed tactics.

Magma surged beneath Vikir.

He cut through it.

The molten rock cooled—

burst into dense dust—

and another wave rose behind it.

Then another.

“…Annoying.”

These attacks were not meant to kill him.

They were meant to delay him.

Souaré had understood that Vikir created the riot in order to reach somewhere deeper.

“Whatever you’re planning—

give it up.”

“My defense is much better than D’Ordume’s.”

She laughed through cigar smoke.

The two Wardens were opposites.

D’Ordume—

attack.

Souaré—

defense.

She drew stone and metal from the surrounding terrain—

building layer after layer of shields.

Vikir frowned.

*Bad matchup.*

He still intended to defeat her.

But it would take time.

Then—

BOOM!

A strike came from the side.

So loud that nearby junior guards clutched bleeding ears.

Souaré’s head snapped sideways.

Her body flew horizontally—

hit the wall—

and collapsed with white eyes.

The same result as D’Ordume.

“…?”

Dust drifted away.

A slender figure stood with one foot on Souaré’s face.

The surrounding whispers spread immediately.

“The new prisoner!”

“Is that her?”

“The one marked for Level Ten?”

“She came in without trial and knocked Souaré out in one hit?”

“Another monster…”

Vikir remembered the rumor.

*A woman.*

*Direct Level Nine.*

*Future Level Ten.*

Black hair streaked with silver.

Sharp triangular ears.

Ash-black markings across the face.

And beneath a fierce expression—

clear eyes like a lake.

She looked at Vikir.

Then smiled.

“Long time no see, Brother.”

After the old Night Fox Akwilla—

a new Night Fox had risen.

**Aiyen.**
