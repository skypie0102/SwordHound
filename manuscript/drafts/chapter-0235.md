The next morning.

Before first period—

Dolores entered—

the newspaper club room.

Dark circles—

under her eyes.

Student council work—

had lasted until dawn.

*National University League next week.*

*Too much work.*

Colosseo.

Magic Tower.

Varangian.

Temisquira.

The Empire's four premier academies—

would soon gather—

for their annual competition.

Every official student organization—

was busy.

Especially—

the student council.

Still—

Dolores's eyes remained bright.

*Finish club work this morning.*

*Class.*

*Free afternoon.*

*Then find Vikir.*

She opened the door—

energetically.

“Good morning!”

Silence.

Then—

smiles.

Strange smiles.

“Our Dolores—

finally has spring in her life.”

“A romance with a younger man.”

“How nice.”

“He's lower-year—

but Dolores is actually younger.”

“Campus couple!”

“You didn't tell us!”

“We had to read about our best friend's love life—

in the newspaper?”

“…?”

Dolores blinked.

Someone—

held out a newspaper.

Not the Academy paper.

An outside publication.

A reporter—

had apparently infiltrated campus.

> **[Exclusive] Spring Comes to Colosseo's Student Council President?**
>
> Student Council President Dolores L. Quovadis is reportedly in a romantic relationship…
>
> Her partner is said to be a first-year student, Mr. V…
>
> The two are believed to have grown close during volunteer work…
>
> Following an ardent pursuit by Dolores, the relationship appears to have succeeded…
>
> Quovadis has not issued a statement…

Dolores—

went blank.

“…What—

is this?”

So—

the recent increase in outside paparazzi—

had been about her.

Her third-year friends—

closed in.

“It's Vikir, right?”

“The first-year?”

“The one from Naphtali?”

“The one dressed as a witch during the festival?”

“If it's Vikir—

we approve.”

“Quiet.”

“Diligent.”

“And handsome.”

“Why didn't you tell us?”

“So your standards were just too high.”

Dolores flushed—

from frustration—

more than anything else.

“No!”

“That's not it!”

“Vikir is just…”

“Suspicious!”

“There are too many strange things about him!”

That—

made everything worse.

“That's how it begins.”

“A little curiosity.”

“We heard the prologue.”

“Now the main story.”

“Suspicious?”

“His face is suspiciously handsome.”

“His eyes.”

“His nose.”

“His skin.”

“Is he even human?”

“And that witch costume!”

Even now—

people still talked about it.

Dolores realized—

resistance was useless.

She escaped.

“Ugh…”

“Now—

finding him will be even harder.”

For a while—

meeting Vikir openly—

would only feed the rumor.

Then—

voices—

behind a building.

“Agh!”

“That crazy dog again!”

“Get it!”

“It bit me!”

Dolores stopped.

Familiar voices.

She turned the corner.

Six second-years.

Three boys.

Three girls.

The same **Cold Department Class B** group—

she had nearly expelled.

Pal Uspear.

Betty Realbelt.

Houser Yellop.

Seaweed Aimecom.

Bison Redmin.

Oiler Southmiddle.

And—

in the middle—

a black puppy.

“Grrrr…”

**Choco.**

Dolores's eyes sharpened.

The six students' families—

had sent representatives—

begging for leniency.

The students had signed promises—

never again—

to prey on the weak.

*Again?*

Dolores stepped forward.

“You six!”

“You're doing this again!?”

“This time—

I really will have you expelled—”

She stopped.

Because—

the six—

were the ones bleeding.

Clothes—

torn.

Arms—

scratched.

Legs—

bitten.

Choco moved—

like a miniature predator.

Small body.

Sharp teeth.

Sharp claws.

Speed—

far beyond an ordinary dog.

The six ran—

crying.

“If not for our promise to the Saintess—!”

“Even with mana—

I can't catch it!”

“Run!”

“What is wrong with that dog!?”

Dolores—

watched—

stunned.

Choco chased them—

for a while.

Then turned—

and trotted away.

“Choco!”

The black dog—

turned its head.

Recognized—

the name.

Dolores approached.

Reached.

Choco backed away.

She followed—

and finally—

picked him up.

“Hm?”

She smelled—

something familiar—

in the fur.

Not street dirt.

Shampoo.

Academy dormitory—

basic issue.

*Someone bathed him.*

*His owner is probably a student.*

*Who?*

That—

was another clue.

Dolores looked at him.

“You're stronger than you look.”

“But you can't fight everyone—

just because those six ran from you.”

“Don't become reckless.”

Choco—

looked away.

Dolores narrowed her eyes—

half joking.

“Keep ignoring me—

and I'll have you neutered.”

Ears—

straight up.

Then—

whoosh.

The dog escaped—

and ran.

“Choco!”

Gone.

Dolores sighed.

“Never lets me get close.”

“Just like someone else.”

Recently—

she had been—

rejected—

or escaped from—

far too often.

Night Hound.

Vikir.

Choco.

Then—

a groan.

“…Ugh.”

One of the six students—

had failed to escape completely.

**Pal Uspear**—

lay on the ground.

Dolores stood over him.

“Why were you bullying the puppy again?”

“President—

this time—

it attacked us first!”

“That tiny dog?”

“You saw it!”

“Those teeth!”

“Those claws!”

“It's faster than our family's trained wolves!”

“Enough excuses.”

Pal Uspear—

looked genuinely wronged.

Then muttered.

“First the Night Hound—

now this.”

“Why is my luck so bad…”

Dolores froze.

“…What did you say?”

“My luck?”

“Before that.”

“…Night Hound?”

Pal Uspear moved his bangs.

A faded—

cross-shaped scar—

on his forehead.

“I got this—

when a masked lunatic caught us—

on a night street.”

“A masked lunatic?”

“At the time—

I didn't know.”

“But thinking back—

he looked just like the Night Hound.”

Dolores's attention—

sharpened completely.

The six—

had met the masked man—

before—

Night Hound's crimes became public.

“When?”

“The freshman welcome party?”

“Yes.”

“Before anyone called him Night Hound?”

“Yes.”

“Before the Imperial Capital attacks?”

“Yes.”

“I remember—

the golden bird-beak mask.”

“And the voice—

like metal scraping.”

Dolores thought—

fast.

The six had even hired—

private investigators.

Found—

nothing.

That was unsurprising now.

The Imperial Guard—

and Imperial Army—

could not catch him either.

But—

one question mattered.

*Why them?*

Dolores had investigated—

all six—

when preparing expulsion.

Their activity—

barely extended beyond—

home territories—

and the Academy.

No significant outside enemies.

No obvious link—

to Imperial terrorism.

So—

why would Night Hound—

target them—

before becoming publicly known?

*He has a connection—

to this Academy.*

The suspicion—

became stronger.

Pal Uspear shivered.

“He knew our names.”

“Our parents' names.”

“Where our families lived.”

Dolores calculated.

*That much Academy information…*

*Probably not a first-year.*

*Second-year?*

*Third-year?*

A theory—

she had considered before—

now gained weight.

Night Hound—

almost certainly had—

an information source—

inside Colosseo.

And—

a much smaller possibility—

Night Hound himself—

might be—

inside the Academy.

Dolores looked down.

White light—

spread.

Pal Uspear's wounds—

closed.

Then—

her voice hardened.

“What you just told me—

stays secret.”

“What?”

“Tell no one.”

“I'll handle it.”

He nodded—

quickly.

People like him—

weak against the strong.

Dolores made him promise—

to keep the other five silent too.

Then—

one final warning.

“If I hear this story—

from anyone else—

you will be expelled.”

Pal Uspear—

went pale.

Dolores turned away.

The investigation—

had become real.
