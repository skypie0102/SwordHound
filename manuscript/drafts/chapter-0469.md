*Go away.*

Darkness.

Once again—

Pigi found himself inside the same kind of vision.

The difference—

was that this time he recognized it.

*…A childhood memory?*

A small version of himself stood nearby.

Facing a middle-aged man.

The face felt familiar.

Yet—

impossible to remember.

Blurred behind some mental fog.

*Trash like this is not of my blood.*

A memory from so long ago—

Pigi himself had forgotten it.

Just like the vision from his first-year midterm—

when he nearly died.

*I really do see a lot of hallucinations.*

At the Academy.

Inside the Hell Tree.

Now.

Whenever death came close—

visions followed.

The crying—

never became easier.

His mother.

Sobbing.

And voices around her.

Contempt.

Hatred.

*Where did this lowborn bastard come from?*

*Throw him out.*

*…Get rid of him.*

The scene changed.

His mother—

running.

Forests.

Mountains.

Pursuers.

A cliff.

A violent river.

Hungry wolves.

The startled faces of passing merchants and mercenaries.

Time moved.

Memories followed.

His mother—

who almost never smiled.

Always looking at Pigi with sadness.

Then—

a new father.

Kind.

Steady.

Supporting her.

Giving Pigi love without reservation.

Working until the family finally became stable.

Slowly—

his mother began smiling again.

Then came Colosseo Academy.

The Empire’s greatest school.

Pigi had wanted to prove something.

To his mother—

who had suffered.

And to the father who had spent his life helping her recover.

But school—

had not gone well.

He was bullied.

Too timid.

Too physically weak.

His mind was excellent—

but practical tests repeatedly crushed his confidence.

Then—

Hell Tree.

Classmates killing classmates.

Then—

a burning world.

Monsters everywhere.

Civil war.

A world collapsing.

And yet—

Pigi endured.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Why?

*It’s okay now.*

A warm voice came from behind.

Friends.

People who had always stood behind him.

That was why the world had not twisted Pigi into something else.

Pigi smiled.

“…What a good person.”

Then—

he remembered.

*That voice…*

He had heard it while fighting Amdusias.

Chimeries.

Andrealphus.

Whenever Pigi’s blood entered a demon—

that strange auditory hallucination appeared.

*A good person.*

A voice that had confused demon after demon.

Then—

WHOOSH.

A face appeared directly in front of him.

*Didn’t you hear me?*

*I told you to get out.*

That voice—

was—

◆◆◆

“…Ah!?”

Pigi opened his eyes.

A face filled his vision.

Vikir.

Looking down at him.

“You’re okay now, Pigi.”

Exactly—

the same voice.

Pigi’s vision blurred.

He managed a weak smile.

“…You really are a good person.”

“Before.”

“And now.”

“Pigi!”

“Stay awake!”

“The bleeding stopped!”

Vikir shook him.

But Pigi had already lost too much blood.

The Hell Tree seeds had spread like shrapnel.

Deep.

Through flesh.

Through organs.

Pigi swallowed a mouthful of blood.

“My only real talent is closing the Hell Tree.”

“I can’t do much else…”

“But I wanted to help.”

“Don’t talk nonsense.”

Pigi shook his head.

“Go.”

“….”

“Kill the demon.”

“Save the world.”

He forced his eyes open.

“If I can’t become a hero—

being a hero’s friend is enough.”

“Go!”

Vikir’s teeth clenched.

Ahead—

the Reaper still stood with his scythe.

Behind him—

Flauros’s long-distance teleportation circle approached completion.

“Move.”

Vikir lowered Pigi carefully.

Then charged the Reaper.

CLANG!

The Reaper accepted the strike.

Deliberately.

Buying time.

Vikir’s sword crashed down.

The scythe rose to meet it.

They locked.

Then—

“…I know who you are.”

Pigi’s voice.

The Reaper flinched.

Pigi—

dying—

looked at him as though he could see something no one else could.

“…He is a demon.”

“You’re being deceived by a lie.”

The Reaper said nothing.

“And…”

Pigi coughed black blood.

“You have the ability to see through that lie.”

Another cough.

More blood.

“I don’t think you’re a bad person.”

“Because…”

“We were friends.”

Pigi smiled.

His arm—

fell.

“PIGI!”

Tudor and Sancho screamed.

They tried to reach him—

but the remaining guards blocked the path.

The Reaper raised the scythe.

Deflected Vikir’s blade.

Blood sprayed from the Reaper’s chest and limbs.

But—

his job was done.

The teleportation circle behind him—

was complete.

[Come.]

[My brave son.]

Flauros smiled.

Strangely—

even while retreating after losing his troops—

he looked satisfied.

A memory seemed to cross the demon’s expression.

A request.

A target.

*Kill one man.*

*If he is alive now—*

*he should look like this.*

Then Flauros shouted.

[Our purpose here is complete!]

[RETREAT!]

[Everyone else—

survive however you can!]

The battlefield froze.

“…!”

Vikir heard it.

Not relief.

Not desperation.

Achievement.

Satisfaction.

He looked toward Pigi.

Bleeding.

Motionless.

And understood.

*Damn it.*

The full army.

The assault on Tochka.

The water-source trap.

Flauros had not come here primarily to kill the demon hunter.

His true objective—

had been **Pigi**.

[We can continue our conversation later, demon hunter.]

[It will not be long.]

[Today was merely an introduction.]

[Soon you will understand.]

[My poison vessels are not limited to this.]

Light gathered around Flauros.

Then Vikir spoke.

“Pigi will not die.”

His voice—

flat.

Absolute.

“Only you will.”

Eight fangs ripped forward.

The attack tore into the teleportation array.

More violent than anything Vikir had used that night.

Even Flauros—

sweated.

[SON!]

[STOP HIM!]

The Reaper immediately raised his scythe.

Then—

Vikir spoke to him.

“…We were friends.”

“Weren’t we?”

“Friends trust each other.”

The Reaper froze.

The hood shifted.

For one instant—

his face was exposed.

Vikir met his eyes.

Straight on.

That single hesitation—

changed everything.

BOOM!

The Eighth Form bit into the column of teleportation light.

[AAAAAGH!?]

Flauros screamed.

The magic circle shook violently.

Crimson poison mist.

Light.

Both swallowed Flauros and the Reaper.

Then—

silence.

The battlefield—

which moments ago had been chaos—

became still as a grave.

A demon escaped.

A friend lay apparently dead.

The reinforcements that had arrived too late—

could only stare.

Far away—

the allied army finished off scattered Poison Human remnants.

Camus.

Aiyen.

Dolores.

Sinclaire.

Kirko.

Everyone—

stood behind Vikir.

No one spoke.

Then Vikir’s lips moved.

Heavy.

Like lead.

“The real battle is about to begin.”

Every face hardened.

This—

had not been the real battle.

Greater sacrifices were coming.

The final act—

was drawing near.
