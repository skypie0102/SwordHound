# Chapter 399: Black Tongue (1)

Step.

Step.

Two sets of footsteps echoed through the damp, salty corridor.

Major Garam Nord walked first.

Captain Kirko Grimm followed.

Vikir—

still wearing Garam’s identity—

was heading toward the hearing-and-audit chamber.

Toward Lieutenant Colonel **Black Tongue**.

*At last.*

*I’ve met all five Wardens.*

D'Ordume.

Souaré.

BDISSEM.

Flubber.

Each had been strange in a different way.

But Black Tongue’s reputation was worse than all of them.

More unpleasant.

More vicious.

More unpredictable.

*How does someone rank lower than Flubber in a popularity poll?*

Among junior and mid-level guards—

Black Tongue always placed dead last.

Even when repeat votes were allowed.

Vikir glanced back.

“Adjutant.”

“Yes.”

Kirko’s face brightened slightly.

This was the first time Vikir had addressed her first since becoming her superior.

The expression vanished with his next question.

“You hate Lieutenant Colonel Black Tongue.”

“….”

“I heard you grinding your teeth earlier.”

Kirko stayed quiet.

Then said:

“I do not speak ill of superior officers.”

“Then state facts.”

Kirko exhaled.

If her superior ordered facts—

facts she could give.

“Name unknown.”

“Age unknown.”

“Rank: Lieutenant Colonel.”

“Known as **Black Tongue**.”

“Also called **the Guard Who Catches Guards**.”

“His tongue is black.”

“And he belongs to the Hearing Audit unit.”

Vikir nodded.

“I heard he uses a sword.”

“And that he is extremely strong.”

“Yes.”

“In raw combat ability—

people say he may exceed D'Ordume or Souaré.”

“But his temperament is extreme.”

“His service evaluations are terrible.”

“He has enough seniority and ability for Colonel—

but remains Lieutenant Colonel.”

“Internal corruption.”

“Military-discipline violations.”

“Internal punishment.”

“Inspections.”

“He handles all of it alone?”

“In theory.”

“In practice—

incidents serious enough to involve him are rare.”

“Unless Black Tongue simply decides to involve himself.”

“Like today.”

“Unpredictable.”

“Yes.”

Kirko’s voice became colder.

“And not merely unpredictable.”

“He has severe impulse-control problems.”

“And sadistic sexual tendencies.”

“He has abused prisoners.”

“Molested and tortured guards.”

“Many of his victims died.”

“Others were crippled.”

“Some simply disappeared.”

Then Kirko began listing names.

“Twenty-three years ago—

Lieutenant **Valentine Grimm**.”

“Lieutenant Farran Farrandel.”

“Twelve years ago—

Lieutenant Yang Bortz.”

“Captain Gordick.”

“Seven years ago—

Captain Aubry.”

“Major Jean Dayle.”

“Three years ago—

Second Lieutenant Marsong.”

“Lieutenant Yodle.”

“And one year ago—

Lieutenant **Virginia**.”

“She was my classmate.”

One name in the list—

clearly mattered more than the others.

Valentine Grimm.

*So that was why she was grinding her teeth.*

Vikir looked away from Kirko’s frozen expression.

Meeting someone tied to the darkest part of one’s past—

required courage.

◆◆◆

Black Tongue’s Hearing Audit chamber lay deep inside a dark limestone cave.

The route twisted constantly.

Left.

Right.

Up.

Down.

Steep.

Narrow.

Confusing.

Yet there was only one path.

No risk of becoming lost.

“This place is absurdly crooked.”

Vikir looked around.

“No normal person lives somewhere like this.”

“I usually dislike judging people by where they live.”

Kirko nodded.

“This time—

I agree.”

Eventually the tunnel widened.

Stalactites hung like teeth.

Saltwater ran down stone walls.

Deep pools gathered beneath them.

And on the floor—

“…What is that?”

Kirko stared.

Black spheres.

Roughly a meter across.

Soft.

Egg-like.

At first only a few.

Then more.

Across the floor.

Walls.

Even the ceiling.

Vikir nudged one with his foot.

GRRRK—

It moved.

Red and yellow fluid began seeping from the surface.

Kirko grimaced.

“Ugh.”

“That smell…”

“Rotting meat.”

Vikir drew his three-section baton.

THUD!

The sphere convulsed.

Its contents erupted from a small opening.

“…!”

Kirko’s eyes widened.

A corpse.

A shriveled skull.

Thick blood.

Vikir kicked several neighboring spheres.

POP.

CRACK.

GURGLE.

Each disgorged something different.

Skeletons.

Blood clotted like ink.

Yellow fat.

Chunks of flesh.

Mummies.

Vikir understood.

“Leeches.”

Parasites that attached to prey—

and drained them.

But these Nouvelle Vague leeches were specialized.

Some drank blood.

Some consumed bone.

Some flesh.

A blood-feeding leech could leave a person as a dry mummy.

A bone-feeding leech could remove the skeleton and leave a living sack of blood and organs inside skin.

A flesh-feeding leech left bone and blood—

the blood coagulating around the skeleton like jelly.

These specimens had grown enormous.

Each had swallowed a person.

Then inflated into a black sphere while digesting them.

And—

astonishingly—

some victims were still alive.

A whisper came from one.

“K-Kirko…”

Barely louder than air.

Kirko went rigid.

“Virginia?!”

Lieutenant Virginia.

Kirko’s former classmate.

Caught helping a low-ranking unit move misappropriated supplies.

At worst—

she had stolen only a few bags of candy intended for birthday gifts.

Black Tongue took her for interrogation.

She never returned.

The voice came from one of the black spheres.

SHING!

Kirko drew her sword.

Aura struck the parasite’s hide.

Slowly—

the tough membrane split.

Kirko tore it open.

SLURP—

Fluid poured out.

And inside—

Virginia.

“…!”

Kirko covered her mouth.

Virginia lay on the ground.

Weeping.

Her body had been destroyed by the parasite.

Vikir stepped closer.

“A bone-feeding one.”

Virginia moved her lips.

Barely.

One last request.

“Let…”

“me…”

“die…”

Kirko could not move.

Could not answer.

Vikir did.

SHK.

The hound ended Virginia’s life—

quickly.

With as little pain as possible.

“Ah…”

Kirko dropped to her knees.

Tears blurred her eyes.

She cradled what remained of her old comrade.

Then—

“Hehehehehe.”

A voice came from deeper inside the cave.

“What are you doing?”

A figure emerged between the countless parasites.

Black—

even against the darkness.

Vikir narrowed his eyes.

The last of Nouvelle Vague’s Five Wardens had finally appeared.

**Lieutenant Colonel Black Tongue.**
