The next day—

Vikir attended the formal ceremony announcing the first-stage results.

Colosseo’s freshmen—

had done something unprecedented.

They had beaten second- and third-years from other academies—

and filled the top thirty.

Six first-years—

Vikir.

Sinclaire.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

Granola.

Had entered the top ten.

Now—

only the second-stage individual assignments remained.

Complete one—

and the ranker earned access to the treasure vault jointly administered by the four academies.

Inside slept treasures that could not simply be bought.

**Talking Window.**

**Time-Stopping Hourglass.**

**Money-Eating Hat.**

**Love Barrier.**

**Orb of Sanity.**

**Arrow with Eyes.**

And more.

The ten successful rankers would eventually enter the vault—

choose an artifact—

and be chosen in return.

*But first—*

Vikir walked toward the awards stage.

*I need to complete the assignment.*

The moment seventh-place Vikir appeared—

cheers erupted.

“Aaaah! Vikir!”

“Brother! Look this way!”

“I love you!”

Temisquira.

Not Colosseo.

Even Merelini Lovegood—

Temisquira’s president and the second-place finisher—

had not received cheers that loud.

Lovegood did not look offended.

Far from it.

“Oh my.”

She stayed unusually close to Vikir.

“To see you this near…”

“I really am a successful fan.”

Lovegood had apparently accepted her own students’ obsession.

Mostly—

because she shared it.

“By the way, Vikir.”

She brightened.

“We founded your fan club yesterday.”

“?”

“It’s called ‘Re.’”

“??”

“The joke is simple.”

Her heart-shaped eyes shone.

“Your sexiness has already passed *Do*, reached *Re*, and is almost at *Mi*.”

“???”

Vikir—

who had faced demons with less confusion—

had no answer.

Temisquira’s students began booing.

“Down with the president’s monopoly!”

“Dictator!”

“Share Vikir!”

“The student council president should put the public good above private desire!”

“Have some shame, old woman!”

“Impeach her!”

Lovegood’s face tightened.

She turned and mouthed furiously at them.

*Shut up.*

*I want a romance too.*

*Can’t you pity your big sister?*

The response became louder.

“Boooo!”

“You’re the only one allowed to date?”

“We suffer too!”

“If this is how it is, make Temisquira coeducational!”

Lovegood sighed.

Then returned to Vikir.

“So…”

“Would you consider a joint mixer after the League?”

“Colosseo and Temisquira.”

The idea—

students from the two academies volunteering to meet socially—

did not interest Vikir.

Everyone else—

immediately became interested.

“I’m Vikir’s right arm!”

Tudor raised his hand first.

“Then I’m the left!”

Sancho followed.

“Left leg!”

Pigi joined.

More boys crowded in.

“Right leg!”

“Left eye!”

“Right eye!”

“Left ear!”

“Save the right ear for me!”

“I’m Vikir’s heart!”

“Stomach!”

“Lungs!”

“Spine!”

“Chest!”

“Waist!”

“Then I’m the ass!”

“I won’t name the body part—but everyone agrees it’s the most important one!”

Vikir—

was being dismembered metaphorically in real time.

“Enough.”

Dolores appeared.

“The professors are almost finished.”

“The award recipients need to go forward.”

“Stop causing trouble.”

Colosseo’s students backed away.

Temisquira’s followed.

Except—

Lovegood’s eyes narrowed at Dolores.

Dolores tilted her head.

“I’ll concede first place in the League.”

Lovegood pointed.

“But I will not concede Vikir.”

“Don’t get careless just because you attend the same academy.”

“…?”

“Don’t pretend you don’t know!”

Lovegood flushed.

“I heard what you said on the terrace yesterday!”

Dolores stared.

Lovegood began quoting.

“You’re special.”

“If you weren’t, I wouldn’t notice you everywhere.”

“?”

“Recently, you’re the only person I keep seeing.”

“??”

“Open your heart to me!”

“???”

Color drained from Dolores’s face.

*Did I really say those things?*

She had.

Only—

not like that.

Not in that context.

She spun around.

Colosseo’s students were staring.

Narrow-eyed.

“No!”

“This is a misunderstanding!”

“You have to hear the whole conversation!”

“Those lines alone completely change the—”

“Winners.”

Banshee’s cold voice cut through the noise.

“Forward.”

The speeches were over.

The awards ceremony began.

Despite his academy’s spectacular performance—

he looked strangely displeased.

“…Congratulations.”

A pause.

“Now we assign the individual tasks.”

That—

was his entire congratulatory speech.

No student complained.

Scrolls were laid out.

The apprenticeship system—

and the academy itself—

existed in part to prepare talented students for the powers that governed the Empire.

The University League—

extended that principle into real work.

More than ten scrolls were available.

Enough—

that contestants could choose.

Vikir looked across them.

*One step closer to Decarabia.*

Complete the assignment written on the chosen scroll.

Receive certification.

Then—

the vault.

Sinclaire—

ranked eighth—

chose Quovadis as she had planned.

The outside of the scroll listed the family—

and the difficulty.

**<House Quovadis / Difficulty — ★★★☆>**

The Imperial territories were suffering from a growing number of heresies and cults.

Her assignment—

approach the problem from the perspective of the Quovadis Inquisition.

Propose a method to eliminate the dangerous sects—

while increasing the prestige of the Rune faith.

An Inquisition task.

Likely—

work connected to **Mozgus Quovadis**—

Archbishop.

Inquisitor.

“I expected something like this.”

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Sinclaire refused to explain quickly.

The idea—

apparently—

was too complicated to summarize.

Dolores—

ranked first—

selected another scroll.

**<House Bourgeois / Difficulty — ★★★★>**

The Imperial Mint had recently issued high-denomination banknotes.

Counterfeiting had risen with them.

From House Bourgeois’s perspective—

identify a business likely to benefit from currency reform—

then propose either a new venture or an improvement to an existing one.

A problem linked to **Bourgeois Damien**—

head of the Imperial Mint.

not the sword.

Baskerville.
