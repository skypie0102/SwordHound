The next day—

Vikir attended the ceremony announcing the first-round rankings.

Colosseo Academy’s first-years had beaten second- and third-years from other schools and pushed their way into the top thirty.

That alone was enough to stun the entire League.

But six first-years had gone even farther.

Vikir.

Sinclaire.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

Granola.

All six had entered the top ten.

Now only one step remained.

If the ten highest-ranked students completed the individual assignments given to them for the second round, they would earn the right to claim priceless artifacts from the treasure vault jointly administered by the four academies.

Among the treasures sleeping inside were things like—

the Talking Window,

the Time-Stopping Hourglass,

the Money-Eating Hat,

the Shield of Love,

the Orb of Sanity,

the Eyed Arrow—

and many more.

Treasures of the century.

Things that could not simply be bought, no matter how much money one possessed.

The ten rankers who completed their assignments would be allowed into the vault to choose.

And even then—

the artifacts themselves would also have to choose them.

*Before that, I have a task to finish.*

Vikir thought as he approached the award platform.

The moment the seventh-ranked student appeared—

cheers exploded from below.

“Aaaah! It’s Vikir!”

“Big Brother! Look over here!”

“I love you!”

Every shout came from the Temisquira section.

Merelini Lovegood—

Temisquira Women’s College student council president and second place overall—

had not received anything close to that level of cheering.

Lovegood did not seem disappointed.

If anything—

she was delighted.

“Oh my, Vikir.”

She beamed from far too close.

“To see you at this distance… I must be the luckiest fangirl alive.”

By her own description—

she had become a “successful fangirl.”

Even below the award platform, she stayed glued to Vikir’s side.

The professors’ speeches were almost finished.

The winners would soon be called forward.

In the middle of the excitement, Lovegood turned to him.

“By the way, Vikir.”

“I founded your fan club yesterday.”

“?”

“It’s called ‘Re.’”

“??”

“Because your sexiness has already passed ‘Do’ and is heading straight toward ‘Mi’!”

“???”

Vikir stared at her.

The wordplay was ridiculous.

Lovegood looked back at him with unmistakable affection.

Her naturally heart-shaped eyes made the effect worse.

Then—

boos erupted from behind them.

Temisquira’s students had begun rebelling against their own president.

“Boooo! End the president’s monopoly!”

“Down with the dictator!”

“Share Vikir with the public!”

“A president should prioritize the public interest over private desire!”

“Know your place, old woman!”

“Impeach her!”

“Impeach!”

Lovegood’s brow twitched.

She turned toward her supposed supporters and mouthed silently—

*Shut up.*

*I want a romance too.*

*Don’t you feel sorry for your big sister?*

The response was immediate.

“BOOOOOOO!”

“You’re the only one who gets to date!”

“We’re suffering too!”

“If you’re going to act like this, make Temisquira coeducational!”

Lovegood’s attempt at justification only made things worse.

At last she sighed and turned back to Vikir.

“Um… Vikir.”

“After the League, would you consider joining a mixer?”

She clarified.

“A joint Colosseo–Temisquira one.”

A mixer was an event where interested students from each academy were selected and introduced to members of the opposite sex.

Vikir had no interest whatsoever.

Everyone else—

apparently did.

“I am Vikir’s right arm!”

Tudor raised his hand first.

“Then I am the left arm!”

Sancho immediately joined in.

“I’ll be the left leg!”

Pigi raised his hand too.

Other boys crowded around Vikir.

“I’m the right leg!”

“Then I get the left eye!”

“I’ll take the right eye!”

“I’m the left ear!”

“Who gets the right ear?”

“I am Vikir’s heart!”

“I am the stomach!”

“I’m the lungs!”

“I’m the spine!”

“I’m Vikir’s belly!”

“I’m the chest!”

“I’m the waist!”

“Then I’ll be the ass!”

“I can’t say exactly which body part I am, but everyone agrees it’s the most important one!”

Vikir was being dismembered metaphorically in real time.

Then—

“That is enough.”

Dolores appeared.

“The professors are almost done speaking.”

“The award ceremony is about to begin.”

“We need to go onstage, so stop causing trouble.”

She pushed the Colosseo crowd back.

Temisquira’s students retreated with them.

Lovegood, however—

did not.

Her gaze had fixed on Dolores.

Suspiciously.

Dolores, who normally got along with her, looked puzzled.

Lovegood spoke first.

“I’ll concede first place in the University League.”

“But I will not concede Vikir.”

“So don’t get careless just because you attend the same school.”

“…?”

Dolores tilted her head.

Lovegood raised her voice.

“Don’t play dumb!”

“I heard everything you said on the terrace yesterday!”

Dolores went blank.

Lovegood began reciting the lines she had overheard.

“You are special.”

“Otherwise, there is no way I would keep noticing you anywhere and everywhere.”

“?”

“These days, my eyes keep finding only you.”

“??”

“Open your heart to me!”

“???”

Dolores’s face lost all color.

Had she really said those things?

*…I think I did.*

But not like that.

Not with that meaning.

Someone had clearly taken them out of context.

Dolores turned.

The Colosseo students were now staring at her with narrowed eyes.

“Oh—no.”

She began to panic.

“This is a misunderstanding.”

“That was only part of the conversation.”

“You need the full context before you interpret those lines—”

Her timing could not have been worse.

A cold voice cut across the hall.

“You noisy awardees.”

Professor Banshee Morgue.

“Come forward.”

The professors’ speeches had ended.

The ceremony began.

◆◆◆

The ten highest scorers in this year’s University League took the platform.

First—

Dolores L. Quovadis.

Second—

Merelini Lovegood.

Third—

Bakilaga Juragio.

Fourth—

Highbro Le Baskerville.

Fifth—

Middlebro Le Baskerville.

Sixth—

Lowbro Le Baskerville.

Seventh—

Vikir.

Eighth—

Sinclaire.

Ninth—

Granola de Leviathan.

Tenth—

Oraorus Hohenheim.

Standing before them was Professor Banshee Morgue of Colosseo Academy, the academy that had produced the largest number of top-ranked students.

Despite Colosseo’s extraordinary result—

Banshee looked strangely displeased.

“…Congratulations.”

His tone was as dry as desert dust.

“Now it is time to assign the individual missions.”

That was the entire congratulatory speech.

The students were grateful.

Several scrolls were placed before the ten rankers.

The apprenticeship system linking the academies and the Seven Great Houses existed to produce talented people ready to enter those enormous institutions after graduation.

The University League’s second stage was an extension of that system.

Each student would choose a scroll.

Each scroll named a house—

and a task.

Vikir reached forward.

*One step closer to the Seventh Corpse, Decarabia.*

Once he completed the mission written on the scroll, the vault would be within reach.

One by one—

the ten students opened their assignments.

Sinclaire, ranked eighth, chose House Quovadis just as she had said she would.

The outside of each scroll listed the house and its general difficulty.

**House Quovadis — Difficulty: ★★★☆**

Three and a half stars.

Sinclaire opened it.

**Faithful House Quovadis / Difficulty: ★★★☆**

**The Empire is suffering from the growing spread of heresies and cults.**

**From the perspective of the Quovadis Inquisition, propose a method to suppress those heresies and cults while strengthening the standing of the Rune faith.**

An assignment related to the Quovadis Inquisition.

There was a good chance Sinclaire would be working under Mozgus Quovadis—

Archbishop.

Inquisitor.

All she had to do now was travel to House Quovadis and complete the commission.

“I expected something like this.”

Sinclaire smiled.

She looked as though she already had a plan.

Tudor, Sancho, Pigi, and Bianca immediately began asking questions.

Sinclaire only laughed and said it would take far too long to explain in words.

Apparently—

the plan was complicated.

Dolores, ranked first, selected another scroll.

**House Bourgeois — Difficulty: ★★★★**

Four stars.

Even before opening it, the assignment looked unpleasant.

She broke the seal.

**Tycoon House Bourgeois / Difficulty: ★★★★**

**The Imperial Mint has recently introduced high-denomination paper currency.**

**Counterfeiting has increased sharply as a result.**

**From the perspective of House Bourgeois, identify a product or industry that would benefit from currency reform and propose either a new business venture or an improvement to an existing one.**

This was related to Count Bourgeois Damian, head of the Imperial Mint.

A mission demanding brains rather than muscle.

The other rankers opened their scrolls as well.

Highbro received an assignment from House Donquixote concerning the Empire’s maritime territory.

**Subjugate sea monsters.**

Difficulty—

**★★★**

A straightforward mission.

Kill large numbers of monsters.

Middlebro drew a task from House Leviathan.

**Capture escaped experimental monsters.**

Difficulty—

**★★★☆**

A far uglier job.

Without significant poison resistance, it could become extremely dangerous.

Lowbro received a civil-engineering assignment from House Usher.

**Prevent the estate from flooding.**

Difficulty—

**★★☆**

Comparatively easy.

Also—

long.

Tedious.

There were Imperial Family assignments as well.

More scrolls had been prepared than there were students, so everyone had some freedom to choose.

And then—

something happened that shocked the entire hall.

Tap.

One hand settled on a dark-red scroll at the far edge of the selection.

Vikir.

He picked it without hesitation.

The label read—

**Iron-Blooded Sword House Baskerville / Difficulty: ★★★★★**

Five stars.

House Baskerville.

The family everyone avoided.

The worst reputation.

The worst difficulty.

And, according to common wisdom—

the worst assignments.
