A vast practice range.

Arrows struck distant targets in steady succession.

Thud.

Thud.

Thud.

Students who specialized in archery gathered here.

Mostly—

Cold Department archery students.

Occasionally—

Hot Department ranged-magic students with unusually long reach.

Ordinary students used bows only in electives—

so they rarely came.

Among the regulars—

one name stood above most.

**Bianca Fo Usher.**

One of the Cold Department's top first-years.

Daughter of the family known for divine archery.

Thud!

Without using mana—

Bianca planted an arrow in the bullseye a hundred meters away.

White ring.

Black.

Blue.

Red.

Yellow.

Every arrow sat inside the yellow center.

“Again.”

“Ten points.”

“That's insane.”

“She'll destroy the practical midterm.”

“Like a goddess of the hunt…”

The nearby students stared.

Bianca—

was dissatisfied.

*Two nines.*

*The wind isn't even bad today.*

Ten arrows.

Eight tens.

Two nines.

Ninety-eight points.

Normally—

acceptable.

Today—

not enough.

“…I don't like it.”

Then—

thud!

A sound from the neighboring lane.

Thud-thud-thud-thud!

No pause.

Before one arrow's impact stopped echoing—

another landed.

Ten shots.

Ten hits.

Perfect.

Bianca lowered her bow slightly.

“…Ten.”

She looked sideways.

Heavy bangs obscuring much of his face.

Plain clothes.

Fair skin.

A lean build slightly smaller than many boys his age.

**Vikir.**

Same Cold Department Class B.

Expressionless as ever—

holding an ordinary practice bow.

Creak—

he nocked another arrow.

One hundred meters.

The central yellow circle—

12.2 centimeters across.

Only 1.3 meters above the ground.

From here—

almost a pinhole.

Twannng.

Release.

Thud!

Ten points.

Again.

Students began gathering.

“He's accurate.”

“Can he even see the center from here?”

“I can barely see yellow.”

“That's Vikir, right?”

“The freshman who ranked first on the written exam?”

“The handsome one with the bangs?”

“So he's an archer?”

“He doesn't have much mana.”

“Archers can work with less mana anyway.”

Bianca found the situation irritating.

Normally—

when House Usher's daughter stood at a firing line—

eyes gathered around her.

She usually hated that.

It made her feel like a spectacle.

But now—

everyone was watching Vikir.

That alone did not bother her.

What bothered her—

was the unspoken judgment.

*They think he's better.*

Bianca examined his form.

Then frowned.

Terrible.

Nocking.

Grip.

Hooking.

Setup.

Drawing.

Anchor.

Full draw.

Release.

Follow-through.

None of it looked textbook.

No polished structure.

No recognizable school.

More like a hunter in a forest—

lifting a bow and firing however felt natural.

*How does that hit?*

She could not understand the mechanism.

Twannng.

Thud.

Another ten.

Bianca fired too.

Thud!

Ten.

Again.

Ten.

The scores remained close.

The shooting speed did not.

When Bianca carefully released one arrow—

Vikir had already fired five or six.

*Fast isn't everything.*

Her jaw tightened.

Except—

Vikir was fast *and* accurate.

Every rapid shot continued landing in the ten-ring.

Bianca's two early nines—

meant she was already two points behind.

*No more mistakes.*

A tiny early deviation—

became a huge difference over distance.

That was basic archery.

So Bianca focused harder.

Much harder.

Eventually—

her final arrow flew.

Thud!

Nine.

*Damn it.*

Sweat had shifted her thumb at release.

For one savage instant—

Bianca wanted to throw the bow.

Too many people were watching.

She forced herself still.

*Do not disgrace House Usher.*

If the eldest daughter of the famous archery house lost to the improvised shooting of a commoner—

people would laugh.

She reset.

Shot again through the full practice sequence.

By the time she finished—

the result was extraordinary.

**141 tens.**

**3 nines.**

Among those tens—

two perfect X-ring shots.

One of them had split a previous arrow.

**144 arrows.**

**1,437 / 1,440.**

Bianca's usual range sat around 1,433 to 1,435.

Today—

Vikir's presence had forced her concentration higher.

The students applauded.

“Amazing.”

“That's House Usher.”

“Every arrow near center.”

Then—

someone looked next door.

“Wait.”

“What about Vikir?”

Bianca turned too.

He had fired **143 arrows**.

One remained.

And for the first time all day—

he was taking his time.

Creak—

the bow stayed drawn.

No trembling.

No collapse in posture.

Everyone whispered.

“Why isn't he firing?”

“Concentrating?”

“What's he trying to do?”

“Ten is already maximum.”

“If he gets another ten—”

“that's 1,440.”

“A perfect score.”

“The Academy record is 1,439 at one hundred meters without mana.”

“About thirty years old, isn't it?”

“Set by the current family head when he studied here.”

“Too bad this isn't an official record attempt.”

The crowd grew quiet.

Bianca swallowed.

Then—

twang!

Vikir released.

The arrow crossed the hundred meters.

Thud.

It struck the target.

Everyone—

Bianca included—

stared.
