*…Ah'Heman?*

Vikir stared.

The undead body—

was dried and ruined.

But unmistakable.

Brown skin.

Black and silver hair.

Ballak tattoos.

**Ah'Heman.**

Shaman of Ballak.

Born from **Rokoko**.

An exceptionally talented black mage.

And—

dead.

Vikir remembered the end.

Ah'Heman's body—

destroyed through the Ballak conflict.

His final escape—

up the thorn tree.

The **Thorn-Tree Punishment** he had designed for others—

turning on its creator.

Then—

fire.

Burned by his own people.

Yet—

hatred had remained.

Tenacity.

Inferiority.

Grief.

Anger.

Jealousy.

Despair.

Greed.

Frustration.

Resentment.

Enough—

for death magic to grab.

The respected shaman—

was gone.

Click.

Click-click.

Only a corpse remained—

its lips rotted away, exposing its teeth and rotting gums.

The teeth clicked together again and again.

A faithful puppet.

The Corpse Queen looked at him.

[The Red and Black Mountains gave me something useful.]

[A relatively fresh corpse—

of a black mage.]

Ah'Heman—

for all his distortion—

had possessed immense magical talent.

Enough that Vikir had once compared his potential—

to **Adolf Morgue**.

Had Ah'Heman devoted himself fully to magic—

instead of desperately imitating the warriors around him—

he might have become a far greater threat.

Instead—

inferiority swallowed him.

Now—

the Abyss had finished the job.

Vikir remembered the others too.

A Donquixote knight who had become Dantalian's puppet.

Staffordshire of Baskerville.

Mozgus of Quovadis.

Rosie of Morgue.

All had become living-dead puppets—

toys of demons.

Collecting corpses and processing them into puppets was one of the demons' specialties.

Click-click-click.

Ah'Heman moved.

Darkness spread.

Bones—

throughout the ruins—

began moving again.

Necromancy had been Ah'Heman's specialty even while alive.

As a lich—

it became worse.

Black currents poured outward.

Three familiar corpses—

rose.

**Ephebo.**

**Pedo.**

**Hebe.**

Dantalian's servants—

destroyed once already.

Unlike Geronto—

their restoration was incomplete.

Bodies torn.

Missing pieces.

Ragged.

Then—

Ah'Heman moved one arm.

Crunch.

Flesh pulled toward flesh.

Bone—

toward bone.

Three bodies—

merged.

Missing pieces—

filled by the others.

One lich.

One flesh giant.

Both—

between Vikir and the Corpse Queen.

Black spear.

The giant—

fist.

“…Hm.”

obvious—

weaknesses.

He retreated.

Then—

the Corpse Queen joined.

Black fire spears—

fell.

melted.

Ah'Heman—

summoned hellfire.

The Corpse Queen.

Ah'Heman.

The giant.

forming.

*Bow alone isn't enough.*

ranged attacks.

Ah'Heman—

The giant—

massive defensive body.

Anubis—

had reached its limit for this formation.

Then—

his left wrist became light.

[Chik!]

Baby Madam—

was gone.

Fast.

Animals—

Rats leaving a sinking ship.

Fish fleeing strange water before an earthquake.

Baby Madam.

A pet abandoning its owner—

An ugly omen.

only once.

“…Right.”

food.

*Live.*
