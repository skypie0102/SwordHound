[Impossible…]

Amdusias stared upward.

In a mental world—

size was thought.

Weight.

Burden.

What one carried inside.

And Vikir’s ego was enormous.

So enormous that ordinary life could not explain it.

Not unless he had accumulated endless karma.

Endless destruction.

The extinction of a world.

[What are you?]

Amdusias’s voice trembled.

[Where were you born?]

[Where did you grow up?]

[How can your mind contain this?]

Vikir gave no explanation.

Only the same answer.

“Demons must die.”

A hound from the Age of Destruction.

A world where everything had burned—

rotted—

died.

The resentment of that world.

Its rage.

Its dead.

Its unfinished mission.

All of it lived inside Vikir.

RUMBLE—

The massive figure raised one fist.

Each finger—

a mountain.

Then brought it down.

BOOM!

The entire mental world shook.

Amdusias vanished beneath the impact.

[KGH!?]

Cracks spread across the mental space maintained by the Demon King.

Amdusias’s field destabilized.

[No!]

[The Hell Tree—]

He could not finish.

This was Vikir’s mind.

Amdusias had entered it willingly.

Now—

he could not leave without the owner’s permission.

BOOM!

Another strike.

BOOM!

Another.

The Age of Destruction itself seemed to collapse under every impact.

Then—

CRACK!

Amdusias’s mental field shattered.

And outside—

the Hell Tree began to shake.

◆◆◆

“…Hm?”

At first—

the movement was tiny.

A father camping in front of the Hell Tree looked up.

He had been waiting for the children swallowed inside.

The massive black tree—

which even gathered heroes had been unable to move—

had tilted slightly.

*Did I imagine that?*

No.

Rumble—

The Hell Tree leaned.

Slowly.

Almost imperceptibly.

Most people did not notice.

Then—

BOOM!

The tree twisted.

Exploded.

The tooth-like roots withered.

Taproot structures snapped like bones.

Concentrated sap sprayed out—

boiling away the instant it touched air.

Fortunately—

most of the blast went upward.

Parents and professors escaped serious harm.

Then someone screamed.

“Monsters!”

“They’re coming out!”

“A dungeon break?!”

“Is it a Gate?!”

“Prepare for battle!”

Creatures poured from the collapsing tree.

Orcs.

Hellhounds.

Ogres.

Goblins.

Harpies.

The waiting adults gathered into combat formation.

Spear King Cervantes.

Roderick Usher.

Other parents.

Professors.

Veterans.

Then—

“What?”

“They’re running away.”

“They don’t want to fight.”

The monsters did not attack.

They fled.

Some cried.

Some dropped to the ground—

kissing the earth.

They looked less like invaders—

and more like prisoners who had suddenly found an unlocked door.

Then came others.

Humanoid races.

Insect people.

Elves.

Dwarves.

Beastfolk.

And among them—

western jungle warriors.

**Ballak.**

Most looked like children.

Young boys.

Young girls.

Yet the pressure around them belonged to seasoned warriors.

They crossed the confused crowd—

vaulted the barriers—

and disappeared like wind.

Only then did the people outside understand.

“These are the things that were trapped inside!”

“My daughter!”

“Where is my daughter?!”

“My son!”

“Mom’s here!”

Students began emerging.

Alive.

Dead inside the tower.

Abandoned missions.

Completed missions.

People who had given up.

People who had continued.

All returned.

Everyone.

“M-mom?!”

“Father?!”

“Where am I?”

“The Academy?”

“I thought a Daylily ate me!”

“I was sleeping in Lost Paradise…”

Parents found children.

Teachers found students.

Tears everywhere.

One student screamed:

“Mom!”

“I missed you for ten years!”

His father stared.

“Ten years?”

“You’ve only been gone for a few months!”

Another student protested.

“What?”

“I was trapped for three days!”

Someone else asked:

“Father, did my letter arrive?”

“The one I sent from Lost Paradise?”

“Yes.”

“It did.”

“The letter let us know you were alive.”

Different internal floors.

Different subjective times.

Different experiences.

All collapsing back into one outside world.

Sssssss—

The bracelets on the students’ wrists turned black.

Then disappeared.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

They emerged too.

“We’re out!”

“We actually got out!”

“He did it!”

“He cleared the tower!”

Tudor and Bianca threw their arms around each other.

“I thought we’d be trapped forever!”

“Stop crying!”

“You’re making me cry too!”

Then both noticed—

everyone watching.

“…Ah.”

They separated instantly.

Awkward.

“Son!”

“Daughter!”

Cervantes and Roderick arrived—

pulling them farther apart.

Sancho and Pigi found their own families.

Both cried.

Dolores emerged too.

She wiped sweat from her face.

In the distance—

Mozgus was running toward her with tears streaming down his face.

Pope Nabokov I was being carried behind him.

Dolores saw them.

But her eyes went somewhere else first.

Toward the abyss beneath the collapsing Hell Tree.

“…Did you succeed?”

Then—

three familiar figures dropped nearby.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

“What happened?”

“We’re outside?”

“We’re really outside?”

The triplets quickly understood.

Someone had cleared the tower.

And they had a good idea who.

A cold-looking young man approached them.

**Osiris Le Baskerville.**

He had remained outside for a long time.

Watching.

Waiting.

“I’m glad you’re safe.”

Highbro bowed for all three.

“Thank you.”

Osiris nodded.

Then scanned the crowd.

“…Where is he?”

Vikir.

Osiris was not the only one looking.

Dolores.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

And countless students who had survived because of him.

“Where is Vikir?”

“He saved us!”

“He was the real hero.”

“I promised I’d thank him if I got out…”

“Wait.”

“Everyone is here except him.”

“He didn’t come out?”

The crowd gradually quieted.

Professor Banshee Morgue—

who had arrived after hearing all students were returning safely—

looked toward the abyss.

Her expression darkened.

Then—

BOOM!

A pillar erupted from the center of the collapsing Hell Tree.

Aura.

Dense.

Material.

A pillar rising like a dragon into the sky.

Power that only the highest martial realm could produce.

“What is that?!”

Cervantes’s eyes widened.

Roderick stared.

“A Sword Master’s aura…”

Every gaze turned toward the light.

Then—

something tore through the curtain of the abyss.

A figure shot upward.

Sword aura around him—

dark and brilliant—

like a black sun.

Vikir.
