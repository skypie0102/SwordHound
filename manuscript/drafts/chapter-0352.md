[Impossible…]

Amdusias stared upward.

In the mental world—

size reflected the magnitude of thought.

The weight carried by the self.

And the Vikir before him—

was colossal.

There was no ordinary human explanation for it.

Not without a lifetime of karma.

Not without endless destruction.

Not without seeing an entire world die.

[What are you?]

[Who are you?]

[What happened to you—

for your mind to become something like this?]

Vikir did not answer.

He repeated only one sentence.

“Demons must die.”

A hound from the Age of Destruction.

A survivor from a world where everything burned and rotted.

He carried the anger of countless dead.

The grief.

The hatred.

The mission they had left behind.

His mind was deeper—

wider—

and darker—

than even Amdusias could measure.

RUMBLE.

Vikir raised one hand.

Every finger—

a mountain range.

Then he brought the fist down.

BOOM!

The entire mental world shook.

A blow large enough to feel like the end of a world.

[GAH!?]

Amdusias vanished beneath it.

At the same moment—

CRACK!

The mental domain sustained by Amdusias began breaking apart.

Fractures spread through the void.

His demonic field destabilized.

[N-no!]

[The Hell Tree—]

He could not finish.

This was Vikir’s mental world.

And no intruder could leave—

until its owner allowed it.

BOOM!

BOOM!

BOOM!

The fist fell again.

And again.

The recreated Age of Destruction collapsed around Amdusias.

Then—

outside the mind—

the Hell Tree began to move.

◆◆◆

“…Hm?”

At first—

the change was small.

A man camping beside the Hell Tree—

waiting anxiously for the children trapped inside—

looked up.

The massive structure had just tilted.

Barely.

*Did I imagine that?*

He rubbed his eyes.

He had not.

RUMBLE.

The Hell Tree was leaning.

Slowly.

So slightly that most of the surrounding parents and professors still failed to notice.

Then—

“W-what?”

The man’s sleepy eyes snapped open.

He shook his wife awake.

“Get up!”

“I think it just—”

BOOM!

CRACK!

The Hell Tree exploded.

Its roots—

spread like giant teeth—

began to dry out.

The main root snapped like bone.

Dense sap burst from within.

The instant it touched the air—

it boiled away into vapor.

Fortunately—

most of the force vented upward.

The waiting families and professors were not seriously injured.

But then—

“Monsters!”

“Monsters are coming out!”

“A dungeon!?”

“A Gate!?”

“Prepare for battle!”

Creatures poured from the ruptured Hell Tree.

Orcs.

Hellhounds.

Ogres.

Goblins.

Harpies.

More and more.

Parents and faculty braced themselves.

Cervantes the Spear King.

Roderick the Bow Demon.

Other powerful veterans.

Then everyone noticed something strange.

“…Why are they running away?”

“They aren’t attacking.”

“They look terrified.”

The monsters fled.

Some hit the ground and cried.

Some kissed the earth.

They did not behave like invaders.

They behaved like prisoners—

released after years of confinement.

And monsters were not the only ones.

Humans.

Elves.

Dwarves.

Beastfolk.

Other races.

They escaped in every direction.

Among them—

warriors from the western jungle.

Ballak.

Most looked young.

Children—

or adolescents.

Yet the air around them belonged to seasoned veterans.

They cut through the confused crowd—

crossed the barriers—

and disappeared toward the distance.

The people waiting outside finally understood.

“Everything trapped inside the Hell Tree is coming out!”

“My daughter!”

“Where is my daughter!?”

“My son!”

“Mom is here!”

Faces appeared.

Lost people returned.

Students from Colosseo.

Those who had died inside.

Those who survived.

Those who gave up.

Those who kept climbing.

All of them—

returned alive.

“Oh, my son!”

“My daughter!”

“My graduate student!”

Families broke down crying.

Teachers found students.

Students found teachers.

“Mom!”

“I missed you for ten years!”

“Ten years?”

“You were gone for a few months!”

“What are you talking about?”

“I was trapped for three days!”

“Did you receive my letter?”

“The one from Lost Paradise?”

“I did.”

“It was the only reason I slept at all.”

Time inside the tower had passed differently for different people.

Experiences that lasted days for some—

had lasted years for others.

One by one—

the Hell Tree bracelets on their wrists blackened.

Then disappeared.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

They emerged together.

“We really made it out!”

“He cleared it!”

“He cleared the tower!”

Tudor and Bianca embraced immediately.

Then realized—

a large crowd was watching.

“…Ah.”

They separated.

Awkwardly.

“Son!”

“Daughter!”

Cervantes and Roderick arrived.

Sancho and Pigi found their families too.

Dolores emerged—

sweat on her face.

She saw Mozgus running toward her in tears.

Pope Nabokov I was being carried behind him.

But Dolores looked somewhere else first.

Down.

Toward the abyss beneath the collapsing Hell Tree.

“…Did he succeed?”

Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro landed nearby.

“What happened?”

“We’re outside?”

“Really outside?”

They looked around.

Then understood.

Someone had cleared the tower.

And they had a strong suspicion who.

A stern young man approached them.

Osiris Le Baskerville.

He looked at his half-brothers.

“I’m glad you’re safe.”

Highbro bowed for the three.

“Thank you.”

Osiris nodded.

Then looked around.

“…Where is he?”

Vikir.

He was not the only one asking.

Dolores.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

Everyone Vikir had helped—

searched the crowd.

“Where’s Vikir?”

“He’s the reason we survived!”

“He was the real hero in there!”

“I promised I’d thank him if I escaped!”

“Why isn’t he here?”

Every student had returned.

Except Vikir.

Professor Banshee Morgue—

who had rushed over after hearing that the students were emerging alive—

went pale.

Then—

BOOM!

A pillar of aura erupted from the center of the abyss.

Huge.

Dense.

Like a dragon climbing into the sky.

Solid aura.

The manifestation of someone in the supreme realm.

“What is that!?”

“That aura—!”

Even Cervantes and Roderick stared.

Sword Master.

A power only the highest level of warrior could manifest so openly.

Everyone looked toward the abyss.

Something rose through the darkness.

A black sun of sword aura.

And beneath it—

Vikir emerged.
