◆◆◆

**Quilt Rune Indulgentia.**

Head of the Indulgentia family.

Devout follower of Rune.

Passionate preacher.

Honorary civic dignitary.

Benevolent director to children with nowhere else to go.

All of it—

a mask.

Behind the saintly performance—

a demon smiled.

Quilt looked down at Geronto's corpse.

“Heh heh heh.”

“You actually killed her.”

His gaze shifted toward Vikir's cloak.

Four black face coverings protruded from it.

Ephebo.

Hebe.

Pedo.

Geronto.

Quilt's smile twitched.

“All four ruined?”

“What a problem.”

“I borrowed those from *that woman*.”

“I was supposed to return them eventually.”

He pressed a hand to his head.

“She'll make such a scene when she finds out…”

Every gesture was theatrical.

Like an actor delivering a monologue to an invisible audience.

Dolores stepped forward.

Anger had burned away her hesitation.

“You wore the face of a saint while doing this?”

“You brought demons into an orphanage?”

“What are you planning!?”

Quilt laughed.

“Planning?”

“Saintess—haven't you already seen what lies beneath the orphanages, monasteries, and hospitals under Quovadis?”

Dolores froze.

Then understood.

*Bones.*

*Human skulls.*

Demons ate humans.

And among their preferred prey—

the youngest were prized most.

Quilt smacked his lips.

“Children really do taste best.”

“Delicious.”

Dolores recoiled.

“You…”

“Cannibal!”

“You really are a demon!”

“Calling me a demon just because I've eaten a few people seems excessive.”

Quilt tilted his head.

“I'm hardly the only one.”

“People in very high places eat them too.”

“What!?”

“People who have sampled every luxury eventually become curious about ingredients they've never tried.”

“I introduced the taste to a few.”

“Then the fashion spread naturally.”

“Liar!”

“No one would do that!”

“Why not?”

Quilt's smile widened.

“There are plenty inside Quovadis alone.”

Dolores staggered.

Quilt watched carefully.

Then pressed harder.

“Humans have many faces.”

“A pious priest in public may be something very different in private.”

“Nonsense!”

“Is it?”

Quilt's thirty-six-faced nature had not yet emerged—

but the argument already carried its theme.

“Can *you* say you've always lived with only one face?”

“Never worn a shameful expression?”

“Never hidden something ugly beneath a virtuous mask?”

Dolores's eyes trembled.

She had.

Recently—

painfully—

she had.

Vikir.

A boy from the same Academy.

The same newspaper club.

Older than her in age—

two years below her in grade.

During the drinking game—

Dolores had lost control and soaked Vikir's clothes.

Then—

while overwhelmed—

she had allowed the blame to fall on him.

Not intentionally.

But it had happened.

And that was only the smallest example.

Saintess—

expected to love everyone.

Potential future head of Quovadis—

expected to satisfy the family.

New Testament figure—

expected to oppose the Old Testament faction.

Model student.

Student council president.

Newspaper-club leader.

Daughter.

Every position demanded a different face.

Dignity.

Justice.

Compassion.

Strength.

Beneath them—

fatigue.

Pressure.

Inferiority.

Resentment.

Laziness.

Ordinary desires.

Dolores could not answer.

Then—

a hand settled on her shoulder.

Rough.

Warm.

The Night Hound stood beside her.

“People make mistakes.”

His altered voice was quiet.

“That's part of being human.”

For a moment—

the pressure inside Dolores released.

Not only the pressure of facing a demon.

Years of pressure.

Saintess.

President.

Leader.

Daughter.

A small part of the weight fell away.

“…Is that really true?”

Vikir nodded.

His intended meaning was much simpler.

*People can lose control when they're desperate.*

He was thinking about the embarrassing accident.

Dolores received something far broader.

Then she caught herself.

*No.*

*Don't relax.*

*I'm the Saintess.*

*I'm a candidate to lead Quovadis.*

*I'm supposed to support others—not depend on someone else.*

Even after resolving that—

her eyes kept drifting toward the Night Hound's broad back.

Quilt noticed.

His smile remained.

His eyes did not.

Dolores possessed extraordinary talent.

But she was young.

Inexperienced.

A few carefully chosen words had nearly destabilized her.

The unexpected Night Hound had ruined that plan.

Quilt turned toward him.

“So.”

“Who exactly are you?”

“You're the one who's been raiding my affiliated branches lately, aren't you?”

“The one Ephebo mentioned before?”

“Why ask when you already know?”

Vikir's answer came low.

Quilt snorted.

“Fine.”

“I'm tired of this middle-aged actor's face anyway.”

“I want something younger.”

“Prettier.”

His gaze moved across the plague-doctor mask.

“I have a habit of collecting beautiful faces.”

“Like yours.”

His authority over faces and masks apparently let him perceive what lay beneath Vikir's disguise.

Dolores blinked.

*The Night Hound is handsome?*

Unexpected.

*Beautiful enough to attract even a demon?*

She knew she should not be thinking about that now—

yet her heart fluttered despite herself.

Quilt kept talking.

“I've wanted Saintess Dolores's face for some time.”

“And now I've found an even prettier one.”

“Two new faces for my collection tonight—”

“Opening.”

Vikir vanished.

Slash.

Quilt's skull split down the middle.

Black blood sprayed.

Vikir landed behind him.

A single perfected draw.

Unsheathe.

Cut.

Return.

Quilt's body toppled from the railing.

Crash!

Dolores stared.

“…?”

That was it?

For the supposed final enemy—

the end looked absurdly easy.

What Dolores did not know—

was how much of Vikir's strength she had never seen before.

Vikir did not relax.

“Of course it's not over.”

He looked down.

“Prepare yourself.”

“The real fight starts now.”

Below them—

Quilt rose.

His split mouth opened.

[What…?]

His eyes filled with blood.

Then—

for the first time—

he smelled Vikir properly.

The scent only demons could recognize.

A hunter of demons.

Until now—

the mask and black cloak had concealed it.

Vikir stopped suppressing himself.

Sssssssss—

black and crimson aura erupted around him.

The soul of a hound that had crossed countless battlefields bared its teeth.

And on those teeth—

demon blood.

The freshest scent—

**Andromalius.**

The Tenth Corpse.

Its dying hatred had marked Vikir's soul and body.

Quilt's smile vanished.

[Andromalius…]

[He has been unreachable lately…]

Vikir said nothing.

He simply let more killing intent escape.

The smell of Andromalius's blood became unmistakable.

Quilt understood.

[No…]

[To open the gate to the Demon Realm, the power of all ten Demon Kings is required!]

[With one gone—the other nine must carry more of the burden!]

[That idiot Andromalius!]

His voice warped.

So did his body.

[Unforgivable!]

[The great work has been delayed by decades!]

A grieving howl—

so distorted it made the stomach turn.

Crack.

Crack-crack-crack.

The human shell broke.

The thing inside Quilt—

the thing that had once walked the Demon Realm—

began to emerge.

Dolores stepped back.

Vikir did not.

*Finally.*

The enemy he had been waiting for.

**Dantalian.**

**The Ninth Corpse.**
