# Chapter 445: The Fall of Usher (5)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/445.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 443 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Existing project terminology controls Madeline, Bianca, Tudor, Vikir, Marquis de Sade, Orca, House Usher, House Donquixote, Red Death, Belial, Dantalian, and Nouvelle Vague. **Lakedog Lake** is retained from the aligned witness as a source-local geographic form pending direct Fandom access.

Madeline led the remaining Usher pursuers after Tudor and Bianca.

Across the swamp bridge.

Toward the mountain.

[Leave the horses.]

[Climb.]

[Tudor may die.]

[Bianca must be taken alive.]

Madeline no longer bothered hiding her true voice.

Rage had stripped away caution.

Her mouth tore wider.

Her eyes—

one damaged by Bianca’s arrow—

had begun turning vertical.

Reptilian.

Something beneath the human shell was showing through.

FLAP.

Her loose clothing moved.

The sound resembled feathers.

[Young flesh.]

[Fresh flesh.]

Madeline climbed faster than everyone behind her.

She followed Bianca’s blood.

Across rock.

Dead leaves.

Broken branches.

The trail became easier and easier.

Sweat.

Saliva.

Footprints.

Hair.

Blood.

The prey—

apparently—

had been too exhausted to conceal anything.

[Desperate.]

[Good.]

[Soon I can throw away this rotting woman.]

[First thing I’ll do in the new body is tear this old shell apart.]

Madeline crossed the ridge.

Then stopped.

A lake should have been there.

Instead—

a dry basin.

Rock.

Mud.

Cracked earth.

**Lakedog Lake.**

The largest body of water in the region besides the swamp around Masyaf.

Now—

almost empty.

[…The drought is this severe?]

Small pools remained.

Fish crowded them.

Madeline reached into one.

Pulled out a large catfish.

Bit through it.

Chewed.

Then frowned.

[Lakedog has never dried like this.]

[Not even during severe drought.]

Her gaze moved toward the red glow of distant wildfires.

Those fires—

were not ordinary.

The drought.

The wildfires.

Monster outbreaks.

All of them—

precursors.

Mana currents distorting the world—

building toward a massive dimensional opening.

[…The Gate of Destruction.]

[It’s opening soon.]

The remaining demons were becoming impatient.

Too many of their allies had vanished.

The gate that should have opened years later—

would be forced early.

The civil war had been accelerated for the same reason.

[Then I really do need a young body.]

Madeline’s breathing grew excited.

[So many plans.]

[Children.]

[Fresh blood.]

[Fresh flesh.]

She descended into the dry lakebed.

Moved toward the center.

Then—

a damp breeze touched her face.

[…?]

Madeline slowed.

The route behind her looked different.

Stone piles had shifted.

Or—

perhaps she had.

Her eyes narrowed.

[A formation?]

The rocks were not random.

They formed walls.

Paths.

A maze.

Walking through it—

naturally created a larger pattern.

Hallucination.

Disorientation.

Loss of direction.

Similar to Masyaf’s conditioning route—

but far more complex.

Madeline laughed.

[You challenge **me** with a formation?]

She had inherited House Usher’s knowledge.

And demon knowledge.

Belial.

Dantalian.

Battle formations used during the Age of Destruction.

She recognized the structure.

[Interesting.]

[Belial and Dantalian used something like this.]

[Did they betray us?]

Impossible.

Both were gone.

Then—

who had recreated their work?

[Bianca?]

[No.]

[A human could not reproduce this so precisely.]

Madeline dismissed the concern.

She knew the standard eight gates.

**Rest.**

**Life.**

**Injury.**

**Obstruction.**

**Scenery.**

**Death.**

**Shock.**

**Open.**

Life.

Scenery.

Open—

safe.

Injury.

Shock.

Rest—

harmful.

Obstruction.

Death—

fatal.

[Simple.]

[Powerful against ignorance.]

[Useless against knowledge.]

She entered a safe route.

Walked.

Then—

stopped.

The half-eaten fish head lay on the ground.

The same fish.

The same place.

She had returned to the beginning.

[…What?]

Again.

Life Gate.

Scenery Gate.

Open Gate.

Same place.

Then—

risking injury—

the other nonfatal routes.

Still—

same place.

Worse—

her sense of direction began collapsing.

Only two routes remained.

Obstruction.

Death.

The two she refused to enter.

[…This isn’t the standard formation.]

Black blood marked the ground.

Walls grew tighter.

No exit appeared.

Madeline’s confidence finally cracked.

[Then the center.]

[Someone wants me at the center.]

She moved.

Half willingly.

Half because there was nowhere else.

At the deepest point—

a dead tree.

A small torch hung from it.

Black blood formed words below.

**A demon will die beneath this tree.**

Madeline’s teeth ground.

[Cheap provocation.]

[You dare—]

Then—

BOOM.

Something heavy struck stone.

The dry basin shook.

Dust rose.

Madeline’s face changed.

Now—

she finally understood why Lakedog had been drained.

A roar came from beyond the maze.

Water.

An enormous wall of it.

Rushing straight toward her.
