# Chapter 351: Outside the Tower (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from the **target-351 portion** of shared raw `source/chinese/chapters/351.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 349 is the verified shifted witness. The Chinese combined raw has no internal Chapter-352 marker and omits a short seam bridge; aligned E349 restores only the missing realization that the five mountain peaks are Vikir's fingers/body and that his enormous ego has risen. Existing project terminology controls Vikir, Pigi, Amdusias, Hugo Le Baskerville, Osiris Le Baskerville, Hell Tree, Eighth Fang/Form, and Age of Destruction. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

[…How can a human be here!?]

Amdusias stared at Vikir in horror.

Vikir answered with his sword.

Slash.

Fragments of crimson aura scattered.

Amdusias’s expression changed.

[That blood…?]

[Why is it here?]

Something in the blood coating Vikir’s blade—

something that twisted the Hell Tree’s causality—

made even the Demon King recoil.

Vikir did not explain.

“Demons must die.”

Across one life.

Two.

And now—

after the impossible years spent inside the River of Flow—

what had once been a purpose had hardened into something closer to law.

Clang!

Amdusias’s horn collided with Beelzebub.

The air cracked like glass.

Black fissures split through space.

Sparks sprayed from every fracture.

Amdusias could feel it.

Vikir was faster.

Stronger.

More precise.

That much could be explained by the tower’s stats.

But the blood—

the cold wrongness carried through every wound—

was something else.

[What did you put on that blade?!]

[Where did you get it?!]

“Die.”

Eight crimson slashes crossed the air.

Amdusias swept them aside with his great unicorn horn.

Not all of them.

Fragments of broken aura cut into his body.

Tiny wounds.

Almost nothing.

Then Pigi’s blood entered them.

[…?]

Amdusias’s vision blurred.

Images flashed.

A swamp of flesh and blood.

Mountains of bone.

Air empty of mana.

A mushroom cloud rising over a dead horizon.

Then—

an endless wasteland.

A desert.

A colossal tower standing alone at its center.

And an old man in black—

walking toward it by himself.

The fragments came without sequence.

Without context.

Amdusias staggered.

[What is this?]

The unknown substance had entered his body—

and now something was bending the Demon King’s perception and causality around it.

Vikir watched.

*There really is something in Pigi’s blood.*

He did not know what Amdusias was seeing.

He did not know why it happened.

But one thing was now undeniable.

Pigi’s blood was poisonous to demons.

Poisonous enough to disrupt even a Demon King.

That was enough.

Vikir raised his sword again.

Amdusias snarled.

[How much longer do you think you can endure?]

Black energy began rising from the abyss.

Nightmares.

Fear.

Hatred.

Grief.

The emotional waste of every challenger trapped inside the Hell Tree.

Amdusias inhaled it.

[You know what this tower is.]

[Every negative emotion produced inside it becomes nourishment for me.]

Sadness.

Pain.

Rage.

Screams.

Betrayal.

Hatred.

Inferiority.

All of it—

food.

[And now I’ll eat yours.]

Vikir tilted his head.

“Mine?”

“Do I still have any?”

[You entered my domain.]

[I can see even what lies beneath your conscious mind.]

[Bluffing will not save you.]

Amdusias’s eyes glowed.

His horn pointed toward Vikir’s chest.

[Your fear.]

[Your anger.]

[Your grief.]

[Every negative emotion inside you will become mine.]

Then the unicorn charged.

A straight black road appeared under its hooves.

Not across the physical floor.

Through Vikir.

Into his inner world.

◆◆◆

BOOM.

Amdusias landed.

Black earth.

Dead trees.

Burning ground.

Smoke.

Ash.

A landscape stripped of life.

[For a human mind—]

[this is unusually barren.]

Amdusias pushed through the smoke.

Eventually—

a shape appeared.

A giant stone statue.

Weathered.

Broken.

But still recognizable.

Hugo.

Osiris.

Amdusias studied it.

[So the heads of Baskerville occupy this much space in your mind.]

[You do have some attachment to family after all.]

In a mental world—

size mattered.

Wounds.

Attachments.

Responsibilities.

Burden.

Strong emotions became large things.

Amdusias walked deeper.

He crushed dead trees.

Broken rock.

The world was large.

Larger than it should have been.

Still—

this was supposedly the mind of a boy not yet twenty.

How much could it possibly contain?

Then Amdusias encountered a mountain.

Steep.

Enormous.

Its summit hidden behind smoke.

[Interesting.]

[At least your burden is impressive for a human.]

He began climbing.

Step.

Step.

Step.

The mountain seemed endless.

Even Amdusias—

a Demon King—

felt fatigue.

The trees were burned.

Soil dead.

Insects gone.

Animal bones scattered everywhere.

A mountain of accumulated obligation.

Responsibility.

Debt.

Mission.

[How much can one human possibly carry?]

Amdusias kept climbing.

Eventually—

he reached the summit.

Bare.

Empty.

Sharp as a spear point.

He looked down.

Smoke hid the ground.

Still—

the sheer height forced a reluctant admission.

[I don’t understand how a human accumulated this much negative emotion.]

[Even a veteran who survived hundreds of battles would struggle to build something like this.]

Amdusias snorted.

[But you are still human.]

[You cannot oppose me here.]

Then—

rumble.

The mountain shook.

Wind tore through the smoke.

The fog broke.

And Amdusias finally saw the world beneath him.

An endless plain.

No visible edge.

And across it—

the Age of Destruction.

Seas of blood.

Swamps of flesh.

Mountains of corpses.

Rivers of red.

Human bodies.

Demon bodies.

Collapsed together into hills—

valleys—

canyons.

Lava erupted.

Sulfur burned.

Monsters tore into the dead.

Screams never ended.

Amdusias stopped breathing.

[How can this exist inside a human mind?]

Even Hell had not shown him something like this.

Not with this scale.

Not with this clarity.

[A human cannot witness such a place and live.]

[Even I have never seen a battlefield like this.]

[How can a child remember it so perfectly?]

Then—

RUMBLE.

The mountain shook again.

Amdusias looked up.

Four more mountains stood nearby.

Larger.

Taller.

The mountain he had struggled to climb—

was the smallest.

Five peaks.

Five impossible masses.

Amdusias froze.

Then the missing perspective finally resolved.

The ridged peaks.

The curved joints.

The scale.

They were not mountains.

They were fingers.

Five fingers—

attached to a hand so vast that the landscape itself seemed small beneath it.

And beyond that hand—

a body began to rise.

Vikir’s ego.

The true owner of this mental world.

A returner carrying the ruins of an entire destroyed world inside him.

Amdusias stared upward—

speechless.
