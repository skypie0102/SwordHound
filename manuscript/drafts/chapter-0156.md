> **CONTENTS**
>
> **For the safety of employees working at night, this orphanage provides the following rules of conduct.**
>
> **※ The contents below must never be disclosed outside the institution. This orphanage accepts no responsibility for accidents caused by violating these rules.**

The notice had definitely not been there during the day.

“…For the safety of night workers.”

Vikir muttered quietly.

Below the heading—

fourteen rules had been written.

> **1.** If, during a night patrol, you find a child alone in a corridor, ignore the child and return the way you came.
>
> If the child attempts to speak to you or hand you something, leave the area immediately at the highest speed possible.
>
> This orphanage strictly forbids children from using corridors at night and will never, under any circumstances, leave a child alone without a guardian.

> **2.** If a group of children comes to the guard room and reports a broken water pipe in Room 66 on the sixth floor of the main building, acknowledge the report and lock the guard-room door.
>
> Then hum your favorite song.
>
> **They** dislike singing.
>
> For reference, there are only sixty-five rooms on the sixth floor. Room 66 does not exist.

> **3.** All sexual activity is strictly prohibited within this orphanage, regardless of gender.
>
> At any time.
>
> In any place.
>
> By any method.

> **4.** If, while patrolling a corridor, you find that no matter how far you walk you never reach your destination—and the same scenery continues to repeat—immediately proceed to the emergency-exit stairs.
>
> Crouch in a corner with your forehead against the wall.
>
> Close your eyes.
>
> Cover your ears.
>
> The daytime guards inspect the emergency-exit stairs first when they begin work.
>
> Remain in that position until you hear a rooster crow in the morning.
>
> **※ If any other rule contradicts Rule 4, do not pay attention to that rule under any circumstances.**

> **5.** Suicides are occasionally discovered. This also occurs at other orphanages. Follow ordinary procedures.
>
> However, if you find the body of a child hanging in an extremely ordinary place, remain calm and check the floor beneath the body.
>
> If there is nothing the child could have stood on, ask yourself how the child climbed high enough to hang there.
>
> Then leave the area immediately at the highest speed possible.
>
> Enter the guard room.
>
> Turn off the lights.
>
> Lock the door.
>
> The order is important:
>
> **first turn off the lights, then lock the door.**
>
> Do not reverse these steps.
>
> In this situation, never sing.
>
> If possible, do not make even the sound of breathing.
>
> **They** have extremely sensitive hearing.

> **6.** If, while patrolling a corridor, there is nothing around you but you hear strange breathing directly beside your ear, immediately crouch and lie flat on the floor.
>
> Curl into the smallest shape possible.
>
> Do not make a sound.
>
> When the breathing stops, call another employee.
>
> Together, locate the stain on the ceiling and remove it.

> **7.** Night patrols always operate in pairs.
>
> There are no exceptions.
>
> **There are no exceptions.**

> **8.** If you see someone wandering in the center of the lake above the orphanage, do not approach.
>
> Lock every corridor door.
>
> Lock all ceiling windows and every attic window on the top floor.
>
> For reference, the lake is thirty-five meters deep.

> **9.** Never knock on the door of an empty room.
>
> Never pull the handle of a locked door more than three consecutive times.
>
> Under no circumstances should you place your ear against a door and listen.

> **10.** If an unfamiliar child asks a night patrol worker for a name, home address, or similar personal information, never answer.
>
> If you are wearing your required name badge, there is no reason for a child to ask your name.
>
> An address is private information and should not be requested under orphanage rules.
>
> As stated in Rule 1, this orphanage does not permit children to move around alone without guardians.

> **11.** If, in the middle of the sixth-floor stairwell of Main Building No. 6, several children lean their upper bodies over a railing and ask for help, do not take a single step toward them.
>
> Do not look back.
>
> Leave the area as quickly as possible.
>
> While retreating, shout or sing as loudly as you can.
>
> Due to the building's structure, that location is actually an ordinary wall.
>
> The main building has no seventh floor.

> **12.** If you need to use the restroom during night work, always bring a companion.
>
> No matter how frequent or inconvenient the need may be, at least two people must enter the restroom together.
>
> The best option is to avoid using the restroom at night at all.

> **13.** If you hear moaning from inside a wall while walking down a corridor, laugh loudly at once.
>
> Continue laughing until the moaning becomes crying.
>
> When the crying stops, leave the area as quickly as possible.
>
> Enter the guard room and lock the door.
>
> During the next morning's roll call, do not check the children's attendance records from the previous three days.

> **14.** Rule 4 does not exist in this manual.
>
> Any orphanage would leave an unlucky number blank.
>
> If text appears under Rule 4, do the exact opposite of what it says.
>
> **Never obey Rule 4.**
>
> **※ If any other rule contradicts Rule 14, do not pay attention to that rule under any circumstances.**

A thoroughly unpleasant list.

Vikir studied it—

then gave a small nod.

*…Cheap tricks.*

Demons fed on human fear.

Rules like these were an obvious way to cultivate it.

If a night guard truly saw something unnatural—

the orphanage could turn the terror back on the victim.

*You broke the rules.*

*You followed the wrong instruction.*

*The accident was your fault.*

Vikir ignored the notice.

Then entered the dark corridor.

The guard room stood in the center lobby.

Lights off.

Door firmly shut.

Not a sound from inside.

*The night guards must have seen something.*

That made Vikir's job easier.

He passed the guard room quietly—

heading toward the route to Dormitory Three.

Then—

“…!”

Vikir stopped.

At the far end of the corridor—

someone stood motionless.

A girl.

White sleepwear.

Watching him.

**Peri.**

“…”

Her expression was strange.

She looked at Vikir for only a moment—

then disappeared around the far corner.

Vikir moved immediately.

But when he reached the turn—

Peri was gone.

Only a torn strip of curtain remained on the floor.

*What was that?*

*Did I see her correctly?*

Vikir frowned.

Bent down.

Picked up the cloth.

Words had been scratched into it.

**HELP ME**

His eyes narrowed.

Rule 1 returned to his mind.

*What an unpleasant place.*

Vikir immediately followed Peri's trail.

He knew the building structure by heart.

So he focused on the spaces where someone could disappear from sight in an instant.

Then—

something changed.

*…What?*

Every time he turned a corner—

the same view appeared.

The same wall.

Same ceiling.

Same floor.

Same windows.

Even the scenery outside—

identical.

Vikir frowned.

*An illusion?*

*A trap?*

The notice came back to him.

Rule 4.

Rule 14.

*If the corridor repeats, go to the emergency stairs.*

*Press your forehead to the wall.*

*Close your eyes.*

*Wait for dawn.*

But Rule 14—

insisted Rule 4 was false.

So which one was the trap?

Four?

Fourteen?

Green mana-light glowed ahead.

An emergency-exit staircase.

If the corridor itself had become a maze—

leaving through the emergency route seemed like the obvious first move.

Creak—

Vikir turned the rusty handle.

Opened the door.

Green light spilled across the stairs.

Long shadows stretched through the stairwell.

Then—

Vikir stopped.

Someone had arrived before him.

A woman stood with her forehead pressed against the wall.

Eyes closed.

Body trembling.

She was praying.

“Our Rune in heaven…”

“May Your name be honored.”

“May Your kingdom come.”

“May Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven…”

“Do not lead us into temptation…”

“But deliver us from evil…”

**Dolores L. Quovadis.**

Student council president.

Saintess.

Standing alone in the emergency stairwell—

obeying Rule 4.

Behind his plague-doctor mask—

Vikir let out a quiet sigh.

*…What is she doing here?*
