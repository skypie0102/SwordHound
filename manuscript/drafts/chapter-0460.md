# Chapter 460: Infiltration of the Water Source (7)

SLASH!

A giant Poison Human opened its mouth.

Vikir’s blade swept across it.

The jaw twisted backward—

then dropped to the ground.

THUD.

Vikir landed lightly.

Several more Poison Humans blocked the slope ahead.

[Grrrrrr—]

[Kiiiik—]

[Hisssss!]

These were not ordinary Poison Humans.

Larger.

Stronger.

More distorted.

Three or four heads—

barely unusual.

Long twisted bodies carried appendages that no longer resembled anything human.

**Mutants.**

Rare Poison Humans born after repeated mutation—

battle—

and survival.

Their bodies radiated enough venom and corpse-stench to blacken nearby grass.

Then—

one especially massive shape emerged.

[Gaaaak…]

[GAK-GAK-GAK…]

Vikir recognized it.

*The one I saw entering the recharge tent.*

The same survivor—

now vastly more powerful.

Why Leviathan had left a monster like this in the rear—

Vikir did not know.

The creature’s mouth twisted.

[Fa…ther…]

[Help…]

[me…]

The words were almost impossible to understand.

Vikir made a decision.

*If this thing reaches tomorrow’s siege—*

*it’ll be a serious problem.*

He prepared to move.

Then—

“Vikir!”

Tudor shouted behind him.

Vikir turned.

Tudor’s face was set.

“You go.”

“We’ll handle this.”

“….”

“Opening the water route matters more.”

“We can manage the mutants somehow.”

“You don’t need to—”

“Understood.”

Vikir was already gone.

Tudor stared after him.

Then laughed.

“Could’ve at least pretended to refuse.”

Sancho shifted his axe.

“That just means he trusts us now.”

“I know.”

Tudor tightened his grip on Gungnir.

“And honestly—

that feels pretty good.”

They let Vikir advance.

Then turned to face the enemy.

“Come on, trash!”

Camus raised both hands.

“Big sister will show you something hot!”

Eight magic circles formed at once.

Her specialty—

**octa-casting**.

Eight simultaneous elemental spells.

Fire.

Iron.

Both rained down.

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

Iron spikes erupted from the ground.

Pierced bodies.

Then melted from extreme heat—

burning the targets from inside.

Sinclaire winced.

“My gold is melting too.”

“…Whatever.”

She drove mana into the ground.

Then—

RUMBLE.

A gold vein deep beneath the earth tore upward.

A massive golden golem ripped through the surface.

It swung a fist like a siege hammer.

Molten gold sprayed through Camus’s fire field—

burning enemies wherever it landed.

Dolores expanded her sacred barrier.

“Everyone hold your breath as much as possible!”

“Stay close to me!”

White light drove back Red Death miasma.

Blood.

Pus.

Poisonous spray.

Anything that touched Dolores’s radiance—

evaporated into harmless steam.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

All four released aura—

holding the line.

“Vikir!”

Tudor shouted while driving Gungnir through a mutant’s palm.

“Can you see anything up there!?”

The voice echoed.

Across trees.

Cliffs.

Rock.

Vikir had just reached the summit.

He heard it.

Clearly.

But—

could not answer.

There was no water.

None.

He stared into the crater.

Dry earth.

Sand.

Hard rock.

Dead reeds rolling in the wind.

No lake.

No moisture.

Nothing.

MiniPin’s information—

had been wrong.

Vikir clenched his teeth.

This was knowledge absent from his previous life.

He had known there was uncertainty.

And now—

that uncertainty had become a critical mistake.

BOOM!

He struck the mountainside.

Rock exploded.

Still—

no water.

*Dangerous.*

They needed to abandon the plan.

Return to Tochka immediately.

But the retreat route was already blocked.

Vikir slid down the cliff.

Fast.

*Clear the mutants.*

*Use the chaos from the destroyed recharge facility.*

*Break through the thinner rear encirclement.*

*Then hope the defenders last long enough.*

Plan after plan formed.

Then—

all of them became irrelevant.

“…!”

A man stepped through the Poison Humans.

Sharp hooked nose.

Thin lips.

Deep-set eyes beneath black hair.

Black priestly robes.

A grim middle-aged face everyone recognized.

The genius said to understand every combination of poison and medicine in the world.

A man even Hugo Le Baskerville preferred not to face carelessly.

**Hopps de Leviathan.**

Head of House Leviathan.

A central figure in the Imperial civil war.

He had arrived earlier than Vikir expected.

Hopps looked across the Night Walkers.

Then smiled.

“Hmm.”

“I can guess why you came here.”

He raised a spear.

Its wooden shaft—

dry and twisted.

Two long snakes coiled around it.

“Go.”

“**Ouroboros.**”

The weapon moved like a living thing.

Each sweep stirred Red Death spirits into a crimson storm.

Huge mutants gathered behind him.

Vikir’s expression hardened.

*Dangerous.*

The **Second Corpse**.

A Demon King that had never lost in the future—

whether outnumbered—

dueling—

or commanding armies.

If Vikir was an experienced demon hunter—

this one was an experienced human hunter.

“Don’t fight him!”

“You can’t win against—”

BOOM!

A lightning-wrapped pillar of fire struck Hopps in the face.

He spat blood.

Flew backward.

Then—

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

More blazing iron pillars crashed into the crater where he landed.

Vikir actually paused.

Camus stood with both hands raised.

“Hehehe.”

“Until Vikir gets here—

I’ll hunt a demon myself.”

“And if I’m doing it—

might as well start with the king.”

For the first time in a long while—

Camus stopped holding back.

Sssssss—

Above the roaring flames—

a huge black dead tree grew.

The **Wraith Tree**.

Fully matured.

Heavy with fruit.
