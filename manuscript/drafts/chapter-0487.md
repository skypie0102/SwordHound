# Chapter 487: The Marquis of Discord (5)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/487.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 485 is the verified shifted secondary witness. This chapter establishes the post-execution state of Vikir’s original timeline and Andras’s place as rank 63 among seventy-two demons. Direct Fandom browsing remains robots-restricted.

The river of blood had finally dried.

The war between the human world and the demons—

after decades—

entered history as the **Age of Destruction**.

Human victory was carved into stone.

And on the gate of the Iron-Blooded Sword House—

a head had been displayed.

**Name: Vikir Van Baskerville**  
**Crime: Collusion with the Enemy**

Vikir.

The illegitimate son of Hugo Le Baskerville.

The shadow whose hands had been soaked in blood behind the family for years.

The hound of Baskerville.

◆◆◆

A severed head could not speak.

The world beneath it—

suffered after the war.

Half the Imperial Capital destroyed.

Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain ruined by fire and flood.

Colosseo reduced to rubble.

Dozens—

hundreds—

of abyssal trees climbing into the sky.

Leviathan.

Donquixote.

Usher.

Still immersed in Red Death.

Baskerville—

nothing but smoke.

Quovadis corrupted.

Bourgeois decayed.

Morgue fighting alone.

Tochka—

filled with corpses after its water dried.

Broken angel statues.

Collapsed clock towers.

Nouvelle Vague—

gone entirely.

Vikir knew these scenes.

How could he forget?

A wasteland dyed by the blood of countless dead comrades.

How could he—

“…!”

His body shook.

Yes.

Andras had not opened a second Gate of Destruction **here**.

He had done something functionally similar.

He had opened a road—

to a world where the Gate was already open.

The timeline Vikir had lived in.

And died in.

[Hehehe.]

[The world line where the Gate of Destruction already opened.]

[A door to there.]

This power did not come from Andras alone.

The Imperial bloodline mattered.

The founding Emperor—

Golding Gerald—

had supposedly possessed future sight.

The First Crown Prince inherited that prophetic blood.

Combined with Andras’s natural power to open gates—

the result was something far greater.

A limited ability—

but still the ability to interfere with parallel worlds and timelines.

That was why Andras had chosen this host.

Unlike other Corpses—

which preferred the strongest bodies they could obtain—

Andras had chosen the body that amplified his authority best.

And because of that—

Vikir had been able to regress.

Andras smiled.

[Interested in my deal now?]

[Tell me who my brother is.]

[Tell me where he is.]

[Then I will spare you the worst outcome.]

“….”

[Are you going to destroy this world too?]

[Do you bring disaster everywhere you go?]

[Shouldn’t you protect at least one?]

Vikir stared into the portals.

His original world.

A place where he himself had already died.

A place where Hell’s destruction had already descended.

The two timelines were now connected.

Indirectly—

the current world had become connected to Hell.

Fire rain.

Monster armies.

Countless human deaths.

It seemed inevitable.

But—

“…Humanity won there.”

Vikir clenched his jaw.

“The Age of Destruction ended.”

It had.

Ninety-nine percent of humanity had died.

Vikir himself had been executed by a final demonic scheme.

But humanity had survived.

The late flood eventually ended the Long Rain of Terror.

The Ten Corpses—

dead—

or spent.

The world-fire disappeared.

The remaining monsters were hunted down.

Eventually—

things returned toward their rightful course.

Humanity had been reduced from countless grains of sand—

to a handful.

But that handful had won.

“So opening a portal to that world won’t bring disaster here.”

“Everything that crossed the Gate there—

was destroyed.”

Andras laughed.

[Do you really believe that?]

Vikir frowned.

[…Do you truly believe destruction ended?]

[That humanity won?]

The demon’s laughter became louder.

Malice.

Confidence.

Then—

more portals opened.

Sssssssss—

Transparent barriers appeared within them.

The view expanded.

Farther.

Wider.

The world beyond—

after Vikir’s execution.

“…!?”

Vikir’s eyes widened.

The wasteland had become worse.

Far worse.

Human survivors clustered on highlands.

Enormous fires rose around them.

Monsters thought extinct—

roared again.

Spiders large enough to wrap mountains.

Cephalopods lifting island-like heads from the sea.

Giants ripping out of the earth.

Birds covering the sky.

Beasts crossing whole plains.

And powerful demonic sorcerers—

leading endless hordes.

The eastern deserts—

mud made from human blood.

Western jungles—

flattened by impossible winds.

Southern oceans—

rotting beneath lethal plague.

Northern snowfields—

consumed by waves of fire—

turned into molten earth.

More monsters.

More demons.

More cruelty—

than anything Vikir remembered from the Age of Destruction.

Only screaming crossed the portals.

Rage.

Grief.

Despair.

The remaining humans fought.

They could not last.

“No…”

Vikir’s voice failed.

“I watched the Human Alliance win.”

“This was after my death?”

Confusion—

real confusion—

was rare for Vikir.

After regression—

almost unheard of.

But the sight before him could shake even someone who had already survived the apocalypse.

“That is my world.”

His instincts knew.

And it was more terrible than the world he remembered.

Voices carried across.

*More demons!*

*We’re wiped out!*

*How can this happen!?*

*How much longer do we have to fight!?*

*Just kill me!*

*It was better when the Gate first opened!*

*All the heroes are dead!*

*It’s over.*

*This is really the end.*

People died.

Heroes who had survived the Age of Destruction—

fell like refuse.

And Vikir finally understood.

The demons that had poured from the Gate before—

the Ten Corpses leading them—

had not been the full invasion.

They had been the tutorial.

Vikir desperately questioned whether this was another mental attack.

Decarabia answered.

[…Human.]

[That is not an illusion.]

[I see it too.]

Reality.

The world after Vikir’s execution.

Andras clutched his torn abdomen—

laughing.

[What did I tell you earlier?]

[I said your world became peaceful after you died.]

[That was the lie.]

“Impossible.”

“I watched all ten of you lose!”

[Correct.]

Andras smiled.

[**We** lost.]

“…!”

[But as long as the Gate of Destruction remains—]

[demonkind can rebuild its forces.]

[Do you know why?]

Then—

Andras said something almost unbelievable.

[The Ten Corpses were among the weakest Demon Kings.]

A cliché.

An absurdly familiar one.

And yet—

clichés existed because reality repeated itself.

Huge shadows appeared beyond the portals.

Sssssssss—

So large—

they darkened both the world beyond—

and the palace here.

Andras spoke.

[I am Andras.]

[Marquis of Discord.]

[Among seventy-two ranked demons—]

[I am only sixty-third.]

His smile widened beyond anything human.

[Do you understand?]

[There are more than sixty demons beyond the Gate—]

[stronger than me.]
