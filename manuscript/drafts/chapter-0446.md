# Chapter 446: The Fall of Usher (6)

**A demon will die beneath this tree.**

Vikir stood on the far embankment.

Madeline’s furious voice carried from the center of Lakedog.

[Cheap provocation!]

[You insects—!]

Vikir gave the order.

“Open the floodgates.”

Night Walkers and Donquixote knights moved at once.

Spears cut ropes.

Struck into the earthen wall.

Tudor and Bianca added their mana.

BOOM!

The dam broke.

Water exploded through the breach.

The drought had lowered Lakedog severely—

but not emptied it.

The volume held behind the temporary dam—

was still enormous.

RUMBLE—

Currents twisted together.

Then surged down into the dry basin.

Madeline could be seen below—

trapped in the formation.

She had already tried the standard safe routes.

Life.

Scenery.

Open.

No exit.

Then Injury.

Shock.

Rest.

Still nothing.

But she had not entered Obstruction or Death.

*She knows the original formation.*

That was expected.

Before regression—

Belial and Dantalian had used this formation to inflict massive damage on the Human Alliance.

Now Vikir returned it to the demons.

Improved.

“Puhishishi.”

Sade grinned beside him.

“Told you my version was better.”

The original formation—

had flaws.

Marquis de Sade found them immediately.

Then added what he had used during the Forty-Seven People Riot.

Thunder.

Wind.

Fire.

Earth.

Marsh.

Sky.

Water.

Mountain.

Eight trigrams layered over eight gates.

A maze nastier than the demons’ own design.

Sade’s smile widened.

“No demon escapes.”

“Watching them crawl around like insects is delightful.”

Orca snorted.

“Your hobbies remain disgusting.”

They watched the dam collapse.

Vikir had experience with this kind of work.

Long ago—

in Ballak territory—

he had built large-scale water-control structures against the Red Death.

The released current hit the ridge.

Dry ground destabilized.

Earth collapsed.

A landslide began.

Then Lakedog’s flood rushed downhill—

toward the swamp supporting Masyaf.

Bianca’s face changed.

“The mansion!”

Water surrounded the estate.

Whirlpools formed around the island.

The ground beneath Masyaf began to erode.

“Father!”

Bianca lunged.

Tudor caught her.

“Bianca!”

“You saw what was inside!”

“…Dad…”

Her knees gave out.

Without Tudor—

she would have fallen into the torrent.

Dolores.

Sinclaire.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Now they all understood the red and black flares.

*If Roderick can escape—red.*

*If he is dead or unable to move—black.*

*Why the colors?*

*You’re better off not knowing.*

Vikir had been right.

A red flare—

might have produced another plan.

The black flare meant the choice was already made.

Destroy Masyaf.

Completely.

Wash away the Red Death.

Drown everything the demon had built there.

Bianca looked down.

“…Father.”

**The Fall of the House of Usher.**

Her lifelong home sank.

Stone.

History.

Family pride.

All disappearing into mud and water.

Then—

[AAAAAAARGH!]

Something screamed inside the torrent.

Shapes burst from the water.

Wings where arms should be.

Beak-like mouths.

Teeth.

Hooked talons.

Distorted chests.

Humanoid birds crawled onto land.

Bianca recognized the largest.

“Isolde!”

**Isolde Usher.**

The traitor who had turned on Roderick.

The man who had helped poison the swamp.

The escort Vikir had once met on the way to Nouvelle Vague.

Isolde raised blood-red eyes.

[You planned this well.]

[I almost drowned.]

“Die.”

Bianca fired.

BOOM!

Isolde knocked the arrow aside with one enormous wing.

[Still trying to fight a demon with human strength?]

His wound closed rapidly.

Then—

Vikir stepped forward.

“…It’s been a while.”

Isolde froze.

He remembered him.

The convoy.

The ship revolt.

The prisoner who should have disappeared into Nouvelle Vague.

Fear flashed.

Then confidence returned.

[I was alone then.]

[Not now.]

Behind him—

more demonized Usher retainers crawled from the flood.

Sade narrowed his eyes.

“Too many birds.”

“They don’t look impressive.”

“But something feels wrong.”

Orca warned him.

“Don’t get careless.”

“They explode when cornered.”

Camus clicked her tongue from the ruined dam structure.

“Drought ruined the full washout.”

“If Lakedog had been at normal level—

we could’ve erased them all.”

The demons had survived because the lake contained less water than planned.

Masyaf itself was only partly destroyed.

Isolde laughed.

[Madeline prepared properly.]

[Not like Passamonte wasting time with the Hell Tree.]

[She raised a real demon army.]

Usher knights who had chosen demon service—

not merely been brainwashed—

spread their wings.

[If we survived—

Madeline survived.]

[You’re finished.]

More came ashore.

Vikir looked at them.

Then spoke calmly.

“In numbers—

we still have the advantage.”

Isolde stared.

[…What nonsense?]

Only Night Walkers.

A few Donquixote knights.

No army.

[Bluffing won’t save you.]

[ATTACK!]

The demons took flight.

Bat wings.

Hawk wings.

Owl.

Falcon.

Then—

SHRRR—

something pulled.

Vines.

Long.

Tough.

Already wrapped around their ankles.

[…What?]

Isolde looked down.

The vines disappeared into the floodwater.

Then—

a massive round face surfaced.

No eyes.

No nose.

No ears.

Only—

a mouth.

Packed with teeth.

**Daylilies of the Blood Tree.**

Countless seeds—

now germinating beneath the water.
