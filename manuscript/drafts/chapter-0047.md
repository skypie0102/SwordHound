# Chapter 47: The Husband Hunt (2)

> Restarted Chinese-first reconstruction. Primary source: `source/chinese/chapters/047.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 47 is an aligned secondary witness only. Established terminology controls **Ballak Tribe**, **Camus Morgue**, **Low Sword Graduator**, **4th Fang**, **Quadra Casting**, **Hugo Le Baskerville**, and **Queen of Red and Black**. Repeated raw-layer “brother” references around Camus's recent loss are normalized to the accepted **Rosie Morgue** continuity. The unnamed Ballak woman remains unnamed until the source reveals her identity.

THOOM!

The sound was like a hole being punched through empty air.

An arrow screamed across the distance and buried itself in the earthen wall.

BOOM!

The hardened wall exploded, leaving a huge hole behind.

The soldier taking cover behind it was struck and killed on the spot.

“——!”

“——!”

“——!”

Raucous cries came from the edge of the dense jungle.

The Ballak Tribe.

Brown-skinned barbarian warriors armed with bows and blades were attacking Morgue's fortress.

THUNK!

Every arrow seemed to bring down a soldier.

The Ballak warriors rapidly eliminated the sentries and climbed the walls.

WHOOSH! CRASH!

They set fire to the cotton fields below the walls and carried away the tribal slaves held there.

Wolves snarled and barked everywhere.

The Ballak rode enormous wolves in groups of two or three, each warrior carrying a noose woven from thick rope.

Whenever one of those nooses flew, it came back with a captive—man or woman.

They moved like hunters.

The sudden raid plunged the entire fortress into chaos.

Fires rose everywhere. Soldiers fell one after another. Captives were dragged away alive.

Into that chaos stepped Vikir.

Several Ballak women spotted him and immediately brightened.

“——!”

“——!”

“——!”

They whirled their nooses overhead and threw them at Vikir's neck in unison.

A moment later—

SNAP!

Three thick rope loops closed around his throat.

The female warriors kicked their wolves into motion and tried to drag him away.

But—

CREAK!

The wolves stopped.

Vikir had not moved a single step.

Even with the ropes tightening around his neck, he remained planted firmly in place.

Then he grabbed the nooses and pulled.

THUD!

The wolves and the three Ballak women were yanked off balance and crashed to the ground.

The expressions of the surrounding men twisted.

They shouted and raised their bows at Vikir.

SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!

Arrows flew at terrifying speed.

Vikir's hand moved faster.

SHING!

He drew his longsword and fed aura into it.

A trace of dense liquid aura—the mark of a Sword Graduator—gathered along the blade.

TATATANG!

Vikir traced a figure eight with the sword tip and cut every incoming arrow apart.

The Ballak warriors recoiled at the sight of his aura.

They knew that kind of power belonged only to someone who had crossed a threshold ordinary warriors never reached.

Vikir narrowed his eyes and studied them.

Brown skin. Silver, gray, and black hair. Faces painted black. Thorned collars around their necks. Huge wolves beneath them and bows in their hands.

*Exactly as I remember them.*

Before his regression, Vikir had fought Ballak warriors countless times.

They were aggressive, and every warrior possessed formidable combat ability.

On an open plain protected by a fortress, fighting them was manageable.

Inside the dark jungle, it would become a grueling battle.

*Hugo warned me repeatedly before we left. Don't start a fight until we join the main force.*

The land technically belonged to Baskerville, but it had been leased to Morgue. Vikir had no reason to risk his life in a private conflict here.

“...”

“...”

He released just enough pressure to suppress the Ballak warriors.

Having seen his swordsmanship moments earlier, none of them rushed him carelessly.

Then—

BOOM!

A violent explosion sent scorching flame into the sky.

An earthen wall collapsed, and a girl stepped through the gap.

Camus Morgue.

The future Queen of Red and Black stared at the Ballak warriors with blood-red eyes.

“Die!”

Camus crossed her hands.

Quadra Casting.

Four powerful offensive spells manifested at once and shook the battlefield.

Fire and wind. Steel and stone.

A storm of flame and a rain of rock tore into the raiders.

The Ballak warriors urged their wolves backward while calling noisy signals to one another.

They were preparing to retreat.

As Camus pressed them, she glanced toward Vikir.

Her eyes fell on the drops of dense crimson aura clinging to the tip of his sword.

“You're a Sword Graduator? That's incredible.”

Her admiration was genuine.

A Sword Graduator was a realm most people could never reach in an entire lifetime.

Even members of Baskerville, praised throughout the Empire for their talent with the sword, generally did not reach that stage until around thirty.

“As expected. You're the only man I acknowledge.”

Camus smiled and moved beside him.

Apparently assuming that releasing Graduator aura had tired him, she took a protective position in front.

“Stay back. It's dangerous.”

She raised walls of steel and stone while hurling spikes of flame and ice across the battlefield.

Third-Circle magic, combined through Quadra Casting.

It was a display worthy of Morgue's fifteen-year-old prodigy and future clan-head candidate.

“I'll avenge Rosie!”

Camus drew up all the mana in her body and poured it toward the Ballak warriors.

But even the greatest genius could not afford carelessness on a battlefield.

PFFT!

Camus frowned at a sharp sting in her neck.

*An arrow?*

If it had been an arrow, she would have died before she had time to wonder.

She reached up and pulled out something tiny and thin, narrow enough to slip between the floating shields surrounding her.

A cactus thorn.

Her vision swayed.

*Paralytic poison.*

“——!”

One Ballak warrior pointed at her and shouted triumphantly.

He had fired the poisoned dart.

Then—

WHIRR!

He threw the noose in his hand toward Camus.

They clearly intended to capture her alive.

But—

SNAP!

The rope stopped halfway.

Vikir had caught it in one hand.

GRRRR—

A contest of strength began between Vikir and the Ballak warrior.

The warrior pulled with everything he had, but Vikir's body had already crossed into the superhuman realm.

There was no contest.

YANK!

Vikir pulled once and ripped the man from his wolf, sending him tumbling across the ground.

*At least half his body should be broken after that.*

Vikir shook his head at the Ballak warrior clutching his waist and rolling in pain.

Then he turned to Camus.

“We should retreat now.”

“What? What about the fields and the captives?”

“Until we join Baskerville's main force, avoiding a full engagement is the better choice.”

Camus hesitated.

Rationally, withdrawing here was clearly correct.

Emotionally, she had not yet recovered from Rosie's death.

The girl had been taken alive by a barbarian clan and must have endured terror until the end.

The thought tightened Camus's fists.

Then—

TWANG!

A razor-sharp sound cut through the battlefield.

Vikir instinctively threw his head backward.

An arrow grazed his neck and slammed into the wall.

BOOM!

The shot was powerful enough to tear away part of the fortification.

“...!”

Still leaning back, Vikir looked toward the shooter.

A lone female warrior sat atop a huge wolf.

Black hair streaked with silver. Sharp, triangular ears. A face covered in black ash.

As if she had expected Vikir to dodge the arrow, she immediately threw a noose after it.

The rope flew like a snake and curved toward his throat from an exquisite angle.

“...”

Vikir remained calm and raised his sword.

Crimson Baskerville aura traced four enormous Fangs through the air.

The noose was cut into pieces before it could touch him.

At that exact moment—

“Danger!”

A hand shoved Vikir in the back.

He turned.

Camus's stiffening body had pushed him aside.

Behind her, the Ballak warrior Vikir had thrown from his wolf was still conscious. Teeth clenched, he had managed to cast another noose.

Vikir had expected the man to be crippled.

He was tougher than expected.

The rope tightened around Camus's waist.

Still weakened by the paralytic, she was dragged away as the warrior fled.

*...So it still happened.*

Vikir let out a quiet sigh.

One moment of bad judgment could carry an enormous price.

Morgue's future heir would learn that lesson painfully.

*In the original history, she was abducted by a barbarian tribe once as well.*

Before his regression, the same thing had happened.

The headstrong Camus had been captured during her youth and used as a hostage in negotiations.

Of course, she had eventually slaughtered her captors and returned alive.

It was during that period that her name as the Queen of Red and Black truly spread.

*Either way, I still have a job to do.*

This was not strictly Baskerville's problem, but the two houses were strategic allies.

Vikir released his aura and moved after her.

CLANG!

A weapon suddenly appeared in front of him and stopped his sword.

*...A bow?*

Vikir narrowed his eyes.

The silver-haired Ballak woman had used the full body of her bow to block his blade.

Their eyes met.

A strange sense of familiarity struck him.

*I've seen her before.*

Before Vikir could search his memory, the woman spoke.

“...Didn't I say we'd meet again?”

Her Imperial was halting, but understandable.
