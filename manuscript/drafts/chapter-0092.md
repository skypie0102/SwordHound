# Chapter 92: The Ghosts of the Ancestors (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/092.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 91 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established terminology controls **Divine Archer Adonai**, **Akwilla**, **Aiyen**, **Vikir**, **Beelzebub**, **High Sword Graduator**, **Kilogram Hammer — Oxbear (A)**, **Madam Eight-Legs**, **River Styx**, and **Sixth Fang**.

A Ballak hero who had once stood against the Empire alone.

A mythical warrior whose name had frightened even the Empire's strongest masters and shaken the continent.

The greatest archer in recorded history.

A Bow Master.

That man was **Adonai**.

And now the strongest archer in all of Ballak's recorded history stood before his distant descendants.

His white hair had been cut short.

The flesh of his face was dry and shriveled.

His lips were gone.

Teeth and gums showed openly.

A black burial robe covered most of his body, but every time it whipped in the dark current surrounding him, the gigantic bow across his back became visible.

The weapon was pitch black.

Its ends were sharply pointed.

Horn-like protrusions rose along its middle.

Its shape was bizarre—

almost as if two enormous insect legs had been broken off and joined together.

“…”

Vikir stood still and watched Adonai.

It had been a long time since he had seen a true master-level presence this close.

The pressure coming from Adonai was rough.

Violent.

Savage in the same way Hugo Le Baskerville's presence could be.

If anything, undeath made that quality even more obvious.

*So this is Divine Archer Adonai.*

The terror of the Empire.

Hero of the jungle tribes.

Seeing a legendary figure with his own eyes was a strange experience.

Vikir did not get long to appreciate it.

Adonai raised the enormous black bow.

There was no bowstring.

No arrow.

Then his free hand grasped empty air.

Black aura gathered at both tips of the bow and drew itself into a thin line.

It looked almost like a spider spinning silk.

Sssssss—

The current connected one end of the bow to the other.

Adonai pulled back on that string of aura.

Another mass of black power formed against it.

An arrow.

Also made entirely from aura.

Then he released.

BOOM!

The projectile exploded forward.

“…!”

Vikir's eyes narrowed.

Fast.

Too fast.

Even as a High Sword Graduator, he could barely see it.

He survived by a mixture of instinct and luck.

Beelzebub extended as far as he could force it.

CLANG!

The blade caught the aura arrow at an angle and deflected it.

Barely.

The shot tore away from Vikir and punched straight through a massive log.

BOOM!

CRASH!

The log did not merely split.

The rocks on the cliff behind it shattered as well, collapsing as though countless blades had cut through them at once.

An Adonai arrow did catastrophic damage even after being deflected.

Taking one head-on was not an option.

“…Hng.”

Vikir frowned.

He had avoided the direct hit.

His arm still went numb.

Pain shot from his wrist toward his shoulder.

The muscles in his forearm had torn.

The bones of his wrist were probably cracked.

Even the liquid aura coating Beelzebub had been ripped apart.

The reason was obvious.

Adonai's aura arrow had density.

Hardness.

It had already entered the realm of a master—

solid aura.

A Graduator's liquid aura could not simply overpower it.

And that was not the only problem.

Sssssss…

The huge log pierced by Adonai's arrow began to darken.

Its bark withered.

The wood blackened.

A tree that might have lived for centuries was dying within moments.

Poison.

Powerful enough to kill a giant tree almost instantly.

Vikir's attention returned to Adonai's bow.

At a glance, the weapon looked huge and cumbersome.

In reality, it was both incredibly sturdy and almost weightless.

Sharp ends.

Horn-like spines.

Two insect-leg shapes joined into one weapon.

Vikir recognized it.

*Remains from Madam Eight-Legs.*

When Vikir had encountered Madam Eight-Legs himself, all eight of her current legs had been intact.

This bow, then, had probably been made from material shed from an earlier body.

Aiyen's words from long ago returned to him.

> *Lord Adonai was the only one who could fight Madam Eight-Legs head-on. You're part of Ballak now. Respect him—and be proud to be his descendant.*

Adonai had apparently fought Madam Eight-Legs in the past and won.

He had fashioned a bow from what remained after that battle.

Her terrible poison had seeped into the weapon itself.

Even after Adonai died and the bow was buried with him, the poison had endured for generations.

“That's troublesome.”

Vikir clicked his tongue.

A Sword Master would have been bad enough.

A Bow Master might be worse.

Death Knights had an obvious weakness.

They attacked relentlessly, fearlessly, often without self-preservation.

In close combat, that could create openings.

But—

BOOM!

BOOM-BOOM!

CRASH!

At range, reckless aggression became nearly pure offense.

Adonai fired again.

And again.

And again.

Black arrows made from solid aura came screaming through the jungle.

Every one carried poison.

Every one tore through Vikir's blood-red liquid aura.

Vikir poured his own aura into Beelzebub and swung.

**Baskerville Sixth Fang — Carnivorous.**

Six bloody traces crossed the air.

They met Adonai's arrow storm.

They were forced backward.

A High Sword Graduator was still a Graduator.

Before a true Master, the difference in realm was merciless.

Liquid could not defeat solid.

The only reason Vikir remained alive at all was everything beyond his basic aura level.

The blessing of the River Styx.

The abnormal strength and mana recovery of his body.

The monster abilities stored in Beelzebub.

“…! …! …!”

Vikir activated **Kilogram Hammer — Oxbear (A)**.

His body became drastically heavier.

The increased weight helped him resist the force behind Adonai's shots.

It kept him from simply being launched through the forest.

But it did not solve the fundamental problem.

Each of Adonai's arrows was harder and stronger than one of Vikir's Fangs.

And there were more arrows than Fangs.

Worse—

a bowstring and arrows made of aura did not wear out.

Adonai did not need a quiver.

He only needed to keep pouring out the dark energy overflowing from his Death Knight body.

CLANG!

CRACK!

THUD!

BOOM!

Vikir stopped trying to block head-on.

He angled his sword and redirected the arrows whenever he could.

Even so, each impact pushed him farther back.

His large Graduator-level mana reserves were already nearing exhaustion.

Whenever Beelzebub's liquid aura touched Adonai's solid aura, it burst apart like thick honey struck by a hammer.

His wrist trembled.

Every part of his body was beginning to send warning signals.

*At this rate, I'll become a target full of arrows.*

Vikir moved.

Adonai moved with him.

Closing the distance caused arrows to pour in even faster.

Opening the distance against a Bow Master was practically suicide.

A surprise attack might have created a chance.

In a direct confrontation—

there was no route to victory.

Not even a reliable route to escape.

*Then I need to kill Ah'Heman somehow.*

Cut the summoner.

End the necromancy.

But Adonai stood squarely between Vikir and Ah'Heman.

Every Ballak warrior nearby fired at once.

Their arrows never reached the shaman.

Adonai's robe, bow, and aura intercepted them all.

Vikir's thoughts raced.

Then—

THUMP!

A long leg kicked him across the face.

“Slave! Move!”

Aiyen shoved Vikir's head aside with her foot.

BOOM!

A black streak passed through the exact space his head had occupied.

A thin cut opened along Aiyen's calf.

Vikir wiped blood from his forehead where her kick had caught him.

“Thanks.”

Aiyen glared at him.

“Now isn't the time.”

“Better a concussion than an arrow through the skull.”

“I said stop joking. Another one's coming!”

She grabbed the back of Vikir's neck and yanked him away.

THUD!

A black aura arrow buried itself in the ground where Vikir's groin had been a heartbeat earlier.

The impact left a deep crater.

Aiyen narrowed her eyes.

“…He aims there too. I suppose it really is a man's vital point.”

Vikir gave a faint, crooked smile.

“‘He’ is the great Lord Adonai.”

“Right now he's just Ah'Heman's puppet.”

Aiyen was already nocking arrows.

“We can honor him after we put him back to rest.”

She drew.

Ten arrows launched almost simultaneously.

Their paths separated in midair.

Each came toward Adonai from a different direction and aimed at a different part of his body.

But—

THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD!

Adonai moved one hand.

He caught all ten arrows.

One hand.

Ten trajectories.

Then—

CRACK!

His fist closed.

Every shaft snapped.

Fragments rained to the ground.

Aiyen stared at them, jaw tight.

“…Even his hands are huge.”

Adonai raised the black bow.

This time there was no ambiguity in the target.

Vikir.

Aiyen.

Both directly ahead.

BOOM!

The bow roared like artillery.

A black vortex shot forward.

The aura spun like a nest of blades, tearing apart the surrounding air as it came.

Too fast to dodge.

Too destructive to block.

Vikir and Aiyen braced themselves.

Then—

BOOM!

Someone stepped between them.

A bare hand caught the black attack.

CRACKLE!

The rotating aura screamed against raw strength.

Then shattered.

Adonai's projectile burst apart into streams of black air.

“…!”

Vikir and Aiyen looked up.

The warrior standing before them did not retreat even though blood poured from the palm that had caught the attack.

The chieftain.

The Night Fox.

**Akwilla.**

Representative of the new age.

Standing face-to-face with the representative of the old.
