# Chapter 92: The Ghosts of the Ancestors (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/092.txt` — `a880ba08795a97b8343f5b49640e2a46b9b8a9ec`. Recovered English Chapter 91 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established terminology controls **Divine Archer Adonai**, **Akwilla**, **Aiyen**, **Vikir**, **Beelzebub**, **High Sword Graduator**, **Kilogram Hammer — Oxbear (A)**, **Madam Eight-Legs**, and **Sixth Fang**.

A hero who had challenged the Empire alone.

A name that frightened even master-class warriors.

The greatest archer in Ballak history.

**Adonai.**

He stood before them as a Death Knight.

Short white hair.

Skin drawn tight over bone.

No lips.

Teeth exposed.

A black burial robe streamed around him.

Across his back hung an enormous black bow.

Its shape was strange.

Two long curved pieces joined together.

Sharp ends.

Horn-like protrusions.

Almost like insect legs.

Vikir watched in silence.

It had been a long time since he had stood this close to a genuine master-level presence.

Adonai's aura felt rough and violent.

Comparable to Hugo's.

Death made it even more ominous.

Then Adonai raised the bow.

No string.

No arrow.

His empty hand pulled backward.

Black aura gathered between the bow's ends.

A line formed.

Like spider silk.

Then a second construct appeared.

An arrow.

Made entirely from aura.

Adonai released.

BOOM.

Vikir barely saw it.

Instinct and luck moved him.

Beelzebub extended.

CLANG.

The arrow deflected.

Only slightly.

It punched through a huge log and shattered rock behind it.

Vikir's arm went numb.

Muscle tore.

His wrist cracked.

The liquid aura around Beelzebub ripped apart.

*Solid aura.*

Master territory.

Graduator aura could not match its density.

Then the log began to blacken.

Rot spread across the wood.

Poison.

Vikir looked again at the bow.

He recognized the material.

“Madam Eight-Legs.”

The bow had been made from old remains or shed material from her body.

Aiyen had once told him the story.

Adonai was the only Ballak warrior said to have fought Madam Eight-Legs directly and prevailed.

The bow was the proof.

Its material still carried her poison even after centuries underground.

Vikir clicked his tongue.

“Troublesome.”

A Sword Master was dangerous enough.

A Bow Master at range was worse.

Death Knights normally attacked without fear or caution.

That created openings in close combat.

At distance—

Adonai had almost none.

Black aura arrows poured out.

Solid.

Poisonous.

Endless.

Vikir answered with Beelzebub.

Six Fangs cut red arcs.

They broke.

One after another.

A High Sword Graduator remained a Graduator.

Liquid aura could not overcome solid master-class aura.

Vikir survived because of everything else he had accumulated.

River Styx.

Beelzebub.

Monster abilities.

Experience.

He triggered **Kilogram Hammer — Oxbear (A)**.

His body became massively heavier.

The added weight helped prevent Adonai's arrows from throwing him away.

It did not stop them.

Each projectile was stronger than one of Vikir's Fangs.

And Adonai could create more.

Vikir's mana fell rapidly.

His wrist shook.

His muscles screamed.

*At this rate, I become a target.*

Closing the distance invited a wall of arrows.

Retreating from a master archer was worse.

A surprise attack might have worked.

A straight fight did not.

Vikir shifted his goal.

*Kill Ah'Heman.*

But Adonai stood between them.

Ballak warriors fired together.

Adonai blocked everything.

Then—

THUMP.

A foot hit Vikir in the face.

“Move, slave!”

Aiyen kicked his head aside.

A black arrow passed through the exact place his skull had been.

A cut opened on Aiyen's calf.

Vikir wiped blood from his forehead.

“Thank you.”

She glared.

“No jokes.”

Another arrow tore into the ground where Vikir had been standing.

Aiyen narrowed her eyes.

“He even aims for vital points.”

“That's Adonai.”

“Right now he's Ah'Heman's puppet.”

She nocked ten arrows.

All ten launched.

All changed direction.

Different trajectories.

Different targets.

Adonai caught them.

One hand.

Then crushed the shafts.

Aiyen stared.

“Big hands.”

Adonai raised the black bow again.

A black vortex formed.

The attack came like artillery.

Vikir and Aiyen braced.

Then someone stepped in front of them.

BOOM.

A bare hand caught the attack.

Aura screamed.

The black projectile shattered under raw force.

Blood dripped from the hand that had stopped it.

The warrior did not move.

**Akwilla.**

Night Fox.

Chieftain of Ballak.

The strongest archer of the new generation now stood directly before the greatest archer of the old.
