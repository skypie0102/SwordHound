# Chapter 369: The Servant (1)

Vikir looked up toward the cliff.

Black skin.

Golden eyes burning through the sulfur-lit darkness.

A giant of a man.

**D'Ordume D'Orcdile.**

Colonel.

One of Nouvelle Vague’s Five Wardens.

In practical terms—

only Warden Orca stood above him.

“A riot?”

D'Ordume’s voice sounded like iron slowly brought to a boil.

Below him—

Bastille answered quickly.

“Yes!”

“A Level Nine prisoner refused labor and injured multiple guards!”

“We were just beginning suppression!”

“…Hm.”

D'Ordume looked at Vikir.

Instantly—

Vikir felt as though countless axe blades hanging from chains had been aimed at every part of his body.

*Dangerous.*

The Reflex stat Vikir had pushed to absurd levels inside the Hell Tree—

made every hair on his body stand upright.

A warning from instinct.

It had been a long time since he felt one this strong.

He trusted it.

And moved.

BOOM!

The ground where Vikir had just stood—

exploded.

D'Ordume had dropped from the cliff.

Two hundred ninety-eight centimeters tall.

Two hundred twenty kilograms.

The impact caused a small earthquake.

He landed with one hand on the ground.

Then slowly stood.

Yellow eyes burned through the hot sulfur haze.

Metal rings and guards covered both forearms.

From them—

spiral axe blades protruded.

WHIRRRR.

Aura filled the blades.

They began spinning rapidly along their twisted grooves.

Like two vortexes of steel—

wrapped around D'Ordume’s arms.

*Exactly as the stories said.*

Vikir remembered the future.

Among the great heroes who later fought on the front—

two had become famous for strange attack methods.

One—

Mozgus Quovadis.

He infused aura through the pages of thick scripture—

split them apart—

and launched the pages like razor blades.

The other—

D'Ordume.

He loaded aura into the bizarre weapons around his forearms—

then turned his punches into storms of blades.

*Seeing it in person is different.*

His enormous body and aura were intimidating enough.

But D'Ordume carried something else.

The pressure of a man who had spent his life—

locking people away.

Torturing them.

Forcing them to labor.

Killing them.

A human livestock keeper.

A butcher of men.

The kind of presence that made ordinary people shrink instinctively.

Like a dog lowering its tail before the dogcatcher.

But Vikir—

was a demon hunter.

A man before whom demons themselves had learned fear.

And he had spent more years on battlefields—

than D'Ordume had been alive.

Their pressure collided.

Vikir’s—

did not lose.

“…!”

D'Ordume’s expression changed slightly.

“You really cannot use mana?”

“If this is all pure physical strength…”

“Impressive.”

Around them—

guards of officer rank lay groaning on the ground.

D'Ordume looked once.

Then said:

“We settle this here.”

Vikir wore BDISSEM cuffs and shackles.

No mana.

Under those conditions—

the advantage belonged overwhelmingly to D'Ordume.

SHING.

The spiral axe blades on his forearms shifted.

“One warning.”

“This may end in summary execution.”

“Nineteen years ago—

there was an unpleasant incident between guards and prisoners.”

“Since then—

insubordination has been controlled and punished severely.”

Vikir said nothing.

D'Ordume’s square jaw tightened.

“No objections?”

“Goodbye.”

Then—

he charged.

THUD!

THUD!

THUD!

Like a giant buffalo.

His forearms crossed.

Vikir raised his BDISSEM restraints.

BANG!

He caught the fists.

But the fists—

were not the real attack.

WHIRRRRRR.

The spiral blades behind them spun.

Compressed aura tore forward—

forming cutting currents.

Vikir’s nerves screamed.

*Stay here—

and I get ground into paste.*

He dropped.

Rolled.

Escaped the flow.

Then—

BOOOOOOM!

A cutting vortex more than ten meters across—

snaked through the worksite.

Rock vanished into it.

Stone became sand.

A human body caught in that—

would become blood in less than a second.

“D'Ordume is serious!”

“Run!”

“Colonel!”

“The guards are getting caught too!”

Everyone fled.

Prisoner.

Guard.

D'Ordume’s power made no distinction.

Vikir watched—

slightly impressed.

*So this is one of the Five Wardens.*

Before regression—

the Nouvelle Vague heroes had usually fought on brutal fronts Vikir himself had never visited.

He knew their reputation.

Not their real strength.

Now—

the stories made sense.

BOOM.

Stone mountains were carved away.

Rocks became powder.

The cutting wind mixed with sulfur gas—

creating fire.

Then explosions.

D'Ordume’s blade storm—

cut through even those explosions—

and came for Vikir.

*There really was a monster like this hidden in Nouvelle Vague.*

*But whether he’ll fight the demons this time—

that’s another question.*

In the future Vikir remembered—

D'Ordume had eventually joined humanity’s war.

But complicated circumstances had forced that outcome.

And even after joining—

his actions had cost many allies and innocent people their lives.

This timeline—

was already different.

*Maybe humanity would be better off without him.*

Far away—

guards and prisoners screamed as the vortex caught them.

Vikir shook his head.

D'Ordume saw the gesture.

A vein rose on his forehead.

“You still have time to look away?”

He inhaled deeply.

His body swelled.

The spiral blades accelerated.

WHIIIIIIIIIR.

Stone caught in the vortex no longer became sand.

It became dust.

*Harder to dodge now.*

Vikir ducked under another wave.

Every nerve in his body—

had been warning him continuously.

One delayed reaction—

and flesh would split.

Bone would vanish.

*No mana.*

*Then I control the body completely through Reflexes.*

*More importantly—

I return to fundamentals.*

Spatial awareness.

Exact body position.

Exact body dimensions.

The precise path through a storm of invisible edges.

Harder than crossing rainfall—

without letting a single drop touch skin.

One calculation wrong—

and the body disappeared piece by piece.

But if every calculation was right—

then there was no reason to be hit.

Vikir moved opposite the direction of every rising hair.

CRASH.

One step—

and the place behind him shredded apart.

A tilt of the head—

and only several threads on his prison shirt snapped.

Another step.

Another.

He penetrated deeper into D'Ordume’s range.

Then—

BANG.

A fist wrapped in BDISSEM chain—

broke through the rotating aura.

Buried itself in D'Ordume’s abdomen.

*Hit.*

Then—

[Human!]

[Danger!]

[Back away!]

Decarabia’s voice—

weak beneath the BDISSEM suppression—

rang from Vikir’s chest.

At the same moment—

Vikir felt it.

Wrong.

A human could train muscle to extraordinary levels.

But eventually—

human flesh was still flesh.

Bone.

Blood.

Skin.

There was a limit to the hardness.

D'Ordume—

did not feel human.

The fist had clearly struck his abdomen.

Yet the resistance was dense.

Heavy.

Almost elastic.

Not flesh.

Something else.

Like—

“…A dragonkin?”

Vikir frowned.

The sulfur smoke and scattered aura fragments cleared.

D'Ordume’s body—

emerged.

Then—

BANG!

Something enormous came down on Vikir.

And whatever it was—

was no longer shaped like a human.
