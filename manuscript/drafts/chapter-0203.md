The center of the Imperial Capital.

Waterways—

bridges—

and beyond them—

the high outer wall of Colosseo Academy.

Dawn made the normally crowded roads almost empty.

A few workers.

A few early risers.

And—

one exhausted black dog.

Vikir crossed the bridge.

Slowly.

Painfully.

His condition—

worse than expected.

Blood from his waist had finally clotted.

The bleeding—

stopped.

Strength—

did not return.

*Even Picaresque's dog body can only recover this much.*

*If I were still human—

I'd probably already be unconscious.*

He dragged himself toward the Academy wall.

Tongue out.

Black fur matted with dirt and blood.

A patrolling guard noticed him.

“Look.”

“What a cute little black dog.”

Another guard stopped him.

“Don't touch it.”

“Looks sick.”

“Could be carrying something.”

“It's filthy.”

“Get it out of here.”

Then—

thump.

A guard kicked Vikir.

The small dog flew sideways.

The guards laughed—

and kept walking.

Vikir landed.

Did not move for a moment.

*So this is street life as a dog.*

He had lived as a wanderer before.

Human homelessness—

was hard.

Being a stray animal—

was different.

People treated you as if violence needed no reason.

Vikir stayed close to the wall.

Avoided attention.

Avoided footsteps.

A drunk passerby waved a sausage.

“Hey.”

“Come here.”

He threw it.

Vikir moved farther away.

The drunk frowned.

“Unfriendly mutt.”

Then left.

Vikir continued.

His eyes—

watched the street.

His mind—

returned to the previous battle.

**Snake Morgue**.

According to the history Vikir remembered—

Snake should become the **Corpse King** much later.

Cindywendy's recent reports—

showed nothing unusual around him.

*So Snake is still human.*

Yet—

the **Corpse Queen** already existed.

A different contractor.

Geronto.

Ah'Heman's corpse.

The Queen's last words.

Too many questions.

And one conclusion—

uncomfortable.

Vikir had failed to kill her because—

he lacked enough sword power.

He remembered **Hugo Le Baskerville** against Andromalius.

Seven cuts—

one strike.

Upper and lower jaws—

closing like a monster's mouth.

The sky itself—

split by seven fangs.

Both called it—

Baskerville's Seventh Fang.

But Vikir's Seventh Fang—

and Hugo's—

were worlds apart.

That was the difference between—

**Peak Sword Graduator**—

and **Sword Master**.

Vikir had crossed that gap once.

Only once.

Against **Dantalian**.

With Dolores's Saintess blessing.

For a few moments—

his strength approached Hugo's level.

Enough—

to destroy a Demon King.

But future battles—

could not depend on Dolores being present.

*I need that power myself.*

*As soon as possible.*

His thoughts blurred.

Stopped.

Restarted.

*Too much blood.*

He needed—

safety.

Sleep.

Then planning.

*Dormitory.*

*Infirmary.*

*Anywhere.*

Vikir reached the hidden hole beneath the Academy wall.

Soft earth.

Leaves.

Easy to reopen.

He dug with his front paws.

Crawled through.

Then—

voices.

“I'm exhausted.”

“We drank too much.”

“Finish the cigarette.”

“It's almost morning.”

Three male students.

Three female students.

Second-years.

Alcohol.

Smoke.

Vikir saw their foreheads.

Cross-shaped scars.

He knew those scars.

Because—

he had carved them.

*The students who bullied Pigi.*

Early in the semester—

they had targeted a nervous first-year.

Vikir had followed them outside the Academy.

Masked.

Then—

beat all six.

And cut a cross into each forehead.

His warning—

had been simple.

*Cause trouble at school again—*

*and I kill you.*

*Your families too.*

The threat had worked.

At school—

they moved carefully.

Quietly.

Not because they changed.

Because—

they were afraid.

Before Vikir's intervention—

they humiliated and tormented juniors.

Peers.

Even seniors—

whenever the target looked weak.

Against strength—

they bowed.

Against authority—

they crawled.

After the masked beating—

they could not even identify who might have arranged it.

They had created too many victims.

Too many possible sources of revenge.

So every day—

they were forced to revisit their own past cruelty,

wondering when and from where retaliation might come.

Fear made them cautious.

Not decent.

Then—

one of them noticed the dog.

“What is that?”

Vikir turned away.

His dog body healed quickly—

but not quickly enough.

The six surrounded him.

“A stray inside the Academy?”

“Could spread disease.”

“Let's clean up the trash.”

“I need to blow off stress.”

“Tie it up.”

“Burn it alive.”

“Throw it into the sewer.”

One laughed.

“Remember that masked bastard?”

“Next time I see him—

I'll carve the same mark into his head.”

“Let's practice on this thing first.”

Cigarette butts.

Broken bottles.

A knife.

One tossed a burning cigarette.

Another—

a bottle.

Glass shattered.

Someone spat.

Someone kicked him.

Vikir thought—

quietly.

*Should I just kill them?*

Patience—

required energy too.

In human form—

one second.

Maybe half.

Six heads—

on the ground.

Even in dog form—

possible.

But then—

the Academy newspaper.

> **Shocking! Students Mauled to Death by Stray Dog!?**

And afterward—

other dogs blamed.

Other cats.

No convenient place to hide six bodies.

No energy—

to clean up.

Most importantly—

Vikir could barely keep his eyes open.

Then—

[Chik…]

A tiny sound above.

Baby Madam—

clinging to the wall.

Ready to leap.

Vikir shook his head.

Second-year Colosseo students—

were not harmless.

Six at once—

too much for a hatchling.

*Fine.*

*I'll change back.*

He was already considering—

the least troublesome way to dispose of the bodies.

Then—

a voice.

Dry.

Cold.

“Cold Department.”

“Second-year.”

“Class B.”

The six froze.

“Attendance number eight—

Pal Uspear.”

“Twenty-nine—

Betty Realbelt.”

“Fifty-eight—

Houser Yellop.”

“Sixty-three—

Seaweed Aimecom.”

“Sixty-six—

Bison Redmin.”

“Seventy-one—

Oiler Southmiddle.”

Every name.

Every number.

Then—

“…What are you doing?”

Someone stood behind them.

Food bowl—

in one hand.

Water bowl—

in the other.

**Dolores L. Quovadis.**
