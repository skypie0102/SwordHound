# Chapter 167: Sin and Punishment (6)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/167.txt` — `51868432649722b2ae41a39858fb4881c4d72b81`. Recovered English Chapter 166 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls Vikir, Dolores L. Quovadis, Dantalian, Peri, Humbert L. Quovadis, Hugo Le Baskerville, Camus Morgue, Ironblood Empress, Madam Eight-Legs, Beelzebub, Sword Master, Peak Sword Graduator, Saintess of Steel, and Night Hound.

The moment Dolores saw Dantalian wearing Peri's face—

something inside her snapped.

The thread of reason she always held so tightly—

broke.

Emotion surged through the opening.

Grief.

Anger.

Protectiveness.

Everything poured toward one person.

The Night Hound.

Vikir.

The blessing entered him—

and the world changed.

**Sword Master.**

Aura erupted from Beelzebub.

Gas—

to liquid.

Liquid—

to solid.

Crimson force condensed like blood hardened by heat.

Then—

it rotated.

Whiiiiine—

solid aura spun around the blade at impossible speed.

Like countless saw teeth revolving together.

Vikir moved Beelzebub slightly.

Sparks erupted.

Stone.

Steel reinforcement.

Debris.

All cut apart by a casual motion.

*…Incredible.*

Even Vikir was surprised.

*So this is what Hugo feels?*

At this level—

everything below looked small.

He understood—

a little—

how Hugo had once viewed ordinary fighters as insects.

And in the previous timeline—

Hugo had eventually climbed even farther.

Two levels beyond the point Vikir had temporarily reached.

Sword Master itself possessed stages.

Lower.

Middle.

Upper.

With Dolores's awakened buff—

Vikir had crossed from Peak Sword Graduator into the **lower Sword Master** realm.

Temporarily.

He engraved the feeling into memory.

Someday—

without a blessing—

he would return here by his own power.

[AAAAAGH!]

Dantalian was being cut alive.

Vikir pressed the rotating solid aura against the demon.

Beelzebub—

normally optimized for piercing—

had gained monstrous cutting force.

Its thrusting power had not weakened.

Everything had simply become sharper.

Stronger.

*This power…*

*This feeling that anything is possible…*

Vikir almost felt exhilarated.

Dolores's holy power moved through him with extraordinary precision.

As though the blessing knew the exact shape of every organ—

every blood vessel—

every channel of mana.

Blood flowed faster.

Mana flowed faster.

Aura amplitude increased.

This was **soul resonance**.

Vikir remembered the phenomenon.

In the Age of Destruction—

the Saintess of Steel had healed everyone without discrimination.

But her greatest buffs—

she had granted only to a handful of heroes.

People whose nature could draw out the blessing's maximum effect.

**Hugo Le Baskerville.**

And **Camus Morgue**—

later known as the **Ironblood Empress**.

Heroes of that scale.

Now—

Dolores's power had concentrated on Vikir alone.

It meant she recognized him at a very deep level.

Vikir—

unfortunately—

interpreted the situation more simply.

*Lucky.*

Dolores experienced the connection very differently.

For a brief moment—

she felt as though her existence and the Night Hound's overlapped.

Not physically.

Spiritually.

A complete emotional resonance.

For years—

others had confessed to her.

Their sins.

Their fears.

Their burdens.

But Dolores had often wondered:

*Who do I confess to?*

*Who hears my burden?*

Talking to Rune was not always the same as speaking to another human being.

Yet she had never permitted herself to depend on one.

President.

Club leader.

Saintess.

Daughter.

Every role demanded strength.

Tonight—

for one moment—

she released all of it.

The Night Hound's broad back became a place where her exhausted mind could rest.

Her companion for the night.

Her soul's counterpart in battle.

Crash!

Vikir drove Dantalian backward.

The demon—

already burned by holy fire—

could not handle the sudden Sword Master pressure.

Vikir extended Beelzebub.

The target—

Dantalian's narrow neck.

Thirty-six faces clustered above it like grapes.

Then the demon played its last card.

[Big Brother!]

[Please!]

Peri's face turned toward Vikir.

So did every orphaned child's face.

[We're still alive!]

[If the demon dies, we die!]

[Please don't kill him!]

[Murderer!]

[Do our lives mean nothing!?]

Vikir's sword slowed.

Barely.

But enough.

Dantalian gathered everything.

Dozens of tongues braided together.

One spear.

**Soul Strike.**

BOOM!

The spear drove into Vikir's heart.

“…!”

He flew backward.

Two stone pillars broke.

Rubble buried him.

“No!”

Dolores screamed.

Dantalian stood.

Barely.

[Heh heh…]

[At this level, a single opening decides the battle.]

He turned toward Dolores.

One face moved forward.

**Humbert L. Quovadis.**

[Daughter.]

[You disappoint me.]

Dolores trembled.

[I sent you to the Academy to increase our family's prestige.]

[And now you expose family matters to the world?]

Humbert's face twisted in contempt.

[I thought you would become a better person.]

“…!”

Dolores shrank.

Before—

she had overcome this image because the Night Hound stood beside her.

Now—

he was buried beneath rubble.

The false Humbert opened his mouth.

Wider.

Wider.

Until it resembled a serpent's maw.

Dolores—

curled like something inside an eggshell.

Dantalian—

the snake ready to swallow it.

[Now.]

[Give me your face—]

Slash.

The voice stopped.

Black blood sprayed.

A blade had crossed Dantalian's neck from behind.

Vikir stood there.

Alive.

Dantalian stared.

[How…?]

[I pierced your heart…]

Vikir opened his cloak.

Inside—

a cracked black sphere.

**Madam Eight-Legs's egg.**

The tongue-spear had struck it first.

The shell was damaged—

but intact enough to save Vikir's heart.

He tucked the egg away.

Looked at Dantalian.

“Nothing else to say.”

Then—

slash.

The head came free.

“Go somewhere worse.”

Dantalian fell.

Thud.

Roll.

Thirty-six faces struck the floor.

For an instant—

it looked finished.

Then—

[Big Brother…]

[It hurts…]

Peri's face moved.

Arms and legs regenerated beneath it.

The remnant crawled.

Trying to escape.

Vikir stepped into its path.

His gaze did not move.

Stab.

Beelzebub pierced the center of the body.

At last—

the movement stopped.

Peri's eyes lifted.

Almost closed.

A failing voice escaped.

[…Thank you…]

[Uncle.]

Vikir's expression changed.

Only slightly.

But it changed.

He knelt.

Cradled the face carefully.

The fading remnant reached up.

Arms around his neck.

Kiss.

A light touch against his cheek.

Sssssssss—

then everything disappeared.

The demonic darkness collapsed.

Only ruined stone remained.

Dolores came close.

Her hand covered the back of Vikir's.

“Night Hound…”

“Are you all right?”

She had been trapped in Humbert's fear moments earlier.

Her first concern—

was still him.

Then—

clink.

Metal struck stone.

Both turned.

A gold necklace lay between broken pillars.

Its engraving caught the light.

**Peri.**
