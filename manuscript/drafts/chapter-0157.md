Vikir sighed softly.

*…What is she doing here?*

Dolores.

Hadn't she gone to wash up?

Why was she this far from the girls' dormitory—

in Dormitory Four—

at this hour?

Vikir adjusted the mask hiding his face.

Then prepared the altered voice he used as the Night Hound.

He reached out.

Tapped Dolores lightly on the shoulder.

“…forgive our sins as we forgive those who sin against us…”

“Do not bring us into trial…”

“Deliver us from evil—aaah!?”

The instant Vikir touched her—

Dolores nearly jumped out of her skin.

She squeezed her eyes shut even harder.

Shook.

And refused to look.

“O-Our Father in heaven…”

“Stop reciting the prayer.”

Vikir deliberately roughened his voice.

“Open your eyes first.”

Dolores cautiously separated her clasped hands.

One eye opened.

“…Ah!?”

Her expression twisted in shock.

She stared at the plague-doctor mask.

Once.

Twice.

Then—

“N-Night Hound…?”

“It's been a while.”

Vikir nodded.

Dolores stared blankly.

After several seconds—

she barely managed to answer.

“…It has.”

There were too many things she wanted to say.

Why had he disappeared without a word after the plague?

Why was he attacking Quovadis-affiliated institutions?

Where had he been?

What had he been doing?

Who was he?

The questions piled up faster than her mouth could form them.

Dolores was only seventeen.

Yet she already handled the duties of a third-year student council president—

newspaper-club head—

and Saintess of Quovadis.

Normally—

she carried those burdens with ease.

Right now—

she fidgeted like an ordinary girl her age.

Vikir did not indulge the silence.

“Why are you here?”

“I…”

Dolores hesitated.

For a completely different reason.

Her mind returned to what had happened only a short time earlier.

◆◆◆

After the boys left—

Dolores finally recovered her senses.

She washed thoroughly with warm water.

Then returned to the room.

Her bedding had already been cleaned.

Her roommates had taken care of everything.

Dolores froze.

*This is bad.*

Only now—

with her head clear—

did she understand the situation.

She had been the one who lost control.

Vikir—

who had done nothing wrong—

had taken the blame.

Worse—

he had also been caught in the accident himself.

He was the victim.

Yet everyone believed he was responsible.

The roles had been completely reversed.

*I can't leave it like this.*

If Dolores stayed silent—

Vikir's reputation would collapse.

A first-year student—

accused of wetting himself while lying over a third-year upperclassman—

who was also student council president—

who was also the Saintess of Quovadis.

Dolores knew exactly how fiercely some students idolized her.

Rumors like this could turn into threats.

Harassment.

Even violence.

*No.*

*I have to correct it.*

Late or not—

she had to tell the truth.

Dolores approached the girls gathered nearby.

They were already whispering about what had happened.

And about Vikir.

“He was so calm afterward.”

“Even with his pants soaked.”

“The contrast was so strange it made my heart flutter…”

“Somehow it makes me want to protect him.”

“Like—change his diaper and breastfeed him.”

The conversation had already wandered somewhere bizarre.

Dolores did not listen long enough to notice.

She closed her eyes.

Then announced the truth.

“Everyone!”

“Vikir didn't do anything wrong!”

“I was the one who wet myself!”

The girls stared.

Silence.

Then—

Bianca sighed.

The others followed.

One by one—

they patted Dolores on the shoulder.

“President…”

“You really are a Saintess.”

“You don't have to take a humiliating nickname for him.”

“Senior, that's too much self-sacrifice.”

“I couldn't protect even a close friend like that.”

“Much less some first-year I barely know.”

Dolores's mouth opened.

They did not believe her.

“No!”

“I'm telling the truth!”

“It was me!”

“Not Vikir!”

The more sincerely she insisted—

the more impressed they became by her supposed sacrifice.

Dolores felt her sanity slipping.

*This is impossible.*

She gave up trying to convince them.

Instead—

she decided to act.

She would find Vikir.

Thank him.

Apologize.

Then personally straighten out the misunderstanding.

It was already late.

But if necessary—

she could ask a dorm supervisor to summon him from the boys' quarters for an emergency meeting.

*The sooner I explain, the better.*

Dolores left the girls' floor.

Started toward the men's dormitory.

Then—

“…!”

A dark shadow crossed the outer wall of the building.

She stopped.

Black cloak.

Long-beaked plague-doctor mask.

The figure she had been searching for.

The mysterious vigilante who had appeared in her dreams again and again.

Suspicious.

Unknowable.

And somehow heart-stirring.

The **Night Hound** moved beneath a moon covered by dark clouds.

Everything Dolores had been thinking about—

vanished.

She turned immediately.

Followed him.

She did not think about arresting him.

Did not think about stopping him.

She only—

wanted to see him.

◆◆◆

“…I just wanted to talk.”

Behind the mask—

Vikir gave a faint, bitter smile.

He could guess what she wanted to know.

Why had he stolen the Saintess's Tears?

Why was he attacking branches connected to Quovadis?

Dolores gripped the front of his cloak.

Her eyes trembled like ripples spreading across still water.

“Why…?”

“Why did you do all of that?”

“And that day too—”

“you disappeared without saying anything.”

“…”

“Tell me.”

“Those attacks on our affiliated institutions…”

“You weren't doing them for no reason, were you?”

“There has to be some misunderstanding.”

“…”

“Say anything.”

“Please.”

Her small fists tightened in his clothes.

Then Dolores lowered her head.

Rested her forehead against his chest.

Her voice became wet.

“You aren't like that.”

“You aren't the villain everyone says you are…”

How was he supposed to answer?

There was something in Dolores's voice beyond simple trust.

A sense of comradeship.

*…Because of the Red Death?*

Shared exhaustion.

Filth.

Danger.

deeper—

than Vikir had expected.
