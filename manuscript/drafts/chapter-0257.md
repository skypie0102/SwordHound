# Chapter 257: The Grave of Swords (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/257.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 255 is the verified shifted secondary witness.

From cradle to grave.

For the hounds of the Iron-Blooded Sword House—

life was nothing but a bed of blades.

*A true Baskerville is born in the Cradle of Swords.*

Vikir murmured the saying everyone in the family knew.

But there was another line.

A continuation almost nobody remembered.

*A true Baskerville dies in the Grave of Swords.*

That sentence survived only on a faded page in an old history book.

Most people treated it as a myth.

Now—

standing before the Grave of Swords—

Vikir no longer did.

The old Baskerville sayings were not metaphors.

From the cradle to the grave—

the path of a hound was made of blades.

Vikir stepped inside.

The tower stairs rose steeply.

Every step projected like a spike.

An ordinary person might not even have recognized them as stairs.

A mage tower was mysterious.

Beautiful.

Elegant.

This place was the opposite.

Bleak.

Oppressive.

Lonely.

Every step brought him close to jagged stone, stalactites, and rusted blades embedded throughout the interior.

The slightest mistake would cut flesh.

Countless sword points seemed to watch him.

A swordsman’s instincts only made the pressure worse.

The farther he climbed—

the more it felt as if his entire body were being worn down.

Shaved away.

Reduced step by step.

If Vikir were not a Sword Master—

he would have been torn apart shortly after entering.

Eventually—

he reached the upper level.

Thousands of swords were driven into the chamber.

The sight resembled the Cradle of Swords.

But colder.

Sharper.

And far more hostile.

Because someone was sitting at the center.

An iron throne—

formed from gathered blades.

Upon it sat an old man in heavy black armor.

A long gray beard fell across his chest.

Beneath gray brows—

the whites of his eyes were pitch-black.

In their center burned red pupils like small suns.

His nose was sharp as a blade.

His lips were sealed.

Bluish skin clung tightly to his skull.

The massive greatsword beside him made the throne look even more like a fortress.

“…!”

Vikir recognized him immediately.

**Cane Corso Le Baskerville.**

One of the oldest Seven Counts.

The strongest among the current generation of Counts.

An elder kinsman whom even Hugo—

after climbing to the position of Head through blood—

had never been able to force into submission.

Ordinary Seven Counts stood somewhere between Peak Sword Graduator and Sword Master.

Cane Corso was different.

Before withdrawing from the world—

he had already surpassed Hugo.

A living sword legend.

He never attended family gatherings.

He refused retainers.

Refused knights.

Refused politics.

Then, late in life—

he erased every trace of himself and vanished.

His accomplishments survived only in records.

And those records sounded so impossible that even direct Baskerville descendants often dismissed them.

But nobody denied one thing.

Cane Corso had been the strongest swordsman in the family.

Hugo himself had once admitted it.

*If my elder brother had wanted the seat of Head, I would never have been able to sit there.*

Vikir looked at the old man.

*So you really were here.*

Before regression—

even during the Age of Destruction—

Cane Corso had never openly appeared.

But one incident had made people suspect that he was still alive.

A massive demonic force once crossed Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Several legions vanished while passing through the Yuuni Desert.

Salt storms and the Basilisk were dangerous—

but not enough to erase entire armies without survivors.

A demonic pursuit force was sent to investigate.

None returned.

Every demon that entered the salt desert disappeared—

or was later found torn into pieces and scattered by the storm.

Humanity’s allied command eventually concluded that something extraordinarily powerful lived there.

Several reclusive masters were proposed as possible causes.

Cane Corso was one of them.

*Hugo probably guessed it too.*

*A Baskerville courier died here trying to reach him.*

That was why Vikir had come.

Hugo Le Baskerville had reached Sword Master and the Seventh Fang.

Cane Corso had supposedly gone beyond him.

If anyone could show Vikir the road toward the Eighth Fang—

and whatever existed above ordinary Sword Master—

it would be this man.

There was another reason too.

The Wraith Tree.

Based on **The Return of the Magic Hound**, Vikir had expected the Wraith Tree to be somewhere in the Yuuni Desert.

His future memories supported that.

During the Age of Destruction—

he had seen its branches extend far beyond the salt desert.

Countless people died trying to prevent its fruit from falling into demonic hands.

Their blood only fed it further.

The tree eventually rose higher than Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain itself.

A monstrous World Tree of death.

Its skeletal branches stretched in every direction.

Skull-shaped fruit hung like corpses.

When demons bit into them—

dense otherworldly mana spilled out with blood-colored juice and agonized screams.

Dying undead soldiers could recover completely after consuming the fruit.

The Wraith Tree had become a catastrophe for humanity.

Vikir pulled himself out of the memory.

Behind the iron throne—

there it was.

Black.

Withered.

Wrong.

A small Wraith Tree.

It fed on resentment and grief.

Its branches bore the potential to carry the powers of the dead.

It was still small now.

But Vikir knew what kind of shadow it could cast in the future.

Cane Corso raised his head.

His voice was deep.

[This is the Grave of Swords.]

[The place reached by those who pursue the ultimate meaning of the sword.]

His inverted eyes fixed on Vikir’s young face.

Curiosity flickered in them.

[…Child.]

[Who are you?]

By blood and generation—

Cane Corso was an elder relative.

Vikir had no interest in family etiquette.

Shing.

He drew his sword.

“Find out yourself.”

There were only two things he wanted here.

The road to Baskerville’s Eighth Fang.

And the Wraith Tree behind that throne.
