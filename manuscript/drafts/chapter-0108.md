# Chapter 108: The Returned Hero (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/108.txt` — `1ff78b25ce13e6d371e39ff8e90ee470586bc83d`. Recovered English Chapter 107 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established terminology controls **Underdog City**, **Chihuahua Baskerville**, **Trust Won by Moving the Stake**, **Cindywendy**, **Pomeranian La Baskerville**, **Rokoko**, **Hugo Le Baskerville**, **Penelope La Baskerville**, and **Lady Roxana**. Vikir's predictions about Hugo's reaction to Pomeranian are explicitly his own pessimistic expectations, not narrator-certified facts.

Vikir rode into **Underdog City**.

The place had changed.

Fewer drunks.

Fewer people sleeping in the streets.

Cleaner alleys.

More order.

One thing felt wrong.

Children were missing.

Normally they would be everywhere.

Running.

Shouting.

Playing.

Now—

almost none.

The few Vikir saw had adults beside them.

Others rushed indoors as though the street itself was dangerous.

Vikir continued toward City Hall.

The old plaque still hung outside.

**Trust Won by Moving the Stake.**

A relic from the ridiculous day he had burned ten billion gold to make his law credible.

Inside, clerks moved frantically.

Vikir found a new employee outside Chihuahua's office.

He tapped her desk.

No response.

Again.

“Take a number.”

Vikir paused.

Fair.

Officially—

he was still dead.

He took a ticket.

**3021.**

Then waited.

Hours.

Finally—

3020 left.

Vikir stood.

BANG.

A nearby clerk jumped up.

“Message from Red Salt Castle!”

Heads turned.

“Lord Vikir is alive!”

Chaos.

“What?”

“Confirmed!”

“Morgue sent the notice personally!”

“He's on his way here!”

City Hall exploded into motion.

“Contact Baskerville!”

“Prepare a red carpet!”

“We only have white!”

“Then stain it somehow!”

The clerk in front of Vikir threw aside her work.

Vikir blocked her.

“My turn.”

“Come back tomorrow.”

“Too late.”

“We have an extremely important guest arriving.”

“I need Chihuahua.”

That slowed her.

Anyone asking directly for the chief administrator could not be ignored completely.

She shoved a form toward him.

“Name and purpose of visit. Quickly.”

Vikir wrote.

> **Name:** Vikir Van Baskerville  
> **Purpose of Visit:** Returned alive

The clerk took the paper without reading it.

She knocked.

“Director! Visitor!”

Chihuahua shouted from inside.

“No meetings today!”

“But—”

“Lord Vikir is coming. Send whoever it is away!”

The clerk turned back.

“Sorry. Next time make an appointment, Mister…”

Her eyes dropped.

“Vikir.”

Silence.

She adjusted her glasses.

Read again.

Then again.

Other employees noticed.

One leaned over.

Then another.

Then ten.

Then almost everyone in the office.

The building went silent.

“Customer…”

The clerk's voice became tiny.

“Customer number 3021 may enter.”

◆◆◆

Chihuahua looked up when Vikir opened the office door.

“At least you used the door this time.”

Vikir collapsed onto the sofa.

Then a small body ran toward him.

“Daddy!”

Pomeranian.

Vikir caught her.

“Uncle.”

“Uncle!”

She laughed and buried herself against him.

Her Imperial had improved.

Chihuahua's education had worked.

Then she proudly presented a rat.

Thin.

Skin and bone.

“Rat!”

Vikir nodded.

“Very cute.”

He looked at Chihuahua.

They exchanged reports.

Cindywendy had solved most of Underdog's administrative problems.

One remained.

Chihuahua removed a file.

> **Unsolved Serial Child-Abduction Case**

Children from the slums had been disappearing.

Repeatedly.

No ransom demands.

Almost no trail.

Possibly one perpetrator.

That explained the empty streets.

Vikir frowned.

“Cindywendy will find something.”

“I hope so.”

Then Pomeranian tugged his sleeve.

“Rat!”

Vikir looked again.

The rat moved.

Wrong.

Not alive.

Dead flesh.

Dead bone.

Yet dancing in Pomeranian's hand.

Vikir stared.

“Pomeranian.”

“Yes?”

“Did you make it move?”

She nodded brightly.

“When did you learn?”

She counted on her fingers.

Five nights.

“Who taught you?”

“The tribe.”

Rokoko.

Vikir understood.

Pomeranian had spent her early life inside a tribe famous for black magic.

But the talent itself was extraordinary.

Five years old.

Already sensing and manipulating negative mana.

Children considered geniuses in Baskerville or Morgue might only begin sensing ordinary mana around eight.

Pomeranian was already reviving a small corpse.

Vikir's thoughts shifted toward Hugo.

How would he react?

Vikir expected badly.

Hugo measured worth ruthlessly.

He despised magic.

Especially black magic.

And Pomeranian also carried blood from outside Baskerville.

That was Vikir's expectation.

Not certainty.

He looked down at the child clinging to his leg.

**Pomeranian La Baskerville.**

Penelope's only daughter.

Hugo and Lady Roxana's granddaughter.

The most direct female descendant Vikir knew.

Perhaps introducing them was a mistake.

Then—

BANG.

Something struck the window.

An owl.

Chihuahua read the attached message.

His face drained.

“Lord Vikir.”

“What?”

“Main house.”

He swallowed.

“The Patriarch heard you're alive.”

Outside—

horses stopped hard.

Heavy knocking shook the entrance.

Chihuahua stared toward the door.

“He came himself.”
