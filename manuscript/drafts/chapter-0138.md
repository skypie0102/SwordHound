# Chapter 138: Test Your Skills (6)

The next morning—

a crowd had gathered outside **Cold Department, Class B**.

First-years.

Second-years.

Third-years.

Even a few fourth-years who normally avoided campus events while preparing for graduation.

Most of the crowd—

girls.

“Who was the guy from yesterday's match?”

“The one who pushed his hair back after washing his face?”

“Was that him?”

“No way.”

“He really looks like that?”

Rumors had spread quickly.

Anyone who had watched the Naphtali match—

in person or through recorded magical images—

had noticed the same moment.

Vikir.

Glasses off.

Wet hair pushed away from his face.

Walking toward the water station after the match.

The student newspaper had noticed too.

A magically animated still now looped on the sports page.

> **[Exclusive] Who Is the First-Year Cold Department Mystery Student From Yesterday's Naphtali Match?**  
> **Views: 29,872**
>
> A previously unidentified first-year student contributed a dramatic point during yesterday's inter-class Naphtali match and played an important role in Class B's victory. Interest surged after his appearance was briefly captured when he removed his glasses and pushed back his wet hair after the game…

The comments multiplied even faster than the views.

“Is that image edited? How can someone actually look like that?”

“I didn't know the Cold Department had someone that handsome.”

“He's really a first-year? I would have noticed him at orientation.”

“I'm a guy and even I got caught staring.”

“I ran Naphtali with him yesterday. He really looks like that. I kept staring too.”

“Look at that jawline and that messy hair…”

“Water running down his jaw—my heart's going to burst.”

“Tomorrow I'm searching the whole Cold Department until I find him.”

“Calm down. You're about to graduate.”

By morning—

people were actively searching.

“Where's Vikir?”

“Tudor's fine, but I came here to see Vikir!”

“Vikir! Where are you? I came because of you!”

“I bought ten thousand handkerchiefs just to wipe the water from his face!”

The rumors drew crowds of female students and some male students alike, with the upperclassmen making the loudest fuss.

Inside the classroom—

Vikir sat at his usual window seat.

Today—

his hood was up.

His glasses firmly in place.

Most of his face hidden.

Pigi had recommended the disguise.

Vikir had accepted the advice immediately.

*This is ridiculous.*

The newspaper attention had been completely unexpected.

Before regression—

his face had been ruined early.

Burns.

Knife scars.

Poison discoloration.

A lifetime of injuries.

He had never needed to think about whether his appearance attracted attention.

In this life—

things were different.

His face had remained mostly unharmed.

Even when he was injured—

the regenerative abilities taken from monsters erased most scars.

*I really didn't account for this.*

Then—

the crowd outside went silent.

A familiar figure had appeared.

**Professor Banshee Morgue.**

Students immediately moved aside.

Banshee looked at the people blocking his classroom.

“I don't know what this commotion is about.”

“But upperclassmen are not permitted to loiter inside lower-year classrooms without authorization.”

His voice stayed flat.

“Leave.”

No one argued.

The crowd scattered.

Vikir relaxed slightly.

*For once—Professor Banshee helped me.*

Then class began.

“All right.”

Banshee placed his materials on the desk.

“Let's review the research assignment from last session.”

He had ordered every student to investigate a specific monster and prepare an original report.

Today's specimen—

> **<Venomphion>**
>
> **Danger Level:** A  
> **Size:** approximately 3 m  
> **Found in:** Eastern Continent — sandfall region of the Cruel Desert  
> **Alias:** **Reaper Beneath the Sand**
>
> A giant scorpion protected by heavy natural armor and exceptionally potent venom.  
> Its exoskeleton is harder than steel.  
> Its massive pincers can tear or crush prey.  
> One drop of venom from the tail is said to be lethal to **44 people**.

A preserved specimen lay across the laboratory table.

Huge.

Black.

Armored.

A rare insect-type monster from the distant eastern deserts.

Preservation magic had been applied immediately after death.

It was one of Banshee's prized specimens.

The professor looked over the class.

“Venomphion burrows beneath sand at high speed.”

“Like a shark showing only its fin above water—”

“it often leaves only the tail visible while moving.”

Students lowered their eyes.

Banshee's questions were rarely easy.

And Venomphion was poorly documented.

Ordinary monster encyclopedias contained only fragments.

Banshee's mouth moved slightly.

Perhaps a smile.

“Good.”

“Who would like to present first?”

He looked toward the Cold Department.

“I'll give you the opportunity before the Hot Department.”

The Hot Department students smiled.

The Cold Department did not.

No one volunteered.

Banshee clicked his tongue.

“No confidence in your own work?”

“How exactly do you expect to graduate?”

“Continue to graduate school?”

“Find employment?”

“Disappointing.”

He scanned the room.

“Fine.”

“I'll choose.”

One long finger pointed.

Pigi stiffened.

“…Me?”

Pigi had ranked third on the written examination.

His research ability was excellent.

He stood and began carefully.

“Venomphion has a thick black exoskeleton harder than steel.”

“The larger right pincer functions more like a hammer than cutting shears.”

“The tail stinger is used to immobilize prey.”

“Official records place the largest specimens near three meters—”

“but several indigenous accounts from severe drought periods describe individuals exceeding five.”

Banshee raised a hand.

“Enough.”

Pigi stopped.

“You found rare material.”

“That deserves credit.”

Then the professor's expression cooled.

“But where is your own conclusion?”

“Everything you said already exists somewhere.”

“Are you a researcher—”

“or a parrot?”

Pigi's face fell.

Banshee looked toward the score system.

“One point deducted from the Cold Department group attitude score.”

Groans followed.

“Now.”

Banshee turned.

“Let's see what the Hot Department produced.”

The Hot Department students stiffened.

Then—

one pale hand rose confidently.

Sinclaire.

Banshee's eyes narrowed.

“Hot Department first place.”

“Written-exam second place.”

“Yes, Professor.”

“Go ahead.”

Sinclaire stood.

“I am Sinclaire of Hot Department First-Year Class A.”

She held up four densely written pages.

“I divided Venomphion hunting behavior into three patterns.”

Banshee's expression sharpened with interest.

“Oh?”

Sinclaire continued.

“Venomphion is among the hardest desert predators to subdue.”

“Other than very large burrowing monsters—such as giant sand worms—it has few natural enemies.”

Banshee nodded.

“Known.”

“Yes.”

“My report goes further.”

She spread out several charts.

“I compared attacks on humans with observed feeding behavior and divided individuals into three behavioral types.”

The first—

**Brute type.**

Exceptional charge speed.

Strength.

Endurance.

Low adaptability.

Poor response to traps.

Recommended strategy—

encirclement, ambush, and prepared terrain instead of frontal confrontation.

The second—

**Erratic type.**

Smaller frame.

Lower raw power.

Highly unpredictable movement.

May attack using middle or rear legs rather than the obvious pincers.

Recommended strategy—

high-power ranged attacks or immediate decisive force before close engagement.

The third—

**Cunning type.**

Strong body.

High intelligence.

Combines the advantages of the other two patterns.

Recommended strategy—

coordinated suppression by advanced knights or mages.

Sinclaire finished.

Banshee took the pages.

Read.

Then nodded.

“There are too few confirmed Venomphion observations to call this a complete classification.”

“But your argument has support.”

“I'll submit the possibility to the academic board.”

Students whispered.

Praise from Banshee was rare.

Sinclaire sat down—

visibly pleased.

And every so often—

she glanced toward the window corner.

Toward Vikir.

Banshee noticed the Cold Department still had nothing comparable.

He folded his arms.

“The Hot Department has offered an original hypothesis.”

“Potentially useful to academia.”

“What about the Cold Department?”

Silence.

Banshee waited.

No volunteers.

Then—

“Vikir.”

The name landed precisely.

Vikir raised his eyes beneath his bangs.

Since the previous class—

Banshee had begun calling on him frequently.

Always with that same dissatisfied expression.

Vikir looked tired of it.

Banshee smiled faintly.

“You seem unusually knowledgeable about monsters.”

“Let's see your report.”

“What did you research?”

“…Nothing special.”

Vikir had been busy every night outside the Academy.

He had barely touched the assignment.

So his report contained only a few sentences.

Basic facts.

Common knowledge.

He read them aloud.

“If you avoid Venomphion's first stinger on the tail—”

“and the second stinger on the abdomen—”

“it is closer to a B+ danger-level monster than an A.”

Silence.

Vikir expected the statement to sound obvious.

Instead—

every head in the classroom tilted.

Banshee frowned.

“…Second stinger?”

He looked genuinely confused.

“What are you talking about?”
