The **Sherpa Guild**.

Not famous—

for elite mercenaries.

Famous—

for guides.

Hunters and guides—

who knew monster ecology.

geography.

migration routes.

nesting grounds.

escape paths.

Most guides—

came from local families—

rooted in the same regions—

for three generations or more.

Knowledge—

their weapon.

The problem—

guides and porters—

were rarely respected.

Combat teams took the glory.

Support workers—

received reduced pay.

Lost credit.

Became shields—

when plans failed.

Sherpa survived—

because of one man.

Creak—

the guild doors opened.

Everyone in the training yard turned.

“Guildmaster!”

“Attention!”

“Good morning!”

A giant entered.

More than 2.3 meters tall.

Near one hundred eighty kilograms.

Cropped hair.

Scars—

across his face.

Muscle—

like plate armor.

**Miniature Pinscher.**

Known—

as **MiniPin**.

A man—

whose appearance alone—

discouraged wage theft.

His rule—

strict meritocracy.

His training—

merciless.

“The more you sweat in training—

the less you bleed in battle!”

Sherpa's porters and guides—

therefore—

were tougher than most support workers.

MiniPin himself—

had reached **Graduator** level.

Strong enough—

for top mercenary guilds.

The Imperial Army—

had tried recruiting him—

as an officer / instructor.

He refused.

*I like hunting.*

*Monsters are my calling.*

One private dream—

remained.

Baskerville.

Western Front.

Monster extermination—

under the Iron-Blooded Sword Clan.

But Baskerville—

had never invited him.

So—

MiniPin protected Sherpa.

And that meant—

protecting its members from—

bad clients.

He sat in his office.

Read requests.

“Live Venompion capture?”

“Too few combatants.”

“Porters die.”

“Reject.”

“Merchant route guidance?”

“Escort team too weak.”

“Guide dies.”

“Reject.”

Request—

after request—

discarded.

Nearly ninety percent.

MiniPin clicked his tongue.

“How many of these idiots—

will still be alive next year?”

People—

constantly hunted above their level.

When things went wrong—

the slowest—

weakest—

support worker—

became bait.

MiniPin refused—

to sacrifice his people.

So—

unless a client could prove—

mercenary credentials.

military service.

real combat ability—

Sherpa refused them.

Then—

one request.

“What?”

“Academy student?”

“First-year?”

> **Request**
>
> Name: Vikir  
> Affiliation: Colosseo Academy  
> Request: Hire one guide  
> Purpose: Monster hunting

No target monster.

No credential.

Just—

a first-year student.

MiniPin scoffed.

“Rich Academy kid—

looking for an adventure?”

He crumpled the paper.

Threw it away.

“Give him milk.”

“Send him home.”

“That brat thinks—

hunting is a game?”

The request—

ignored.

And—

that—

became the start—

of MiniPin's worst week.

◆◆◆

Moonless night.

MiniPin came home late.

His wife—

asleep.

His daughter—

asleep.

On the table—

eggs.

bacon.

toasted cornbread.

Cold—

but prepared.

“…Honey.”

He looked toward the bedroom.

Wife—

married young.

Worked hard.

Daughter—

growing up.

MiniPin—

always felt—

he had not given them enough.

“Just a little longer.”

“One day—

I'll land a big commission.”

“Then I'll make it up to you.”

His wife had fallen asleep—

putting their daughter down.

So MiniPin—

took the larger bed—

alone.

Closed his eyes.

Rustle.

Second-floor window.

“…?”

A silhouette—

entered.

Black mask.

No visible weapon.

MiniPin almost laughed.

*A thief.*

Of all houses—

this one.

MiniPin had dealt with thieves before.

Hungry men—

who climbed fences.

He beat them.

Lectured them.

Sometimes—

hired them.

He planned—

the same here.

“Stop.”

“Surrender.”

“Explain yourself.”

“If the reason is good—

I may let you go.”

The masked intruder—

threw one punch.

BOOM!

It missed MiniPin's head—

by centimeters.

Hair—

cut by wind.

Behind him—

a fist-shaped hole—

appeared in the wooden wall.

“This is a warning.”

MiniPin—

went cold.

*If that hit—*

Skull—

gone.

“Who the hell are you!?”

Mana gathered—

around MiniPin's fist.

Low Graduator.

Between gas—

and liquid density.

The intruder raised—

both fists.

No mana.

MiniPin realized—

the first punch—

had been—

pure muscle.

*What kind of body…?*

Then—

relief.

*At least he can't use mana.*

Mana—

erupted from both of the intruder's fists.

MiniPin's eyes widened.

Too late.

BAM!

A fist—

under the jaw.

Then—

nothing.
