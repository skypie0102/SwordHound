# Chapter 370: The Servant (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/370.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 368 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical English **D'Ordume D'Orcdile** and **Kirko Grimm** are freshly verified through the designated Fandom index. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Nouvelle Vague, BDISSEM, Decarabia, Garam Nord, Bastille, Level Nine, and the Five Wardens.

CRUNCH.

A gigantic palm pinned Vikir beneath it.

“…Definitely not human strength.”

He was right.

The hand pressing him down—

was no human hand.

A palm almost as large as a bed.

Rough, thick scales across the back.

Black nails curved like hooks.

Thin webbing between enormous fingers.

Beyond them—

black scales.

Burning yellow eyes.

A **saltwater crocodile**.

D'Ordume had become something half human—

half crocodile.

“I hate this form.”

“My clothes always tear.”

He bared terrifying teeth.

Vikir looked at the transformed warden.

*So the rumor may be true.*

*All five wardens of Nouvelle Vague—

nonhuman.*

Beastfolk.

Humans born with animal traits mixed into their blood.

Like orcs.

Dwarves.

Elves.

Intelligent races—

capable of living alongside humans.

Most of the time—

they looked human enough.

But when emotion reached an extreme—

animal traits could erupt dramatically.

That difference had brought centuries of prejudice.

Now—

beastfolk had become rare.

*Saltwater-crocodile lineage.*

*That’s exceptionally uncommon.*

Vikir shoved his hands into the soft soil beneath him.

Then—

slipped free beneath D'Ordume’s palm.

D'Ordume snapped his jaws.

Pursued.

A thick tail tore from beneath his ruined uniform.

WHAM!

One swing—

collapsed the stone mound behind Vikir.

Vikir entered through the space the tail created.

His BDISSEM chain whipped into D'Ordume’s ribs.

CLANG.

No effect.

Hard scales covered the body beneath the uniform.

That explained the earlier punch.

“You cannot win.”

D'Ordume drove his spiraling forearm blades into the ground.

BOOOOOOM!

A violent storm erupted from below.

Rock.

Dirt.

Sand.

Lava.

Everything caught inside—

was ground to fine dust.

Sulfur gas was dragged into the rotating center.

Ignited.

The air itself began to boil.

BOOM.

BOOM.

BOOM.

The worksite looked as though a god of destruction had descended.

Prisoners and guards fled to the edges—

shaking.

Vikir reached a conclusion.

*No.*

*Not like this.*

With BDISSEM restraints on—

he could not beat D'Ordume directly.

Even resurrection would not change the basic disadvantage.

Without the Hell Tree stats—

he would have been torn apart already.

*Then—

how about this?*

Vikir avoided another shockwave.

Ran.

Toward the slope where he had been moving boulders earlier.

D'Ordume followed.

The instant the warden reached the middle of the incline—

CLICK.

Vikir kicked away a prepared support.

Burning boulders began rolling downhill.

BOOM!

One massive rock crashed toward D'Ordume.

*Terrain familiarity is fundamental to combat.*

Vikir always surveyed terrain before a fight.

He had done it in Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

At the orphanage.

And now—

inside Nouvelle Vague.

D'Ordume did not flinch.

“You underestimate a Nouvelle Vague warden.”

He inhaled.

Raised one enormous arm.

Then—

BOOM!

One punch shattered the boulder.

The spiraling blades followed—

grinding every fragment into dust.

Cheers erupted from the guards.

Vikir nodded.

*Strong.*

“Try another.”

He kicked more supports.

GRRRRRR.

Larger rocks began rolling.

A bead of sweat appeared on D'Ordume’s forehead.

His subordinates—

were watching.

Retreat here—

and the warden lost face.

Exactly as Vikir expected.

D'Ordume planted his feet.

BOOM!

One boulder—

destroyed.

BOOM!

Second.

CRASH!

Third.

Then—

the fourth.

“…!”

His punch cracked it—

but failed to erase it completely.

The remaining mass struck him.

D'Ordume flew backward.

Straight into the lava pool below.

SPLASH.

HISSSSS.

Molten rock swallowed him.

Silence.

The guards—

who had been cheering seconds before—

froze.

Then—

BOOOOM!

The surface of the lava exploded.

A massive black shape rose from it.

D'Ordume.

Burned.

Uniform reduced to rags.

Furious.

“YOU BASTARD!”

“I’LL KILL YOU!”

His muscles swelled even larger.

Rage—

had pushed the beast transformation farther.

Then—

“Enough.”

Vikir raised one hand.

D'Ordume stopped.

Only for an instant.

But that was enough.

“I’m done resisting.”

“Spare my life.”

“…What?”

For perhaps the first time that day—

D'Ordume looked genuinely confused.

Level Nine prisoners—

were monsters with enormous pride.

Once one began a riot—

the usual ending was execution.

Yet now—

Vikir was surrendering.

“I can’t beat you.”

“If I could use mana—

maybe.”

“But like this—

I don’t want to die like a dog.”

“I’ll accept punishment.”

“From now on—

I’ll work properly.”

Every guard stared.

D'Ordume’s jaw tightened.

“You started all this—

just to surrender now?”

Vikir moved slightly to one side.

“One more thing.”

“While I was working—

I loosened a lot of the supports.”

“…!”

“If you refuse to spare me—

everything I worked on today—

goes back to zero.”

Vikir had done far more than remove the supports he had just kicked loose.

Several anchor points above—

were already compromised.

If the remaining boulders came down—

the entire Level Nine section could lose its day’s construction.

D'Ordume ground his teeth.

If Vikir truly attempted escape—

he could eventually be caught.

But the damage—

would be enormous.

And now—

everyone was watching.

Every other work crew had stopped.

The disturbance needed to end.

“…Fine.”

“If you surrender—

I waive summary execution.”

“Good.”

Vikir did not descend the slope until D'Ordume said it publicly.

The instant he came down—

guards rushed him.

One BDISSEM restraint.

Then another.

Then another.

Double.

Triple.

Layers of suppression.

D'Ordume approached.

Vikir knelt.

Then—

BANG!

A full-power punch struck Vikir’s face.

Blood spilled.

Vikir collapsed.

D'Ordume snorted.

“Take him to solitary.”

“Three months.”

He paused.

“No.”

“One hundred days.”

Bastille nodded.

“Yes.”

“A hundred days of solitary confinement is essentially a death sentence.”

Guards grabbed Vikir’s chains.

Dragged him away.

His body scraped across the ground.

Toward a deep tunnel.

Exactly where he wanted to go.

D'Ordume turned back.

“…Riot over!”

“Resume labor!”

Nobody moved.

“….”

Prisoner.

Guard.

Everyone—

simply stared.

D'Ordume roared again.

“What are you doing?!”

“GET BACK TO WORK!”

Kirko Grimm—

standing nearby—

raised a hand hesitantly.

“Colonel…”

“This may be disrespectful…”

“but there’s no work left.”

“…What?”

D'Ordume frowned.

Kirko pointed behind him.

He turned.

Collapsed mountains.

Shattered rock.

Excavated ground.

The worksite—

had advanced enormously.

During his furious fight with Vikir—

D'Ordume had destroyed and cleared so much material—

that he had accidentally completed the prisoners’ entire daily quota.

“…!”

“…!”

“…!”

The veins on D'Ordume’s forehead bulged.

He had done everyone’s work—

himself.
