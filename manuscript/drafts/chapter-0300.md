SWISH.

A whip had gone straight through Winston’s heart.

Then—

laughter filled the auditorium.

“Hehehehehe!”

A woman in glossy black tights and towering heels stepped from behind the curtain.

**Ms. Ouroboros.**

No explanation was necessary.

The whip piercing Winston belonged to her.

For one moment—

everyone simply stared.

This was the first time the criminal who had terrorized the Imperial Capital had appeared so openly.

Ouroboros gave them no time to process it.

“Ugh…”

Winston collapsed across the stage.

A Sword Master-level swordsman.

The Headmaster of Colosseo.

Reduced to this—

in a single instant—

in front of everyone.

Then—

CRUNCH.

Ouroboros drove one narrow heel into him again.

A confirmation strike.

Winston’s ruined heart stopped.

CLANG.

His sword rolled across the stage.

A famous weapon shaped like a unicorn horn.

The **Winter Sword Orwell**.

Ouroboros picked it up.

“Ah.”

“Finally.”

“The key.”

That single sentence broke the frozen silence.

Screams erupted.

Students.

Parents.

Professors.

Then Ms. Ouroboros removed her mask.

Banshee’s eyes widened.

“P-Professor Sadi?!”

The identity was undeniable.

The criminal responsible for chaos across the Capital—

was Colosseo’s own problem professor.

**Professor Donatien Alphonse François Sadi de Sade.**

“Hehehe.”

“Ah.”

“What a wonderful feeling.”

Sadi seemed almost intoxicated by the panic.

Banshee shouted:

“You’ve finally lost your mind!”

“You murdered the man who raised you!”

Winston had protected Sadi for years.

Supported her.

Prevented her expulsion and ruin.

Even faculty normally hostile to Banshee joined in.

“How could you do this to Winston?!”

“He raised you from childhood!”

“You ungrateful monster!”

“Just like your grandfather!”

“Blood never lies!”

Sadi covered one ear.

“So loud.”

“Is this a duck farm?”

Her other hand moved slightly.

The whip barely shifted at the handle.

But the force multiplied along its length.

BOOM!

The ground beneath several professors exploded.

Skilled swordsmen and mages were thrown backward.

Everyone saw the aura coating the whip.

Liquid.

Dense.

Violent.

“Peak Sword Graduator!”

“Thank God she hasn’t crossed into Sword Master!”

“But that weapon is a whip…”

That qualification changed the calculation.

Whips.

Bows.

Chains.

Other irregular weapons.

In the hands of true specialists—

they could produce force far beyond what the user’s raw rank suggested.

A small movement at the handle became enormous at the tip.

Sadi’s peak-Graduator aura—

amplified through her whip—

struck with power approaching Sword Master territory.

Banshee stepped forward.

“Have you thought this through?”

“Students.”

“Professors.”

“Parents.”

“Do you believe you can fight everyone here alone?”

Sadi clicked her tongue.

“Old man.”

“I told you not to call me by name.”

Then smiled.

“Also—”

“when did I say I came alone?”

BOOM!

Explosions struck the Academy’s outer barriers.

Faces appeared outside the windows.

Men.

Women.

Old people.

Young ones.

Different bodies.

Different ages.

Two things united them.

Every one was a notorious criminal wanted by the Empire.

And every one had recently vanished from pursuit.

A chill ran through Banshee.

*So that’s why the arrest rate collapsed.*

The missing fugitives were here.

Ripper Jack.

The Beast of Gévaudan.

The Tiger of Champawat.

The Recluse of the Pavel House.

Sam’s Son.

The Serial Clown.

The Fashion Master.

The Demon of Lorelei.

The Frog Dissector.

The Hangman of Hillside.

The Green River Killer.

Bloody Mary.

Names that had once terrified entire districts.

All of them stared at Sadi.

Adoration.

Fear.

Obsession.

“My Queen!”

“We broke the barrier as ordered!”

“Please punish me again!”

“Whip me!”

“Step on me!”

Sadi had conditioned them completely.

Mind.

Body.

Fear.

Desire.

She raised one finger.

“All of you.”

“Explode together.”

The criminals answered as one.

“Yes, Your Majesty!”

Then forced mana through their bodies.

Beyond control.

BOOM!

BOOM BOOM BOOM!

Mana rampage.

Self-detonation.

The grand auditorium collapsed.

Steel.

Stone.

Glass.

Dust.

Sadi laughed beneath the falling wreckage.

Banshee raised a barrier and forced debris aside.

“You…”

“Why are you doing this?”

“Are you truly insane?”

He wanted the answer.

But he also wanted time.

Reinforcements.

Sadi knew that.

She indulged him anyway.

“I’m not insane.”

“The world is.”

“…What?”

“I endured.”

“I waited.”

“All so I could ring a bell loud enough for this diseased world to hear.”

“What were you waiting for?”

“This.”

“More precisely—”

“for Winston to return.”

“And for him to gather the parents here.”

“A message carries farther when the audience matters.”

Banshee’s eyes narrowed.

“…The Forty-Seven People Riot.”

Sadi smiled.

“So you already knew.”

“Good information network, old man.”

She looked toward the students.

“You little Academy idiots probably learned that event as a coup by forty-seven greedy fools blinded by power.”

“Wrong.”

She repeated the public history.

Thirty-five years ago.

Forty-seven family heads.

An attack on the Imperial Palace.

Forty-six executed.

One survivor imprisoned in Nouvelle Vague.

Motive supposedly—

the throne.

Sadi shook her head.

“My grandfather and the other forty-six rebelled because they missed the old world.”

Banshee frowned.

“The old world?”

“An age where strength was truth.”

“Where the strong lived.”

Her eyes shone.

“The **Warring States Era**.”

Before unification—

hundreds called themselves kings.

States appeared and vanished constantly.

Weak powers disappeared.

Strong ones rose.

Strength—

was law.

Revenge.

Private punishment.

Blood feuds.

Honor.

All decided through force.

Then unification arrived.

Large wars ended.

Bureaucracy expanded.

Law replaced personal violence.

Even weak people—

if clever enough—

could rise through examinations and command those physically stronger than them.

At one point—

some had seriously discussed restricting swords and magic altogether.

“Peace turns people into pigs.”

Sadi’s face twisted.

“Humans exist to struggle.”

“To grow stronger.”

“To win.”

“And yet this world lets things with no qualification to call themselves human wear human faces and demand protection.”

Banshee remained cold.

“All right.”

“Then why Winston?”

“The man raised you.”

“Protected you after your grandfather’s rebellion.”

“Prevented collective punishment from destroying a child who had lost everything.”

“That is how you repay him?”

Sadi laughed once.

“Winston was the one who defeated and captured my grandfather.”

“He was injured by mana rampage during that fight.”

“You think I never learned that?”

Banshee stiffened.

Sadi pointed around them.

“The Academy itself is part of the problem.”

“A system for trapping warriors inside institutions.”

“Polishing the cage with words like success.”

“Status.”

“Career.”

“Teaching people with swords and magic to beg the powerful for scraps.”

“A warrior should sharpen themselves on battlefields.”

“Build a family.”

“A nation.”

“Take what they can defend.”

Banshee answered:

“And what about everyone too weak to protect themselves?”

“Should every insult become a duel?”

“Every private grudge a blood feud?”

“This isn’t a barbarian wasteland.”

“Why must the strong care about the weak?”

Sadi snarled.

“Why do the weak think they deserve things for free?”

“Do they complain to earthquakes?”

“Monsters?”

“Storms?”

“No.”

“They only complain to other humans because humans can understand them.”

“They beg whoever will listen.”

“Then I’ll become something they cannot complain to.”

She raised her whip.

“A natural disaster.”

“Accept me.”

Aura exploded again.

More criminals poured through the damaged walls.

Then—

THUD.

A head hit the floor.

Another.

Then another.

The attackers stopped.

A black cloak moved through the blood mist.

Black hair.

Red eyes.

Dark crimson aura.

A man stepped through the criminals as though they were grass.

The warmongers—

for the first time—

hesitated.
