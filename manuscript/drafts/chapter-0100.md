# Chapter 100: Madam Eight-Legs (6)

> **Fly of Gluttony ‘Beelzebub’ / Awl**  
> **Slot 1:** Incinerate — Cerberus (A+)  
> **Slot 2:** Silent Heel — Mushuhushu (A+)  
> **Slot 3:** Super High-Speed Regeneration — Bog Salamander (A+)

The third jewel beneath Beelzebub's blade shone red.

Inside it—

the Bog Salamander's soul thrashed violently.

That destructive energy transformed into spectral power.

It spread through every blood vessel in Vikir's body.

**Super High-Speed Regeneration.**

Sssssss—

Black steam burst from his muscles.

His body rebuilt itself at an astonishing rate.

A new limb grew from the severed leg.

Organs regenerated.

Bones fused back together.

Torn wounds closed on their own.

The Bog Salamander possessed one of the strongest regenerative abilities in the floodlands.

Even if an entire arm were severed at the shoulder—

it could restore the limb all the way to the fingertips.

Of course, rebuilding so much flesh consumed enormous stamina.

That was still infinitely preferable to permanent mutilation.

And in the middle of battle—

there was no reason to hesitate over the cost.

Within seconds of being reduced to a torn mass—

Vikir stood whole again.

[Skree?]

Madam raised a foreleg and scratched at her head.

She seemed unable to understand what had happened.

Vikir took one cautious step backward.

*If I'd continued the attack earlier, I'd probably be dead.*

He had survived because his instincts told him to retreat the instant his first attack succeeded.

If he had ignored that warning and pushed only a little deeper—

Madam would have crushed his skull.

No amount of Bog Salamander regeneration could restore him from that.

Regeneration required something intact enough to regenerate from.

Meanwhile—

[Skree…?]

Madam had become restless.

Something smelled wrong.

Burning.

Fire.

The scent spiders hated.

She searched for the source.

Then realized—

it was coming from her own rear abdomen.

Crackle—

FWOOSH!

The silk-producing sac Vikir had cut earlier was burning.

Black hellfire flickered inside the wound.

“How does hellfire smell?”

Vikir moved toward Madam's left side.

The earlier strike had carried more than aura.

When Beelzebub cut into Madam—

Vikir had activated another stored ability.

**Incinerate — Cerberus (A+).**

Anything cut or pierced by Beelzebub could be branded with a fatal burn.

The spectral hellfire left behind by Cerberus continued to torment the wound.

[SKREEEEEE!]

Madam dragged her rear against the ground.

The fire did not go out.

It grew.

Fluid leaking from the damaged silk sac kept feeding the flames like fuel spilling from a punctured barrel.

Once the curse caught—

it wanted to burn forever.

Only now did Madam fully understand how troublesome Cerberus's hellfire could be.

SPLASH!

A huge quantity of blood and internal fluid gushed out.

Vikir jumped farther back to avoid it.

The mass of liquid tore the burning material away from Madam's rear.

She had forcibly emptied the wounded sac and surrounding fluid to get rid of the flame.

[SKREE! KRAK—]

Her legs trembled with rage.

Then—

“…?”

Madam did something Vikir had not expected.

She reached one leg into the nest.

Rummaged around.

Pulled out a small round object.

An egg.

Only then did Vikir understand the reason for her endless hunger.

Madam was carrying countless eggs.

She was pregnant.

And now the intruder had consumed enormous amounts of her stamina while leaving little available food.

Crack.

Crunch.

Madam ate the egg without hesitation.

Concentrated nutrition.

Her own offspring—

used as fuel.

“What a cold mother.”

Vikir did not waste another word.

He raised the bow.

TWANG!

An arrow tore through the air.

It buried itself in another eye on Madam's left side.

POP!

The eyeball burst like a raw egg.

Aura spun across the arrowhead—

and the force of the shot carried flame with it.

[SKREEEEEEEE!]

Madam lashed out.

Vikir met the approaching leg with Beelzebub.

CLANG!

The night seemed to split.

This time Madam's right foreleg was severed.

THUD!

It rolled across the ground.

Vikir stepped back from the poisonous blood spraying from the wound.

[Chitter… chitter…]

Madam did not panic.

She simply reached into the cave again.

Pulled out another egg.

Ate it.

Then the severed stump split.

Two new legs grew.

Crack!

Pop!

Madam now had ten legs.

Every severed limb returned doubled.

Cutting blindly would only make the situation worse.

Vikir watched her carefully.

Then nodded.

“So one becomes two.”

Beelzebub protruded from the artery at his wrist.

Six blood-red fangs gleamed.

“Then let's see how many you can carry.”

Dark-red aura erupted from Vikir's entire body.

**Baskerville Sixth Fang — Carnivorous.**

The aura gathered at the point of the sword until it burned almost as brightly as a small sun.

Then descended toward Madam's legs.

CLANG!

CLANG!

Six predatory trajectories crossed one after another.

The two newly regenerated legs were severed.

The stumps immediately responded.

Crack!

Crack-crack!

Four legs grew where two had been.

Vikir attacked again.

Aura streamed farther from Beelzebub.

It felt as though the blood inside his body were being transformed directly into aura.

Every blood vessel burned.

Vikir charged.

The Graduator-level aura rotated at high speed and cut through Madam's exoskeleton.

More legs fell.

More legs grew.

Vikir continued burning mana.

Madam became more crowded with limbs after every exchange.

[SKREEEEEE!]

She struck back with the multiplying legs.

BOOM!

BOOM!

BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!

Vikir's body broke beneath the attacks.

Pain threatened to dissolve thought itself.

His focus did not disappear.

He had endured the Age of Destruction.

A hound trained in that world did not stop because pain became unreasonable.

The Bog Salamander's power rebuilt him again.

Torn flesh closed.

Bones returned.

But regeneration could not solve everything.

Madam's poison had entered his body.

That poison dissolved into the blood and spread everywhere.

Vikir used Graduator aura to burn through his own blood vessels and suppress it as much as possible.

He was buying time.

Nothing more.

He could not last like this.

His vision blurred.

Ahead of him—

Madam looked increasingly pleased.

[SKREE—KRAK!]

She raised two forelegs at once.

A leisurely motion.

As though she intended to crush the staggering Vikir in a single final attack.

Then—

“…?”

Madam staggered.

Her body tilted.

Not exhaustion.

She still possessed enormous stamina.

So why had she lost balance?

The answer came a moment later.

Too many legs.

The repeated regeneration had made the front of her body absurdly heavy.

At the same time—

Cerberus's fire had forced her to empty the silk sac and surrounding internal fluid, reducing weight at the rear.

Her center of gravity had shifted.

Madam hesitated, confused by the unfamiliar imbalance.

Vikir moved.

“…!”

He launched himself forward with a sharp cry.

Poison clouded his mind.

Instinct told him one thing clearly.

*Last chance.*

Madam tried to stabilize herself.

The mass of forelegs pulled her to one side.

Three eyes on her left side were now gone, leaving the blind angle Vikir had deliberately created.

She failed to follow his leap accurately.

A middle leg struck out.

Missed.

Vikir reached the opening beneath her body.

Beelzebub drove into the exposed space.

CLANG!

The aura blade did not pierce.

A hard plate beneath Madam's lower abdomen stopped it.

The armor was strong enough to resist even High Sword Graduator aura.

Beelzebub jammed into a groove.

Madam had been lucky.

Vikir had not.

But—

penetration had never been the plan.

“AAAAAAH!”

Vikir put everything into the hilt.

Pushed.

The armor did not break.

It moved.

That was enough.

Madam was already off-balance.

Heavy front.

Empty rear.

Then Vikir's superhuman strength added force from the side.

Madam tipped.

Beside her—

the summit's foundation consisted of rotten meat and weakened bone.

The ground collapsed.

This time—

fortune changed sides.

[SKREEEE!?]

Madam flailed.

Vikir's remaining aura cut across the weaker joints near the ends of her legs.

Precise.

Almost purely instinctive.

His vision was too blurred for conscious swordsmanship at that level.

New limbs began sprouting.

Too late.

CRASH!

Madam's huge body struck the soft edge of the summit.

Then rolled over it.

For the first time since her birth—

she experienced falling.

Fear appeared unmistakably in the few eyes she had left.

She tried to release webbing from the rear silk sac and attach herself to the cliff.

Nothing came.

Cerberus's fire had already forced her to empty it.

THUD!

THUD!

THUD!

Madam bounced off the rock face as she fell.

Then dropped into open air.

Vikir went with her.

“…!”

At the last moment—

Madam's newly grown limbs reached out.

She refused to fall alone.

One caught the enemy who had pushed her.

Both plunged from the summit toward the ground below.

Madam thrashed wildly.

Vikir prepared his last card.

THUMP!

He kicked away from Madam's chest.

The recoil separated them in midair.

Madam continued falling toward hard ground.

Vikir moved farther to one side—

but he was still falling just as quickly.

“…”

He closed his eyes.

Held his breath.

Not from fear.

Preparation.

Preparation for landing.

The ground rushed upward.

What waited beneath Madam and Vikir was completely different.

Madam had only rock.

Vikir had prepared something else in advance.

A huge swollen mass.

Like an overfilled water balloon.

A cushion of soft material sealed inside resilient skin.

The dead **Bog Salamander**—

bloated with decomposition gas—

waited directly beneath him.
