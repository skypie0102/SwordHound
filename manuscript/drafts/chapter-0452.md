# Chapter 452: Declaration of War (3)

“They will attack Tochka.”

“Because my son is there.”

Hugo’s answer produced a wave of disbelief.

“Tochka has excellent walls.”

“But no sustainable water source.”

“An army once abandoned the place for exactly that reason.”

“It was even called a fortress that makes commanders weep.”

“Why would Leviathan choose it?”

Hugo did not care.

“So?”

No one answered.

The name **Baskerville** carried enough weight on its own.

Hugo looked at the map.

“My son is there.”

“That boy does not waste effort.”

“If he chose Tochka—

there is a reason.”

“And the enemy will have noticed that too.”

Unexpectedly—

support came from Raspane Morgue.

“Hm.”

“My daughter is there as well.”

She sounded almost irritated by the fact.

“That girl has become completely obsessed with one man…”

Martin Luther nodded next.

“Our Saintess is there.”

Damien followed.

“And technically—

our family head is there too.”

Camus Morgue.

Dolores L. Quovadis.

Sinclaire Bourgeois.

All at Tochka.

None had formally reported their exact position.

But the heads of the great houses had their own intelligence.

They already knew about the **Night Walkers**.

The refugee camp.

The concentration of unusual fighters.

Then someone asked:

“Wait.”

“Isn’t Night Hound a Baskerville?”

“I heard he was imprisoned in Nouvelle Vague.”

“No.”

“I heard he died there.”

Hugo answered almost casually.

“He escaped.”

The room went silent.

Then—

mouths opened.

Nouvelle Vague.

The prison from which even a soul supposedly could not escape.

Hugo looked almost proud.

“That boy is clever.”

Not school-ranking proud.

Not exam-score proud.

The pride of a father whose son had escaped the worst prison in the world.

“Either way.”

“My son’s decisions have reasons.”

“I’m going to Tochka.”

Adolf nodded slowly.

“I trust Vikir’s judgment too.”

“If he chose that fortress—

there is probably a plan.”

“But the water problem remains.”

“So Baskerville will send an army?”

“No.”

Hugo shook his head.

“I am going.”

“…Alone?”

“As a father.”

“Not as the head of House Baskerville.”

“Public and private matters should be distinguished.”

Adolf stared.

“…I never knew you cared about your children this much.”

Hugo looked offended.

“Do you want to become my son?”

“Why are you asking how much I love my children?”

“…Never mind.”

Of course—

if the head of Baskerville personally moved—

the family would naturally support him.

But formally—

a father visiting his son was difficult for other factions to oppose.

Then Osiris stepped forward.

“I’ll go with you, Father.”

“My lover is there too.”

Highbro raised a hand.

“Us too.”

Middlebro.

“Same.”

Lowbro.

“Obviously.”

The Morgue side began shifting as well.

Raspane sighed.

“My daughter is reckless enough to be worrying.”

Adolf nodded.

“We should go.”

Highsis, Middlesis, and Lowsis immediately joined the chorus.

“Take us too.”

“If we hide until the war ends—

we’ll never hear the end of it.”

“I’d rather fight than spend the rest of my life being tormented for staying behind.”

Martin Luther folded his hands.

“Our Saintess is there.”

“We should go.”

Damien nodded.

“I need to protect our head.”

The mood of the meeting changed.

Reinforce Tochka.

Relocate headquarters.

Concentrate there.

The idea that had sounded absurd minutes earlier—

was beginning to gain momentum.

Then—

“You can’t enter!”

“There’s an important meeting—!”

A guard shouted outside.

Struggling.

Trying to stop someone.

BANG!

The doors flew open.

Five figures walked in.

Step.

Step.

Step.

Step.

Step.

The room froze.

Some people recognized them immediately.

Others had no idea who they were.

Banshee—

did.

He straightened.

“…The all-stars of Nouvelle Vague.”

**D'Ordume.**

**Souaré.**

**BDISSEM.**

**Flubber.**

And—

at the rear—

**Black Tongue.**

Five of Nouvelle Vague’s most feared wardens.

The recovered witness made the missing transition clear.

Nouvelle Vague was gone.

The volcanic disaster had erased it.

The surviving guards—

once Imperial civil servants stationed at the world’s most notorious prison—

were now effectively homeless.

Souaré’s eyes settled on Hugo.

“Your son had quite a lot to do with that.”

“…My son?”

Hugo looked toward her.

Souaré immediately looked elsewhere.

BDISSEM spoke stiffly.

“We are still—

technically—

Imperial personnel.”

“So we needed somewhere to go.”

“And…”

Her eyes shifted toward the person behind them.

“We are following the current captain.”

Flubber crouched nearby.

D'Ordume and Souaré—

for once—

did not argue.

That silence alone drew attention.

Those two had spent years competing over Nouvelle Vague’s leadership.

Yet now—

both accepted someone else.

Black Tongue stepped forward.

Red eyes.

Different face.

Different presence.

The current leader of the five.

“We are going to Tochka.”

Then he looked around the room.

“So should all of you.”

“Everyone you can move.”

“All of you.”

Several nobles immediately bristled.

“You barge in and issue orders?”

“Do you even understand this meeting?”

“Do you know how many high nobles are here?”

“And Black Tongue…”

“Did you always look like that?”

“You look completely different.”

None of the former wardens cared.

Black Tongue’s gaze went cold.

“…I tried saying it nicely.”

SHING.

He drew **Asmodeus**.

The demonic blade struck the conference table.

CRACK!

The table split.

The broken blade segments reconnected—

lengthening like a whip.

Everyone went silent.

Black Tongue rested the weapon against one shoulder.

Then spoke again.

“If you don’t want to die—

go to Tochka.”
