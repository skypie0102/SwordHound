**Humbert L. Quovadis.**

Both hands gone.

“…Ah.”

GRIT.

How Night Hound had hidden her.

The orphanage.

The moment she learned fear could be confronted rather than obeyed.

*I will not remain his burden.*

Courage.

Resolve.

“Enough.”

Her fist moved.

White.

Brilliant.

Different from before.

“I don’t really understand it myself.”

“Yes.”

“That is natural.”

“And young men and women often discover that without needing anyone to explain it.”
