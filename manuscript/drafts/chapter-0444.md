“PUHA!”

Tudor and Bianca finally crawled onto dry land.

Night had deepened.

The air was freezing.

Wind cut like a blade.

Behind them—

Masyaf loomed over the Red-Death swamp.

“Take my hand.”

“…Yeah.”

Tudor gripped Bianca’s hand.

They ran.

Dead trees rose around them like blackened spines.

Branches cut their faces.

Thorns tore clothing.

Neither cared.

The last of Dolores’s protection was fading.

And behind them—

FLAP.

FLAP.

Something with wings was getting closer.

Then—

THUD.

Tudor dropped to one knee.

Bianca caught him.

“Tudor!”

“Are you all right?”

“…Yeah.”

He was not.

The wound Bianca’s arrow had cut through his side—

was still bleeding badly.

Even a partial hit from Bianca was dangerous.

Bianca hesitated for barely a second.

“Get on.”

Tudor shook his head.

“The demon could reach us any moment.”

“Vikir said not to relax until we crossed the ridge.”

“Does this look like relaxing?”

“Yes.”

Tudor gently pushed her away.

“You go first.”

“I’ll rest.”

“Then follow.”

“Stop talking nonsense.”

Bianca closed the distance again.

Grabbed his collar.

“You know this isn’t cool, right?”

“I’m not doing it to look cool.”

“You know me better than that.”

“And do **you** know me?”

Silence.

Tudor’s voice weakened.

“That night.”

“When my family fell.”

“You saved me.”

“You risked your life.”

Bianca said nothing.

“Now it’s my turn.”

“I can’t go much farther anyway.”

“So let me do one last thing for you.”

The light in Tudor’s eyes was fading.

Blood loss.

Cold.

Exhaustion.

Yet something still burned there.

The final spark of someone who had accepted death.

Bianca’s eyes hardened.

“If you have enough energy to say stupid things—

use it to walk.”

“That night—

I saved you.”

“But you saved me too.”

Tudor looked up.

“Do you know how relieved I was when I reached the end—

and saw you alive?”

“How grateful?”

She yanked him closer.

“So live.”

“However you have to.”

“As long as I’m alive—

you live too.”

“After I die—

do whatever you want.”

Tudor had no answer.

So they walked.

Mana nearly gone.

Bodies torn by traps.

Clothes soaked.

Temperature dropping.

FLAP.

The wings were closer.

“…Bianca.”

Tudor leaned on Gungnir like a cane.

“Thank you.”

“Don’t say ominous things.”

“Just thought I might not get another chance.”

“Seriously—”

Tudor collapsed.

Bianca stopped.

Then quietly sat beside him.

“…You did well.”

“This was enough.”

No response.

Only shallow breathing.

Bianca finally broke.

Tears ran.

She hugged him.

They had failed to reach the agreed ridge.

But they had lured the demon out.

They had fired the black flare.

And from the beginning—

the plan had never promised that the two decoys would survive.

“Thank you.”

“For coming with me.”

She kissed Tudor.

Then—

FLAP.

Closer.

Bianca looked up.

No strength left.

Still—

she raised her bow.

“I’d rather die than give you my body.”

Two red eyes appeared in the darkness.

Like the hound of death from an old legend.

Then—

a familiar voice.

“You still look lively.”

“Good.”

Vikir stepped out.

Bianca’s eyes widened.

“Vikir?”

“Why are you here?”

“The meeting point was over the ridge!”

“I came to pick you up.”

“It involved some risk.”

He raised one hand.

Behind him—

dead monsters.

Werewolf.

Ghoul.

Giant centipede.

Mountain turtle.

Griffin.

Others.

Bianca realized—

they had crossed the entire escape path without encountering a single monster.

Because Vikir had been clearing the route ahead of them.

The plan had treated Tudor and Bianca as expendable decoys.

Vikir—

had not.

“…Vikir.”

He waved it off.

Then looked toward the black flare above Masyaf.

The signal meant one thing.

Roderick Usher—

dead.

Or alive in a condition worse than escape.

Bianca explained.

“My father is alive.”

“But the whole mansion is full of Red Death…”

“I understand.”

“Save your strength.”

Vikir checked Tudor.

Pulse.

Breathing.

Then nodded.

“He’ll live.”

“Potion.”

“Divine treatment.”

“He’ll recover.”

Bianca stared.

“Really?”

“But we haven’t crossed the ridge—”

“You only need to stay still.”

“What does—”

Vikir lifted Tudor onto his back.

Then tucked Bianca under one arm.

“W-wait—!”

He ran.

Even carrying both—

Vikir climbed the steep rock face almost instantly.

Bianca squeezed her eyes shut.

Fast as flight.

And Vikir—

for once—

felt relieved.

*This is where Tudor died before regression.*

*And where Bianca broke.*

In the old future—

the House Usher recovery happened later.

But here.

The same place.

Tudor—

trying to stop a brainwashed Bianca—

was mortally wounded.

Then died covering everyone’s escape.

Bianca failed to recover her family.

And guilt over injuring the childhood friend and lover who had stayed beside her—

destroyed her mind.

This time—

fate changed.

Tudor lived.

Bianca remained herself.

Because at the decisive moment—

Madeline’s hypnosis failed.

Vikir looked at the pink heart-shaped hairpin in Bianca’s hair.

“…Lovegood deserves thanks.”

**Love Shield.**

The artifact Merelini Lovegood had received during the University League.

A heart-shaped hairpin.

Its ability—

block one instance of even extremely powerful mind control or hypnosis.

Under one condition.

The person the wearer truly loved—

had to be nearby.

Lovegood had mailed it to Vikir later—

mixed among the ridiculous mountain of fan gifts.

Vikir kept it.

And before Bianca entered Masyaf—

he gave it to her.

The hairpin to Bianca.

The two flares to Tudor.

Those preparations—

had broken the old fate.

“…First time my face has actually been useful.”

Vikir glanced briefly at his reflection in a puddle.

Then away.

Behind him—

Tudor lay unconscious.

Bianca held his hand tightly.

◆◆◆

At the temporary camp—

Vikir uncovered a leaf-hidden supply cache.

Potion.

Medicine.

Tudor drank.

Then—

“…Huh?”

“Why am I alive?!”

He shot awake.

Bianca immediately burst into tears.

At that moment—

FLAP.

A distant wingbeat.

Vikir climbed the ridge.

Looked toward Masyaf.

[…FIND THEM!]

[…DON’T LET THEM ESCAPE!]

Madeline’s furious voice echoed.

House Usher fighters moved behind her.

Tudor and Bianca went pale.

Vikir drew his blade.

“Get ready.”

“It’s time to return everything.”
