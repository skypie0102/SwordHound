# Chapter 414: End game (6)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/414.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 412 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established terminology controls **Flubber J. Tarbond**, D’Ordume, Souaré, Aiyen, doppelganger leeches, Poseidon, Decarabia, Sadi / Ms. Ouroboros, and Black Tongue. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

**Flubber J. Tarbond.**

Brigadier General.

A massive—

shape-indeterminate mass of living mucus.

Two hollow-looking eyes.

A long split mouth.

And a body whose true volume nobody knew.

Together with BDISSEM—

Flubber supported one of Nouvelle Vague’s most fundamental systems.

A thin coating of its slime covered the entire outer prison—

keeping ten thousand meters of seawater outside.

Now—

it surrounded Vikir and Aiyen with obvious hostility.

Vikir stared.

“I’m surprised.”

“So you can think.”

Aiyen nodded.

“Looks like the Shadowless King of the Black Sea.”

“I didn’t expect it to talk either.”

Even D’Ordume and Souaré seemed slightly uncomfortable with Flubber’s behavior.

“That monster only moves on its own when it thinks Nouvelle Vague is threatened.”

“Maybe it’s attached to the prison.”

“Always cleaning.”

“Repairing.”

“Sealing the outside.”

But Flubber had not descended to help the two Wardens.

Not directly.

It had sensed the abnormality around **Poseidon**.

It wanted to prevent Nouvelle Vague itself from being destroyed.

Why?

Nobody knew.

Attachment?

Instinct?

Something else?

D’Ordume grinned.

“Whatever.”

“Our objective matches.”

Souaré cracked her neck.

“I don’t love working beside that thing.”

“But I’ll take the help.”

Then the three moved together.

BOOM!

D’Ordume attacked.

Souaré defended.

Flubber filled the entire battlefield.

Vikir dodged D’Ordume—

and found slime already covering the place he intended to step.

Aiyen fired.

Souaré raised overlapping layers of earth and ore.

Then D’Ordume attacked again.

And again.

And again.

“…Annoying.”

Aiyen laughed dryly.

“They’re three Wardens.”

“What did you expect?”

Vikir nodded.

This was not simple addition.

Attack.

Defense.

Control.

Each compensated for the others.

Even Vikir struggled to find clean openings.

“…Good thing I handled Black Tongue and BDISSEM first.”

Aiyen looked surprised.

“Black Tongue too?”

“One of the Wardens?”

“Yes.”

“They would have complicated the escape.”

“So I removed them.”

Vikir reached into a leather pouch at his waist.

Then scattered black pearl-like objects.

D’Ordume and Souaré stopped.

“Explosives?”

The black shells cracked.

Tiny creatures crawled out.

The doppelganger-leech eggs—

stolen from Black Tongue.

Vikir cut his palm.

Blood sprayed across them.

The smell triggered hatching.

The young parasites immediately climbed onto D’Ordume and Souaré.

“Damn it!”

“Black Tongue’s parasites!”

“Did that freak betray us?!”

The two Wardens knew exactly what the creatures were.

They clawed at their bodies frantically.

Vikir clicked his tongue.

*So these only work well on people who don’t know what they are.*

Within Nouvelle Vague—

their usefulness would be limited.

Flubber—

meanwhile—

showed no meaningful reaction to them.

Still—

the distraction created time.

That was enough.

Vikir and Aiyen moved closer to Poseidon.

“Die!”

D’Ordume began spinning again.

**Death Roll.**

The murderous vortex returned.

Neither Vikir nor Aiyen tried to stop it.

“Do what we discussed.”

“Got it.”

Aiyen moved behind Poseidon.

Vikir moved to the opposite side—

using the sphere itself as a shield.

BOOM!

D’Ordume’s attack struck Poseidon.

Lava roots burst.

Impact disappeared into the sphere.

FLASH!

Its blue light intensified.

D’Ordume frowned.

“What?”

“They aren’t counterattacking.”

“It feels like they want us to hit that thing.”

“And it’s brighter than—”

“Who cares?!”

Souaré charged.

“The upper floors need us!”

“And if we fail here—

Orca will kill us himself!”

She smashed the ground.

BOOM!

Jagged stone and magma erupted.

Volcanic projectiles tore apart the terrain.

Poseidon shone even more brightly.

Vikir felt its vibration through the ground.

Decarabia shouted inside his mind.

[Human!]

[The mana pattern is changing.]

[Approximately half the required power is accumulated!]

*Fifty percent.*

Vikir’s confidence rose.

He had spent years in Nouvelle Vague.

Poseidon had been collecting ambient energy throughout that entire time.

Once it reached ninety percent—

the remaining volcanic shocks should carry it the rest of the way.

*What happens at one hundred?*

Then—

slither.

Something approached from above.

Vikir looked up.

Flubber smiled down from the stalactites.

It had moved silently across the ceiling.

Aiyen fired.

The arrow vanished directly into Flubber’s body.

Physical force—

simply absorbed.

Flubber slid behind them.

A support position for D’Ordume’s frontal assault.

*Bad.*

*Flubber does not attack through ordinary physical impact.*

*It won’t charge Poseidon.*

*Worse—

it absorbs force.*

If Flubber fully intervened—

it could prevent Poseidon from receiving the very impacts Vikir needed.

He clenched his jaw.

He had underestimated the being he thought mindless.

Aiyen—

however—

still looked relaxed.

“Don’t worry, Brother.”

“They have something to rely on.”

“So do I.”

Vikir remembered her earlier words.

*I wasn’t alone.*

Then—

BOOM!

The ceiling stalactites vanished.

Flubber’s body was slashed apart—

then burned.

The creature screamed and recoiled.

At the same time—

a massive cutting storm struck D’Ordume and Souaré from behind.

BOOM!

All three Wardens were hurled away.

Poseidon’s blue flames surged.

“Who—?!”

D’Ordume lifted his head.

Souaré did too.

A figure walked through the fire.

A mocking laugh.

“Hehehehe.”

“Blinded by rage.”

“Where exactly are you looking?”

A burning whip curled through the smoke.

Black bodysuit.

High heels more than thirty centimeters tall.

Vikir’s eyes widened.

For the first time since entering Nouvelle Vague—

genuine surprise crossed his face.

**Ms. Ouroboros.**

Professor **Sadi**—

had arrived.
