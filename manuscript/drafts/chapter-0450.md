# Chapter 450: Declaration of War (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/450.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 448 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Fresh Fandom indexing confirms **Juskin de Leviathan** and **Hopps de Leviathan**. The source reveals only **First Corpse** and **Second Corpse** here; later canonical true names are deliberately reveal-guarded. Existing project terminology controls Red Death, Poison Humans, Gate of Destruction, Tochka, Night Walkers, and Ten Corpses.

A dark underground corridor.

Water dripped from the ceiling.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Nothing else moved.

Then—

footsteps shattered the silence.

“Huff!”

“Huff!”

“Cough—!”

A young man ran through the passage.

Naked.

Thin.

Heavy shackles around wrists and ankles.

**Juskin de Leviathan.**

Eldest son of House Leviathan.

The clan’s heir.

He stumbled to the bottom of a staircase and pressed himself into a corner.

Several armed guards passed overhead.

Juskin waited until their footsteps faded.

“…How did everything become this?”

He gripped his hair.

One day—

without warning—

the heir of House Leviathan had been seized inside his own home.

Then thrown into the family’s underground prison.

Juskin knew exactly what kind of place it was.

A dungeon for enemies of the clan.

Political opponents.

Dangerous blood relatives who could not be controlled.

And—

a laboratory.

A place for experiments no family would admit existed.

*Thomas got involved in the demon incident.*

*Then there was Principal Winston’s rampage at the Academy.*

*There has to be a connection to our family.*

Thomas’s demonization.

The Hell Tree that grew through Colosseo Academy.

Winston Smith’s rampage.

Then the First Prince quietly burying the whole incident.

Juskin had kept protesting.

Kept asking why House Leviathan would side with that faction after everything they had seen.

The price of common sense—

was imprisonment.

He had been dragged from bed before fully waking.

Inside the dungeon—

he found others.

Leviathan blood relatives.

Retainers.

People who opposed the civil war.

Only then did Juskin understand.

Something had already consumed the family.

Something so deep—

that returning to the old House Leviathan might no longer be possible.

TAP!

Juskin moved as soon as the patrol disappeared.

He sprinted through darkness.

The experiments had battered his body.

But he had survived because he was still a Leviathan elite.

Arrogant.

Privileged.

Difficult.

Yet disciplined.

A man who had never neglected training.

At the end of a hidden passage—

he found a secret door.

Only the head of the clan and the heir were supposed to know it existed.

Hope flickered.

He pushed.

GRRRR—

Heavy brick slid aside.

A staircase descended even farther underground.

Juskin heard another patrol approaching.

He slipped through—

closed the wall behind him—

and descended.

*There’s no escape route down here.*

*But there should be somewhere to rest.*

*I can recover first.*

*Then find another way out.*

That hope lasted only minutes.

At the bottom—

the darkness opened into a hidden chamber.

Jars.

Hundreds of them.

Maybe more.

Everywhere.

And inside each vessel—

a crimson aura crawled like dancing evil spirits.

Juskin stopped.

“…Red Death.”

Impossible.

The family had spent years researching the plague.

The first version caused fever.

Spread from person to person.

Later versions drove victims into violent madness.

But after the scandal—

the research was supposed to have been abandoned.

“Why is this here?”

He approached the jars.

Each one held a person.

Men.

Women.

Curled like fetuses inside red fluid.

Eyes barely open.

Looking at him.

Every expression said the same thing.

*Kill me.*

A maid who used to clean his room.

The old gardener.

A cook who made excellent desserts.

A strict butler.

The instructor who taught him martial arts.

A younger distant relative he saw on holidays.

An elder from a collateral branch.

Familiar faces.

Everywhere.

Juskin’s legs shook.

He moved deeper.

At the far end—

one container was larger than the rest.

Half buried in the floor.

Hiss.

Hiss.

Two enormous snakes coiled around each other inside it.

Every breath they released—

sent more crimson miasma into the chamber.

Juskin stared.

“…Are these the source?”

The Red Death.

An apparently endless supply.

Cold sweat formed.

Then—

HISSSS.

Both snakes raised their heads.

Looked directly at him.

Juskin stepped back.

A voice came from behind.

[You found it.]

He spun around.

A man in black robes stood there.

Juskin’s face changed instantly.

“…Father.”

**Hopps de Leviathan.**

Head of the Leviathan Clan.

Hopps smiled faintly.

[I am not your father, Juskin.]

[I am merely a demon that consumed your father and wears his skin.]

Juskin went pale.

He had suspected something.

But not this.

Not an admission delivered so casually.

He clenched his teeth.

“What happened to the others?”

Hopps’s smile did not change.

[One has already become something like Thomas.]

“Bullshit!”

“Thomas was twisted from the beginning!”

“But he isn’t—”

“He’s the kindest of us!”

“He would never willingly become a demon—!”

Juskin never finished.

SPLASH!

The two snakes exploded from the jar.

One caught his neck.

The other—

his waist.

They dragged him backward.

Juskin disappeared beneath the red fluid before he could scream.

Hopps sighed.

[Ah.]

[What a waste.]

[I had intended to use that body as my next vessel.]

The crimson spirits entered Juskin.

His eyes rolled apart.

Then turned red.

Spots spread across his skin.

Hopps watched.

[It is unfortunate that he must become a Poison Human.]

[Still.]

[I have another replacement body.]

Then—

a new voice came from the staircase.

[How is progress, Second?]

A second black-robed man looked down into the laboratory.

Hopps smiled.

[First.]

[What brings you here?]

[I came to discuss the civil war.]

[And the Imperial succession.]

[Too many eyes are still watching.]

[Send an owl next time.]

[Occasionally meeting in person is worthwhile.]

[It has been a long time since only the two of us could speak like this.]

Hopps looked toward him.

[Only you and I remain.]

[Yes.]

[Because of that damned demon hunter.]

The **Second Corpse**.

The **First Corpse**.

The final two.

[The Third seems to have fallen.]

The First Corpse’s voice lowered.

[Before dying, a faint trace reached my mana path.]

[It seems the Third tried to force the Gate of Destruction open at the end.]

Hopps laughed.

[If death was inevitable, opening it first would have been more useful.]

[Pathetic.]

[This is not amusing.]

[We may not care for one another.]

[But our objective remains the same.]

[Opening the Gate.]

Hopps nodded.

[Then do not worry about the civil war.]

[The mass-production batch is almost complete.]

He gestured toward the rows of jars.

[Once these are ready—

war becomes trivial.]

[The earlier prototype Poison Human?]

[A failure.]

Hopps scoffed.

[The body enhancement worked.]

[So did the induced aggression.]

[But it lacked the most important property.]

[Contagion.]

He turned toward the huge central jar.

[These are the improved version.]

[The real product.]

Two snakes circled each other.

Each seemed to bite toward the other’s tail.

[My magical essence made flesh.]

[The venomous water-serpent **Ouroboros**.]

[An endless cycle.]

[The two generate poison without end.]

[As long as they remain—

Poison Humans can be produced without limit.]

The First Corpse sounded pleased.

[Useful.]

[Extremely.]

[An assault force of maddened plague-carriers.]

[Immense tactical value.]

Then the First Corpse asked:

[Why not test them on the Night Walkers at Tochka?]

Hopps blinked.

[Who?]

[The demon hunter who recently killed the Fourth and Third Corpses is based there.]

[Refugees are gathering around him.]

Hopps considered it.

[Tochka is tall.]

[Defensible.]

[But it has no proper water source.]

[An absurd place to shelter refugees.]

[Yet they are buying food and drinking water in enormous quantities.]

[Preparing for a siege.]

Hopps burst out laughing.

[Idiots.]

[Without water—

the walls become their coffin.]

[Release the Poison Humans and they will barricade themselves until thirst kills them.]

Behind Hopps—

figures covered in red spots gathered.

Rows.

Ranks.

An army.

[The others waited to be attacked.]

[I will not.]

[If I know where the hunters are—

I strike first.]

[Be cautious.]

[There are civilians there.]

[The demon hunter may use them as shields.]

[Human shields?]

Hopps laughed.

[Meaningless.]

[The more humans—

the better.]

[Infect them.]

[Turn every shield into another Poison Human.]

The First Corpse chuckled.

[For someone with so much to protect—

you are an ideal enemy.]

Hopps bared his teeth.

[Gather every unit.]

[One month from today—

we march.]

[We will take the Night Hound’s head.]

He paused.

[No.]

[Perhaps I should make him a Poison Human too.]

[Him—

and everyone under him.]

The First Corpse nodded.

Then—

[One more thing.]

[Do me a favor.]

Hopps turned.

[A favor?]

[From you?]

[What?]

[…Kill someone.]

A sheet of paper landed before Hopps.

A face.

[If he still lives—

this is roughly what he should look like now.]

Hopps frowned.

[Who is this?]

[He looks insignificant.]

[I think so too.]

The First Corpse’s voice tightened.

[Still.]

[Kill him if you find him.]

Hopps studied the portrait.

Then nodded.

The request itself was strange.

The **First Corpse**—

the being called the **Marquis of Discord**—

did not lower himself to ask favors.

Not like this.

And whoever the man in that portrait was—

the reason he mattered remained unknown.
