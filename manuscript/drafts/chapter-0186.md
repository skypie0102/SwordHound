Professor Sadi—

Very late.

Not on campus.

Not near the arena.

At a small alley bar outside the Academy.

Drunk beyond reason.

The owner had finally called the authorities after she damaged property—

and disrupted business.

Professor Banshee Morgue stared at the woman being carried in.

Disgust plain on his face.

“Take the key.”

“Immediately.”

“And open the barrier.”

That finally ended the emergency.

**42 minutes, 13 seconds.**

By the time the wall came down—

and the professors rushed inside—

everything was already over.

The faculty faced another problem.

Explaining why they had arrived so late.

Jurisdiction.

Responsibility.

Two-key safeguards.

A missing professor.

A malfunctioning system.

None of it looked good.

So—

the public version changed.

The near-catastrophe faded.

Vikir's heroism—

grew.

The Academy aggressively promoted the birth of—

its most heroic first-year student.

Interview requests flooded in.

Vikir's answer stayed short.

“It was everyone's effort.”

The thick poison fog had hidden most of the interior from spectators.

But Tudor—

Sancho—

Pigi—

Bianca—

all told the same story.

Vikir had entered.

Protected them.

Fought until the danger was over.

That was enough.

Then came another shock.

Vikir's public combat ability—

**Sword Expert Advanced**.

For an eighteen-year-old commoner—

already remarkable.

Yet still—

on paper—

not enough to explain what he had done against so many Worker Ants and Soldier Ants.

Vikir supplied the explanation himself.

“Read my written midterm paper.”

The written portion contained—

free research.

And an essay.

Vikir had chosen—

**Killer Ant strategies**.

Worker Ant—

mana-field biological compass.

Soldier Ant—

critical point hidden in the skull.

Methods that did not require overwhelming mana.

Methods useful to—

ordinary soldiers.

Mercenaries.

Front-line troops.

Anyone who had to kill monsters without elite magic.

For the Empire—

this was practical military knowledge.

A way to reduce casualties against one of the monster types that harmed humans most often.

The Academy reacted.

Then the Imperial Capital.

Then—

the wider Empire.

“Who is Vikir?”

“The Western Front commander wants to meet him.”

“His Highness the Crown Prince sent a commendation.”

“The director of the Monster Research Institute wants an interview.”

Within Colosseo—

almost everyone knew his name.

**Andromalius.**

**Dantalian.**
