# Chapter 51: The Husband Hunt (6)

> Restarted Chinese-first reconstruction. Primary source: `source/chinese/chapters/051.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 51 is an aligned secondary witness only. Canonical/project terminology controls **Madam Eight-Legs**, **Danger Rating S**, **Ballak Tribe**, **Aiyen**, **Ahun**, **Camus Morgue**, **Staffordshire Baskerville**, **River Styx**, **Beelzebub**, **5th Fang**, and **Mid Sword Graduator**. The isolated Chinese phrase implying “broad daylight” conflicts with the chapter's repeated, explicit nighttime setting and is treated as raw-layer drift; the scene remains night. The source's reference to theories of ten Baskerville sword techniques is not converted into an unsupported “10th Fang.”

Something was watching from the darkness.

A shadow darker, denser, and heavier than the night itself spread across the flooded jungle.

Deeper.

Thicker.

Heavier.

Blacker.

It was *there*.

The creature had unquestionably been drawn by the light and noise of the battle between Ballak, Baskerville, and Morgue.

“M-Madam Eight-Legs is here.”

The Ballak warriors visibly panicked.

These were battle maniacs who seemed not to understand fear.

Seeing *them* react like this was enough for the Morgue and Baskerville warriors to realize how serious the situation had become.

Vikir, however, had some idea what was lurking beyond the darkness.

He remembered countless journeys through the flooded jungles of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain before his regression.

He also remembered something an old native had once told him during one of his long, miserable wanderings.

*The Madam with eight legs.*

A fragment of mountain folklore, told almost like a ghost story.

A nameless horror said to live deep beneath the flooded jungle, among the rotten root hollows where almost no human ever went.

“...Shh. Don't make a sound.”

Vikir covered Camus's mouth and whispered close to her ear.

“That old monster doesn't see well, but its hearing and skin are incredibly sensitive.”

Light and sound.

Those were what drew it.

KRRR... KRAAAK...

An unearthly sound came from beyond the tangled roots.

It resembled the pained groan of an ancient person mixed with the dying cry of someone drowning in a swamp.

RUSTLE... RUSTLE...

Then came the sound of thick, coarse hair scraping against leaves and branches.

Judging by the scale of the noise, the approaching creature was enormous.

Even Adolf Morgue could not identify what kind of monster was hiding in the dark.

“Is it a new species that isn't in the monster compendiums? How could something this enormous remain undiscovered until now?”

His confusion was reasonable.

But Vikir knew just how vast the flooded regions of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain were.

There were places here no imperial explorer had ever reached.

He silently recalled information from the far more complete monster compendiums that would exist after many future revisions.

*Madam Eight-Legs.*

*A nation-destroying monster.*

*And over the next ten years, it will grow even more terrifying.*

**Danger Rating: S.**

A level of danger at which a single individual could destroy an entire nation.

Their chances in a head-on battle were miserable.

Worse, they were inside the creature's own territory, in the flooded jungle at night.

SPLAT... SPLAT... SPLAT...

Something viscous began dripping from above.

It struck leaves, branches, armor, hair, and skin.

Drops fell from so many places at once that it sounded almost like light rain.

Then—

Silence.

Perfect silence.

There had not been so much as an insect chirping in the flooded jungle for some time.

Now Vikir understood why.

Veteran swordsmen of Baskerville.

Experienced mages of Morgue.

Fearless warriors of Ballak.

At that moment, every one of them was reduced to the level of a helpless insect.

No one moved.

No one breathed more loudly than necessary.

All they could do was pray that the enormous nightmare above them would pass without noticing.

Then—

“AAAAAAAH!”

A piercing scream ripped through the silence.

Vikir snapped his head toward Camus.

She blinked at him in horror and immediately crossed her hands in front of her chest.

*Not me.*

Vikir exhaled.

The scream had come from Ahun.

“My head! Madam's venom!”

He clawed frantically at his hair.

Then, acting with surprising speed, he drew a knife and hacked the contaminated section off.

HISSSSSSS—

The severed hair hit the ground and melted.

Avoiding one drop did not save him from what followed.

The scream had drawn the creature's attention.

The darkness shifted toward the Ballak warriors.

Aiyen grabbed Ahun by the back of the neck and ran.

“Idiot! If you weren't the shaman's eldest son, I would have—!”

Ahun could only shrink under her fury.

The Ballak warriors scattered instantly.

Each ran in a different direction through the flooded jungle.

“We're retreating too!” Adolf shouted.

The Morgue and Baskerville forces turned and fled.

But there was one crucial difference between them and Ballak.

The Ballak hunters scattered separately.

Morgue and Baskerville stayed together.

That difference made the creature's choice easy.

KRAK-KRAK-KRAK—

Leaves hard as blades snapped apart.

A vast mass of coarse hair forced its way through the dense foliage and thorns.

Fast.

Far too fast for something so huge.

THUD! CRASH! THOOM!

Heavy impacts sounded throughout the dark.

Swordsmen and mages were struck down by attacks they could not even identify.

“What in the world is that thing!?”

Adolf ran while carrying Camus.

Staffordshire surged forward as well, carrying the injured Baskerville triplets.

Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro had blood at the corners of their mouths after being struck, yet all three kept staring backward at the thing chasing them.

Then—

“This is far enough.”

Vikir stopped.

At this rate, everyone would die.

He turned around.

Camus saw him and screamed.

“V-Vikir! What are you doing!?”

Vikir did not turn back toward her.

He merely raised one hand in farewell.

“I'll buy you time.”

Madam Eight-Legs was not an opponent he could face while pretending to be weak.

And this crisis had become an opportunity.

A perfectly legitimate chance to slip completely outside Hugo's surveillance.

Vikir had thought about this possibility for a long time.

Death.

More precisely, the appearance of death.

Once a hound dispatched into the field was believed dead, the master's observation and control over that hound ended with it.

Vikir had already memorized the theory behind Baskerville's ten sword techniques.

His mana and aura were steadily recovering toward the level of his previous life.

What he needed now was time.

Time somewhere no one was watching him.

This flooded jungle was almost perfect.

He could disappear here, build his strength in secret, and return when the moment was right.

When he was strong enough to stand against Hugo—or at least skilled enough to conceal and control his real strength perfectly—he could go back to Baskerville under very different circumstances.

“No! Vikir! Please come back!”

Camus screamed through her tears.

Vikir remained facing the darkness.

If he returned someday after supposedly sacrificing himself for a daughter of Morgue, Baskerville might even welcome him as a hero.

That would be useful.

He adjusted his stance.

*If I don't use everything I have, I'll die.*

That did not mean he intended to fight Madam Eight-Legs to the death.

From the beginning, Vikir had no intention of meeting an opponent this dangerous in a straightforward battle.

What mattered was selling the performance convincingly and surviving it.

He stood before the approaching monster with the appearance of a doomed sacrifice.

*Everyone will die if this continues.*

*I'll hold it here. Run.*

*Tell the family I died bravely.*

Vikir did not bother saying any of those things aloud.

His actions had already said them for him.

“...Nephew-in-law.”

Adolf bit his lip hard enough to draw blood.

He looked ashamed to run, but Camus was still poisoned and needed to be returned to Morgue as quickly as possible.

“...Young Master.”

Staffordshire, carrying the injured triplets, looked back at Vikir with trembling eyes.

And finally, Camus.

“...”

Her face was covered in tears, snot running from her nose. Her voice had gone hoarse from screaming.

She reached toward Vikir again and again, but Adolf's arms held her securely as he ran.

“Go.”

Vikir drew his sword with his back to them.

A moment later, Adolf shouted in a voice filled with anguish.

“Don't let my nephew-in-law's sacrifice be in vain!”

The surviving Morgue and Baskerville warriors fled deeper into the distance.

Vikir waited until he was certain they were far enough away.

“...”

Then—

RUSTLE-RUSTLE-RUSTLE—

The enormous thing surged through the darkness toward him.

At the same moment, Vikir released everything he had hidden until now.

Mana strengthened by the waters of the River Styx.

Aura refined through the higher principles of Baskerville swordsmanship.

And the rare demonic sword **Beelzebub**, emerging from his wrist and baring its long teeth.

BOOM—!

Aura red as blood and black as a bottomless abyss erupted from Beelzebub's tip.

The power twisted violently through the night.

Then it formed **five enormous Fangs**.

They tore into the approaching darkness.

SKREEEEEE—!

Madam Eight-Legs screamed.

For the first time, the sound carried unmistakable pain.

BOOOOOOM!

A flash like a falling sun exploded across the jungle.

Vikir's full aura forced an artificial dawn into the night.

Adolf and Staffordshire were too focused on escaping to look back.

But Camus and the Baskerville triplets were being carried facing the rear.

Through the exploding light, all four of them saw it clearly.

Vikir's hidden strength.

The unmistakable, overwhelming aura of a **Mid Sword Graduator**.
