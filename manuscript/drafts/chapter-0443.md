# Chapter 443: The Fall of Usher (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/443.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 441 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Existing project terminology controls Tudor, Bianca, Madeline, Roderick Usher, Gungnir, Pigi, Red Death, and House Usher. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

Tudor remembered Sinclaire’s old story.

The Emperor.

The Usher patriarch.

Two trusted generals.

The lesson Tudor thought he understood—

was that House Usher gained access to targets by earning trust long before the assassination.

Reality went farther.

A cold edge moved behind him.

Tudor twisted.

Bianca’s short blade sliced his side.

“Bianca!”

She did not react.

Her pupils were dull.

Empty.

Madeline laughed.

“Everyone raised inside this mansion is conditioned without knowing it.”

“The route through Masyaf itself draws a hypnosis formation.”

“Walking the correct path completes the spell.”

Then she winked.

“And the phrase you just repeated?”

“That is the trigger.”

“Once activated—

outsiders become the first target to eliminate.”

“Damn it.”

Tudor raised Gungnir.

Bianca had already drawn her bow.

BOOM!

An arrow came.

Tudor spun the spear and knocked it aside.

Then another.

Another.

Bianca’s rate of fire was as terrifying as ever.

Maybe worse.

“…You’re faster brainwashed than sober.”

Tudor forced the joke.

Bianca’s face remained blank.

Madeline looked delighted.

“At last.”

“A young body.”

“A fresh body.”

“I’m sick of this rotten shell.”

She tore away the cloth around her abdomen.

Beneath it—

decayed flesh.

An empty, crumbling cavity.

Tudor’s face darkened.

“…The real Madeline has been dead for a long time.”

“Correct.”

Madeline smiled.

“You have no idea how difficult it is to hide the smell.”

“Perfume.”

“Dried flowers.”

“Constant maintenance.”

“But soon—

I won’t need any of it.”

She glanced toward Roderick.

“Poor brother.”

“If only you had given me your body when I asked.”

“Now your daughter will pay for your stubbornness.”

Roderick’s pupils trembled.

Only slightly.

Tudor seized on it.

“Roderick!”

“Bianca is in danger!”

“The demon wants her body!”

“Wake up!”

He dodged another arrow.

Roderick did not move.

The eyes trembled once—

then the Red Death fog swallowed him again.

Madeline laughed.

“Why are you surprised?”

“You saw the poison rising from the swamp.”

The entire mansion was being poisoned through its own moisture.

Vikir had been right.

Inside Masyaf—

they could not win.

The demon had to be lured outside.

But now—

Bianca herself blocked Tudor’s escape.

And Madeline had already obtained the body she wanted.

Why would she follow him?

“Wake up, Bianca!”

Tudor pushed her aside with the shaft of Gungnir.

Then charged Madeline directly.

If Bianca would not awaken—

he would remove the source.

Madeline did not move.

Not even a step.

Bianca raised her bow behind Tudor.

Madeline’s eyes narrowed.

Tudor did not turn around.

Blue aura gathered around Gungnir.

He focused everything forward.

Madeline smiled.

“Trusting your friend?”

“You really believe she won’t shoot?”

“Shut up!”

Tudor continued.

The arrow behind him—

fully drawn.

Madeline laughed.

“Poor fool.”

“Donquixote really does produce idiots.”

“To die by the arrow of the woman you love…”

“How romantic.”

Then—

“Bianca.”

“Shoot.”

The bowstring released.

BOOM!

The arrow carried everything Bianca had.

Mana.

Force.

Precision.

It struck Tudor’s back.

Blood burst.

Madeline laughed.

“Good!”

“Now Usher and Donquixote both—”

Her laughter stopped.

The arrow had gone through Tudor.

But not cleanly.

At the exact instant it arrived—

Tudor shifted his body.

Let it carve only through his side.

A bloody channel—

rather than a killing wound.

The arrow continued.

Still carrying its original momentum.

And now—

Tudor’s blood.

THUD!

It buried itself in Madeline’s forehead.

Through the skull.

Out the back.

“…!”

Madeline froze.

Vision vanished.

Thought broke.

Her body convulsed.

The black blood on the arrowhead—

was not ordinary.

A strange voice echoed through her mind.

*…May you reach a peaceful place.*

A cold body.

A rough hand covering dying eyes.

A scarred face.

Another whisper.

*…What a good person.*

Madeline’s fingers curled.

Her toes twisted.

Her entire body spasmed like an insect dying.

“Ghhhhh—!”

Then—

“RUN!”

Bianca’s eyes cleared.

She grabbed Tudor by the collar.

Pulled him away.

Madeline’s impact had broken the ice wall.

Bianca and Tudor jumped through the window.

At the same time—

Tudor pulled the flare he had already prepared.

The **black** one.

Lit it.

Madeline screamed behind them.

“Impossible!”

“The hypnosis worked!”

“Why did you recover?!”

SPLASH!

Tudor and Bianca hit the swamp.

Red Death swirled over the surface.

The black flare rose.

BOOM!

A black pillar burned into the sky.

Clear enough for Vikir to see from beyond the ridge.

“It worked!”

Tudor and Bianca grabbed each other.

“Great shot!”

Tudor coughed.

Bianca shook her head.

“…Without Pigi’s blood on the arrowhead—

it wouldn’t have.”

Then—

[AAAAAAAGH!]

A scream erupted from the mansion.

Not human.

Not anymore.

The demon had begun showing its real nature.

Tudor and Bianca exchanged one look.

Then dove.

Their mission was not to defeat Madeline inside.

Only lure the demon out.

The bridge was too far.

So they moved beneath the swamp.

Black weeds.

Red ghost-lights above.

Heavy mud below.

Behind them—

windows shattered.

Debris hit the water.

[…WHERE ARE YOU?]

[…WHERE ARE YOU?!]

The demon’s voice sounded directly above the surface.

Tudor and Bianca went deeper.

Held their breath.

Slipped between the weeds.

Moved slowly—

toward the ridge.

Toward Vikir.

Toward the trap waiting outside.
