Into the hollow he had dug earlier.

*So this is the first-stage pressure.*

Meaning—

rain.

“Then rain.”

“Then this temperature.”

Turned.

Sinclaire had buried her face between her knees.

Her breathing—

too quiet.

*Hypothermia.*

No answer.

“Sinclaire.”

Magic Train on Floor 5.
