Supply planning.

Intelligence analysis.

Information collection.

A respectable comrade.

*Try bullying him one more time.*

*I’ll kill you.*

*And your parents too.*

A tiny act.

Almost nothing.

[I suppose the me from that world also thought of you as my only real friend.]

Looked once.

It was war.

Saving a great hero’s wounded arm—

The world prioritized efficiency.

Large gears.

Small gears.

Its role.

Its value.

Pigi—

Alone.

Full of distrust.

Loneliness.

Cynicism.

Hatred of the world.

someone touched his face.

A hand gently closed his eyes.

Wrinkled.

Scarred.

Covered in blood.

Burn marks.

Sword wounds.

Rough beyond recognition.

Very warm.

The one who had shared a dormitory room at the Academy.

The boy who sometimes appeared when Pigi failed an exam.

When he missed his mother.

A person who—

for one small moment—

The only boy who had treated him kindly throughout his student life.

Brief.

Almost trivial.

Warm as the sun.

Only now—

*It’s okay now.*

The pain of being born.

Guilt toward his parents.

Responsibility as their child.

Academic pressure.

Loneliness.

Failure to make friends.

The pain of dying.
