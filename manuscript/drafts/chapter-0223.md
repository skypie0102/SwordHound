[And I will carry out—

“Accepted.”

“Contract.”

[That fast?]

[You really are intelligent.]

[Interesting.]

[My name is **Seere**.]

[Eighth Demon King—

with the human **Camus Morgue**.]

tried to take control.

Then—

[WHAT!?]

[Why aren't you dead!?]

another consciousness—

jammed into part of her body.

*Teacher.*

Snake—

had absorbed the fatal share.

Camus—

was only half dead.

The contract said—

after death.

only the dead half—

answered.

[Impossible!]

[That backlash should have killed any human!]

[Aaaaah!]

[That mage!]

[I should have taken his body instead!]

Camus's eyes sharpened.

“Taken?”

[No.]

[No, no.]

[I meant—

contracted fairly.]

“You were planning to trick me.”

[Absolutely not!]

Camus tried to stand.

Her body—

worked.

Poorly.

Half obeyed one will.

Half—

another.

Balance—

awful.

[Fine.]

[Half a day—

you.]

[Half a day—

me.]

[Yes.]

“What do I get?”

[Excuse me?]

[Without me—

you'd be dead.]

“I'm lending you my body.”

“What do I get?”

[I can find hidden treasure.]

[People too.]

[I am also excellent—

at slaughtering livestock and beasts.]

[Useful for a black mage—

the second ability.

“You can find people?”

Two consciousnesses.

One body.

“Sister.”

“Snake?”

“He helped Camus.”

No Vikir.

No remains.

Nothing.

“…What is this?”

A dead black mage.

Powerful mana.

Powerful resentment.

A high undead—

**Ah'Heman**.

her servant.

passed.

searching.

research.

resurrection.

was furious.

[Dantalian.]

[Definitely Dantalian.]

[That thief sees something well made—

[Always did.]

had entered the Human World—

around the same period.

Camus cared about—

one missing undead.

“Others can be replaced.”

“Rosie cannot.”

[Eighth Corpse Seere.]

[King of Corpses.]

[So you're—

“You.”

“Newspapers?”

“I have no attachment—

to this world.”

[Demons die.]
