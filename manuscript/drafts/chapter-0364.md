## Admission Ceremony — Stage One: Belongings Inspection

There were three stages to the admission ceremony.

Until it actually began—

the prisoners still did not understand the seriousness of what was happening.

A belongings inspection made sense.

And after that—

a health examination and a bath?

Those sounded like unexpected luxuries.

So most of the prisoners were cheerful.

“Who’d be stupid enough to smuggle a weapon into a place like this?”

“I’m not hiding anything.”

“I’ll be fine.”

“I just want a bath already.”

Vikir believed none of it.

*They dragged people all the way to Nouvelle Vague—

and these idiots are still this naive.*

How long would those smiles last?

Soon—

the guards led Vikir and the others into a vast open plaza.

Only then—

did expressions begin to change.

“…What?”

“You said this was a belongings inspection.”

“Then why are we standing on a cliff?”

The sight ahead was horrifying.

A cliff—

sheared nearly flat.

Another cliff—

far away.

And stretched between them—

one enormous, razor-sharp axe blade.

That was not all.

Above the blade bridge—

massive iron demolition balls swung back and forth like pendulums.

“Don’t tell me we have to cross that.”

“That’s an axe blade!”

“What are those iron balls supposed to be?!”

Faces turned white.

Vikir only nodded.

*Of course.*

Nouvelle Vague’s admission ceremony was never going to be ordinary.

The names were mundane.

Belongings inspection.

Health examination.

Bath.

The reality—

was something else entirely.

SHING.

At last—

the guards showed their teeth.

Swords.

Clubs.

Spears.

Three-section staffs.

They jabbed prisoners forward.

Anyone who refused to step onto the blade bridge—

would be killed immediately.

So—

one by one—

the prisoners stepped onto the steel edge.

Barefoot.

Hundreds of meters long.

A single loss of balance—

meant falling into a bottomless abyss.

One prisoner trembled.

“What happens if we fall?”

Garam Nord answered with bureaucratic indifference.

“…Use your imagination.”

The line moved.

The blade cut into their soles with every step.

Once skin split—

body weight deepened the wound.

Cold steel sliced farther.

Deeper.

Until—

“AAAAAAH!”

One prisoner lost footing.

Fell forward.

His body hit the axe edge.

CRUNCH.

Flesh and blood dropped into the darkness below.

The prisoners behind him clenched their toes desperately.

Big toe.

Little toe.

Gripping the blade as tightly as possible.

Advancing only through toe strength.

But that was merely the simplest danger.

WHOOM.

One of the huge iron balls swept past a prisoner’s face.

The balls moved at tremendous speed.

And carried immense weight.

One direct hit—

would launch a body from the bridge like mashed meat.

“Hah!”

“Idiots!”

“There’s an easy way to avoid them!”

One prisoner dropped onto all fours.

Crawling along the blade like a centipede.

His palms and feet were already bleeding—

but his body remained low enough for the swinging balls to pass overhead.

At first—

it looked clever.

Then—

the ball came back.

WHOOM.

The prisoner lowered his head even farther.

“…Huh?”

His right hand suddenly jerked upward by itself.

BANG!

The hand slammed onto the passing iron ball.

“W-what?!”

“A magnet?!”

Exactly.

Every iron ball above the blade bridge carried tremendous magnetic force.

The prisoner had an iron pin embedded inside one finger.

Now—

that tiny metal piece pulled his entire hand toward the ball.

Then—

RIP.

The pin tore through flesh and flew free.

“AAAAAGH!”

The prisoner screamed.

Lost his grip.

Then rolled sideways onto the axe edge.

His body split vertically—

before vanishing into the abyss.

The same thing happened again.

And again.

The swinging balls found metal everywhere.

A hidden blade inside one prisoner’s anus—

was ripped free with horrifying results.

Old shrapnel embedded in scars—

tore back through flesh.

Prosthetic limbs—

were yanked violently away.

Each powerful magnetic swing also shook the narrow bridge.

More prisoners fell.

Those who had not yet stepped onto the blade—

began shaking.

“This isn’t an admission ceremony!”

“This is execution!”

Garam shook his head.

“No.”

“Nouvelle Vague has a separate execution ceremony.”

“And it’s much worse.”

He pointed.

“Besides—

someone is crossing just fine.”

Everyone looked.

On the blade bridge—

walking casually—

was Vikir.

*Good.*

*Beelzebub and Decarabia aren’t affected by the magnetic pull.*

Vikir walked barefoot along the blade.

His calluses—

built through a lifetime of training—

were thick enough that he did not even need mana to withstand the edge.

All he had to do—

was avoid the occasional iron ball.

Soon—

he reached the other cliff.

“…What kind of monster is that?”

“Hell!”

“If that kid can do it—

we can too!”

The sight of Vikir reaching the far side gave the others just enough courage to move.

## Admission Ceremony — Stage Two: Medical Examination

Far fewer prisoners remained after the first stage.

Still—

anyone who had survived the descent to Nouvelle Vague was already exceptional.

So it had not become a total massacre.

Even so—

many survivors were covered in wounds and blood.

“Next.”

Garam spoke.

“Medical examination.”

Nobody believed him anymore.

And—

as expected—

the prisoners were herded into a narrow ravine like laboratory animals.

“What now?”

“Wait—look up!”

“Something’s falling!”

Black spheres rained down from above.

At first—

they looked like rubber balls.

Smaller than an adult fist.

Soft.

Round.

Then they hit the ground.

And uncurled.

Long black bodies.

Thin.

Oval.

Sharp nail-like teeth pushed from the head.

“Leeches!”

Screams broke out.

Thousands of tiny black parasites fell over the prisoners.

They attached themselves—

and began feeding.

But not only on blood.

“Huh?”

“These aren’t just drinking—”

“BONE!”

“They’re eating bone!”

“Skin too!”

Panic spread.

Different leeches consumed different things.

Blood.

Bone.

Skin.

And the guards—

watched.

Recording measurements.

“Number twelve—

bone density normal.”

“Number thirty-six—

blood volume low.”

“Stop that one before it goes too far.”

“Number forty-three—

skin condition acceptable.”

“Work capacity unaffected.”

“Report everything to Lieutenant Colonel **Black Tongue**.”

The so-called health examination—

was simply the leeches testing how much the prisoners’ bodies could endure.

The more aggressively the parasites fed—

the healthier the prey probably was.

Then—

HISSS.

Something strange happened.

The leeches covering one prisoner suddenly went rigid.

Twitched.

Then began dropping away.

“Krrk!”

“Krrk!”

“Krrk!”

The prisoner beneath them—

smiled.

**Sakkuth de Leviathan.**

A man so vile—

even House Leviathan had expelled him.

The parasites stiffened and fell one after another.

Apparently—

his blood or marrow was poisonous enough to kill them.

*Troublesome.*

Vikir quietly let the leeches crawl over him.

He gave them exactly what they wanted—

while watching Sakkuth from a distance.

*Good.*

*The more troublemakers here—

the more useful this place becomes.*

If handled correctly—

someone like Sakkuth could be valuable.

## Admission Ceremony — Stage Three: Bath

The prisoners who crawled out of the leech pit—

collapsed.

Blood loss.

Reduced bone density.

Reduced muscle density.

Most were barely alive.

Only one stage remained.

Bathing.

Nobody looked excited anymore.

The survivors were dragged into a wide circular plaza.

Human skeletons covered the floor.

Bleached bones.

Broken bones.

Bones with countless tiny holes.

One prisoner crouched.

“…What happened to these?”

“Burned?”

“Corroded?”

“What are they going to do to us now?”

Then—

SHHHHHH.

Yellow water erupted from the ceiling.

Boiling.

Stinking of sulfur.

“AAAAAAH!”

The prisoners scattered.

But the scalding sulfur water sprayed evenly across the entire chamber.

There was nowhere to hide.

SSSSSSS.

Skin blistered.

Flesh tore.

The prisoners whose bodies had been weakened by the previous stages collapsed one after another.

Then—

the survivors noticed something.

Corpses.

Cover.

“Here!”

“My umbrella!”

“Shut up!”

“He was my friend!”

“Let go!”

“I found him first!”

They fought over the bodies of the dead.

Dragged them overhead.

Used them as shields against the sulfur rain.

In the struggle—

corpses were ripped into pieces.

One body—

became several umbrellas.

Hours passed.

At last—

the sulfur shower stopped.

Gas-masked guards entered through the thick yellow steam.

Garam looked around.

Then nodded.

“All prisoners disinfected.”

“Floor assignments begin now.”
