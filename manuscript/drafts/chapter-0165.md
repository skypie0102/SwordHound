# Chapter 165: Sin and Punishment (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/165.txt` — `273b653625d710a42fb936579b557299162526e3`. Recovered English Chapter 164 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls Vikir, Dolores L. Quovadis, Hugo Le Baskerville, Pomeranian La Baskerville, Andromalius, Seth Le Baskerville, Beelzebub, Seventh Fang, Dantalian, and the memory Sacks. The damaged execution placard remains unreadable rather than being editorially reconstructed.

Dark-red sky.

Crows circling.

A severed head displayed high on a pole.

Dolores stared.

*Where is this?*

She knew of no battlefield—

no conflict zone—

this barren.

This dead.

And the head—

*Whose is it?*

It had to be connected to the Night Hound.

If she could see the face—

perhaps she could learn something about his identity.

Then—

caw.

A crow landed.

Pecked.

Another descended.

Then dozens.

Black feathers covered the head.

By the time Dolores could see it again—

there was almost no face left.

Rot.

Bone.

The work of beaks.

Nothing recognizable.

*Is this his shame?*

She looked toward the Night Hound.

He stood motionless.

The mask revealed nothing.

Then—

“Reminds me of the past.”

Dry words.

But underneath them—

regret like ash.

A ruined execution notice flapped in the wind.

Most of the name was torn away.

So was the charge.

Only fragments remained.

Dolores learned nothing useful about who the Night Hound was.

Instead—

her curiosity deepened.

And so did something closer to sorrow.

*What kind of life did this man live?*

*How much weight has he carried?*

She remembered treating Red Death patients beside him.

His silence had moved her then too.

What she felt now had grown beyond simple compassion for a suffering neighbor.

Beyond the generalized love expected of a Saintess.

Vikir—

meanwhile—

recognized the world.

His old world.

His abandoned future.

A place he sometimes remembered—

and never wanted to return to.

The timeline where he had been branded a traitor.

Executed.

His head displayed.

A hound betrayed by the master it had served.

*The core revenge is already done.*

The conspiracy behind his execution had led back to Andromalius—

wearing Seth Le Baskerville.

Andromalius was dead.

Its collaborators inside Baskerville had been rooted out and executed.

That root had been cut.

His unresolved feelings toward Hugo—

those remained.

Later.

Caw.

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

Slash!

The landscape split.

The displayed head shattered.

Crows exploded into the sky.

Black feathers scattered.

Then the entire world tore like paper.

Dantalian's voice came from the mist.

[What are you?]

[Who are you!?]

[Why is a landscape from *my homeland* inside your memories!?]

For once—

there was no laughter.

Only confusion.

Vikir did not answer.

[…Fine.]

[I can look for myself.]

The Sack of Fear opened.

It searched.

Then produced an old man.

Vikir recognized him immediately.

*Hugo.*

**Hugo Le Baskerville.**

White hair.

Age spots.

Wrinkles.

Burns.

Scars.

A face hardened by years of war.

Dolores stared.

“Who is that?”

She had met Hugo many times.

Quovadis and Baskerville crossed paths at major Imperial functions.

But this Hugo—

the Hugo of Vikir's prior-life memory—

looked decades harsher than the current man.

War had aged him.

Scarred him.

Stripped away the dignity of peace.

A Baskerville hound should have frozen before its master's presence.

Yet Vikir's old instinctive fear had weakened.

*Maybe seeing him act like an idiot around Pomeranian ruined the effect.*

The present Hugo—

softened by his granddaughter—

had become much harder to regard as an untouchable monster.

He had even stopped shaving his beard lately to suit Pomeranian's preference.

And Dantalian still lacked enough accumulated power to recreate the full strength of the person drawn from memory.

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

Seven teeth formed.

The **Seventh Fang**.

This time—

the hound bit the master.

*Soon.*

*One day, in reality too.*

The false Hugo was torn apart.

Dolores carefully asked:

“If you don't mind…”

“Who was that?”

“Father.”

“…Ah.”

Dolores fell silent.

Her own fear had taken the face of her father.

The Night Hound's fear had done the same.

For an instant—

she saw herself in him.

And hoped—

perhaps—

he might see something of himself in her.

If that could comfort him even slightly.

Dantalian screamed.

Damage dealt to the illusion—

returned to the demon.

[How!?]

[How do you overcome shame and fear so easily!?]

[Inhuman monster!]

Then the final sack began to move.

Love.

The most primal human attachment.

The thing necessary for people to continue living beside one another.

Now—

the Sack of Love would show what Vikir loved most.

Dolores became tense.

*This is ridiculous.*

They were fighting a Demon King.

This was not the time to be curious.

And yet—

*I want to know.*

She forced herself to remain alert.

Whether she was watching for tactical reasons—

or personal ones—

even Dolores could no longer say.

Sssssssss—

the final sack opened.

Dantalian laughed.

[No matter how hard your heart is—]

[love will make you hesitate.]

[There's a reason romance survives every age.]

[Love surpasses—]

He stopped.

Dolores's eyes widened.

The Sack of Love stood completely open.

The face Vikir loved most—

was about to appear.
