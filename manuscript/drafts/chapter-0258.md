# Chapter 258: The Grave of Swords (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/258.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 256 is the verified shifted secondary witness.

“Find out yourself.”

Vikir drew up every reserve of strength.

Beelzebub extended as a blade.

Dense crimson aura spun across its surface.

Solid aura—

the proof of a Sword Master.

[…!]

Cane Corso’s eyes widened slightly.

A red flame had appeared in the darkness.

Curiosity.

That was unmistakable.

The old man rose from the iron throne.

The Grave of Swords was already oppressive.

With Cane Corso standing—

the entire chamber seemed to lose what little air it had.

[You’re an ill-mannered brat.]

[Who is your father—]

“No need for conversation.”

Vikir moved.

Flash.

The Fourth Fang.

Across two lives—

it was the technique Vikir had practiced more than any other.

He could now produce up to the Seventh Fang.

But when he needed to strike without thought—

without wasting concentration or power—

nothing surpassed the Fourth.

The movement had been carved into his body beyond instinct.

Minimal aura.

Perfect physical familiarity.

A brutally efficient ordinary attack.

Cane Corso leaned his head back.

That alone was enough to avoid every strike.

[…That degree of mastery.]

[And at your age.]

His eyes narrowed.

[You are certainly Baskerville.]

[Whose descendant are you?]

[Boston Terrier?]

[Great Dane?]

[No. You don’t resemble either line.]

[Could it be Hugo, that brat?]

Then Cane Corso answered with the same technique.

The Fourth Fang.

Crack!

Space seemed to rupture.

Black aura and crimson aura collided violently.

Clang!

Screech!

The impact shattered the air into blade-like pressure.

Vikir gritted his teeth and continued swinging Beelzebub.

Cane Corso noticed the demonic sword erupting from Vikir’s wrist.

His expression changed again.

[The lost demonic sword Beelzebub.]

[Even Cain and Abel—gifted twins of the old days—wasted three years trying to obtain it and failed.]

[How did a child like you—]

Vikir gave him no answer.

Boom!

Before Cane Corso’s sword could reach his neck—

Vikir pulled the black mask over his face.

**<Picaresque Mask>**

**Kin-Slaying:** +0  
**Human Face, Beast Heart:** ON

His body shrank instantly into the form of a small black dog.

Cane Corso’s sword cut through empty air.

Vikir shot between the old man’s legs—

removed the mask—

returned to human form—

and counterattacked from behind.

[…What is this?]

For the first time—

Cane Corso looked genuinely baffled.

It was not dignified swordsmanship.

It was dogfighting.

Pure survival combat.

No human pride offered.

None expected.

Exactly how Vikir fought monsters.

[Hehehe.]

[It has been a long time since I saw a fight like this.]

Unexpectedly—

Cane Corso seemed to enjoy it.

A blade of aura came for his groin.

He blocked it with the hilt of his greatsword—

then blasted Vikir backward.

“Giving me distance is a mistake.”

While flying back—

Vikir caught the black bow hidden beneath his cloak.

Anubis bared its fangs.

Thunk!

Five killing trajectories converged on Cane Corso.

[…Archery too?]

[I have never met a Sword Master this versatile.]

To Cane Corso—

the attack was still beneath notice.

Black aura expanded in a circular shell.

Every arrow shattered.

At the same instant—

Cane Corso leapt.

His monstrous body rose into the air.

The greatsword came down.

Then—

stopped.

[…?]

Invisible threads had wrapped around him.

Baby Madam sat on Vikir’s shoulder.

Proud.

“Good.”

Vikir stroked her once.

Then launched himself forward.

There.

An opening.

The only chance to bring down the giant before him.

*If I can defeat him—*

*I may see the answer to the next realm.*

And perhaps—

obtain the Wraith Tree too.

Flash!

Seven fangs appeared.

The true Seventh Fang of Baskerville.

Cane Corso’s eyes opened wide.

[The Seventh Fang!?]

[And that swordsmanship—]

[The Lurking Ambush!?]

[How can a brat like you know that!?]

That surprised Vikir too.

The original manuals had been destroyed.

Even Hugo did not know everything hidden in that old swordsmanship.

How did Cane Corso recognize it?

There was no time to ask.

Picaresque.

Anubis.

Baby Madam’s thread.

Every strange tool and fighting method had combined to create one opening.

Vikir took it.

Boom!

The seven fangs tore across Cane Corso’s body.

*Got him.*

The victory came so suddenly—

Vikir felt an instant of emptiness.

Then—

“…!”

Something was wrong.

Aura fragments dispersed.

Steam and wind cleared.

Cane Corso was standing exactly where he had been.

Uninjured.

All seven attacks had struck the heavy armor covering his body.

None had penetrated.

Then—

clamp.

Cane Corso’s huge hand closed around Vikir’s neck.

Black armor covered even his fingers.

Its material was as unknowable as the tower itself.

Vikir slashed at the arm.

Again.

Again.

Nothing.

Even Sword Master aura could not cut it.

Cane Corso looked down at him with interest.

[To enter the higher realm at such a young age.]

[Remarkable.]

[The family has produced a monster.]

Vikir frowned.

The armor covered Cane Corso completely.

No obvious target remained.

Then Cane Corso spoke again.

[Even within the higher realm—]

[there are levels.]

“…!”

Vikir’s eyes sharpened.

At this height—

a single sentence from someone stronger could become a breakthrough.

That was exactly why he had come here.

He had found the clue he wanted.

The only problem—

was that he might die before understanding it.

Cane Corso slammed him toward the floor.

Boom!

Vikir twisted in midair.

He caught himself with one hand.

The skin of his palm tore away—

but his neck remained intact.

Cane Corso spoke calmly.

[Experts are divided into Low, Mid, and High.]

[Graduators are the same.]

[Why should Sword Masters be different?]

“…!”

Cane Corso smiled faintly.

[You are only a fledgling who has just stepped into the higher realm.]

[Your face tells me exactly what you’re thinking.]

[I thought the same thing when I first entered this tower.]

[Though I was much older than you are now.]

He studied Vikir.

[Perhaps.]

[At your age—perhaps you might actually reach the last page of that sword book.]

“?”

Vikir frowned.

Cane Corso smiled.

Far too brightly.

[Very well.]

[Then I will show you.]

[Consider it guidance for the next master of this tower.]

Black aura began pouring from his body.

Rumble—

The entire Grave of Swords shook.

Vikir’s instincts screamed at him.

**Run.**

Cane Corso lifted the heavy greatsword.

Then—

brought it straight down.

A black bolt of lightning.

One simple movement split into multiple trajectories.

They rushed toward Vikir.

And Vikir saw them.

“…Nine?”

Nine fangs.

The ultimate version of the hidden Baskerville swordsmanship Vikir had believed only he possessed.

Nine teeth—

tearing the world apart.
