*I don’t know what it is.*

*But bring it back, child.*

*…*

*I’d go myself—

but I can’t leave while things are tense with Morgue.*

*If necessary—

I’ll lend you the entire knight order.*

Hugo had been investigating the newly installed Morgue leadership when he gave Vikir the Wraith Tree mission.

Along with it—

he had temporarily entrusted Vikir with House Baskerville’s complete military authority.

The authority lasted only half a day after activation.

But within that window—

Vikir could summon and command all seven knight orders.

Including the Seven Counts.

For that half-day—

Vikir effectively became Baskerville’s military commander.

Osiris bowed.

“…Your orders.”

The six Counts behind him lowered their heads as well.

Vikir answered simply.

“Bite it to death.”

The hunter released the hounds.

The shadows behind him exploded forward.

Their prey—

Amdusias.

Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro attacked first.

The **Three Swords**.

Three blades whose power multiplied when used together.

The brothers poured aura into them—

and struck.

CLANG!

Amdusias barely reacted.

[Hmph.]

[That tickles—]

“Children.”

“Step back.”

“Let’s see if **this** still tickles.”

Two huge figures leapt past the triplets.

Boston Terrier Le Baskerville.

Commander of the Pit Bull Knights.

Great Dane Le Baskerville.

Commander of the Mastiff Knights.

The two Counts—

famous for hating each other—

now shared the same prey.

BOOM!

Their slashes were nothing like the triplets’.

Six dense fangs each.

Twelve in total.

They pierced Amdusias’s hide—

then tore through it.

[AAAAARGH!]

Amdusias screamed.

The remaining Counts joined.

Isabella Le Baskerville of the Doberman Knights.

German Le Baskerville of the Shepherd Knights.

Metzgerhund Le Baskerville of the Rottweiler Knights.

Cu-Chulainn Le Baskerville of the Wolfhound Knights.

Cane Corso’s seat remained absent—

the old Count still guarding the Grave of Swords.

Boston Terrier chuckled.

“Heh.”

“Feels like yesterday we were executing Seth.”

Great Dane and the others ignored him.

Every Count led a hundred Graduators.

And every unit attacked Amdusias.

Vikir issued orders one after another.

“Break the horn first.”

“Then the neck.”

“Then the hind legs.”

“Then the lungs and liver.”

Osiris.

The Counts.

Every hound—

obeyed.

Only now did the surrounding crowd understand.

“Vikir is Baskerville?”

“I’ve never heard of someone like him!”

“No—

wait.”

“I have!”

“The prodigy who appeared in House Baskerville!”

“Wasn’t there a record of him saving noble daughters and dying heroically at fifteen?”

“The one the Emperor recognized?”

“I heard there’s even a golden statue of him.”

“I thought that story was fake.”

“Why would a family as closed as Baskerville need to invent something like that?”

“So he was real…”

“And we were going to school with him…”

The whispers spread.

Vikir ignored them.

He pointed at Amdusias.

“Your sons.”

“Your daughters.”

“Your students.”

“This demon nearly dragged them all into hell.”

That woke everyone up.

Professor Banshee moved first.

“Professors!”

“Protect the students!”

Six magic circles formed.

Earth.

Fire.

Wind.

Water.

Iron.

Wood.

Magic wrapped around Amdusias.

Other professors joined.

Then the parents.

Cervantes grinned bitterly.

“I lost focus for a moment.”

Roderick’s aura sharpened.

“You threatened my daughter.”

“You’ll pay for that.”

BOOM!

A storm of attacks fell on Amdusias.

Even a demon specialized in one-on-one combat—

could not ignore numbers like this.

Especially after losing the Hell Tree—

his greatest weapon and source of power.

[…!]

Still—

Amdusias stood.

Nineteen holes had been punched through his body.

His horn was broken.

His flesh was collapsing.

Yet all four hooves remained on the ground.

He aimed the broken horn at Vikir.

[The Great Work must not fail.]

[You must die here.]

The conviction in his voice—

was every bit as hard as Vikir’s own.

Even if his body ruptured.

Even if his soul tore apart.

Amdusias would stand again.

He charged.

BOOM!

Straight toward Vikir.

Aura and magic hammered him from every direction.

He kept moving.

A suicide attack.

A final strike.

“Vikir!”

“Move!”

Camus launched black fire.

Dolores poured divine power into Vikir.

But Vikir did not retreat.

He watched.

Waited.

Searched for the single flaw—

inside Amdusias’s absolute conviction.

Then—

BOOM!

Something enormous dropped from above.

A massive vault.

Amdusias slammed into it.

[…A safe?]

The metal structure was so absurdly durable—

even his charge could not destroy it outright.

He recognized it.

[Belial’s…?]

Amdusias turned.

Damien stood nearby.

And beside him—

Sinclaire.

Expressionless.

One hand rested on her hat.

She chanted.

The vault melted.

Then transformed.

A rabbit holding a pocket watch.

Made from adamantium—

and orichalcon.

The rabbit swung the watch.

BOOM!

Amdusias’s leg broke.

Camus’s black lightning crashed down immediately afterward.

[AAAAAH!]

The heroes’ attacks intensified.

Amdusias finally began to sink under the combined assault.

Then—

STEP.

Vikir moved forward.

Dolores’s blessing shone across his hands.

He stroked Beelzebub once.

Crimson aura erupted.

Then stretched into a massive greatsword.

“…Demon.”

Layers of aura gathered at the blade tip.

BOOM.

A black sun rose over the Academy.

Eight fangs.

Compressed into a sphere.

Vikir’s fully realized Baskerville Eighth Form—

now wielded from the middle stage of Sword Master.

“Die.”

No hesitation.

Not at the beginning.

Not at the end.

Demons must die.

FLASH!

The black sun fell.

Amdusias’s disintegrating body was pulled into its center.

Crushed.

Ground down.

Erased.

[…!]

No final speech.

No death cry.

Only annihilation.

The Fifth Corpse—

Amdusias, the Unicorn of the Abyss—

was destroyed.
