Even if each person threw only one stone—

the number was staggering.

Refugees packed Tochka’s walls.

And Tochka stood on rocky ground.

Ammunition—

was everywhere.

Pebbles.

Fist-sized stones.

Broken masonry.

The anger of people who had been ground down by cruel fate—

people exhausted by hardship—

people numb from accumulated despair—

finally had somewhere to go.

THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD!

The rain became a bombardment.

CRACK!

CRUNCH!

Poison Humans climbing the walls lost fingers.

Teeth.

Eyes.

Balance.

Then fell.

The effect resembled Andrealphus’s old hailstorm.

For the first time—

the enemy’s full assault hesitated.

“NOW!”

Sade saw it.

So did Orca.

“PRESS THEM FROM BOTH SIDES!”

The two ancient commanders moved.

Orca—

from the defensive side.

Sade—

from the counterattack side.

Their last remaining organized troops squeezed the stalled enemy.

Vikir drove his own detachment directly into the center.

The battle—

became more brutal than any he remembered.

Before regression.

After regression.

This was among the worst.

SLASH!

Vikir severed the neck of a gigantic Poison Human.

*Use the momentum.*

*Take the demon’s head now.*

That was the only way to end the war.

The six Counts and their knights followed him through a river of enemies.

Hounds pushed into the deepest section of the front.

Heads flew.

Blood sprayed.

BOOM!

A giant palm tore open the ground.

Vikir evaded sideways.

SLASH!

He opened the attacker’s chest.

Black blood exploded.

Then—

“…!”

Cold touched the back of his neck.

A familiar battlefield instinct.

Enemy—

behind.

Normally—

one step sideways solved it.

But before Vikir moved—

THUD.

The killing intent vanished.

The attacker behind him—

died.

Vikir tried to look back.

Could not.

More enemies came from the front.

CLANG!

SLASH!

CRACK!

Beelzebub moved constantly.

And the strange protection continued.

Every enemy approaching Vikir’s blind side—

fell.

Again.

Again.

Again.

*Someone is supporting my back.*

Between attacks—

Vikir glanced behind him.

Dust.

Blood.

Flying stone.

Falling bodies.

Too much chaos.

He could not identify the person.

[Hmm.]

[Too fast even for me to see clearly.]

Decarabia sounded puzzled.

Still—

one fact was obvious.

Whoever stood behind Vikir—

was perfect.

No mistakes.

No wasted movement.

*Camus?*

*Aiyen?*

*Dolores?*

*Sinclaire?*

*Kirko?*

No.

All five were elsewhere.

The Counts and knights—

had fallen behind as fatigue accumulated.

*Then I trust them for now.*

Vikir stopped looking back.

And focused forward.

BOOM!

A grotesquely huge Poison Human appeared.

Eight fangs formed.

Then—

CLANG!

Vikir’s hand stopped.

The monster—

had caught his blade.

Vikir recognized it immediately.

“…Juskin.”

**Juskin de Leviathan.**

The eldest son of House Leviathan.

Once—

a celebrated prodigy.

A man expected to become one of the Empire’s pillars.

Now—

a deformed Poison Human.

He roared.

Pushed against Beelzebub with monstrous strength.

Even after Vikir’s slash opened his body—

Juskin regenerated.

*That size.*

*That healing.*

*Annoying.*

Vikir prepared to add force.

Then—

BOOM!

Eight more trajectories struck.

Exactly along the line Vikir had already created.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

Another Black Sun.

Juskin’s giant body exploded apart.

Blood.

Flesh.

Bone.

All scattered.

And for the first time—

Vikir saw the person protecting his back.

Black coat in the blood wind.

Eyes—

like looking into a mirror.

An unshaved beard from days of battle.

The supreme authority of the Iron-Blooded Sword House.

Master of all its hounds.

**Hugo Le Baskerville.**

Father and son stood facing one another.

Eight fangs—

on both sides.

“….”

“….”

Neither spoke.

Hugo broke the silence first.

SHING.

He flicked blood from a famous sword—

**Valmont**—

recovered through Bourgeois channels.

Then said—

quietly.

“You’ve grown.”

A pause.

“I gave you nothing.”

Vikir froze.

For once—

no answer came.

“….”

Hugo seemed about to say more.

Then stopped.

Turned away.

BOOM!

His Eighth Form split the battlefield.

Vikir remembered where they were.

No time.

He moved beside Hugo.

Back to back.

Where Vikir’s sword passed—

Hugo stabbed through the remaining opening.

Where Hugo cut—

Vikir completed the kill.

On a battlefield—

someone you could trust behind you—

was priceless.

Between father and son—

something else moved too.

Blood.

History.

Silence.

Awkward.

Unresolved.

Then—

[HAHAHAHA!]

Vikir recognized it.

Flauros.

The origin point of this nightmare.

The demon waved toward the battlefield.

[Struggle all you want!]

[My poison is infinite!]

Black tent stretched over the top.

Vikir recognized that too.

A mobile poison-recharge station.

The same mechanism he had destroyed near the water source.

To refill.

To mutate.

To return stronger.

[As long as this exists—

my Poison Humans multiply forever!]

[My poison can even raise corpses!]

Then—

BOOOOOOM!

Fire swallowed it.

In real time.

“…?”

now?

[W-WHAT!?]

[WHY IS MY OUROBOROS—!?]

The Reaper.

House Leviathan’s third son.

**Granola de Leviathan.**

In front of a shattered vessel.

*Sometimes I wonder whether I’m living the right way.*

[NO!]

[THAT BASTARD—]

[HE BETRAYED ME!?]

had already reached his back.
