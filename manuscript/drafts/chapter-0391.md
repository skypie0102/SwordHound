# Chapter 391: The Worst Torture (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/391.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 389 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Sakkuth de Leviathan, Kirko Grimm, Lieutenant Colonel Bastille, D'Ordume D'Orcdile, Souaré, Orca, Flubber J Tarbond, BDISSEM, Black Tongue, Red Death, Level Ten, and Nouvelle Vague. Guard-rank wording follows already accepted canonical forms where the local raw drifts.

BOOM!

Stone shattered.

The entire labor zone trembled.

At the center of the guards’ encirclement—

a huge man roared.

**Sakkuth de Leviathan.**

The instigator of the Level Eight riot.

The guards shouted.

“Sakkuth!”

“How long has it even been since you came out of solitary?!”

“This is the second time!”

“You want to go back in?!”

“Keep this up and we’ll make the rest of your life hell!”

Sakkuth did not care.

Swords.

Axes.

Clubs.

None of them quieted him.

“The Queen abandoned me!”

“I failed the command she gave me!”

“I failed to bring out **that person**!”

“That’s why she abandoned me!”

“She hasn’t sent even one clue about my escape!”

“That proves it!”

“AAAAAH!”

“I’m done!”

“Let me out!”

“Or kill me!”

He was visibly deranged.

Saliva hung from his mouth.

His bloodshot eyes looked ready to burst.

BDISSEM chains rattled around his wrists.

Every movement reopened wounds.

Dark blood scattered.

The guards recoiled.

“Don’t touch it!”

“Plague blood!”

“Red Death—”

“or something like it!”

“I heard a disease from that man nearly destroyed the western-jungle natives!”

“Even the admission sulfur bath couldn’t clear whatever he’s carrying!”

“Stay away!”

“Then what are we supposed to do?”

“You grab him!”

“He came from Leviathan!”

“They couldn’t control him either!”

Sakkuth bit his tongue.

Then spat blood.

A red mist spread.

The encirclement staggered backward.

In a sealed fortress like Nouvelle Vague—

epidemic disease was a nightmare greater than ordinary violence.

Lieutenant Colonel Bastille frowned.

“In terms of raw strength—

he isn’t the real problem.”

“The plague is.”

An adjutant leaned closer.

“Should we call one of the senior wardens?”

“How?”

“D'Ordume and Souaré are on the surface.”

“Then Flubber J Tarbond—”

“We already sent people.”

“They haven’t come back.”

“Do not feed more men into that problem.”

“BDISSEM?”

“She is too important to Nouvelle Vague’s security infrastructure.”

“If Orca returned and learned we dragged her into a scene like this—

he’d explode.”

That left one obvious name.

The adjutant hesitated before saying it.

“…Black Tongue?”

Bastille’s expression hardened instantly.

“No.”

“But aside from the others—

isn’t Black Tongue the one person here who could definitely suppress Sakkuth?”

“That lunatic?”

“To catch one Level Eight prisoner?”

“Absolutely not.”

“That would be worse than the riot.”

Bastille was usually balanced.

Strength.

Temperament.

Judgment.

Moderate in everything.

Seeing him this uncomfortable said enough.

“Damn it.”

“This prisoner influx is the largest we’ve ever had.”

“Orca is gone.”

“D'Ordume and Souaré are both on the surface.”

“That has never happened before.”

“What is going on up there lately?”

Three central powers of Nouvelle Vague—

gone at once.

Small disturbances had multiplied ever since.

Then—

CRASH!

Sakkuth broke through the line.

Blood spraying—

he seized one junior guard who failed to move quickly enough.

Kirko Grimm.

His hand closed around her throat.

“Come closer.”

“I break her neck.”

Kirko’s face changed twice.

First—

at Sakkuth’s unexpected strength.

Then—

at the smell of blood covering him.

One mistake could mean a broken neck.

Or contact with the disease everyone feared.

“A hostage?”

“What do you want?!”

“What are you trying to do?!”

Sakkuth laughed.

“Want?”

“Nothing.”

“My life is already ruined.”

“I just want to watch you panic.”

“Oh.”

“And Level Ten construction stops for a few months.”

“I’m going to turn this place into a bloodbath.”

He spat on the dirt.

Sssssss—

The ground hissed and began to erode.

Bastille narrowed his eyes.

“A hostage now?”

“You really are using every trick you have.”

“There should be a hostage.”

Sakkuth grinned.

“Fits the mood.”

“If you harm her—

you will not die easily.”

“Try me.”

“I’ve already been abandoned.”

Then—

“Lieutenant Colonel!”

Kirko forced the words out.

“I’m fine!”

“Do not negotiate with a prisoner!”

“Suppress him by force—”

Sakkuth slammed her into the floor.

Then hauled her back up.

“Ghk!”

Blood ran from Kirko’s nose and mouth.

She glared.

Sakkuth planted a foot against her face.

“You misunderstand something.”

“They aren’t staying back because they care about **you**.”

“You’re a disposable junior guard.”

Kirko reached toward her sword.

Sakkuth trapped the blade with the chain between his cuffs.

Then forced it against her own neck.

Even with aura—

Kirko could not fully overcome his strength.

Sakkuth leaned closer.

“Keep moving.”

“I spit in your face.”

“….”

The threat of plague stopped her.

Then he laughed again.

“Maybe you learned your place early.”

“Child born after a prisoner raped a guard.”

“Garbage born down here.”

Kirko’s jaw tightened.

Rage moved through her.

But strength—

and fear of contamination—

left her with almost no options.

Bastille watched.

His face became bitter.

“…There is no choice.”

One life—

against the group.

Organizations survived because somebody was sacrificed.

A junior guard was cheap compared with a wider outbreak.

Bastille waited for Sakkuth’s attention to remain fixed on Kirko.

Then began signaling the senior guards.

*Prepare.*

*We attack at once.*

But—

the order stopped halfway.

Step.

Step.

Step.

Someone walked through the guard line.

No panic.

No rush.

As though taking an ordinary stroll.

Garam Nord.

Vikir.

His voice was as flat as ever.

“Your filthy blood cannot be allowed to stop the construction.”

Everyone looked at him.

“…?”

Vikir paused.

*Too direct.*

He adjusted.

“Release my colleague.”

“Then we can talk.”

**Garam Nord.**

Age twenty-one.

Second Lieutenant.

Junior Nouvelle Vague guard.

Standing calmly in front of a Level Eight hostage-taker.
