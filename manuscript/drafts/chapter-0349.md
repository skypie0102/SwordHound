**<Basement Floor 11 — River of Flow>**

Countless monster corpses floated on the boiling river.

Among them—

the largest Daylily of Blood Tree raised its massive head.

Fangs opened.

Then—

BOOM!

Eight enormous crimson strikes descended.

Crack!

The Daylily’s head split apart.

Fluid and flesh rained into the water.

Seven huge fangs.

And one eighth fang—

slightly smaller.

Around them—

a vast aura took the shape of a hunting hound.

The sheer presence of it made the nearby river boil and steam.

At the center of the corpse field—

an old man sat with his eyes closed.

Wrinkles covered his face.

Moss clung to him.

His posture—

still straight as a sharpened blade.

Vikir.

He opened his eyes.

Ran one hand through white hair and a long beard.

“…Time to leave.”

Time had flowed like a river.

Vikir no longer knew exactly how many years had passed.

He knew only one thing.

Every stat had finally reached 798.

Crack!

The **Scale of Forced Equality** shattered.

Its purpose fulfilled.

“That’s enough.”

Vikir stepped down from the last dead Daylily.

He had hunted the entire colony.

Golden candy filled his hands.

One by one—

he converted the rewards into Level candy.

Then—

a familiar sound behind him.

[Hack… hack…]

Vikir turned.

Baby Madam—

no longer really a baby—

had grown large enough that he needed to look upward.

Decarabia peeked through Vikir’s white beard.

[Time really flies.]

[Years disappear quickly, don’t they?]

Vikir had remained in the River of Flow long after killing Tudor, Sancho, Pigi, Bianca, and the Ballak warriors.

Then—

[Are you still not leaving?]

A familiar voice floated above.

The fairy.

It looked utterly exhausted.

[Seriously.]

[What kind of shameless bastard are you?]

Vikir had ignored repeated orders to move on.

He had hunted creatures the fairy had told him not to hunt.

Stayed on the floor.

Farmed rewards.

Refused to leave.

The fairy rubbed its head.

[You’ve eaten enough, haven’t you?]

[Can you please go now?]

Vikir finally answered.

“Yes.”

“I think it’s time.”

[…!]

The fairy’s eyes widened.

For years—

every time it told Vikir to leave—

he had ignored it.

Today—

he responded.

[Good!]

[Very good!]

[When are you leaving?]

Vikir waved.

“That depends on you.”

“Come closer.”

“We should talk.”

[Talk?]

The fairy immediately looked suspicious.

But it desperately wanted Vikir gone.

So—

very slowly—

it approached.

Still keeping what it believed was a safe distance.

That distance meant nothing anymore.

Flash!

Vikir’s hand closed around its neck.

The fairy did not react in time.

“I only asked to talk.”

“Why are you so nervous?”

[W-wha—]

Vikir squeezed its cheeks.

Forced its mouth open.

Then poured in a dark-red liquid from a leather pouch.

Gulp.

Gulp.

Gulp.

The fairy swallowed.

Then—

[AAAAAAAGH!?]

Black smoke erupted from its mouth.

Its body convulsed.

As though its insides were dissolving.

“…Hm.”

Vikir pinned the writhing creature beneath one foot.

Fairies—

normally—

were effectively indestructible tower constructs.

Products of demonic engineering.

Things ordinary challengers could not truly kill.

Yet this fairy—

was dying.

Slowly.

Certainly.

And painfully.

[W-why…?]

Even the fairy seemed unable to understand.

Vikir’s voice stayed dry.

“Maybe this is revenge.”

“For all the humans who suffered in this tower.”

The fairy no longer heard him.

Fwoosh—

Its body disintegrated.

Gone.

“…Effective.”

Vikir looked at the leather pouch.

Inside—

Pigi’s blood.

It had not evaporated.

Had not spoiled.

Had remained strangely stable over the long years Vikir spent on the floor.

Not normal blood.

Vikir had collected it after “killing” Pigi—

when he noticed that black fluid from the small wound.

*Pigi.*

*Was your blood always black?*

*Yeah.*

*Always.*

*And before you ask—*

*I’m not becoming a Majin.*

*Doctors said I just had too much iron in my blood.*

*Although it does seem darker since entering the Hell Tree…*

Now—

Vikir looked at the place where the fairy had disappeared.

*If I’m right…*

◆◆◆

“…you may be the key to destroying Amdusias’s abyss.”

The younger Vikir stood before everyone.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Aheul.

The Ballak warriors.

Pigi pointed at himself.

“M-me?”

He looked overwhelmed.

Vikir nodded.

“I had a lot of time to think about it in the River of Flow.”

“Your blood has unusual properties.”

Pigi’s blood reacted to demons.

Fairies.

Tower monsters.

Possibly other demonic constructs.

By itself—

the reaction was limited.

But on a blade—

or mixed with aura—

the effect became far stronger.

“I intend to use it.”

“To clear this tower in one push.”

Vikir had spent decades considering this possibility.

Before regression—

the Hell Tree had been cleared later.

By then—

many heroes were already gone.

And only one person had ultimately conquered it.

**Camus Morgue.**

A genius among geniuses.

*Back then—*

*we didn’t even understand that the tower itself was created by a demon.*

Camus had still cleared it.

That alone proved how extraordinary she was.

But in this timeline—

Camus was not inside this tower.

Someone else had to finish it.

Vikir looked at Pigi.

*And before regression—*

*Pigi never even stayed at the Academy long enough to reach this stage.*

Perhaps bullying had not been the only reason he eventually left school.

Maybe—

somewhere inside himself—

Pigi had already sensed that his blood was different.

That something about him did not fit.

Vikir did not know.

And would not pretend to know.

What he **did** know—

was that Pigi had become a variable capable of distorting the tower’s expected causality.

Vikir put a hand on his shoulder.

“The reason you woke first—

is probably because there was something only you could do.”

“…Something only I can do.”

Pigi looked at his hands.

Then—

“Vikir.”

Tudor stepped forward.

He held out a closed fist.

Opened it.

A golden candy rested on his palm.

“Take this.”

Vikir frowned.

“Why?”

Bianca answered instead.

“Because it makes more sense.”

Sancho held one out too.

Then Pigi.

Then Aheul.

Then the rest of Ballak.

“These are our mission-clear rewards.”

“You’ll use them better than we will.”

Pigi swallowed.

“I can’t do much besides give you blood.”

“Hunting Chief.”

Aheul held hers out.

“Please.”

Everyone looked at him the same way.

Vikir was silent.

Then nodded.

“I won’t waste them.”

Golden candy accumulated in his hands.

Combined with everything he had already farmed—

the total was enormous.

Tudor’s eyes lit up.

“So now you eat all of those at once—

and go straight for the tower core?”

“No.”

Vikir shook his head.

“There’s something I have to do first.”

He reached into his clothing.

Pulled out an item.

Everyone stared.

**<Return Scroll> / Scroll / Rank: ?**

**A one-use scroll that returns the user to the Tutorial Zone.**

**It contains the answer to the question: ‘Lord, where are you going?’**

**※ Level resets upon use.**

The Return Scroll.

The one-time item Vikir had preserved—

all the way from the beginning.
