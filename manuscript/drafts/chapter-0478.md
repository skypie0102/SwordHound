The Nouvelle Vague volcano had erupted.

And the trigger—

was Poseidon.

One small ball Vikir had set in motion—

had become an enormous butterfly effect.

The Great Flood swept across the world.

For the next one hundred fifty days—

torrential rain would swallow almost every land below the highest plateaus.

Wildfires raging across the Empire.

Monster hordes that had flourished in the chaos.

The endless drought.

All of it—

washed away.

Naturally—

everyone in Tochka found salvation.

The flood never reached the high plateau.

Hard rock and fine sand beneath the fortress drained water quickly.

The fortress itself was strong enough to endure the storm.

And its one fatal weakness—

the lack of drinking water—

disappeared beneath endless rain.

Under ordinary circumstances—

this would have been remembered as an unprecedented natural disaster.

A Great Flood.

But the Gate of Destruction had already brought worse.

Wildfire.

Drought.

Plague.

Monster hordes.

The Long Rain of Terror.

Against those disasters—

the flood became a miracle.

Destruction—

canceled by another destruction.

◆◆◆

Deep drainage trenches were dug around the tents inside Tochka.

Huge barrels stood outside every shelter.

Refugees filled them with rainwater—

then drank until they could drink no more.

After days without a single proper mouthful—

no one held back.

“The conditions outside make another attack impossible for now.”

Orca surveyed the fortress.

“It’s time to focus on internal order.”

Nouvelle Vague personnel were reassigned to security.

Rules and command structures were rebuilt.

Marquis de Sade—

on the other hand—

already looked restless.

“Hehehe!”

“The revolution we failed to finish forty years ago can finally be completed!”

“Where is that Crown Prince?”

“The Emperor is dead!”

“Now we kill the brat!”

“Grandpa!”

Sadi grabbed him.

“Wait until I recover!”

“Then we can go together!”

It took considerable effort to stop Sade from immediately boarding a raft for the Imperial Capital.

Elsewhere—

reconstruction continued.

Osiris.

Cindywendy.

Soldiers.

Refugees.

Everyone worked together to expand and reinforce Tochka.

They were already preparing for the day—

one hundred fifty days from now—

when the Great Flood would finally end.

Hope had returned.

And with hope—

faces brightened.

Almost all of them.

One person remained expressionless.

Vikir.

He stood at the highest point of the fortress—

letting the rain strike him.

CRASH—

Beyond the walls—

the world had become an ocean.

White foam rose over violent waves.

Vikir compared this Tochka—

with the one he remembered from before regression.

“….”

Originally—

the wheel of fate had rolled in a far crueler direction.

The demon invasion had turned the world into hell.

Sadi—

taking advantage of the chaos—

had led troops in an assault on Nouvelle Vague.

Marquis de Sade had escaped with the help of his granddaughter.

Orca—

trying to stop that escape—

had detonated Poseidon during the prison operation.

The resulting Great Flood had ended the Long Rain of Terror.

But too late.

Countless people had already burned to death.

And the sudden flood—

unprepared for—

had devastated humanity almost as severely as the demons.

This time—

things were different.

The timing was off by roughly a week.

That delay had nearly killed Tochka through lack of drinking water.

But the flood still began at almost the expected moment.

The Long Rain of Terror had been suppressed.

The Gate of Destruction had cooled.

[At this point—]

Decarabia sounded almost smug.

[isn’t this a perfect victory, human?]

Vikir shook his head.

“No.”

“We won one great battle.”

“The war isn’t over.”

[The First Corpse?]

“Yes.”

Vikir looked across the endless new sea.

Decarabia’s eye widened.

[Ah.]

[So that is why, after escaping Nouvelle Vague, the first great house you restored was Donquixote.]

[You needed its fleet.]

“The Invincible Armada.”

Vikir nodded.

“In a world like this—

whoever owns the fleet becomes the hegemon.”

Decarabia fell silent.

Then—

[Speaking of hegemons.]

“?”

[Flauros.]

Vikir looked down.

[In Hell, he was famous even among rulers for two things.]

[Lies.]

[And an absurdly tenacious life.]

“What are you getting at?”

[I’m wondering whether he truly vanished.]

[What if even his death was a lie?]

Flauros—

the Lying Leopard.

A being so deceptive—

even his death deserved suspicion.

Vikir gave a dry smile.

“I don’t care anymore.”

[What?]

[Why?]

“Whether he died there or escaped—”

“he won’t survive.”

Rain hammered the fortress.

Waves crashed across the plains.

Far away—

tributaries from Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain spilled into the newborn sea.

Vikir stared into that dangerous world.

“This place is basically Hell too.”

◆◆◆

A jungle drowned in rain.

A man ran through a forest of blade-edged leaves.

[Huff!]

[Huff—!]

[Khk—!]

Blood covered him from head to toe.

The body—

was unmistakable.

Thomas de Leviathan.

Second son of the Poison Shadow House.

But the sharpened teeth—

and Red Death aura—

belonged to someone else.

Flauros.

At the last possible moment—

before his original body was completely destroyed—

he had shifted his soul.

Taken over Thomas’s dying body.

Demons used hosts.

Bodies could be replaced.

Thomas—

already half-dead—

had become Flauros’s final spare vessel.

So Flauros had escaped the battlefield.

Barely.

Now—

he staggered through the jungle with a body falling apart around him.

[The body is collapsing…]

[It won’t last…]

[Damn it…]

[Opening the Gate by force…]

[even my soul is shredded…]

[I have to reach the Imperial Capital…]

[I need help from Number One…]

At least he had succeeded in killing Pigi.

That—

Flauros believed—

might give him something to bargain with.

Then—

ssssss.

Cold entered his bones.

The temperature around the rocky passage dropped rapidly.

A place called **Cold Valley** by the indigenous people of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

Even in midsummer—

frost formed there.

Flauros knew none of that.

His body temperature fell.

Muscles stiffened.

Joints ached.

The wounds at his neck and abdomen reopened.

More blood flowed.

Then—

[…!]

Flauros dropped flat.

A vast shadow moved between rain and leaves.

An enormous spider.

Searching.

Not alone.

More gigantic spiders moved through the forest beyond it.

[…They’re looking for me.]

Flauros understood immediately.

If they found him—

he would die.

No escape.

He flattened himself against the ground.

Held his breath.

Crawled through mud like an insect.

Slowly.

Painfully.

Eventually—

he slipped beyond the spiders.

Reached a river swollen by the storm.

SPLASH.

Flauros entered the water.

Then—

CHOMP!

Agony tore through his lower body.

[What!?]

[Fish!?]

Saw-toothed fish clung to his flesh.

**Nateri.**

Man-eating river fish native to Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

The smell of blood had drawn them in.

[Damn it!]

Flauros twisted toward shore.

HISSS—

Darkness filled his vision.

A gigantic mouth.

A massive river serpent—

a **Mushuhushu**—

had approached without sound.

In a blink—

it swallowed Flauros whole.

[AAAAARGH!]

[You filthy creature!]

Flauros spent nearly everything he had left—

and burst through the snake’s belly.

Fortunately—

it was still immature.

Killing it was possible.

Escaping its tough hide—

cost him almost all remaining strength.

[…Shelter.]

[I need somewhere to rest.]

[If I stay out here—

I’ll die.]

His eyelids kept closing.

So Flauros tore them away.

Left the eyeballs exposed.

Vision cleared.

Then—

under the roots of a large tree—

he saw a deep hollow.

Dry.

Sheltered.

Warm enough.

Flauros staggered toward it.

Perhaps exhaustion had destroyed his awareness.

Because he never noticed—

the enormous claw marks carved into the trunk.

The result was immediate.

ROOOOAR!

A thunderous bellow erupted behind him.

Flauros could not even turn.

A blind old female **Oxbear** swung one forepaw.

Tens of tons of force.

CRACK!

Flauros’s skull collapsed.

[AAAGH—!]

[Uuuugh…]

The Oxbear stared at the poisonous thing bleeding near her den.

Disgusted.

She washed Flauros’s blood from her paw in the rain—

then crawled back into the hollow.

He was not even worth eating.

Flauros crawled through the mud.

[…!]

[…!]

His crushed mouth could no longer form words.

What was he trying to say?

Pain?

Hatred?

Rage?

Vengeance?

Fear of annihilation?

Regret—

for ever entering this world?

All of it mixed together inside his ruined mind.

Then—

something found him.

Bzzzzzzzzzz—

Mosquitoes.

Thousands.

Drawn by blood.

Some drank blood.

Some tore flesh.

Some fed only on skin.

Some drove their proboscises deeper—

into bone.

And among them—

were mosquitoes that did not seem to feed on anything physical at all.

They hovered with dull—

clouded eyes.

One of them—

despite taking nothing—

seemed to dominate the swarm.

An ominous presence.

Flauros lay on the ground—

staring at it through bloodshot eyes.

[…!]

The mosquito descended.

Landed on his ruined head.

Then—

aimed its proboscis at something no ordinary eye could see.

Pierced.

Suuuck—

Red vapor flowed into the insect.

The mosquitoes most feared by the indigenous people of the floodlands.

Soul-feeding mosquitoes.

Flauros—

Second Corpse.

Lying Leopard.

One of the great demons who had once treated humanity as prey—

became food for insects.

A miserable end.

And this time—

there was no lie left in his death.
