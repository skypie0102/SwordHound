Familiar.

A comrade from before regression.

Subordinates.

[Life after regression is comfortable, isn’t it?]

[Fine.]

[Forget everyone who died for you.]

[Live happily.]

[Did you regress just to surround yourself with women?]

[Did you really do everything you could to prevent humanity’s extinction?]

[And you call yourself a demon hunter?]

[Have you forgotten all the people who died beside you!?]

[…Traitor.]

[…Turncoat.]

STAB!

[No.]

[Uncle.]

**Nymphet.**

A child who had died long ago.

The first deep guilt Vikir had carried after regression.

[You talk about humanity’s desire to develop.]

[Its instinct to rise.]

[Are you qualified to say that?]

“…That…”

Vikir answered without thinking.

Nymphet did not wait.

[An awl piercing through a pocket?]

[Like this tongue?]

STAB!

The final destination of the living.

the dead.
