At the edge of the Academy—

inside the headmaster’s office—

Professor Banshee Morgue stepped into a darkened room.

Then stopped.

A pair of yellow eyes burned in the darkness.

“…You called for me, Headmaster?”

Banshee spoke carefully.

A face emerged.

A middle-aged man with long hair.

Clean-cut.

Approachable.

A warm smile at the corner of his mouth.

**Winston Smith.**

Headmaster of Colosseo Academy.

“I heard you handled quite a few incidents during the University League.”

“…There were still many shortcomings.”

“Not at all.”

“You handled my responsibilities extremely well.”

“I don’t know how to thank you.”

Banshee cleared her throat.

Looked away.

“You’ve been away a long time.”

“Did you accomplish what you intended?”

Winston nodded faintly.

“During training—

I suffered a mana rampage.”

“It became life-threatening.”

“But I overcame it.”

“…That is fortunate.”

Banshee’s eyes moved to the sword beside Winston.

The Winter Sword—

**Orwell**.

Winston’s beloved weapon.

Its shape resembled a unicorn horn.

Legend said only someone who had reached the highest sword realm could properly hold its hilt.

Winston had been cleaning it.

The blade was unsheathed.

The lower portion gleamed white.

The middle—

black.

The upper portion—

red.

Then Banshee noticed something.

“…?”

A scar.

A deep one.

Across a blade famous for surviving a thousand broken swords without retaining a mark.

The damage looked strange.

Part arrow wound.

Part trail left by a crawling serpent.

*What could possibly have damaged Orwell like that?*

Winston spoke before she could ask.

“I heard Professor Sadi caused considerable trouble while I was gone.”

“…More than words can express.”

“That is why I prepared this report.”

Banshee placed a mountain of files on the desk.

THUD.

Winston stared at them.

A bead of sweat appeared.

“…Haha.”

“You worked hard, Vice Headmaster Morgue.”

“Sadi is unfit to be a professor.”

“To be frank—

she belongs in Nouvelle Vague Prison.”

“Don’t be too harsh.”

“She is the final descendant of the once-prestigious Marquis de Sade family from before Imperial unification.”

“The former Marquis de Sade—

who supported that family—

is also currently imprisoned in Nouvelle Vague.”

“….”

Winston sighed.

He obviously knew about Sadi’s constant incidents.

“Still—

keeping her inside the Academy is probably safest.”

“We can’t simply send her back to the Imperial Palace.”

“And we cannot let a high-ranking noble descendant fall into destitution.”

“Final exams are approaching.”

“So are the parent-observation classes.”

Banshee’s voice hardened.

“The final ranking match will bring parents from every family to the Academy.”

“If Sadi repeats what she did during midterms—

we will have a disaster.”

“Then keep her away from actual duties as much as possible.”

“…If that is the Headmaster’s decision.”

Banshee nodded.

Sadi—

however—

was not the most important issue.

Winston Smith had returned.

His sabbatical was over.

A legendary swordsman.

A man rumored to possess strength rivaling the heads of Baskerville, Donquixote, Usher, and Morgue.

For Banshee—

who had ambitions regarding the next headmaster seat—

his return complicated everything.

Winston came from the Cold Department.

Banshee from the Hot.

Their institutional interests often conflicted.

And with finals and parent-observation events approaching—

the political balance between the Headmaster faction and Vice Headmaster faction had become complicated again.

◆◆◆

Ryukeion.

Colosseo Academy’s newspaper club.

The clubroom was chaos.

Mana-driven printing presses ran continuously.

Newspapers piled up.

Bundles were distributed.

**[Breaking News] Headmaster Winston Smith Returns to Colosseo Academy!**

**—Winston Smith, long-serving headmaster and symbolic leader of Colosseo’s Cold Department, returned last night after an abrupt sabbatical.**

**Rumors claim the leave was caused by a mana rampage requiring recuperation in his homeland.**

**Witnesses say the returning headmaster now appears even more formidable than before, leading to speculation that he may have overcome the mana rampage and reached a higher realm.**

Students talked while preparing the evening edition.

“Did you hear?”

“The Headmaster is back!”

“Great!”

“I liked Winston’s gentle personality.”

“Vice Headmaster Banshee is always so cold when collecting fees.”

“Our Cold Department finally gets sunlight again!”

“Banshee only favors Hot Department students.”

“Speaking as a Hot Department student—

the favoritism isn’t pleasant for us either.”

“He probably rushed back because finals and the parent events are close.”

Amid the noise—

one person was busier than anyone.

Dolores.

Student council president.

Head of Ryukeion.

Top-ranked student from the University League.

As student council president—

she had to plan a celebration for Winston’s return.

As newspaper head—

she had to report on it.

Winston also wanted to congratulate the University League winner personally.

That meant Dolores had to prepare an acceptance speech.

A motivational speech for future League participants.

There were medals and commendations for top third-year midterm performers.

Ceremonies.

Schedules.

Documents.

*I wish I had ten bodies.*

Dolores pushed through the work.

Her eyes—

despite exhaustion—

remained bright.

Because something mattered more than all of it.

*Remove the demon from House Bourgeois.*

*Return Bourgeois and Quovadis to normal.*

A goal she had carried for a long time—

now felt possible.

When she finally finished the urgent work—

Dolores entered an empty classroom.

On the desk—

the competition flyer.

**Mock Investment Competition.**

Sponsored by House Bourgeois.

Night Hound had given her instructions the previous night.

*Start making money.*

*Make House Bourgeois notice you.*

He had provided a significant amount of seed capital.

The goal—

turn it into more money.

Enough that House Bourgeois would be forced to pay attention.

*He never explained exactly how we’re supposed to multiply it…*

But he had asked Dolores for something specific.

Two highly competent assistants.

People who could handle information.

Administration.

Office work.

Secretarial tasks.

Dolores had created a temporary club using the mock competition as cover.

**<Mock Investment Club — Oracle>**

On the surface—

an honors-student investment team entering a Bourgeois competition.

In reality—

the money moved through Oracle would be real.

And eventually—

it would become a blade aimed at the demon hidden inside House Bourgeois.

Knock.

Knock.

The classroom door opened.

Two students entered.

They had beaten numerous applicants through written tests and interviews.

Pigi.

Sinclaire.

“Come in.”

Dolores stood.

Pigi—

despite being a first-year—

had exceptional skill in information gathering and analysis.

Sinclaire—

nearly flawless at administration, paperwork, and secretarial work.

“President!”

“I gathered the latest information and activity reports on every major Bourgeois public figure you asked for!”

“And I analyzed the entire schedule, structure, and rules of the Mock Investment Competition.”

Dolores smiled.

Reliable.

Exactly what Night Hound had requested.

Oracle needed to look like an ordinary Colosseo honors-student team.

And these two made that plausible.

*I can’t let them become involved in the real fight.*

The demon battle would begin soon.

Dolores had already planned to remove Pigi and Sinclaire from the operation before that point.

Then Sinclaire asked:

“President.”

“If this is a mock investment—

why do we need actual seed capital?”

“Because the competition is mock investment.”

Dolores paused.

“But we’re also making real investments.”

“…What?”

Sinclaire stared.

Pigi did too.

Dolores continued.

“Exactly what I said.”

“We win the mock competition—

while simultaneously using real money to generate a large real profit.”

“If losses occur—

I’ll take responsibility.”

“D-do we really need to go that far?”

“First—

because I’m confident.”

“Second—

because we need to leave a strong impression.”

Pigi and Sinclaire looked at each other.

Dolores was the student council president.

A proven prodigy.

If she was this certain—

they were willing to follow.

“I trust you, President!”

“I’ll add some of my own seed money!”

“Me too!”

“If we’re doing this to win—

I’ll prove my resolve.”

“I’ll put in everything I saved from part-time work.”

Neither Pigi nor Sinclaire believed they could dominate the competition alone.

If Dolores was the ship—

they intended to board fully.

“…You two.”

Dolores looked genuinely moved.

Then Sinclaire suddenly remembered something.

She turned toward Pigi.

Dolores’s eyes widened.

“…Wait.”

“Should we ask my brother to join too?”

“He’d definitely be useful!”

Dolores froze.

That suggestion—

was complicated.
