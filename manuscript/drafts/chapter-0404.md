# Chapter 404: Jailbreaker (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/404.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 402 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Gate of Good and Evil, Nouvelle Vague, BDISSEM, Poseidon, Kirko Grimm, Bastille, Orca Javert, D’Ordume, Souaré, Gulper Eel, and Decarabia.

There was a gate.

Perfectly circular.

White on top.

Black on the bottom.

A vast black-and-white pattern dividing the two halves.

Seventy meters across.

Five meters thick.

6,535 tons.

Mithril and adamantium—

coated in copper against corrosion.

The **Gate of Good and Evil**.

Nouvelle Vague’s main entrance.

Its greatest symbol.

The final line separating inside from outside.

Standing beneath it—

Vikir looked up.

“…Even if Nouvelle Vague collapses—

this gate might survive.”

[Agreed.]

Decarabia sounded impressed.

[How did humans build something this absurdly strong?]

Even the artifact once known as the Wailing Wall acknowledged the gate’s toughness.

It was larger and more formidable than the Bourgeois vault.

Even Vikir—

who had lived through the Age of Destruction—

could not imagine destroying it by ordinary means.

[Human.]

[Why have you been staring at that gate?]

[Don’t tell me you plan to break through it.]

[Human strength can’t do that.]

Vikir answered with a strange question.

“Do you like surfing?”

[…?]

Decarabia’s eye rolled.

[Just say what you are thinking.]

[Why must you always keep everything inside?]

“Is it frustrating?”

[…Are you seriously asking?]

Vikir smiled faintly.

“Sorry.”

“I was trained my whole life to keep my mouth shut.”

“The talkative brothers and comrades around me tended to die mysteriously.”

[…Tsk.]

Near the Gate of Good and Evil—

high-ranking guards gathered under Bastille.

The induction ceremony would begin in only a few hours.

“Warden Orca is finally coming back.”

“He’s never been away this long.”

“Two years?”

“Colonels D’Ordume and Souaré are returning too.”

“It’s been a long time since Nouvelle Vague’s Big Three were all here.”

“Did the prisoner convoy really require all three?”

“They say this intake is the largest in history.”

“The surface is collapsing into chaos.”

“Crime has exploded.”

“Why?”

“Another coup?”

“You really have been underwater too long.”

“There’s war on the surface.”

“Not gangs.”

“Countries.”

“And people are saying demons invaded.”

“Nonsense.”

“That belongs in scripture.”

“Whatever happened—

the surface is a mess.”

“Nouvelle Vague may be safer than anywhere else.”

Another rumor spread.

“There’s a Level Ten observation target coming.”

“A woman.”

“Sent directly to Level Nine without trial.”

“She’s supposed to be beautiful too.”

“You idiot.”

“Someone sent directly to Level Nine without trial is a monster.”

Vikir ignored them.

*Everyone watching the induction ceremony is exactly what I need.*

The incoming prisoners would arrive in BDISSEM restraints.

Guards would focus on them.

The prison’s older inmates would receive slightly less attention.

Vikir had studied this rhythm for years.

That was when he intended to neutralize **BDISSEM herself**.

He remembered their earlier fight.

Her desperate words as she lay shriveled on the ground.

*If I die…*

*the prisoners’ restraints…*

*the bars…*

*they’ll all lose their strength…*

Vikir had already suspected the truth.

BDISSEM’s body was the source.

If she died—

or withdrew the power—

the restraints made from her would fail.

Handcuffs.

Shackles.

Cell bars.

Across Nouvelle Vague.

All at once.

The prisoners at the induction level would rebel first.

Then deeper floors would join.

Level One.

Level Two.

Level Three.

Stronger prisoners rising from lower and lower levels.

Eventually—

every layer of the prison would pour toward the Gate of Good and Evil.

The largest riot in Nouvelle Vague’s history.

*Crisis creates opportunity.*

Vikir planned to use that chaos—

activate Poseidon—

and escape.

*First—

BDISSEM.*

He stepped away from the gathered guards.

Before carrying out the plan—

there were two people he intended to meet.

The first found him before he could move.

“…!”

Something touched his back.

Vikir turned sharply.

Kirko stood behind him.

“What are you doing here, Adjutant?”

“Aren’t you off duty?”

“My superior is working.”

“I cannot rest alone.”

“I came to help.”

She was in full uniform.

Vikir sighed lightly.

“I was about to go to your room.”

Kirko’s face reddened.

“J-just because something happened that night does not mean my room is so easy to—”

“No.”

“Don’t go back to your room tonight.”

“….”

Her face became even redder.

“Is that an order?”

“If you insist so firmly…”

“I am your subordinate, after all…”

“…?”

Vikir frowned.

The conversation had somehow gone completely off course.

He changed his tone.

Cold.

Sharp.

“Go to the Gulper Eel kennel on the fifth floor.”

“…?”

“Do not ask why.”

“Wait there until I arrive.”

“That is an order.”

Kirko stared at his face.

Then nodded.

“…Something is about to happen.”

“Understood.”

She was intelligent enough to know when not to push.

Vikir turned away.

Now—

it really was time to say goodbye to Nouvelle Vague.

◆◆◆

Drip.

Drip.

Vikir crossed empty corridors and arched bridges between towers.

Several junior guards saluted him.

People he had known back when he was still one of them.

Vikir returned the salute.

Then told them:

“You don’t need to remain at your posts.”

“Go toward the fifth floor.”

“The kennel itself is restricted—

so get as close as you can.”

It was the only advice he could give them that might keep them alive.

He repeated the warning to most junior guards he passed.

Endless black corridors.

Spiral stairs.

Ten thousand meters beneath the sea.

Outside Flubber’s translucent mucus windows—

deep-sea fish drifted through darkness.

Two more turns—

and he would reach BDISSEM’s quarters.

Then—

“Where are you going in such a hurry—

Major ‘Rotten Dog’ Garam?”

A familiar laugh came from the darkness.

“No.”

“Should I call you Major **Night Hound Vikir**?”

“Hehehe.”

Vikir’s mouth lifted dryly.

There was only one being in Nouvelle Vague with that disgusting voice.

Black skin.

Wriggling flesh.

Horrible eyes and teeth.

**Black Tongue.**

He had been waiting in ambush.
