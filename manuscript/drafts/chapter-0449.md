# Chapter 449: The Fall of Usher (9)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/449.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 447 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Fresh Fandom indexing confirms **Andrealphus**, **Madeline Usher**, **Roderick Usher**, **Masyaf**, and the Third Corpse / Ice Duke forms. Existing project terminology controls Vikir, Bianca, Tudor, Marquis de Sade, Orca, Gungnir, House Usher, Red Death, and the Night Walkers.

BOOM!

The impact struck the back of Andrealphus’s head.

Its left-side vision vanished.

[WHAT?!]

The demon twisted around.

Atop the highest surviving spire of the half-ruined Masyaf—

a man stood.

Tall.

Gaunt.

Pale as a corpse.

Large, sunken eyes.

Bloodless lips.

Hair thin as spider silk.

**Roderick Usher.**

The **Bow Demon**.

He had dragged his Red-Death-ravaged body upright one final time—

and taken up his bow.

Now—

his old eyes were fixed on the sky.

[Roderick?]

[How did you regain your senses—!?]

Andrealphus’s face twisted.

The answer was below.

Lakedog’s flood had crashed through the swamp surrounding Masyaf.

For one brief interval—

the Red Death had been washed away.

Blue foam carried the crimson haze downstream.

The pressure of death and disease around the mansion thinned.

Clean air entered.

CREAK—

Roderick drew again.

His cold—

dry gaze—

locked onto one target.

“Madeline.”

The voice came from a body already falling apart.

At the same time—

the arrow flew.

BOOM!

It punched through Andrealphus’s chest.

A huge hole opened.

Flesh.

Blood.

Fragments scattered.

[AAAAAGH!]

Andrealphus screamed.

Then—

its voice changed.

[Brother!]

[What are you doing?!]

[How can you do this to me?!]

A familiar trick.

When cornered—

demons often used the voice of the body they had stolen.

Roderick’s eyes only reddened further.

“Madeline!”

He drew again.

BOOM!

The remaining side of Andrealphus’s face exploded.

Only the lower jaw and tongue remained.

Still—

the voice came.

[Brother!]

[It’s me!]

[Madeline!]

[The sister you buried alive!]

[Are you going to kill me again?!]

The memory had haunted Roderick for decades.

A sickly little sister.

A terrible fever.

A body gone cold.

A burial conducted in grief.

Then—

one rainy night—

the grave opened.

Madeline clawed her way out.

Alive.

Or so he had believed.

She recovered.

Grew.

Lived beside him.

And Roderick—

crushed by guilt over nearly burying his own sister alive—

spent the rest of his life indulging her.

Protecting her.

Yielding to her.

But the truth was simpler.

And crueler.

Madeline really had died from the fever.

The thing that returned from the grave—

had only been wearing her body.

Roderick’s eyes burned gray.

“Andrealphus.”

For the first time—

he no longer called it Madeline.

He had finally buried his sister.

Decades late.

And now—

he would repay the thing that had used her memory.

CREAK.

Roderick drew deeper.

Too deep.

Blood vessels rose across his dried skin.

Bones cracked.

The Red Death had already ruined his body.

Now he forced everything that remained through it anyway.

“Father!”

Bianca shouted.

“Stop!”

“Your body—!”

Then she saw his eyes.

A man who had already chosen death.

And someone willing to spend everything he still had.

Bianca stopped.

“…Dad.”

Her knees weakened.

Tudor caught her.

“Bianca?”

She barely answered.

“My father…”

“…He’s going to die here.”

Everyone looked toward the spire.

Roderick prepared one final shot.

ZZZZT—

Light gathered around the arrowhead.

Every remaining fragment of mana.

Life converted into force.

Then—

BOOM!

The sound was too large for a bow.

A vortex opened.

Lightning filled it.

Wind ripped the clouds apart.

From the center—

an arrow flew like a comet.

A shot that overturned the sky.

Greater even than the force Hugo had once used to bring down Andromalius.

The arrow crossed the distance instantly.

CRACK!

BOOM!

It tore through Andrealphus’s heart—

then erased most of its chest.

[AAAAAAAAAGH!]

Even that did not kill the Third Corpse immediately.

But its flight broke.

Its body tilted.

Altitude dropped.

And waiting below—

were two old monsters.

Marquis de Sade.

Orca.

“Puhishishi.”

“That Usher boy handed us a nice present.”

Orca lifted his club.

“This is repayment for the hail.”

They had spent the battle being pelted from above.

Now—

their turn.

WHIP!

BOOM!

Sade’s lash twisted like a venomous serpent.

Orca’s club struck like an earthquake.

Andrealphus’s wings tore apart.

What remained of its body dropped lower.

Then—

the freed Usher marksmen opened fire.

BOOM!

BOOM!

BOOM!

Arrows filled the air.

Andrealphus became a pincushion.

Its cry—

half human.

Half bird.

Entirely monstrous.

[AAAAAAAAAAAAH!]

One final bowstring tightened.

Bianca.

“….”

She aimed.

Released.

BOOM!

Her arrow tore through the final surviving wing.

Andrealphus fell.

No wings.

No balance.

No strength left even to scream.

Its enormous body dropped toward Masyaf.

And beneath the falling demon—

stood Roderick.

He looked toward Tudor.

Then asked one question.

“…Cervantes.”

“What happened to him?”

Bianca could not answer.

Tudor did.

He closed his eyes.

Then shook his head.

Roderick’s gaze moved down.

To Gungnir in Tudor’s right hand.

To the empty left sleeve moving in the wind.

He understood.

“…So that is what happened.”

No explanation was necessary.

Tudor’s body told him enough.

Roderick closed his eyes.

Then—

in a voice already drying away—

said:

“Take care of House Usher.”

Those were his last words.

BOOOOOOM!

Andrealphus’s ruined body crashed directly onto Masyaf.

The mansion—

already half-destroyed—

collapsed again.

The island beneath it cracked.

Sank.

Stone towers disappeared into the swamp.

Walls followed.

Then—

the estate itself.

[Ah…]

[No…]

The Third Corpse’s voice faded into bubbles.

The Ice Duke Andrealphus—

one of the great architects of the Age of Destruction—

ended miserably.

Alone.

Beneath the ruins of the family it had destroyed.

◆◆◆

The hail stopped.

The storm faded.

Thunder rolled away.

In the center of the swamp—

only ripples remained.

And foam.

**The fall of House Usher.**

Bianca stared at the place where her home had vanished.

Blood-tinged tears ran down her face.

Tudor placed his hand on her shoulder.

Bianca’s voice shook.

“…The demons.”

“I’m going to kill every last one.”

Tudor looked down at Gungnir—

the relic inherited from his father.

“I feel the same.”

Two young successors.

Both had lost their fathers.

Both had lost their homes.

And both—

now sharpened themselves toward the same enemy.

Then—

step.

Step.

Step.

Someone approached from behind.

Vikir.

Night Hound.

The man who had begun hunting demons in secret before the world even knew a war was coming.

Tudor and Bianca turned toward him.

Their eyes no longer held doubt.

Only trust.

Vikir looked at the new hunters beside him.

Then spoke.

“Only two remain.”

Of the Ten Corpses that had brought about the Age of Destruction—

most had already fallen.

Only two remained.

The First Corpse.

And the Second Corpse.

If those two were eliminated—

the Age of Destruction could still be stopped.

It would not be easy.

Those final two might prove harder than everything that came before them combined.

But the work had to be finished.

And Vikir—

was no longer alone.

The Night Walkers stood behind the Night Hound.

Camus.

Dolores.

Sinclaire.

Tudor.

Bianca.

Sancho.

Pigi.

His Academy companions.

Marquis de Sade.

Orca.

And beyond them—

the countless people of Tochka supporting the war from every possible direction.

Vikir looked toward the horizon.

“The final phase begins now.”

The surviving demons had lost too many allies.

They would not sit still any longer.

They would use everything they had prepared.

Everything they had accumulated.

Everything they had hidden.

The Night Hound turned away from the drowned remains of House Usher.

The others followed.

The prelude was over.

All-out war was beginning.
