# Chapter 470: The Prelude to a Counterattack (6)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/470.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 468 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Fandom-backed project canon controls **Granola de Leviathan**; shifted witnesses also render the name as “Grenouille,” which is not adopted here. Established project terminology controls Flauros, Hopps de Leviathan, Reaper, Comprachicos, Ouroboros, Red Death, Poison Humans, Pigi, Vikir, Sinclaire Bourgeois, and **Lips of Sweet Talk**. The artifact’s actual yes/no answer remains unrevealed at this family boundary.

House Leviathan’s true nature.

BOOM!

The underground chamber flashed white.

A massive long-range teleportation array—

expensive enough to require enormous money and manpower to install—

collapsed after completing its work.

Flauros staggered from the light.

His body was torn apart.

Left arm—

mangled.

Chest—

split.

[Damn demon hunter.]

[Stubborn until the end…]

If Flauros had not moved his heart to the right side at the final instant—

Vikir’s attack might actually have killed him.

One careless moment.

That was all it had taken.

*Demons die.*

Vikir’s voice still echoed in Flauros’s mind.

The demon shuddered.

Then stopped.

[…Am I trembling?]

Fear.

Buried deep.

Flauros shook his head violently.

*Impossible.*

*Mana exhaustion.*

*Fatigue.*

*Nothing more.*

Yet the bitterness remained.

A demon—

had been hunted until he fled from one human.

Flauros sat in a stone chair.

Rubbed his bloodshot eyes.

Then issued an order.

[Send every poison vessel toward Tochka again.]

[This time—

far more than we used at the water source.]

Even if Tochka obtained water—

that only extended its life by days.

The fortress—

cut off from stable supplies—

was still vulnerable.

[Our poison is infinite.]

[This war cannot be lost.]

The demon hunter and his allies remained moths inside a jar.

More troops.

More isolation.

More pressure.

Eventually—

they would break.

It had become personal now too.

Flauros wanted back the pride he had lost at the water source.

*No one in Tochka survives.*

He looked toward Ouroboros.

The two snakes crawled from the Abyssal-Water shaft.

Entered another vessel.

Bit one another’s tails.

Rotated.

And poison began forming again.

Infinite venom.

Infinite Poison Humans.

In Flauros’s mind—

no family.

No alliance.

No army—

could resist Leviathan forever.

*Soon the First Prince will become Emperor.*

*Civil war or racial war—*

*I win either way.*

*Then the great work can finally begin.*

While Flauros imagined the future—

someone moved inside the damaged teleportation circle.

The black-robed Reaper.

He sat up.

Flauros—

wearing Hopps’s face—

smiled.

[Well done, my successor.]

[Because of you—

the Crown’s request has been completed.]

[Throwing the Hell Tree seeds at the end was the correct decision.]

“….”

The Reaper rose silently.

Flauros assumed exhaustion.

Waved one hand.

[Yes.]

[You must be tired.]

[Rest.]

[We will be busy mass-producing poison vessels again.]

[Once you recover—

return to the production line.]

Still—

no answer.

[And once the army is ready—

we march on Tochka.]

[This time—

finish everything.]

[Soon our family will rule the world.]

The Reaper bowed.

Deeply.

◆◆◆

Later—

he removed the black hood.

Pale skin.

Deep shadows beneath the eyes.

A young face—

still carrying something boyish despite the years.

**Granola de Leviathan.**

Third son of the Poisonous Leviathan family.

Former Colosseo student.

Second-ranked student of the Hot Department.

An early graduate who had returned home with enormous honors.

And now—

commander of the **Comprachicos**.

The strongest mutated Poison Human corps—

one of Leviathan’s central military assets.

Everything he once wanted—

he had achieved.

Rank.

Authority.

Recognition.

Respect inside the family.

Even the reputation of having surpassed older brothers who once looked down on him.

And yet—

Granola felt hollow.

*When did it start?*

When his uncle’s mind began collapsing after poison experiments?

When classmates died one after another inside Hell Tree?

When he had to watch endless people become Poison Humans?

The chain of thoughts pulled up one voice.

*I don’t think you’re a bad person.*

*Because we were friends…*

Pigi.

An ordinary-looking classmate.

Not especially powerful.

Easy to overlook.

Yet somehow—

the memory stayed.

Granola’s hand trembled.

For the first time in a long while.

Then another old voice surfaced.

*Why are you being so nice to me?*

*…Because we’re friends.*

Granola bit his lip.

“Vikir.”

He did not fully understand what he felt after seeing him again.

But one thing—

was certain.

Those eyes had not changed.

Vikir never bent.

Never acted without a reason.

Even as a classmate of the same age—

Granola had found himself following him.

Respecting him.

Trying to catch up.

Trying—

and failing—

to overtake him.

Now that same man—

after surviving battles Granola could barely imagine—

stood in opposition to everything House Leviathan was doing.

Just as Vikir’s actions had always been driven by conviction before—

there had to be a reason now too.

Granola whispered.

Then—

the question he had spent years avoiding—

finally surfaced.

*Am I living correctly?*

Mass-producing Poison Humans.

Destroying enemy factions.

Helping the First Prince become Emperor.

Unifying seven fragmented great powers under one ruler.

CLINK.

Something touched Granola’s chest.

A necklace.

A small brooch shaped like lips.

He had worn it for years—

barely thinking about it.

Tonight—

he could not look away.

“…**Lips of Sweet Talk**.”

The artifact he had received during the University League.

A prize.

A ranking he would never have achieved without Vikir.

At the time—

Granola had resented it.

Other students received obvious treasures.

He received—

this.

An artifact that could answer **one** question.

No matter what was asked—

it would answer only:

**Yes.**

Or—

**No.**

Made—

according to legend—

from the lips of an ancient sage.

One question per person.

A cooldown of roughly a century.

For all practical purposes—

a single chance.

Granola stared at the dried lips.

And remembered Sinclaire.

A conversation—

from the day they received their artifacts.

*I’m the only one who got something like this.*

*Why?*

*I think it’s useful.*

*Sometimes I wonder whether I’m living the right way.*

*That’s when I’d ask it.*

Granola—

back then—

had scoffed.

*Why would you doubt yourself?*

*I’m living just fine.*

*A man should have confidence.*

Sinclaire had smiled.

Now—

that old confidence felt very far away.

Granola looked back over his life.

Older brothers who dismissed him.

The pressure of family expectations.

An uncle who became his only refuge—

then broke.

Academy.

Other prodigies.

The small friendships born inside Hell Tree.

Rapid promotion after returning home.

Command after command.

Poison Human after Poison Human.

Turning people into weapons.

War.

And more war.

Granola looked up.

The black robe—

hanging nearby.

The enormous scythe.

The uniform of the **Reaper**.

Beyond it—

rows of iron cages disappeared into darkness.

One-eyed figures scratched at the bars.

With nails.

With teeth.

Things that had once been human.

Now engineered—

through drugs and abuse—

to hate everything alive.

Granola turned away.

Pressed one hand over the artifact at his chest.

“…Father says they are evil.”

“And we are righteous.”

“That House Leviathan will become the greatest family.”

“That we will restore humanity.”

“That these people are small sacrifices for that day.”

“That after death—

the gods will reward them.”

“That they are the glorious vanguard of a holy war.”

The thoughts kept coming.

His father’s voice.

His uncle’s final condition.

His classmates.

Pigi—

bleeding.

Vikir—

standing in his way.

Granola closed his eyes.

He thought through everything.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

Then decided what he truly wanted to ask.

He placed his hand over the artifact.

“Is the decision I’m about to make—

the right one?”

A faint light emerged from his chest.

The lips—

which had remained closed for years—

began to move.

Preparing—

to answer.
