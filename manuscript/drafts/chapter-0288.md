Gold rained across the vault.

BOOM!

Yet Vikir walked through the golden dust—

unharmed.

Before him stood the **Wailing Wall**.

The Seventh Corpse—

Decarabia.

A shield that had once stopped humanity—

now protected a human.

[…It has been a long time, Belial.]

Belial’s eyes widened.

[DECARABIA?]

[WHAT ARE YOU DOING HERE?!]

[I appear when I wish.]

[That is not what I asked!]

[What happened to your mission?]

[Opening the Gate?]

Decarabia’s voice remained indifferent.

[Did you not just say you needed no companions?]

[Then open it alone.]

Belial ground his teeth.

Only moments ago—

he had boasted exactly that.

[Decarabia.]

[This betrayal will bring you nothing good.]

[Betrayal?]

[Wrong word, youngster.]

[I was never truly on anyone’s side.]

Belial’s tone changed.

[Then can you withstand **their** anger?]

[The anger of the one who is all and one—]

[one and all?]

Decarabia fell silent.

Then answered:

[…That answer is sealed forever by oath.]

Vikir cut off the exchange.

“Belial is a demon of commerce.”

“And a demon of words.”

“The two go together.”

“So talking with him is a bad idea.”

[I know his tongue is slippery, human.]

[More importantly—]

[how does it feel to use me?]

[Wonderful, is it not?]

Vikir looked at the red translucent barrier.

Its mana consumption was enormous.

But its physical defense—

astonishing.

Size and hardness could also be adjusted.

With precise enough mana control—

it could become extremely efficient.

“…Useful.”

[Useful?]

[That is all?]

Decarabia grumbled.

Then noticed Dolores behind Vikir.

[Well.]

[Protecting beautiful women is worthwhile.]

[Handsome men too.]

[The finest subjects, naturally, are beautiful men dressed as women—or beautiful women dressed as men.]

[I, who adore good and evil and all forms of chaos—]

“Quiet.”

“It’s coming again.”

Vikir raised the barrier.

Belial unleashed another ocean of coins.

CLANG!

CLANG!

CLANG!

Sparks burst across the Wailing Wall.

It did not move.

*Excellent defense.*

*Terrible mana cost.*

Vikir’s mana core churned violently.

Using Decarabia efficiently would require much greater reserves—

and practice.

Then—

“…Um.”

“You can put me down now.”

Dolores covered her face.

Her ears were crimson.

Vikir lowered her carefully.

Then said:

“Don’t push yourself too hard.”

“We’re comrades.”

“…!”

Dolores froze.

**Comrades.**

Night Hound had called her that.

THUMP.

Her heart accelerated.

*What is this?*

*Something hot…*

Warmth spread from the left side of her chest—

through her whole body.

*Is the Soul Mate resonance returning?*

It resembled the feeling from the Dantalian battle.

No time to analyze it.

[DIE!]

Belial attacked again.

Coins came first.

Then—

a black wave blasted from his mouth.

A storm of gold and darkness.

[The wave is troublesome.]

Decarabia blocked the physical barrage—

but strained against the magical force.

Dolores stepped forward.

“Don’t worry!”

WHUMP!

A much larger wall of holy light rose.

The black wave crashed into it.

Divine power shredded the demonic energy.

Belial snarled.

[AN EMBER BEFORE A STORM!]

[NOTHING MORE!]

More treasure flowed into him.

His demonic output thickened.

Dolores’s barrier bent.

“Ugh…”

“So much power…”

Behind her—

Vikir measured distance.

Timing.

Angle.

He waited for the opening.

“This is already a huge help.”

“Thank you.”

Dolores’s determination surged.

*But it’s still so much weaker than against Dantalian.*

She wanted to help more.

Carry more.

Be someone Night Hound could actually rely on.

*We’re Soul Mates.*

The thought hardened into a decision.

“Night Hound!”

“…?”

“Please tell me your name!”

Vikir turned slightly.

“What?”

Dolores forced herself to speak through the strain.

“You can see how hard it is to maintain this barrier!”

“That resonance from before—

I need it.”

“If we connect more deeply—

even a little—

my divine power may increase!”

Soul Mates—

who did not even know each other’s names.

“It doesn’t have to be your full name.”

“A nickname.”

“One piece.”

“Anything I can call you.”

Vikir judged the situation.

No time for hesitation.

He stepped close.

Leaned toward her ear.

Then whispered—

“…Van.”

*Van.*

*Maybe I’m the only one who knows.*

THUMP.

THUMP.

THUMP.
