Midterms ended.

Colosseo Academy—

finally—

breathed.

The school followed a simple philosophy.

Study hard when it was time to study.

Play hard when it was time to play.

So every year—

after midterms—

came the festival.

Classes shortened.

Breaks lengthened.

Students were expected to use the time to plan—

stalls.

performances.

games.

food.

anything that made the festival worth remembering.

Of course—

not every professor agreed.

“Shortened classes?”

Professor Banshee Morgue looked disgusted.

“Absurd.”

Festival season—

somehow—

made him even stricter.

“Students pay enormous tuition to attend lectures.”

“Reducing class time violates their rights.”

“I cannot understand professors who skip instruction under the excuse of ‘considering the students.’”

“That is simply selfish laziness.”

Outside the windows—

banners.

garlands.

temporary stages.

tents.

sound equipment.

firework rigs.

Banshee glared at all of it.

“So.”

“Today's class will be extended by one hour.”

No one argued.

Tudor—

in the front row—

moved only his lips.

*Damn it.*

*I'm on the festival committee.*

*We were supposed to leave thirty minutes early.*

Bianca whispered back.

“I heard he does this every festival season.”

“He probably never had fun when he was a student.”

“Only studied.”

“For once—

we agree.”

Then—

Banshee looked directly at them.

“You two.”

“What are you whispering about?”

Silence.

“Festival committee members—

and you're willing to waste tuition?”

He narrowed his eyes.

“And who told you I never enjoyed festivals?”

Tudor.

Bianca.

Said nothing.

Banshee snorted.

“Do not get overexcited just because it is festival season.”

“The festival period produces more accidents than any other time of year.”

Minus one attitude point—

Tudor.

Minus one—

Bianca.

Then—

music exploded outside.

Brass.

Drums.

Imperial Army musicians tuning for a performance.

Invited dancers—

rehearsing on temporary stages.

Students—

shouting over one another.

Banshee sealed the windows with soundproofing.

Closed the curtains.

Added silence magic.

The noise—

still leaked through.

He sighed.

“…This is why I hate festivals.”

◆◆◆

Eventually—

even Banshee gave up.

The students were no longer truly listening.

“Fine.”

“If this is how you plan to attend class—

go outside and play.”

He gathered his materials.

Left.

The room—

came alive instantly.

This was a combined class—

Cold Department.

Hot Department.

Everyone present.

Perfect timing.

Festival committee students moved to the front.

Tudor—

representing the Cold Department.

Granola—

representing the Hot Department.

Tudor clapped his hands.

“All right!”

“Let's decide our festival concept.”

Each class or group needed something that could earn money.

Temporary tavern.

Game room.

Restaurant.

Haunted house.

Hunting attraction.

Anything workable.

“But!”

Tudor raised a finger.

“There is one Academy rule.”

Everyone nodded.

Not an official regulation.

Something stronger.

Tradition.

**TS.**

Festival shorthand for—

a gender-swap costume rule.

Boys dressed as girls.

Girls dressed as boys.

At Colosseo—

that was simply what one did during festival season.

Sinclaire clapped happily.

“That sounds fun.”

“Can we try it before the festival?”

“Of course.”

Granola agreed immediately.

Props arrived.

Wigs.

Fake facial hair.

Costumes.

Makeup.

Sinclaire stuck on a fake mustache.

Then laughed.

“Bianca.”

“Look.”

“Don't I resemble my café boss?”

Bianca inspected her.

“That isn't Mr. Pringles's actual mustache—

is it?”

Bianca—

meanwhile—

had transformed herself with huge fake eyebrows.

A long old-fashioned wig.

A padded muscle suit.

Sinclaire stared.

“That's… intense.”

“Are you really wearing that during the festival?”

Bianca looked offended.

“Are you insane?”

“Obviously I'll make the final version more androgynous.”

“And sexier.”

“Good.”

“Help me too.”

Bianca's eyes shifted.

Then—

brightened dangerously.

“Oh.”

“We also need to do the boys.”

She turned toward Tudor.

“Hey.”

“I'll handle your makeup personally.”

Soon—

Tudor wore long golden extensions.

It did not help.

His face—

too sharply masculine.

Bianca shook her head.

“Your features are too hard.”

“You're terrible at this.”

Sancho laughed.

Tudor glared.

Then Bianca looked at Sancho.

“…You went past ‘hard features’ entirely.”

Sancho crossed his arms.

“Hahaha!”

“This is my first time dressing like a woman.”

“Not bad.”

Sinclaire blinked.

“I expected you to hate it.”

“Nonsense!”

Sancho declared.

“Cross-dressing is one of the most masculine things a man can do!”

“Women cannot cross-dress as women.”

“Therefore only men can do it.”

“In the North—

warriors even hold contests for the greatest cross-dresser under heaven!”

Technically—

the logic was difficult to attack.

Sancho looked into a mirror.

Long messy hair.

Pale makeup.

He nodded.

“I look exactly like my sister.”

“Blood doesn't lie.”

Pigi's eyes lit up.

“Your sister must be beautiful.”

“I like strong women.”

Sancho grinned.

“Want me to introduce you?”

“She likes men shorter than her.”

“Cute ones too.”

Pigi went red.

“Someone like me?”

“Why not?”

“If you get along—

keep seeing each other.”

“If not—

don't.”

A northern warrior's matchmaking philosophy.

Simple.

The girls—

had already lost interest in that conversation.

Their attention—

had unified.

“Where is he?”

“Did you see him?”

“Find him.”

“Hurry.”

Cosmetics.

Wigs.

Dresses.

Skirts.

Stockings.

Rings.

Necklaces.

Accessories.

Props.

Most handsome boy in the Academy.

Coolest.

Cutest.

Most wanted for festival styling.

Most wanted to hug.

Most wanted to dress up.

Polls answered by—

mostly girls.

And—

a surprising number of boys.

someone pointed.

“There!”

By the window.

Asleep.

**Vikir.**

Academic meetings.

Awards.

Midterms.

Assassinations.

His schedule—

had become unreasonable.

And so—

for once—

His bangs and glasses—

no longer hiding as much of his face.

“I found him!”

“Look at his face!”

“I'll do the nose.”

everywhere.

“…!?”

Gone.

◆◆◆

Black dog form.

Out of the main building.

*Even exhausted—*

*I shouldn't be losing to kids.*

If his comrades from the Age of Destruction could see him—

startled awake by a group of students with makeup kits—

*I've gotten too soft lately.*

His road remained long.

Baskerville.

Hugo.

Humanity's traitors.

The Ten Corpses.

The Demon World.

A path of killing.

A path of revenge.

The meaning of—

*returner*—

still weighed heavily.

Clothes—

hidden beyond the wall.

A small hole—

large enough for Picaresque's dog body.

and accumulating demerits.

Almost no one came here.

Perfect.

*Through the hole.*

*Change outside.*

*Enter the city.*

*Maybe they aren't imitating me at all.*

Perhaps the public—

had simply attached his name to someone else's crimes.

Even Cindywendy—

Vikir's instinct—

however—

had begun pointing in one direction.

*One of the Ten Corpses.*

Not proof.

But close enough—

for a hound to investigate.

*Then let's start.*

Almost reached it.

“…?”

“Choco!”

A familiar voice.

“We meet again.”

**Dolores L. Quovadis.**

Student council president.

Smiling brightly.

a formal tailcoat.

And—

*Why is she here?*

Repeatedly.

“You wander around here too?”

“There are lots of stray cats and dogs.”

“There must be a hole somewhere.”

“So I come here to feed them.”

“I don't do it near the dorms.”

“Some people don't like strays.”

No volunteer points.

No audience.

No praise.

“Abandoned pets increase a few months after freshmen move in.”

“Roommate fights.”

“People get tired of them.”

“Then they leave them.”

“I love animals.”

“But sometimes I think dormitory pets should be banned.”

“I want to push for a rule before my term ends.”

“Of course—

“Some even think I dislike animals.”

“Were you abandoned too?”

Silence.

“You're always alone.”

Choco was a stray.

*She's not entirely wrong.*

A hound—

betrayed by its master.

Thrown away.

Burdened with every dirty accusation.

Then—

executed.

Andromalius—

the conspiracy's immediate architect—

Or Hugo.

Blood debt—

remained blood debt.

picking up trash.

Dog waste.

Cat waste.

Cleaning a place—

no one saw.

*Busy woman.*

Vikir slipped away.

He disliked being touched.

And—

there was another issue.

“Oh!”

Dolores suddenly remembered.

“Choco!”

“You still need to be neutered!”

Vikir ran.

Immediately.

Fast enough that Dolores lost him almost at once.

Would she ever know—

that she had just nearly tried to neuter the **Night Hound**?

“…Gone again.”

“So hard to get close to.”

“Shy little thing.”
