# Chapter 360: Voluntary Escort (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/360.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 358 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Amdusias, Hell Tree, BDISSEM, Isabella Le Baskerville, Banshee Morgue, Mozgus Quovadis, and Nouvelle Vague. Hell Tree title names remain source-local witness forms pending direct Fandom access.

CRACK.

SNAP.

The BDISSEM restraints fell apart.

The cage bars bent aside.

Vikir stepped free—

as calmly as though he had opened an ordinary door.

*So this is one of the effects of the Hell Tree titles.*

Even Vikir had only recently discovered this interaction.

Two titles remained in his body—

even though the status window itself was gone.

**Daylily Lumberjack.**

Greatly increased damage against plant-type monsters.

And slightly prolonged a wide range of status ailments.

Then—

**Leading Boatman.**

Greatly increased damage against aquatic-type monsters.

And likewise extended status effects.

Something about those accumulated title effects—

combined with Vikir’s post-tower body—

made BDISSEM restraints break far more easily than they should.

*Interesting.*

*Whatever BDISSEM actually is—

those titles clearly interact with it.*

The only major physical obstacle in his plan—

had just become much less serious.

Everyone stared.

Pedro.

Isolde.

Thomas.

And the four loyal escorts.

Isabella whispered:

“He broke BDISSEM…”

Banshee looked equally stunned.

“I’ve never seen that before.”

“Is that even possible?”

No one had time to study the answer.

Pedro, Isolde, and Thomas revealed their true forms.

Black blood surged from the corners of their hearts.

It raced through muscle and vein.

Their bodies changed.

Bones cracked.

Flesh swelled.

Human skin distorted.

Vikir had seen the same kind of transformation before—

most recently among demonized humans inside the Hell Tree.

Three enormous demonic forms towered over the deck.

Their transformation alone scorched wood.

Burst ropes.

Sent debris flying.

Isabella, Banshee, Mozgus, and Lovebad—

unable to use mana—

survived only through physical skill.

Isabella cut debris aside.

Banshee kicked flying barrels and ropes away.

Mozgus and Lovebad caught the others before they were thrown overboard.

“They were demons…”

“People at this level in the Seven Families…”

Pedro stepped forward.

His jaws had distorted.

His teeth protruded.

Then he repeated Vikir’s own words from the trial.

[Retreat is not flight.]

[When danger exceeds hope—

waiting is not wisdom.]

[A wise man preserves tomorrow.]

[He does not risk everything on one day.]

Pedro grinned.

[Those words were for my master—

weren’t they?]

Isolde snarled.

[Our masters realized you had not given up.]

[They suspected something from the moment you surrendered.]

Thomas laughed.

[Your own careless warning exposed you.]

[So you die here.]

Power intoxicated them.

They believed Vikir had only hunted lesser contractors.

They believed—

now that they had transformed—

that they were beyond him.

That confidence lasted only a moment.

Vikir released his mana.

The air darkened.

A black sun rose.

The same overwhelming force that had crushed Amdusias.

Pedro’s face changed.

So did Isolde’s.

Thomas’s.

Something was wrong.

They understood it—

too late.

FLASH.

Eight giant fangs folded into a spherical mass.

The Black Sun tore through the deck.

Isolde and Thomas had no time to attack.

No time to defend.

No time to run.

Their bodies were ripped apart.

Only enough remained for them to stay alive.

Vikir stood before them.

“Three of you were sent together.”

“Does that mean your three masters are now cooperating?”

Isolde gasped.

Thomas did the same.

No answer.

Pedro—

however—

had already run.

The instant Vikir raised the Black Sun—

Pedro turned and fled across the sea.

His demonic body moved at impossible speed—

feet striking the water like solid ground.

*I can still make it.*

*Those two idiots stayed too long.*

He ran faster.

Then—

“Too late.”

Vikir’s voice came from beside him.

Pedro almost stumbled.

*How?*

He was sprinting across the sea at full demonic speed.

Yet Vikir had approached without noise.

Without presence.

Pedro looked down.

Then understood.

Vikir was stepping across the droplets and splashes created by Pedro’s own running feet.

Using each burst of water—

as another foothold.

“You should have fled the moment the cuffs broke.”

Pedro’s terror deepened.

“If you had detonated yourself beside the ship—

you might have caused trouble.”

Vikir moved.

“But you jumped away first.”

“Too late.”

His blade flashed.

Pedro lost both arms.

Then both legs.

Before Pedro could self-destruct—

Vikir drove fingers into the back of his neck.

And tore free a bundle of demonic nerves—

like removing a fuse from a bomb.

Then he carried the mutilated demon back to the ship.

THUD.

Vikir landed on the rail.

Pedro hung from one hand.

Isolde and Thomas struggled on the deck.

It had all happened too quickly.

Isabella.

Banshee.

Mozgus.

Lovebad.

None of them even noticed at first—

that their mana had begun returning.

And no one attempted to put Vikir back in restraints.

The atmosphere made the idea absurd.

Vikir crouched beside the three demons.

“Your masters will be disappointed.”

The plan had clearly been to sink the ship—

erase Vikir—

and hide everything.

Now—

the plan had failed.

Only interrogation remained.

Pedro grinned through pain.

[Kill us.]

Isolde hissed:

[We will never betray our masters.]

Thomas spat black blood.

[We will tell you nothing.]

Vikir smiled.

It was the first smile any of them had seen from him during the entire transfer.

All three demons became uneasy.

“Good.”

Vikir’s voice carried faint anticipation.

“Then don’t talk.”

The trial process had been stressful.

He had accumulated tension.

And before entering prison—

Vikir intended to relieve some of it.
