Colosseo's indoor training center.

Advanced equipment filled the facility.

Strength machines.

Virtual dungeon rooms.

Monster holograms.

Mana-measurement chambers.

Damage-quantification devices.

And—

the most popular technology of all.

The **Gravity Room**.

Inside—

a student could select a gravity multiplier.

The room recorded how long they endured.

If the user's condition became dangerous—

the system automatically shut down.

The record reset.

Now—

hiss!

A first-year gravity chamber opened.

A muscular—

sweating—

black-haired boy emerged.

**Granola de Leviathan.**

Third son of House Leviathan.

“Haa…”

“Seven times gravity without mana.”

“Difficult.”

He wiped his face.

Then smirked.

“The burden of destiny placed upon the noble from birth…”

Students nearby looked impressed.

Most struggled even at six times gravity.

Granola had endured **7×**—

without mana—

for more than thirty minutes.

He also felt like vomiting.

That part—

he hid.

Then he noticed something.

*They're not looking at me.*

Every gaze pointed toward the next chamber.

**Gravity Room F-2**

> Gravity: **8×**  
> Current Time: **59:12**  
> Mana Use: **No**

Granola stared.

The timer kept moving.

*Impossible.*

Seven times for thirty minutes had nearly broken him.

Who was surviving eight times—

for an hour?

The safety system would cancel the field if the user deteriorated too far.

So whoever stood inside—

was genuinely enduring it.

At 8× without mana—

even upper-year students would struggle.

Then—

hiss.

Exactly one hour.

The chamber opened.

Steam rolled out.

Granola leaned forward.

*Who?*

Tudor?

Sancho?

Someone from the Cold Department?

A figure emerged.

Vikir.

The commoner boy.

Expressionless.

Granola's face twisted.

“No.”

“How could that weak-looking—”

“The machine must be wrong—”

He stopped.

Steam cleared.

Vikir's clothes had been removed for training.

The body underneath—

did not match the narrow silhouette his uniform suggested.

Dense muscle.

No wasted mass.

Every line trained.

Granola swallowed.

“…Fine.”

“With that physique—”

“eight times might be possible.”

“But without mana—”

“that's the limit for someone with poor reserves.”

Click.

Vikir adjusted the panel.

> **Gravity: 9×**  
> Time: 0  
> Mana Use: No

Then walked back inside.

Granola stared.

“He won't last a minute.”

Everyone thought the same.

Vikir—

inside—

thought differently.

*Mana works best through a healthy body.*

*Train the body without mana whenever possible.*

He was still young.

His skeleton and muscles had not fully matured.

Ordinarily—

this training should have been impossible.

But Vikir's body had never been ordinary.

Seven minutes in the **River Styx** at birth.

Brutal childhood conditioning.

Years surviving places that killed weaker people.

And the powers held in **Beelzebub**—

continually reinforcing a body already difficult to destroy.

Muscle tore.

Regenerated.

Tore again.

Regenerated again.

Bone complained under pressure.

Regeneration answered.

An hour passed.

Vikir emerged.

Stronger.

Then—

increased gravity again.

Students whispered.

“I never noticed under his clothes…”

“Pure physical strength—”

“he might be the strongest first-year.”

“First-year?”

“He'd compete with third-years.”

“Maybe graduating students.”

“But physical strength alone doesn't matter.”

“Mana is what decides high-level combat.”

“Mana amount.”

“Mana density.”

“If he'd been born into a better family…”

Vikir ignored them.

Click.

> **Gravity: 10×**  
> Time: 0  
> Mana Use: No

He went back inside.

**Bianca Fo Usher.**
