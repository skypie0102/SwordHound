# Chapter 42: Morgue's United Front (3)

> Restarted Chinese-first reconstruction. Primary source: `source/chinese/chapters/042.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 42 is an independently aligned secondary witness only. Established terminology controls **Highbro / Middlebro / Lowbro**, **Baskerville Trident**, **1st Fang**, **Highsis / Middlesis / Lowsis Morgue**, **Three Flowers of Morgue**, **Samhwa / Three Plagues**, **Staffordshire Baskerville**, and **Camus Morgue**. Chinese + aligned chapter content controls the Morgue triplets' elemental assignments: **Lowsis = water, Middlesis = earth, Highsis = plant**. A conflicting current Fandom summary does not override Chinese semantics.

Vikir thought,

*...?*

What exactly was happening here?

For the first time since his regression, something had occurred that he genuinely had not expected.

The three infamous young Baskervilles who would one day be known as **Hugo's Trident** had begun treating him like an ally.

Before Vikir's regression, those three had put his life in danger more than once.

In the end, they had even played a part in his capture and execution.

And yet now?

Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro had formed a protective circle around him.

In the past, they had surrounded Vikir to bully him.

This time, they were surrounding him to protect him.

“He's the face of our family. We can't let just anyone pick a fight with him. There has to be some standard.”

“We protect him.”

“We protect him.”

Lowbro even glanced back and winked at Vikir.

That only made the situation more absurd.

*Did losing a finger really make them this obedient?*

But this was not the first sign that the triplets had changed.

*Come to think of it, they became noticeably more docile after I killed that Troll in front of everyone.*

Perhaps it had begun even earlier, when Vikir killed the Hellhound during the practical examination at age eight.

Even the time they had approached him while he was eating and awkwardly pretended to be friendly now seemed suspicious in hindsight.

Vikir had never paid much attention to brothers his own age.

They were simply too weak to matter to him.

But perhaps that total indifference had changed something in the triplets' minds.

Baskerville, after all, was a family that worshiped strength.

More importantly, the three appeared to be laboring under another misunderstanding.

“Leave this to us, Vikir. We'll repay you for recommending us for the Academy.”

“Leave it to us, Vikir. We'll repay you.”

“Leave it to us, Vikir. We'll repay you.”

The triplets spoke with complete sincerity.

Only then did Vikir remember his recent conversation with Hugo.

When Hugo had asked which brothers he was close to, Vikir had casually named the three of them.

Apparently, learning that Vikir had recommended them had affected the triplets far more than he had expected.

Vikir chose not to explain.

“...”

Children who believed they had just done something tremendously important could be oddly endearing.

Even these three, unpleasant personalities and all, were at least outwardly cute enough at this age.

Vikir thought for a moment.

*They may be useful.*

The three would eventually grow into extremely effective killing weapons.

They were poor at independent judgment, but excellent at carrying out orders.

In Vikir's previous life, they had been used repeatedly for covert, dirty assignments.

Their skill and loyalty had been proven many times over.

They were hounds in the most literal sense—creatures that obeyed their master's commands without hesitation.

Vikir understood that better than most.

He had once felt their teeth himself.

At last, he gave a small nod as though reluctantly yielding.

“I'll trust you, brothers.”

The triplets' faces brightened immediately.

They looked happier than when Hugo praised them during the monthly evaluations.

They moved in front of Vikir with renewed determination.

*If they insist this strongly, there's no reason to stop them.*

Vikir decided to stand back for the moment.

Soon, the three Morgue sisters and three Baskerville brothers faced one another.

They had clashed repeatedly during annual family events in the past.

Their relationship had always been terrible.

The Morgue sisters attacked first.

“You idiots don't know how to do anything unless all three of you move together.”

Lowsis placed both hands against the ground.

She specialized in powerful water magic.

Countless droplets formed around her and shot forward.

Pupupupupup!

The water flew with frightening speed.

Ordinary dripping water could eventually bore through stone given enough time.

The mana-packed droplets Lowsis fired could punch through rock almost instantly.

The earth was riddled with holes.

At the same time, the barrage soaked the soil until it became wet and heavy.

Middlesis stepped forward next.

She specialized in earth magic.

With a sweep of both hands, she gathered the saturated soil together.

RUMBLE—

At her command, the wet earth rose into walls around the three sisters.

Sharp stone spikes covered their surfaces, making the barriers both defensive and offensive.

Finally, Highsis joined in.

Her specialty was plant magic.

She drew dormant seeds from beneath the ground.

Thick stalks and vines erupted rapidly, feeding on Lowsis's water and Middlesis's fertile earth.

Earth, water, and vegetation reinforced one another.

The Morgue sisters' position grew stronger by the second while the same growth pressed toward their opponents.

The talent of the **Three Flowers of Morgue** was genuine.

But the Baskerville triplets were not helpless either.

“Hehehe. It's not even meat. What are you going to do with a bunch of weeds?”

“What are you going to do?”

“What are you going to do?”

Highbro charged first.

A pale, gaseous aura formed into a single sharp fang and struck the earthen wall.

BANG! CRACK!

The wall split open, exposing the space behind it.

The sisters were still only using First-Circle magic, so there was a limit to the barrier's durability.

Middlebro and Lowbro attacked immediately after him.

Their rapidly moving aura blades vaporized incoming water droplets and cut through the growing vines.

BANG! CLANG! BOOM!

Sword and magic collided violently.

Magic gathered external mana and recreated elemental forces on an ever-greater scale.

Swordsmanship burned the mana and vitality within the body and released them explosively through the blade.

Which was stronger?

Magic or swordsmanship?

It was an argument the neighboring Morgue and Baskerville clans had repeated for generations.

Today, the three sisters and three brothers stood at the very front of that old debate.

All six fought with everything they had to uphold their family's banner.

“...”

Vikir found the entire thing unbearably dull.

Three young mages using First-Circle spells.

Three young swordsmen who had only just mastered Baskerville's **1st Fang**.

What tension was there supposed to be in a fight like this?

Vikir had no particular affection for House Baskerville either.

So the contest meant even less to him.

*It would be simpler if they all died.*

His outlook was thoroughly cynical.

The other Baskerville knights clearly felt differently.

None said it aloud, but many were watching with obvious excitement.

Staffordshire glanced at Vikir's indifferent face.

“Doesn't this interest you, Young Master?”

“What part?”

“Isn't this a chance to see the talent and potential of the young people who may one day become pillars of their families?”

“By that logic, I'm one of those young people too.”

“Young Master, you are already... rather difficult to compare with anyone else in your age group.”

“This is just wasting time. We should finish quickly. There is still plenty to discuss with the fortress commander—”

Vikir never finished the sentence.

BOOOOOOM!

A violent explosion swept across the entire area.

“Aaaagh!”

“Ugh!?”

The Morgue sisters and Baskerville brothers were all thrown off balance.

Flames stretched outward like scarlet tongues, consuming everything in their path.

At the same time, steel spikes burst from the earth, transforming the ground into a forest of iron thorns.

PUPUPU! CRACK!

The spikes tore through the earthen walls and collapsed them in an instant.

A wave of searing heat evaporated the water and burned away the vegetation.

Even the Baskerville triplets' blades were knocked aside.

Fire and iron.

Red and black.

A single violent boundary appeared between Morgue and Baskerville.

The older Baskerville knights who had been enjoying the children's fight instantly reached halfway for their swords.

Crackle! Snap! Roar!

In the blink of an eye, the surrounding cotton fields became a sea of fire.

The Baskerville triplets barely escaped the flames.

Even so, their hair had been scorched black.

“Wh-What the hell was that!? We almost burned to death!”

“What the hell!”

“What the hell!”

The three brothers looked up, ready to protest what they assumed was Morgue's excessive response.

Then they saw the sisters.

Highsis, Middlesis, and Lowsis were in even worse condition.

The flames had burned them.

The iron spikes had scratched and torn their bodies.

All three were crawling on the ground with naked fear in their eyes.

“U-Ugh... I really almost died...”

“If I'd dodged just a little later, I really would have died...!”

“S-Sisters... I'm scared...”

The Baskerville brothers quietly closed their mouths.

Whoever had attacked showed no mercy even toward fellow Morgues.

Every head turned toward the direction from which the fire had come.

A low voice sounded from beyond the flames.

“...What are you doing?”

Cold.

Yet still slightly young.

Then the person responsible for the flames and iron spikes stepped into view.

In the future she would be known by a fearsome imperial title.

She would also become famous as the **Queen of Fire and Skewers**.

The **Queen of Red and Black**.

A terrifying warrior-mage of the Morgue Clan.

*...But for now, she's still just a kid.*

Vikir looked up.

In his memory, she was an eight-year-old girl.

Now she was fifteen.

**Camus Morgue.**

Seven years after their last meeting, she stood above them and looked down at Vikir.
