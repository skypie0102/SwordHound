Deep inside a pitch-black cave—

a man sat alone.

The cave was a secluded training chamber near the edge of Baskerville territory.

Seth Le Baskerville.

Hugo's second son.

A three-eyed raven had delivered a letter to him.

Seth read it once.

“…Hmm.”

His lips curled.

“So Vikir is going to enroll in the Academy?”

He folded the letter shut.

Ssssss—

The parchment caught fire.

Black flames consumed the writing until nothing remained.

Seth opened his eyes.

For an instant, the two pupils rolled in different directions.

Then darkness spread across them until both sockets appeared filled with black.

“Good.”

He leaned back against the cave wall.

“If he's gone, that's one less obstacle when the family is swallowed.”

A vast black shadow stretched behind him.

“Hugo and Osiris are troublesome enough.”

He clicked his tongue.

“Then there are the Counts. And now that brat has exposed himself too.”

Seth's smile twisted.

“I should have dealt with him while he was still in the cradle.”

His voice hardened.

“If only that foolish mother and the nanny had done their jobs.”

Irritated, Seth reached to one side.

A child stood there.

Pale.

Trembling.

“V-Viscount?”

The child's voice shook.

“Where is this?”

“Hmm?”

Seth smiled gently.

“Don't worry. This is where you'll live from now on.”

“B-but the director said I was being sent to a good family…”

The child looked into the darkness.

“This is a scary cave.”

It was so black that the child could barely see a few steps ahead.

The air reeked.

A foul, sickening stench filled the chamber.

Seth took the child's arm.

“A cave?”

He chuckled.

“Oh, you mean this place.”

The child stared at him.

“You've misunderstood.”

Seth's smile widened.

“Of course this isn't where you're going.”

Relief loosened the child's face.

Then Seth bent closer.

“Where you're going—”

His mouth opened.

“—is in here.”

Rows of razorlike teeth filled it.

A huge eye opened inside his throat.

Its black gaze illuminated the child's face.

The child never had time to scream.

Crunch!

Seth bit down over the child's head.

Crunch.

Crunch.

The child's shadow twisted horribly across the stone wall.

Blood splashed over the rock.

Almost at once, its color faded.

The final trace of warmth disappeared into the darkness.

Seth smacked his lips.

He spat out a few bones and tossed them aside.

“Human flesh really does taste best.”

He sighed with satisfaction.

“I can never get enough.”

His eyes drifted toward the floor.

“Maybe I should raise them in batches.”

A thought amused him.

“Breed them.”

Bones already covered much of the cave floor.

Old remains.

New remains.

The smell of rotting flesh rose from them.

Seth inhaled deeply.

His expression softened in pleasure.

“Mmm.”

He stretched himself across a heap of bones.

“The smell of home.”

A grin.

“Home sweet home.”

Then—

one of the three-eyed ravens perched nearby opened its beak.

[Ten.]

Seth jumped.

“What the—!”

He spat out the bone he had been chewing.

The raven's three eyes glimmered.

[How is the work progressing?]

“Don't do that.”

Seth frowned.

“You surprised me.”

[We must work together to open the **Gate**.]

The raven ignored him.

[The new age is close. The beginning of a new era.]

Its voice became colder.

[You haven't forgotten, have you?]

“I know, I know.”

Seth waved a hand.

“Worry about the others too.”

His irritation returned.

“Am I the only one working?”

He looked at the raven.

“What about you?”

The bird's eyes narrowed.

[I don't have time to concern myself with you for long.]

A pause.

[Another human has appeared whom I need to watch.]

Seth's expression changed.

“Another human?”

He studied the raven.

“Eight. You're personally keeping an eye on someone?”

[The situation is similar to yours.]

Seth frowned.

*If he means a human worth watching like the one I'm watching…*

Vikir came immediately to mind.

*The Eighth Corpse infiltrated Morgue.*

His thoughts turned.

*Then who has risen there recently?*

The answer came quickly.

“You mean that newcomer who joined the Dark Faction?”

Seth tilted his head.

“What was the name?”

A short pause.

“Camus?”

He grinned.

“What happened to her?”

[…]

The raven refused to answer.

Instead, it reprimanded him.

[Do not ask unnecessary questions.]

Its three eyes shifted toward the bones.

[More importantly—haven't you been consuming too many human bodies lately?]

Seth's expression flattened.

[Reports of kidnappings and missing children are increasing everywhere.]

The raven's voice sharpened.

[Baskerville's hounds have excellent noses.]

[Do not leave a scent for them to follow.]

“Relax.”

Seth waved the warning away.

“I'm careful.”

He looked around the cave.

“No one has ever found me.”

[Looking at that pile of bones, I remain unconvinced.]

The raven clicked its beak.

[What happens if someone comes in?]

“They can't.”

Seth smiled.

“A barrier covers this cave.”

His black eyes gleamed.

“Only demons and human children under eight can enter.”

He spread his arms.

“No one will ever find it.”

The raven finally seemed satisfied.

[Good.]

Its voice became ceremonial.

[For the destruction of humanity.]

The three eyes shone one final time.

[For the Ten Corpses.]

The demonic presence vanished.

The raven dropped dead.

Seth stared down at it.

“Always says whatever he wants and leaves.”

He stepped on the corpse.

Crunch.

“Annoying bastard.”

Then his expression darkened.

“Damn it.”

He leaned back.

“I'm still nowhere near the amount of demonic power needed to open the Gate.”

His fingers tapped against the stone.

“How much more human blood am I supposed to absorb?”

He calculated.

“At this pace…”

His gaze sharpened.

“Another ten years, at least.”

Seth's mind was cunning.

Methodical.

He calculated the power required.

The time required.

More precisely—

twelve and a half years.

For that long, he needed to remain hidden inside the human world.

Only then could they construct the enormous Gate connecting the Demon Realm and the human world.

Only then could the great war begin.

“We need to open the Age of Destruction as soon as possible.”

A feverish smile spread across Seth's face.

“Ah…”

He imagined it.

Cities shattered.

Humanity drowning in blood.

The world turned into hell.

“Just thinking about it makes me excited.”

The ecstasy might have lasted much longer.

If not for the voice that came from inside the cave.

“What are you opening?”

Seth sprang upright.

“!”

He had sensed nothing.

No footsteps.

No movement of mana.

No change in the density of demonic energy.

Not even the faintest sound.

Then Seth saw the intruder's face.

His expression froze.

Vikir Van Baskerville stood inside the cave.

The younger brother Seth had been watching all this time.

“You—”

Seth stared.

“How are you here?”

His voice rose.

“Aren't you going to the Academy?”

“I am.”

Vikir's answer was calm.

“But before that, I wanted to see your face.”

He stepped forward.

Seth narrowed his eyes.

Something was strange.

There was an extremely faint presence of demonic energy on Vikir.

Almost impossible to notice.

But real.

Especially around his right wrist.

*Is that how he passed through the barrier?*

Seth expanded his senses.

Then he found it.

A narrow tear had been ripped through the barrier as though something sharp had punctured it.

Just wide enough for one person.

Vikir.

At the same time—

Vikir looked into Seth's eyes.

No ordinary eyeballs remained there.

Only blackness.

And from that darkness leaked the unmistakable aura of a demon.

“So you're not even going to bother pretending anymore?”

Seth shrugged.

“You already found this place.”

A smile.

“What's left to hide?”

Vikir's gaze dropped.

Children's bones lay across the floor.

His throat tightened.

Before regression—

he had been falsely accused of these horrific crimes.

He had been condemned for them.

Executed for them.

Even after the Age of Destruction had finally ended.

That had made the injustice all the more bitter.

And now—

the real culprit stood in front of him.

Smiling.

Because of this creature, Vikir had been deceived again and again in his previous life.

Driven toward death again and again.

Until eventually—

he truly died.

Pomeranian had lost her family and endured a brutal childhood because of this conspiracy.

Hugo's suffering—

Baskerville's suffering—

much of it led back here.

Seth Le Baskerville.

No.

*Whatever was wearing Seth Le Baskerville.*

“Stop smiling.”

Seth's grin widened.

“Pfft—”

Then laughter filled the cave.

“Hehehehe…”

His eyes narrowed.

“Little brother.”

“I don't know how you found this place.”

Seth reached for his weapon.

“But you're awfully confident for an Intermediate Sword Graduator.”

A serpentine flamberge came free from his waist.

Aura gathered around it.

Dense.

Powerful.

The level of a High Sword Graduator.

“You're too arrogant.”

Seth swung.

At that exact instant—

Schk!

A short sound of flesh being severed.

Seth blinked.

For a moment, he did not understand what had happened.

Then—

Thud.

Something landed on the cave floor.

An arm.

His left arm.

The one that had not been holding the sword.

Blood sprayed from the perfectly clean cut.

“…?”

Seth looked up.

Vikir's blade was already coming toward him.

Beelzebub.

And around it—

seven serrated Fangs.

The **Seventh Fang of Baskerville**.

But even that was not what shocked Seth most.

The aura covering Vikir's sword was thick as honey.

Red as blood.

It had crossed beyond the ordinary High Sword Graduator realm and was approaching the threshold of Sword Master.

The aura of a **Peak Sword Graduator** exploded through the cave.

“I told you.”

Vikir's sword flashed toward Seth's mouth.

“Stop smiling.”

Those were the last words Seth heard—

before Vikir tore open both corners of his grin.
