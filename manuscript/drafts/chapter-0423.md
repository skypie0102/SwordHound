# Chapter 423: How to Become a Wandering Knight (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/423.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 421 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established terminology controls Gnoll, Poison Gnoll, Night Walkers, Knight of the Night, Tudor, and the Ark.

The warning horn echoed across Wakewack.

This was not a bandit raid.

Not another wildfire.

Monsters had crossed the village defenses.

Gnolls.

Dozens.

Then hundreds.

The drought had starved them too.

Their bodies were thin.

Their fur dull.

Their eyes bloodshot.

With forests burning and prey disappearing—

weaker monsters had been pushed toward human settlements.

A gnoll smashed into a house.

It tore through cloth and wood—

following the freshest human scent.

BANG!

A closet door burst open.

A man charged out with a pitchfork.

“Get away!”

CRACK!

The gnoll’s skull broke.

It died immediately.

Then the second arrived.

And the third.

Fourth.

Fifth.

Too many to count.

“Honey!”

“Take the children through the back!”

“Get to the cellar!”

A woman ran with three children.

The family reached the backyard shelter.

The parents descended.

Two children followed.

Then—

“MOM!”

A little girl had fallen behind.

A nail protruding from the doorframe had caught her clothes.

Blood darkened the torn fabric.

“Sweetheart!”

The father froze.

The gnolls were almost on her.

If he opened the door—

everyone inside might die.

If he closed it—

his daughter certainly would.

The parents stared at each other.

Then—

BANG.

The door shut.

Outside—

the girl clawed at it until her fingernails tore.

“Mom!”

“Dad!”

“Please!”

“I’ll listen!”

“I won’t cry!”

“I’ll eat less!”

“I’ll clean!”

“I won’t fight with my brothers and sisters!”

The only answer—

was the growling behind her.

A gnoll lunged.

Then—

THUNK!

A pitchfork punched through its mouth.

“RUN!”

The father had reopened the door.

The mother came out too—

armed with broken bricks.

The girl stumbled toward them.

Her parents stood between her and the pack.

Strong parents.

But no human strength was enough against the whole world.

The gnolls charged.

RIP—

Blood sprayed as claws tore through leather.

Then—

CRACK!

The gnoll biting the father’s leg lost its head.

A dark figure stood in front of the family.

A ragged cloak.

Black plate armor.

One arm.

The wandering knight.

He raised the long object from his back.

Bandages tore away.

A spear appeared.

Blue liquid aura flowed along its blade.

Graduator.

The knight moved with almost no wasted motion.

One thrust—

one kill.

Another enemy crippled.

Then another.

And another.

A veteran’s technique.

Not the beautiful spearwork of tournaments.

The efficient violence of someone who had survived too many battlefields.

The hood fell back.

He was young.

Broad-shouldered.

Lean-waisted.

Built for war.

His left arm was gone.

Even so—

his spear moved like a living thing.

The backyard became silent.

Then the little girl opened the cellar door.

“Mister!”

“Who are you?”

The knight looked back.

“My name is **Shammua**!”

“What’s your name?”

He almost answered—

then more howls came from beyond the fence.

The fight was not over.

CRASH!

Something three times larger than an ordinary gnoll smashed through the wooden barricade.

Black-green fur.

Bloodshot eyes.

Oily fluid dripping down its face.

A smell of rotting flesh.

**<Poison Gnoll>**

**Danger Rating:** A  
**Size:** 3 m  
**Discovery Location:** All continents  
**Aliases:** ‘Poison Gnoll’, ‘Deadly Poison Gnoll’

A rare gnoll variant with an abnormally evolved digestive system.

Ordinary gnolls consume carrion.

Poison Gnolls ferment rotting flesh inside their stomachs and accumulate toxins in their skin and saliva.

Roughly one appears in a pack larger than three hundred.

Most serve as pack leaders.

The rescued family collapsed in fear.

The wandering knight—

only smiled.

“…Poison Gnoll.”

“Been a while.”

Blue aura rose from his remaining arm.

His spear did not tremble.

Before Shammua’s mother pulled her back underground—

the knight spoke.

“I belong to the vigilante group **Night Walkers**.”

“My nickname is embarrassing.”

“They call me the **Knight of the Night**.”

Shammua’s eyes widened.

One of the legendary Night Walkers.

One of those said to guide refugees toward a land of salvation—

the **Ark**.

The one-armed knight winked.

“My name is **Tudor**.”

Then he turned toward the Poison Gnoll.

His expression became cold.

“No surname.”
