# Chapter 408: Jailbreaker (6)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/408.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 406 is the verified shifted secondary witness. BOOM!

Three layers of steel doors collapsed.

Only a grid of BDISSEM material remained.

Beyond it—

Brigadier General BDISSEM sat upright on her bed.

“What?”

“What is happening?”

She had recovered from their previous encounter.

Her body once again resembled a small girl.

Then she saw the Major entering through the ruined doorway.

Her eyes dropped to the rank insignia.

“What kind of Major breaks into my room like this?”

“Are you insane?”

Vikir removed his hat.

Showed his real face.

BDISSEM went white.

BANG!

The mattress exploded beneath her as she sprang backward.

“You!”

“You bastard!”

“Didn’t you escape!?”

That had been the story she told.

Not the truth.

She had never actually witnessed Vikir’s death.

Only invented a convenient explanation for D’Ordume.

*Where did the escaped prisoner go?*

*He died!*

*Then where is the body?*

*He rode a Gulper Eel into the deep sea.*

*Then he is not dead.*

*He didn’t know the current leading to the surface!*

*He must have been trapped in the abyss forever!*

*So you did not actually see him die?*

*I did!*

*I saw him leave through the hole before I passed out!*

A lie.

And now—

the result stood in front of her.

Sweat ran down BDISSEM’s face.

“If you move another step—

I’ll call D’Ordume and Souaré!”

She hid behind the living restraint-grid.

Vikir touched it.

CRACK.

The plant structure dried instantly.

A hole opened.

Large enough for a person.

Vikir walked through.

Step.

Step.

Step.

BDISSEM pressed herself against the bed.

The old fear returned all at once.

The hound approached through darkness—

red eyes burning.

“Stretch out your neck.”

The delayed death sentence had finally arrived.

BDISSEM snarled.

“I am still one of the Five Wardens!”

“If I stay alert—

you cannot defeat me so easily!”

Huge kelp leaves erupted around her.

Seaweed whips.

Dense enough to form a forest.

They wrapped toward Vikir.

“No use.”

Beelzebub cut through them.

But BDISSEM had not intended to fight.

She stepped onto one of the broad leaves—

using it like a surfboard.

“Hehehe!”

“If I decide to run—

no one can catch me!”

Vikir watched her.

Then asked:

“…No one?”

Something snapped around BDISSEM’s body the moment she crossed the broken doorway.

Vines.

“…What?”

She was yanked upside down.

The kelp beneath her dried instantly.

Roots crawled across its surface.

A plant she had never seen before.

**<Daylily of the Blood Tree>**

**Danger Rating:** S  
**Size:** ?  
**Discovery Location:** Deep Polar Hell — Inaccessible Zone, Area 4, Blood Swamp Flesh Region  
**Aliases:** ‘Blood Swamp Daylily’ / ‘Nightmare of the Blood Swamp’

A mysterious demonic plant found at the Blood Lake deep within Hell.

It can float even in the Blood Swamp—

a region where everything else sinks.

Among the flora and fauna of the Blood Lake—

it is feared for overwhelming appetite and predatory growth.

Legends claim a single specimen once grew large enough to threaten humanity.

The seeds Vikir had scattered earlier—

had absorbed Nouvelle Vague’s moisture.

And germinated.

“A-ah…”

BDISSEM finally understood the instinctive terror she had felt the first time Vikir displayed his drying power.

Plants of the human world—

had natural predators.

Demonic plants.

Species evolved in the brutal Demon World treated ordinary plants as helpless prey.

BDISSEM was no exception.

The vines wrapped tighter.

Moisture drained from her body.

“The Daylily of the Blood Tree is vicious even among demonic plants.”

“Other demonic plants wither around it.”

“Human-world plants have no chance.”

Vikir approached.

He seized BDISSEM’s cheeks.

Forced her mouth open.

Then held a handful of seeds in front of her face.

“If you refuse my orders—

I put these in your mouth.”

BDISSEM stared at the seeds.

Then imagined them reaching her stomach.

Tears filled her eyes.

“P-please…”

Vikir loosened his grip just enough for her to speak.

“Please spare me.”

“I’ll do anything.”

She already knew she could not defeat him.

Vikir nodded.

“Normally—

killing you would be faster.”

“Hic!”

Her face drained of color.

“But I have another use for you.”

“If you do it properly—

you may live.”

“What?”

“What do you want?”

“Two things.”

“First—

remove the restraints.”

“Handcuffs.”

“Shackles.”

“Cell bars.”

BDISSEM blinked.

“You can already break those yourself.”

“You just did.”

“Not mine.”

Vikir looked directly at her.

“Every prisoner in Nouvelle Vague.”

Her expression froze.

Only then did she understand.

Why he had not simply escaped once his own restraints were gone.

Why he had returned voluntarily.

Why he had spent years inside this hell.

His target was not his own freedom.

It was the prison’s entire control system.

BDISSEM trembled.

The monster in front of her did not want to break one chain.

He wanted to break them all.

“You don’t want to?”

Vikir moved a seed closer to her mouth.

“I-I’ll do it!”

“I’ll do it!”

“Please!”

“Good.”

“Second.”

Vikir leaned toward her ear.

“Outside the fifth-floor kennel window—

someone will appear.”

“When that person does…”

He whispered the rest.

BDISSEM nodded frantically.

“You understand what happens if you break either promise?”

“…What happens?”

“You see me again.”

“After that—

use your imagination.”

Vikir laughed.

Actually laughed.

The effect on BDISSEM was immediate.

She lost control of herself from fear.

Vikir lightly touched her throat with the weapon tip.

“Now.”

“Fulfill the first promise.”

“O-okay.”

BDISSEM squeezed her eyes shut.

A few seconds passed.

Then she opened them.

“Done.”

That was all.

Vikir frowned.

Could something so simple really erase a system that had controlled Nouvelle Vague for generations?

All those mana-suppressing cuffs.

Shackles.

Bars.

Every restraint made from BDISSEM.

“You aren’t lying?”

Before he finished—

THUD!

The floor shook.

A short—

heavy shockwave.

Vikir’s instincts answered immediately.

*The restraints are gone.*

He expanded his senses.

Nearby BDISSEM material had all withered.

Even fragments stored in pockets.

Even pieces far down the corridor.

Then the same had happened throughout the prison.

Level One.

Level Two.

All the way to Level Nine.

“I-it’s true!”

“I released everything!”

“Please believe me!”

Vikir nodded.

“…Yes.”

“It seems you did.”

Normally—

he would have finished her here.

But she still had one remaining use.

The Night Hound turned away.

The jailbreak had reached its midpoint.

For the first time in Nouvelle Vague’s history—

every prisoner had been released from the same chains at once.

The great uprising—

had begun.
