“…What?”

Seth's eyes widened.

“How is that possible?”

He stared at Vikir.

Vikir Van Baskerville.

One of Hugo's illegitimate sons.

An insignificant bloodline.

A hound that should have been disposable.

At least—

that was what Seth had always believed.

Of course, Vikir had been unusual from the beginning.

He had passed the Cradle of the Sword faster than anyone before him.

He had received the blessing of the Styx.

Even as a child, he hunted monsters such as trolls and Cerberus.

Two venomous snakes had been placed in his cradle to eliminate him early.

They failed.

Then, after disappearing on the frontier, Vikir returned alive.

At the Great Banquet, he revealed strength at the level of an **Intermediate Sword Graduator**.

Seventeen years old.

Intermediate Sword Graduator.

It was an achievement without precedent in Baskerville.

Even Osiris—

the eldest son praised as a genius—

had been nearly thirty by the time he reached that level.

And yet Seth had looked at Vikir during the banquet and privately sneered.

*Idiot.*

*Showing that much of your hand will get you killed one day.*

Power needed to be displayed in moderation.

And hidden in moderation.

Show enough to gain position.

Hide enough to prevent everyone around you from becoming wary.

Only a child desperate for adult praise revealed everything he possessed.

So in Seth's mind—

Vikir was still a child.

Talented.

Dangerous someday.

But not yet someone who required true fear.

That was what Seth had believed.

Until now.

The Vikir standing in front of him was beyond anything he had imagined.

Unbelievable.

A boy who had not even reached twenty—

standing at a height that should have taken decades.

*High Sword Graduator…?*

Seth's mouth fell open.

*No.*

His eyes trembled.

*Peak?*

The sight was so absurd that if Vikir had not been his enemy, Seth might almost have admired it.

Vikir, meanwhile, made no attempt to hide anything.

He unleashed his combat power without restraint.

“It doesn't matter.”

Seth blinked.

“What?”

“No one is going to see.”

Seth forced a laugh.

“…No one?”

He spread his remaining arm.

“Your older brother is looking right at you, little brother.”

Vikir's expression did not change.

“Not for long.”

Then he moved.

Crack-crack-crack—

The **Seventh Fang** appeared.

A Fang traditionally associated with the highest authority of Baskerville.

Small.

Sharp.

Distinct.

Combined with the aura of a Peak Sword Graduator—

its killing power was enough to alarm even a high-ranking demon.

Seth recoiled.

“That's insane!”

His face twisted.

“How can you use the Seventh Fang?!”

But he had no time to demand an answer.

Vikir did not explain the deeper Baskerville sword theory he had learned—

the eighth.

The ninth.

Even the tenth.

Knowledge was not the same as present mastery.

For now, he simply drove the Seventh Fang with perfect control—

and pushed against the wall beyond his current realm.

Boom!

Crack!

Stone shattered.

Stalactites and stalagmites snapped like reeds.

Seth was driven backward without a moment to breathe.

*What kind of experience does he have?*

Seth clenched his teeth.

*Even Baskerville's old veteran knights aren't this seasoned.*

Fighting Vikir felt like standing before a mountain.

It reminded Seth of facing one of the Seven Counts.

He raised his flamberge.

The **Sixth Fang** of Baskerville erupted.

Six Fangs collided with seven.

But—

the shape of Vikir's attack was different.

Seth's eyes narrowed.

*Stabbing Fang?*

No.

*What is that?*

Too late.

Puck-puck-puck-puck-puck!

Seth's body burst open in several places at once.

The attack was less like a clean slash—

and more like an ambush by hidden teeth.

A Fang shaped not merely to cut—

but to penetrate.

To tear deep.

To inflict pain.

Seven killing thrusts struck across Seth's body.

“Aaaagh?!”

He staggered backward.

His left arm was already gone.

His balance collapsed.

Vikir did not allow him to recover.

Thud!

Seth retreated one step.

Vikir advanced two.

Seth swung once.

Vikir answered twice.

Puck-puck-puck-puck-puck-puck-puck!

The hound of Baskerville tore into him without pause.

Not slicing.

Piercing.

Biting.

“Ghh—!”

Pieces of flesh dropped from Seth's body.

He could barely think.

The swordsmanship was too relentless.

Too efficient.

Too familiar with killing.

This was not the technique of someone who had killed once or twice.

*Impossible.*

Seth's mind screamed.

*He's seventeen.*

*How many of his own kind has he killed to move like this?!*

He forced himself to counter.

Whoosh!

Vikir closed the distance again.

Then—

Seth smelled something.

Not on Vikir's skin.

Not in his clothes.

Something deeper.

A scent soaked into the soul.

A scent that could never be washed away.

Demon blood.

The smell demons gave off when they bled—

when they suffered—

when they died.

Fear entered Seth's eyes.

*…A demon hunter?*

The human standing before him was still young.

But the soul inside that body carried a scent that should not have existed.

The scent of a butcher who had slaughtered countless demons.

A dog could recognize the butcher who killed its kind.

And Seth's body reacted the same way.

It stiffened.

Shrank back.

*How many demons has he killed?*

Whenever Vikir's killing intent flared, Seth imagined an impossible landscape.

Mountains of demon corpses.

Rivers of demon blood.

“No…”

Seth's voice shook.

“No, impossible!”

His mouth opened wider.

“How could someone this young have survived a hell like that?!”

Opening his mouth merely gave Vikir a larger target.

Slash!

The blade tore through Seth's gums.

Several teeth flew.

His tongue split.

The edge cut deep through his mouth and throat.

Shhk.

Vikir withdrew the blade cleanly.

“…”

For an instant, it looked as though he might ask a question.

Then he decided against it.

There was no need.

He already understood enough of Seth's plan.

Enough of his purpose.

Combining what Vikir remembered from before regression with what he knew now—

the answer was obvious.

*This thing came from the Demon Realm.*

*Its purpose is probably the destruction of the human world.*

Before regression—

a Gate into the depths of the Demon Realm had opened.

Ten Demon Kings emerged as the central calamities of the Age of Destruction.

They became known as the **Ten Corpses**.

The name referred both to the ten demons—

and to the ten human corpses they had taken as vessels.

Some identities had eventually been exposed.

Others had never been uncovered.

Seth Le Baskerville had belonged to the latter group.

*…I didn't know what he really was even on the day I was executed.*

Even after heroes closed the Gate and brought the Age of Destruction to an end—

they had failed to identify and punish every one of the Ten Corpses.

But this life was different.

The Age of Destruction had not yet begun.

The deep Gate had not opened.

The Ten Corpses had not yet gathered enough power to fulfill their role as its keys.

And Vikir—

had already found one.

“You die here.”

The declaration was flat.

Absolute.

If Vikir killed one of them now—

the future would change.

One death would not necessarily prevent the Age of Destruction forever.

But it could delay it.

Significantly.

That delay mattered.

More time to strengthen himself.

More time to prepare.

More time to remove variables.

*I will not fail again.*

Vikir attacked.

He had regained the level of power he possessed before regression.

But his swordsmanship was now sharper than in that first life.

His aura—

stronger.

Beelzebub blazed red like the sun.

Seth could not stop it.

His own strength had hovered between Intermediate and High Sword Graduator.

“Gaaaah!”

Blackened blood poured from his wounds.

Seth was weak only by Baskerville's monstrous standards.

He was still Hugo's second son.

His talent had never been poor.

The problem was his body.

He had inherited a frail frame from his mother—

a vessel incapable of carrying all the talent inherited from Hugo.

No matter how desperately he trained—

his body imposed a ceiling.

High Sword Graduator.

That was as far as it could go.

For a man born with enormous potential, the limit had been humiliating.

And humiliation was nothing new to Seth.

Before Vikir—

there had always been Osiris.

“Ghh…”

Dead black blood ran from Seth's eyes.

His nose.

The corners of his mouth.

A lifetime of inferiority twisted inside him.

Vikir paused for the smallest fraction of a second.

*Seth's soul…*

His gaze sharpened.

*Is some of it still in there?*

The real Seth appeared to have died by hanging himself before the demon took his body.

And yet—

deep inside that body—

traces of Seth's memories and personality still seemed to remain.

*That changes nothing.*

The hesitation existed only in Vikir's mind.

His hand never stopped.

Puck!

Puck!

Crack!

Seven Fangs tore through Seth again.

Finally—

something inside him broke.

“You filthy Baskerville!”

The voice no longer sounded human.

“How dare a dirty hound do this to me!”

Seth's disguise collapsed.

No—

the thing wearing Seth's skin finally revealed itself.

Sssssssss—

Demonic energy poured from every opening in Seth's body.

Black power wriggled around him like mud spreading over a doll.

It swelled.

Twisted.

Took shape.

And Vikir finally recognized the demon that had devoured Seth's body and soul.
