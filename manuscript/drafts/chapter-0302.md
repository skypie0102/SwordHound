# Chapter 302: The Age of the Warmonger (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/302.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 300 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Night Hound, Professor Sadi / Ms. Ouroboros, Winter Sword Orwell, Beelzebub, Belial, Andromalius, and the Fifth Corpse. Sadi’s whispered request is deliberately kept ambiguous because the source does not identify the referent yet.

Criminals continued pouring through the broken barriers.

“Hehehe.”

“I’ve always wanted to see inside this Academy.”

A man rummaged through the ruined auditorium.

People called him the **Fashion Master**.

His hobby—

making clothing from victims’ skin.

The Imperial Guard had nearly captured him once.

Sadi saved him.

Since then—

he had become one of her fanatics.

“Ah.”

“The smell of higher education.”

“The sacred ivory tower.”

He laughed while searching.

“Rich young masters.”

“Young ladies raised in greenhouses.”

“The best skin.”

“The best hair.”

“I’ll make the Queen a winter coat.”

Then he saw them.

A mother and daughter trapped under debris.

The student was slender.

The mother—

even more fragile.

“Oh!”

“Material!”

He approached carrying enormous scissors.

“Don’t be scared.”

“I only need your skin.”

“I won’t necessarily take your life.”

The two screamed.

Then—

PFFT.

A fang entered the Fashion Master’s throat.

“…Huh?”

Before he understood—

the bite twisted.

CRACK.

His neck broke.

The corpse hit the floor.

Night Hound stood between the women and the wreckage.

“…Run toward the smoke.”

“There’s an exit there.”

The mother and daughter stared at his mask.

Fear deepened.

“Night Hound…”

Apparently—

the criminal who had just saved them was more frightening than the criminal who had threatened them.

Vikir sighed beneath the mask.

“If you don’t run now—

I’ll kill you.”

“Aah!”

That worked.

They fled.

Vikir waited until they were out of sight.

Then killed three more criminals chasing them.

A few exchanges later—

he noticed something.

*There aren’t as many attackers as I expected.*

*But every group is positioned where it can cause maximum damage.*

Someone had known the internal layout.

The schedule.

The response pattern.

Then—

“Hehehe.”

A familiar laugh.

Ms. Ouroboros.

Professor Sadi.

She walked toward Night Hound almost lightly.

“What luck.”

“To meet Night Hound here.”

Her voice—

normally mocking—

carried genuine respect.

Vikir nodded once.

Sadi smiled.

“I’m a fan.”

“….”

Coming from one of the Capital’s most infamous lunatics—

the words meant little.

She continued anyway.

“You like the Old Era too, don’t you?”

“Strength above everything.”

“The Warring States logic.”

“I can smell it on you.”

“You’re like me.”

“Someone living in the past.”

In one sense—

she was right.

Vikir had lived through an era that no one here remembered.

He had been trapped by it ever since regression.

But the meaning was completely different.

“I live in the future.”

Sadi blinked.

Then brightened.

“The future?”

“Wonderful!”

“The Warring States Era I want will return someday.”

“So I’m a person of the future too?”

“Hehehe.”

“As expected.”

“So much to learn from you.”

Vikir abandoned any attempt at normal conversation.

Then noticed what she was holding.

The **Winter Sword Orwell**.

Winston’s beloved weapon.

A legendary blade said to survive a thousand broken swords without damage.

Long.

Needle-like.

Its lower grip making it resemble a short lance.

Sadi noticed his stare.

“You recognize it.”

“….”

“You’re interested?”

“Then you really are from **that side** too?”

Vikir ignored the nonsense.

“Give it to me.”

Orwell mattered.

Enough that—

if necessary—

he was prepared to kill Sadi for it.

Sadi surprised him.

“Sure.”

“But one condition.”

She lowered the zipper on the back of her tight suit enough to expose the upper chest area.

“Sign here.”

“….”

Vikir stared.

Making Sadi an enemy in the middle of this crisis offered no advantage.

He took the pen.

And wrote:

**Night Hound**

The sharp pen scratched across skin.

Small drops of blood mixed with the ink like a crude tattoo.

Sadi looked down at the autograph—

ecstatic.

“Ah.”

“Night Hound’s personal signature.”

“I’m the first, right?”

“….”

“If I’m not—

that’s fine.”

“I can just kill whoever got one before me.”

Vikir said nothing.

Sadi—

amazingly—

kept her bargain.

She handed him Orwell.

“I need the **front-gate key** too…”

“But there are other ways.”

Then she leaned close.

“One more favor.”

Her voice dropped to a whisper.

“If you ever get the chance…”

“bring it out too.”

The referent—

unspoken.

Vikir did not ask.

“…If I get the chance.”

“So cool.”

The exchange ended.

Vikir placed Orwell inside Andromalius’s dimensional barrier.

Then turned away.

And stopped.

“…!”

A smell.

Very faint.

Demonic.

*Where?*

He concentrated.

The source became clear.

Sadi’s **left eyepatch**.

Her left eye had been destroyed during the midterm incident.

There should have been nothing behind the patch.

Yet—

a thin demonic scent leaked from that empty socket.

*Similar to the smell left by Belial’s corpse.*

*Needs investigation.*

Vikir looked back.

Sadi tilted her head.

“Something else?”

“Want to give me something better than a signature?”

“….”

“Spit?”

“Here.”

“Ahhh—”

She opened her mouth.

Vikir frowned—

then the ground shook.

BOOM.

The damaged auditorium began collapsing again.

Even Vikir staggered.

And at the same moment—

a second demonic scent struck his nose.

So strong it almost burned.

Far stronger than the trace from Sadi’s eye.

Both Vikir and Sadi turned.

Someone stood in the ruins.

Winston.

Clouded eyes.

Behind him—

a gigantic shadow rose.

Four heavily muscled legs.

Black mane falling like a waterfall.

Dark eyes.

A single enormous horn—

broken at the tip.

A unicorn.

But there was nothing sacred about it.

Only malice.

Terror.

Vikir understood instantly.

*The Fifth Corpse.*

One of the Ten Corpses.

And among them—

a monster feared above nearly all others in direct combat.
