# Chapter 447: The Fall of Usher (7)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/447.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 445 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Existing project terminology controls Vikir, Bianca, MiniPin Baskerville, Professor Banshee Morgue, Decarabia, Camus Morgue, Seere, Isolde, House Usher, Daylily of the Blood Tree, and Andrealphus. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

A massive face rose from the flood.

No eyes.

No nose.

Only a mouth—

split wide with teeth.

Even without features—

its expression was obvious.

Hunger.

And the joy of finally feeding.

The **Daylilies of the Blood Tree** opened their mouths beneath the surface.

Waiting.

The demonized Usher retainers screamed.

[WHAT IS THIS?!]

[THE VINES!]

[I CAN’T BREAK FREE!]

[HELP—!]

The flying monsters were dragged back down.

CRUNCH.

One Daylily closed its jaws.

A demon disappeared halfway inside.

Black blood poured through crushed flesh and shattered bone.

The plant shook almost happily.

Then cast out more vines.

SHRRR—

More winged demons were pulled from the sky.

[WHEN DID THESE GET AROUND MY LEGS?!]

[THE WATER!]

[THEY CAUGHT US WHEN WE CAME OUT!]

[TOO STRONG!]

The river turned dark.

Dozens of Daylilies clustered around struggling prey.

Isolde stared in horror.

Vines bound his ankles.

Wings.

Torso.

[…Where did these come from?!]

He looked toward Vikir.

Vikir gave no answer.

Before Madeline’s pursuit force arrived—

when Lakedog’s drained bottom was exposed—

Vikir had planted the seeds.

*A lake that has not dried for thousands of years should not crack this deeply from drought alone.*

The Daylily seeds had consumed what little water remained in the soil.

Now—

the flood returned.

And the seeds bloomed.

Isolde snarled.

[Stop acting calm!]

[Those monsters eat humans too!]

He was right.

Ordinary Daylilies of the Blood Tree were carnivorous.

Humans were perfectly acceptable food.

Then—

SPIT.

One Donquixote soldier slipped into the water.

A Daylily caught him.

Wrapped him in vines.

Dragged him toward its mouth.

Then paused.

Its tongue extended.

Licked him once.

And—

threw him away.

The soldier splashed back into the river.

Alive.

Isolde’s expression became incomprehensible.

Vikir nodded.

“Good.”

“Modification succeeded.”

A man stepped forward behind him.

Chest proudly out.

**MiniPin Baskerville.**

The monster-ecology specialist.

“That took a lot of work.”

“We selected individuals with unusually low appetite for human blood.”

“Then bred only those.”

“Generation after generation.”

He continued excitedly.

Human blood—

paired with repulsive additives.

Demon blood—

paired with substances the Daylilies strongly preferred.

Selective breeding.

Conditioning.

Repeated experiments.

At Tochka’s underground facility.

Professor Banshee Morgue had even sent a research team to assist.

The result—

plants with reduced aggression toward humans.

And extreme appetite toward demonized blood.

SPIT.

SPIT.

The Daylilies actively avoided humans.

But when demon blood appeared—

they went mad.

Isolde strained against four vines.

[NO!]

He tore several apart through sheer demonic strength.

More wrapped around his neck.

[I AM MADELINE’S LAST TRUE VASSAL!]

[I WILL RULE HUMANS BENEATH ME!]

[I WON’T DIE HERE!]

[THIS ERA IS MINE!]

[I’VE WAITED TOO LONG!]

“Waited?”

Bianca stepped forward.

Bow raised.

“Opportunity isn’t something you wait for.”

Isolde looked down.

Bianca drew.

“You take it.”

BOOM.

An arrow pierced between his brows.

The skull cracked.

Black matter spilled.

Isolde remained alive.

Demonic vitality—

unnatural.

Bianca fired again.

And again.

And again.

Every arrow struck the same point.

THUD.

THUD.

THUD.

Finally—

Isolde fell.

Six vines tightened around his body.

Below—

too many mouths waited.

CRUNCH.

His death became the beginning of the purge.

Daylilies swallowed demonized retainers one after another.

The water grew blacker.

Vikir watched.

“What happens to the plants afterward?”

“Do we need to remove them?”

MiniPin shook his head.

“No.”

“The modified strain has a short lifespan.”

“Without demon blood—

they wither within a month.”

“Seeds?”

“Sterile.”

“They can’t spread into the surrounding ecosystem.”

He made a strange analogy.

“Think of them as empty shells.”

Vikir decided not to comment.

The demonized Usher fighters were disappearing.

Brainwashed ordinary knights caught in the flood—

on the other hand—

were able to cling to roots and vines without being eaten.

Then Decarabia’s eye moved.

[Something is wrong.]

Vikir looked down.

“What?”

[The lesser demons are almost gone.]

[But the main source of demonic power is still here.]

At almost the same moment—

Seere tapped Camus.

Camus frowned.

“What?”

“You want to die?”

Seere shrank back—

then still pointed.

Vikir and Camus followed the gesture.

The river churned.

Several enormous Daylilies rushed toward one point.

Something inside the vortex—

something that smelled especially desirable to them.

Then—

BOOM!

The plants exploded apart.

Petals scattered.

The largest individuals were ripped like paper.

A beam of power rose into the sky.

[Hohohoho.]

[This body has rotted so badly that even returning to my true form takes time.]

The thing that had worn **Madeline’s** body emerged.

The force behind House Usher’s collapse.

The thing that wanted Bianca’s body.

One of the great calamities of the Age of Destruction.

**<Third Corpse — Andrealphus>**

**Danger Rating:** S+  
**Size:** ?  
**Discovery Location:** Deep within the Gate of Destruction — ‘Serpent’s Womb’  
**Alias:** ‘Third Corpse’

One of the Ten Corpses.

A natural enemy of mankind.

Incomprehensible.

Supposedly unkillable.

**“Hail shall pour down.”**

**—『The Decalogue』 10:Upper—**

The **Ice Duke**.

**Andrealphus.**

The Third Corpse—

finally revealed its true form.
