[…Did he have a sister?]

Impossible.

The family she remembered—

barely produced daughters at all.

After one daughter vanished long ago—

it was spoken of almost like a curse.

So—

who was the woman in the kitchen?

A witch-costumed student.

Pale skin.

Straight nose.

Sharp jaw.

Deep eyes.

Heavy lashes.

Black hair—

like night.

Red eyes—

like rubies.

If not for one problem—

the Corpse Queen might have believed—

*he* had returned.

The problem—

was obvious.

The person in the kitchen—

looked like a woman.

[…Blood relation?]

[But that still doesn't explain—

that night.]

She muttered.

After—

*that day*—

the Corpse Queen had severed herself—

from the world.

To sink into the Abyss—

she cut every rope—

connecting her to the surface.

People.

Information.

Relationships.

Everything.

So she knew almost nothing—

about what happened to him afterward.

And—

thinking too deeply had another cost.

Pain.

A spike—

through the skull.

The Corpse Queen staggered.

Her half-dead brain—

seized.

Whenever thought reached too far—

something pushed back.

*Stop thinking about painful things.*

A sweet voice—

inside.

*I'll solve them for you.*

*Forget complicated questions.*

*React only to what is in front of you.*

The demon's whisper.

The Corpse Queen—

clenched her teeth.

[…I am not a corpse.]

[I can think.]

[I can judge.]

[I am not a puppet—

moving only by your will.]

The voice—

paused.

Silence.

The Queen covered her ears—

even though that did nothing.

[I told you.]

[I only handed you—

half of each day.]

[And I still have to fulfill my side of the contract.]

[I have to go to that tavern.]

[Do not forget—

why I agreed to you.]

The thing—

answered softly.

*Of course.*

*That is why I found your sister.*

*With my power.*

The Corpse Queen looked—

at Geronto.

Rosie.

If alive—

the girl would have been sixteen this year.

The Queen's voice—

hardened.

[Your ability.]

[“Let me meet the person I want to meet.”]

[Yes.]

[You found my sister.]

[But there was another person—

I kept searching for.]

[You know that.]

Silence.

[The greatest reason I accepted your proposal—

was to find his corpse.]

[You failed.]

[For two years—

I searched the Red and Black Mountains.]

[Everything.]

[Still nothing.]

Silence.

[Now—

whether through your power—

or fate—

I found his trail again.]

[If I go there—

I will know.]

[So do not stop me.]

For the first time—

the Corpse Queen openly resisted—

the demon inside her.

Their arrangement—

was not master and servant.

Not the usual Demon King contract—

where a dying human surrendered everything—

and the demon took the body.

This was—

reciprocal.

A bargain.

The demon tried again.

*Danger.*

*I smell danger.*

[…What?]

*That tavern.*

*A demon hunter.*

*The stench is unmistakable.*

Geronto—

nodded too.

The Queen frowned.

*Why would a demon hunter be here—*

*now?*

*What happens during the half of the day—

when I sleep?*

The arrangement worked both ways.

Half the day—

hers.

Half—

the demon's.

The Queen set her jaw.

[I came prepared.]

[What you fear—

is probably the Night Hound.]

[I will unmask him myself.]

[And I will learn who that woman is.]

Silence.

Then—

one final warning.

*Not much time.*

*Midnight is coming.*

*After midnight—*

*this body is mine.*

[…Enough.]

The Queen ended the conversation.

A crow—

crossed through the magical cherry petals.

Caw.

Her eyes—

settled.

Enough thought.

Enough doubt.

Time—

for answers.

She stepped forward.

Then—

the crowd surged.

“She's so pretty!”

“Who is that witch!?”

“That's not a sister—

that's a brother!”

“Witch!”

“Look over here!”

“Cast a spell on me!”

“Please take my drink!”

Mana-camera flashes—

everywhere.

The Corpse Queen—

became trapped—

in the crush.

The pressure squeezed her so hard that even the face beneath the skull mask bulged like a pancake.

[…These people.]

Black mana—

gathered.

Her instinctive solution—

simple.

Remove—

everything in the way.

Then—

“Stop.”

Bianca.

Still trembling.

Still—

standing there.

The Queen's brow moved.

*The girl who couldn't speak earlier?*

Bianca swallowed.

“I understand.”

“But fandom has limits.”

[…?]

“Everyone wants to get closer.”

“But you don't get to use mana—

to force through the line.”

[…??]

Sinclaire appeared—

beside Bianca.

White hair.

Sleepy eyes.

“Right.”

“Only staff get special access to our witch.”

The Corpse Queen—

stared.

[…What are these blood-smeared brats talking about?]

Bianca blinked.

“Blood-smeared?”

“You sound like you crawled out of a coffin.”

Sinclaire waved.

“Take a number.”

“Wait like everyone else.”

Enough.

The Queen released—

a fraction—

of her power.

Hiss.

Black mana.

Abyssal pressure.

Bianca.

Sinclaire.

Both—

went pale.

*Black mage.*

Not—

a licensed Imperial practitioner.

Not—

someone operating under state supervision.

The skull armor—

perhaps—

was not a costume.

“…!”

“…!”

The two first-years—

elite though they were—

could not withstand it.

Then—

another figure stepped in.

Black coat.

Nailed boots.

Chains.

Daggers.

Plague-doctor mask.

A rough voice.

“…Who are you—

making trouble on someone else's ground?”

A **Night Hound**—

stood between them.
