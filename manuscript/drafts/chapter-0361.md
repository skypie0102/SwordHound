# Chapter 361: Voluntary Escort (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/361.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 359 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Nouvelle Vague, Isabella Le Baskerville, Banshee Morgue, Mozgus Quovadis, and the remaining demon-aligned jurors. Demon true names are preserved from source/aligned witness as source-local forms pending direct Fandom access.

[Our masters are Passamonte!]

[Madeline!]

[Hobbes!]

Pedro, Isolde, and Thomas screamed over each other.

Then—

their masters’ demonic names.

[Chimeries!]

[Andrealphus!]

[Flauros!]

[We’ll tell you anything!]

[Anything!]

[What else do you want to know!?]

Their voices were desperate enough to chill anyone listening.

Vikir’s expression did not change.

“I don’t want anything else.”

“I’m enjoying myself.”

He dragged a twisted shard of metal across one of them.

Another scream.

The interrogation methods Vikir had learned in the Age of Destruction—

had broken soldiers.

Demon contractors.

Monsters wearing human bodies.

Even these three—

men who had risen high inside the Seven Families—

begged him to stop.

Isabella.

Banshee.

Mozgus.

Lovebad.

All four watched in silence.

Even Mozgus—

an experienced inquisitor—

looked disturbed by the efficiency.

Eventually—

Vikir rinsed black blood from his face with seawater.

*Useful.*

The process had not been merely stress relief.

These three served the surviving Ten Corpses directly.

They knew routes.

Contacts.

Signals.

Plans.

Vikir had extracted everything he could use.

He threw the ruined demonized men onto the deck.

THUD.

“Salt them.”

“Put them in the fish hold.”

“They must stay alive.”

“Feed them once a week.”

“A few drops of human blood mixed with alcohol will do.”

The order was so direct—

no one argued.

Isabella finally asked:

“…Where are we going now?”

Night Hound had broken his restraints.

The cage was destroyed.

He could take the ship.

Disappear.

Never allow himself to be captured again.

That was the obvious assumption.

And if Vikir wanted the vessel—

who could stop him?

The four escorts had already seen the Black Sun.

Even together—

they were not confident.

Vikir looked at Isabella.

“That is a strange question for an escort officer.”

“…What?”

“Where else?”

“To prison.”

“To Nouvelle Vague.”

Then—

as if nothing unusual had happened—

Vikir walked back into the broken cage.

Sat down.

The four escorts stared.

Isabella finally spoke.

“You aren’t going to steal the ship?”

“No.”

“I have no intention of causing unnecessary trouble for everyone else.”

The answer was perfectly reasonable.

And completely insane.

◆◆◆

Vikir kept his word.

He waited until the escorts’ mana fully recovered.

He allowed the ship to continue.

Through the storm.

Through the enormous waves.

Until they reached the destination.

A vast whirlpool opened in the sea.

**Maelstrom.**

The Navel of the Sea.

Water near the bow darkened—

not from ink—

but from impossible depth.

The ship threw dozens of anchors into surrounding reefs.

Only then—

did it stop being dragged toward the center.

Isabella pointed toward the vortex.

“It opens only around midnight.”

“This is the intermediate gate to Nouvelle Vague.”

The prison existed roughly ten thousand meters below.

Prisoners did not descend by stairs.

Or elevator.

Or ship.

They were thrown in.

Alone.

Many died from pressure.

Many broke under claustrophobia.

Only a small fraction reached the prison alive.

Vikir stepped toward a coffin fixed near the bow.

**Iron Maiden.**

A human-shaped metal coffin.

Its exterior carried the face of a gentle woman.

Its interior—

was filled with alloy spikes.

When the lid closed—

those spikes entered the body.

Not through vital organs.

That would be merciful.

They were placed to maximize pain.

Blood then flowed through tiny openings near the maiden’s eyes—

creating the appearance that the statue itself wept for the condemned.

Vikir wore a new set of BDISSEM handcuffs.

New ankle restraints.

New chains.

He entered the coffin voluntarily.

Then looked at the four escorts.

“Close it.”

The calmness of the request—

made all four uneasy.

Banshee stepped forward.

“…Vikir.”

“Yes?”

Banshee paused.

Then forced himself onward.

“I am a professor at Colosseo.”

“And you are a student of Colosseo.”

“….”

“Whatever you are now.”

“Whatever crimes are written beside your name.”

“You are still my student.”

He took a breath.

“I’m not speaking as an official.”

“Or as an escort.”

“I’m speaking as your teacher.”

Then—

“Run.”

Isabella.

Mozgus.

Lovebad.

All turned toward him.

Banshee did not back down.

“I still don’t understand everything.”

“Demons.”

“The Hell Tree.”

“Good and evil.”

“I thought I was old enough to understand the world.”

“Apparently not.”

“But I watched you at the Academy.”

“You are not evil.”

“That much I know.”

“Nouvelle Vague is living hell.”

“People who enter do not return.”

“I cannot knowingly send a brave young man there.”

Mozgus stepped forward next.

“I heard Dolores’s full account.”

“At first—

I did not believe it.”

“A demon hunter?”

“Demons embedded all through human society?”

“As a priest—

I found that almost impossible to accept.”

He looked toward the three demonized prisoners.

“Now I’ve seen them with my own eyes.”

“And I understand.”

“You need to live.”

Lovebad stepped forward.

“Do you remember my relative, Merelini Lovegood?”

“She fell for you during the University League.”

“Since learning who you are—

and where you’re being sent—

she barely eats.”

He looked embarrassed.

“But that isn’t the main point.”

“When we return—

we’ll use these demonized men as evidence.”

“We’ll demand a retrial.”

“So escape now.”

“We can erase your trail.”

“Money won’t be a problem.”

Three Great Houses—

Morgue.

Quovadis.

Bourgeois.

Their escorts—

were asking the prisoner to flee.

Only Isabella remained.

She had said nothing.

Then raised her head.

“Actually…”

“I received another order.”

Vikir looked at her.

“Separate from the Imperial transfer.”

Isabella’s voice became cold.

“Baskerville ordered me to kill everyone on this ship if necessary.”

“And free you.”

Silence.

Banshee.

Mozgus.

Lovebad.

All three stared.

Isabella continued.

“I brought core Doberman Knights disguised as ordinary sailors.”

“The Head ordered that even if I had to sink the convoy—

you were not to enter Nouvelle Vague.”

Baskerville had been willing to make enemies of the Imperial Family—

and the other Great Houses—

to retrieve Vikir.

Banshee rubbed his forehead.

“…We almost died for reasons we didn’t even know.”

Mozgus sighed.

“That explains why you weren’t more shocked when demons appeared.”

Lovebad muttered:

“Baskerville really is insane.”

Vikir looked at Isabella.

“Earlier—

you told me I should kill myself.”

“That was for public ears.”

“You were never going to do it.”

Now all four urged him to escape.

The storm had forced most sailors below deck.

The three demonized conspirators were salted and locked away.

Only these four escorts would witness Vikir’s supposed final moments.

If they agreed on a story—

Vikir could disappear.

The opportunity was perfect.

Vikir answered:

“No.”

Banshee stared.

“What?”

“I refuse.”

“You know what Nouvelle Vague is!”

“It’s worse than death!”

Vikir’s voice did not change.

“If I wanted to escape—

I could have done so at any point.”

“Why do you think I surrendered?”

He looked toward the black whirlpool.

Then smiled faintly.

“I have been waiting nineteen years—

to go to Nouvelle Vague.”
