# Chapter 484: The Marquis of Discord (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/484.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 482 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established project terminology controls First Corpse Andras, Marquis of Discord, Vikir, Beelzebub, Baskerville Eighth Form, Asmodeus, Kirko Grimm, and the demon-contract framework. The flaming sword’s specific name is not introduced until the next source beat and is not imported early.

**First Corpse Andras.**

Another name—

**Marquis of Discord.**

White feathered wings gave him an almost sacred silhouette.

A raven’s head.

Owl-like eyes.

The lower body of a wolf.

One blazing sword in his hand.

A destroyer eternally dissatisfied.

A being that hated harmony.

Loved disorder.

And flourished wherever confusion met chaos.

SHING.

Vikir drew Beelzebub.

Andras smiled.

[Hehehe.]

[Why so tense?]

[There is no need.]

[I only revealed my true form so we could speak honestly.]

[Communication is difficult when both sides hide themselves.]

“….”

[We each want something.]

[Those goals do not necessarily conflict.]

[So put down the sword.]

Vikir almost found the situation absurd.

A demon revealing its true body—

and still refusing to fight.

*An ordinary demon hunter might fall for this.*

Vikir would not.

He remembered Andras from before regression.

Marquis of Discord.

A monster that had swept across battlefields and killed countless heroes of the Human Alliance.

A friendly smile did not change what stood before him.

Andras sighed.

[This is my first time showing my real body without immediately fighting too.]

[But conversation requires concessions.]

[I am currently at a disadvantage.]

“….”

[So.]

[Can we return to my offer?]

His eyes shone.

[I can send you back to your original world.]

[I can restore some of those you remember.]

“No.”

Vikir’s answer remained unchanged.

“There is no true method to return the dead to life.”

“Even demons cannot ignore the natural order.”

His blade rose.

“And demons die.”

“That is also natural order.”

A hound hunted.

Prey and hound did not negotiate.

Conversation ended.

Sword began.

FLASH!

Vikir opened with everything.

Baskerville Eighth Form.

A Black Sun gathered from the fangs of the sky.

BOOOOM!

The throne—

already damaged—

collapsed like soft clay.

[Ah.]

[If that’s your choice—]

[then I suppose we fight.]

Andras swung the burning sword.

A slash like flowing flame crossed the hall.

Vikir dodged.

The floor behind him split—

then melted.

Eighth Form and infernal fire collided.

The air itself tore.

Bright cracks appeared everywhere.

[What a shame.]

Andras laughed while forcing Vikir backward.

[Half of what I offered was true.]

Vikir knew the method.

Half truth.

Half lie.

The oldest structure of demonic temptation.

“You came here to use humans as food.”

“Making a deal with you is absurd.”

[Really?]

Andras tilted his head.

[I think the opposite.]

“…?”

[Demons can make humanity prosper.]

[In a broad sense—]

[we are beneficial.]

“Bullshit.”

[No.]

[You misunderstand our purpose.]

Andras continued fighting—

and persuading.

[Do you know chickens?]

“….”

Of course.

One of humanity’s most familiar livestock animals.

[Humans breed them.]

[Slaughter them.]

[Eat them.]

[Do you think chickens are unhappy?]

“Yes.”

[No.]

[Not if you view the species instead of the individual.]

Andras smiled.

[Before humans bred them—]

[chickens were smaller.]

[weaker.]

[more vulnerable.]

[Left entirely to nature, perhaps they would have disappeared.]

[Most young would be eaten before maturity.]

“….”

[Then humans chose them.]

[Protected them.]

[Multiplied them.]

[Now their numbers exceed almost every other bird.]

[From the standpoint of the species—]

[was domestication inefficient?]

“What are you trying to say?”

Vikir slashed.

Andras evaded—

then leaned close.

[Humans are weak too.]

[And life exhausts you.]

[War.]

[Beasts.]

[Famine.]

[Drought.]

[Flood.]

[Fire.]

[Hunger.]

[Disease.]

[Then competition with your own kind.]

[Fighting.]

[Winning.]

[Trying to build a home.]

[Raise children.]

[Support aging parents.]

[Always exhausted.]

“….”

[People compete for partners.]

[Compete through examinations.]

[Compete for social recognition.]

[Work through night after night.]

[And where is the promised happiness at the end?]

“….”

[Did you choose to be born?]

[No.]

[Humans are forced into a difficult and lonely world.]

[Then forced to die.]

Andras leapt backward.

Returned to the broken throne.

Spread his arms.

[And even that describes the fortunate ones.]

[Most children do not live long.]

[Infants die.]

[Orphans die.]

[War.]

[Disease.]

[Hunger.]

[You saw what happened in Dantalian’s orphanage.]

Darkness gathered around him.

Vikir cut through it.

The fog scattered—

then thickened.

[Human desire exceeds human ability.]

[Lust.]

[Hunger.]

[Greed.]

[Envy.]

[Wrath.]

[Sloth.]

[Pride.]

[None can ever be completely satisfied.]

[People gain intelligence enough to understand everything they lack.]

[Then live in inequality and injustice—]

[feeling inferiority, guilt, failure.]

[Brief happiness only interrupts the dissatisfaction.]

Born by force.

Dying by force.

Suffering as children.

Suffering as adults.

Suffering at the end.

Andras smiled.

[So I offer a better arrangement.]

[Most humans now die hungry and poor.]

[Many before thirty.]

[Some before ten.]

[Wouldn’t becoming demon livestock be happier?]

“Bullshit.”

[You can say that.]

[Would the children dying in alleys say the same?]

[The children suffering in orphanages?]

Andras parried another attack.

[Under demons—]

[humans could live healthy lives beyond one hundred years.]

[Eat.]

[Stay warm.]

[Breed freely.]

[No disease.]

[No war.]

[No famine.]

[They could pursue happiness.]

[Then at one hundred—]

[offer the soul to demons.]

[The body is slaughtered.]

[Like livestock classified by age and growth.]

His smile widened.

[Is that so different from your idea of salvation?]

[How different is it from a god?]

Darkness thickened.

Andras’s mouth became a red slit within it.

[Long ago—]

[a human proposed a similar arrangement first.]

Vikir stared.

[That was “God.”]

Andras laughed.

[And that god was worse than me.]

[It promised peace and happiness—]

[then failed to provide them.]

[It even took humans before they matured.]

[Yet humanity still follows one-sided contracts.]

“What do you want?”

[What I already told you.]

[My brother.]

[I need to find him.]

Andras’s face briefly shifted back into the Crown Prince’s.

[Help me.]

[And I will make you administrator of the system.]

[Emperor.]

[As the Imperial family once was.]

Vikir raised his sword.

“You learned bad ideas.”

[…What?]

“That logic.”

“It belongs to the old Warring States age.”

“The sort of idea dead demonic hegemonies used.”

Beelzebub’s tip leveled directly at Andras.

“Human beings cannot be permanently contained by a livestock system.”

“Desire to improve.”

“To rise.”

“Even when suppressed—”

“it eventually pierces through.”

“Like an awl in a pocket.”

“That is human.”

BOOM!

The throne disintegrated completely.

Andras emerged through dust—

wings open.

“Demons die.”

[You really cannot be reasoned with.]

Sword met sword.

Fire and crimson aura exploded.

Vikir thought—

*Good.*

*He only has one sword.*

Before regression—

Andras had fought with two demonic blades.

One of them—

Asmodeus.

Now—

Asmodeus belonged to Kirko.

Andras gathered darkness dense enough to swallow Vikir’s aura.

[Very well.]

[If you refuse to make a contract—]

[I will make you beg for one.]

Vikir tightened his grip.

A demon did not need a sword to be dangerous.

Andras’s true authority—

was beginning.
