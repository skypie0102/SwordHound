# Chapter 376: Poseidon (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/376.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 374 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Vikir, Decarabia, BDISSEM, Hell Tree resistances, Bastille, D'Ordume D'Orcdile, Souaré, Nouvelle Vague, Level Ten, and Poseidon.

“What happened?”

Bastille hurried toward the labor zone.

Captains pointed toward the deep pit.

“There was an unexplained explosion in the center of Level Nine.”

“The scale is different from previous incidents.”

“It feels ominous.”

“We can’t investigate yet.”

“The lava and gas make another collapse likely.”

Bastille listened.

Then said coldly:

“Send prisoners.”

The guards hesitated.

“What?”

“But if they go down now—

they could be buried alive.”

“That is why I said prisoners.”

“Offer them one day free from labor.”

“Add a few loaves of bread.”

“You’ll get volunteers.”

Silence.

Even in Nouvelle Vague—

the idea of selling a prisoner’s life for rest and bread made some guards hesitate.

Then—

“I’ll go.”

Vikir stepped forward.

Even Bastille looked surprised.

“You?”

“What are you plotting?”

“Nothing.”

“I want bread.”

“…Hmm.”

Bastille considered him.

Vikir had caused trouble once—

when he first arrived.

Since then—

two years of perfect behavior.

No incidents.

No disobedience.

Eventually—

Bastille nodded.

“Fine.”

“Can you get through that fire and gas?”

“If you remove the BDISSEM restraints—

probably.”

“Don’t play games with me.”

“I’m joking.”

“I’ll be back.”

Dry as ever.

Vikir entered the pit.

BOOM!

Violent gas erupted from below.

Flames.

Lava.

Even Vikir struggled.

His Hell Tree physical and magical resistances mattered now.

“Decarabia.”

“Can you make a barrier?”

[Without mana—

only briefly.]

“That’s enough.”

A small shield formed around him.

Only several meters wide.

Vikir pushed forward behind it.

Lava reached his knees.

*I can’t stay here long.*

Without mana—

his legs would eventually burn away completely.

He moved faster.

Sulfur clouded the air.

Flames surged.

Lava cast brilliant red light.

Then—

a huge shadow appeared.

“…!”

Vikir recognized it immediately.

The first time he had seen it with his own eyes.

But there could be no mistake.

At the center of Nouvelle Vague.

Deepest Level Nine.

A blue sphere burned inside the magma.

About five meters across.

Oval.

Covered in blue fire.

Like an egg.

Vikir looked up quietly.

“…Poseidon.”

He reached out.

Touched the surface.

Beneath the sphere—

root-like channels of aura spread through the ground.

Even minor contact caused violent resistance.

BOOM!

RUMBLE!

Explosions erupted around it.

Vikir drew back his fist.

Then struck.

THUD!

Something strange happened.

The sphere absorbed the impact.

WHOOSH.

The blue flames grew—

only slightly.

The surrounding light brightened too.

“As expected.”

“It grows by absorbing external impact.”

Finding Poseidon—

was not the end.

It had to be fed.

Hit.

Struck.

Again.

Again.

Until it matured.

*The idea of hatching from both inside and outside isn’t wrong.*

Vikir stroked his chin.

Discovering Poseidon had required many people digging—

even if they did not know why.

Activating it—

would also require more than one person.

*But finding it means ninety percent is done.*

*The rest is time.*

Vikir already had a plan.

Then—

he turned back.

Step.

Step.

Step.

Through the lava swamp.

When Vikir emerged—

everyone stared.

“You’re alive?!”

“How did you go in there?”

“You went straight into hell and came back!”

Vikir ignored them.

“There’s a strange lava mass inside.”

“It seems to absorb impacts.”

“…Absorb?”

Bastille frowned.

An explosive object would have been bad enough.

Something that absorbed impact—

was worse.

Especially for people who needed to excavate tunnels.

As smoke and steam thinned—

the blue sphere became visible.

Blue flame.

Aura roots extending underground.

Violent explosions from even slight contact.

Bastille raised his greatsword.

Then struck.

BOOOOM!

The blue sphere absorbed even his aura attack.

One guard stared.

“It absorbed everything!”

“Wait.”

“Doesn’t it look slightly larger now?”

“It really does look like an egg.”

The guards began murmuring.

The unidentified object sat directly in the middle of the work site.

Construction could not proceed normally.

Pickaxes.

Shovels.

Blows from tools.

Poseidon absorbed them all.

Worse—

its root-like aura veins spread through the earth.

Touching one—

caused another explosion.

Bastille’s expression darkened.

“If touching a tiny vein does this…”

“What happens if the main body explodes?”

At best—

Nouvelle Vague itself might be destroyed.

At worst—

the volcano everyone believed dead—

might awaken.

“Damn it.”

“What if Nouvelle Vague isn’t fully extinct?”

The aura veins.

Sulfur gas.

Steam.

Boiling magma.

Now that Bastille looked closely—

it did not feel like harmless leftover geothermal heat.

“Call the geologists.”

“We need a full investigation.”

“We have to confirm whether Nouvelle Vague is actually an extinct volcano—”

“What nonsense.”

A large man in black uniform approached.

**D'Ordume D'Orcdile.**

Bastille immediately straightened.

Saluted.

D'Ordume waved casually.

“Say that again.”

“Full geological survey?”

“Yes, Colonel.”

“An unidentified object appeared in the Level Nine labor zone…”

D'Ordume frowned.

“Show me.”

Bastille brought him to the sphere.

“It absorbs every impact.”

“The aura channels beneath it explode when touched.”

“And the sphere absorbs those explosions too.”

“If enough force accumulates inside—

it may all discharge at once.”

“If Nouvelle Vague is dormant rather than extinct—”

“Nonsense.”

“Nouvelle Vague is extinct.”

D'Ordume cut him off.

“Do not delay construction because of uncertainty.”

“Do you know how long a full survey would take?”

“At least ten years.”

“But—”

“And who pays the astronomical cost?”

“You?”

Bastille fell silent.

D'Ordume drove the point home.

“Labor proceeds as planned.”

“You will meet the deadline.”

“….”

“This is for your benefit too.”

“The sooner Level Ten is completed—

the sooner I beat that woman Souaré in the promotion evaluations.”

“If I become the next prison head—

you rise faster too.”

Bastille nodded.

“Yes, Colonel.”

“Besides.”

“If there is an accident—

only prisoners die.”

“If you’re worried—

keep the guards away from the area.”

A dark light entered D'Ordume’s eyes.

“Personnel evaluation season is near.”

“….”

“Souaré has Angajuman in isolation.”

“She does almost nothing—

yet gets near-perfect evaluations for that one thing.”

“….”

“And me?”

“I suppress riots personally.”

“I secure food outside.”

“I work constantly as the current head’s right hand.”

“….”

“Then my quarterly score ends up close to hers.”

“How is that fair?”

“Why should she stand beside me as a candidate for the next prison head?”

Bastille had never heard D'Ordume talk so much.

D'Ordume ground his teeth.

“So the Level Ten project under me will finish on schedule.”

“No matter what.”

“That is the only way I surpass Souaré.”

“When I become the next head—

you become the next warden.”

“Understand?”

“Yes, Colonel.”

D'Ordume nodded.

Satisfied.

Then disappeared down the corridor.

Bastille remained.

Uneasy.

*I have a terrible feeling.*

*If that thing explodes—

destroying Nouvelle Vague may be the smaller problem.*

*It might affect the climate above the surface.*

He thought for a long time.

Then shook his head.

*No.*

*I always worry too much.*

Labor would resume normally tomorrow.

With D'Ordume and Souaré competing for the top post—

Bastille had little room to resist.

And—

hidden behind a stone pillar—

Vikir listened to everything.

Then quietly disappeared into the dark.

A bag of bread—

his reward—

in one hand.

*For me—

this is perfect.*
