FLASH!

Vikir’s slash struck the center of the vortex.

A black pillar of aura drove vertically through the sea.

“AAAAAAAGH!”

Black Tongue’s scream became bubbles.

BOOM!

The severed section of corridor shattered apart.

Black Tongue—

caught in the blast—

was hurled deeper into the abyss.

At ten thousand meters below the surface—

exposure to the open sea meant death.

Even a superhuman body could not withstand that pressure for long.

Then—

slurp.

A transparent membrane descended.

Flubber’s mucus spread over Nouvelle Vague’s damaged exterior.

Whenever the prison’s shell was breached—

the substance sealed the opening and slowed the incoming water.

The torrent weakened.

Water drained down the sloped corridor and spiral stairs.

Vikir stood on the soaked stone floor—

looking through the transparent membrane.

Something moved outside.

Fast.

Very fast.

[…ROOOOAR!]

A huge shape rushed out of the darkness.

At first glance—

it resembled Black Tongue.

Then Vikir looked again.

No.

Black Tongue was already dead.

The thing swimming toward Nouvelle Vague had lost any true human shape.

Countless leech-like tendrils had fused into a long body.

A grotesque sea serpent.

Or deep-sea eel.

Its melted face opened into a mouth packed with irregular teeth.

*Asmodeus.*

That was what remained.

The demonic sword had consumed all of Black Tongue’s blood after the pressure ruptured his lungs.

His desire.

His obsession.

His resentment.

Everything became nourishment.

And before it could dry again—

Asmodeus restored its original living form.

[ROOOOAR!]

The monster opened its jaws.

Vikir clicked his tongue.

“So this is what a sword looks like after eating its master.”

Beelzebub vibrated in his right hand.

Vikir tapped the blade once.

“Don’t even think about it.”

His eyes glowed red.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

**Black Sun.**

Eight fangs curved into a sphere.

The attack devoured the space ahead.

Asmodeus twisted violently—

but could not escape.

CRACK!

CRUNCH!

The demonic sword shattered.

When the black sphere dispersed—

nine broken blade fragments remained.

The madness was gone.

They sank silently into the deep sea—

like cooled pieces of a fallen star.

“…A dog that bites its master gets boiled.”

Vikir watched them disappear.

One of the Seven Demon Swords—

destroyed.

Six remained.

*Though with something that tenacious—

I can’t say it is truly dead forever.*

Could anyone retrieve and use the fragments?

Perhaps.

But Vikir had little interest.

Handling even one demonic sword carried enough risk.

Trying to command multiple such weapons at once—

was madness.

He let Asmodeus sink.

Instead—

he looked at what he had actually taken.

A heavy leather pouch.

Inside—

black egg sacs.

The doppelganger-leech strain.

The same parasite type that had attached itself to Kirko—

slowly copying her body.

Given enough time—

it might have become indistinguishable from her.

“This may be more useful than the Blood Daylily.”

Vikir opened Andromalius’s dimensional barrier.

Stored the eggs.

Then closed it again.

*Now for BDISSEM.*

The final stage was beginning.

Vikir turned away.

His next objective—

destroy every control system that kept Nouvelle Vague functioning.

◆◆◆

The **Gate of Good and Evil** opened.

A roar of seawater poured downward.

Iron Maidens dropped onto the first-floor receiving area one after another.

At the same time—

the Gulper Eels that had carried guards from the surface reached the fifth-floor dock.

“Open the coffins!”

Bastille ordered.

Guards forced open the Iron Maidens.

Prisoners were dragged out.

Many were barely conscious.

Some were already dead.

Others had been reduced by the descent into masses of blood and flesh.

“Feed that one to Brigadier General Flubber.”

“This one’s alive!”

“Send him to induction—”

“…Never mind.”

“He just died.”

“Stack the corpses separately!”

“Anyone raving gets the whip!”

Clubs cracked.

Whips snapped.

The surviving prisoners staggered toward the induction area.

There were so many arrivals—

the guards were overwhelmed.

Then—

THUD!

The final Iron Maiden landed.

Several guards moved toward it.

“Stop!”

“Do not touch that one!”

Two voices came from behind.

Colonel D’Ordume.

Colonel Souaré.

Both had just returned.

They took hold of the last Iron Maiden themselves.

“This prisoner is Level Nine.”

“And because of the severity of her crime—

she will be transferred directly to Level Ten once construction is complete.”

Every nearby guard swallowed.

What kind of person required two Wardens to escort her personally?

D’Ordume turned toward Bastille.

“Warden Orca will arrive shortly.”

“Prepare the welcome ceremony.”

“Yes!”

Bastille snapped around.

“Garam!”

“Where is Major Garam?”

No answer.

The reception area was too chaotic.

Too many new inmates.

Too many guards running in every direction.

Then Bastille spotted Kirko.

“Captain Kirko!”

She looked over.

“Find Major Garam!”

“The Warden is nearly here.”

“We need the welcome ceremony ready.”

“And this is a good opportunity for the two of you to show your faces to him.”

“Yes.”

“I’ll find him immediately.”

Kirko left the induction area.

Garam had already ordered her toward the fifth-floor kennel.

This gave her a legitimate reason to go.

She descended quickly.

Several corridors.

Several bridges.

Toward the Gulper Eel pens.

Drip.

Drip.

She passed junior guards she had known before promotion.

“Captain Kirko!”

“Salute!”

One of them looked surprised.

“Captain—

are you going to the fifth floor too?”

Kirko stopped.

“What do you mean?”

“Major Garam came through earlier.”

“He told us not to stay at our posts.”

“Said we should get as close to the kennel as possible.”

“…!”

Kirko’s expression hardened.

Something was happening.

Something serious.

“Do not blindly trust the Major’s order.”

She ran forward.

Two junior guards tried to stop her.

“Captain!”

“The area ahead is restricted to Major rank and above—”

They never finished.

BOOM!

A black corona rose beyond the distant windows.

The shockwave shook the entire fifth floor.
