“…What are you doing?”

**Dolores L. Quovadis.**

Student council president.

Youngest early entrant in the Academy.

Top-ranked third-year.

Saintess of **Quovadis**—

one of the Empire's Seven Great Houses.

And—

one of the most influential young nobles in the Empire.

The six second-years—

froze.

Older than Dolores in school years—

but beneath her in—

ability.

grades.

family power.

authority.

Anyone with sense—

would understand the danger.

Dolores looked at the bowls in her hands.

Food.

Water.

For stray animals.

Then—

at the wounded black dog.

Kicked.

Spat on.

Cut with broken glass.

Burned with cigarettes.

One student swallowed.

“…How long were you watching?”

Dolores answered—

with a face none of them had seen before.

“I didn't see the beginning.”

Relief.

For half a second.

Then—

“I saw enough—

to despise you.”

Their faces went white.

Dolores was known for kindness.

Warm smile.

Gentle voice.

The perfect image—

of a Saintess.

Now—

she looked cold enough to freeze the air.

Someone who rarely became angry—

was often more frightening when anger finally came.

Dolores walked through them.

Bent down.

Lifted Vikir.

Carefully.

As if afraid that even holding him—

might hurt.

“That hurt—

didn't it?”

Her voice carried—

faint holy power.

Even hearing it—

eased some pain.

The six students began—

quietly—

to retreat.

Their expressions said everything.

*Wrong person.*

*Dolores hates this.*

*We should have killed the dog before she saw it.*

Dolores looked up.

“…Do you know that animal abuse can be an early warning sign for later violent crime?”

Everyone stiffened.

One tried to defend himself.

“That's too far.”

“We're Academy elites.”

Another nodded quickly.

“We were under academic stress.”

“We were rough with one ownerless dog.”

“That's all.”

A third added—

“We admit we were drunk.”

“But it's a filthy stray.”

“It could spread disease.”

Dolores became—

colder.

“Elite?”

“You?”

Silence.

“This Academy exists to train people—

who will carry responsibility for the Empire.”

“To graduate from here—

and take responsibility for the state—

you must learn to be gentle toward the weak—

and firm toward the strong.”

Her conviction—

felt harder than steel.

“But you are the opposite.”

“Cruel toward the weak.”

“Cowardly toward the strong.”

“There are already enough people like that outside these walls.”

“Why should the Empire entrust power—

to more of them?”

Unworthy nobles.

Bad seedlings.

People who called themselves elite—

but would only hurt more people—

the higher they climbed.

Dolores had no patience—

for them.

“As student council president—

I am assigning demerits to all six of you.”

“All of the disciplinary authority available to me this year—

will be used.”

That—

was not a small threat.

Colosseo granted the student council significant autonomy.

A determined president—

could push multiple students toward expulsion.

Powerful families—

might challenge such punishment.

These six—

did not have powerful families.

Minor countryside nobility—

could not casually confront Quovadis.

Only then—

did the six understand.

“President!”

“Please!”

“Expulsion over a dog!?”

“I'm from the noble faction!”

“Please have mercy!”

They dropped to their knees.

Admission to Colosseo—

cost a fortune.

Graduation—

opened wealth.

status.

career.

Expulsion—

turned honor into humiliation.

And antagonizing Quovadis—

could destroy an Imperial career before it began.

Dolores looked down at them.

Unmoved.

“Did any of you—

show mercy to this helpless dog?”

No answer.

No negotiation.

◆◆◆

Warm water.

Vikir sank into it.

Splash.

His tail—

wagged despite himself.

Apparently—

even the Night Hound—

could appreciate a hot bath.

Especially while wounded.

Especially while trapped—

in the body of a black puppy.

Vikir closed his eyes.

Remembered Dolores's words.

*Did you show mercy?*

Her anger—

had been unusually decisive.

For a moment—

Vikir had seen the future woman—

known in the Age of Destruction as the **Iron Saintess**.

That surprised him.

Before the war—

people often criticized Dolores—

for hesitating too much.

Dantalian—

had changed her.

Vikir sank deeper.

The water—

carried faint holy power.

Dolores had blessed it.

Almost—

holy water.

His body recovered steadily.

And Vikir—

returned to the problem that mattered.

The Eighth Corpse.

The Corpse Queen.

He had almost killed her.

Almost—

was failure.

*If my realm had been higher…*

A **Peak Sword Graduator** could represent a nation.

That still was not enough—

to hunt a Demon King alone.

And the Corpse Queen—

had been incomplete.

Only partially capable of using Seere.

Against Andromalius—

Vikir had Baskerville forces.

Against Dantalian—

Dolores's Saintess blessing.

To kill the Ten Corpses alone—

he needed more.

So—

the path to **Sword Master**.

And—

completion of the **Seventh Fang**.

Baskerville's sword—

resembled the jaws of a hunting hound.

Upper jaw.

Lower jaw.

Different teeth—

different functions.

The **Sixth Fang** and **Seventh Fang**—

had to interlock perfectly.

Vikir's seventh tooth—

still too small.

Too unstable.

The manuals—

contained a contradiction.

The Sixth Fang teaching said:

> To cross the wall of the Sixth Fang—
> abandon the human emotions of joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure.

Then the Seventh Fang teaching said:

> To reach the Seventh Fang—
> recover the human emotions of joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure.

Discard emotion—

to reach the peak of Graduator.

Recover emotion—

to become Sword Master.

*What does that mean?*

*Throw them away—

then find them again?*

Before regression—

Vikir had reached Graduator territory.

Battle.

Death.

Comrades lost—

again and again.

He became precise.

Cold.

Efficient.

A weapon.

Graduator—

came naturally.

Sword Master—

never did.

He had neither theory—

nor method.

Now—

the road existed.

Hugo's Seventh Fang.

His own incomplete version.

A path beyond.

*To become iron—

discard emotion.*

*To go beyond iron—

need emotion again?*

Hugo's training—

had taught Baskerville hounds to erase feeling.

Useful.

Fast.

Efficient.

Perfect—

for producing large numbers of functional Graduators.

But perhaps—

that training itself—

created the ceiling.

Vikir needed to change.

Not merely more fighting.

Not merely more killing.

He had to understand—

how to revive emotions worn dry by war.

Then—

creak.

Vikir's ears rose.

The bathroom door—

opened.

“…!”

For perhaps the first time that morning—

his complicated thoughts vanished completely.

A cheerful voice entered.

“Choco!”

“I'm coming in too.”

“Let's take a bath together!”

Dolores—

entered the bathroom.
