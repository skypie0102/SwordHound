The entire Imperial Capital was in an uproar.

The head of House Bourgeois—

Bartolomeo—

had been assassinated.

And Humbert—

a cardinal of the Faithful Holy House Quovadis—

had disappeared.

The culprit was identified as the **Night Hound**.

According to the public story—

Night Hound had broken into the dinner where Bartolomeo was entertaining Academy students.

He took Saintess Dolores hostage.

Then—

during the chaos—

Bartolomeo sacrificed himself heroically to save the students.

A sacred and noble death.

At least—

that was the official version.

Unfortunately—

most of the survivors remembered almost nothing.

Pigi said he had fallen asleep before anything happened.

Sinclaire—

who had been present—

was diagnosed with aphasia and short-term memory loss caused by excessive sleeping medicine, a blow to the head, and psychological shock.

Dolores—

the hostage herself—

said she had been blindfolded and seen nothing.

Acting Head **Damien Bourgeois** then released a statement.

**“The situation will be brought under control as quickly as possible. A successor candidate already exists, so the confusion surrounding the headship will not last long.”**

And with that—

the incident was temporarily closed.

◆◆◆

No matter how enormous a national event might be—

ordinary people still tended to care more about whatever problem was directly in front of them.

At Colosseo Academy—

that meant final exams.

And parent visitation day.

Even an assassination scandal involving one of the Seven Great Houses could not compete with the immediate terror of exams.

“Start repairing the arena!”

“Check the magic stones ahead of time!”

“The parent route is a mess!”

“Plant more trees!”

“If we’re planting them, use big ones!”

“Scatter flower seeds too!”

The entire Academy had become a construction site.

The moment Headmaster Winston returned—

he began a huge beautification project.

Trees.

Flowers.

Soft music throughout campus.

He had also expressed regret over the magic-barrier failure during the midterms—

then ordered even more magic stones brought in to reinforce it.

Beautification.

Security.

Those were Winston’s two priorities.

The students were divided.

“Why start massive construction the moment he gets back?”

“He was away too long.”

“He needs achievements quickly.”

“Half the school is already trees!”

“Why plant even more?”

“Did you see how many magic stones arrived?”

“The barrier is going to be ridiculous now.”

“He only cares because parents are visiting.”

“They say representatives from all Seven Great Houses are coming.”

“I heard some family heads may come personally.”

◆◆◆

Dolores had also returned to work.

Suppliers.

Magic stones.

Landscaping trees.

Price negotiations.

Transport.

Placement.

Scheduling.

She handled everything.

“Parent visitation is approaching.”

“Many senior figures from the Seven Great Houses and other institutions will come.”

“Do not let a single detail slip.”

The student council worked frantically.

Most Houses would send representatives.

But occasionally—

the actual head of a great family came in person.

That possibility forced everyone to be even more careful.

Dolores watched the work continue.

Then sighed inwardly.

*I need to see Pigi and Sinclaire soon.*

She had seen Pigi several times since the Bourgeois incident.

Sinclaire—

not once.

The girl who had always been diligent—

had stopped attending class.

Stopped working part-time.

Stopped volunteering.

Stopped joining club activities.

She remained inside the dormitory almost constantly.

Everyone noticed.

Everyone talked.

*She must be confused.*

*She saw someone die in front of her.*

Dolores had seen countless people suffer and die while serving as a Saintess.

She had some resistance to that kind of trauma.

Sinclaire—

as far as Dolores publicly knew—

was an ordinary commoner student who had lived a comparatively clean life.

Of course she would be shaken.

*At least she said nothing to reporters.*

Sinclaire was smart.

She was not the kind of person to blurt out something explosive in an interview without understanding the consequences.

Then Dolores remembered something.

*Bianca shares a room with her.*

But lately—

Bianca and Sinclaire had not been getting along.

Bianca rarely returned to the room.

Dolores made up her mind.

*No.*

*I’m going tonight.*

She would visit Sinclaire personally.

But first—

the paperwork.

“All right.”

“One more push.”

Dolores picked up her pen.

◆◆◆

**<Ranking Battle Schedule Notice>**

The first-years’ final exams began.

One week later—

most results were already decided.

The Ranking Battle used midterm performance to divide students into comparable tiers.

Students within each tier then fought practical matches for additional points.

Winners advanced.

Losers stayed where they were.

Simple.

Brutal.

Effective.

Vikir—

the overall midterm leader—

defeated Lowbro and Middlebro from the Cold Department.

Then lost to Highbro.

A result respectable enough to preserve appearances.

Bianca defeated Sancho in a hard fight and advanced to the final—

where she would face Tudor.

In the Hot Department—

Granola de Leviathan and Sinclaire advanced to the final.

Tudor versus Bianca.

Granola versus Sinclaire.

Both championship matches would be held in front of the visiting parents.

The four finalists trained obsessively.

But Sinclaire—

more than anyone—

made people uneasy.

PSSSHH—

The gravity-chamber door opened.

Sinclaire stepped out.

Drenched in sweat.

Black sleeveless shirt.

Combat trousers.

No mana.

Eleven times normal gravity.

She dropped into a chair.

“…Haa.”

Nobody approached.

During the days she had hidden in her room—

something had changed.

Once the final exams began—

Sinclaire emerged again.

Cold.

Silent.

Merciless.

Every Hot Department elite in her bracket—

gone in under a minute.

Students whose grades and ability should have put them near her level—

flattened.

Sleep.

Eat.

Train.

Sleep.

Eat.

Train.

Nothing else.

She stopped talking to friends.

Stopped volunteering.

Letters from the orphanage—

children saying they missed her—

were crumpled unopened.

All part-time work—

abandoned.

Even Bianca—

her roommate—

received only silence.

Sinclaire had become another person.

Then—

“…!”

Her head snapped up.

Another gravity-chamber door opened.

PSSSHH—

Thirteen times normal gravity.

No mana.

Only the body.

Vikir walked out through the steam—

sweat running down him.

Sinclaire spoke immediately.

“…Bro is still amazing.”

She had been cutting everyone else out of her life.

Vikir—

was the only person she still approached.

He nodded and wiped the sweat from his face.

Sinclaire jumped to her feet and followed.

“What does thirteen times gravity feel like?”

“How do you last without mana?”

“What do you eat?”

“When do you sleep?”

“What kind of training made you this strong?”

“You lost to Highbro on purpose, right?”

“You didn’t want attention?”

To Vikir—

she almost seemed unchanged.

Still curious.

Still energetic.

Still talking rapidly.

But then—

her tone changed.

“Bro.”

“What are you doing tonight?”

“Assignments.”

“You won’t work all night.”

“They aren’t even that hard.”

“I have other subjects.”

“Night work too.”

“…Tch.”

Sinclaire looked dissatisfied.

“What about really late?”

“Dawn?”

“You barely sleep anyway.”

“At dawn—

the assignments should be finished.”

“Why?”

Vikir asked directly.

Sinclaire hesitated.

Then—

as if gathering every bit of courage she had—

said:

“…If you’re okay with it—

would you come to my room for a late-night snack?”
