“The poison took long enough.”

Vikir watched Andromalius stagger.

At the same time—

the wounds on his own chest and forearm rapidly closed.

The spirits sealed in Beelzebub's slots were churning violently.

> **<The Fly of Gluttony — ‘Beelzebub’> / Awl**
>
> **Slot 1:** Venom — Madam Eight-Legs (S)  
> **Slot 2:** Silent Heel — Mushuhushu (A+)  
> **Slot 3:** Super High-Speed Regeneration — Bog Salamander (A+)

The Bog Salamander's regeneration restored the flesh Andromalius had torn away.

Clack-clack-clack—

The red beads beneath Beelzebub's blade rattled against one another.

One spirit in particular was raging.

Madam Eight-Legs.

Her pride appeared to have been badly wounded by the fact that Andromalius had remained functional for even a short time after being poisoned.

Vikir, on the other hand—

was impressed.

Madam's venom was powerful enough to make even one of the Ten Corpses falter.

A poison so vicious that it could place even something already dead in danger again.

Slowed movement.

Dulled senses.

Blindness.

Paralysis.

Simply being able to inflict those effects on a Demon King-class existence was an enormous advantage.

Andromalius stared in horror as Seth's body darkened.

[Poison that even the dead cannot escape…?]

His red eyes spun.

[Where did you get something like this?!]

He struggled to keep his vision steady.

[How can a human possibly—]

Andromalius searched his memory.

How many creatures in the world possessed poison this lethal?

There were the cunning black mountain spiders.

The hydras of the land of giants.

The colossal jellyfish of the black sea.

Other candidates existed in the deepest territories beyond ordinary human reach.

But that only made the situation more absurd.

*Poisons like this are so lethal that even great demons avoid their owners.*

*They can't simply be captured and stored.*

His mind raced.

*How is a human using one?*

There was no time to solve the mystery.

Andromalius retreated.

The surviving bloodsucking flies withdrew with him.

There was no other source of blood nearby.

Only Vikir.

And Vikir's blood—

was poison.

Drinking more would only make Andromalius's condition worse.

Vikir looked at the retreating swarm.

“If you're a picky eater—”

He raised one hand.

“—you're going to have a hard time.”

He bit his index finger.

Then flicked blood into the air.

Splatter!

Blackening droplets flew like bullets.

Ordinary blood—

until Vikir willed harm.

Then—

venom.

Hemotoxic.

Neurotoxic.

Corrosive.

The droplets burned Andromalius's body and spiritual form together.

[Aaaaaagh!]

Beelzebub was a magic sword.

Its attacks could strike a demon's spirit directly.

That meant Andromalius could not simply ignore wounds to Seth's flesh.

Every one of Vikir's attacks mattered.

And Vikir's own Peak Sword Graduator strength was more than sufficient to draw out the magic sword's power.

Six large Fangs.

Then—

the smaller, distinct seventh.

The **Seventh Fang** tore across Andromalius again.

[How?!]

The demon nearly ground his teeth to pieces.

Humiliation burned through him.

Attacked by a human.

Inside his own territory.

Driven backward.

Bzzzzzz—

Andromalius gathered the remaining flies.

They formed a black cloud.

He slipped behind it and fled deeper into the cave.

Vikir did not chase immediately.

“That's exactly what I wanted.”

He reached behind his back.

Click.

Two black rods came free.

He joined them together.

A great black bow took shape.

**Anubis.**

The strongest bow in Ballak history.

The legendary weapon once wielded by the Divine Archer Adonai.

Vikir drew.

Thrum!

An arrow shot through the darkness.

It punched straight through Andromalius.

Boom!

The demon crashed into the cave wall.

He looked down.

A black arrowhead protruded through his chest.

Coated—

of course—

in Madam Eight-Legs' venom.

[A bow?!]

Andromalius stared.

A Baskerville swordsman—

using a bow?

Vikir walked toward him.

Step.

Step.

Step.

His movements were fluid as a snake.

And utterly silent.

Killing intent filled the cave—

but not the sound of footsteps.

Andromalius felt a chill.

*This is bad.*

Fighting Vikir inside the cave had become intolerable.

Bzzzzzzzz—

He called the flies again.

Every insect that answered him gathered into one enormous swarm.

[They shall swarm like flies.]

Graveyard insects.

Flies fattened on the remains of murdered children.

Countless creatures answered their master's authority.

Vikir looked at them.

“Flies are spider food.”

He cut his wrist.

Black blood flowed.

Then became mist.

Poisoned blood spread through the air.

The swarm died almost instantly.

Pat-pat-pat-pat-pat—

Flies fell in sheets.

Those that touched the venomous fog dissolved as though ice had been thrown into flame.

And Vikir did more.

He contaminated the groundwater flowing along the cave walls and slopes.

Water the flies might land on—

poisoned.

Moist stone—

poisoned.

The cave itself became hostile to Andromalius's swarm.

Vikir had taken control of the terrain.

The demon's breathing grew heavier.

Andromalius's expression twisted.

*Damn it.*

One regret finally surfaced.

*I should have kept a few children alive.*

The captives he once stored here as reserves of blood—

he had eaten them all.

Now—

there was nothing left to drain.

Except poisoned Vikir.

But even cornered—

Andromalius forced himself to smile.

[Impressive.]

His voice became calm again.

[For a mortal to drive me this far deserves praise.]

He looked toward the cave's outer boundary.

Beyond the barrier—

a large river curved around the training grounds.

Earthen banks held it back.

If Andromalius reached the surface—

if he destroyed the embankment—

he could flood the entire cave.

*Human flesh cannot regenerate as endlessly as a demon's.*

The plan would cost demonic power.

It would delay the opening of the Gate.

But survival came first.

[Hehehe…]

Andromalius stared at Vikir.

[You did well, human.]

[For a moment, you even made me panic.]

His smile vanished.

[But that's over.]

[You crossed the line.]

He took his first step toward leaving the cave.

Then—

released the barrier.

Sssssssss—

The demonic ward covering the training cave dissolved.

It had allowed entry only to demons and human children under eight.

Maintaining it consumed enormous amounts of demonic power.

It had been powerful enough to escape even an archmage's detection.

Breaking through it from outside would have required Demon King-class force.

Now that Andromalius released it voluntarily—

the power spent sustaining it returned to him.

He used that recovered demonic energy to suppress the poison.

Then leapt upward—

toward the opening in the center of the cave ceiling.

[Wait here.]

He kicked off the rock.

[I'll show you hell soon enough.]

Andromalius climbed rapidly.

Even as he fled—

another thought comforted him.

*It's good that I met this human now.*

Give Vikir a few more years—

and he might become impossible to handle.

*If we'd prepared only for Hugo, Osiris, and the Seven Counts, this one would have become a catastrophic variable.*

Better to kill him now.

Before the Ten Corpses moved openly against Baskerville.

Then—

Andromalius reached the upper end of the cavern.

Night sky appeared above him.

He stopped.

[…?]

Far below—

Vikir remained standing exactly where he had been.

He was not pursuing.

He simply looked upward with his usual expressionless face.

Something strange was in his hand.

A horn.

Long.

Tooth-shaped.

Andromalius's pupils contracted.

Vikir raised it to his lips.

Bweeeeeee—

A piercing note echoed through the cavern.

The instant Andromalius heard it—

cold ran through his entire body.

[…!]

He had worn Seth Le Baskerville's skin for decades.

He knew Baskerville.

He knew what that horn was.

And what that sound meant.

Then—

the night seemed to deepen.

Clouds covered the moon.

Darkness fell like a curtain.

Outside the opened ceiling—

long shadows appeared.

Many.

Far too many.

Flutter—

Black cloaks moved in the night wind.

Andromalius slowly raised his bloodshot eyes.

At the edge of the opening stood Hugo Le Baskerville.

Beside him—

Osiris Le Baskerville.

Behind father and son—

the Seven Counts.

The commanders of Baskerville's seven great knight orders.

Pit Bull.

Mastiff.

Doberman.

Shepherd.

Rottweiler.

Wolfhound.

And the remaining order beside them.

Behind each Count—

one hundred hounds.

Seven orders.

Seven hundred Sword Graduators.

The full military mobilization of the Ironblood Sword House.

Every hound of Baskerville had gathered.

Hugo looked down into the cave.

His face was unreadable.

“…Son.”

Andromalius froze.

Hugo's voice was almost casual.

“What are you doing down there?”
