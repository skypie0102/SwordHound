# Chapter 265: The Season of Redemption (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/265.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 263 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Existing glossary terminology controls Ms. Ouroboros, Dantalian, Quovadis, Old Testament Faction, Pigi, Professor Sadi, and the Night Hound.

The night sky was deep blue.

Clouds gathered overhead—

like sharks rising from an abyss toward the surface.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Rapid footsteps echoed through the quiet before dawn.

A narrow alley between red-brick buildings.

Stagnant water pooled underfoot.

“Huff… huff…”

A man in a black coat—

armor hidden beneath it—

wiped blood from the corner of his mouth.

“I should’ve lost them by now.”

His hand tightened around the axe at his waist.

He leaned out and checked the street.

Empty.

Only then did he exhale.

“Damn it.”

“If it weren’t for that Ms. Ouroboros, swallowing the whole Zodiac District would’ve been—”

Something passed over his head.

“…!?”

The man looked up.

A figure stood with one foot braced against each side of the alley—

high above him.

Black cloak.

Plague-doctor mask.

The Night Hound.

“W-who are you?”

No answer.

Only a question.

“Edward Bourbon Jr.”

“That’s your name, correct?”

The man stiffened.

“Who are you?”

“Did Ouroboros send you?”

Then his eyes shifted.

“No.”

“That woman always moves alone.”

“Then are you really the Night Hound?”

“The one she’s been chasing…?”

He kept talking—

but his hidden hand was already gripping the axe.

Vikir did not need an answer.

“Also known as Saint Bourbon.”

“Mid Sword Graduator.”

“High priest of the emerging **Ohm Cult**, influential across the northern region.”

“Twenty-five years ago, you molested and murdered your cousin, offered her as a sacrifice, and used that act to form a contract with a demon.”

“Afterward, you ruined thousands of families through religious exploitation and forced donations.”

“Near the end of your life, you joined the Demon Army.”

“You infiltrated Quovadis territory as a spy, using your knowledge of scripture.”

“During the Holy War, you turned against the Human Alliance from behind.”

“And you inflicted grave wounds on heroes including Tudor Donquixote and Bianca Fo Usher.”

Vikir’s voice came coldly from behind the mask.

“I asked if that was correct.”

The man shouted.

“What nonsense is that?!”

“The priesthood and my cousin are true, but I’ve never had any feud with Donquixote or Usher—”

He never finished.

A single drop of blood—

falling from Vikir’s fingertip—

entered his open mouth.

“…Ghk!?”

Bitterness flooded his tongue.

Nausea followed.

His body convulsed.

He vomited.

Clutched his throat.

Nothing helped.

The venom was so violent that the muscles around his tongue and throat seized.

He tried to wrap aura around his axe.

The aura dispersed before it formed properly.

A few seconds later—

he collapsed into a pool of blood.

Dead.

A pitiful ending for a Mid Sword Graduator.

Vikir stared down.

This had been the final assassination target of the night.

Number nineteen.

Then—

he remembered the man’s last muttering.

*If it weren’t for Ms. Ouroboros…*

*Is this really the Night Hound—the one she’s chasing?*

There were several possible meanings.

*Was that lunatic woman hunting him too?*

Apparently—

Vikir and Ms. Ouroboros were targeting some of the same criminals.

And she seemed to have two goals of her own.

One—

fight some unknown organization while hunting criminals throughout the Imperial Capital.

Two—

find the Night Hound.

Vikir remembered their earlier clash while he had been hunting for tuition money.

*She attacked me the moment she saw me.*

*I thought she was just insane.*

*Apparently she has a plan.*

He still did not know what that plan was.

But their paths overlapped too often to ignore.

And she clearly wanted to meet him.

“Tsk.”

Vikir searched the corpse.

A crumpled paper rested in one pocket.

**<Indulgence>**

**‘All sins of this faithful believer are forgiven.’**

**—Issued and guaranteed by the Old Testament Faction. Counterfeiting is punishable.—**

An indulgence from Quovadis.

The paper was crushed badly.

Only the words **sins forgiven** remained clearly visible.

A fittingly ironic possession—

for a man who had sacrificed a relative to a demon and spent his life preying on others.

Then—

[…There.]

[…There.]

[…Strong.]

Red light flared from Vikir’s chest.

Decarabia opened its single eye.

It stared at the indulgence.

[The smell of a companion—]

It immediately corrected itself.

[No.]

[Former companion.]

[Not a companion now.]

[Completely unrelated.]

[No—an enemy.]

Decarabia glanced nervously at Vikir.

Apparently worried he might draw his sword and announce that demons must die again.

Vikir barely noticed.

“…There’s demonic energy on the indulgence.”

Dantalian—

the Ninth Corpse—

was already dead.

Yet the scent remained.

Decarabia focused.

[Yes.]

[This is unquestionably the Sixth Corpse.]

[The Sixth was always close to Dantalian.]

Vikir nodded.

“…Then I have to investigate Quovadis again.”

He left the alley.

◆◆◆

A few minutes later—

another shadow entered.

“Oh?”

A helmet with two huge serpent-scale projections like horns.

Glossy black clothing.

High heels.

**Ms. Ouroboros.**

Even the Imperial Guard had never uncovered her true identity.

She looked down at the corpse.

“Hmm.”

“I went through all that trouble following the trail, and someone already came and went?”

She raised her head toward the opposite alley.

The eyes visible beneath her mask curved faintly.

“Chasing that bastard finally paid off.”

“I should follow.”

“If I’m lucky—”

“maybe I’ll finally see that noble face.”

Ms. Ouroboros disappeared in the direction of Colosseo Academy.

◆◆◆

By the time Vikir—

in dog form—

crossed back over the Academy wall—

the wake-up horn was sounding.

He dragged his tired body into the dormitory room.

Pigi was already sitting up, rubbing his eyes.

“Vikir?”

“You’re up early.”

“….”

Vikir sat on the edge of the bed for only a few seconds.

Then morning routine began.

All students gathered for exercises.

Then bathing.

Breakfast.

Fortunately—

it was the weekend.

No morning lectures.

Midterms were over.

The University League was over.

Finals were still some distance away.

For most students—

this was a short recovery period before the next academic push.

Vikir washed quickly.

Ate.

Then went to the newspaper-club room to check the morning reports.

“Professor!”

“Wait!”

“That notice was posted with permission!”

“Please don’t tear it down!”

“Hehehe.”

“So noisy.”

Vikir heard the argument before entering.

He opened the door slightly.

Dolores and the newspaper-club members were inside.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Sinclaire.

All angry.

And facing them—

Professor Sadi.

She held a pile of notices torn from the hall and clubroom doors.

One read:

**Correction Notice**

**In the article published on Month 0, Day 0, acts previously attributed to the Night Hound are corrected as acts committed by Ms. Ouroboros.**

**The mistake resulted from the authorities’ delayed identification of Ms. Ouroboros, a copycat criminal associated with Night Hound reports.**

**—Ryukeion Newspaper Club—**

Now—

Sadi had removed every notice mentioning either Night Hound or Ms. Ouroboros.

“Hehehe.”

“Children.”

“I’m assigned environmental cleanup this week.”

“So stop covering the walls with this garbage.”

She pushed her bangs back.

“Why am I suffering through this ridiculous punishment anyway?”

“All I did was open a **Serious Crime Assistance and Counseling Center**.”

Dolores frowned.

“Because you were counseling perpetrators and victims without distinction.”

Sadi ignored her.

She swept out of the hallway.

Vikir remained hidden until she passed.

The two of them had history—

the left-eye incident.

Nothing good would come from meeting her unnecessarily.

Then Sinclaire spotted him.

“Bro!”

She leaned through the window.

“Did you see that?”

“Professor Sadi ruined everything.”

“The whole atmosphere is wrecked.”

“I saw.”

“She looked terrifying.”

“She’s always strange, but today was worse.”

“Did her boyfriend dump her last night?”

Sinclaire paused.

“Actually…”

“Who could possibly endure dating her?”

Vikir simply nodded.

He took the morning paper.

Sinclaire followed him for a short distance.

Then remembered she had Mock Investment Competition practice that afternoon—

and turned back reluctantly.

Vikir continued toward Professor Banshee’s office.

Knock.

Knock.

Before waiting for an answer—

he opened the door.

Banshee spoke without turning.

“You don’t even wait for permission now?”

“How arrogant.”

“One point deducted for attitude.”

She already knew who it was.

“I need permission to leave campus.”

That got her attention.

“…Off campus?”

“The Academy has everything you need.”

“Why?”

“I’m going to the Rune Church.”

“To repent.”

Banshee raised an eyebrow.

“Repentance?”

“Unexpected.”

“You don’t look like the type who attends weekend mass out of devotion.”

Still—

she signed the permit.

“Good.”

“This is the first sincerely pious attitude I’ve seen from you in some time.”

Vikir accepted the paper.

His face remained blank.

*I never said I was going out to repent.*

He was going out—

to make someone else repent.
