The **Ten Corpses**.

Ten Demon Kings who would open the Gate to the Demon Realm and usher in the Age of Destruction.

And—

the ten corpses that became their vessels.

Before regression, Vikir had known of the Ten Corpses in broad terms.

He had never known that Seth was one of them.

*Meeting him this early is fortunate.*

Vikir retreated one step and studied the enemy.

If Andromalius had remained hidden inside Baskerville—

more innocent children would have died.

Someone else would have been blamed for those deaths.

Just as Vikir had been before regression.

He cleared his mind.

Behind Seth's battered body—

the Tenth Corpse rose like mist.

Demonic energy gathered.

A shape emerged.

Red eyes.

Black flesh.

Horns curved like a ram's.

From the stump of Seth's severed left arm, an enormous snake grew outward.

It writhed through the air.

Andromalius spoke through Seth's mouth.

[I never expected to reveal my true form to a human.]

“You should have hidden better.”

[…]

For a moment, Andromalius simply stared.

Why?

Why was the human in front of him not surprised?

Vikir had no intention of explaining.

He was already comparing the knowledge of his previous life with the information of this one—

building the most efficient plan possible.

*So the culprits who trigger the Age of Destruction years from now are already hiding among us.*

And one of them—

had been this close.

Vikir severed the final trace of sentiment he still associated with Seth.

This was no longer the vaguely amiable half-brother from his memories.

Not a monster.

Not merely a fiend.

A demon.

True demonic blood.

An existence whose place in the Abyss belonged among Demon Kings.

“Still.”

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

“A blade has no eyes.”

Aura condensed around the magic sword.

Then—

Puck-puck-puck-puck-puck-puck!

Vikir attacked.

In the brief interval while Andromalius descended fully into Seth's body and reshaped it—

Vikir stabbed more than thirty-six times.

Not a duel.

Not a contest.

Every line of attack existed for one purpose.

Killing.

Beelzebub followed brutally efficient paths—

seeking only vital points.

[Gh—?!]

Andromalius convulsed.

[Why does it hurt?!]

Another thrust tore through him.

[This is only the body's pain! Why is it reaching my soul?!]

Then Andromalius finally looked properly at Vikir's weapon.

A thin, awl-like magic blade.

Demonic power radiated from it.

The Tenth Corpse's red eyes widened.

[No…]

Recognition became horror.

[Impossible!]

His voice rose.

[Beelzebub—the Sword of Gluttony?!]

The ancient relic.

A remnant of the demonic throne of the **Lord of Flies**—

one of the seven great Demon Kings said to have ruled the Demon Realm in ages before Andromalius was even born.

Only then did Andromalius understand the source of Vikir's confidence.

An ordinary weapon could injure the corpse he possessed.

It could cut Seth's flesh.

Break Seth's bones.

But the spiritual body behind the vessel was another matter.

Pure condensed mana—

an aura blade—

could reach it.

And magic swords and holy swords were greater exceptions still.

They could physically interfere with spiritual bodies in ways ordinary matter could not.

A magic sword covered in aura—

was a weapon even a Demon King could not casually ignore.

And the one wielding it was Vikir.

A veteran warrior whose previous life had been spent killing demons.

“Die.”

Vikir pressed forward.

He wanted this battle finished before Andromalius fully understood him.

Before possession, Seth's own strength had been around the Intermediate Sword Graduator realm, with High Sword Graduator power at his limit.

But with Andromalius completely controlling the vessel—

the body could now release force at **Sword Master level or higher**.

Even so—

Vikir believed he could kill him.

A body strengthened by the Styx.

Combat experience accumulated across two lives.

Baskerville swordsmanship.

Beelzebub.

And surprise.

Even a Sword Master could die to an ambush or assassination if the killing blow landed correctly.

Vikir sank his Fangs into Andromalius without hesitation.

But then—

[…Yes.]

Andromalius's voice steadied.

[I admit it.]

The snake replacing his left arm lifted.

[You surprised me.]

A smile.

[But that's all.]

Andromalius was not merely a strong demon.

He was one of the beings whose conspiracy would nearly destroy the human world.

An end-of-the-world enemy.

And after enduring Vikir's relentless assault—

he began to adapt.

Hissss!

The giant snake lunged.

Its mouth opened wide.

Vikir swept his cloak across its head to deflect it.

The fangs still grazed his forearm.

A long red wound appeared.

Andromalius smiled.

[That's enough.]

He extended his right hand.

[You're finished.]

Vikir's injured arm suddenly turned pale.

Then blood erupted from the wound.

Gurgle—

gurgle—

gurgle—

The blood did not fall.

It streamed through the air toward Andromalius.

His right hand absorbed it.

Then—

Bzzzzzz.

Vikir heard wings.

Flies.

Fat.

Ugly.

Swollen.

One of them clung to the wound and drank.

“…Bloodsucking flies.”

Vikir crushed it.

He pressed the wound shut.

It accomplished nothing.

Bzzzzzzzzzz—

Flies poured in from every direction.

The same insects that had been licking the bones of murdered children now swarmed over Vikir.

Gurgle.

Gurgle.

Blood continued to leave his body.

Distance did not matter.

The flies acted as extensions of Andromalius's authority.

Thin streams of Vikir's blood twisted through the air like snakes—

all of them drawn toward Andromalius's right hand.

[Hehehe…]

The demon's damaged body began to repair itself.

[My strength is returning.]

An endless blood-drain conducted through a swarm of flies.

This was one of Andromalius's authorities.

A power approaching immortality.

As long as wounded and exhausted living things remained nearby—

his supply of life force could continue.

The method itself was not unique among demons.

Many knew how to invade wounds and steal vitality.

But Andromalius excelled at it.

*Seth became one of his victims long ago.*

Vikir watched the demon.

Seth had carried two deep wounds.

A desperate hunger for his father's recognition.

And an inferiority complex toward his older brother.

For Andromalius—

those weaknesses had been ideal openings.

The demon devoured him from within.

Eventually leaving only a shell.

Vikir remembered things Seth used to say before regression.

*I want Father to acknowledge me.*

*I want him to look at me and realize I'm here.*

And—

*Brother is incredible.*

*I wanted to become someone like him.*

*I wanted to stand beside him.*

Vikir did not know whether those feelings were fragments of Seth's remaining subconscious—

or merely phrases Andromalius had memorized and imitated.

But one thing was clear.

The real Seth had possessed those feelings while alive.

Andromalius had used them to erode his mind.

“…”

Vikir found the realization deeply unpleasant.

He had never been close to Seth.

Yet part of that hunger—

part of that resentment—

felt familiar.

Crack!

Vikir ripped away the snake and the flies tearing into his chest.

The blessing of the Styx kept the injuries from becoming fatal.

But chunks of flesh had still been torn away.

Andromalius sneered.

[Good.]

His smile widened.

[Keep accumulating wounds like that.]

[Eventually, you'll die.]

The flies circled.

[Now…]

His right hand lifted again.

[Let me have another drink—]

The sentence stopped.

Ping.

Andromalius's vision went black.

For an instant, he thought it was an illusion.

Then his head rang.

His balance failed.

The strength vanished from his legs.

[Gh?!]

Something was wrong.

Terribly wrong.

A choking sensation spread through him.

Not only through his nose.

Not only his throat.

Everywhere.

His arms and legs went numb.

They trembled uncontrollably.

Inside the possessed body—

thick clots began blocking blood vessels.

Blackened blood coagulated.

Circulation failed.

One abnormal condition after another crashed through Andromalius's vessel.

Paralysis.

Sensory failure.

Damage like a stroke.

And as the blood stopped moving—

so did the flow of mana.

For one critical moment—

Andromalius lost control of Seth's body.

[What?!]

His voice cracked.

[What is happening?!]

The demonic spirit destabilized.

Power scattered from the host.

Dead flies began dropping from the air.

Pat.

Pat-pat.

Pat-pat-pat.

Across from him—

Vikir stood calmly.

The injuries on his forearm and chest were already closing at frightening speed.

Drip.

Drip.

He raised his wounded arm.

Red blood fell from it.

The drops struck the ground—

and became black stains like tar.

Hissssss—

The blood touched old bones.

They blackened.

Bubbled.

Corroded.

Andromalius stared.

Then—

understanding arrived.

[Your blood…]

His expression changed.

[Your blood?!]

Normally—

Vikir's blood was merely blood.

But when its owner willed harm—

it became poison.

Lethal poison.

A venom vicious enough to force even one of the Ten Corpses into crisis.

The nightmare of the Depths.

**Madam Eight-Legs.**

Her S-rank venom—

now flowed through Vikir's blood.

And Andromalius—

had just drained it directly into himself.
