Deep wrinkles covered the old man’s face.

The kind carved only by too many battlefields.

Cold, sunken eyes hid beneath thick brows.

A broad chest.

Heavy shoulders.

A black fur coat like a lion’s mane.

Even soaked in seawater—

his uniform remained perfectly buttoned and sharply pressed.

Only the belt buckle sat slightly crooked.

A heavy club hung from his waist—

its end reinforced with a pointed lead weight.

**Orca Montreuil-sur-Mer Javert.**

Warden of Nouvelle Vague.

For nearly a century—

while generations changed on the surface—

he remained here.

The prison’s unchanging symbol.

Orca put out his cigarette.

Then slowly surveyed the devastation.

“What is this?”

He pulled something from inside his coat.

A blackened—

shriveled pocket watch.

Apparently made from BDISSEM material.

“I expected something may have happened to BDISSEM.”

“But still…”

“ORCA!”

A roar came from behind him.

“I stayed alive for this!”

“Prepare yourself!”

A Level-Nine prisoner charged.

**Mammatamuz the Piercing Tusk.**

Once a bandit king who ruled an entire mountain range.

A supervillain of the previous generation.

More than 2,800 consecutive life sentences.

He blasted junior officers aside with his shoulder.

Then crashed into Orca.

BOOM!

Bones broke.

Leather-like flesh tore.

But not Orca’s.

“Aaaaaagh!”

Mammatamuz collapsed.

His own shoulder bones had shattered through the skin.

Orca had not moved a step.

“…What is this mess?”

He raised the club.

Then struck once.

BOOM!

Mammatamuz’s head burst.

A shockwave spread outward—

warping the ground.

The Level-Nine prisoners laughed nervously.

“Hehehe.”

“Still alive, old man?”

“You’re weaker than before.”

“Age finally caught you?”

“Mammatamuz was all noise anyway.”

They began closing in.

Orca looked at them with disgust.

“Move.”

“Trash.”

His club swung.

BOOM!

Heads flew.

Dozens.

The black end of the club—

marked by two pale spots like an orca’s head—

vanished into blood and gore.

Where Orca walked—

crowds split apart.

Even Level-Nine monsters hesitated.

Once the spikes and chains on his weapon were so coated in human fat and blood that the original color disappeared—

Orca finally asked:

“Where are D’Ordume and Souaré?”

“They arrived before me.”

Bastille—

his face marked by wolf-claw wounds—

crawled forward.

“Forgive me.”

“They’re over there.”

Orca looked toward the lower stairway.

D’Ordume—

face-down.

Souaré—

collapsed against a wall.

Both unconscious.

Orca sighed.

“Get up.”

No response.

His brow twitched.

“…Get up.”

A quieter voice.

Far more frightening.

“YES!”

Both Wardens snapped awake instantly.

They scrambled upright and saluted.

“LOYALTY!”

“Warden!”

“Forget the salute.”

“Who did this to you?”

Then Orca stopped himself.

“No.”

“Never mind.”

“Where did he go?”

“D-down!”

Orca sighed again.

“I’ll handle this level.”

“You two chase him.”

“Repay the debt.”

“….”

“A person carrying unpaid debts cannot become the next Warden.”

“Understood?”

Both flinched.

Then—

SMACK!

Orca struck each of them on the back with an open palm.

Their uniform backs split.

Red skin showed.

“Go.”

“Restore what you lost.”

Orca’s intent was obvious.

Whoever had humiliated Nouvelle Vague’s Wardens in public—

would become the convenient culprit for the riot.

And then—

be executed.

◆◆◆

Level Ten.

The deepest labor zone in Nouvelle Vague.

Vikir and Aiyen descended toward its lowest pit.

Lava flowed.

Steam and fire twisted through the air.

Hot spring water boiled from cracks—

then evaporated before reaching the ground.

At the center of the pit—

something rested inside blue flames.

**Poseidon.**

The true reason Vikir had entered Nouvelle Vague.

Aiyen stared.

“…Are we destroying it?”

“Close enough.”

“It activates by absorbing impact and mana.”

Vikir demonstrated.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

**Black Sun.**

His strongest attack struck Poseidon.

BOOM!

The sphere did not crack.

It absorbed everything.

Aura.

Mana.

Impact.

The blue flames around it grew brighter.

The sphere—

seemed slightly larger.

Aiyen nodded.

“Then I’ll help.”

She raised the bow she had stolen from a guard.

Bakira prepared fire beside her.

Then—

“STOP!”

“What are you doing?!”

D’Ordume and Souaré appeared behind them.

Recovered.

Aiyen’s face soured.

“I spared you.”

“And you came back?”

Souaré laughed.

“You ambushed me once.”

“Don’t get arrogant.”

D’Ordume stared at Vikir—

far more carefully than before.

“I underestimated you.”

“You’re in the realm of true monsters.”

“….”

“But I will not lower my guard again.”

“No second surprise.”

Vikir analyzed them.

*They were already defeated.*

*Yet they came down without hesitation.*

*No fear.*

*That means—*

“Back!”

Vikir shouted.

Aiyen obeyed instantly.

SPLAT!

Green slime hit the place they had just occupied.

More followed.

A massive translucent column of mucus descended.

It spread into countless tentacles—

covering the space behind D’Ordume and Souaré like a living shield.

“…So that’s why.”

Vikir swallowed.

**Flubber J. Tarbond.**

The being he had excluded from the jailbreak plan—

because he assumed it possessed no meaningful intelligence.

A mistake.

Flubber—

had entered the battle.
