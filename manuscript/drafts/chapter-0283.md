# Chapter 283: Power Inflation (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/283.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 281 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Dolores L. Quovadis, Pigi, Sinclaire, Oracle, Vikir, Night Hound, Humbert L. Quovadis, and House Bourgeois.

One quiet weekend evening—

a carriage left Colosseo Academy for the Bourgeois estate.

Inside were three people.

Dolores.

Pigi.

Sinclaire.

All three had received the extraordinary honor of dining with the head of the richest family in the Empire.

Naturally—

Pigi and Sinclaire were excited.

“Oracle really did something incredible this time!”

“First place in the Mock Investment Competition!”

“And the insane part is that the returns were real!”

“Not to mention that party.”

“How much money did that cost?”

“I can’t even imagine.”

Pigi leaned closer.

“Did you hear where we’re eating tonight?”

“The Bourgeois vault!”

“Mhm.”

“I heard.”

“They say the dining table is surrounded by mountains of jewels, gold bars, coins, notes, checks, drafts, property deeds, art, antiques…”

Pigi sucked in a breath.

“That much?”

“Isn’t that exaggerated?”

“Why would they keep everything there?”

Sinclaire looked at him.

“Where else would a rich family keep money except a vault?”

“What if a guest steals something during dinner?”

“How would anyone steal all of it?”

“…Fair.”

Pigi turned toward the rear of the carriage.

Several treasure chests had been loaded there.

Gifts for the Bourgeois family head.

“I wonder whether our presents will become part of that treasure mountain.”

“That would actually be kind of an honor.”

Sinclaire looked at the carefully prepared gifts.

Then murmured—

“…I wish Brother could have come too.”

Pigi blinked.

“Vikir?”

“Why bring him up now?”

“…No reason.”

Wind brushed through Sinclaire’s short white hair.

“We promised he’d visit my home someday.”

“For a meal.”

“?”

Pigi tilted his head.

Sinclaire smiled faintly.

“I just thought it was a shame.”

“He’s been so busy lately that even eating together is difficult.”

“Oh, that.”

“I know.”

“I barely eat with Vikir anymore either.”

“Except breakfast.”

Pigi started describing how busy his roommate had become.

Sinclaire listened—

but her thoughts had gone somewhere else.

Back to an old conversation at the orphanage.

*Brother.*

*You don’t have money?*

*…No.*

*Why not?*

*You’re an Academy student.*

*These children don’t have parents.*

*We should help them.*

*They don’t need something like parents.*

*Huh?*

*People eventually have to make their own way through the world.*

*Parents are necessary only during the childhood period when a person cannot survive alone.*

*After that, they are unnecessary.*

Sinclaire stared out the carriage window.

Meanwhile—

Dolores heard almost none of the conversation behind her.

Her attention remained fixed on the gift chests.

*I’m sorry.*

The presents Pigi and Sinclaire had chosen were no longer inside.

They had been moved somewhere else.

One of the supposedly empty boxes now contained something entirely different.

**Night Hound.**

An assassin waiting to strike the head of House Bourgeois.

According to Damien—

Bartolomeo did not search guests before allowing them into his vault.

Arrogance.

Confidence.

The belief that no assassin could threaten him.

For a demon—

that confidence was also a weakness.

*I have to get these two out before the fighting starts.*

Dolores squeezed the small vial hidden in her hand.

A sleeping medicine.

The plan was simple.

When they reached the estate—

the three Oracle members would wait in a reception room.

Dolores would put the medicine in Pigi’s and Sinclaire’s tea.

Once they were asleep—

she would move them somewhere safe.

*It would have been better if they hadn’t come at all.*

But both had spent days looking forward to meeting the Bourgeois head.

And the invitation specifically requested **all members of Oracle**.

There was no natural excuse for Dolores to arrive alone.

Even now—

Pigi was writing a list of questions.

“The future direction of the Imperial economy.”

“Current financial indicators.”

“How to identify new businesses.”

“Promising small merchant guilds.”

“Mergers with mercenary guilds…”

He paused.

“And whether tycoons eat chicken.”

Sinclaire smiled.

“I have a question too.”

Pigi turned.

“What do you want to ask?”

“We can compare lists.”

“…No.”

“I only have one.”

Pigi stared.

“One?”

“That’s all?”

“Yes.”

“You’re meeting the richest man in the Empire!”

“You should learn everything you can!”

“This may never happen again!”

Sinclaire only smiled.

Three people.

One carriage.

Three entirely different sets of thoughts.

Before long—

they reached the Bourgeois main gate.

“Dinner reservation.”

“Oracle.”

Dolores announced them.

The gate opened immediately.

Their carriage proceeded through the luxurious estate—

using a route reserved for important guests.

No inspection.

No search.

The butler guiding them smiled.

“We have seen quite a few distinguished guests from Quovadis lately.”

Dolores stiffened.

“Someone else from my family came?”

“Yes.”

“Cardinal Humbert visited several hours ago.”

“His business is already complete, and he has left.”

Cold ran down Dolores’s spine.

*Humbert was here?*

*At least he already left.*

She extended her senses.

No divine power nearby.

Good.

If Humbert were still present—

the entire operation might become far more complicated.

The butler led them to a reception room.

“Please wait here.”

“I will inform the head of the family.”

Tea.

Small refreshments.

Then the door closed.

Pigi looked around.

“This room is incredible.”

“Everything looks expensive—

but not gaudy.”

“It feels old.”

“Elegant.”

Sinclaire nodded.

“Very tasteful.”

Dolores’s hand trembled.

*I have to do it.*

She had never slipped anything into someone else’s drink before.

The idea alone made her nervous.

Then—

Pigi and Sinclaire both looked away.

Dolores moved.

A few drops into each cup.

Done.

They drank without suspicion.

Soon—

Pigi yawned.

“Hm?”

“Why am I suddenly so sleepy?”

Sinclaire blinked slowly.

“Me too…”

“And dinner is about to start.”

Dolores’s voice shook.

“H-hey.”

“If you’re tired—

just close your eyes for a while.”

“I’ll wake you.”

“Really?”

“Thanks.”

Pigi and Sinclaire leaned back on the reception sofa.

Closed their eyes.

Dolores waited.

Then waved one hand in front of their faces.

No reaction.

She exhaled.

*It worked.*

Dolores wiped sweat from her forehead and left the room.

Almost immediately—

she encountered the returning butler.

“Saintess.”

“My lord was delayed by urgent business.”

“He is preparing now.”

“Yes!”

Dolores hesitated.

“One more thing.”

“The friends who accompanied me suddenly need to return.”

The butler looked surprised.

“But the meeting is about to begin.”

“If necessary, we can reschedule—”

“No!”

“That won’t be necessary.”

“I’m the club representative.”

“I can meet him alone.”

The butler looked uncertain—

but accepted it.

A little past the appointed time—

Dolores loaded the prepared “gifts” onto a trolley.

Several attendants escorted her deeper into the mansion.

They walked for a long time.

Then—

the vault appeared.

Its entrance felt less like a door—

and more like the entrance to a vast plaza.

“My lord is waiting.”

The butler bowed.

The enormous door opened.

Cold darkness waited inside.

Attendants pushed the trolley in.

Guided Dolores across the threshold.

Then—

one by one—

they bowed and withdrew.

The door shut.

Dolores stood alone inside an enormous black space.

“…My lord?”

She spoke cautiously.

Then—

FLASH.

Light erupted everywhere.

Dolores’s mouth opened.

Gold.

A world of gold.

A landscape made entirely of impossible wealth.
