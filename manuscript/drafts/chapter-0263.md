# Chapter 263: The Inverted Pentagram (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/263.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 261 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Chinese controls Decarabia’s **S+** danger rating; the shifted witness’s S is rejected. Existing glossary terminology controls **Seventh Corpse**, **Ten Corpses**, **Serpent’s Womb**, and **『The Decalogue』**.

An ominous voice thundered from the black currents.

[HAHAHAHAHA!]

[EVERY HAIR!]

[EVERY TOE!]

[THIS ENTIRE BODY OVERFLOWS WITH POWER!]

[AT LAST, I HAVE ENOUGH MANA!]

[I SWEAR IT HERE—AT THE MOST FORBIDDEN PLACE IN THIS DIMENSION!]

[I WILL OPEN THE GATE OF HELL LEADING INTO THE ABYSS AND EXTERMINATE HUMANITY FROM THIS LAND!]

[DESPAIR, WEAK AND FOOLISH CREATURES!]

[SCREAM!]

[THE BRINGER OF GREAT DESTRUCTION—DECARABIA—HAS DESCENDED!]

The Seventh Corpse.

Decarabia.

Its crimson eye cast an ominous light across the world.

**<‘Seventh Corpse’ Decarabia>**

**Danger Rating:** S+  
**Size:** ?  
**Discovery Location:** Deep within the Gate of Destruction, ‘Serpent’s Womb’  
**Alias:** ‘Seventh Corpse’

A natural enemy of mankind.

One of ten incomprehensible and supposedly unkillable calamities.

**‘They shall plunder food like locusts.’**

**—『The Decalogue』 10:Upper—**

Vikir stared at the gigantic inverted pentagram.

*It doesn’t even have hair or toes.*

Meanwhile—

Decarabia’s eye darted around in confusion.

It clearly did not understand what was happening.

Vikir did.

*Decarabia.*

*The only inanimate-type demon among the Ten Corpses.*

Demons existed in many forms.

Decarabia was one of the strangest.

For ages—

people had argued over what it actually was.

An object?

A demon?

An artifact?

Depending on the era—

it had been classified as all three.

Its ability, however, was unquestionably beyond ordinary artifacts.

It had one primary function.

Simple.

Absolute.

**Barrier generation.**

The more mana Decarabia absorbed—

the larger and stronger its barrier became.

Its defensive strength had no obvious fixed ceiling.

Growth depended on the mana supplied.

That made it terrifyingly versatile.

Vikir remembered the Age of Destruction.

*After consuming the mana of the other nine Demon Kings—*

*Decarabia became an enormous shield that stopped the Human Alliance cold.*

On a battlefield where everything burned—

with exhausted heroes throwing everything they had—

the red barrier remained standing.

Calling it a shield was almost ridiculous.

It was a wall.

An impossible wall.

The **Wailing Wall**.

Only a handful of heroes had ever been able to break through it.

The towering red barrier—

with the black Wraith Tree rising behind it—

had been enough to destroy morale before the battle even began.

But now—

Vikir already possessed the Wraith Tree.

And Decarabia was before him.

Weak.

Unfed.

Alone.

*I can destroy it now.*

He had found Decarabia before the other Ten Corpses could.

Before it could absorb enormous reserves of mana.

All that remained was to eliminate it.

Vikir raised his sword.

Then—

[…Excuse me.]

[Who are you?]

Decarabia spoke.

“The one who woke you.”

[…A person?]

[A human?]

Its eye widened.

[Interesting.]

[The humans in my memory were bent over and covered in hair.]

[They carried polished stone spears and stone blades.]

It peered at him.

[You’re really human?]

“Yes.”

Vikir raised his sword again.

This Decarabia was nothing like the Wailing Wall of the future.

With enough time—

he could destroy it.

Then Decarabia said something unexpected.

[GOOD.]

[WEAK HUMAN, YOU ARE FORTUNATE.]

[I WILL CAST OFF MY OLD BOND.]

[THE CONTRACT WITH THE DEMON RACE ENDS HERE.]

[FROM THIS MOMENT, I ENTER A NEW CONTRACT WITH HUMANITY.]

[YOU WILL BE THE REPRESENTATIVE CONTRACTOR.]

“…?”

[COME!]

[ISSUE YOUR DIRECTIVE, HUMAN.]

[WHAT DO YOU WANT?]

[USE ME TO ACHIEVE YOUR GREAT AMBITION!]

Vikir lowered the sword slightly.

“Directive?”

Decarabia blinked.

“…?”

[…?]

An awkward silence settled between them.

Then Decarabia spoke carefully.

[Human.]

[Did you wake me without knowing what I am?]

“I know.”

“You’re a demon.”

[HAHA!]

[Half correct.]

[More precisely—]

[I am something that **was** a demon.]

Its voice suddenly became far more conversational.

[I am influenced by the being who awakens me from my long sleep.]

“Influenced?”

[Yes.]

[Every thousand years, I fall dormant.]

[The first being to awaken me determines my disposition for the next thousand years.]

Humanity had known Decarabia as a demon for only about a millennium.

What it had been before that—

nobody knew.

During Vikir’s remembered future—

demons awakened it.

So it became demonic.

In an earlier era—

Decarabia claimed—

it had once even been regarded as an angel.

“Hm.”

Vikir thought for a moment.

“So because I woke you—”

“you will obey me for the next thousand years?”

[Correct.]

[Our bond will likely survive in the legends of your species.]

[Your kind does not live very long, after all.]

Decarabia sounded proud.

Vikir did not care.

“…Understood.”

Whoosh!

Aura ignited at the tip of his sword.

[…What?]

[Human, why are you pointing that at—AAAGH!?]

Vikir struck.

Decarabia twisted in pain.

Tears squeezed from its single eye.

[WHAT ARE YOU DOING!?]

[HUMAN—NO, CONTRACTOR!]

[WHY ARE YOU ATTACKING MY BODY!?]

“Because I can destroy you now.”

[WHY WOULD YOU WANT TO DESTROY ME!?]

“Demons die.”

Vikir swung again.

Crash!

Aura carved across Decarabia’s body.

[WAIT!]

[WAIT A MOMENT!]

[WHY ARE YOU TRYING TO KILL DEMONS!?]

“Because demons harm humans.”

[THEN I’M NOT A DEMON ANYMORE!]

[I WAS NEVER TRULY A DEMON OR A GOD!]

[I’M AN OBJECT-TYPE ARTIFACT—]

“I don’t believe demons.”

Slash!

Decarabia shrank under the impacts.

It sobbed.

[I’M SERIOUS!]

[I STOPPED BEING A DEMON THE MOMENT YOU WOKE ME!]

“I don’t believe demons.”

[YOU’RE INSANE!]

[CHECK!]

[CAN YOU EVEN FEEL DEMONIC ENERGY FROM ME NOW!?]

Vikir paused.

Now that Decarabia mentioned it—

the demonic presence had vanished.

Moments earlier—

it had flooded the entire vault.

Now—

nothing.

[I’M TELLING THE TRUTH.]

Decarabia began crying like a young girl.

Vikir stopped hitting it.

“For the moment.”

“So you’re no longer a demon?”

[YES!]

[I DON’T LIE!]

“Even so…”

“It may be safer to destroy you before another demon gets hold of you.”

[NO!]

[YOU WOKE ME!]

[EVEN IF DEMONS TAKE POSSESSION OF ME NOW, I CAN ONLY SERVE HUMANITY FOR THE NEXT THOUSAND YEARS!]

Decarabia desperately continued.

[I’M USEFUL!]

[I CAN IDENTIFY ARTIFACTS!]

[I KNOW THE ANCIENT WORLD!]

[AND I CAN MAKE BARRIERS!]

That last word caught Vikir’s attention.

Barrier.

He remembered the Wailing Wall.

With enough mana—

Decarabia could create something close to absolute defense.

Decarabia noticed his hesitation.

[SEE?]

[LOOK HOW USEFUL I AM!]

Its eye snapped toward one of the artifacts lying nearby.

A huge crimson sword.

Until recently—

it had rested on the rack that was Decarabia.

[THAT IS THE **GREAT SWORD OF RAGE DISORDER**.]

[THE WEAPON OF A LEGENDARY GREAT-SWORD KING FROM THE ANCIENT WORLD.]

[ITS ATTACK POWER IS EXTRAORDINARY.]

[IT WAS SEALED BECAUSE ITS BATTLE LUST IS SO INSANE THAT IT ATTACKS EVERYTHING AROUND IT.]

Decarabia blinked.

The chains binding the sword snapped.

The giant blade launched itself through the air toward Vikir.

[HUMAN!]

[PUT YOUR HAND ON ME.]

[USE YOUR MANA.]

[AND FORM AN IMAGE IN YOUR MIND.]

“What image?”

[ANYTHING THAT PROTECTS YOU.]

[A SHIELD.]

[A WALL.]

[A BARRIER.]

Vikir placed his hand on Decarabia.

Mana drained rapidly.

Then—

zzzzing.

A translucent inverted pentagram appeared before him.

It shone red.

The giant sword struck.

BOOM!

The blade ricocheted across the vault—

and broke in half.

“….”

Vikir stared.

That sword had clearly been a high-grade artifact.

Yet the barrier had not merely stopped it.

The rebound had destroyed it.

And the mana cost had been modest.

The Wailing Wall’s reputation made sense.

[WELL?]

Decarabia’s single eye curled smugly.

[YOU WANT ME NOW, DON’T YOU?]

[YOU WANT TO OWN ME?]

[POSSESS ME?]

Vikir ignored the expression.

“Give back the mana you took.”

[…What?]

[You gave it to me and now you want it back?]

[Humans really are—]

Vikir raised his sword.

[…Fine!]

[Fine! Put that away!]

Decarabia reluctantly returned the mana.

Power flowed back into Vikir.

At the same time—

Decarabia shrank.

Smaller.

Smaller.

Until it fit in the palm of a baby’s hand.

It glanced at Vikir—

then quickly attached itself near his neck.

Clink.

A thin red chain appeared.

It fastened neatly to Vikir’s collar.

Decarabia had turned itself into a pendant.

[…So.]

[What would you like me to do now, human?]

It sounded nervous.

Vikir thought.

Then—

“Tell me everything about your contractors.”

“Every one.”

“Leave nothing out.”

[…My contractor information?]

Decarabia hesitated.

Then looked at the aura still burning at Vikir’s sword tip.

[…Fine.]

[I’ll cooperate.]

[But please stop talking about destroying me.]

Vikir tucked the now-small Decarabia close.

There was no longer any reason to destroy it.

Becoming Decarabia’s master was a larger loss for the demons than simply breaking it.

He tapped the pendant once.

[One more thing.]

[I respond to no living being except the representative contractor.]

[Remember that.]

Vikir nodded.

Time to leave.

The other artifacts lay scattered around the vault.

Dark.

Silent.

Cowering in Decarabia’s presence.

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

Aura gathered.

The Eighth Fang.

His encounter with Cane Corso had changed him profoundly.

Seven enormous fangs formed—

and beside them—

a smaller but distinct eighth.

The Sword Master’s aura tore through the dimensional barrier.

RIP!

A crack opened.

Vikir stepped out.

The Night Hound had achieved his objective.

Now—

he could move on to the next prey.

Decarabia’s voice drifted from the pendant.

[Ahhh~]

[How fortunate I am.]

[To serve such a beautiful and adorable **mistress** in this life.]

Vikir looked down.

“….”

His newest companion—

was apparently a little strange.
