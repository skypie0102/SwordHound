# Chapter 58: The Hunter and the Hunted (4)

Recovered English Chapter 57 is the verified aligned secondary witness; the English corpus is one chapter behind from this target onward. Established terminology controls **Aiyen**, **Bakira**, **Ballak Tribe**, and **Oxbear**. Animal reproductive behavior and Ballak teasing are retained clinically and without erotic embellishment.

*Where there's a female, males are easy to find.*

Vikir finally understood what Aiyen had meant.

They were hidden in front of a den deep in the jungle.

Inside were the huge female Oxbear and the much smaller male she had selected.

The pair had remained there for three days.

The female's strength and reproductive drive were extraordinary.

Her enormous body blocked the entrance, preventing the exhausted male from leaving.

He had endured for the first two days.

By the third, his strength was visibly failing.

The female, however, was approaching hibernation.

She appeared determined to conceive before settling in for the long sleep.

Whenever the male collapsed from exhaustion, she groomed and roused him, then kept him in the den.

Vikir sighed.

“What am I even doing?”

His arms were full of carp, salmon, berries, and mushrooms.

Aiyen had gathered another load.

The food was not for them.

For three days they had been supporting the Oxbears' breeding cycle.

Whenever the female emerged in search of food, the hunters left supplies that could be inspected and carried back.

Most ultimately went to the exhausted male.

Aiyen picked up a cluster of berries.

“These give a short burst of energy, but they're bad for you over time. Body temperature rises quickly, then crashes.”

Vikir knew the effect.

The ingredients temporarily boosted strength by consuming the body's reserves.

The female seemed to understand that too.

She still fed them to her partner.

The male understood as well.

He had little choice.

Whenever Vikir accidentally left scent or traces nearby, the old female came out to investigate.

Her eyesight had deteriorated with age.

Her hearing had not.

GRRRR—

Claws tore apart thick logs.

Stone cracked.

Trees old enough to have stood for centuries toppled under her strength.

Only when she was satisfied that nothing remained nearby did she return to the den.

Vikir watched from the brush.

“I'm not sure whether that display is a warning to intruders or to the male.”

Aiyen almost laughed.

The stronger the female, the more completely she could keep a smaller mate from escaping.

That was exactly why Aiyen had selected this pair.

◆◆◆

Hunters still had to eat.

Aiyen chose a position where the wind would carry their smell away from the den.

She boiled clear water in a coconut shell with crayfish from the waterfall, a little salt, and soft water plants.

Then she pushed the largest pieces toward Vikir.

“Eat. You need to recover.”

Once again, it was difficult to tell master from slave.

Vikir cracked a red crayfish shell.

Steam rose from the white meat.

He chewed thoughtfully.

“They really haven't stopped for three days.”

The den had barely gone quiet.

Aiyen gave him a mischievous look.

“Isn't that normal?”

“What?”

“Ballak men are expected to have stamina. Are Imperial men different?”

Vikir frowned.

After the public-urination trick, Aiyen's claims about Ballak customs were not especially trustworthy.

She kept needling him anyway.

“You couldn't keep up?”

“……”

“Maybe Imperial men are delicate.”

“Who are you calling delicate?”

“Then you could?”

Vikir ignored her.

Arguing would only encourage her.

Then—

GROWL!

The sound from the den changed.

Aiyen's playfulness vanished.

She was on her feet with a bow in hand almost instantly.

Bakira bared her teeth.

Vikir dropped the crayfish claw and crouched beside them.

The female Oxbear had turned on the male.

She showed her teeth and unsheathed her claws.

The male, apparently still trusting the previous three days of tolerance, hesitated.

The female did not.

WHAM!

One forepaw struck him across the face.

Teeth broke.

Blood scattered.

The male scrambled upright.

Aiyen's eyes lit.

“She must be confident she conceived.”

The female no longer needed him nearby.

In Oxbear territory, a former mate quickly became another predator competing for food and space.

ROAR!

The female warned him away.

The male fled.

His legs shook.

His coat had lost its shine.

The reserves of fat around his body were gone, and one side of his face was swelling from the blow.

He was exhausted, underfed, and injured.

Exactly the condition Aiyen had been waiting for.

“Follow me, slave.”

“……”

Aiyen took up her bow.

Vikir followed.

Only now was the real hunt beginning.
