The giant bird spread four pairs of wings.

Peacock-like.

Beautiful—

in a grotesque way.

Eyes covered its body.

Frost.

Ice.

Snow.

Armor made of winter.

A huge eye socket held clusters of crimson pupils.

The sharp beak—

lined with teeth.

**Andrealphus.**

Third Corpse.

Ice Duke.

One of the demons that had led humanity toward extinction.

[Water.]

[An intelligent idea.]

The demon flapped once.

Then rose instantly into the sky.

A long frost-covered tail painted a white trail.

[But useless against me.]

Andrealphus was right.

Flooding worked on something trapped on the ground.

Not something that could fly.

Had Lakedog been full—

the water might have reached higher.

The drought had reduced the volume too much.

[What will you ground-crawlers do now?]

Andrealphus climbed.

Higher.

Laughing.

The Daylilies could not reach.

Sade’s formation no longer mattered.

Sade stepped forward.

“So flying makes the formation useless.”

“Annoying.”

Orca raised his club.

“Let’s see if we can reach.”

Whip.

Club.

Aura exploded upward.

BOOM!

Andrealphus dodged easily.

Flying.

Diving.

Climbing.

Changing altitude faster than ground fighters could respond.

House Usher knights—

those freed from brainwashing—

gathered around Bianca.

“Lady Bianca!”

“What is happening?”

Bianca took command immediately.

“God-Killing Sniper Squad!”

“Stop staring.”

“Shoot that thing down!”

Bows rose.

The shooters were among the greatest marksmen in the Empire.

Still—

Andrealphus was too fast.

[Hohohoho!]

[Once I take the sky—

no one catches me.]

The boast ended quickly.

Vikir stepped onto a dead trunk slanting from the ground.

Then jumped.

Far higher than seemed possible.

*All that time in the Academy gravity chamber paid off.*

In an instant—

Vikir reached Andrealphus.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

Black Sun.

BOOM!

The rotating aura storm tore through the demon.

Andrealphus twisted away.

Barely.

A wing came off.

Then one leg.

[AAAAAGH!]

The remaining eyes burned red.

Andrealphus stopped playing.

Deep-blue magic circles opened around it.

Space twisted.

Then split.

Huge masses of ice emerged.

Not hailstones.

Icebergs.

Chunks more than ten meters across.

They fell like rain.

RUMBLE!

The ground disappeared beneath impacts.

Everyone below had to defend.

Vikir cut an incoming slab in half.

Camus burned and shattered others.

Dolores struggled—

her sacred barrier was not ideal against such overwhelming physical mass.

Sinclaire pulled gold from underground.

Built shields.

Even she began breathing hard.

“Bro.”

“We won’t last like this.”

“Maybe five minutes.”

Andrealphus laughed.

[Then perhaps I should open the Gate of Destruction right now.]

Everyone looked up.

The demon smiled.

[If I release everything I have—

I can force it partially open.]

[The cost would be my life.]

Vikir said nothing.

Andrealphus noticed.

[You.]

[You actually understand what that means.]

[I do wonder how a human knows so much.]

[But relax.]

[I’m not opening it yet.]

The demon’s beak split wider.

[Why sacrifice myself when I am already winning?]

More ice fell.

[Once you all die—

I will join the remaining two.]

[We will open the gate safely.]

[Then demons will cover this world.]

[There are so many worthless humans who crave power.]

[Recruiting servants is easy.]

Vikir looked upward.

Still steady.

“Are you really that confident?”

Andrealphus paused.

[What?]

“People who overstate confidence often hide anxiety.”

The demon laughed.

[Anxiety?]

[Me?]

[You crawl below.]

[I rain death from the sky.]

[I lost one wing and one leg because I was careless.]

[Those wounds will recover after I eat a few hundred children.]

“Maybe.”

“But you still look afraid.”

[Insect.]

“If you’re not—

prove it.”

Andrealphus’s eyes narrowed.

Vikir spoke.

“Where is the person who can kill me?”

The demon froze.

The same phrase Madeline had used on Bianca and Tudor.

Vikir continued.

“Say it three times.”

“If you can—

I’ll acknowledge your confidence.”

“I’ll accept defeat.”

“And death.”

“What?”

“Afraid?”

Andrealphus laughed.

[Pathetic provocation.]

[But fine.]

[If buying time was your goal—

you succeeded in amusing me.]

It looked toward Bianca.

The circumstances were completely different now.

Bianca had been trapped inside the mansion.

Andrealphus—

believed itself untouchable in the sky.

So it shouted.

[WHERE IS THE PERSON WHO CAN KILL ME?]

[WHERE IS THE PERSON WHO CAN KILL ME?]

[WHERE IS THE PERSON WHO CAN KILL ME?]

Pride.

Absolute confidence.

Then—

[…?]

The left half of its vision vanished.

A fraction of a second later—

BOOM!

The head snapped forward.

Eyes burst.

Bone exploded.

The left side of the skull disappeared.

[AAAAAAGH!]

Andrealphus screamed.

Vikir looked past it.

“The flood wasn’t only for trapping you.”

The torrent had done something else.

It had washed the Red Death mist away from Masyaf.

For one brief interval—

the poison receded.

Clean air entered.

And on the half-collapsed mansion’s highest spire—

a man stood.

Tall.

Emaciated.

Pale as a corpse.

Yet the sunken eyes—

still carried fire.

**Roderick Usher.**

The Bow Demon.

He held his bow toward the sky.
