# Chapter 286: Power Inflation (4)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/286.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 284 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established repo terminology controls Belial, Vikir, Night Hound, Dolores L. Quovadis, Decarabia, Baby Madam, Beelzebub, Dantalian, Andromalius, Cane Corso, and Baskerville Eighth Form.

**Belial.**

The **Worthless One**.

Sixth Corpse.

The sixth of the ten Demon Kings that descended to destroy humanity.

An ambitious monster that had worked with Ninth Corpse Dantalian—

seeking to consume both Quovadis and Bourgeois.

Now—

its true form was visible.

**The most splendid being cast out of heaven.**

That description applied only from a distance.

Behind the wealth.

Behind the decorations.

Behind Bartolomeo’s elegant human shell—

Belial was grotesque.

A giant pig’s head.

Goat horns.

Lipless gums.

Rows of blade-like teeth.

Lightning burning in the eyes.

Light billowing from them like smoke from a vast signal fire.

Breath carrying a nauseating stench.

A mouth split wide like a crack in polar ice.

Horns and wings broad enough to dominate a battlefield.

Belial’s enormous spiritual body floated like a dark haze above Bartolomeo’s flesh.

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

*Good.*

*I found him before he built his army.*

Fraud.

Confusion.

Incitement.

Division.

Belial’s specialties became most dangerous when armies—

cities—

nations—

were involved.

One-on-one was preferable.

Decarabia spoke from Vikir’s chest.

[It has been a long time.]

[The name Belial comes from the dead language of an ancient magical kingdom.]

[It means **worthless**.]

“Hack!”

Baby Madam’s entire body bristled.

She clung to Vikir’s shoulder—

eyebrows raised.

Her master’s enemy—

was apparently her enemy too.

“Saintess.”

“Behind me.”

Dolores moved closer.

Belial looked directly at her.

[When priests abandon their own god and become blasphemers—]

[there are few names worshiped more often in temples and at altars than mine.]

Mockery.

Belial had already corrupted nearly half of Quovadis.

Dolores’s jaw tightened.

*Humbert…*

Did Humbert know Bartolomeo’s true identity?

Had he knowingly cooperated with a demon while Quovadis rotted?

She remembered her adoptive father’s gaze.

Shivered.

Humbert was human.

Yet—

to Dolores—

he could still feel more frightening than a demon.

“No need to be afraid.”

Night Hound’s voice came calmly.

Dolores looked up.

The same words.

The same steadiness.

During the Dantalian battle—

when the Ninth Corpse had forced Humbert’s image into her mind—

Dolores had nearly collapsed under fear.

Then Night Hound had spoken.

*No need to be afraid.*

And she had recovered.

“Thank you.”

Dolores moved directly behind Vikir.

Then prayed.

“Lord Rune is a mighty fortress.”

“A shield.”

“A weapon.”

“He saves us in the hour of great calamity.”

White fire rose around Vikir.

It burned fiercely—

without heat.

“The ancient enemy raises his strength.”

“Schemes and power are his weapons.”

“Who can stand against him?”

“If I rely only on myself—

I will fail.”

“So one stronger than I shall fight in my place.”

“And that sacred name is…”

Dolores hesitated.

Then continued.

“…Night Hound.”

“Pilgrim of Rune.”

“Lord of hosts.”

“Who can stand against him?”

“Victory will come.”

Her hands pressed against Vikir’s back.

They were trembling.

“If I knew your real name…”

“would the blessing become stronger?”

Names carried power.

Being called could affect cause and consequence.

The boundary between spirit and flesh.

Life and death.

That was why demons guarded true names.

Demon hunters—

for different reasons—

often did the same.

“This is enough.”

Vikir stopped her.

Partly because he did not want another bond tightened unnecessarily.

Mostly because—

BOOM!

Belial attacked.

His body existed somewhere between spiritual and material.

The impact carried both kinds of force.

[INSIGNIFICANT HUMANS!]

The usual arrogance of a Demon King.

Vikir had already hunted four.

He was not impressed.

“It’ll shake.”

He warned Dolores.

Then—

FLASH!

Crimson aura erupted.

Seven large fangs.

And one slightly smaller eighth.

All eight spun like wheels—

then tore toward Belial.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

BOOM!

Belial reeled.

[HOW CAN A HUMAN POSSESS THIS MUCH POWER?!]

Vikir pressed forward.

*If there is no opening—

make one.*

The eight fangs bit again and again.

Dolores’s blessing strengthened the smallest—

the newest—

eighth fang.

CRACK!

Belial’s teeth broke.

One horn fractured.

His ornate armor split.

Bartolomeo’s human body beneath the manifestation began failing too.

*The Eighth Form still isn’t fully stable.*

*Finish this quickly.*

Vikir advanced.

Memories flashed.

Andromalius falling.

Cane Corso’s impossible strike shaking the Grave of Swords.

Dolores’s blessing surged behind him.

Vikir pushed Beelzebub harder.

BOOM!

Gold mountains collapsed under the shock.

Belial staggered.

Wounds covered the demonic body.

Dolores healed Vikir as he prepared another strike.

Then she spoke—

frustrated.

“…It’s weaker.”

“My blessing.”

“Compared with the Dantalian fight—

it’s much weaker.”

Her disappointment was obvious.

“I’ve trained so hard since then.”

“But it feels like I went backward.”

Vikir disagreed.

*Awakening is not that simple.*

What Dolores had done against Dantalian—

had been a miracle.

A momentary leap decades ahead in the understanding of her own divine power.

Whatever caused it—

whatever the conditions—

one could not build progress by waiting for luck to repeat itself.

Vikir attacked again.

Then—

[HEHEHEHE.]

Belial rose.

The damage—

already vanishing.

“…!”

Vikir cut across Belial’s chest once more.

A deep wound opened.

[Useless.]

[Ignorant human.]

Belial stretched both enormous hands outward.

Then—

CLINK.

CLINK.

CLINK.

Metal screamed from every direction.

Dolores turned.

Gold was moving.

Coins rolled in rippling streams.

Jewels followed.

Treasure moved like enormous golden serpents—

all toward Belial.

Then—

the wealth disappeared into his body.

CRACK.

A broken horn regrew.

Wounds closed.

The massive frame expanded even farther.

[EVERY TREASURE IN THIS VAULT IS MY LIFE!]

[MONEY IS POWER!]

[MONEY IS LIFE!]

[IN A WORLD THAT WORSHIPS WEALTH—]

[WEALTH ITSELF IS VITALITY!]

Dolores went pale.

The vault contained an unimaginable fortune.

Belial had turned it into a reserve of life.

Now she understood the true reason outsiders were always brought here.

The vault did not merely intimidate guests.

This was Belial’s home ground.

A place where his authority—

power—

and vitality—

were at their highest.

Vikir remained calm.

“I already knew.”

Belial’s face twisted.

Beelzebub lengthened.

Crimson aura rose again beneath Vikir’s cloak.

His red eyes darkened behind the mask.

“If money is your life…”

His voice emerged through the hound mask.

“…then I’ll bankrupt you.”

A declaration of war—

against the ruler of the golden world.
