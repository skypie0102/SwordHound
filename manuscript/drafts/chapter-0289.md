“W-what is this?”

“Am I dreaming?”

Bartolomeo—

barely conscious—

Night Hound and Dolores fought together.

Again.

Again.

Nothing became more sensible.

Dolores—

on the other hand—

went pale.

*Why is Sinclaire here?!*

*She drank the sleeping medicine!*

“Sinclaire!”

“Are you all right?”

“You look like the medicine affected you…”

“I’m…”

“I’m fine.”

“Mostly.”

“I use sleep aids a lot because my schedule is ridiculous.”

“I guess I’ve built some tolerance.”

Classes.

Part-time work.

Independent study.

Outside activities.

she was clearly not fully awake.

“I got so sleepy I stumbled.”

“I fell onto an open chest.”

“Then it closed.”

“And now…”

Then Night Hound.

“…what is happening?”

“I’ll explain later!”

“For now—

come here!”

[THEN I MAY AS WELL REMOVE THE USELESS ATTACHMENTS.]

[THE PERFORMANCE ENDS NOW.]

Demonic power surged.

BOOM!

“Sinclaire!”

Not fast enough.

Then—

CRACK!

Another from the wall.

they caught Belial’s fist.

[…?]

Then—

PSSH!

piercing him.

The vault began moving.

CLINK.

CLINK.

CLINK.

Waves of coins.

Rivers of treasure.

Sinclaire.

A worn black witch’s hat sat in her hands.

Treasure vanished into it.

Her mana rose.

**Money Hat.**

The artifact that had chosen Sinclaire after the University League.

A hat that strengthened its wearer by consuming money.

Its brim was torn.

Its fabric worn.

Its appearance unimpressive.

Its function—

perfect for this battlefield.

Sinclaire put it on.

Then cast again.

Gold coins and bars melted.

Reformed.

An enormous fist took shape.

BOOM!

Belial’s head snapped sideways.

[ANNOYING.]

Killing intent filled his goat-like eyes.

Bartolomeo moved.

Balmung bent through a bizarre path—

like a serpent with countless joints.

Sinclaire barely retreated.

Blood appeared along several shallow cuts.

“Patriarch!”

“What happened to you?!”

Dolores shouted.

“Sinclaire!”

“That isn’t the real head of House Bourgeois anymore!”

“A demon took his body!”

“What?!”

“A demon?”

“Please—

believe me!”

the explanation was interrupted.

A wire snapped taut.

Vikir flew across the battlefield.

Caught Sinclaire around the waist.

Then pulled her upward.

BOOM!

Balmung destroyed the place where she had been standing.

Sinclaire stared at the red eyes behind Night Hound’s mask.

“Put me down!”

“You criminal!”

Too much had already gone into the giant golden constructs.

*The hat can make me stronger with money.*

*But it can only consume so much so quickly.*

Vikir landed.

Set her down carefully.

Sinclaire blinked.

The gentleness surprised her.

Vikir roughened his voice deliberately.

“Stay out of this.”

“If you become a hostage—

it’ll be troublesome.”

Sinclaire’s eyes sharpened.

“…Troublesome?”

“For you?”

“If they take **me** hostage—

why would Night Hound care?”

“Do you know me?”

“You’re not exactly a hero.”

“Then why save me?”

“Am I bait?”

“Did you do this to Bartolomeo?”

“What is your relationship with President Dolores?”

Sharp questions.

Calm wording.

A blade held by someone not yet ready for what it might cut.

Vikir knew the type.

“Demons are absolute evil.”

“You’re not stupid enough to misunderstand that.”

“Why are you talking to me like a child?”

“We don’t look that far apart in age.”

“Opposing absolute evil does not automatically make someone good.”

“But when the decisive moment comes—

people still have to choose where they stand.”

“What you believe.”

“No persuasion is necessary.”

“No conversion.”

Balmung raised.

SHING!

Value.

Greed.

“Going in.”
