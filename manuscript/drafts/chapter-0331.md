# Chapter 331: Draw (1)

Highbro, Middlebro, and Lowbro stared at the fairy.

Almost mockingly.

“Perfect timing again.”

“Look at it crying.”

“It finally broke.”

“Idiot.”

The fairy was indeed screaming.

Ugly.

Desperate.

[WH O ARE YOU?!]

[WH Y ARE YOU DO ING THIS TO ME?!]

[HOW MUCH MORE ARE YOU GOING TO TAKE?!]

Vikir rubbed his chin.

Before regression—

the memoirs of great heroes had described the relationship between Amdusias and the fairies like this:

A department store—

and the brand shops inside it.

Amdusias expanded the Hell Tree.

Created the internal worlds.

Drew challengers inside.

The fairies—

managed stages.

Ran missions.

Distributed rewards.

After experiencing the tower himself—

Vikir disagreed.

A better comparison was—

a casino.

And the fairies were the gamblers working inside it.

The house never truly wanted the customer to win.

The same was true here.

Fairies usually bet that challengers would fail.

Die.

Lose everything.

The candies they carried—

became profit.

The tower’s traps were designed carefully enough that the losses would still look fair.

Perfume left in ruins.

Useful items mixed with Mimics.

Black Sea water with no buoyancy.

Hell Tree sap that became dangerous when overconsumed.

Each mechanism created conflict.

Mistakes.

Deaths.

And every death enriched the system.

Even when rewards were paid—

the fairies sometimes skimmed from them.

Hid stock.

Pocketed value.

Vikir had repeatedly broken that structure.

Partly through pre-regression knowledge.

Mostly—

through instinct sharpened by endless combat.

That was why the fairy now looked like a casino dealer who had lost every bet.

[AT THIS RATE I’M GOING BANKRUPT!]

It began vomiting candy.

Golden candy.

A lot of it.

**<Strange Candy — Level / Gold>**

Raises Level by 1.

Sweet.

**Level +1**

One Level candy was worth roughly three hundred ordinary candies.

More than twenty dropped.

Vikir collected all of them.

*I need one hundred before a certain stage.*

The plan—

was to remain Level1 until then.

Then jump directly to Level100.

He had already unlocked all six stats.

There was no need to waste more resources trying to open hidden slots.

Now—

he needed to collect golden candies while continuing to strengthen his base stats.

That would compensate for the one thing the Eighth Fang truly demanded.

*Practical experience.*

Life-and-death pressure.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Cane Corso’s lesson remained clear.

Even after stepping into the higher realm—

someone who stopped struggling—

stopped progressing.

Before entering the Hell Tree—

Vikir had already reached the Seventh Fang.

Sometimes—

an incomplete eighth appeared.

*Seven-point-five?*

The number was nonsense.

Seven was seven.

Eight was eight.

Still—

it described the gap.

If he maxed his stats inside the tower—

then restored all outside power afterward—

perhaps he could realize a complete Eighth Fang even without Dolores’s temporary amplification.

And beyond that—

one day—

the Ninth.

Then—

DING.

A system notice appeared.

**<Mission End>**

**Survivors: 76**

*When later arrivals clear their mission, earlier occupants who completed theirs will be reverse-summoned out of the tower.*

*Only eligible ship owners who completed the mission may be reverse-summoned.*

More windows followed.

**<Mission Complete>**

**Capture the Sap Pool.**

Take it from the terrifying previous occupants and survive.

**<Mission Complete>**

**Build a ship and cross the Black Sea.**

Avoid the Shadowless King of the Black Sea.

Then—

a failed mission.

**<Mission Failed>**

**Kill all living things.**

**Survivors: 0 / 77**

Dogma’s Majin mission.

The mission windows belonged to different groups.

Students.

Beetlemen.

Dogma.

The Beetlemen realized what the message meant.

They fell to their knees.

[Thank you!]

[We can finally go home!]

More than fifty of them kissed Vikir’s hands.

His boots.

Anything they could reach.

A new message appeared before them.

**<Reverse Summon of Initiators in Progress>**

**Do you accept the reverse summon?**

[Finally!]

[We’re leaving!]

[We’ll repay you outside!]

Every Beetleman accepted.

Then—

FLASH.

A pillar of light dropped from the sky.

More than fifty Beetlemen vanished.

The triplets watched.

“I’m jealous.”

“But also not jealous.”

“They were trapped here a long time.”

Vikir did not celebrate.

He walked to the place where the Beetlemen disappeared.

Then bent down.

Picked something up.

A long white hair.

Probably from an elder’s beard.

The tip—

burned black.

Vikir stared at it.

“…Did they really leave the tower?”

The triplets went silent.

The light.

The sound.

Something about it—

had not resembled normal spatial transfer.

It might have been some unknown teleportation method.

Or—

something else.

Vikir’s voice was calm.

“Temptation is everywhere.”

“If something is given without your own blood paying for it—

doubt it first.”

The triplets nodded grimly.

Then—

“Um…”

A small voice behind them.

The surviving Commoner Faction students.

Around twenty.

All terrified.

“We wanted to apologize…”

“We only followed Dogma…”

“We didn’t mean…”

They watched Vikir carefully.

Of course they did.

He had defeated Beetlemen.

A Majin.

An S-rank monster.

Opposing him was obviously suicidal.

They bowed.

Lower.

Lower.

Vikir answered simply.

“Why are you apologizing to me?”

“…What?”

“You have the wrong people.”

He turned.

Highbro.

Middlebro.

Lowbro.

The actual victims.

The commoner students went pale.

They finally understood.

Apologizing only to the person with power to punish them—

was not repentance.

It was self-preservation.

They had been afraid the triplets would retaliate.

Afraid of dying.

Afraid of being crippled.

Afraid of what might happen to families outside.

Now—

they lowered themselves before the triplets.

“We’re sorry.”

“We’ll accept whatever punishment you want.”

“Please…”

“Just leave our families out of it.”

Highbro stepped forward.

Everyone flinched.

Then—

he knelt.

One knee.

Eye level.

“I understand why you hated us.”

The commoners stared.

“Our brothers probably gave you plenty of reasons before the tower.”

Middlebro knelt too.

Lowbro followed.

“But in here—

we have to stand together.”

“The enemy is a demon.”

“Noble.”

“Commoner.”

“Tycoon.”

“None of that matters now.”

“There are only humans—

and demons.”

Highbro gave the answer.

“We forgive what you did to us.”

“And we ask you to forgive what we did before.”

Some students cried.

Others lowered their heads in silence.

Vikir watched.

*That’s what Dogma never reached.*

Not cheap mercy.

Not pretending harm never happened.

Something simpler.

When the Age of Destruction came—

class distinctions became meaningless.

Humanity survived only by standing together.

The triplets—

were beginning to understand that already.

Then—

[CHALLENGER?]

The fairy had crept close again.

[YOU’VE DONE SO WELL SO FAR.]

[NORMAL MISSIONS MUST BE BORING NOW.]

Vikir waited.

The fairy smiled nervously.

[SO…]

[HOW ABOUT WE CALL ALL THE NEXT FLOORS A DRAW?]
