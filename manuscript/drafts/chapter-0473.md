# Chapter 473: Tochka Annihilation Battle (3)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/473.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 471 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Established project terminology controls Butler Barrymore / John Barrymore, Marquis de Sade, Sadi, Isabella La Baskerville, Souare, Pavlov Van Baskerville, Flauros, Red Death, Tudor Donquixote, Bianca Usher, Cindywendy, and Tochka. Source-local refugee name **Shammua** follows the aligned witness.

The battle intensified.

Four in the afternoon.

CRACK!

A giant Poison Human split apart.

Butler Barrymore watched Marquis de Sade flick blood from his whip.

Then politely clapped.

“Your technique remains remarkable.”

Sade laughed.

“Hehehe.”

“Remains?”

“You remember my old technique?”

“To be honest—

I knew more from books and military papers than from direct observation.”

Barrymore smiled.

“But the records undersold you.”

**John Barrymore.**

A man who had served House Baskerville across four generations.

And now—

cutting down Poison Humans with frightening ease.

Sade glanced over.

“Strange, isn’t it?”

“What is?”

“The fact that all of us—

including me—

are following your command.”

Barrymore’s sword moved without disturbing his posture.

“People who once fought one another over the fate of the Empire—

now fighting on the same side.”

Vikir watched.

Almost distracted.

“I didn’t know the butler was this skilled.”

Barrymore chuckled.

“I may look civilized now—

but my body has plenty of experience with dogfights.”

“When I was young—

I even sparred with the Head regularly.”

“My win rate was a little better then.”

Vikir blinked.

He remembered elderly Baskervilles once muttering—

*That old delinquent finally grew into a proper man.*

*Age must have softened him.*

Looking at the perfect mustache and immaculate manners—

it was hard to imagine.

Barrymore looked at Vikir.

His expression softened.

“You truly have grown, Young Master.”

“I still remember the day you picked chocolate in the food warehouse.”

“The Head would be proud.”

“….”

At the word **Head**—

Vikir went quiet.

The full military strength of Baskerville seemed gathered here.

Except—

Hugo.

Barrymore understood the look.

“The Head has already assembled the regular forces.”

“He is marching here directly.”

“He’s slower than the units that went around the civil-war fronts—

because he’s breaking through them.”

Then—

BOOM!

The conversation vanished beneath an explosion.

A Poison Human flew apart.

Two furious voices collided.

“You insane bitch!”

“Watch where you swing!”

**Sadi.**

Demonized through Belial’s Eye.

Across from her—

another woman.

“Oh.”

“Sorry.”

“I didn’t realize you were human.”

“With that much demonic energy—

be careful where you stand.”

**Isabella La Baskerville.**

Her apology—

was not remotely sincere.

She swung again.

The blade passed far too close to Sadi.

Sadi ducked.

Growled.

“Oho.”

“You poisonous bitch.”

“Still trying to stab me while pretending it was an accident?”

Isabella’s eyes were flat.

“You’re one to speak.”

“Traitor blood rarely improves with age.”

Sadi smiled with too many teeth.

“Then why did you recommend traitor blood for the Imperial capture unit?”

“And as your successor?”

“One of the greatest regrets of my life.”

Isabella seemed fully willing to erase that regret—

during the battle.

Sadi looked just as enthusiastic about killing her former superior.

Then—

BOOM!

A stone spear burst from the earth between them.

A falling Poison Human was pinned before touching the ground.

Both women lost a few hairs.

And stepped apart.

A mocking voice came from nearby.

“Shame.”

“If it were just a little wider—

both of you would be dead.”

**Souare.**

Three women looked at one another.

Sadi laughed.

“What is this?”

“A university reunion?”

“All three classmates together?”

Isabella’s lip curled.

“Don’t mention my alma mater while standing beside me.”

Souare smiled.

“Nice seeing old dorm roommates.”

“Prison life gets boring.”

Sadi.

Isabella.

Souare.

All graduates of Temisquira Women’s College.

Three talented women—

who had gone in completely different directions.

One—

a villain who shook the Empire.

One—

a Count supporting a great house.

One—

a symbol of its worst prison.

Three people who probably never expected to meet again.

Now—

doing the same thing.

Trying to survive.

BOOM.

Another giant fell.

Smaller Poison Humans filled the gap immediately.

The higher the corpse piles grew—

the thicker Red Death became.

Six in the evening.

The enemy looked more numerous.

Perhaps—

because the allies were fewer.

Vikir remained at the very front.

Killing.

Without pause.

No one knew how long the priests’ blessings could continue.

Then—

Vikir saw an old familiar face.

An elderly Baskerville.

One he had seen several times—

before regression and after.

From Hugo’s generation.

*Pavlov?*

Yes.

**Pavlov Van Baskerville.**

An old hound.

His sword teeth were almost completely gone.

The blade—

more like a battered saw.

CRUNCH.

He struck a Poison Human in the skull.

The weapon no longer cut.

It crushed.

*Can’t even produce a thread of aura anymore?*

Vikir moved to help.

Then—

THUNK!

A spear came from the front.

Pierced Pavlov through the abdomen.

“Hey!”

Vikir rushed over.

Caught the old man before he fell.

“Stay conscious!”

Pavlov looked at him.

Blankly.

Then smiled.

“You’re a good man.”

“….”

“It’s fine.”

“I’ll do my work.”

“You do yours.”

He drew one final breath.

Then—

used it all.

“HIGH-RANKING LEVIATHAN ON THE FRONT!”

The shout crossed the battlefield.

Everyone turned.

The fog ahead—

thicker.

Redder.

Then the figure emerged.

**Flauros.**

The Lying Leopard.

One arm had become grotesquely long.

He grinned down at Vikir.

There was no doubt—

Flauros had thrown the spear that killed Pavlov.

[You’re finished.]

Flauros looked past Vikir.

Toward Tochka.

[You survived this long without drinking.]

[Impressive.]

[But prayer has limits.]

[Time to face reality—

insects.]

Then the wave arrived.

Poison Humans.

More.

And more.

“RETREAT!”

Sade’s voice—

for once—

carried genuine urgency.

“EVERYONE BACK!”

“INSIDE!”

“CLOSE THE GATES!”

Orca clenched his teeth.

BOOM!

The giant Poison Humans began climbing the walls.

Smaller ones used their bodies as ladders.

“Oil!”

“Fire!”

“Powder!”

“Archers!”

“Gunners!”

“USE EVERYTHING!”

“Nothing left to save!”

Orca and Sade—

same conclusion.

Tochka emptied every reserve into the assault.

Still—

not enough.

Poison Human corpses formed stairs.

The once-steep walls—

became slopes.

“…It’s over.”

Cindywendy watched from the tower.

Her voice was hoarse.

She had not properly drunk in days.

And she—

who understood supplies better than anyone—

could see defeat.

So could everyone else.

BOOM!

Blood ran from Tudor’s forehead.

His legs shook.

Saintess’s Tears had nearly lost their strength.

Flags—

torn.

Knights—

gray-faced.

Priests—

crying instead of praying.

Heads rose from the Red Death sea.

“…Is this really the end?”

Tudor smiled weakly.

Even heroic epics—

eventually ended.

Dying beneath a blood-red sky—

maybe that was not the worst final page.

Then—

tap.

A tiny sound reached him.

Clear—

even through explosions.

Tap.

Again.

A pebble.

It struck a Poison Human on the forehead.

“…?”

Tudor turned.

A girl stood on the wall.

Trembling.

Terrified.

But not retreating.

He remembered her.

*My name is Shammua!*

*Benefactor, may I know your name?*

*I’m Tudor.*

*No surname.*

A girl from a small village.

**Shammua.**

A common name.

Meaning—

**God has heard.**

Now—

she was throwing stones.

“Tudor!”

“I’ll help too!”

Tudor stared.

Bianca gave him a sideways look.

“When did you charm her?”

“What?!”

“This is dangerous!”

“She needs to get down—”

Then Tudor stopped.

Behind Shammua—

her family.

Behind them—

the entire village.

And behind the village—

refugees.

Hundreds.

Thousands.

Angry.

Resolved.

“LET US FIGHT!”

“We can’t only hide while you protect us!”

“We can help!”

“I’m a veteran!”

“You shared food and water with us!”

“We repay debts!”

“Protect the Night Walkers!”

The bunker doors opened.

Refugees poured out.

Men picked up bows.

Spears.

Tools.

Stones.

Women treated wounded soldiers.

Carried whatever precious water remained.

Tochka—

an impregnable fortress—

had been built so that even a single defender could hold against many attackers.

Now—

every hand became a defender.

Stones rained down.

Poison Humans climbing the walls—

fell.

Again.

And again.

“…!”

From the tower—

Cindywendy’s eyes changed.

The fire she thought had gone out—

burned again.

A counterattack no strategist had predicted—

was beginning.
