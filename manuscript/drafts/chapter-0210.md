**Bianca Fo Usher.**

Eighteen years old.

Prodigy of House Usher—

“I've been watching you for a long time.”

“I like you.”

She remembered him.

A few joint classes—

with the Hot Department.

Bianca stroked—

the fake mustache beneath her nose.

Then—

evaluated.

Her vision—

sharp enough to hit a needle's eye from a hundred paces—

moved across him.

*Makeup too heavy.*

*Minus eleven.*

**7 / 100.**

The boy—

was not actually ugly.

Bianca's standards.

Badly.

*If you're going to dress up—*

*at least be pretty.*

beautiful things.

Good food.

Pretty dolls.

Fine weapons.

Handsome men.

Beautiful women.

Beautiful horses.

Elegant saddles.

her indifference to men.

Most men—

did not try hard enough to be beautiful.

other men mocked them.

So Bianca—

generally—

lost interest.

Since entering school—

she had rejected—

seniors.

classmates.

everyone.

“Bianca!”

“Want to see the new play?”

“I already watched the first three.”

“Dinner?”

“I already ate tomorrow's lunch.”

“Temple this weekend?”

“I'm a heretic.”

“When are you free?”

“Never.”

“Christmas—”

“No.”

Pretty girls—

were easier company.

If there was an exception—

it was probably—

Tudor.

Her childhood enemy.

Friend.

Irritation.

Whatever he was.

*I just like beautiful things.*

Bianca had tried—

once—

to feel guilty about that.

Taste—

was taste.

*If a man scores over twenty—

maybe.*

status—

*This is who I am.*
