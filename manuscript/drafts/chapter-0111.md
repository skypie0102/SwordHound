# Chapter 111: The Great Banquet (2)

Moonlight from the window fell across the intruder's face.

Vikir released the hand around her throat.

Short green-gold hair framed a familiar, cheerful expression.

Cindywendy.

She had come in person after learning Vikir had returned alive.

She winked up at him.

“Why not stay like this a little longer? It isn't exactly bad for you.”

“Enough nonsense. Get to the point.”

Vikir moved off her and sat at the edge of the bed.

Cindywendy rubbed the neck he had nearly crushed.

“You really do charge straight ahead every time. Doesn't living without detours get boring?”

“Get to the point before I spank you and throw you out.”

“Wow. Spanking? Are you sure you're only seventeen? Sometimes you sound forty… no, sixty.”

She sprawled diagonally across the bed as if it belonged to her and propped herself up.

Then narrowed her eyes.

“More importantly, you sent people looking for me. How did you know where I was?”

“Your habits.”

“My habits?”

“You find someone with money but little experience. You help increase their wealth, earn their trust, and eventually take your share.”

“How rude. I don't *take* anything. I'm a financial planner. I collect a proper commission.”

Vikir thought of the flower girl whose fortune had exploded under his old special ordinance.

He had made sure she received guards so she could live safely without losing the money.

But protecting wealth and knowing how to use it were different things.

The girl had possessed an enormous fortune and no experience managing it.

Cindywendy had noticed.

She approached the girl, became her financial planner, and began handling the money.

“We're practically sisters now,” Cindywendy said. “I've already made her a great deal more. She's so innocent that a real swindler would have eaten her alive.”

“That was why I assigned guards.”

“Guards protect people. Since when do they screen financial scams?”

“Some of them are financial specialists. They were ordered to filter out petty fraudsters.”

Cindywendy's expression emptied.

“…Those guards never interfered with me at all. I thought they knew nothing about finance.”

“They were also ordered to let you approach.”

Silence.

Vikir had expected the rumor of a flower girl possessing ten billion gold to draw Cindywendy sooner or later.

The girl had been bait.

Her fortune, the hook.

The guards, the net that would tell Vikir where Cindywendy surfaced.

He had captured Cindywendy directly at the slave auction before the bait could serve its original purpose.

But it had still allowed him to track her later.

Cindywendy fell backward onto the bed.

“No wonder. I couldn't understand how Chihuahua knew where to send his message.”

She stared at the ceiling.

“So everything was inside your calculations from the beginning?”

“Confirmation is useful.”

“…You're no fun.”

She complained under her breath, then reached into the front of her clothes.

A moment later, she produced a crushed bundle of papers and tossed it over.

“The information you wanted.”

Vikir unfolded the pages.

“…Next time, use a folder.”

The papers contained information on people expected at tomorrow's Great Banquet.

*If they intend to reward my achievements, an invitation was inevitable. Researching the attendees beforehand was the correct choice.*

Cindywendy's appearance the night before the banquet was part of Vikir's preparation.

Then she raised one finger.

“That's only half.”

Vikir looked up.

Cindywendy pointed toward the other side of her chest.

“The rest comes after you pay the information fee.”

“Information fee?”

“Did you think I worked for free?”

Vikir nodded.

Cindywendy stared at him.

A moment later, he reached into a pocket and tossed something across the room.

Snap.

She caught it.

A white, sharpened tooth lay in her palm.

An **Oxbear fang**.

Cindywendy's eyes widened.

“This? You're joking.”

An Oxbear fang was enormously valuable.

Carved into luxury pieces, one could be worth millions in gold.

But by the standards of the information she had gathered, it was still cheap.

Her mouth opened to complain.

Vikir spoke first.

“I have thousands more.”

“…?”

She stopped.

“Tens of thousands, perhaps. Follow my plan, and you can receive a cartload every year.”

Cindywendy's eyes sharpened.

Vikir was talking about trade with the tribes of the Depths.

He had become a hero among the people living throughout Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.

With Vikir mediating, those tribes would willingly supply rare medicines and luxury materials gathered from the wilds.

Antlers.

Bear gall.

Seal kidney.

Truffles.

And countless other products the Empire valued far more than the tribes did.

In exchange, cheap Imperial manufactured goods could flow the other way.

A trade route on that scale would create a distribution network for luxury goods unlike anything the region had seen.

A mega-deal.

Any fund manager or financial planner would salivate over it.

Cindywendy more than most.

Her eyes shone.

She immediately produced the remaining papers.

“The tribes will really agree?”

“They will.”

Vikir had support across the allied tribes.

If he chose to broker the arrangement, a vast trade route was possible.

It could reduce the pressure that created border war while triggering enormous economic changes.

For the moment, only two people understood the scale of the opportunity.

Vikir.

And Cindywendy.

She straightened, folded her legs beneath herself, and presented both sets of documents with exaggerated respect.

“Starting today, I think I'll call you Chairman.”

“Call me whatever you want.”

Vikir was already reading.

The first report concerned the identity he most wanted confirmed.

**Pomeranian La Baskerville**

**Relationship:** Granddaughter of Hugo Le Baskerville, Patriarch of House Baskerville  
**Estimated age:** Five  
**Mother:** Penelope La Baskerville  
**Maternal grandmother:** Lady Roxana

The names in the raw records varied, but the lineage matched what Vikir had learned from Barrymore.

Cindywendy's investigation confirmed it.

Pomeranian was Hugo's direct descendant.

Hugo had married Lady Roxana when he was twenty.

Roxana gave birth to Penelope La Baskerville.

Later, Roxana died of illness.

Penelope eventually disappeared in an unexplained incident and fell into the hands of Rokoko hunters.

She gave birth to Pomeranian and later died as well.

*There is information about that unexplained incident too.*

Vikir noticed a note at the bottom.

For now, however, the important part was certain.

Penelope had disappeared roughly one year before Vikir was born.

Hugo had been about forty-one.

The dates fit.

A suspicion had become fact.

Vikir turned the page.

The rest of the file listed major figures expected at the Great Banquet.

**Hugo Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Patriarch  
**Title:** Marquis  
**Sword Realm:** Sword Master  
**Other:** Father of numerous direct, illegitimate, collateral, and adopted descendants…

The remaining details were abbreviated.

Hugo naturally stood first.

The important names began beneath his.

**Boston Terrier Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Commander of the Pit Bull Knights; Upper Council member  
**Title:** Count  
**Sword Realm:** Peak Sword Graduator  
**Other:** Half-brother of Hugo Le Baskerville. Belligerent, passionate, and intensely hostile to restraint.

Vikir paused.

Boston Terrier commanded one of Baskerville's seven great knight orders and was one of the **Seven Counts** who represented the clan's military power.

He normally hated stiff ceremonial gatherings.

*Why is he attending the Great Banquet?*

Vikir turned the page.

**Great Dane Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Commander of the Mastiff Knights; Upper Council member  
**Title:** Count  
**Sword Realm:** Peak Sword Graduator  
**Other:** Half-brother of Hugo Le Baskerville. Reserved and taciturn. Avoids needless enemies, but has a notoriously poor relationship with Boston Terrier.

Great Dane had also officially announced his attendance.

Another of the Seven Counts.

Another man who almost never appeared at internal family functions.

And now both half-brothers were rushing to a banquet hosted by Hugo.

Cindywendy laughed quietly.

“Are you trying to figure out why they're coming?”

“Yes.”

“You really don't know?”

She pointed directly at Vikir.

“Because of you. What else?”

“…Me?”

“The Seven Counts and the other upper councillors almost never interfere in ordinary family affairs. Yet suddenly all seven wanted seats at the Great Banquet.”

Her grin widened.

“They wanted to see the little legend of Baskerville who came back from the dead.”

“All seven?”

“Every one of them requested to attend.”

That did surprise Vikir.

Most ordinary Baskervilles could live their entire lives without seeing one of the Seven Counts up close.

Only Boston Terrier and Great Dane appeared on the final attendance list.

“The other five are deployed too far away,” Cindywendy explained. “They couldn't return in time. Boston and Great Dane were working close enough to ride through the night.”

“They did not come only to look at my face.”

“Of course not. They want you in their knight orders.”

A direct-blood banquet required personal attendance.

No proxy could take a seat.

That left the two Counts whose deployments allowed them to return.

“You're going to be buried in recruitment offers.”

Cindywendy looked delighted by the thought.

The Pit Bull Knights and Mastiff Knights were already famous rivals.

Tomorrow, their commanders would sit at one table.

And Vikir would be between them like a piece of meat both wanted.

Vikir's answer was simple.

“…I'm interested in neither.”

He only hoped they would not become too troublesome.

He turned the page.

**Osiris Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Lower Council member  
**Title:** Viscount  
**Sword Realm:** Peak Sword Graduator  
**Other:** Eldest son of Hugo Le Baskerville. Current first heir.

Osiris was regarded as the greatest talent among Baskerville's next generation.

A cold, merciless genius said to resemble Hugo in his youth.

He could already manifest **six Fangs** from the tip of his blade.

Rumor claimed Sword Master lay within reach.

“…Osiris.”

Vikir remembered watching him from a distance before regression.

Silent.

Cold.

Someone who did not greet collateral relatives and barely greeted his own siblings.

His overwhelming strength and presence discouraged anyone from approaching first.

A solitary genius.

“Aside from Hugo and the Seven Counts,” Cindywendy said, watching Vikir's expression, “he should be the strongest Baskerville, shouldn't he?”

“…”

“Unless you're stronger?”

Vikir turned the page without answering.

**Seth Le Baskerville**

**Position:** Magistrate of Underdog City; Lower Council member  
**Title:** Viscount  
**Sword Realm:** Intermediate Sword Graduator  
**Other:** Second son of Hugo Le Baskerville. Current second heir.

Seth remained vivid in Vikir's memory.

He had never possessed his older brother's sword talent.

He had been sickly from childhood.

Osiris earned a viscountcy through military achievement.

Seth's title was largely honorary.

Gentle and considerate by Baskerville standards, he had always looked weak in Hugo's eyes.

Hugo treated his second son with particular coldness.

Seth remained kind anyway.

He was the only direct descendant Vikir remembered mourning the hounds who died like discarded tools.

Vikir had seen him leave flowers at memorials more than once.

*He's supposed to be in closed training. He must have come out for the banquet.*

Vikir continued.

Near the bottom were three familiar names.

**Highbro Le Baskerville**

**Status:** Academy candidate  
**Title:** Young Master  
**Sword Realm:** Advanced Sword Expert  
**Other:** Direct descendant of Hugo Le Baskerville.

**Middlebro Le Baskerville**

**Status:** Academy candidate  
**Title:** Young Master  
**Sword Realm:** Advanced Sword Expert  
**Other:** Direct descendant of Hugo Le Baskerville.

**Lowbro Le Baskerville**

**Status:** Academy candidate  
**Title:** Young Master  
**Sword Realm:** Advanced Sword Expert  
**Other:** Direct descendant of Hugo Le Baskerville.

Vikir closed the dossier with a dry smile.

“Those are unexpected names.”

For the first time—

he found himself mildly curious about tomorrow's Great Banquet.
