CRACKLE—

A fissure suddenly tore open in midair.

Passamonte recoiled.

“What is this?!”

Nothing inside the Hell Tree should have been visible to anyone except its master, Amdusias.

Yet now—

a burning portal split apart.

A blade thrust through.

And for one instant—

mist clouded Passamonte’s sight.

A strange voice brushed across his mind.

*…What a good person.*

Weak.

Unsteady.

Like the final breath of a dying beast.

Then came visions.

A swamp of flesh and blood.

Mountains of bones.

Air drained of mana.

A gigantic mushroom cloud rising beyond the horizon.

An endless wasteland.

A colossal tower in the middle of that wasteland.

And—

an old man in black—

walking alone toward it.

The images stabbed into Passamonte in disconnected fragments.

“…Amdusias’s memories?”

“What did that bastard see before he died?”

Passamonte rubbed his temples.

Something had distorted causality itself.

Something capable of closing the spaces demons opened.

Whatever it was—

it had to be connected to the sword cutting through the world in front of him.

Then—

Vikir stepped through the torn Hell Tree.

Passamonte stared.

“How did you rip open the Hell Tree?”

“That doesn’t look like the sword’s innate ability.”

“Blood.”

“Whose blood?”

Vikir gave no further answer.

He drew Beelzebub.

“Find out yourself.”

FLASH.

**Baskerville Fourth Form.**

The fastest—

most efficient—

most natural technique in Vikir’s body.

Passamonte had no choice but to rise.

BOOM!

A stone pillar split.

Dust filled the hall.

Behind Vikir—

Dolores.

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Sinclaire.

All of them emerged with him.

And as the dust cleared—

the true enemy inside House Donquixote finally stood exposed.

Black armor.

Black skin.

A body so dark that eyes and nose disappeared into shadow.

Only white—

razor-sharp teeth—

remained visible.

A huge spear rested in one hand.

Below the waist—

the body merged with a black horse.

The horse’s head—

nothing but skull.

The blazing black knight before them—

was no longer merely **Passamonte**.

**<Fourth Corpse — Chimeries>**

**Danger Rating:** S+  
**Size:** ?  
**Discovery Location:** Deep within the Gate of Destruction — ‘Serpent’s Womb’  
**Alias:** ‘Fourth Corpse’

One of the Ten Corpses.

An incomprehensible and supposedly unkillable calamity—

a natural enemy of mankind.

**“Fearless frogs shall rise.”**

**—『The Decalogue』 10:Upper—**

The **Reckless Knight**.

Chimeries.

Another central monster of the Age of Destruction.

Vikir spoke to everyone behind him.

“Do not breathe his exhaled aura if you can avoid it.”

“It creates confidence without reason.”

“Excitement.”

“Recklessness.”

“It ruins judgment.”

Like a frog puffing itself up to rival a bull—

until its body burst.

Chimeries’s authority made opponents brave beyond sanity.

Combined with strategists like Dantalian and Belial—

the result had once been catastrophic.

Before regression—

the Invincible Cavalry of House Donquixote had charged windmills under Chimeries’s influence.

And been annihilated.

*He dies here.*

*Before the great upheaval.*

*And Donquixote comes back to humanity.*

Vikir stepped forward.

“Vikir!”

Dolores moved beside him.

“I’ll support you.”

Her blessing descended.

Their souls—

now deeply synchronized—

resonated.

Light wrapped around Vikir’s aura.

BOOM!

Sword and spear collided.

Neither side moved.

Again.

BOOM!

CRACK!

Chimeries’s spinning spear produced a black storm.

It twisted—

lengthened—

became the shape of a serpent.

Vikir’s fang-shaped trajectories gathered into a sphere.

A snake opening its jaws—

to swallow the sun.

Their clash tore the castle apart.

One thrust from Chimeries—

and layered walls burst open.

One swing from Vikir—

and everything behind the target line split—

revealing the world beyond the castle.

[Impossible.]

[How can a human possess such power?]

Chimeries sounded genuinely baffled.

Vikir was thinking about something else.

*He’s already recovered to roughly his old strength.*

*The demons are accelerating.*

The Fourth Corpse.

Then the Third.

Second.

First.

Waiting any longer would be dangerous.

Vikir made a decision.

“We attack together.”

Everyone froze for half a second.

The old Vikir—

the one they remembered—

would have tried to handle everything himself.

This Vikir did not.

Their faces brightened.

“Let us help!”

“Trust us!”

“We haven’t spent four years training for nothing!”

“Use my blood!”

“Just give me an opening!”

“Bro—keep some distance and I’ll cover you with magic!”

Dolores’s sacred shield.

Tudor’s spear.

Sancho’s axe.

Pigi’s sword.

Bianca’s arrows.

Sinclaire’s magic.

Six different powers struck at once.

[Annoying insects—!]

Chimeries swept his spear backward.

[Long time no see, Chimeries.]

A red inverted pentagram appeared.

Decarabia blocked the strike.

Before Chimeries could react—

THUD!

Night Hound’s fangs sank into his neck.

Sharpened further by Dolores’s blessing.

Black flesh tore.

[GUAAAGH!]

Dark foam spilled from Chimeries’s mouth.

He staggered back—

letting a chunk of his own throat rip free.

Then—

Passamonte’s face surfaced from the horse-like chest.

Blood ran from its eyes.

[Fine.]

[I admit it.]

[You are troublesome enemies.]

His voice—

was still calm.

This was his stronghold.

His prepared battlefield.

House Donquixote itself was still under his control.

[Then I will answer with everything I have.]

A blue horn appeared.

Passamonte’s mouth blew into it.

Tudor’s face changed immediately.

“That horn…”

“It summons the Invincible Cavalry!”

The unit had already been recalled from the civil-war front.

The result came quickly.

TOOOOOOT—

The floor began to tremble.

Small stones hopped.

THUD.

THUD.

THUD.

Dolores and Sinclaire looked toward the distance.

“Horses…”

“The cavalry!”

Countless riders were approaching.

House Donquixote’s greatest land force.

The **Invincible Cavalry**.

Together with the Invincible Armada—

one of the two pillars of the family.

And this—

was the full cavalry strength.

Tudor shouted.

“Vikir!”

“The Invincible Cavalry can compete with Baskerville’s Seven Counts!”

“If they join this fight—

we lose!”

The lowered sea level at least kept the Armada away.

If the fleet’s bombardment joined the cavalry—

there would be no answer.

Vikir parried Chimeries’s spear.

“I know the Invincible Cavalry.”

And he knew they were worse now.

Chimeries’s authority had made them recklessly confident.

Even Vikir could not face every knight in a blind full-force charge.

Yet—

he remained calm.

“Don’t worry.”

“I prepared for them.”

Beyond the damaged wall—

the cavalry became visible.

A massive charge rolling across the horizon.

Then—

BOOM!

The first horse went down.

Knight and mount tumbled together.

Then another.

And another.

CRASH!

RUMBLE!

The formation collapsed.

Iron loops protruded from the ground.

Small traps—

like nails bent into arches—

positioned exactly to catch hooves.

The cavalry’s own speed became its enemy.

Horses fell.

Riders collided.

Momentum disappeared.

Formation shattered.

[What is this?!]

Chimeries stared.

The Invincible Cavalry could not even cross the coastal approach.

Then—

FLAP.

A red cloak snapped from the roof of an opposite tower.

“Hey!”

“Boyfriend!”

“Over here!”

Everyone looked up.

The person who had planted thousands of bent iron traps across the approach—

and complete confidence.

**Camus Morgue.**
