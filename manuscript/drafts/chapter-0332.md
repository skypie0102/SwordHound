# Chapter 332: Draw (2)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/332.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 330 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Existing repo terminology controls Return Scroll, Daylily of Blood Tree, golden Level candies, hidden pieces, and tower stat mechanics. Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

[HOW ABOUT WE CALL ALL THE NEXT FLOORS A DRAW?]

The fairy’s proposal was absurd.

Vikir was currently on Basement Floor4.

Next came Floor5.

Then Floor6.

And so on.

The fairy wanted to skip them.

[THAT’S EXACTLY WHAT I MEAN.]

If Vikir continued normally—

he would likely clear hidden stages.

Find hidden pieces.

Exploit traps.

Take rewards meant to be practically impossible.

The fairy had already seen enough.

So it proposed a deal.

Skip a large portion of the floors under its management—

and receive ordinary stage-clear rewards anyway.

“So if I behave—

you’ll move me quickly through the next section.”

[YES.]

“And what do I get?”

The fairy raised ten fingers.

Vikir understood immediately.

“Ten golden candies.”

[YES…]

The fairy nodded.

Then begged:

[PLEASE.]

[IF THIS KEEPS GOING I REALLY WILL GO BANKRUPT.]

The triplets exploded.

“You shameless thing!”

“Our master never takes an easy compromise!”

“Never!”

“Never!”

Vikir ignored them.

“Add more.”

The triplets froze.

The fairy did too.

“You want me to skip multiple floors—

for only ten Level candies?”

[TH-THEN…]

[A LITTLE MORE?]

Vikir shook his head.

“I have enough candy.”

“There’s an item I want.”

The fairy blinked.

Until now—

Vikir had prioritized candy.

Tower items could not normally be taken outside.

So they ranked lower.

Now—

he named one directly.

“What item?”

“The **Scale of Forced Equality**.”

The fairy’s face changed.

[THAT IS A HIDDEN PIECE FROM FLOOR3—]

It stopped.

Too late.

Vikir had heard enough.

“So it exists on Floor3.”

“The Daylily area.”

The fairy recovered slightly.

[BUT YOU ALREADY PASSED FLOOR3.]

Vikir pulled out a scroll.

**<Return Scroll>**

**Rank:** ?

One-time item.

Returns the user to the Tutorial Zone.

Contains the answer to the question:

*Lord, where are you going?*

**Using it resets Level.**

Vikir was still Level1.

The drawback meant nothing.

Stats would remain.

He looked at the fairy.

“Thanks for the information.”

“I’ll use this.”

“Return to the tutorial.”

“Clear every hidden stage again.”

“Take every hidden piece.”

“And when I reach Floor3—

I’ll get the Scale of Forced Equality myself.”

The fairy’s face collapsed.

[HIC.]

It dug through its belongings.

Then produced a thick book.

**<Item Catalog — Hidden Pieces>**

1. Demon-Calling Flute — 500 Red Candies  
2. Retribution Iron Armor — 250 Green Candies  
3. Beginner Regeneration Potion — 400 Blue Candies  
4. Secret Pickpocket Gloves — 50 Green + 50 Blue  
5. Ten-Thousand-Swing Greatsword — 1,000 Red + 800 Blue  
6. Hourglass of Lust — 1 Golden Candy

Vikir flipped farther.

Then found it.

**203. Scale of Forced Equality**

**Stock:** 0  
**Price:** 20 Golden Candies

An absurd price.

The fairy pointed at the stock count.

[ZERO.]

Vikir did not move.

“You hid some.”

[WHAT?]

“I’m paying.”

“Not stealing.”

He placed twenty golden candies on the ground.

Then added miscellaneous candies gathered from the commoner students.

The fairy hesitated.

Vikir leaned closer.

“Or I can use the Return Scroll.”

“Take every hidden piece.”

“Then report to upper management that Floor4’s administrator has been hiding inventory.”

“Maybe they should audit your stock.”

The fairy folded instantly.

[FINE!]

[I’LL SELL IT!]

Crying—

it accepted the candy.

The catalog page shifted.

What looked like one sheet—

was actually two pages stuck together.

The fairy peeled one away.

**203. Scale of Forced Equality**

**Stock:** 0  
**Price:** 20 Golden Candies

*Vendor note: Hidden Piece obtainable only on Floor3.*

*Good ability, terrible cost efficiency.*

*Nearly useless early.*

*Extremely valuable late.*

*Almost nobody survives long enough while still carrying it.*

Then—

POP.

The catalog page transformed.

A weight.

Skull-shaped.

A scale motif carved across it.

**<Scale of Forced Equality>**

**Grade:** S

All stats gradually rise toward the value of the owner’s highest stat.

The effect continues until all stats are equal.

Then the item disappears.

If Strength were 10—

and every other stat 1—

the other stats would slowly rise toward 10.

Powerful.

But painfully slow.

Unless someone expected to remain inside the tower for years—

it seemed inefficient.

That was why the fairy had finally agreed to sell it.

Then Vikir noticed a small note attached.

The legitimate acquisition condition.

**<Hidden Piece Acquisition Condition>**

Challengers who encounter the Daylily of Blood Tree must vote each day to choose one sacrifice.

But—

the order is reversed.

The strongest are sacrificed first.

One after another.

The final weak survivor—

receives the item.

Vikir stared.

*So that was how it was obtained.*

Before regression—

tower veterans had discovered that one late-game villain possessed this item.

They understood what it did.

Never how it had been acquired.

Now Vikir knew why.

The method was absurd.

A whole group—

systematically sacrificing its strongest members—

until only the weakest remained.

And yet—

someone before regression had truly obtained it.

The fairy muttered bitterly.

[YOU’RE GOING TO FLOOR5 NOW.]

[DIFFERENT FAIRIES FROM HERE ON.]

[MEANER MANAGERS.]

[SCARY, RIGHT?]

[YOU WERE LUCKY TO HAVE ME.]

A gate appeared.

Basement Floor5.

Single-person entry.

Everyone would be scattered again.

[ONE AT A TIME.]

The fairy watched the challengers enter.

But its eye stayed on Vikir longest.

Then—

quietly—

[BEING WITH YOU WAS DISGUSTING.]

[LET’S NEVER MEET AGAIN.]
