# Chapter 290: Power Inflation (8)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/290.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 288 is the verified shifted secondary witness. The counterfeit-currency sequence is preserved at story level only; production prose does not add practical real-world replication detail. Established repo terminology controls Cindywendy, Damien Bourgeois, Belial, Vikir, Dolores L. Quovadis, Decarabia, Old Testament Faction, indulgence, Baskerville Eighth Form, Sword Master, Beelzebub, and the Worthless One.

**Inflation.**

An excess of currency—

reducing the value of currency itself.

◆◆◆

Across the Imperial Capital—

and other major commercial centers—

huge balloons rose at the same time.

They climbed.

Expanded.

Then burst.

Money rained down.

Coins.

Banknotes.

More than anyone could immediately verify.

The sudden flood produced exactly what Vikir wanted.

Distrust.

“Banknotes?”

“No.”

“I’d rather barter.”

“How do I know these coins are real?”

“I’m not accepting deposits for now.”

“I thought I was rich when money started falling from the sky.”

“Guess I celebrated too early.”

“At this point we may as well burn the paper for fuel.”

“Use the coins as weights.”

“Is the country collapsing?”

People began hoarding necessities.

Merchants became unwilling to trade useful goods for currency they could not trust.

High above the Capital—

two people watched the chaos.

Cindywendy.

And Damien Bourgeois.

Cindywendy looked sideways.

“…Is the Director of the Imperial Money Manufacturing Bureau really allowed to do this?”

Damien said nothing for a moment.

Below them—

the fake currency looked almost indistinguishable from the genuine article at a glance.

Damien had arranged the temporary imitations.

Cindywendy—

with her enormous logistics network—

had handled distribution.

Then Damien spoke.

“If you’re digging graves—

dig two.”

“One for my brother.”

“One for me.”

Cindywendy smiled faintly.

“Good attitude.”

“Been sharpening that knife a long time?”

“I’ve hated my family for years.”

“I never imagined I’d finally act through someone else’s hand.”

“…I can relate.”

Both had old scores.

Both—

for different reasons—

had allied themselves with Night Hound.

Cindywendy looked across the storm of paper and coins.

“We both chose a frightening man to work with.”

Damien gave a humorless smile.

A fake coin bounced against him.

Light.

Much lighter than real gold.

“The imitations are designed only for temporary confusion.”

Cindywendy looked at a drifting banknote.

“And the paper?”

“The same.”

“Two hours.”

“After that—

the magic coloring disappears and the false currency becomes obvious refuse.”

He had no intention of destabilizing the market longer than necessary.

But for those two hours—

trust in money would collapse.

People would look toward food.

Tools.

Land.

Materials.

Actual goods.

Not coins.

And as desire for **money itself** weakened—

so did the value feeding Belial.

Cindywendy frowned.

“Two hours.”

“Is that really enough?”

Damien looked toward the distant Bourgeois estate.

“For ordinary people?”

“No.”

“But two hours is enough to decide the fate of a world.”

He lowered his voice.

“For someone extraordinary—

fighting right now.”

◆◆◆

Belial panicked.

[NO.]

[IMPOSSIBLE!]

[WHY AM I RECOVERING SO SLOWLY?!]

He swallowed more wealth.

Nothing.

Money.

Human greed.

Value created by that greed.

One link in the cycle had broken.

[HUMANS ALWAYS WANT MONEY!]

[THEIR GREED HAS NO END!]

[HOW CAN THIS HAPPEN?!]

Vikir advanced.

“Gold is not omnipotent.”

*At least for two hours.*

He left that part unsaid.

The vault would open soon.

Until then—

the mountains of treasure around Belial were steadily losing the very thing that gave them power.

**Value.**

The Empire had—

temporarily—

returned to barter.

Coins without trust—

were metal.

Paper without trust—

was paper.

For Vikir—

temporary was enough.

FLASH!

Eight fangs—

strengthened by divine power—

ripped through Belial.

Dolores continued blessing Vikir.

“**Lunianus homo omnium dominus est liberrimus, nulli subiectus.**”

“A believer of Rune is a free master of all things—

subject to none.”

“**Lunianus homo omnium servus est officiosissimus, omnibus subiectus.**”

“A believer of Rune is also the faithful servant of all—

subject to everyone.”

Two souls that had lived utterly different lives—

resonated.

Vikir released everything.

Sword Master.

Intermediate solid aura.

**Baskerville Eighth Form.**

The eight rotating fangs cut through Belial.

Body trained beyond ordinary human limits.

Mind tempered through death after death.

Every muscle tightened like a drawn bow.

Vikir found the single line for the final strike.

Then—

moved.

BOOM!

Belial’s body shredded beneath the aura storm.

Bartolomeo’s human vessel was already ruined.

Belial’s manifested form—

worse.

One horn gone.

The other cracked.

Black blood pouring from the pig-like head.

Vikir stood before him.

“Stretch your neck.”

Once the hound caught prey—

it did not release.

A hound killing a boar.

Natural.

Then—

[HAHAHAHA!]

Belial laughed.

Vikir’s eyes narrowed.

“What’s funny?”

[YOUR FACES.]

[I CAN’T WAIT TO SEE THEM TWIST.]

Black smoke poured from Belial’s mouth.

Decarabia spoke quietly.

[His last authority.]

[**Oppression**.]

“What does it do?”

Decarabia hesitated.

Then answered.

[The one who kills Belial must bear the corresponding guilt.]

Belial laughed harder.

[YES!]

[WHOEVER KILLS ME DIES WITH ME!]

His eyes moved between Vikir and Dolores.

[ONE OF YOU WILL KILL ME.]

[ONE WILL DIE.]

[THE OTHER WILL GRIEVE FOR A LOST COMRADE.]

Dolores stepped forward.

“Killing a demon is not a sin.”

“And if some law calls it one—

I’ll bear it.”

Belial’s snout twitched.

[Does Rune’s doctrine not say demons too are creatures under creation?]

[Is killing a living being not still killing?]

[Are you certain, Saintess?]

A shadow of doubt crossed Dolores’s face.

Vikir cut in.

“Don’t listen to a demon’s tongue.”

SHING!

Beelzebub took Belial’s arm.

Black blood sprayed.

Belial stared.

[YOU—]

[WHY AREN’T YOU AFRAID?]

[MY WORDS—]

[THE SIN—]

Vikir ignored him.

Then slapped a blood-stained piece of paper onto Belial’s forehead.

Belial froze.

**<Indulgence>**

**‘All sins of this faithful believer are forgiven.’**

**—Issued and guaranteed by the Old Testament Faction.—**

Belial recognized it.

A memory returned.

A young man in an Old Testament temple.

*If repentance can forgive a past sin—*

*can I repent in advance for a future one?*

*What terrible crime are you planning?*

*I intend to kill my neighbor’s pig.*

*For one pig?*

*Fine. Take the indulgence.*

*Your sin is forgiven~*

One gold coin.

That was all Vikir had paid.

Belial’s expression collapsed.

[…No.]

The great Demon King—

who had monopolized enormous wealth—

was being answered with a piece of paper worth one gold.

Vikir raised Beelzebub.

“It’s over.”

“**Worthless One.**”

One short arc.

THUD.

The Sixth Demon King’s head—

fell.
