BOOM.

Rock fragments and waves of lava exploded outward.

The impact sent tremors through the worksite.

Every prisoner.

Every guard.

All of them looked up at Vikir.

“I’m not working.”

A short sentence.

But inside Nouvelle Vague—

almost revolutionary.

Prisoners worked.

Guards supervised.

That system—

was the foundation keeping the prison alive.

Silence spread.

Vikir thought calmly.

*Now the guards should start beating me.*

According to the Nouvelle Vague rules he remembered—

if a prisoner refused labor—

the guards could use whips.

Clubs.

Blades.

Whatever was necessary.

But—

that was not what happened.

“AAAAH!”

“LEVEL NINE RIOT!”

“This is beyond us!”

“Request backup!”

“Blow the alarm!”

Faces turned white.

The guards screamed.

Then—

TOOOOOOT.

The red horn.

The prison’s highest-level warning.

It echoed through the entire construction site.

Far-off prisoners reacted immediately.

Hands over eyes and ears.

Mouths open.

Bodies flattened.

Hips raised.

A practiced posture—

for surviving a massive shockwave.

Vikir watched.

Then understood.

*Right.*

*Before regression—

the people I knew who escaped from Nouvelle Vague had been around Levels Three and Four.*

A riot from Level Three—

was one thing.

A riot from **Level Nine**—

apparently meant something entirely different.

Vikir had only refused to work.

The prison was reacting as if he were about to destroy the entire fortress.

THUD.

THUD.

THUD.

Heavy boots arrived.

Senior guards.

Dozens.

Badges.

Weapons.

Captains and officers formed a ring.

At the front stood **Bastille**.

The worksite commander.

One of Nouvelle Vague’s senior enforcers beneath the five wardens.

“Night Hound.”

“Resume labor immediately.”

“Otherwise—

only punishment remains.”

Vikir raised his right hand.

Then slid it into the pocket of his prison uniform.

Every guard tensed.

Slowly—

Vikir withdrew his hand.

Only one finger extended.

The middle one.

A vein bulged on Bastille’s forehead.

“Suppress him!”

“If he dies—

there will be no punishment!”

The first guards charged.

Curved blades.

Heavy clubs.

Longswords.

Axes.

Spears.

All of them—

surrounded in mana.

To Vikir—

their attacks looked slow.

*Still not the one I’m looking for.*

*Then I should keep making noise.*

BDISSEM prevented Vikir from using mana.

That was true.

But the Hell Tree had given him more than mana.

Physical Resistance.

Magic Resistance.

Reflexes.

Stats pushed into absurd territory.

Combined with—

a lifetime of combat instinct.

WHOOSH.

Vikir moved his head slightly.

One attack passed.

Then another.

Then another.

He slipped through the first line of guards.

The officers forming the second ring panicked.

“Stop him!”

“Don’t be afraid!”

“He can’t use mana!”

“Kill him!”

Three-section staffs.

Swords.

Blades.

They came from every angle.

“Bad judgment.”

Vikir moved his wrists.

The BDISSEM chains connecting his limbs—

whipped through the air.

CLANG.

CRACK.

The restraints—

became weapons.

One chain smashed into a guard’s face.

Nose collapsed.

Another guard went down to a fist.

Another—

a kick.

Another—

an elbow.

Vikir broke through the officer line.

“Ghk!”

“Ugh!”

“What is he?!”

“He really can’t use mana?!”

One guard kept chasing.

Vikir stepped down.

CRUNCH.

The man’s face hit the ground.

Vikir thought:

*The stories were true.*

*Most Nouvelle Vague guards are criminals wearing uniforms.*

Ordinary guards—

would not choose to work in a place like this.

Many Nouvelle Vague officers had been transferred or demoted here—

after excessive brutality.

Corruption.

Abuse.

The difference between guard and prisoner—

was often only clothing.

Vikir had watched enough.

He had heard enough testimony.

Then—

another group entered his view.

Younger faces.

Cleaner.

Lowest-ranking lieutenants.

They held weapons toward him.

Every pair of legs—

shaking.

“The seniors lost!”

“Now it’s our turn!”

“As guards of Nouvelle Vague—

we cannot retreat!”

“Everyone—

be brave!”

Nobody moved.

They were too young.

Too weak.

Too aware—

that stepping forward meant death.

*Around the same age as Colosseo students.*

Then—

SHING.

Something sharp passed Vikir’s cheek.

Several strands of hair fell.

“…!”

He turned.

A young woman stood with her sword already returning to its sheath.

Late teens.

Beautiful face.

Hard expression.

One diamond marking lieutenant rank.

Nameplate—

**Kirko Grimm**.

Vikir’s eyes sharpened.

That draw.

That return.

Excellent.

*Rare talent.*

*Comparable to Tudor.*

*Bianca.*

*Sinclaire.*

*Maybe even Camus or Dolores.*

Someone with potential equal to the Academy’s greatest young talents.

And yet—

she was here.

Inside Nouvelle Vague.

*Kirko Grimm.*

*I never heard that name among the future heroes.*

In the early Demon War—

Nouvelle Vague’s guards had claimed neutrality from surface affairs.

Later—

when the world became desperate—

even they had joined the war against demons.

And still—

Vikir did not remember a famous hero named Kirko Grimm.

*Why?*

*With this talent—

how did she disappear from history?*

“Hah!”

Kirko alone stepped forward.

Another draw.

Lightning-fast.

At the absolute peak of Sword Expert.

*What a waste.*

Vikir moved aside—

by the width of paper.

Then—

GRAB.

His hand shot forward.

Closed around Kirko’s throat.

BOOM.

He slammed her into the ground.

“Ghk—!”

Kirko coughed—

rolling.

Vikir stepped past her.

Then—

“No!”

Another guard blocked him.

**Garam Nord.**

The same young lieutenant mocked by guards and prisoners alike.

Reckless.

Weak.

And now—

standing between Kirko and Vikir.

“….”

Vikir looked down.

Kirko—

on the floor.

Garam—

desperately protecting her.

Whatever relationship connected the two—

Vikir did not particularly enjoy looking at it.

“Move.”

“No!”

“Kirko—!”

Garam never finished.

SMACK.

Vikir slapped him.

BOOM.

Garam flew away.

Collapsed unconscious.

The surrounding guards could not stop Vikir.

But Vikir—

without mana—

could not simply escape through an entire army either.

So he kept searching for openings.

Then—

a voice came from above.

“What is happening?”

Heavy.

Dense.

Like molten iron.

Every guard’s face brightened.

Relief.

Someone stood on the cliff overlooking the worksite.

Black skin.

Golden burning eyes.

Short hair.

Scars across the body.

Arms thick as logs.

A torso packed with muscle so dense the uniform looked ready to tear.

**D'Ordume D'Orcdile.**

One of the five pillars holding Nouvelle Vague together.

One of its **Five Wardens**.

At last—

someone Vikir had actually wanted to draw out—

had appeared.
