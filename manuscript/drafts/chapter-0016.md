# Chapter 16: Solitary (3)

> Restarted Chinese-first reconstruction. Primary source: `source/chinese/chapters/016.txt`. Canonical English forms for *Lurking Embedded Teeth*, the 10,000 Book Library, Baskerville Fangs, and the established sword ranks are normalized from current indexed Fandom evidence. Recovered English Chapter 16 is an aligned secondary reference only.

It had all begun by accident.

Before Vikir’s regression, the hound unit to which he belonged had been raiding a dungeon near the border of Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain when they discovered a strange relic.

A fragment of a swordsmanship manual.

One torn page.

Vikir immediately sensed that the writing on it was unusual, so before turning it in, he memorized the contents.

When the page eventually reached Hugo, the patriarch recognized its value at once.

Hugo searched the 10,000 Book Library from end to end for a book matching the fragment.

The volume he finally identified was *Lurking Embedded Teeth*.

It had not been preserved in the restricted sections containing the clan’s highest martial arts.

It was an ancient book abandoned among miscellaneous volumes in the shallowest part of the library, where no one paid it any attention.

The discovery sent the Baskerville Clan into an uproar.

‘This swordsmanship is the true essence of Baskerville.’

Hugo had praised it that highly.

At first glance, the manual appeared ordinary—almost nonsensical.

Once the missing page was restored, however, the swordsmanship began to reveal an entirely different structure.

It seemed defensive.

Patient.

Almost passive.

Then, without warning, it struck and sank its fangs into an enemy’s most vulnerable point, continuing only to inflict pain until death.

The work had clearly been written by some unnamed Baskerville ancestor.

And it described the path toward ten Fangs.

The Baskerville 10th Fang.

Hugo, who had already become a Sword Master with seven Fangs, could hardly ignore such a possibility.

… Yet the page Vikir had found did not make the manual complete.

More pages were missing from the latter half.

Seven pages had been torn out in total.

Vikir had recovered one.

The remaining six were nowhere to be found.

From then on, Hugo devoted years to recovering the missing pages of *Lurking Embedded Teeth*.

Baskerville pursuit was relentless.

After enormous expenditures of time, money, and lives, Hugo discovered that the pages had somehow been scattered through the territories of the seven great families supporting the empire.

Naturally, he ordered them recovered one by one.

The project became known by two names.

**Operation Recover the Torn Pages.**

And—

**Operation Restore the Missing Fangs.**

Remembering it now, Vikir slowly shook his head.

He could almost smell blood again.

‘… That was a very long operation.’

Hugo released his hounds to recover the pages.

Whether the other families understood what those scraps of paper were or not, they resisted fiercely.

Of course they did.

No clan welcomed another house’s hunting dogs suddenly breaking into its own territory.

In practice, Baskerville came close to declaring war on nearly every other great family.

Countless hounds vanished in the process.

Brothers and comrades Vikir had grown up beside disappeared at a terrifying rate.

A familiar face would be present in the dining hall one day and gone the next.

A missing tooth would be replaced by a new one.

Soon that tooth would fall out too.

Through those endless clashes, one permanent fang remained in place.

Vikir.

He became a one-eyed black hound that fought and fought without stopping.

In the end, he collected all seven missing pages and laid them before his master.

Several fingers and toes.

Both ears.

Hideous burns and wounds covering his body.

Brothers who never came home.

The reward for all of it had been simple.

‘Well done.’

One sentence of praise from Hugo.

Why had that seemed sufficient at the time?

“… What a stupid life.”

Vikir ground his teeth.

Before the regression, Hugo had taken everything Vikir and the other hounds retrieved and used it for himself and his two sons.

With the recovered scripture, Hugo’s swordsmanship advanced beyond seven Fangs to nine.

The prestige of the Iron-Blooded Sword Clan rose with him.

And beneath that glory lay the blood and corpses of the hounds who had retrieved it.

“That will not happen again.”

Vikir closed his eyes and recalled the missing pages.

Every line written on them remained inside his memory.

Yet in his first life, even knowing those fragments had not allowed him to surpass the Fourth Fang.

He had possessed only seven isolated pieces.

Without the original text connecting them, the fragments had been almost useless.

… But now things were different.

The original *Lurking Embedded Teeth* lay in Vikir’s hands.

Seven pages were missing.

He already carried their contents in his head.

During the retrieval missions, there had been times when preserving the physical pages was impossible.

Vikir had survived by memorizing them word for word while fighting his way through encirclements.

That brutal experience was finally paying him back.

He began to read.

‘… The beginning of the Tenth Fang returns to the principle of the First. If aggression is exposed in the very first trajectory when the blade leaves rest, every path that follows inherits that limitation…’

So that was it.

To anyone ignorant of the missing material, passages like this would have sounded like meaningless nonsense.

Ten Fangs?

How could such a thing possibly exist?

But once Vikir restored the torn sections from memory, every absurd fragment locked into place like part of an enormous picture.

He made sure no one was watching.

Then he began moving according to the text.

The four Fangs he had refined to their absolute limit before regression were only the beginning.

Beyond them, the scripture explained how to reveal the fifth Fang.

How to create the sixth.

Why the seventh was dangerous.

When the eighth could emerge.

Whether the ninth truly existed.

And what the tenth Fang actually was.

Vikir still possessed the body of an eight-year-old.

His mana was insufficient.

His muscles and bones were immature.

He could not reproduce everything written here yet.

Someday, he would.

‘… Fifteen.’

Before turning fifteen, he would restore all the strength he had possessed before regression.

And that would only be the beginning.

Afterward, he would surpass the wall he had never crossed in his first life.

Fifth Fang.

Sixth.

Seventh.

In the old timeline, Vikir had accumulated the aura of a High Sword Graduator while lacking swordsmanship worthy of that power.

Direct-line relatives had pushed him aside, mocked him, bullied him, and treated him as inferior because his technique could not keep pace with his strength.

Those humiliations would never happen again.

‘First, I memorize the entire book.’

Once that was done, *Lurking Embedded Teeth* would disappear from the world forever.

Hugo would never discover the scripture.

He would never use it to create nine Fangs.

For an eight-year-old, it was a remarkably satisfying form of revenge.

Vikir gave a dry laugh.

He still had more than twelve hours remaining in the 10,000 Book Library.

For a Baskerville child, memorizing one thin volume in that time was hardly impossible.

Half a day later, Vikir would no longer be quite the same swordsman.

He continued reading.

…

…

…

More precisely, about ten hours and fifty minutes later—

Fwoosh!

A small fire would break out in the shallowest part of the 10,000 Book Library, among the neglected miscellaneous books.

No injuries.

No deaths.

The only loss would be one supposedly worthless old volume.

An incident insignificant enough for the servants themselves to quietly bury.
