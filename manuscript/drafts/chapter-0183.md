# Chapter 183: Midterm Random Defense (5)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/183.txt` — `3d3082eb12bb4e7e66d6dbeb205ea06e29c963e8`. Recovered English Chapter 181 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Canonical/project terminology controls Dolores L. Quovadis, Tudor Donquixote, Sancho Barataria, Pigi, Bianca Fo Usher, Professor Banshee Morgue, Professor Sadi, Killer Ant, and Soldier Ant. Fandom independently confirms **Professor Sadi** as the established English form; **Banshee Morgue** remains the canonical professor name.

Student council president Dolores—

had been smiling.

Her first-year juniors had just set a new record.

Then her smile disappeared.

“…Why isn't the exam ending?”

She was not the only one who noticed.

Students.

Professors.

Spectators.

Confusion spread.

“Their HP is zero.”

“Why are they still inside?”

“They're first-years!”

“Who activated Phase 2!?”

The phase-control system centered on a blue crystal magic device.

Staff crowded around its panel.

One operator paled.

“There is another powerful life signal inside the arena.”

“The system thinks something is still capable of fighting.”

A professor stared.

“What are you talking about?”

“There are only four students inside!”

That—

should have been true.

Their four registered life signals were linked to the HP suits.

All four—

zero.

So the arena should have detected no remaining active combatant.

Instead—

the crystal still registered a vigorous living presence.

Professors peered through the barrier.

Nothing.

Only the four students.

“The life-detection spell must be conflicting with the phase-control spell.”

“Then fix it!”

“Why did this happen!?”

“We only manage phase control.”

“Life detection belongs to another section.”

“Who cares whose section it is!?”

“Stop the golems!”

A worse answer came back.

“This year the golem generation and movement systems were automated.”

“The spell code is tied into the same magic stone.”

“To stop one system cleanly—”

“we first have to disable the life-detection and phase-control arrays.”

The room froze.

Then someone said—

“Drop the arena barrier.”

“That gets the students out immediately.”

“Yes.”

“Do that!”

Another hesitation.

“The barrier isn't our department either.”

“If we interfere without authorization—”

“liability becomes complicated.”

Two words—

**jurisdiction**.

**liability**.

Words capable of freezing even sensible adults at exactly the wrong moment.

Small procedural problems—

stacked.

Routine habits—

became paralysis.

The solution itself remained simple.

Open the transparent dome.

Get the students out.

A senior professor snapped—

“Find whoever has the barrier key.”

Staff scattered.

Soon—

**Professor Banshee Morgue** arrived from another examination site.

A leading candidate for the Academy's next vice-principal.

He listened to the explanation.

Then pulled out a key etched with one letter.

**A.**

“One key is insufficient.”

Banshee's expression darkened.

“Without Key B—”

“A does nothing.”

The barrier-release authority had been split for safety.

The professors immediately started searching.

Then another piece of bad news arrived.

“Professor Sadi has Key B.”

“And she left her post without permission!”

“What!?”

“That woman again!?”

“She was disciplined for negligence only days ago!”

“Where is she!?”

“She said she was going to the restroom.”

“No one has seen her since.”

Minutes vanished.

Inside—

Tudor.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

Still trapped.

Still at—

> **HP: 0 / 100**

The protection of the scoring system—

was effectively over.

Now—

if the golems hit them—

their bodies received the real damage.

“Damn it!”

“The HP suits aren't protecting us anymore!”

“Take them off!”

“These things are only restricting movement now!”

“Be careful!”

“The next hits are real!”

The Killer Ants kept spawning.

Defense-stage monsters—

were designed to continue appearing.

And Phase 2—

was built for second-years.

Not exhausted freshmen.

A huge shape rose from the swarm.

[GRRRAAA—]

A **Soldier Ant**.

Broader than the others.

Heavier.

A shield-like head covered in spikes.

Pigi recognized it.

“…Soldier Ant!”

Tudor raised his damaged spear.

“We have to stop it!”

Sancho lifted an axe.

“I'll try!”

Neither had enough mana left for a strong aura.

The Soldier Ant lowered its head.

Charged.

Boom!

Tudor's spare spear broke.

Sancho's spare axe—

shattered.

Then more Soldier Ants pushed forward.

Boom.

Boom.

Boom.

Like a wall of shield infantry.

The four students were buried under the pressure.

“Too heavy!”

“More are coming!”

“I can't—”

“I think I'm going to die!”

“Where are the professors!?”

Outside—

the faculty situation became worse.

“Destroy the magic stone!”

“Who authorizes that?”

“That artifact is priceless!”

A professor from the golem-generation side snapped back.

“This started because your life-detection array malfunctioned!”

Another voice cut in.

“Nonsense. Golem generation layered automation onto the original summoning array this year. If that caused the conflict, your side should pay for the magic stone!”

“That is not our fault. Life detection is what kept reporting an active combatant after all four HP values hit zero!”

“Then what about phase control? It should never have advanced freshmen into Phase 2!”

“The phase-control team only reacts to the life signal it receives!”

“Enough. Determine which system actually caused the fault before deciding who pays!”

The students were fighting for their lives.

The adults—

argued over causal responsibility and liability.

Then someone shouted—

“Has Professor Sadi answered!?”

“No!”

“Don't you understand?”

“There's a rumor one of this year's first-years is secretly Imperial blood!”

“If the palace hears about this—”

“we're all finished!”

Dolores heard enough.

She stepped forward.

*Hold on.*

*Just a little longer.*

She grabbed the sword at the waist of a nearby professor.

Drew it.

And ran toward the magic stone.

“Student council president!”

“What are you doing!?”

“Stop!”

“If you destroy that—”

“expulsion will be the least of it!”

“Quovadis could be charged for the entire artifact!”

Dolores did not slow.

The juniors inside—

were dying.

The value of the Imperial artifact—

the threat of expulsion—

compensation—

none of it mattered.

She raised the blade.

Then—

BOOM!

Another explosion sounded inside the arena.

Dolores stopped.

Everyone turned.

The barrier—

still sealed.

But inside—

through the poison fog—

a new figure stood in front of Tudor, Sancho, Pigi, and Bianca.

Bow in hand.

“…Stay back.”

**Vikir.**
