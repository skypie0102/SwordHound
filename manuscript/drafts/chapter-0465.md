# Chapter 465: The Prelude to a Counterattack (1)

> Chinese-primary reconstruction from `source/chinese/chapters/465.txt`. Recovered English Chapter 463 is the verified shifted secondary witness. Fandom indexing confirms **Kirko Grimm** and her survival bond with Asmodeus / Flubber. Established project terminology controls Black Tongue, BDISSEM, Asmodeus, Flubber J. Tarbond, Aiyen, Camus Morgue, Dolores L. Quovadis, Sinclaire Bourgeois, Flauros, Red Death, and the Four-House alliance.

For the first time—

Flauros had taken a truly effective hit.

The one who drove a blade into his back—

was Black Tongue.

And yet—

Black Tongue looked nothing like the man Vikir remembered.

Shorter.

Slender.

The old cloudy eyes were gone.

In their place—

a direct and unwavering gaze.

Black veins still covered the body like armor.

But the figure—

was unmistakably female.

**Kirko Grimm.**

She stood behind Flauros—

staring at Vikir.

THUNK!

Asmodeus tore free from Flauros’s back.

The magic sword had swollen—

bloated from all the blood it had consumed.

[What is this nonsense…!?]

Flauros turned—

Red Death already gathering in his mouth.

Then—

SPLASH!

Green swallowed his vision.

A liquid mass surged from behind Kirko.

**Flubber J. Tarbond.**

The creature wrapped around Flauros.

HISSSSS—

The Red Death miasma pouring from the demon—

was absorbed.

Vikir stared.

“…You.”

Kirko.

She should have disappeared with Nouvelle Vague.

Yet she stood here.

On the surface.

With BDISSEM restraints.

Asmodeus.

And Flubber.

Kirko’s lips moved faintly.

“…You were the one who saved me.”

Vikir did not deny it.

Before escaping Nouvelle Vague—

he had made one additional arrangement.

Back when he was forcing BDISSEM to release restraints and prison barriers—

Vikir had demanded one more thing.

*There will be someone outside the fifth-floor arena window.*

*Save her.*

*Bring her to the surface alive.*

*Her name is Kirko Grimm.*

Vikir had valued Kirko’s potential highly.

If she survived—

she could become a major asset to humanity.

In the history Vikir remembered—

she should already have been dead.

Instead—

his intervention had changed her fate.

“…Then why are you Black Tongue now?”

Kirko answered simply.

“When Nouvelle Vague collapsed—

I heard something calling to me beneath the sea.”

Fragments.

Nine broken pieces.

**Asmodeus.**

One of the Seven Demon Swords—

shattered by Vikir and lost in the deep.

No one should have been able to recover it.

But the sword—

had called for a new host.

Its desire was simple.

Blood.

To obtain blood—

it needed a symbiotic body.

Kirko—

being crushed by the pressure of the deep sea—

accepted.

Her fate changed again.

Asmodeus gave her the abnormal physical traits once associated with Black Tongue.

A body that could endure deep-sea pressure.

Strength far beyond an ordinary human.

The ability to move for days without breathing.

And with that body—

Kirko helped the scattered wardens reach the surface.

Those who survived under her protection naturally began accepting her authority.

Kirko glanced at Flubber.

“This one came with us too.”

“Maybe because it was so attached to Nouvelle Vague.”

Flubber had existed there long before the prison.

Ancient documents recorded its strange attachment to the place.

Kirko had chosen to remain beside it—

even while Nouvelle Vague collapsed.

Apparently—

that had mattered.

Ever since then—

Flubber had stayed close to her.

Its acid did not attack Kirko.

And somehow—

she could regulate it.

Vikir studied her.

*Talent.*

*Potential.*

*And the luck to seize every opportunity placed in front of her.*

Perhaps—

the butterfly he had saved beneath fate’s wheel—

had grown into a storm.

BOOM!

Kirko combined all three powers at once.

BDISSEM restraints disrupted Flauros’s mana.

Flubber devoured the Red Death.

Asmodeus struck through the opening—

and drank more blood.

[Where did all these absurd things come from!?]

Flauros swung Ouroboros.

The attack finally forced Kirko away.

But she had already bought what they needed most.

Time.

Kirko landed beside Aiyen.

“I met Aiyen afterward.”

“We traveled here together.”

“She also told me quite a bit about you.”

Aiyen grinned.

“She was confused about life on the surface.”

“So I gave her advice.”

“Don’t underestimate me.”

“I’m a very warm woman, West Wind.”

Kirko looked directly at Vikir.

“As soon as I reached the surface—

I looked for you.”

“You made me survive.”

“So take responsibility.”

Then—

tap.

Camus touched Vikir’s shoulder.

“…We’re discussing that later.”

Dolores’s eyes narrowed too.

“I’m also curious.”

“Responsibility?”

Sinclaire tilted her head.

“Bro?”

“Who is this uniformed woman?”

Vikir suddenly found himself surrounded.

Then—

[Hehehe.]

Sadi’s voice cut through the tension.

[Could we perhaps flirt **after** surviving enemy territory?]

She cracked her whip through another group of Poison Humans.

Kirko and Aiyen straightened.

There were plenty of stories they had not told yet.

A raft made from corpses.

Shark attacks.

A battle against a mysterious ghost ship.

All of it—

later.

If they lived.

Flauros glared at Vikir.

[You have brought many troublesome toys.]

[But oddities alone do not overturn destiny.]

Ouroboros tightened in his grip.

Red Death began gathering again.

Vikir lowered his stance.

Aiyen and Kirko were enormous reinforcements.

Still—

not enough to guarantee Flauros’s death.

Vikir began recalculating.

Then—

“WOOOOOAH!”

Tudor screamed from behind.

He was looking toward the horizon.

Sancho.

Pigi.

Bianca.

All of them joined him.

The ground trembled.

Vikir had already felt it.

Then came the sound.

THUNDERING HOOFBEATS.

And—

cheering.

Four great banners appeared.

Armies advanced at full speed.

Poison Human lines collapsed beneath them.

**House Morgue.**

**House Quovadis.**

**House Bourgeois.**

And—

**House Baskerville.**

The four allied great houses—

had arrived.

Kirko wiped sweat from her brow.

Then smiled.

“At least buying time paid off.”

The death squad’s expressions hardened with renewed hope.

Vikir looked back toward Flauros.

[… ]

The demon’s face twisted.

He had not expected this.

The stage—

was moving toward its final act.
