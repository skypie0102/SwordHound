The day of transfer finally came.

News that Night Hound was being sent to Nouvelle Vague—

the most infamous prison in the world—

filled the streets with spectators.

“So Night Hound really got caught.”

“I heard he surrendered.”

“Why would he do that?”

“Who knows?”

“I just want to see what he looks like.”

“He killed that many people.”

“He must look terrifying.”

“I heard he’s three meters tall.”

“And covered in tattoos.”

From early morning—

crowds packed the route.

Then the convoy appeared.

Layer after layer of guards.

At its center—

a wagon carrying a black iron cage.

Inside sat Vikir.

His wrists were bound.

His ankles shackled.

Chains wrapped around his body.

Every restraint was made from a mysterious material called **BDISSEM**.

A substance that neutralized mana—

and forcibly restricted physical strength.

Such restraints were reserved for condemned prisoners and criminals destined for Nouvelle Vague.

Normally—

people threw stones.

Rotten food.

Insults.

Today—

the objects in their hands slowly lowered.

“…That’s Night Hound?”

“He’s that young?”

“Is this some kind of portrait session?”

Vikir sat in silence.

Even with his mana sealed—

the pressure around him was sharp enough to chill the crowd.

No one shouted.

No one threw anything.

Then—

“You bastard!”

“My father died because of you!”

A boy broke through the crowd.

He threw a dagger with remarkable skill.

It slipped through the gaps between the guards.

CLANG!

A longsword knocked it aside.

Black hair—

shaped almost like pointed ears.

Ruby eyes.

A black-and-brown uniform.

**Isabella Le Baskerville.**

Countess.

Commander of the Doberman Knights.

One of the Seven Counts.

And today—

Vikir’s escort officer.

“…Sending my own nephew to Nouvelle Vague.”

“Leaves a bitter taste.”

She was a distant relative.

They had only met directly a handful of times—

including Seth’s execution and the Hell Tree incident.

Still—

her regret was genuine.

“I should have attended that banquet.”

“If you had joined the Doberman Knights—

maybe none of this would have happened.”

Vikir answered with something else entirely.

“You’ve had trouble since Professor Sadi disappeared.”

Isabella clicked her tongue.

Originally—

she had served with the Imperial arrest force.

After Sadi took over—

Isabella returned to Baskerville.

But Sadi’s betrayal during the Hell Tree incident—

and her escape—

had left the Imperial side short of a proper escort commander.

Which was how Isabella—

despite her blood relation to the prisoner—

ended up here.

“Sadi has always been impossible to read.”

“Strong.”

“Capable.”

“And completely unpredictable.”

“I warned people about her.”

Silence followed.

Prisoner.

Escort.

Relative.

There was not much to say.

Finally—

Isabella spoke again.

“Nephew.”

Vikir looked over.

Her face was deadly serious.

“You’d be better off killing yourself now.”

If he died here—

he could still be buried beneath open sky.

That was how terrible Nouvelle Vague was.

And Vikir—

according to the court—

was supposed to remain there for 3,021 life sentences.

◆◆◆

Some time later—

the convoy sailed into the northern sea.

Vikir did not kill himself.

He simply sat inside a cage on the deck.

Rain hammered the ship.

Icy waves rose tens of meters high.

The storm looked capable of swallowing the vessel whole.

*The road to Nouvelle Vague is as bad as expected.*

Then three men approached.

“How does it feel to be sailing toward death?”

“You’d be better off jumping into the sea.”

“You can stay calm only because you have no idea what Nouvelle Vague is.”

Vikir recognized them.

**Pedro Donquixote.**

**Usher P. Isolde.**

**Thomas de Leviathan.**

Because Isabella was related to Vikir—

the Seven Families had agreed to send additional escorts for balance.

These three represented Donquixote, Usher, and Leviathan.

They leaned toward the cage.

“Nouvelle Vague is buried in the deepest sea.”

“You’ll probably die before you reach it.”

“Kill yourself now.”

“It’ll be kinder.”

Vikir ignored the words.

He focused on their smell.

Faint.

Rotten.

Demonic.

*Minions.*

The odor was similar to Passamonte, Madeline, and Hobbes at the trial.

But Vikir did not need smell alone.

He already knew these men.

Their faces.

Their histories.

Their future crimes.

“Pedro Donquixote.”

“Usher P. Isolde.”

“Thomas de Leviathan.”

All three stiffened.

Vikir continued.

“You sacrificed subordinates.”

“A cousin.”

“People under your rule.”

“In exchange—

demons gave you power.”

“And with that power—

you rose inside your families.”

His eyes sharpened.

“Was it worth it?”

The three exchanged looks.

Then smiled.

“So you really do know.”

“I understand why our masters ordered us to eliminate you.”

“Even Nouvelle Vague isn’t enough.”

“This ship will be your grave.”

To them—

Vikir was helpless.

BDISSEM restraints had sealed his mana.

Then—

“…What exactly are you discussing?”

A fourth voice cut in.

Isabella stood behind them.

Her hand rested on her sword.

“Demonic power?”

“Masters?”

“And why is this ship supposed to become Vikir’s grave?”

The three men clicked their tongues.

“So she heard.”

“Does it matter?”

“We were going to kill everyone anyway.”

“Might as well eliminate Baskerville too.”

Their hostility became open.

Three against one.

Even Isabella—

one of the Seven Counts—

would struggle against three elite representatives of other Great Houses.

Then three more figures appeared behind her.

“You’ve planned something interesting.”

“May Rune judge this wickedness.”

“What kind of mess is this?”

Banshee Morgue.

Mozgus Quovadis.

And **Lovebad Bourgeois**.

The other escort representatives.

Banshee—

a leading Colosseo professor.

Mozgus—

a powerful New Testament cleric.

Lovebad—

a Bourgeois noble related to Merelini Lovegood.

Now the balance was four against three.

Lovebad frowned.

“You were the first to show killing intent.”

“That alone makes you suspicious.”

Mozgus nodded.

“And once demons are involved—

Vikir’s testimony must be reconsidered.”

Pedro smiled.

“Do you think we moved without preparation?”

Thomas checked a pocket watch.

“…It should be working by now.”

Isabella’s expression changed first.

“My mana…”

Banshee.

Mozgus.

Lovebad.

All four stiffened.

Thomas laughed.

“The tea you drank contained powdered BDISSEM material.”

“Only a tiny amount.”

“You never noticed.”

“The dose inside each cup cost enough to buy this entire ship.”

“For the next few minutes—

your bodies are effectively wearing BDISSEM restraints.”

Pedro smiled.

“We only need a few minutes.”

Isolde added:

“We’ll report that Night Hound went berserk.”

“The ship sank.”

“And every escort died heroically.”

The four loyal escorts tried to summon mana.

Nothing.

“Damn it.”

“I really can’t use magic.”

“This feels exactly like BDISSEM.”

“How does a powder like this even exist?”

Then—

from inside the cage—

Vikir spoke.

“BDISSEM?”

Everyone turned.

Vikir raised both hands.

“You mean this?”

CRACK.

The handcuffs broke.

Pieces fell to the deck.

The three demon-aligned escorts stopped smiling.

Vikir stood.

Then gripped the bars.

CREAK.

The cage bent like soft wood.

He stepped out.

“Don’t sink the ship.”

Night Hound looked at the three men.

“I need to reach Nouvelle Vague.”
