## Material corrections versus recovered English MTL

1. **John Barrymore restored cleanly** as the Baskerville head butler who has served four generations.
2. **Morgue Clan canonicalized from Chinese transliteration `米尔格`.** The MTL entity is useful as a witness but Chinese determines the scene itself.
3. **Red Fang Mountain used for the ruby-mine dispute.** The MTL’s “Red Cane Mountain” is not retained.
4. **The stray `Advertisement` line is discarded as non-narrative contamination.**
5. **Two-suns omen rendered from Chinese.** Skilled mana users were away preparing the practical examination, supporting the servants’ interpretation of the phenomenon as an omen.
6. **Hugo’s brutality preserved.** On hearing of a severe fight among the children, his first question is how many died.
7. **Triplet injuries and recovery kept distinct.** Highbro’s teeth, Middlebro’s jaw, and Lowbro’s index finger were physically treated, but their unity/fighting spirit remained broken.
8. **Vikir’s mana concealment is experience-based.** It comes from surviving the demon war/Age of Destruction, not from a new named technique.
9. **Three bad swords versus one well-forged sword metaphor restored naturally.** This is Vikir’s judgment on the triplets as weapons.
10. **Final doctrine preserved:** Vikir asks how the strong can be wrong, directly invoking the Baskerville principle that weakness itself is sin.
