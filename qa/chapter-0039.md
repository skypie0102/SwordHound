## Important decisions and corrections

1. Current indexed Fandom independently confirms **Cindywendy** as first appearing in Chapter 39 and as the sole surviving member of the **Messinadnaro Family**.
2. The chapter's operative name sequence is preserved: she is introduced as **Sen Rose Cindywendy** and later revealed as **Messinadnaro Sen Cindywendy**. Later titles, marriage, and future Baskerville affiliations are not imported.
3. Preserved the reason Vikir spared her during the auction purge: he explicitly ordered a Pit Bull Knight to capture her alive and unharmed.
4. Preserved Cindywendy's critique of Vikir's rule: harsh law can establish authority quickly, but durable rulership requires virtue and creates fewer accumulated enemies.
5. Preserved her strategic recommendation that Vikir step down, return authority/credit upward, conceal his strength, pursue study, cultivate talent, respect the vulnerable, and prepare for the future.
6. Preserved the Messinadnaro frame-up: the other seven houses manipulated a Baskerville child and the eight-year-old Messinadnaro boy into exposing secret Baskerville swordsmanship publicly, causing the innocent family's destruction.
7. Preserved the existence of one surviving younger girl and the source's statement that she suffered cruel, degrading, humiliating abuse; no explicit acts are invented because the narration itself declines to describe them.
8. Preserved the seven perpetrators' forced confessions and blood-written apologies across the solitary cell, including their headless corpses and worn-away fingers.
9. Preserved Vikir's admission that **House Baskerville remains the final object of Messinadnaro revenge** and his direct apology on behalf of the Baskervilles.
10. The chapter closes exactly on Cindywendy's promise that Vikir will never lack money, establishing the family title's **sponsor** payoff.
