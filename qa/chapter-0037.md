## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved the strategic reveal that Vikir asked Hugo, before leaving the estate, to borrow **one knight order** for half a day / **six hours**.
2. Preserved Hugo's statement of trust and the political significance of temporarily granting Vikir a portion of Baskerville military authority.
3. **Pit Bull Knights** and **Count Boston Terrier Le Baskerville** follow current indexed Fandom evidence. Fandom identifies Boston Terrier as the Knight Commander of the Pit Bull Knights and Vikir's uncle.
4. Preserved the force size at **100 Sword Graduators** and the Pit Bull Knights' reputation as extermination specialists.
5. `木鸡之德` is rendered descriptively as the **virtue of the wooden fighting cock**: the highest-level fighting dog remains utterly calm and does not react to provocation. It is not promoted as an unsupported canonical proper technique.
6. Preserved Vikir's surrender condition: anyone who lies absolutely still is not to be killed; anyone who moves is executed. The subsequent limb severing of compliant prisoners is also preserved—Pit Bull Knights do not carry handcuffs, so wrists and ankles are cut to prevent escape.
7. Preserved Boston Terrier's physical presentation, cigar, approval of Vikir's methods, and invitation for Vikir to join the Pit Bull Knights.
8. Preserved Chihuahua's objection to mutilating nobles and Vikir's response that they will soon be executed anyway, making handcuffs wasteful.
9. Preserved the operation ending in roughly **one minute**, with Underdog City's principal criminals killed or captured and ledgers/contact records sufficient to identify absentees.
10. Preserved the seizure of auction cash and records for the city treasury / cleanup operation.
11. The feral tribal girl remains **unnamed**. Current Fandom identifies her later as Aiyen and lists Ch. 35 as her first appearance, but the Chinese source has not disclosed her name here. The draft therefore protects reveal chronology.
12. Preserved Vikir healing the girl's whip/abuse injuries, recognizing Cerberus-like bite/claw marks, inferring a link to the tribe that had fought Cerberus years earlier, speaking a basic tribal command meaning **“Go,”** and allowing her to escape with the remaining potion.
