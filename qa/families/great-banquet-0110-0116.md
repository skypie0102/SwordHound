## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Hugo chronology:** C110 says he received the Sword Star name / Marquis title around age **40**, roughly ten years before the present chapter. E109's age fifty is rejected.
- **Pomeranian chronology:** Hugo's extreme reaction is preserved without early confirmation of what he recognizes. Vikir and Barrymore continue concealing Pomeranian's lineage.
- **Roxana:** C111 controls Lady Roxana's death as **illness**. E110's “died in battle” is rejected.
- **Cindywendy:** her flower-girl financial-plan operation, Vikir's embedded financial specialists, the Oxbear-fang information fee, and the prospective large-scale tribal trade are all retained.
- **Seven Counts:** Boston Terrier and Great Dane are the two Counts able to return in time; all seven wanted to attend because of Vikir and potentially recruit him.
- **Knight orders:** Pit Bull Knights are 100-Graduator extermination specialists; Mastiff Knights are 100-Graduator formal-war specialists. Vikir intends to place all seven Baskerville orders beneath him rather than join either.
- **Public vs hidden strength:** Vikir publicly demonstrates only **Intermediate Sword Graduator**. His hidden realm is **Peak Sword Graduator**.
- **Osiris:** the family reframes his cold demeanor as awkward warmth and protective instincts, but does not rewrite him into an openly affectionate person.
- **Seth:** C114 only reveals that he is suspected not to be human. C115 establishes demonic scent, absent reflection, the post-suicide personality change, and the child-disappearance connection. The later canonical demon identity exposed by external reference pages is **not** imported.
- **Hugo culpability:** Vikir only considers the possibility that Hugo may have been deceived in the pre-regression case. The text does not absolve Hugo.
- **Child disappearances:** the adoption-route evidence leads to Seth's training grounds and connects back to the bloodstained bones used to frame Vikir before regression.
- **Combat terminology:** numbered Baskerville techniques remain **Fangs**, never Forms. In C116 the Seven Counts are Peak Sword Graduators with Sixth-Fang-level swordsmanship while Vikir possesses the Seventh Fang.
- **C116 combat estimate:** Vikir believes one current Seven Count is manageable and two would be difficult; no stronger ranking is added.
- **Old conspiracies:** source evidence in C116 links Seth's side to the Bloody Mambas sent to infant Vikir and to leaking Penelope's route/schedule to Rokoko. Ah'Heman is named as the seller of the snakes.
- **Unsupported MTL proper noun rejected:** E115's “Yuasa Castle” is not supported by C116 and is rendered descriptively as Vikir's old childhood castle.
- **Triplets:** Highbro / Middlebro / Lowbro end the family by offering the formal imperial knight's oath; the meaning and dialogue that follow belong to target 117.
