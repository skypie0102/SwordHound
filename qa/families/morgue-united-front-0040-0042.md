### 41 → 42

Ch. 41 ends with the Morgue triplets provoking the Baskerville delegation and Highbro/Middlebro/Lowbro stepping forward while pointing to Vikir as Baskerville's representative face. Ch. 42 immediately continues the same confrontation, explains the brothers' changed attitude toward Vikir, and proceeds into the six-way duel.

### 42 → 43 boundary

Ch. 42 ends with Camus Morgue appearing after seven years and looking down at Vikir. Ch. 43 changes title family to *The Fiancée (1)* and immediately identifies Camus as Morgue's supernova / Raspane's only daughter before continuing her exchange with Vikir. This cleanly closes the three-part *Morgue's United Front* family.
