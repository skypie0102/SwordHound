## Continuity gate

### Chapter 17 → Chapter 18 — PASS

Chapter 17 ends after Vikir memorizes and burns *Lurking Embedded Teeth*, then prepares for Hugo's expected summons. Chapter 18 opens with Hugo immediately calling him in and asking what he gained from the library. No intervening event or duplicated library material is invented.

Vikir's true state entering Chapter 18 is preserved from Chapter 17: **Low Sword Graduator**, stable Fourth Fang, unstable Fifth Fang. Chapter 18 deliberately shows less power in public.

### Chapter 18 → Chapter 19 — PASS

Chapter 18 ends with Vikir asking for an opponent larger than the Orc. Chapter 19 begins from the same training-ground moment and has Hugo select the imprisoned Troll.

Publicly displayed progression is coherent:

- Chapter 18: **1st Fang / Low Sword Expert**
- Chapter 19 final strike: **2nd Fang / Mid Sword Expert**

This is deliberate concealment, not a regression in Vikir's true ability.

### Chapter 19 → Chapter 20 — PASS

Chapter 19 stops immediately after the Troll is decapitated by the two-Fang strike.

Chinese Chapter 20 then establishes:

- the public rumor/state as **Mid Sword Expert / 2nd Fang** at age eight;
- Vikir's hidden real state as **Low Sword Graduator / 4th Fang**, with Fifth Fang progress near at hand;
- Beelzebub's third slot replacing the rat ability with the Troll-derived **Superspeed Regeneration**.

The Chapter 19 draft correctly does **not** import that post-kill slot update before Chapter 20.
