## Continuity

### 100 → 101
Target 101 deliberately reprises the cliff-impact / Madam-death / soul-absorption sequence from the previous family before ending on Vikir's rescue. This repetition exists in both Chinese and recovered English and is not editorial duplication.

### 101 → 102
Target 101 ends with Vikir blacking out among Aiyen/Bakira/Ballak rescuers. Target 102 opens with him waking fully healed in Aiyen's tent.

### 102 → 103
Target 102 ends when Aiyen asks Vikir for help. Target 103 opens on that request and the Bakira breeding problem.

### 103 → 104
Target 103 ends with Aiyen hesitating outside Vikir's tent. Target 104 skips forward two months: Bakira has five pups and Vikir prepares to leave Ballak.

### 104 → 105
Target 104 closes the Ballak/floodlands departure with Aiyen's farewell and Vikir approaching Red Salt Castle. Target 105 begins *The Returned Hero (1)* inside Red Salt Castle.
