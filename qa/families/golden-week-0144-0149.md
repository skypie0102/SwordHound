## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Golden Week:** ten-day break formed from the weekend, Academy founding anniversary, Rune Religion Resurrection Festival, and class scheduling.
- **Volunteer companions:** Tudor, Sancho, and Pigi voluntarily join Vikir and initially interpret his intense workload as genuine altruistic dedication.
- **Vikir's actual motive:** he uses cleaning/service assignments to map the orphanage, monitor movement, and prepare a route to Quilt's hidden central quarters.
- **Indulgentia structure:** military-grade construction, children housed in four buildings around Quilt's hidden central area, and admission/record practices that make unregistered children difficult to trace.
- **Quovadis faction terminology:** production naming is **Old Testament Faction** and **New Testament Faction**.
- **Humbert / Martin Luther split:** Humbert L. Quovadis leads the Old Testament Faction; Cardinal Martin Luther leads the New Testament Faction. Indulgence sales are a major ideological dividing line.
- **Dolores:** prominent New Testament figure described as the “Awakened Young Blood,” following a path opposed to her father Humbert.
- **Quilt Rune Indulgentia:** remains publicly the Indulgentia family head/orphanage director and an Old Testament power broker. His later demon name/title is not imported into this family.
- **Chapter 146 idiom guard:** Chinese `十次中有九次` is ordinary “nine times out of ten,” not a numbered Ten Corpses reveal.
- **John Tetzel:** the Chinese primary clearly names former Old Testament cardinal **John Tetzel** in the indulgence doctrine line. The trailing extracted family-name segment is unstable, so no unsupported surname is invented.
- **Four escorts:** **Ephebo, Pedo, Hebe, Geronto** are source-revealed by name. Ephebo continuity matches the previous family. Geronto is a powerful female mage carrying an ominous black sword hilt/relic.
- **Peri:** production name follows the project's Fandom-canonical naming layer. The recovered-English “Nymphet” alias is rejected for production use.
- **Peri chronology:** target 145 visually estimates her around ten, while Dolores says she was born at the orphanage and has seen volunteers come and go for thirteen years. The draft preserves the approximate visual impression and the source dialogue without inventing a reconciled hard age.
- **Sinclaire:** explicitly seventeen, one year younger than Vikir; remains female throughout despite recovered-English pronoun corruption.
- **Sinclaire interest gag:** she deliberately requests Vikir's volunteer assignment and follows him closely. Pigi interprets this as obvious personal interest; Vikir misreads it as possible conspiracy because of apocalypse-conditioned hypervigilance.
- **Vikir's “Big Brother / Uncle” preference:** source comedy is preserved and directly seeds the next family title.
- **Indulgence records:** criminal confessions/payment records remain explicit, but Vikir distinguishes ordinary human criminals from demon collaborators who are Night Hound targets.
- **Madam Eight-Legs egg:** retains Ballak / Le Rouge et Le Noir continuity and behaves unusually around the children without narrator-confirmed sentience.
- **Dolores's reevaluation:** her trust in the Night Hound, embarrassment over Quilt, and improving view of Vikir remain her perspective rather than objective narrator judgments.
- **Sewer rescue:** Vikir retrieves Madam Eight-Legs's egg because it is dangerous/valuable while the children perceive it as their last ball; Dolores reads the act as selfless concern and experiences an unfamiliar physical/emotional reaction.
- **Scene breaks:** normalized to **◆◆◆** where applicable.
