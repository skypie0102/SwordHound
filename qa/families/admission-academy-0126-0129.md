## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Colosseo Academy:** remains the broad elite institution emphasizing both scholarship and combat, contrasted with the Magic Tower and Varangian.
- **Venetior:** source details retained—basin/water city, 112 islands, nearly 400 bridges, extensive canals, floating traffic, and major cultural institutions.
- **Academy scale:** roughly 20,000 residents including students/professors/staff; crossing the grounds by diameter takes an ordinary person more than four hours.
- **Departments:** normalize to **Cold Department / Hot Department** rather than recovered-English “Cold Class / Hot Class” where the text refers to the broad academic divisions.
- **Cold practical ranking:** Tudor Donquixote / Bianca Fo Usher tied first; Highbro second; Middlebro third; Lowbro fourth; Sancho Barataria fifth.
- **Hot practical ranking:** Sinclaire first; source also gives her second place in written theory.
- **Glorious Generation:** the unusually dense concentration of great-family and elite talent in one admission year earns this cohort the label.
- **Vikir's theory rank:** first in comprehensive written/theory evaluation, largely ignored because Colosseo prioritizes practical assessment.
- **Commoner cover:** Vikir temporarily omits **Van Baskerville** and enrolls publicly as the commoner **Vikir**; the commonness of the name helps conceal him.
- **Academy objective:** locate remaining Demon King-class infiltrators / collaborators in the Imperial Capital using Cindywendy's intelligence while maintaining an ordinary-student cover.
- **Theory answers:** prior-life combat experience allows Vikir to answer advanced questions about swordsmanship, Mushuhushu, yetis, and divine-power-resistant undead.
- **Dolores:** canonical **Dolores L. Quovadis**; third-year top student, student council president, newspaper-club head. She previously knew only the masked Night Hound, so Vikir avoids unnecessary exposure.
- **Pigi:** canonical spelling. Target 127 is his source introduction as Vikir's roommate. Only source-present prior-life memories are used; later bloodline / Corpse material remains spoiler-guarded.
- **Pigi's information skill:** timid but observant, good at gathering/analyzing information, weak in practical assessments, and vulnerable in the Academy's power-first culture.
- **Sinclaire:** canonical female character; inconsistent recovered-English pronouns are not followed.
- **Sancho:** scholarship student from the Northern Mercenary Guild Alliance; Pigi reports that he chose Colosseo over a top Varangian opportunity.
- **Orientation:** Vikir intentionally skips O.T. because outsider status helps covert movement; he later returns only to establish an alibi and because he is concerned about Pigi.
- **Blacklisting:** target 129 distinguishes Bartison's already-committed demon-pact crime from the later military betrayal Vikir remembers him committing in the previous timeline.

- **Cindywendy:** receives Vikir's field observations for network analysis.
- **Rum:** Vikir cleans the Night Hound cloak with strong rum after the night's work; this is the scent Bianca later detects.
