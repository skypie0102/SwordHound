## Continuity gate

### Chapter 13 → Chapter 14 — PASS

Chapter 13 ends with Vikir deciding that he has taken everything he can from Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain and planning to submit the Cerberus corpse after the practical exam. Chapter 14 opens after that exam has ended and immediately moves into rewards/results and Hugo receiving the Cerberus report. No missing or duplicated scene is introduced.

### Chapter 14 → Chapter 15 — PASS

Chapter 14 ends with Hugo granting Vikir access to the library and recommending the **Baskerville Sixth Fang**. Chapter 15 opens with Vikir entering the **10,000 Book Library** using Hugo’s signed pass, then proceeds directly to that manual before he searches for his true target.

### Chapter 15 → Chapter 16 — PASS

Chapter 15 ends when Vikir finds **Lurking Embedded Teeth**, recognizes the torn-page damage, and recalls that he personally recovered the missing pages in his first life. Chapter 16 opens by narrating exactly how that recovery story began. The retrospective is therefore a source-authentic continuation, not an editorial insertion.

### Chapter 16 → Chapter 17 — PASS

Chapter 16 ends with the plan and flash-forward that the reconstructed scripture will be memorized and burned. Chapter 17 opens immediately after Vikir has completed memorization, demonstrates the Fifth Fang, advances to Low Sword Graduator, and eventually performs the deliberate burning. The fire is not duplicated.

### Chapter 17 → Chapter 18 — PASS

Chapter 17 ends with Vikir leaving the library while preparing for Hugo’s expected summons. Chinese Chapter 18 opens with Hugo directly summoning Vikir after he exits the library and asking what he learned. The Solitary family therefore stops at the correct endpoint and does not import the next family’s test scene.
