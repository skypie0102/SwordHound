## Continuity gate

### Chapter 19 → Chapter 20 — PASS

Chapter 19 ends with the Troll decapitated after Vikir publicly reveals **2nd Fang / Mid Sword Expert**. Chapter 20 immediately codifies that public reputation while separately restating Vikir's hidden true state as **Low Sword Graduator / 4th Fang**. It also reveals, at the correct later point, Beelzebub replacing Brown Rat Rapid Regeneration with Troll **Superspeed Regeneration**.

### Chapter 20 → Chapter 21 — PASS

Chapter 20 ends with eight-year-old Camus asking Hugo whether he is the thief who stole Morgue's ruby mine. Chapter 21 opens on the same accusation and its immediate fallout.

### Chapter 21 → Chapter 22 — PASS

Chapter 21 ends with Vikir entering the chamber and accusing Morgue of coveting another family's property. Chapter 22 repeats that intervention and proceeds directly into the child-level ownership debate.

### Chapter 22 → Chapter 23 — PASS

Chapter 22 ends with Vikir apparently drawing a sharp short blade toward Camus's arm. Chapter 23 immediately resolves the cliffhanger: the weapon is a cheap rubber toy; Adolf's Sixth-Circle pressure and Hugo's response follow without missing action.

### Chapter 23 → Chapter 24 — PASS

Chapter 23 ends as fire/explosive spillover from the neighboring fifteen-year-old arena reaches Vikir and Camus. Chapter 24 continues the same accident, clothing damage, minor injury, and match conclusion before moving to the ruby-mine political aftermath.

### Chapter 24 → Chapter 25 — PASS

Chapter 24 ends after Adolf says his whispered warning is not intended for Vikir and Vikir replies that he therefore stopped listening. Chapter 25 repeats that line, then has Adolf request a direct test of Vikir.

### Chapter 25 → Chapter 26 — PASS

Chapter 25 ends immediately after Vikir wins Adolf's water-pot handicap by deliberately breaking his own sword so a fragment pierces the pot.

Chapter 26 begins a new title family and states that **seven years have passed**. It introduces Staffordshire Baskerville, fifteen/sixteen-year-old trainees, an Oxbear exercise, and graduation from seven years of training. No Chapter 26 material is imported into Chapter 25.
