## Family decisions

- **Ghost Castle of the Ultra-Deep Sea** is a three-part family.
- Nouvelle Vague is a vertical ultra-deep-sea prison carved into a dormant volcano and entered through the **Gate of Good and Evil**.
- The prison is divided into nine official levels; Vikir’s 3,000+ life sentences place him at Level Nine.
- **Sakkuth de Leviathan / Plague Leper** is introduced as the Red Death creator and a voluntary surrender seeking an unnamed person.
- Admission consists of three institutional euphemisms whose real forms are violent survival tests:
  - belongings inspection → axe-blade bridge with magnetic demolition balls;
  - health examination → blood/bone/skin-consuming parasites;
  - bath → prolonged boiling sulfur-water exposure.
- Vikir crosses the first stage without mana through hardened calluses; Beelzebub and Decarabia are not magnetically affected.
- Sakkuth’s blood/marrow poisons the examination parasites; Vikir identifies him as potentially useful.
- Dead admission candidates remain officially “alive and imprisoned” because they were never formally executed.
- Level Nine cells are fully BDISSEM-lined and isolated by the 10,000-meter deep sea.
- Forced labor excavates deeper into the prison volcano under a schedule allowing only three hours of sleep and one ten-minute meal break.
- Construction is explicitly creating **Level Ten**, intended for prisoners too dangerous even for Level Nine.
- The Level Nine notorious-prisoner names remain source-local aligned-witness forms pending direct Fandom access.
- Solitary confinement is foreshadowed as worse than ordinary Level Nine; only one prisoner is rumored capable of enduring it.
- The family closes with the Level Nine laborers returning and Vikir asking for a newspaper.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; no source-local name is falsely presented as wiki-confirmed.

## Verdict

PASS.
