## Continuity

### 119 → 120
Target 119 ends with Vikir asking for all seven Baskerville Knight Orders for half a day to hunt a dangerous monster hiding inside the family. Target 120 moves to Seth's hidden cave, shows the murdered-child operation directly, and ends with Vikir revealing Peak Sword Graduator power plus the Seventh Fang. Andromalius's name is deliberately withheld.

### 120 → 121
Target 121 continues the same duel. Seth realizes Vikir's real realm, recognizes his demon-hunter experience, and the narration explains the Ten Corpses. Only at the end does Seth's disguise collapse and the source explicitly identify **Andromalius, Tenth Corpse, S+**.

### 121 → 122
Target 122 intentionally repeats the Andromalius information window, then develops the spiritual-body mechanics of Beelzebub, Andromalius's blood-drain authority, Seth's exploited psychological wounds, and Vikir's poisoned-blood counter.

### 122 → 123
Target 123 opens on the delayed poison effect and Beelzebub's three current slots. Vikir combines Madam Eight-Legs' venom, Bog Salamander regeneration, Anubis, terrain poisoning, and the released barrier to force Andromalius toward the surface. Vikir's signal summons Baskerville's full seven-order force.

### 123 → 124
Target 124 begins on Hugo's “son” line. Seth's body freezes from ingrained fear of Hugo, Andromalius tries to manipulate Osiris, Vikir invokes the half-day command, and seven hundred hounds attack. Osiris apparently beheads the demon; Andromalius survives as a severed head until Hugo erases both body and soul.

### 124 → 125
Target 125 is aftermath, not another combat chapter: public investigation, compensation, punishment, memorialization, political purge, Ten-Corpse intelligence work, Hugo/Pomeranian domestic change, and Osiris sending Vikir toward the Academy.

### 125 → 126
Target 125 ends with Osiris promising to settle the family before Vikir's first Academy vacation. Target 126 begins *Admission to the Academy (1)*. No target-126 enrollment material is imported backward.

## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Reveal chronology:** targets 114–120 may suspect/detect a demon inside Seth, but the source does not give the name **Andromalius** until target 121. That timing is preserved.
- **Ten Corpses:** accepted canonical group name. Target 121 defines them as ten Demon Kings and the ten human vessels they occupy; early removal of one Corpse can delay the Age of Destruction but is not stated to guarantee prevention.
- **Andromalius:** **Tenth Corpse**, **Danger Level S+**, associated with **Serpent's Womb** and “They shall swarm like flies.”
- **Camus / Eighth Corpse:** target 120 allows only the fact that the Eighth Corpse has infiltrated Morgue and is watching the rising Camus. The Eighth Corpse's individual identity/host is not imported.
- **Seth:** current evidence permits that the original Seth died by hanging before full possession while memories/personality remained; the exact boundary between Seth and Andromalius is intentionally not overclaimed.
- **Baskerville Fangs:** Seventh Fang terminology is retained. Target 121's references to eighth/ninth/tenth techniques are treated as Vikir's deeper knowledge, not unsupported present mastery.
- **Vikir's hidden strength:** targets 120–123 source-control him as a **Peak Sword Graduator** capable of deploying the Seventh Fang, while Andromalius's full possession can push Seth's vessel to Sword Master-class force.
- **Beelzebub:** magic sword capable of physical interference with spiritual bodies. Current slots remain **Venom — Madam Eight-Legs (S); Silent Heel — Mushuhushu (A+); Super High-Speed Regeneration — Bog Salamander (A+)**.
- **Poisoned blood:** Vikir can weaponize Madam Eight-Legs' venom through his own blood; Andromalius's blood-drain therefore becomes a liability.
- **Anubis:** legendary black bow formerly wielded by Adonai; recovered-English grammar implying Adonai “led Anubis” is rejected.
- **Barrier:** allows demons and human children under eight, consumes enormous demonic power, evades archmage detection, and is released by Andromalius in target 123.
- **Seven-order mobilization:** Vikir's horn summons Hugo, Osiris, Seven Counts, and seven knight orders totaling about **700 Sword Graduators**. The source does not supply all seven order names in the list, so no missing name is invented.
- **Half-day command:** Hugo's prior reward makes Vikir temporary master of Baskerville's military power until dawn; his first command is to bite Andromalius to death.
- **Andromalius's death:** Osiris's beheading is not final. The severed head attempts to reach the river/dam; Hugo's seven blood-red lightninglike slashes erase Andromalius's body and spirit.
- **Victims:** the cave's murdered children remain central to Osiris's response; his regret extends beyond Seth to Baskerville residents and future children he failed to protect.
- **Nute Baskerville:** canonical spelling controls raw/recovered Noot/Nut variants.
- **1,642 charges:** target 125 retains Nute's indictment, including the source's new **“murder of the First Lady”** allegation. This is recorded as a later legal claim and does not retroactively rewrite earlier chapters' statement that Lady Roxana died of illness because the source does not reconcile the two.
- **Public aftermath:** missing-infant truth/compensation procedures, Vikir Special Law, trials, severe punishments, memorials, and annual observances remain explicit.
- **Political interpretation:** the claim that Hugo uses the purge to eliminate rivals and consolidate the patriarch's position remains **Vikir's analysis**, not an objective narrator verdict.
- **Cindywendy:** receives Seth's secret-contact data to investigate possible disguises/locations of the remaining Ten Corpses; trade with the Depths' native peoples is the leverage that gets her cooperation.
- **Hugo / Osiris:** Hugo's concern about Vikir entering a power struggle is relayed by Osiris and remains something Vikir doubts. Osiris's growing sense of duty as an older brother continues without erasing his established reserve.
- **Boundary discipline:** target 126's Academy-admission material is excluded from this family.

## Family verdict

PASS.

Targets 120–125 form a coherent six-chapter arc from hidden-cave exposure through Andromalius's source-timed reveal and destruction to the legal/political aftermath. Chinese remains semantic authority, canonical register resolves established names/terms, recovered English remains a shifted secondary witness, and the family stops cleanly before *Admission to the Academy (1)*.
