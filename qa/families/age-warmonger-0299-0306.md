# Title-Family QA — The Age of the Warmonger (1)–(8)

**Family status:** PASS  
**Target range:** Chapters 299–306  
**Mapping:** 299→E297 through 306→E304  
**Following family:** target307→E305 begins *Hell Tree (1)* and continues through target313→E311.

## Family decisions

- **The Age of the Warmonger** is an eight-part family.
- Target299 closes the Last Semester setup by moving the major families into the closing ceremony; Winston is struck through the heart by a whip exactly at the end of his mortality speech.
- Target300 source-reveals **Ms. Ouroboros = Professor Sadi**. Her full source-local name is Donatien Alphonse François Sadi de Sade.
- Sadi remains a **Peak Sword Graduator**; her irregular whip mechanics can create practical force near Master territory, but she is not promoted to Sword Master.
- The recently missing wanted criminals are Sadi-controlled attackers, explaining the earlier Imperial Guard arrest anomaly.
- Sadi’s Warring States worldview and contempt for institutionalized peace are her ideology, not narrator endorsement.
- Sadi’s revenge motive includes learning that Winston defeated/captured Marquis de Sade during the Forty-Seven People Riot.
- Osiris Le Baskerville and the other Great-House parents/faculty immediately counter the attackers; Osiris uses six fangs and orders the triplets to move Pomeranian to safety.
- Tudor’s first lethal encounter and Bianca’s response remain source-timed rather than making the students suddenly battle-hardened.
- The “innocent civilian” bomb-hostage story is used as a criminal feint; Humbert is indeed among Sadi’s mutilated captives, but his later role remains target305 material.
- Target301 source-reveals the new Morgue enrollee as **Camus Morgue**.
- Sadi admires Night Hound and misreads his “I live in the future” line through her own ideology.
- Vikir receives **Winter Sword Orwell** from Sadi after the source autograph bargain; the scene is preserved without gratuitous sexualization.
- Sadi’s whispered request made during the Orwell exchange remains deliberately ambiguous; no later referent is imported.
- A faint Corpse-like demonic scent leaks from Sadi’s empty left-eye socket. This remains a clue, not a solved identity.
- Winston rises with the Fifth Corpse behind him.
- **Amdusias = Fifth Corpse, Danger Rating S+** from Chinese target303. Shifted E301’s S is rejected.
- Amdusias is among the Ten Corpses’ strongest direct one-on-one fighters; his role is distinguished from the more tactical/defensive Corpses already encountered.
- Winston’s three-denial history is presented through memory fragments during demonic erosion: childhood charity disaster, betrayal at sea, and Sadi’s attack.
- The aligned E302 witness resolves the sea-voyage pronoun ambiguity: Winston’s partner explicitly tells the sailors to throw **him** overboard.
- Vikir rejects Winston/Amdusias’s moral-philosophy bait; his position remains that he is human and protects what is on humanity’s side.
- Dolores enters the battle and still addresses Night Hound only by the fragment **Van**.
- Dolores confronts Humbert, breaks the fear/trauma hold he had over her, and awakens a new level of divine power.
- Nabokov frames Dolores’s awakening through naturally flowing love/charity and the cracked-container metaphor. This is sacred-system/character interpretation, not proof of reciprocal romance.
- Dolores’s buff allows Vikir to perfectly realize the **Eighth Fang/Form**; he sees the direction of the Ninth-Fang door but does not reach it.
- Vikir reasons that Amdusias’s hidden ability may let him **experience** death without requiring final literal death; payoff remains later.
- Nabokov’s full combat ability is source-revealed in target306; her pressure reminds Vikir of Cane Corso.
- Quovadis’s family-name story continues beyond the three denials: three declarations of love and **Quo Vadis, Domine?**
- Camus recognizes Vikir but stops short of exposing Night Hound publicly; her reaction to Dolores remains comic characterization.
- Banshee notices Night Hound wearing a student-only Winston bracelet but does not complete the identity deduction.
- Amdusias’s unique phenomenon is **Child Hunting**, tied to the Decalogue line about reaping the first life born that year.
- Winston’s greening project, new magic stones, and student bracelets are revealed as one delivery/growth network for Amdusias’s black plant.
- The family ends when every Colosseo student is swallowed into the **Hell Tree**. No Hell Tree interior content is imported.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; established glossary canon is reused and newly introduced source-local forms remain explicitly unverified where appropriate.

## Verdict

PASS.
