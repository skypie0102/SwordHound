## Family continuity checks

### 31 → 32

**PASS.** Chapter 31 ends when the influential local youth committee requests an audience over Vikir's Special Laws. Chapter 32 opens inside **Burning Suspension**, the luxury club where the committee's seven core heirs are drinking and planning how to manipulate the new Deputy Magistrate.

### Chapter 32 progression

**PASS.** The club's luxury is funded by illegal slave trafficking and other black-market activity. The heirs plan to lure Vikir into an extravagant party and dump the bill on him. Vikir instead walks into the VVIP room, defeats the staff, identifies himself, and destroys the 385-glass champagne tower from the top downward through precise mana resonance.

### 32 → 33

**PASS.** Chapter 33 begins at the exact instant after the tower collapses. The seven heirs recognize the feat as Sword-Graduator-level control, realize Vikir's childhood rumors may be genuine, fail to reframe their extortion scheme as a moral test, and are violently beaten with the same stake from the Special Laws demonstration.

### 33 → 34

**PASS.** Chapter 33 closes with Vikir continuing the beating after the heirs begin begging. Chapter 34 opens with the resulting arrests already spreading through Underdog City, then reveals that Vikir applies the existing Baskerville statutes literally rather than treating the confrontation as a symbolic political victory.

### 34 → 35

**PASS.** Chapter 34 ends in the City Hall dungeon with Vikir preparing Age-of-Destruction torture tools to force information about the criminal network. Chapter 35 changes title and location to a clandestine slave auction on the outskirts of Underdog City, directly continuing the anti-crime operation as a new title family.

## Family-wide terminology and source decisions

- **Burning Suspension** — accepted club name from current indexed Fandom evidence.
- **Messinadnaro Family** — accepted as the removed eighth merchant/local house from current indexed Fandom evidence; later massacre details are not imported into Ch. 32.
- Seven active local houses are kept as **Montblanc, Pierre, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Ferragamo, Hermes, and Prada** from source/transliterated witnesses; no unsupported claim of independent Fandom verification is made for each.
- The local political organization is rendered descriptively as the **Youth Self-Governance Committee** rather than treating corrupted MTL syllables as a canonical proper noun.
- Established rank terminology is **Sword Expert / Sword Graduator**. Recovered-English `Gradient` is rejected.
- The 385-glass tower arithmetic is preserved; the isolated Chinese `80` slip in the 9×9 explosion tier is normalized to 81 because the chapter itself establishes 81 and the total 385 depends on it.
- All criminal activity remains explicit: illegal slave auctions, abduction, trafficking, forced prostitution, sexual assault, drugs, bribery, extortion, violence, and murder.
- Existing Baskerville law remains the governing mechanism. Chapter 34's mutilations/executions are not invented punishments; they are literal enforcement of statutes already introduced in Chapter 29.
- The Chinese Chapter 34 `Hugo will not remain Deputy Magistrate long` line is treated as referent drift and corrected to **Vikir**, the actual officeholder in immediate context.
- The line that evidence may be **manufactured if necessary** is preserved without editorial moralization.
- Torture tools and Age-of-Destruction interrogation memories remain explicit and unsanitized.
