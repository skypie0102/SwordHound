## Continuity / decisions

- Ryukeion's festival concept combines haunted-house atmosphere with a tavern.
- Costume lots: Tudor Death Knight; Sancho Snow Maiden; Pigi Fairy; Bianca Frankenstein's Monster; Sinclaire Spider Queen; Vikir Witch; Dolores Night Hound.
- Dolores's Night Hound costume remains controversial, but plague-doctor masks predate the criminal persona and are normal Halloween wear.
- Banshee disapproves but does not misuse faculty authority to dictate student clothing.
- Banshee's food-review sequence remains source comedy; Vikir's battlefield cooking is the genuine quality jump.
- C208 survival cooking is grounded in Age-of-Destruction scarcity and the exact Morgue-potato treatment already shown in C46.
- **C208 raw-name repair:** isolated `马尔甘` is restored to **Camus Morgue** because the remembered “cook at my house / enter the Academy together” exchange matches C46 verbatim.
- Naming Camus in Vikir's independent memory does **not** identify the Corpse Queen. The Queen's identity remains unrevealed throughout Festival Night.
- Vikir realizes he still has emotional capacity to care about others, directly advancing the Mixed Bath Sixth/Seventh Fang emotion thread.
- Osiris Le Baskerville and Pomeranian La Baskerville arrive secretly; Pomeranian's household influence remains comic exaggeration.
- Osiris reports successful Ballak/Baskerville/Cindywendy trade, historical-investigation cleanup, Messinadnaro apology/restitution, execution of old conspirators, and Hugo/Osiris consolidation against the old elder bloc.
- Andromalius's artifact is source-window rendered **Ring of Divine Inviolability**; Fandom treats the item generically as a Ring and confirms **Finit hic Deus**.
- Bianca's 7/100 and Vikir's 100/100 appearance scoring remain her subjective aesthetic worldview.
- Corpse Queen's festival entry is humanizing: cherry blossoms, compliments, snacks, games, and brief positive emotion.
- Queen's memories repeat the same Academy/cooking promise Vikir recalls, but her identity remains guarded.
- Queen/Seere contract is reciprocal: each controls roughly half the day; after midnight the body passes to Seere.
- Seere's “meet the person you want to meet” power found Rosie, while the Queen's primary contract motive was finding another person's corpse after two years searching the Red and Black Mountains.
- The searched-for person remains unnamed within Festival Night.
- Dolores's Choco room supplies the Night Hound scent trail, producing the Queen's mistaken assumption that Dolores knows his whereabouts.
- Dolores calls Night Hound her **“Soulmate”**; this remains source dialogue rooted in soul resonance, not an objective relationship label.
- Queen's jealousy/rage remains partly inexplicable to herself.
- Real skeletons are initially mistaken for festival performers; Dolores's holy power restrains them and suppresses fear.
- Vikir arrives as the true Night Hound and deliberately frightens Bianca/Sinclaire away from the battlefield.
- Dolores requests only limited professor backup to protect civilians without trapping Night Hound.
- Banshee and Sadi defend students/civilians before Vikir isolates the duel.
- **Ring of Divine Inviolability / Finit hic Deus** creates an overlapping hidden space. Outsiders cannot peer in except monsters or children under eight.
- Vikir reads the ring-space landscape as likely reflecting Seth Le Baskerville's inner world; that remains inference.
- The private duel allows Vikir to reveal **six complete fangs plus a half-formed seventh**, preserving incomplete Seventh Fang continuity.
- Vikir's killing stroke veers from the Queen's throat for the first time in either life; he recognizes emotion/mental movement, not physical failure.
- Queen states her contract prevents her from voluntarily showing her face or revealing her name.
- C215 cracks the mask and triggers the childhood voice memory, but **does not name the Queen**.
- Target 216 / E214 begins *My Neck Will Be Your Sheath (1)* and is the first explicit source reveal that the Corpse Queen is **Camus Morgue**.
