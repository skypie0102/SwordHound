## Family decisions

- **The Night Walkers** is a five-part family.
- **Tochka / Crying Castle** is the abandoned northern highland fortress chosen as the Ark; its historical weakness is lack of natural drinking water.
- Night Walker titles source-revealed in this family: Dolores = Saint of the Night, Tudor = Knight of the Night, Sancho = Warrior of the Night, Pigi = Gatekeeper of the Night, Bianca = Sniper of the Night, Cindywendy = Wealthy of the Night.
- Chinese describes Cindywendy as **Countess Cindywendy Baskerville** and as Osiris Le Baskerville’s lover/partner; no extra marriage chronology is invented.
- The **Ark myth is knowingly fabricated** by Dolores, Martin Luther, and senior Quovadis clergy to move refugees to Tochka. Pope Nabokov I explicitly approves the strategy on free-will / myth-making grounds.
- Night Walkers receive covert support from Quovadis, Morgue, Bourgeois, and Baskerville plus Vikir-linked figures including Gordon, Underdog, and Lovegood.
- Ballak trade has resumed; the new “reliable companion” guardian remains unidentified.
- Vikir’s Tochka order requires at least **150 days / five months** of food and water.
- Cindywendy has not exhausted her wealth; the real bottleneck is transport/logistics as Donquixote forces deliberately choke Tochka supply lines.
- Tudor initially proposes leaving to draw pursuit away. Dolores rejects the sacrifice and commits the group to confronting Passamonte / the Fourth Corpse themselves.
- **Fourth Corpse Chimeries** is the accepted project form; aligned E427’s alternate Cimeries spelling does not override established glossary continuity.
- Bianca’s Usher-family collapse mirrors Tudor’s Donquixote collapse: Roderick becomes mysteriously ill, Madeline takes control, and Bianca becomes a fugitive.
- Pedro Donquixote is Passamonte’s adjutant, regenerates from Bianca’s lethal shot, and uses/relays demonic control over the Invincible Cavalry.
- The Invincible Cavalry are brainwashed rather than willing demon collaborators.
- Sinclaire returns at the end of part 3 and uses Money Hat plus her strengthened post-Hell-Tree capabilities to break the encirclement.
- Sinclaire’s return is motivated by reassessing her friends, her former Bourgeois-headship ambition, and Bartolomeo/Belial after the Hell Tree and vault events.
- Chinese-primary correction: Bianca tells Sinclaire **“We were already friends.”** The aligned witness’s “we were never friends” is rejected.
- The reunited student group explicitly misses Vikir and plans to rescue him; none of them knows he has escaped.
- Dolores identifies the cavalry control as Ten-Corpse-level influence and uses Nabokov I’s hammer after holy power alone cannot end the battle.
- Pedro is captured after combined suppression and **twenty-four sanctified wooden stakes**.
- Pedro’s interrogation reveals:
  - Passamonte’s demon is **Fourth Corpse Chimeries**.
  - Chimeries can push humans toward abnormal courage/recklessness.
  - most Invincible Cavalry is away on civil-war fronts.
  - the Invincible Armada is stranded away from home by drought-lowered sea levels.
  - Tudor’s capture alive is a priority.
  - Cervantes is alive but in poor health.
  - the reason Tudor/Bianca must be captured alive remains unresolved.
- Tudor promises Pedro life “on House Donquixote’s honor,” then kills him after reaffirming **“My name is Tudor. No surname.”** The source’s morally sharp action is preserved without softening.
- Night Walkers decide on a deceptive withdrawal / bloodless entry into Donquixote territory followed by infiltration against Passamonte/Chimeries.
- Cindywendy alone possesses Nouvelle Vague’s official report declaring **Vikir Van Baskerville [DECEASED]** and withholds it so the Night Walkers’ faith is not broken.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

## Verdict
