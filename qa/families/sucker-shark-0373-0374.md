- Vikir’s post-solitary diligence is deliberate and aimed at accelerating Level Ten construction.
- **Poseidon** is explicitly the ancient human counterweapon to the Ten Corpses’ **Long Rain of Terror**.
- Vikir intends to infiltrate the Imperial Family after escaping Nouvelle Vague if required.
- Prison food desperation includes lava-shark hunting, literal cannibal exploitation, and guard-fed “Sucker Shark” dependency.
- **Sakkuth de Leviathan / Plague Leper** coerces **Garam Nord** using threats against **Kirko Grimm**.
- Kirko despises Garam’s weakness, but no romance is narrator-confirmed.
- Garam’s social isolation among guards is preserved.
- Vikir only identifies Garam/Kirko as a possible lever for the escape plan.
- The family ends with the escape opportunity arriving sooner than Vikir expected.
