## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Ephebo status:** remains a high-ranking undead subordinate, not a Ten Corpse.
- **Seventh Fang:** production term controls target 158's seven-tooth killing technique.
- **Quilt reveal timing:** target 158 source-reveals Quilt as a **Demon King-class demon**. Later personal demon name/epithet remains withheld until source timing.
- **Dolores / faction scope:** Vikir's observed attack pattern supports suspicion around Old Testament-linked branches; it does not establish universal innocence/guilt for every Quovadis member.
- **Ephebo face:** resembles Mozgus Quovadis but is actually Mozgus's Old Testament twin brother.
- **Mozgus twin-name guard:** the Chinese extraction contains a distinct twin-name token, but no stable project/Fandom English spelling is available. Production uses the unambiguous relational identification rather than inventing a canonical name.
- **Ninth Corpse:** target 159 source-reveals the **Ninth Corpse** as the demon behind the face-stealing / corrupted-undead network. Do not import the later personal name or “Thousand Faces” epithet yet.
- **Camus Morgue:** target 159 introduces Vikir's concern because Camus moved from Morgue's Light Faction to Dark Faction and entered closed-door training. No later Camus outcome is imported.
- **Dolores faith crisis:** Quovadis is no longer treated by Dolores as automatically/incorruptibly good; this is her changing worldview, not narrator condemnation of the entire family.
- **Martin Luther report:** Dolores sends evidence toward New Testament leader Cardinal Martin Luther rather than Old Testament leader/father Humbert L. Quovadis. The pigeon is intercepted before the message leaves.
- **Pedo / Hebe combat:** poison is largely ineffective against undead; Vikir uses **Silent Heel — Mushuhushu** for soundless movement.
- **Pedo/Hebe source repair:** the Chinese extraction momentarily substitutes Vikir's name where Pedo is clearly the immobilized undead under Dolores's sealing spell. Local combat sequence and E159 resolve the intended pair as **Pedo and Hebe**.
- **Super Regeneration:** production canonical ability is **Super Regeneration — Swamp Marsh Salamander**.
- **Mask joke:** Dolores thinks Vikir is offering to reveal the Night Hound's face; he actually means Pedo and Hebe's masks.
- **Hebe identity:** Donquixote-affiliated young man, indicated by family tattoos; exact personal identity is not yet supplied.
- **Pedo identity:** **Staffordshire Baskerville**, Pit Bull Knights member and experienced guide-dog trainer, previously removed/sent away around the Seth purge.
- **Geronto:** powerful young female mage, at least fifth-circle and possibly sixth-circle, using iron/fire magic.
- **Geronto ≠ Camus:** Vikir's suspicion is explicitly disproved through physical differences and a remembered identifying mole below Camus's collarbone from a childhood training accident.
- **Geronto family inference:** red hair + iron/fire magic strongly point to **Morgue**, but no exact personal name is revealed.
- **Grave-robbing clue:** recent thefts from famous martial-house tombs suggest the corpses themselves—not burial wealth—are being harvested.
- **Four black face coverings:** all recovered; together they carry demonic power on a scale approaching Beelzebub. Later exact origin remains spoiler-guarded.
- **Family close:** all four guards—Ephebo, Hebe, Pedo, Geronto—are eliminated before Quilt appears.
