## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Vikir's blame motive:** first, repayment for the previous-timeline Saintess of the Battlefield saving him; second, a practical need to end the gathering and preserve the Night Hound operation.
- **Party aftermath:** Dolores's temporary inability to speak is panic, not deliberate willingness to let Vikir take the blame.
- **Laundry-room alibi:** Vikir's supposedly soiled clothing gives him a natural excuse to separate from the group.
- **Infiltration infrastructure:** volunteer repairs were also route preparation—drainage handholds, roof recesses, access points, and memorized paths.
- **Dormitory rings:** target 155 source bands are 17–19 / 13–16 / 8–12 / newborns moving inward toward Quilt's quarters.
- **Safety notice:** all fourteen rules are preserved. Their contradictions are intentional horror mechanics and are not editorially “fixed.”
- **Rule 3 correction:** Chinese `任何形式的性行为` bans **all sexual activity** regardless of gender/time/place. The recovered-English narrower rendering is rejected.
- **Rules 4 and 14:** mutually contradictory meta-rules remain unresolved by design.
- **Rule 11:** geometrically impossible stairwell wording stays conservative rather than inventing a clean architectural interpretation.
- **Fear mechanism:** Vikir interprets the notice as a demonic fear-feeding/deniability device; the narrative does not separately certify every rule as true.
- **Peri:** production/Fandom canonical name replaces recovered-English “Nymphet.” Her **HELP ME** curtain fragment triggers Vikir's pursuit.
- **Looping corridor:** repeated scenery recreates the Rule 4/14 dilemma exactly before the emergency stairwell.
- **Dolores age:** target 157 explicitly says she is **seventeen**.
- **Dolores truth attempt:** after washing, she tries to confess that Vikir took her blame; the other girls misinterpret the confession as saintly self-sacrifice.
- **Night Hound pursuit:** Dolores follows the Night Hound because she wants to talk, not arrest or stop him.
- **Saintess's Tears / Quovadis attacks:** remain unresolved questions from Dolores's perspective.
- **Red Death comradeship:** Vikir interprets Dolores's trust partly as camaraderie formed while treating plague victims in Saint Meka; later romance conclusions remain excluded.
- **Ephebo:** target 157 closes with Ephebo as the first of Quilt's high-ranking undead guards to appear in this sequence. He is not promoted to Ten Corpse status.
