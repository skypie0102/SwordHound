## Family decisions

- **Money War** is a six-part family.
- Oracle’s apparent prediction genius is a probability-filtering scheme: repeated split predictions leave a small group that sees only correct calls.
- Pigi handles target-list research; Sinclaire handles large-scale mailing and administration.
- The final real investment is routed through Cindywendy’s western-jungle trading company, producing genuine dividends and a credible wealth trail.
- Vikir declines Oracle publicly as Vikir while continuing to direct the operation covertly as Night Hound.
- Dolores’s suspicion that Night Hound is an Academy insider increases, but she refuses to use the Mirror of Truth against him.
- The chosen internal Bourgeois contact is **Damien Bourgeois**, former succession rival to current head **Bartolomeo Bourgeois**.
- Damien’s promotion into the Imperial Money Manufacturing Bureau is treated as political exile / hostage-style survival, not simple advancement.
- Oracle burns nearly all visible profits—and additional borrowed money—on an intentionally absurd party designed to force elite attention.
- **Sinan Saltsail** and the **Puss in Boots** disguise remain source-local forms pending direct Fandom access.
- The party’s restroom/main-hall escalation, diamond “ice,” red-cloak dress code, alcohol pool, gold-dust decor, and deer/horse joke are preserved as deliberate excess.
- Vikir’s party design derives from pre-regression end-times luxury culture.
- Additional lenders are demon collaborators who trusted Oracle under false identities; Dolores/Pigi/Sinclaire are explicitly not liable.
- Vikir approaches Damien while disguised among the servants; even Dolores does not know his location/role.
- Juliet’s death is introduced only as Damien’s publicly known grief point; deeper cause remains reveal-guarded.
- Damien attacks first after Vikir deliberately touches that grief point.
- Damien’s sword style is an old **Bahamut-derived** system ancestral to later Baskerville fusion; direct canonical confirmation is unavailable.
- Damien can use a fourth-form Baskerville-like technique with Sword Graduator aura, but Vikir reads and defeats it.
- Vikir explicitly states he did **not** come to kill Damien.
- Chapter 278 closes by surfacing Damien’s death wish; the reason/payoff belongs to the next title family.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; source-local names are not falsely represented as wiki-confirmed.

## Verdict

PASS.
