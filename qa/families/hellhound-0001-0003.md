## Cross-chapter continuity review

### Chapter 1 → Chapter 2

**PASS.** The overlap is intentional source structure, not accidental duplicated translation.

Chapter 1 presents the Cradle of Swords externally through Hugo/the household and ends while the infant remains underwater, with the knights discovering that he is drinking from the River Styx.

Chapter 2 begins from Vikir’s internal perspective after regression, rewinds through Hugo’s nursery judgment, then retells the Cradle traversal from inside Vikir’s mind. It continues beyond Chapter 1’s endpoint through his detailed Styx strategy and resurfacing.

The repeated events have different narrative function and source wording. Neither chapter is trimmed merely to avoid repetition.

### Chapter 2 → Chapter 3

**PASS.** Chapter 2 ends with Vikir emerging from the Styx and Hugo reacting to his newly grown teeth. Chapter 3 opens later with infant Vikir in the communal nursery, reviewing his former life. No source event is missing between the chapter endpoints.

Chapter 3’s Styx references correctly assume the blessing established in Chapter 2: hardened body, mana improvement, and the ability to resist the snakes’ scales.

### Chapter 3 → Chapter 4

**PASS.** Chapter 3 ends explicitly with “Eight years passed.” Chinese Chapter 4 opens eight years later with eight-year-old Vikir receiving lessons. The timeskip is complete and the base title changes, establishing a clean family boundary.
