## Family continuity checks

### 34 → 35

**PASS.** Chapter 34 ends with Vikir preparing to extract information about Underdog City's criminal network using the captured heirs and specialized interrogation tools. Chapter 35 opens at the illegal slave auction that concentrates the city's underground wealth and criminal participants in one place. Chihuahua is already operating under a forged Montblanc identity, showing that the purge has moved from intelligence gathering into infiltration.

### Chapter 35 progression

**PASS.** The auction establishes the trafficking system, masked buyers, Chihuahua's cover, the Freak Show label, and the Infernal Buffalo Murcielago. The human auction escalates until the untamed tribal girl fails to sell and is dragged away after biting off a slaver's finger. The chapter ends by unveiling a shackled fifteen-year-old boy as the premium final lot.

### 35 → 36

**PASS.** Chapter 36 immediately identifies the premium slave as Vikir, continues the auction without a time/location break, and escalates bidding to Baron Gambino's six-billion-gold purchase. Gambino's assault triggers Vikir's break from the restraints and the planned violent takeover. The chapter ends with 100 Pit Bull Knights appearing in response to Hugo's hidden horn and Vikir ordering them to attack.

### 36 → 37

**PASS.** Chapter 37 begins by explaining the preparation behind that arrival: before leaving Baskerville, Vikir requested temporary command of one knight order, and Hugo granted the Pit Bull Knights for half a day / six hours. The chapter then completes the auction-house purge, establishes Boston Terrier's role, seizes evidence and cash, releases the tribal girl, and returns to the Infernal Buffalo that Vikir actually wanted for Beelzebub.

### 37 → 38

**PASS.** Chapter 37 ends with Vikir preparing to feed Beelzebub the Infernal Buffalo. Chinese Chapter 38 changes title to `赞助商 (1)` and recovered English Chapter 38 is *Sponsored (1)*, beginning a distinct post-purge sponsorship/aftermath family.

## Family-wide terminology and source decisions

- Production family title = **Slave Auction (1)–(3)**. Chinese Ch. 35's `拍卖场之花 (1)` is a heading anomaly; Chinese 36–37 and recovered English 35–37 establish the coherent numbered family.
- **Freak Show** — retained from the Chinese source's embedded English phrase; no dedicated current Fandom entry claimed.
- **Chihuahua Montblanc** — cover identity for Chihuahua's infiltration; preserves his established handwriting/imitation skill.
- **Infernal Buffalo ‘Murcielago’** — Danger Rating A, 3 m, Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain 2nd Ridge, Oil Realm, nineteen hearts. Exact Murcielago form is Chinese/transliteration + aligned-English controlled; no dedicated current Fandom monster page was found.
- **Baron Gambino** — retained from Chinese/aligned English. No dedicated current Fandom entry was located during this family pass.
- Gambino's greenish-blond secretary remains unnamed. Later Fandom identification is deliberately withheld to protect reveal chronology.
- The tribal girl remains unnamed. Later Fandom identifies her, but the Chinese 35–37 does not reveal her name.
- **5th Fang**, **Sword Graduator**, **Beelzebub**, **Pit Bull Knights**, and **Boston Terrier Le Baskerville** use established project/Fandom terminology.
- Current indexed Fandom independently identifies Boston Terrier as Count / Knight Commander of the Pit Bull Knights and Vikir's uncle.
- Pit Bull force size remains **100 Sword Graduators**. Their role is a temporary six-hour command delegated by Hugo.
- `木鸡之德` is kept descriptive as the virtue of a wooden fighting cock rather than promoted to an unsupported canonical named technique.
- Slave trafficking, coercive conditioning, sexual exploitation, Gambino's assault, killings, severed limbs, and abuse injuries are preserved without sanitization and without adding detail absent from Chinese.
- Ch. 37's surrender rule and mutilation are both preserved: those lying still are not killed, but wrists/ankles are cut because the Pit Bull Knights do not carry handcuffs.
- The auction's cash and records flow into the city treasury / follow-up criminal investigation.
- Chapter 38's English production title is **Sponsored (1)**, not the earlier placeholder *Sponsor (1)*.
