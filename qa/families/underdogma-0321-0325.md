## Family decisions

- **Underdogma** is a five-part family.
- Basement Floor4 is the **Island in the Black Sea**, a forced world-fragment patchwork with black no-buoyancy water and white fungal soil.
- Hell Tree root sap can sustain life only in limited quantities; overconsumption risks monsterization.
- **Dogma** is a Colosseo third-year Hot Department student, Student Council vice president, and leader of the tower-born Commoner Faction.
- Dogma’s class resentment and inferiority complex are preserved as his viewpoint rather than narrator endorsement.
- The Commoner Faction enslaves surviving noble/tycoon students; Highbro / Middlebro / Lowbro are revealed in collars and chains.
- Highbro explicitly chooses survival over pride, postponing revenge; Middlebro and Lowbro follow his judgment.
- Vikir frees the triplets and rejects Dogma’s envy-based logic.
- The tower distinguishes ordinary humans, demons, and monsterized humans; Dogma later becomes the latter.
- Rhinoceros Beetlemen and Stag Beetlemen are ancient humanoid peoples from Giant Beetle Island, trapped when Amdusias tore away a world fragment.
- Vikir defeats 50+ Beetlemen primarily through joint locks rather than brute-forcing the exoskeleton.
- Dogma overconsumes Hell Tree sap, kills followers during transformation, and becomes an A+ Majin: **Blazing Inferiority Complex / Underdogma**.
- Mission state becomes dual: survivors must kill one Majin; Dogma must kill all 77 living targets.
- The triplets contribute strategically by collecting candies once their weakened bodies cannot fight directly.
- Vikir remains **Level1**, raises Strength/Stamina to 300 and Agility to 260, then uses his deeply mastered **Fourth Fang**.
- Vikir exploits the Black Sea’s no-buoyancy property to drown Dogma after disabling his limbs.
- First A+ Majin clear grants **10 gold Level candies**.
- Vikir reverses the Candy Shop exchange, trading 10 Level candies for roughly 3,000 ordinary stat candies.
- Baby Madam + Conversion Bug converts those candies; most rolls are poor, but the final conversion yields **White Magic Resistance +1**.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; established glossary canon is reused and source-local tower/race forms are not falsely represented as wiki-confirmed.

## Verdict

PASS.
