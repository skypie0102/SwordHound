## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Quilt → Dantalian timing:** target 162 completes the personal demon-name reveal. From this point forward production may use **Dantalian**.
- **Andromalius / gate statement:** Dantalian says ten Demon Kings were meant to combine power to open the Demon Realm gate and Andromalius's death delays the plan by decades.
- **Dantalian window:** Chinese primary controls **Danger Level S+**, not recovered-English S.
- **Dantalian title/epithet:** **Ninth Corpse** and **A Thousand Faces** are source-revealed in target 163.
- **Dantalian form:** thirty-six faces/identities and blade-like extensible purple tongues.
- **Orphanage purpose:** Dantalian explicitly describes Indulgentia as a breeding/food farm for children. Golden necklaces mark his “breeding stock.”
- **Indulgence evidence:** Dantalian possesses Old Testament-issued absolution/remission certificates and states that **Humbert L. Quovadis** issued them for large donations.
- **Child-face manipulation:** Dantalian weaponizes actual stolen faces and voices, including children known by Dolores.
- **Memory devices:** normalize extraction drift to **Sack of Shame / Sack of Fear / Sack of Love**.
- **Dolores's Sacks:** Shame = recent urination incident; Fear = Humbert; Love = Night Hound.
- **Vikir's Shame:** prior-life execution and displayed head. Damaged execution placard remains unreadable.
- **Vikir's Fear:** old wartime **Hugo Le Baskerville**. His present softened Pomeranian relationship helps weaken Vikir's instinctive fear.
- **Vikir's Love:** the Sack is genuinely empty, establishing that prior-life Vikir never learned/experienced love in the sense Dantalian's spell searches for.
- **Seth memory:** Dantalian's attempt to evoke familial feeling with **Seth Le Baskerville** backfires and intensifies Vikir's attack.
- **Peri:** production name replaces raw/recovered Nymphet throughout the Dantalian-face sequence and on the surviving necklace.
- **Soul resonance / soulmate:** target 166 explicitly ties Dolores's awakened Saintess buff to soul resonance with the Night Hound. Preserve the source's relationship language without turning it into reciprocal romance from Vikir.
- **Temporary Sword Master:** target 167 places buffed Vikir at **lower Sword Master**, temporarily crossing from Peak Sword Graduator. Sword Master has lower/middle/upper stages.
- **Prior-life compatible heroes:** **Hugo Le Baskerville** and **Camus Morgue / Ironblood Empress** are examples of heroes who could maximize Dolores's buffs.
- **Soul Strike:** Dantalian exploits Vikir's hesitation at the children's faces, but **Madam Eight-Legs's egg** intercepts the apparent heart strike.
- **Dantalian death:** Vikir decapitates Dantalian and destroys the regenerating Peri-faced remnant. A physical Peri necklace remains afterward.
- **Target 168 witness gap:** there is no recovered-English counterpart in the current sequence. No neighboring witness is shifted or duplicated to fill the gap.
- **Morning aftermath:** no sleeping resident is found dead/injured from the battle, but many remains of children around thirteen are recovered beneath Quilt's residence.
- **Mozgus:** source-present as Archbishop/Inquisitor; acknowledges his elder twin was used as Ephebo and had been raised separately with a different factional faith.
- **First public ledger:** target 168 is Chinese-only. Its one-off offender names are conservative romanizations, not promoted into project canon.
- **Target 169 evidence:** preserves the second set of indulgence cases plus investment diversion, illegal lobbying, cult ties, and false-relic sales.
- **Ninety-five articles:** source lists selected articles; production does not fabricate the unlisted articles.
- **Article numbering:** Chinese primary controls **Article 50** and **Article 90**, rejecting recovered-English 51/99 drift.
- **Awakening remains temporary:** after Night Hound leaves, Dolores's extraordinary buff state disappears. She does not yet know how to make the awakening permanent.
- **Press misunderstanding:** Dolores's “I have to see him again” becomes a false “Saintess declares holy war against Night Hound” headline.
