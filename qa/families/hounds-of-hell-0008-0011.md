## Continuity gate

### Chapter 7 → Chapter 8 — PASS

Chapter 7 ends with Vikir deliberately taking raw, highly concentrated **Bloody Beans** rather than prepared chocolate. Chapter 8 moves into the midterm practical survival examination. Nothing in Chapter 8 prematurely explains the full combat purpose of the beans; the setup remains available for the later payoff.

### Chapter 8 → Chapter 9 — PASS

Chapter 8 ends when **Pavlov Van Baskerville**, acting as a Guide Dog/instructor, rings the bell and starts the practical examination. Chapter 9 opens on the familiar bell and immediately shows the children scattering into their combat/survival strategies. No time jump or duplicated setup is introduced.

### Chapter 9 → Chapter 10 — PASS

Chapter 9 ends with Vikir facing a Hellhound after establishing its straight-line charge and water weakness, then revealing the prepared Bloody Beans/chocolate countermeasure. Chapter 10 immediately pays off that exact setup by flicking a Bloody Bean into the charging Hellhound’s open mouth.

The poisoning sequence remains internally consistent across the boundary: chocolate is established as a canine-monster weakness, explicit symptoms follow, and Vikir targets the kidneys rather than inheriting the MTL’s `height` error.

### Chapter 10 → Chapter 11 — PASS

Chapter 10 ends on the complete **Cerberus** information window and its three-headed appearance. Chapter 11 opens with the same monster/window context and moves directly into the fight. The repeated identification is source-authentic rather than an accidental duplicate.

Rank terminology remains consistent across the transition: Vikir is **High Sword Expert**, while Chapter 11’s comparison table expands the accepted Sword Expert → Sword Graduator → Sword Master hierarchy without changing his current rank.

Current canonical terminology for the numbered Baskerville techniques is **Fang**, not the earlier fallback `Form`. Chapter 11 therefore uses **1st Fang / 2nd Fang / 3rd Fang / 4th Fang** in accordance with the dedicated Fandom Sword Skills entry. This is a terminology-only correction; source-governed mechanics and continuity are unchanged.

### Chapter 11 → Chapter 12 — PASS

Chapter 11 ends after Cerberus takes the Bloody Mamba venom and collapses after exactly seven steps. Chinese Chapter 12 opens on the collapsed Cerberus, with saliva/excrement flowing from its bodies, before beginning *The Gluttonous Flies (1)* material.

The Chapter 11 draft therefore stops at the correct endpoint and does **not** import Chapter 12’s new-family events early.
