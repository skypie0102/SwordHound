## Family decisions

- **The Lion King** is a four-part family.
- Warm-sea/fish/weather anomalies remain environmental setup only; no unsupported cause is assigned.
- Night Walkers infiltrate House Donquixote inside returning Invincible Cavalry armor and use Tudor’s servant-route access.
- Two guards outside Cervantes are fully demonized, not merely brainwashed.
- **Cervantes Donquixote** deliberately kills himself with **Gungnir** before Chimeries can seize his body.
- Dolores identifies the lingering poison/plague signature as a more advanced **Red Death / Leviathan-derived toxin** based on prior direct experience.
- **Gungnir** chooses Tudor and responds to legitimate Donquixote succession.
- **Chimeries** admits Cervantes was the prepared replacement body for more than a decade and that Leviathan poison was used to weaken him.
- After Cervantes denies the possession, Chimeries shifts his body-theft target to Tudor.
- Tudor’s capture-alive requirement and Gungnir are overlapping motives; production does not collapse them into one.
- Chimeries’s authority pushes humans toward irrational courage/recklessness. It influences the Night Walkers’ escalation, but production does not erase all prior agency.
- **Amdusias** remains Fifth Corpse, Danger Rating S+, and appears only as a remnant thought-fragment.
- Chimeries combines his own influence with Amdusias’s surviving Hell-Tree authority to create a mind-breaking inner-world trap.
- The replicated mindscape is **Vikir’s inner world** as preserved through the Amdusias remnant.
- The Amdusias fragment itself fears that inner world and warns that even demons mentally collapse there.
- The Vikir figure at the end is present inside the copied mental world; this is **not** a physical-return or resurrection claim.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.

## Verdict

PASS.
