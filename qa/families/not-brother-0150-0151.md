- **Peri production name:** keep **Peri**, not recovered-English “Nymphet.”
- **Vikir and parents:** his claim that parents are unnecessary after childhood is a functional worldview shaped by Baskerville and the Age of Destruction, not an objective narrator truth.
- **Hugo continuity:** Vikir does not donate because he feels no affection/respect for Hugo at this point; later family reconciliation is not imported.
