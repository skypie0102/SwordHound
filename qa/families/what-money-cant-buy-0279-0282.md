## Family decisions

- **What Money Can't Buy** is a four-part family.
- Damien's loss of will to live is caused by Juliet's condition and his guilt, not by losing the Bourgeois succession struggle.
- Bourgeois successor candidates are raised in common society with surname/support sealed away; **Esse, Non Videri** is preserved as the heir creed.
- Romeo is a sickly commoner/bard whose indifference to money/status contrasts with Damien's values.
- Damien admits arranging Romeo's beating and forced separation; source wrongdoing remains unsanitized.
- Public rumor is wrong: Juliet did not die in the carriage crash. Romeo died; Juliet survived physically and later became permanently unresponsive.
- Vikir offers only communication, not a cure.
- **Pomeranian** is the soul-sensitive summoner; unstable Chinese name extraction is normalized to established canon.
- The **Wraith Tree** manifests spiritually through Pomeranian and summons Juliet's living spirit plus Romeo's dead spirit from the accident site.
- Damien apologizes to both and recognizes Juliet/Romeo as married.
- Romeo's fully dead spirit is time-limited; Juliet remains tethered to her living body.
- Juliet chooses to follow Romeo. Damien ultimately allows that choice rather than repeating his earlier coercion.
- Vikir officiates the wedding; Pomeranian assists.
- Damien deliberately ends Juliet's remaining bodily life so her soul can cross the Gate of Death with Romeo. This is preserved strictly as narrative source material and not framed as guidance.
- Juliet briefly reopens the Gate of Death to say goodbye and tell Damien she loves him.
- Damien's gratitude completes Vikir's bargain: he agrees to provide **the head of House Bourgeois**.
- Dolores is introduced to Damien only after this resolution; she is not told the full Juliet/Romeo details.
- Bartolomeo's outsider dinners occur inside an approximately **100 m × 100 m × 100 m** vault lined with mithril/orichalcon and layered anti-teleportation magic.
- The vault remains sealed for two hours because the **Pit Bull Knights**, roughly one hundred Sword Graduators, required two hours to breach it during testing.
- Damien does **not** already know Bartolomeo is a demon; Vikir's Sixth-Corpse identification is new information to him.
- Vikir's certainty that Bartolomeo is the Sixth Corpse is now near-complete based on Cindywendy's investigation and accumulated evidence.
- Chapter 282 ends on an unrevealed outside-the-vault plan that Damien and Dolores believe threatens the whole Empire; no *Time Flies* material is imported.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; source-local forms are not falsely represented as wiki-confirmed.
