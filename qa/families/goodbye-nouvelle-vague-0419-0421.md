## Family decisions

- **Goodbye, Nouvelle Vague** is a three-part family.
- Current 3021 is the only functional Level-Five current that reaches the surface.
- Vikir's pre-uprising forged-order staging explains why many intended survivors were already positioned on Level Five.
- Aiyen warns that the surface world has changed; Vikir's knowledge comes from pre-regression memory of the 150-day Rain of Fear, not a newly invented source.
- Chinese target419 contains a visible gap immediately after Kirko asks about Garam. Only the localized aligned-E417 material is restored: Garam died two years earlier during the Sakkuth riot while saving Kirko, was buried, and wanted to show her the outside world.
- Kirko refuses to leave because Nouvelle Vague is the place where she was born and raised. Her final fate is not resolved beyond the prison being submerged.
- Marquis de Sade and incapacitated Sadi hitch a ride on the Gulper Eel.
- Orca's killer-whale beastman physiology makes him overwhelmingly advantaged in the deep sea.
- Marquis de Sade promises three lives in repayment for one life saved, conditional on surviving to the surface.
- Vikir and Sade cut the whip so Aiyen/Sadi can continue upward rather than remain trapped in the deep-sea battle.
- Gate of Good and Evil remains 70 m diameter / 5 m thick / 6,535 tons; its accidental impact removes Orca from the immediate fight and uncorks the eruption.
- Target421 uses Gate of Good and Evil as the lower shell and Decarabia's Wailing Wall as the upper dome for the ascent.
- Orca asks to be taken to the surface and Vikir accepts, partly because Orca was a major future Human Alliance combatant.
- Original-timeline Orca/Sade/Poseidon history remains historical memory, not future certainty.
- Local Basilisk-regeneration wording is recurring drift and does not override accepted **Immortality — Gargoyle (S)**.
- Family closes with Vikir, Marquis de Sade, and Orca rising ten thousand meters toward the surface.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked.
