# Title-Family QA — End game (1)–(10)

**Family status:** PASS  
**Target range:** Chapters 409–418  
**Mapping:** 409→E407 through 418→E416  
**Following family:** target419→E417 begins *Goodbye, Nouvelle Vague (1)* and continues through target421→E419.

## Family decisions

- **End game** is a ten-part family.
- The prison-wide BDISSEM release immediately destabilizes Nouvelle Vague, but lower-floor prisoners remain physically degraded until Level Nine joins.
- D’Ordume and Souaré initially suppress the lower-floor revolt and offer Sucker Shark / reduced-sentence incentives before the Level-Nine breakout changes the balance.
- Winter Sword Orwell’s fragment is used repeatedly as a high-value distraction around Gate of Good and Evil; it remains only part of the key system and does not solve the deep-sea escape problem by itself.
- Level-Nine source-local forms **Megidio** and **Snow White of the Killing Clan** remain unverified by Fandom.
- Vikir openly returns as Night Hound, drops D’Ordume in one strike, then rethrows Orwell and descends toward Poseidon.
- Souaré delays him until the new Level-Nine / future-Level-Ten female arrival appears.
- That new prisoner is source-revealed as **Aiyen**, successor to Night Fox Akwilla.
- Aiyen survived a separate **prototype Hell Tree** alone, killed its Dragon Horse, and escaped only after Vikir’s victory against Amdusias affected the tower system.
- Aiyen reports only four active apocalypse signs; Vikir attributes the missing six to the six Demon Kings already eliminated.
- Aiyen reached Cindywendy and launched attacks against Pedro Donquixote / Usher P. Isolde / Thomas de Leviathan plus Pasamonte / Madeline / Hobbes; the Leviathan-linked Demon King is **Flauros**.
- **Bakira** remains with Aiyen through a phantom-beast contract after death.
- **Orca Montreuil-sur-Mer Javert** is the source-local full form for Nouvelle Vague’s Warden.
- Orca easily kills Mammatamuz the Piercing Tusk and reasserts control at Level One before descending later.
- Poseidon absorbs aura/impact instead of breaking; Vikir intentionally redirects battle force into it.
- **Flubber J. Tarbond** intervenes because Poseidon / prison integrity is threatened, not because of simple loyalty to D’Ordume and Souaré.
- Aiyen reveals she did not come alone; **Sadi / Ms. Ouroboros** arrives as an allied infiltrator.
- Sadi’s infiltration follows Vikir’s pre-prison Cindywendy instructions; she used former Imperial arrest-team contacts and the huge returning convoy as cover.
- **Sakkuth** is the Leviathan-born operative Sadi says she planted earlier; Vikir has not met him.
- Sadi’s golden left eye is source-revealed as **Belial’s relic** and enables dangerous partial demonization/Majin combat with a long recovery cost.
- Vikir blocks Orca while Aiyen charges Poseidon. Vikir’s internal combat assessment places Orca above Nabokov I but below Cane Corso and as the strongest living human he has personally fought.
- Vikir avoids a death match with Orca because of Orca’s future Human Alliance contributions and the old-timeline destruction of Nouvelle Vague.
- **Baskerville Eighth Form — Black Sun** remains the controlling form.
- Sadi’s original Orwell bargain is paid off when the solitary cells collapse and **Old Man Angajumang** is released.
- Source-timed reveal: Old Man Angajumang is **Angajumang Cedric Sadi de Sade / Marquis de Sade**.
- The Marquis’s Forty-Seven Riot motive is source-revealed as a desire for excitement and return to Warring States violence, not throne ambition; this remains his ideology, not narrator endorsement.
- Orca opposes releasing the Marquis as a duty to prevent social chaos and protect ordinary people.
- Vikir deliberately inserts Black Sun into the Orca/Sade clash and Aiyen rolls Poseidon into the convergence of all three attacks.
- Poseidon’s shell cracks and part 10 reveals a palm-sized black-scaled winged creature surrounded by blue fire.
- The hatchling remains intentionally unnamed; do not assign a species beyond the source’s description or Aiyen’s rejected salamander guess.
- Decarabia says such ancient anti-demon beings were thought extinct and that Mage Emperor Tzersi personally eliminated them one by one.
- Critical character turn: after fulfilling his Nouvelle Vague objective, Vikir explicitly decides he wants to live and see the future beyond what his previous life witnessed.
- The family ends with Vikir taking Aiyen’s wrist and deciding it is finally time to leave Nouvelle Vague.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; established glossary forms are reused and new source-local forms remain explicitly unverified.

## Verdict

PASS.
