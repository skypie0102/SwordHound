## Family continuity

### 45 → 46

Ch. 45 closes on Camus's long-awaited reunion with Vikir after seven years. Ch. 46 advances more than ten days at the Morgue fortress and turns her renewed attachment into the immediate family setup: repeated shared meals, political-marriage suspicion, and academy talk before the raid alarm.

### 46 → 47

The Ch. 46 alarm becomes the Ballak assault in Ch. 47. Vikir and Camus fight together until Camus is poisoned, pushes Vikir away from a lasso, and is captured. The chapter closes when an unnamed Ballak woman blocks Vikir's pursuit and reminds him they said they would meet again.

### 47 → 48

Ch. 48 immediately identifies the unnamed woman by continuity as the tribal captive Vikir freed during the slave-auction arc, while still withholding her personal name. The Ballak escape with Camus, Adolf arrives, and Vikir leads a night pursuit into the flooded jungle. The rescue party reaches the Ballak camp.

### 48 → 49

Ch. 49 remains at the same camp and supplies the hunters' identities/custom context. The physical Chinese file has a verified missing connective block near the opening; the aligned recovered-English witness restores only that localized gap. The source then continues with Ahun's coercion, Aiyen's intervention, the rescue attack, and Aiyen's evaluation of Vikir culminating in **“Passed.”**

### 49 → 50

Ch. 50 begins directly on Vikir wondering what “Passed” means. His attempt to identify Aiyen leads through Ballak history, Divine Archer Adonai, and contemporary Night Fox speculation. Combat resumes, Adolf reaches Camus, and the chapter ends as the Ballak panic at the arrival of Madam Eight-Legs.

### 50 → 51

Ch. 51 opens on that same darkness and names/establishes Madam Eight-Legs as an S-class nation-destroying threat. The monster attacks the noisy clustered forces. Vikir turns the crisis into a staged-death opportunity, remains behind, and finally unleashes five Fangs and hidden Mid Sword Graduator power witnessed by Camus and the Baskerville triplets.

### 51 → 52 boundary

Ch. 51 closes the six-part spouse-hunt/rescue sequence on Vikir's hidden-power reveal and planned disappearance. Chinese Ch. 52 changes title to `野蛮部落的奴隶 (1)` and recovered English changes to **Slaves of the Savage Tribe (1)**, establishing a clean new title-family boundary.

## Cross-chapter semantic / canonical decisions

- **Rosie Morgue continuity:** raw-layer `younger brother` language in Ch. 46–47 is referent/kinship drift. The recently lost person is the already accepted Rosie, Camus's younger foster/adopted sister.
- **Sword ranks:** recovered-English `Gradient/Gradual` forms are rejected. Use **Low / Mid Sword Graduator** according to source strength.
- **Fangs:** the Ch. 47 display is four Fangs; the Ch. 51 full release is five Fangs. Never convert to Forms/teeth as rank terminology.
- **Aiyen reveal chronology:** she remains unnamed through Ch. 47–48 and is named at the Ch. 49 camp setup. Canonical Fandom later parentage, marriage, chieftainship, and Night Fox succession are excluded.
- **Ahun:** canonical name enters with the Ch. 49 camp setup; later family biography remains excluded.
- **Husband-hunt custom:** Ch. 49 explains a coercive spouse-capture practice used to bring outside blood into the small Ballak population. Do not romanticize it or sanitize captivity/enslavement/killing implications.
- **Ch. 49 source gap:** the missing Aiyen/Ahun/husband-hunt setup is restored only because the Chinese file visibly resumes mid-explanation and aligned English preserves the missing connective block. This is a scoped raw-gap exception, not a general source-policy change.
- **Divine Archer Adonai:** accepted canonical English name/title at his source reveal in Ch. 50.
- **Night Fox:** Ch. 50's current Night Fox remains unidentified. Later Fandom identification of Akwilla and Aiyen's future inheritance is chronology-blocked.
- **Madam Eight-Legs:** accepted canonical English form. Ch. 51 itself source-reveals Danger Rating S and nation-destroying capability.

- **Baskerville ten techniques:** Vikir has memorized the theory behind ten Baskerville sword techniques; this does not establish an unsupported `10th Fang` in the current text.
- **Hidden strength:** Ch. 51 reveals Vikir's **Mid Sword Graduator** power to Camus and the triplets only; Adolf and Staffordshire are focused forward and do not witness it.
