## Cross-chapter decisions

- The huge golden monument in Red Salt Castle is Vikir’s statue; the guards’ heroic account is intentionally distorted and exaggerated.
- Adolf Morgue, not Vikir, did most of the allied force’s work during the old pursuit; Vikir privately recognizes this while acknowledging his own final stand against Madam Eight-Legs.
- The Three Flowers / Three Plagues retain the accepted spell ordering **Highsis = plant, Middlesis = earth, Lowsis = water**.
- Camus personally continued searching the Depths for Vikir until only weeks before his return.
- C107 contains a local referent slip naming Adolf as the one who “withdrew from the Light Faction.” Immediate Chinese context says Camus had followed Adolf, her act shocked Morgue, Adolf gave no statement, and he watched the defector enter the Dark Faction. Indexed Fandom evidence independently identifies **Camus** as the defector. The reconstruction therefore repairs the subject to Camus.
- **Snake Morgue** is accepted at his source reveal as leader of Morgue’s **Dark Faction**, an archmage/Marquis and Raspane relative. Later necromancy/resurrection plot details are spoiler-guarded.
- Underdog City’s improved order contrasts with the disappearance of children, establishing the unresolved serial child-abduction thread without inventing a culprit.
- Ch.108 keeps the already accepted translation **Trust Won by Moving the Stake** for 移木之信.
- Ch.108 corrects two MTL substitutions from Chinese: the white-carpet joke uses **coffee**, not blood; Vikir writes his visit purpose as **reporting survival / return alive**, not “reunion.”
- **Pomeranian La Baskerville** is five and already able to animate a dead rat with negative mana / corpse revival. Rokoko influence is Vikir’s hypothesis, not certainty.
- Hugo personally rushes to Underdog City when he learns Vikir is alive and shows warmth Vikir does not know how to interpret.
- Vikir’s distrust remains intact because he remembers Hugo ordering his pre-regression execution.
- Vikir sells Hugo a trade/monster-hunting plan while privately intending to keep Ballak away from direct conflict with Baskerville.
- Hugo explicitly excludes **Rokoko** from the trade plan and demands its extermination; Vikir does not agree internally, but he avoids challenging the order during this negotiation.
- C109’s raw-layer Lucina/Freya/Firian-type naming drift is normalized to already established canonical forms **Lady Roxana → Penelope La Baskerville → Pomeranian La Baskerville**.
- The family closes before Hugo’s reaction to Pomeranian is explained.
