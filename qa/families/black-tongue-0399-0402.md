## Family decisions

- **Black Tongue** is a four-part family.
- Major Garam and Captain Kirko enter Black Tongue’s one-person Hearing Audit system together.
- The chamber’s black spheres are specialized **leeches** that separately consume blood, bone, flesh, or other tissues.
- Lieutenant Virginia is still alive inside a bone-feeding leech and asks for death; Vikir ends her suffering when Kirko cannot.
- Black Tongue’s body is itself covered/constructed by leech-like fibers, but no demon identity is inferred.
- Black Tongue clears Garam after probing the sudden post–Night Hound performance change and hearing Vikir’s plausible surface-family cover story.
- **Valentine Grimm** is the aligned-witness parent-name form Black Tongue initially mistakes Kirko for.
- Source-timed correction: Kirko’s parents were **two consenting junior guards in a secret relationship**.
- The earlier prisoner-on-guard rape story was **fabricated by Black Tongue / prison authorities** as a disciplinary example and to deepen hostility toward prisoners.
- Black Tongue admits he detained Kirko’s parents and simply forgot to release them, leaving them in leeches for decades.
- Kirko attacks Black Tongue after the parent reveal; rage forces a Graduator-level breakthrough.
- Black Tongue’s sexual/pregnancy taunt is preserved as abuse, not eroticized.
- Vikir returns, stops Kirko’s attack, deliberately lets his blood fall to bait Black Tongue, and removes Kirko.
- Vikir infers Black Tongue likely killed Kirko in the pre-regression future before she could become a known hero; this remains Vikir’s inference.
- A Black Tongue leech larva is discovered rooted in Kirko, draining blood/mana and copying her appearance.
- The copy-form parasite is unknown even to Decarabia; it is treated as a newly developed species, not an established demon.
- Vikir destroys the larva, stabilizes Kirko, and decides to obtain parasite eggs during the jailbreak.
- Kirko’s half-conscious plea not to be left alone remains emotionally ambiguous; Vikir explicitly recognizes that he is not Garam.
