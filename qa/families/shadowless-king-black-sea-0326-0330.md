## Family decisions

- **The Shadowless King of the Black Sea** is a five-part family.
- Vikir has all six tower stats unlocked while deliberately remaining Level1 to exploit reward scaling.
- **Fire of Inferiority / Blaze** is an A+ flint-like item fueled by the wielder’s inferiority complex.
- The Beetlemen escape ship is made from fallen insect remains/wings and can float on the Black Sea; it sank because of hull damage / the deep-sea threat, not because the sea universally prevents it from floating.
- Vikir dives with the D-grade water-resistant oil-paper bag as an air source and later as a timed seed container.
- The S-rank **Shadowless King of the Black Sea** is a primordial independent creature, not an Amdusias-controlled familiar.
- Beelzebub continuity remains: Venom — Madam Eight-Legs (S); Immortality — Gargoyle (S); Starvation Drought — Daylily of Blood Tree (S). Local Basilisk drift is rejected.
- Starvation Drought prevents tentacle regeneration by dehydrating the jellyfish.
- Daylily seeds hidden inside the dissolving oil-paper bag germinate in Black Sea water and provide enough buoyancy to raise the ghost ship.
- Additional Daylily seeds are embedded into the Shadowless King, exposing its location and forcing it toward the surface.
- Rapid ascent damages the creature through story-world pressure change; production does not overclaim precise real-world physiology.
- Beetlemen then propel the recovered ship toward the island while Vikir holds off the Shadowless King.
- Vikir knots Daylily stems/air sacs across the monster so the sacs remain clustered during ignition.
- Baby Madam’s silk lets the triplets pull Vikir away before the final ignition.
- **Fire of Inferiority** triggers the Daylily gas-sac chain reaction that kills the Shadowless King.
- Bianca’s pre-regression flaming-arrow solution against Daylilies is preserved as Vikir’s remembered tactical precedent.
- The system separately recognizes the earlier A+ Majin clear and the S-rank Shadowless King clear; the latter earns an **Impossible** achievement.
- The fairy’s final reaction is near-despair at Vikir’s continued Level1 exploit.
- Direct Fandom verification remains robots-blocked; established glossary canon is reused and source-local deep-sea/tower forms are not falsely represented as wiki-confirmed.

## Verdict

PASS.
