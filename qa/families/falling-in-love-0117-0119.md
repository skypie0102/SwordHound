## Continuity

### 116 → 117
Target 116 ends when Vikir recognizes Highbro / Middlebro / Lowbro's posture as an Imperial knight's oath to a chosen master. Target 117 begins the following morning but explicitly flashes back to the oath conversation, supplying the triplets' motive: repaying Vikir for saving their lives and becoming his Trident. No target-117 dialogue was imported into target 116.

### 117 → 118
Target 117 ends on Hugo declaring Lady Roxana the only woman he ever loved. Target 118 begins with Vikir's physical shock at hearing the word “love,” then continues Hugo's account of Roxana, Penelope, and the transformation of Baskerville after Penelope's abduction.

### 118 → 119
Target 118 ends on Vikir telling Hugo that Penelope left behind a daughter and that Hugo has a granddaughter. Target 119 begins with the immediate emotional aftermath: Hugo meets Pomeranian, confirms the lineage evidence, learns Penelope's later circumstances, and openly thanks Vikir.

### 119 → 120
Target 119 ends after Vikir asks to borrow all seven Baskerville Knight Orders for half a day to hunt a dangerous monster hiding inside the family. Target 120 moves to Seth's hidden cave and begins *The Hunt for the Second Son (1)*. No Seth-cave material is imported into target 119.

## Cross-chapter decisions

- **Colosseo Academy:** target 117 preserves the exact recruitment notice: Imperial Year 19 November 4–26 applications, eight semesters / four years, and Imperial Year 20 January 1 entrance.
- **Academy scale:** 7,000+ current students; 20,000+ counting graduates; roughly 4,000 faculty/staff.
- **Vikir's admission response:** Chinese controls **“I'll go.”** Recovered-English “I'll be back” is rejected.
- **Triplet oath:** Highbro / Middlebro / Lowbro place their swords against their own right feet with hilts toward Vikir, explicitly surrendering their lives to their chosen master.
- **Baskerville Trident:** the triplets' vow to become Vikir's Trident is preserved, along with their knowledge that Vikir had already reached Intermediate Sword Graduator two years earlier.
- **Academy purpose:** Vikir intends both to escape Hugo's close observation so he can grow stronger and to work through his pre-regression blacklist.
- **Roxana / Penelope / Pomeranian:** raw-layer Lucina/Freya/Firian-type drift is normalized to accepted **Lady Roxana → Penelope La Baskerville → Pomeranian La Baskerville** continuity.
- **Roxana's death:** target 118 reaffirms that Roxana died of illness shortly after Penelope's birth.
- **Hugo / Roxana romance:** Hugo's noble birth, Roxana's commoner background, love-at-first-sight, Roxana's initial distancing, Hugo's pursuit, and Baskerville's attempt to retrieve him are retained while rejecting severely corrupted witness phrasing.
- **Penelope's abduction:** Rokoko takes Penelope during a walk; target 118 keeps the family-history viewpoint that the route leak was unresolved at the time, without exposing Vikir's separately known Seth evidence to Hugo.
- **Origin of frontier Baskerville:** Hugo abandons the center, expands his military household through political marriages and children, receives broad Imperial support, and builds Baskerville into the Ironblood Sword House.
- **Red Salt Castle parallel:** Hugo's willingness to cooperate with Morgue after hearing of a Rokoko-abducted girl is retained as an emotional echo of Penelope's loss. Prior accepted Rosie continuity overrides the witness's relationship drift.
- **Pendant evidence:** Hugo personally made the pendant, originally gave it to Roxana, and later placed it with Penelope. Its survival confirms the Rokoko/Penelope connection.
- **Hugo's emotional withdrawal:** his fear that Penelope died hating him helps explain his later refusal to show affection to his other children; this is characterization, not exoneration of prior cruelty.
- **Pomeranian evidence:** Baskerville hair/eyes, inherited mole/birthmark, Rokoko provenance, pendant, and direct memories of Penelope support the granddaughter reveal.
- **Madam Eight-Legs:** Pomeranian's child-language account of the monster blocking Penelope's return is normalized to the accepted monster identity; Vikir truthfully states he killed it.
- **Osiris:** target 119 lets Osiris learn from Hugo's unexpected warmth and smile naturally, but does not rewrite him into an openly affectionate personality.
- **Final request:** Vikir asks to hunt a dangerous monster of unknown exact species **inside Baskerville** and requests all **seven Baskerville Knight Orders for half a day**—roughly half the family's military power.
- **Boundary discipline:** Seth's cave, demon dialogue, child murders, gate plan, and target-120 combat are excluded from this family.
