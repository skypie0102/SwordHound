## Continuity gate

### Chapter 11 → Chapter 12 — PASS

Chapter 11 ends with Cerberus collapsing after exactly seven steps from Bloody Mamba venom. Chapter 12 opens on the same collapsed/dead Cerberus, with greasy saliva and feces flowing from its mouths/rear while its death-energy enters Vikir.

No additional battle beat is invented between the chapters, and Chapter 12 does not contradict the Chapter 11 kill sequence.

### Chapter 12 → Chapter 13 — PASS

Chapter 12 reaches the hidden reward chamber, sword hilt, Baskerville-blood inscription, and Vikir’s shock at recognizing the relic name. Chapter 13 intentionally repeats/completes that inscription and names **Beelzebub**.

The overlap is source-authentic rather than accidental duplication. Chapter 12 therefore does not need to absorb Chapter 13’s relic mechanics early.

### Chapter 13 → Chapter 14 — PASS

Chapter 13 ends with Vikir identifying the hidden **10th Fang** material among ordinary books and anticipating that Hugo will unknowingly hand it to him.

Chinese Chapter 14 opens only after the practical examination has ended and the young hounds are being evaluated/rewarded. Recovered English Chapter 14 likewise changes title to *Solitary (1)*.

No Chapter 14 result/report scene is imported into Chapter 13.
