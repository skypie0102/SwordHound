## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved the seven local houses as **Montblanc, Pierre, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Ferragamo, Hermes, and Prada** from the Chinese/transliterated witnesses. No claim is made that all seven have independently indexed Fandom entries.
2. Preserved the heirs' social position: local houses wealthy and influential enough to pressure previous Baskerville officials, yet still subordinate to Baskerville rule.
3. Preserved the exact point of Vikir's champagne-tower feat: mana resonance travels vertically and shatters the glasses **from the top downward**, demonstrating control far beyond ordinary Expert-level manipulation.
4. Corrected recovered-English `Gradient` to the established project rank **Sword Graduator**. The seven heirs recognize Vikir's visible aura/control as belonging to the Graduator realm.
5. Preserved the heirs' realization that Vikir's rumored childhood feats may be real; no later wiki lore is imported beyond the rumors actually stated in the chapter.
6. Preserved the heirs' attempt to reframe their extortion scheme as a test of Vikir's moral character.
7. Preserved Hugo's teaching exactly in function: **a master may test a dog, but a dog may never test its master**.
8. Preserved Vikir bringing out the same wooden stake used in the ten-billion-gold law demonstration.
9. Preserved the sudden, severe beating: facial, head, neck, and shoulder strikes; broken teeth; split lips/tongues; blood; and continued blows after they begin begging.
10. The chapter closes on Vikir continuing the punishment after the heirs claim to understand why they are being beaten.
