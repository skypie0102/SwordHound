## Notable recurring failure patterns

- explicit blood/internal-organ/flesh detail generalized into nonspecific injury;
- direct killing/assassination wording softened into broader “eliminate,” “beaten,” or generic confrontation language;
- bodily-function and humiliation details omitted;
- source-present attraction/body-state/suggestive framing replaced with safer general admiration;
- explicit insults/dehumanizing labels dropped while retaining only the surrounding action;
- occasional unsupported intensification or invented specificity;
- source-explicit corpse/decomposition states reduced to generic damage.
