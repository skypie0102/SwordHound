## Documented terminology/source decisions

### Cradle variant / “Tower of Swords”

The Chinese raw varies its descriptive blade-cradle wording and contains a later localized form corresponding to a “blade-point tower.” Context is unequivocally the same infant trial introduced in Chapter 1. Fandom explicitly names that trial **Cradle of Swords**. The MTL’s isolated “Tower of Swords” is therefore not treated as a separate location.

### Poisoned-heel warrior

Chinese describes an ancient, virtually invulnerable Baskerville warrior whose only weak point was the heel/ankle area covered by his nurse’s hands during Styx immersion. Fandom’s Baskerville Clan page repeats this River Styx legend. The final uses a descriptive “peerless swordsman/warrior” rather than manufacturing a canonical proper epithet from a Chinese localized phrase.
