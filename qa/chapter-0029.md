## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved **Dortmeyer Street** as the central commercial street where the public notices are posted.
2. Kept both statute groups as atomic information windows rather than flattening them into prose.
3. Preserved the source punishments without softening them: death for illegal human trafficking; wrist amputation for illegal gambling; removal of the eyes for illegal usury; facial tattooing for illegal entertainment establishments; exile for illegal lobbying; kneecap removal for tax evasion; drowning for smuggling; tongue removal for illegal drugs; extermination of three clans for military-supply embezzlement; 600 strokes for adultery.
4. Corrected recovered-English semantic errors including **waterboarding → death by drowning** and **“extermination of three legs” → extermination of three clans**.
5. Preserved the citizens' core grievance: the statutes punish commoners while nobles and wealthy offenders historically escape enforcement.
6. Preserved Vikir's deliberate tolerance of mockery so the crowd grows bold enough to reveal how little faith it has in the law.
7. Preserved Chihuahua's local authority and his intervention when the crowd goes too far.
8. Preserved the stake dimensions at approximately **70 cm / 3 kg**, driven only about **10 cm** into the ground so even a child could pull it free.
9. Vikir's first special statute is kept explicit: **100,000,000 gold** to whoever pulls the stake.
