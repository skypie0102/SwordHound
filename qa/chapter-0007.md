## Material corrections versus recovered English MTL

1. **Hugo’s instrumental worldview retained.** Other people, especially children shaped into weapons, are tools in his eyes.
2. **Weakness-as-sin doctrine kept consistent** with Chapter 6 and Baskerville ideology.
3. **Age/elder-status line repaired from Chinese context.** Vikir dismisses seniority as something that changes simply with time; the MTL substantially corrupts the exchange.
4. **Hugo’s fratricide implication preserved.** His remark about “working hard to become the eldest” leads Vikir to understand that Hugo killed older brothers to become heir/patriarch.
5. **Forgiveness anecdote restored.** A surviving woman from a family destroyed by Baskerville becomes a nun and forgives Hugo; his remembered response equates forgiveness with the weakness of those unable to take revenge.
6. **Hugo’s approval preserved.** He laughs and treats Vikir’s answer as evidence that Vikir truly thinks like his child.
7. **Under-fifteen diet restored from Chinese.** Water plus a haggis-like mash made from meat, offal, and some vegetables; sweets/chocolate function as performance rewards.
8. **Morgue chocolate reference normalized** without adding unrelated clan lore.
9. **Raw cocoa request corrected.** Vikir wants unprocessed, intensely flavored cocoa beans rather than ordinary finished chocolate.
10. **Bloody Beans retained.** More than one hundred red beans fill the small pouch; the source claims one can flavor roughly one hundred liters of chocolate, yielding roughly ten thousand liters for the pouch.
11. **Barrymore’s misunderstanding preserved.** He assumes Vikir simply loves chocolate, while Vikir’s refusal to process the beans shows another purpose.
12. **No unsupported pantry/castle proper name introduced.** Descriptive location wording remains where canonical naming is unresolved.
