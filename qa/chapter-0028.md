## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved Vikir arriving **one day early** at Underdog City Hall and being mistaken for an employee's child.
2. Preserved his official role as **Deputy Magistrate** while functioning as **Acting Magistrate** because the higher office is vacant.
3. Canonicalized **Chihuahua Baskerville** as male, an outsider-born administrator and **General Secretary / office manager** of Underdog City Hall.
4. Preserved Vikir's first apparent order, **“Bring me wine and women,”** as deliberate bait for the city's corrupt interests rather than sanitizing or moralizing it.
5. Preserved Chihuahua's resentment toward bloodline discrimination and Vikir's preference for proven outsiders over unreliable blood relatives.
6. Preserved the illegal-economy inventory: gambling, entertainment establishments, slave auctions, illegal finance, bribed officials, and the black ledgers remembered from Vikir's previous life.
7. Vikir's goal remains to destroy the city's underground economy within **one week** while respecting existing laws and adding only one new special law.
8. The odd Chinese/MTL line literally suggesting Vikir **“went to the Academy”** immediately after declaring the crackdown is treated as inherited source-layer idiom/translation drift because the surrounding scene never changes location and continues in City Hall. The draft preserves the scene continuity rather than inventing an Academy trip.
9. The welcome banquet continues without Vikir; he intentionally uses it as bait—**putting out filth to attract flies**—while planning the next day's move.
