## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved the immediate consequence of the stake demonstration: the story spreads throughout Underdog City and Baskerville territory, and crime falls by roughly half as citizens begin believing that laws will actually be enforced.
2. `移木之信` is rendered descriptively as **Trust Won by Moving the Stake**. No unsupported claim is made that this is a canonical English proper title.
3. Preserved Chihuahua Baskerville's twenty-year administrative service, calligraphy/forgery skill, outsider background, local loyalty, and prior-life reputation for blunt integrity.
4. Preserved Vikir **asking** Chihuahua to teach him calligraphy rather than ordering him, which is the point of Chihuahua's surprise.
5. Rejected the recovered-English substitution of **Camus Morgue** in the retrospective precedent. Chinese Chapter 31 names a separate Morgue figure, `夏穆 / 卡穆` in the Chinese layer; later Chinese chronology independently repeats **Kamu** as a distinct historical Morgue ruler. The draft therefore uses **Kamu Morgue**, not Camus.
6. The epithet `侧天武侯` has no verified current Fandom English form. **Heaven-Tilting Martial Marquis** is explicitly provisional/source-descriptive and must be rechecked if stronger canonical evidence appears.
7. Preserved the source's future-history point that Kamu's harsh law-and-order rule was unpopular but increased human survival during the Demon Realm war and left Morgue territory with the largest surviving population after the Generation of Destruction.
8. Preserved Vikir's fiscal logic: recover the large reward by seizing criminal black money, increase public revenue, then strengthen welfare for the poor.
9. Preserved the scale of Vikir's prior-life knowledge: over one hundred present/future hideouts and roughly twenty-five years of underground money-flow knowledge.
10. Beelzebub's wrist-artery reaction remains a bloodlust cue, not a new ability reveal.
11. Preserved the first organized backlash from the local youth self-governance group / influential-family heirs; Chihuahua characterizes it as a decadent **social club**.
12. Final line remains **“The flies smelled it first.”** This closes the bait motif from Chapter 28 and flows directly into Chapter 32, *The Social Club (1)*.

## MTL issues rejected

- `Camus of the House of Morgue` and the associated pronoun/title contamination for the separate figure Kamu.
- `Auspicious Weather` as an unsupported rendering of `侧天武侯`.
- `Guardian Knights` or swordsmanship details where the Chinese only contrasts sword-user incentives with administrative service.
- `rob everything here` where Vikir means raiding criminal organizations and returning illicit funds to the treasury.
- `dung beetles smelled it first`; Chinese returns to the **flies** motif established in Chapter 28.
- pronoun, first-person, and office-title drift throughout the recovered English witness.
