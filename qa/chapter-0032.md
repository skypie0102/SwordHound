## Important decisions and corrections

1. **Burning Suspension** is accepted for the elite Underdog City club; current indexed Fandom location evidence independently lists **Burning Suspension (Club)**.
2. Preserved the physical setting: 69th–70th floors of a high-rise hotel, VVIP room costing 10,000,000 gold for six hours, and 100,000,000-gold champagne bottles.
3. Preserved the **385-glass champagne tower** arithmetic: 100 + 81 + 64 + 49 + 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 385. A later Chinese explosion sequence momentarily prints 80 on the 9×9 tier; the established tier definition and total require 81, so the draft keeps 81 and documents the source slip here.
4. Retained **Donquixote Perignon 666** as the source's fictional luxury champagne label. No claim is made that Fandom independently canonicalizes it.
5. Preserved the waiters licking spilled champagne from the table while the heirs throw gold at them and call it the **trickle-down effect**; the class-degradation scene is not softened.
6. Rendered the local body descriptively as the **Youth Self-Governance Committee** rather than promoting corrupted Chinese/MTL committee syllables into a false proper name.
7. Preserved the political arrangement: old local houses receive limited autonomy from Baskerville in exchange for information, tribute, and taxes.
8. Preserved the illegal source of the heirs' wealth: clandestine slave auctions exploiting displaced tribal people and other abducted victims created by frontier instability.
9. **Messinadnaro Family** is accepted from current indexed Fandom evidence as the wealthy eighth merchant family of Underdog City. Chapter 32 itself only lets the seven heirs speak of the house as gone/framed/removed; later massacre details are not imported early.
10. Preserved the heirs' planned `rookie-official training`: lure Vikir into a lavish alcohol/women party, then dump the bill on him.
11. Rejected MTL gender drift around Vikir and the nonsensical `Barnes` rendering of his middle name **Van**.
12. Preserved Vikir's entrance behind the women, the waiter's broken arm, the sub-second defeat of the other waiters, and Vikir identifying himself without added theatrics.
13. Preserved the dark-crimson Baskerville aura / mana-resonance feat that destroys the champagne tower **top-down**, one tier at a time.
14. Chapter endpoint is the seven heirs, drenched in glass and champagne, conceding: **“We'll pay for the drinks.”**
