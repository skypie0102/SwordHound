## Important decisions and corrections

1. Hugo's relatively new nose scar is preserved as the result of a skilled **female archer** during frontier subjugation. She escapes; Hugo says he left the same kind of mark on her face. Her identity remains unrevealed because Chinese does not name her here.
2. Preserved the completed training structure: 3.5 years basic + 3.5 years practical.
3. Preserved Vikir's expected old-life route: apprentice in the **Pit Bull Knights**, then roughly **21 months** in Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain.
4. Hugo's unexpected alternative is rendered as an elite **administrative/political track**, not MTL nonsense such as `bard` or a modern parliamentary career.
5. Chinese `下议员` / `上议员` is translated descriptively as **lower councilor / upper council** for the clan's administrative structure. These are not promoted as modern real-world parliamentary titles.
6. Canonical operational office is **Deputy Magistrate of Underdog City**. Current Fandom independently lists Vikir in that role.
7. Preserved the actual scope of the Deputy Magistrate job: administration, legislation, judiciary, projects, and territorial affairs.
8. Preserved the vacancy logic: Hugo's second son entered prolonged secluded training; the affiliated deputies were removed together over bribery allegations.
9. Preserved **Underdog City population: 500,000**, with Vikir functioning as de facto ruler while technically Deputy Magistrate.
10. Preserved Hugo's rationale that Vikir is at least ten years ahead of the clan's normal geniuses and therefore receives both extraordinary authority and responsibility.
11. Preserved Vikir invoking the reward promised seven years earlier over the Morgue ruby-mine dispute.
12. The object/request in `Could you lend it to me once?` remains deliberately unrevealed. No wiki/MTL/later-chapter knowledge is imported early.
13. Preserved Hugo calling Vikir `Son` and Vikir's continued discomfort with it.
14. Preserved Barrymore's interpretation of the posting as leadership/ruler training and his prediction that adequate results will lead to **Colosseo Academy** admission.
15. Chapter ends with Vikir deciding to secure achievements by the fastest, most reliable route, which flows directly into Chapter 28's **Special Laws of Vikir** administration arc.
