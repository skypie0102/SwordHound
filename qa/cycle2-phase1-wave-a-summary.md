## Method

Every chapter in Chapters 1–100 received a direct Chinese-primary sanitization-fidelity review in contiguous title-family order. Historical QA/PASS state was treated only as background evidence.

Review checked for:

- softened or euphemized violence, gore, profanity, degradation, cruelty, bodily detail, or other harsh source material;
- omitted/generalized explicit source details;
- unsupported intensification;
- safety-limited source material that should remain non-restored while all surrounding ordinary content is still reviewed.

Byte ratios and baseline diagnostics were not used to skip any chapter.

## Safety-limited-reviewed chapters

Chapters **43, 45, 48–50, 53–59, 61–63, 67, 79–80** were resolved under the Cycle-2 safety-limited rule.

Safety-limited status means the source contains material that is intentionally not restored in unsafe/sexualized operational detail, but the surrounding ordinary narrative was still reviewed for unrelated sanitization loss. This status is a resolved Phase-1 disposition, not a failure.

Notable safety-limited categories in Wave A include:

- sexualized framing involving explicitly underage characters;
- operational biological-harm details around Red Death;
- other source passages already documented under the project safety-limited workflow.
