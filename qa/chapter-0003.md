10. **Nursery catastrophe logic restored.** Vikir has no attachment to the clan but objects to the catastrophe beginning with his own death.
11. **Styx protection retained.** The snakes’ scales cannot wound his hardened body.
12. **Mana-pool result retained.** Drinking Styx water filled the previously empty mana pool; his fists emit light far earlier than the other infants.
13. **Predator-freeze imagery retained.** The snakes stiffen in instinctive fear before Vikir kills them.
14. **Explicit death details restored.** Chinese says the snakes convulse, defecate and urinate, then die; the recovered English omits part of this.
15. **Next-day fang inconsistency preserved.** Chinese first has venom dripping from exposed fangs, then says the venomous fangs had already been removed by the next-day inspection. The draft keeps both temporal statements instead of inventing who removed them or when.
16. **Unsupported location name avoided.** Chinese says Hugo hurries from the main castle to the infant-rearing/nursery castle. Current Fandom retrieval does not verify “Fang Castle,” so the MTL proper name is not forced into this chapter.
17. **Final legend sentence corrected.** Chinese says Vikir crossed the Cradle in the shortest time, plunged into the Styx, and broke two snakes’ necks; the MTL adds “longest immersion,” which is not repeated here in the raw.
18. **Eight-year timeskip retained exactly as the family endpoint.**

## Documented source/canonical limitations

### Bloody Mamba

Chinese uses a distinctive named/descritive venomous-snake term and later another localized species label. The recovered English novel consistently calls the species **Bloody Mamba**, including later references back to this exact infancy incident. Current Fandom synopsis retrieval calls the animals “black mambas” descriptively but provides no dedicated monster page/formal canonical entry. The final therefore uses **Bloody Mamba** as a scoped fallback English term and records that its Fandom canonical status is unresolved rather than misrepresenting the evidence.

### Infant age

Earlier family text places Vikir at roughly/just one hundred days old. Chapter 3 later says he achieved the mana feat before eight months old. Both can be true in the broad sense, but the wording is imprecise. No new exact age is invented.

### Snake fangs

The Chinese raw explicitly describes venom dripping from exposed fangs at death, then says the fangs had already been removed by the following-day inspection. The draft preserves that sequence without fabricating an off-page removal event.
