## Source inconsistency — age line

Chinese Chapter 18 repeatedly establishes Vikir as **eight years old**, including the setup, audience dialogue, record comparison, and endpoint context. One isolated late sentence literally calls him a `15-year-old youth`.

This conflicts with:

- the rest of Chapter 18;
- Chapter 19's repeated eight-year-old framing;
- Chinese Chapter 20's explicit formula that **Low Sword Expert / 1st Fang is the normal Baskerville milestone around age fifteen**.

The draft therefore resolves the isolated phrase as a **comparison to the level normally expected of a fifteen-year-old Baskerville**, while keeping Vikir's actual age eight. This is an explicit source/translation inconsistency resolution, not an undisclosed rewrite.
