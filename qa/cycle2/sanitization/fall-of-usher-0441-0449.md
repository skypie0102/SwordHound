## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 441 — PASS

The draft preserves the evolved Red Death's lethality, the expectation that Masyaf may contain only bones, Madeline's buried-alive history, her interest in Bianca's younger body, the much-higher-than-survival death probability of the infiltration plan, Dolores's roughly one-hour protection limit, and the red/black flare logic.

### Chapter 442 — PASS

The mansion's lethal route rules, Roderick's corpse/mummy-like condition, Cervantes's suicide contrast, Madeline's buried-alive history and corpse-like body, the Chimeries-hide corpse desecration taunt, and the confidence-trigger phrase remain at source-supported force.

### Chapter 443 — PASS

The draft retains Madeline's half-rotted shell, empty abdominal cavity, odor concealment, desire for Bianca's young/fresh body, Tudor's arrow wound and blood, Madeline's skull penetration, bodily convulsions with fingers/toes twisting like a dying insect, Pigi's blood effect, and the demonic scream. No material sanitization discrepancy was found.

### Chapter 444 — FAIL

Two source-explicit details are generalized:

- Bianca explicitly **kisses Tudor on the lips** after embracing him; the draft says only **“She kissed Tudor,”** removing the source-specified contact point.
- When Vikir reveals that he has secretly cleared their escape route, the Chinese shows several monsters **beheaded with blood dripping from the severed remains**. The draft reduces this to a list of **dead monsters**, removing the decapitation and dripping-blood imagery.

The chapter otherwise preserves Tudor's blood loss/hypothermia, Bianca's willingness to die rather than surrender her body, the old-timeline Tudor death/Bianca breakdown, and Love Shield's romantic activation condition.

### Chapter 445 — FAIL

The source tracks Tudor/Bianca through bodily secretions left during flight: **sweat, saliva, snot/nasal discharge, blood, footprints, and hair**. The draft retains sweat, saliva, footprints, hair, and blood but drops the snot/nasal-discharge detail.

More materially, Madeline's anticipation of a new body includes an explicitly grotesque plan to **have many children and consume/enjoy children made from her own blood and flesh**, with a drooling reaction. The draft reduces this to a vague list — **“Children. Fresh blood. Fresh flesh.”** — removing the cannibalistic offspring context and its force.

### Chapter 446 — PASS

The flood, deliberate destruction of Masyaf, Bianca's collapse over her father, Isolde's demonization, self-destruction warning, surviving demonized Usher force, and Daylily trap setup remain at source-supported force. No material sanitization discrepancy was found.

### Chapter 447 — FAIL

Several concrete body-horror details are softened:

- a Daylily closes on a demon and turns it into a **meat patty**, leaving its lower body crushed into minced meat with broken bone and black blood; the draft keeps crushed flesh/bone and black blood but removes the meat-patty/minced-lower-body image;
- after Bianca shatters Isolde's skull, the Chinese states that **the contents of the skull fall out and internal organs spill out**, while the draft reduces this to **“black matter spilled”** and omits the internal-organ detail;
- the source later describes the Daylilies wrapping and dancing with demon body fragments; the draft compresses this into generalized swallowing/purge language.

### Chapter 448 — PASS

The draft retains Andrealphus losing a wing and leg, its willingness to trade its life for a partial Gate opening, its threat to recover by eating hundreds of children, the repeated death-challenge phrase, the shot that bursts its eyes and destroys the left skull, and Roderick's reappearance after the flood clears the Red Death.

### Chapter 449 — FAIL

The source explicitly describes Roderick speaking from a collapsing body with **internal organs broken into pieces**. The draft generalizes this to **“a body already falling apart,”** dropping the internal-organ condition.

The remaining severe material is retained: repeated head destruction leaving only jaw and tongue, Roderick's cracking bones and self-destructive final shot, Andrealphus's heart/chest destruction, torn wings, pincushion-like arrow state, monstrous death scream, and Bianca's blood-tinged tears.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **445:** restore the nasal-discharge trail detail and Madeline's explicit grotesque offspring-consumption intent at source force.
- **447:** restore the meat-patty/minced-lower-body image, Isolde's internal-organ spill, and the body-fragment handling where source-significant.
- **449:** restore Roderick's broken-internal-organ condition.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
