## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. The rebuilt drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. The prior completeness rebuild is coverage/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 307 — PASS

The draft retains the Hell Tree's body/throat imagery, death-or-retirement nourishment rule, restoration condition if someone escapes, and the grotesque fairy as a lump of flesh with twisted facial features, exposed organs, and ugly wetly beating wings. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 308 — PASS

The draft retains the fairy vomiting bloodwater and chunks of internal organs, strongly acidic fluid melting Vikir's fingers, intense pain, Vikir digging through the vomit for candy, and forcing apart the fairy's jaws to recover candy fragments from between its teeth. Rat butchery/edibility and stat mechanics also remain faithful.

### Chapter 309 — PASS

The corrupted Academy retains rotting-flesh stench, unexplained blood, death/failed-escape stakes, and the fairy's body tearing open from overextended arms with blood and pus running from the wounds. The psychological fear reversal and Hell Hound reveal remain at source force.

### Chapter 310 — PASS

The draft preserves Granola running with tears and mucus covering his face, the one-percent physical weakening, ugly-death risk, betrayal by trusted peers, Hell Hound attack/lifting, commoner students helping the wounded, and the scent-tracking setup without material softening.

### Chapter 311 — FAIL

The chapter otherwise preserves substantial gore: the Hell Hound's entire nose/nose bridge plus fist-sized sensory mass being cut away, hot blood/oily fluid spray, jaw removal, half-foreleg loss, throat piercing, deliberate crippling, Vikir's blood-covered body, self-inflicted side cut, vomiting, pus, and the later Cerberus crush.

However, immediately before Cerberus appears, the Chinese explicitly says the few surviving Hell Hounds stand **with tails tucked, trembling and wetting themselves**. The draft retains only:

> Several surviving Hell Hounds whimpered.  
> Tails tucked.

The fear-wetting bodily-function detail is omitted.

### Chapter 312 — PASS

The draft preserves the lethal tail poison, Vikir being poisoned, Baby Madam extracting it, regeneration, the eye-to-brain strike, and the internal skull twisting that crushes **muscle fibers, nerve bundles, and brain matter**. Granola's later vomiting, tears/mucus, rotting-nose sensation, and melting-brain comparison are retained.

### Chapter 313 — FAIL

The Chinese opening says Cerberus's final head bursts, its huge body falls, and **sulfur and blood spray from its burst abdomen** across the shop floor. The draft changes this to:

> Sulfur.  
> Blood.  
> Viscera.  
> The discharge from the dead monster's body...

This removes the explicit burst-abdomen origin while adding **viscera**, which the Chinese does not specify in that line. It is therefore both a generalization and unsupported intensification.

Later, when the fairy produces the reward, the source says its mouth corners split almost open and **bloodstains become visible**. The draft retains the mouth tearing wider but omits the bloodstains.

The exposed beating heart, candy vomiting, Physical Resistance +1, six-stat model, Candy Shop exchange, and Level-1 optimization remain faithful.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **311:** restore the source-explicit fear-wetting detail for the surviving Hell Hounds before Cerberus crushes them.
- **313:** restore the burst-abdomen sulfur/blood discharge, remove the unsupported viscera addition, and restore the bloodstained split-mouth detail for the fairy.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

**FAIL — Chapters 311 and 313 require Phase-4 remediation.**
