The draft changes these to **“Healthy organs”** and a mind that is simply **“far softer.”**

Those substitutions remove the source's predatory, bodily objectification while preserving only the functional possession logic.

However, the Chinese separately describes a **swamp made of blood and flesh** as part of Vikir's inner landscape. That explicit flesh-and-blood environmental image is omitted.
