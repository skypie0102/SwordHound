### Chapter 254 — FAIL

The source explicitly describes Vikir's Imperial-Capital assignment as locating and **secretly assassinating hostile forces**. It later states that the principal members of three demon-linked factions were **assassinated**, with 174 people removed over three months and the deaths disguised as natural causes, accidents, rival assassinations, or disappearances.

The draft repeatedly substitutes softer language:

- the mission becomes identifying hostile forces and **“quietly remov[ing] them”**;
- the 174 targets are said to have been **“eliminated”**.

The following sentence does make clear that these were deaths, so the event is not omitted. However, the source-explicit assassination wording is still euphemized, which fails the strict Phase-1 sanitization-fidelity gate.

The chapter otherwise preserves the 174-person count, demon-only targeting limitation, Hugo's marriage questioning, erased-public-identity operation, noisy people being dealt with, the Wraith Tree, Black Whistle, full military authority, and Eighth Fang objective.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 254:** restore explicit source-supported assassination terminology for Vikir's capital mission and the 174 demon-linked targets rather than euphemizing it as quiet removal/elimination.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
