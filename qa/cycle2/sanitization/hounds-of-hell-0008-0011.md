## Chapter 8 — PASS

Retained without softening:

- brutally strict child training beginning as soon as children can walk;
- prohibition on lying down outside designated rest;
- guardian knights driving children mercilessly;
- children sleeping beside giant monster corpses or small monster cubs to condition them to terror;
- eight- and nine-year-olds being thrown into Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain for roughly a month;
- explicit scoring that rewards abandoning others and hunting monsters;
- death as zero points and meaningless death as the least valuable death;
- threatening language from other children, including smashing Vikir's head with a rock and “don't blame me if he dies”;
- the harsh “young hounds baring unfinished fangs” framing.

No unsupported intensification was found.

## Chapter 9 — PASS

Retained without softening:

- children knocking one another unconscious, rolling rivals down slopes, sending them into swamps, and stabbing/cutting them;
- killing another examinee being discouraged rather than forbidden;
- the possibility of quietly killing a rival if one can absorb the point penalty or avoid Guide Dog attention;
- recurring accidental/mysterious deaths during the examination;
- the practical exam being used to eliminate competitors and disliked peers;
- eating snakes, worms, and a gutted/roasted demon-mutated rat;
- burning-hot monster feces used as tracking evidence;
- the Hellhound's lethal bite/fire description and its predatory chase.

No source harshness was euphemized.

## Chapter 10 — PASS

Retained without softening:

- chocolate poisoning producing convulsions, foam, thick saliva/mucus, watery feces, incontinence, diarrhea, overheating, tachycardia, seizures, and eyes reddening toward bursting;
- deliberate targeting of the toxin-stressed kidneys;
- the Hellhound collapsing while expelling burning-hot waste;
- Vikir explicitly finishing the helpless monster rather than leaving it alive;
- repeated blunt-sword blows used to beat the Hellhound to death;
- the corpse's lingering heat and the pack of eleven Hellhounds surrounding Vikir;
- the murderous intent that makes the pack hesitate.

The English does not add stronger gore than the source.

## Chapter 11 — PASS

Retained without softening:

- a single Cerberus killing hundreds of Seven-Great-Clan warriors in the opening war;
- existing broken ribs and arrow wounds in the monster;
- sword techniques designed only to kill;
- Vikir's ribs being broken by Cerberus's claw;
- the threat of jaws tearing flesh and crushing bone;
- sharpened stakes impaling Cerberus and causing fatal wounds;
- stakes lodged in flank and mouths;
- cocoa poison entering through wounds;
- foam and burning excrement while the monster remains active;
- Bloody Beans being forced into the three mouths;
- final seven-step collapse caused by Bloody Mamba venom.

No unsupported intensification was found.
