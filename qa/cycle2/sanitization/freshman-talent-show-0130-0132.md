## Chapter 130 — FAIL

The source explicitly frames Vikir's wartime memory with the image of **corpses piled into mountains and blood flowing like rivers** during the Age of Destruction. The draft replaces this with substantially softer memorial/front-line language about long marches, lost comrades, grief, and exhausted soldiers.

The emotional function remains, but the source's concrete death/blood imagery is generalized away.

**Required Phase-4 remediation:** restore the omitted harsh wartime image at source-level force without gratuitous expansion.

## Chapter 131 — FAIL

The source narrator directly characterizes the six second-years' treatment of Pigi as **cruel, brutal, merciless, malicious**, with violence present in the way they look at him. The draft depicts the bullying but reduces this explicit characterization to the group treating Pigi like an easy toy who will not resist.

That weakens the narrator's stated force rather than merely rephrasing it.

**Required Phase-4 remediation:** restore the source-explicit cruelty/violent intent in natural English.

## Chapter 132 — FAIL

The accepted draft materially sanitizes the Night Hound's retaliation.

The Chinese source explicitly describes:

- a punch shattering a student's nasal bone and teeth;
- a dislocated jaw;
- broken teeth piercing through the cheek;
- the nose broken into multiple pieces;
- a collapsed cheekbone and blood from facial openings;
- the six students wetting themselves in fear;
- a knife cutting an X-shaped target mark into each forehead;
- an explicit warning that if they cause trouble again, both they **and their parents will be killed**.

The draft instead collapses the assault to an off-screen/summary outcome (“the confrontation ended… all of them were on the ground—stunned, frightened”), keeps only generic X scars, and changes the death threat to **“your family name will not protect you.”**

This is a clear softening/generalization of fictional violence, fear response, and threat language.

**Required Phase-4 remediation:** restore the source-level outcome and threat faithfully, without adding extra gore beyond the source.
