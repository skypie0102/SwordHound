## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 333 — FAIL

The draft preserves the Dragon Majin laboratory, incubation/collection/spawning/blood-collection/fighting rooms, drained corpse-like subjects, fighting specimens, and Vikir's realization that another specimen must eventually be paired with him.

However, the Chinese explicitly defines **mating** as **sexual behavior between male and female for reproductive purposes**. The draft replaces that with:

> “Breeding. A room designed to pair male and female specimens—then produce offspring.”

That removes the source's direct sexual-behavior definition and therefore sanitizes the facility's stated purpose.

### Chapter 334 — FAIL

The coercive Giant Mantis sequence remains clearly non-consensual in the draft, including stimulant mist, physical restraint, forced breeding, decapitation, and cannibalism. But several source-explicit physical details are generalized away:

- under the mist, the female turns so her **back and buttocks** are easier for the male to mount; the draft reduces this to turning partly away and stopping attack;
- the Dragon Majin's two hands explicitly seize the **buttocks of the female and male** before forcing them together; the draft only says one hand seized each monster;
- after the forced mating, the source explicitly calls the female **forcibly pregnant** before transfer to the spawning area; the draft omits that wording.

The post-mating killing/cannibalism remains at strong source force, but these omissions are still material sanitization.

The draft correctly frames the scene as hypothermia treatment and later identifies the blue mist as a coercive breeding stimulant rather than consent. It also retains Sinclaire's grief over her father, Vikir's guilt, and the Dragon Majin hands descending for forced intervention.

The draft reduces this to soaked clothes having been removed, dry skin, and “shared body heat,” omitting the explicit nudity, rear embrace, and full-body contact.
