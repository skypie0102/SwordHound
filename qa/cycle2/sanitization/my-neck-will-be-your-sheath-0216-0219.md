## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 216 — PASS

The draft preserves the reveal of Camus, her years of crying/screaming/collapsing until her voice is permanently hoarse, her search for Vikir's corpse and plan to resurrect him, her wish to remain with him even beyond death, and Vikir's continued insistence that demons must die.

The source's emotional and physical force is also retained: Camus repeatedly orders Vikir to kill her, cries and insults him, frames herself as the troublesome ex-girlfriend against the Saintess, gives him her diary, exposes her neck, kneels, places his hand at her throat, touches her lips to his forearm, and says her neck will become his sheath. Vikir's self-description as a loveless killing machine and Seere's midnight spike through his abdomen are likewise preserved.

### Chapter 217 — PASS

The draft retains Seere's feminine/seductive presentation through Camus's body and the predatory taunt that, with Camus's face and body, she would charm men, feed on them, and become stronger. The Complete Resurrection requirements and catastrophic backlash also remain: bodily remains, reversed mana through the brain and blood vessels, immediate death in most cases, madness or catastrophic loss of bodily function in rare survivors, ruptured channels, half-dead brain, and Camus's living-corpse state.

No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 218 — PASS

The draft retains the cut across Camus's throat and blood, Vikir's concealed bluff and hesitation, twenty-four hellfire-heated spikes through his left arm, Camus's transmitted love/longing/resentment and years of grief, Seere's threat to kill every child in the Academy, Camus's blood tears and soul damage, her exposed neck/chest, the renewed mana-surge death risk, and Vikir's Sword-Master breakthrough while trying to save her.

### Chapter 219 — FAIL

Two source-explicit harsh/bodily details are generalized or replaced:

- the Chinese says Vikir considers reaching out and **cutting off Geronto/Rosie's head** before the teleport completes; the draft changes this to **“One strike could end Rosie,”** removing the explicit decapitation image;
- a student says merely hearing the Night Hound's voice nearly made him **wet his pants**; the draft replaces this with **“Just hearing those voices—nearly killed me.”** This both sanitizes the bodily-function humiliation and introduces stronger lethal language not supported by that line.

The chapter otherwise retains Seere's destruction, the absence of even flesh/soul remains, Vikir's restored emotions, the hidden-space aftermath, Sadi's praise of demonic handiwork, and the survival-to-dawn emotional beat.
