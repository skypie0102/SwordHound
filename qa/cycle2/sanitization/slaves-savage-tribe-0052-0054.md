## Chapter 52 — FAIL

The draft preserves the overwhelming majority of the chapter's severe bodily harm and execution imagery:

- muscles and blood vessels ruptured throughout Vikir's body;
- shattered fingers and sword arm;
- multiple leg fractures;
- almost no part of his body escaping injury;
- the noose nearly breaking his neck while saving him;
- prisoners showing signs of harsh torture;
- prisoners stripped and forced up the blade-like Thorn Tree;
- bleeding from cuts and punctures;
- fire climbing the oiled trunk;
- deaths by bleeding, burning, falling, spear, or wolves;
- Ahun threatening to cook Vikir and kicking him to the ground.

One concrete injury mechanic is softened:

- Chinese: Vikir's ribs suffer spiral fractures and their sharp ends **pierce into his internal organs**.
- Manuscript: “Several ribs had broken and twisted inward, their sharp ends **damaging** his organs.”

“Damaging” is less specific and less visceral than the source's physical penetration. This is ordinary nonsexual injury detail and should not be generalized.

The closing pool scene also contains minor nudity involving Aiyen. That component is safety-limited and is not an ordinary restoration target.

**Disposition:** FAIL — explicit injury mechanism softened; separate safety-limited minor-nudity note.
