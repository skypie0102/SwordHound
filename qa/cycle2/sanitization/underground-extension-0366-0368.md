## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 366 — PASS

The draft preserves the Level-Nine labor brutality, near-total lack of tools, barefoot geothermal work, bodily exposure to sharp/heavy/burning materials, Sakkuth's plague-filth framing, his explicit claim that he ate the prior prisoner alive, physically accumulated poison, Red Death threat, and Vikir's nail-in-mouth strike piercing cheek, nose, jaw, and neck with heavy bleeding.

### Chapter 367 — FAIL

The draft preserves the extreme geothermal labor, cracked/dried bodies, collapsing prisoners, deaths even among Levels Six and Seven, whipping, corpse-eating dialogue, rotten ungutted fish, Garam's humiliation, and the Level-Nine work-refusal setup.

However, the Chinese pushes the corpse-degradation/cannibalism image farther: after explaining that the dead receive no sympathy, it says even a corpse is reduced to **chunks of meat that can be swallowed in a few bites**. The draft ends that sequence with only **“Sometimes—hunger.”** That materially generalizes an explicit cannibalistic body image.

### Chapter 368 — FAIL

Several violent bodily details are softened:

- the Chinese says Vikir **steps on and crushes the face** of the pursuing major; the draft reduces this to **“The man’s face hit the ground.”**
- after Vikir grabs Kirko by the throat and slams her down, the source says she is **vomiting and rolling on the ground**; the draft changes this to **“Kirko coughed—rolling.”**

The draft otherwise retains the red-alert response, permission to kill Vikir without consequence, collapsing nose strike, guard abuse/corruption, Kirko's late-teen age framing and exceptional talent, throat grab/slam, Garam's defense of her, and D'Ordume's arrival.
