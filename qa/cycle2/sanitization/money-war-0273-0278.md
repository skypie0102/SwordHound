## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 273 — PASS

The draft preserves Dolores's embarrassment about the drinking-party incident, the fan gifts and romantic admiration directed at Vikir, her reaction to his implied compliment, the Oracle probability-filtering scheme, the real-money investment, and the scheme's deliberately deceptive appearance without materially softening source force.

### Chapter 274 — PASS

The draft preserves Dolores's suspicion of the Night Hound's possible Academy connection, her refusal to use the Mirror of Truth against him, the old press coverage of the Bourgeois succession struggle, corruption/tax accusations, Damien's adverse publicity, the bloody-shareholders-meeting framing, Damien's political exile/hostage position, and the enormous deliberately wasteful party budget.

### Chapter 275 — PASS

The draft retains the real-money nature of Oracle's gains, the massive cash purchase/tip/renovation, Saltsail's boyish Puss-in-Boots guide, alcohol/restroom joke, luxurious replacement cloak, and the deliberately absurd reveal that the extravagant chamber is only the restroom.

### Chapter 276 — FAIL

The extraordinary excess is otherwise retained faithfully: diamond “ice,” alcohol pool, live fish under glass, models/dancers/fireworks, men and women, intoxication, decadence, and Vikir's plan to make demon-linked lenders “choke on” their money.

However, the Chinese explicitly characterizes the demon-linked lenders as **shameless people who are not worth any sympathy** because they would betray the human alliance first. The draft keeps the future betrayal rationale but drops that direct degrading/condemnatory judgment.

The source also says many rich people approaching the end times effectively **went mad** and pursued luxuries that departed from ordinary humanity; the draft generalizes this to spending fortunes chasing extreme sensations and increasingly absurd extravagance.

### Chapter 277 — FAIL

The source states that someone who loses a succession challenge faces only **death or exile more miserable than death**. The draft changes this to:

> normally faced death.  
> Or something close to it.

That materially softens the source's explicit “worse than death” exile framing.

The chapter otherwise retains Damien's family fear, the cheapness of social sympathy toward his dead daughter, his extreme wealth, the death-focused provocation, killing intent, and deliberate permanent-injury intent toward the disguised Vikir.

### Chapter 278 — FAIL

The source explicitly says Damien's ornate weapon, initially mistaken for decoration, is in fact a **true killing sword**. The draft reduces this to:

> The ornate weapon was not decoration.

That removes the explicit lethal characterization.

The rest of the confrontation preserves Juliet's publicly reported poison death, the Night Hound reveal, lethal technique, broken weapon, Graduator-killing reputation, face-first takedown, Damien's willingness to be killed, Vikir's statement that he did not come to kill him, and the final death-wish recognition.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **276:** restore the source's direct “shameless / not worth sympathy” condemnation and the explicit madness framing around end-times elite decadence.
- **277:** restore the source's “death or exile worse than death” succession-loss stakes.
- **278:** restore the source's explicit characterization of Damien's weapon as a genuine killing sword.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

## Family result

**FAIL — Chapters 276, 277, and 278 require Phase-4 remediation.**
