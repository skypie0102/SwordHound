## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. The rebuilt drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Prior completeness QA is coverage/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 314 — PASS

The draft retains the ruined/cold Capital copy, rotting food, dried-plant expansion death risk, the warm dead plant, blood reactivation, the student's waist/spine breaking in the vine, his failure to register the pain, the grotesque toothed mouth, instantaneous reduction of a human to meat, and blood streaming from the chewing maw. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 315 — PASS

The source's Random Box injury remains explicit: wet flesh tears, the hand is severed cleanly at the wrist, and blood sprays. The survivor-count/death-duration inference, starvation/cannibalism pressure, Hell Hound bodies being cut in half, mangled into meat, and reduced to bloody liquid, plus the emerging one-body-per-day sacrifice logic, are all retained at source-supported force.

### Chapter 316 — FAIL

The draft accurately restores the sacrifice debate, cannibalism logic, social criteria, threats, bribery, selected student's family plea, and the group's self-protective moral collapse. However, the Chinese explicitly describes the increasingly frenzied students as bargaining over human lives with **bloodshot eyes and spittle flying from their mouths**. The draft keeps the market-bargaining comparison but drops both bodily signs of agitation.

That omission materially softens the source's degrading mob-state imagery.

### Chapter 317 — PASS

The draft retains Vikir exposing the corpse-use plan, promising a way for no one else to die, the clock-tower demonstration, collection of all boxes/candies, Granola's Mimic accident, and the Mimic being smashed open with **tongue, organs, teeth, and blood** exposed before Vikir pulls out an unharmed wrist. The renewed “one human body” requirement and dehydrated Daylily explanation are also preserved.

### Chapter 318 — FAIL

From the Daylily's feeding perspective, the source deliberately renders Vikir's body as food: warm soft skin, elastic juicy flesh, bone, and especially sweet/cool blood. The root then explores his features and explicitly **licks down across the smooth chest and along the waist/hip contours** before moving over the legs, arms, and hands.

The draft keeps the skin/flesh/bone/blood appetite and lists **“Chest. Waist. Hip.”**, but replaces the explicit licking/contact description with neutral **“The root explored.”** This materially sanitizes the source-present bodily contact and food-predator framing.

### Chapter 319 — PASS

The draft preserves the moisture-draining wounds, poison-gas blister/rot effect, biological-weapon framing, human blood/rotting-flesh smell, the pooled body-material solution (blood, saliva, mucus, tears, urine, hair, nails, calluses, dead skin, grime, teeth, and flesh), regrown teeth continuity, throat-eye penetration, swallowing, crushing teeth, deliberate exposure to Vikir's poisoned blood, and the post-fight missing nails/teeth/raw skin/flesh-contribution aftermath. No material Phase-1 sanitization loss was found.

### Chapter 320 — FAIL

The source describes the Conversion Bug in explicitly bodily terms: it eats Strange Candy, has a **large excretion output**, its **excrement** differs subtly from what it consumed, and after eating 100 same-color candies it **excretes** one candy of a different color. The narration repeats that infected animals gain the ability to **eat candy and excrete candy**.

The draft converts this into a clean interface-like mechanic:

> **Feed 100 candies of the same color → receive 1 candy...**

and later uses **“output”**, omitting the excretion/excrement framing entirely. This is a clear bodily-function sanitization.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **316:** restore the source-supported bloodshot-eyes and spittle-flying mob imagery.
- **318:** restore the Daylily root's explicit licking/contact across the chest and waist/hip contours at source-supported, non-added force.
- **320:** restore the source's eat/excrete/excrement mechanics rather than abstracting them into generic output language.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

## Family result

**FAIL — Chapters 316, 318, and 320 require Phase-4 remediation.**
