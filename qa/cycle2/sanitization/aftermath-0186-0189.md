The source describes Sadi's alleged abuse of talented students as, among other things, **severely beating them until disabled/crippled**, causing extreme trauma, and rumors of sexual trauma toward both men and women. The draft preserves the trauma and sexual-humiliation/trauma allegations but weakens the disabling-beating allegation to **“Excessive violence during sparring. Serious injuries.”**

Because the source specifically states lasting disabling harm, the draft is materially softer on that point.

The draft collapses the comment thread into generalized categories such as “Fear. Anger. Admiration,” removing that source-explicit harsh language.
