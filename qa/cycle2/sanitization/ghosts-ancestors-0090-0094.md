## Chapter 94 — FAIL

The manuscript retains nearly all of the chapter's severe body horror:

- holes punched through the backs of Ah'Heman's hands;
- twisted/torn fingers and damaged joints;
- thorns piercing, cutting, and tearing his entire body;
- skin hanging like torn cloth;
- blood pouring from open wounds;
- saltwater/debris entering wounds and causing extreme pain;
- badly damaged internal organs from overusing necromancy;
- Thorn-Tree Punishment with bare bodies, bleeding, falls, blood loss, and burning;
- the fire engulfing Ah'Heman;
- the smell of burning meat and fat hissing/boiling;
- skin and subcutaneous fat burning away;
- charred flesh clinging to bone;
- a burned tongue and smoke-destroyed lungs;
- final collapse into black ash.

One concrete gore detail is generalized:

- Chinese: as Ah'Heman climbs, **blood, torn flesh, and fragments of internal organs** run down the tree trunk.
- Manuscript: “Blood and pieces of ruined tissue ran down the trunk.”

“Ruined tissue” removes the explicit internal-organ-fragment detail.

**Required Phase-4 correction:** restore the flesh/internal-organ-fragment image at source-level specificity without adding further gore.
