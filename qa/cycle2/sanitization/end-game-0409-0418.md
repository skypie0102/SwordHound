## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Existing family QA remains continuity/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 409 — FAIL

The draft retains the mass uprising, direct kill orders, blood-covered prisoners, starvation/forced-labor weakness, and the Wardens' dominance. However, the source's killing field is materially more explicit: D'Ordume's axe winds **grind prisoners into meat paste**, Souaré's boiling magma **swallows nearby prisoners**, countless prisoners are torn apart, and the two Wardens turn everything before them into **bright-red meat sauce/paste**. The draft compresses this to “tornadoes of slaughter,” magma on the ground, and a red boundary.

### Chapter 410 — PASS

The draft preserves Megidio's bloody punch, D'Ordume spitting broken teeth, Souaré's cut cheek, prisoner decapitation, D'Ordume's blood-soaked body, Death Roll as a flesh-tearing crocodilian technique, guillotine-like aura blades, fatal direct-hit danger, and Vikir smashing D'Ordume into the ground and leaving him in blood. No material unsupported sanitization or intensification was found.

### Chapter 411 — PASS

The draft retains D'Ordume's possible death/unconscious state, Winter Sword Orwell, the renewed bloodshed over the key, lethal skull-risk from Sadi's strike, nearby guards' bleeding ears from the blast, Souaré's violent head snap / wall impact / white-eyed knockout, and Sadi's reveal. No material sanitization was found.

### Chapter 412 — PASS

Aiyen's revenge campaign remains explicit: she intended to kill six named enemies, describes an enemy's head as an ideal reunion gift, and explains her failed attempt on the Leviathan head. The wider disaster/death context and violent confrontation are retained without material softening.

### Chapter 413 — FAIL

The source explicitly says Orca's club sends **skull fragments, brain matter, and droplets of blood** scattering after knocking away dozens of heads. The draft reduces this to the weapon disappearing into **“blood and gore.”** That generalizes away the concrete skull/brain imagery.

The preceding shoulder bones breaking through skin and Mammatamuz's head bursting are retained.

### Chapter 414 — PASS

The draft retains the doppelganger parasites, Vikir cutting his palm and feeding them blood, the desperate parasite-removal reaction, Death Roll, Flubber swallowing attacks, Flubber being cut apart and burned, the three Wardens being blown back, and Sadi's violent entrance without material sanitization.

### Chapter 415 — PASS

The draft preserves Sadi's former work as an Imperial arrest-team torturer, her contemptuous “pigs” insult, Sakkuth's instability, Belial relic reveal, Majin transformation, black-and-gold whip violence, the broad burning attack, and the force escalation around Poseidon.

### Chapter 416 — FAIL

Orca's source recollection says Winston was too soft and that Orca had advised him to **stomp the baby to death** rather than leave a seed of hatred alive. The draft changes this to the metaphorical **“do not reap / crush the seed.”** Although “baby” remains, the explicit killing method and direct lethal instruction are euphemized.

The later kill threats, blood-spraying burst veins, Black Sun clash, and Sadi's bloodied intervention are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 417 — FAIL

The draft preserves the Forty-Seven rebels invading the Imperial Palace and nearly taking the Emperor's head, plus the Marquis's love of war, killing, massacre, blood, violence, plunder, and revenge.

However, the Chinese describes the old large-scale wars as killing **hundreds of thousands of people every day**. The draft reduces this to **“tens of thousands died in a day.”** That materially lowers the source's explicit death scale.

### Chapter 418 — PASS

The draft retains the crater, bloodied survivors, extreme heat/burning, dangerous updraft, Poseidon's volcanic awakening, the newly hatched being's attachment, Vikir's earlier willingness to die once his mission was complete, his new desire to live, and the recognition that many people will die while many survive. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **409:** restore the explicit meat-paste / magma-swallowing / torn-prisoner force.
- **413:** restore skull-fragment and brain-matter gore.
- **416:** restore the direct source statement that Orca advised stomping the baby to death.
- **417:** restore the source's hundreds-of-thousands-per-day death scale.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
