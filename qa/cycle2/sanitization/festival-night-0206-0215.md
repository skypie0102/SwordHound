## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 206 — FAIL

The draft strongly preserves Vikir's first-life bodily damage and social humiliation: childhood limp, facial knife/burn scars, women crying or fleeing, mockery, spitting, discarded objects he touched, and people refusing to share the same air.

However, the source also says Vikir's transformed beauty is so striking that some male onlookers experience explicit **gender-identity confusion**. The draft reduces this to the boys merely **“need[ing] a moment,”** materially softening the comic/suggestive reaction.

### Chapter 207 — PASS

Dolores's controversial Night Hound costume, Banshee's harsh criminal comparison, the food being spat out, skull-smashing food joke, sanitation insults, and his contemptuous review remain at source-supported force. No material unsupported softening or intensification was found.

### Chapter 208 — FAIL

The source crowd repeatedly and explicitly frames Vikir as **sexy**, escalating into absurd jokes about a “Golden Sexy” award, all other sexy beings retiring, Vikir monopolizing the world's sexiness, his sexiness unifying nations, and crowds raising hands over how sexy he is.

The draft replaces this with a generalized summary about beauty awards, attractive people retiring, and his face unifying nations. The repeated sexualized wordplay and force are sanitized away.

### Chapter 209 — FAIL

Pomeranian's tearful reunion is generalized: the source has her hug Vikir, rub her face against him, and leave his abdomen wet with tears; the draft preserves the hug and tears but removes the face-rubbing/body-wetness detail.

The draft also says old Baskerville conspirators **“lost their heads,”** while the Chinese says they were **executed** without specifying beheading. That adds a specific execution method not supported by the source and is an inappropriate intensification.

### Chapter 210 — FAIL

The draft retains Bianca's 7/100 and 100/100 aesthetic scoring, her attraction to beauty, and the adults-only-comic comparison. But the Chinese image is more specific: Vikir appears like the decadent female lead from a 19+ comic Bianca had viewed while having a nosebleed when younger. The draft changes this to a generic **“impossibly glamorous character”** from an adults-only comic and removes the nosebleed/female-lead force.

The later fear response to the Corpse Queen—instinctive submission, sweat, weak legs, and Sadi comparison—is retained.

### Chapter 211 — PASS

The draft retains the Queen's unrealized Academy-life longing, her damaged/half-dead body history, renewed heartbeat, lock-melting intrusion, and explicit guilt from having cut apart, reassembled, and experimented on animals including dogs. The festival compliments and unfamiliar positive emotion are also retained without sanitization.

### Chapter 212 — PASS

The Queen's skull-piercing pain, half-dead brain, demon pressure, reciprocal half-day possession arrangement, search for a corpse, sixteen-year counterfactual age for Rosie/Geronto, crowd pressure, “blood-smeared brats” language, and lethal black-magic threat remain at source-supported force.

### Chapter 213 — FAIL

The Skeleton information window is materially sanitized. The Chinese explains that zombie bodies are inconvenient because even **dried flesh made into jerky eventually rots away**, while sealed soul fragments used for ghosts eventually evaporate. The draft compresses this to skeleton fragments being more portable than zombies requiring flesh or ghosts requiring soul fragments.

The chapter also omits the source's explicit crowd-conformity example: people may stay seated during a fire as smoke enters because others remain calm, and can ultimately **suffocate to death**. That harsh death-risk illustration is replaced by a much more general “Halloween saved the panic” explanation.

### Chapter 214 — FAIL

The combat, civilian targeting, claws/teeth, professor intervention, shattered skeletons, crushed skull, death threats, and hidden-space rule are all retained.

However, Dolores's reaction to the Night Hound is compressed. The Chinese explicitly describes her watching him with **deep affection**, seeing him as a pilgrim walking a path of sacrifice and suffering, and casting a pitying gaze on him as he bears all the blood and wounds alone. The draft reduces this largely to *“Please don't get hurt”* plus a general statement that she believes him noble/sacrificial. The source's romantic/physical-suffering force is materially softened.

### Chapter 215 — PASS

The draft retains Seth's self-hatred/despair, the idea that a broken heart means abandoning life, Demon Kings preying on people at their lowest point, Vikir's lethal intent, six-and-a-half-fang slaughter of undead, plan to cut the Queen's neck first, the unprecedented involuntary deflection of his killing stroke, trembling hand/emotional disturbance, the Queen's hoarse voice from crying and shouting, and the final mask-shattering memory trigger.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **206:** restore the source's explicit male-onlooker gender-confusion reaction.
- **208:** restore the repeated source-supported “sexy” crowd hyperbole rather than replacing it with generic beauty language.
- **209:** restore the face-rubbing/tear-wetness detail and replace the unsupported beheading implication with source-supported execution wording.
- **210:** restore the omitted nosebleed/female-lead force of the adults-only-comic comparison without adding beyond the source.
- **213:** restore the dried-flesh/rotting and sealed-soul evaporation details plus the explicit fire/smoke suffocation example.
- **214:** restore Dolores's source-supported affectionate/pitying framing and the blood/wounds burden she is reacting to.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
