## Chapter 139 — FAIL

The orphanage confrontation is materially softened.

The source spells out Doxeller's criteria for the children he treated as premium merchandise (noble birth, appearance, literacy/education), then has him incriminate himself further through dehumanizing blood/flesh language. The draft compresses this to children he considered “high quality” and cuts off the exchange before the source-level commodification is expressed.

The source also states that Vikir is about to swing Beelzebub so Doxeller's head will separate from his body and roll away. The draft raises the weapon but removes that explicit intended outcome.

**Required Phase-4 remediation:** restore the source-explicit trafficking/commodification exchange and imminent beheading image faithfully, without embellishment.

## Chapter 140 — FAIL

Multiple violence and trafficking details are materially generalized or omitted:

- Vikir explicitly says he will **cut off Ephebo's hands and feet**, because only the mouth is needed for interrogation; the draft changes this to taking away the ability to run.
- The Seven-Fang sequence explicitly bites, enlarges/severs wounds, crushes Ephebo's body to prevent regeneration, and can reduce a target to a rag-like state; the draft abstracts this into opening lines, breaking defense, and knocking Ephebo down.
- Vikir kicks the badly wounded Ephebo onto a protruding iron support, bends it into a hook, and leaves the body impaled; the draft reduces this to restraint against a damaged support.
- Vikir plans to obtain information by **dissecting Ephebo's body piece by piece**; the draft preserves forensic observation but removes the dissection intent.
- Doxeller digs through rubble until his fingers are bloody; the draft omits the bleeding.
- Ephebo's escape leaves flesh and internal-organ remnants on the metal; the draft reduces these to unspecified traces.
- The source explicitly connects the orphanage's illicit wealth to children sold to demons **as food**; the draft calls it proceeds from trafficking.
- The recovered child victims are described as dozens of bodies reduced to skin and bone, and guards cite splattered blood and flesh as scene evidence; the draft substantially generalizes both descriptions.

**Required Phase-4 remediation:** restore the source-level fictional violence, trafficking consequence, and crime-scene imagery while avoiding unsupported intensification.
