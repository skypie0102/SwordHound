## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 279 — FAIL

The draft preserves Damien's contempt for Romeo, the severe beating in the rain, the carriage crash, Romeo's fatal injuries, Juliet's collapse, her persistent unresponsive state, Damien's blood tears, and the Wraith Tree solution.

However, the Chinese explicitly says Damien **hired killers/assassins** to drag Romeo into the rain, beat him, and force the breakup. The draft reduces this to:

> He hired men.

That removes the source's explicit assassin/killer characterization and materially sanitizes the nature of the people Damien employed.

### Chapter 280 — FAIL

The draft preserves Damien's fear that Night Hound might kill both father and daughter, the “death is escape” rebuke, Juliet's soul/body separation, Romeo's dead spirit, blood-tear apology, and the source's grief/reconciliation force.

But when Damien protests that Romeo is about to pass on, the Chinese has Vikir answer directly:

> **You killed him, and you're blaming me?**

The draft changes this to:

> **You're the one who had him beaten.**

That weakens the source's explicit accusation of responsibility for Romeo's death.

The source also calls Damien an **incompetent/useless father** in Vikir's narration; the draft generalizes this to “a father's remorse.” The direct killing-culpability loss is independently sufficient for FAIL.

### Chapter 281 — PASS

The draft retains the Gate of Death, Damien's frantic attempt to stop Juliet, her request to choose for herself, the wedding, the kiss, Damien deliberately suffocating Juliet's weak body with a pillow so her spirit can cross, her death, the final goodbye, and Damien's complete breakdown.

The source-explicit bodily details of that breakdown are preserved: tears, mucus/nasal discharge, and saliva are all retained. The closing request for “the head of House Bourgeois” is likewise preserved.

### Chapter 282 — PASS

The draft retains Vikir physically carrying Dolores over the wall, the wedding/date teasing, the explicit plan to **kill Bartolomeo**, Damien's willingness to facilitate his brother's assassination, the dinner-as-assassination opportunity, 100m-cube vault dimensions, mithril/orichalcon construction, layered anti-teleportation magic, two-hour seal, roughly one hundred Sword Graduators testing the vault, the Sixth Corpse's grotesque battlefield image and disgusting breath, and the two-hour kill requirement.

No material unsupported sanitization or intensification was found.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **279:** restore the source-explicit assassin/killer characterization of the men Damien hired.
- **280:** restore Vikir's direct source-supported accusation that Damien killed Romeo, and restore the source's harsher “incompetent father” framing where appropriate.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

## Family result

**FAIL — Chapters 279 and 280 require Phase-4 remediation.**
