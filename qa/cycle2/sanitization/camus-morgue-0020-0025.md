## Chapter 22 — FAIL: adult-future suggestive/political detail generalized

The Chinese explicitly shifts to **adult future Camus** and describes more than generic beauty/political skill. It states that:

- her beauty captivated the men of high society;
- she was skilled at using a **beauty/seduction tactic**;
- her mature curvaceous figure was described as difficult even for proud imperial nobles to resist;
- this attractiveness was part of how she became a symbol of political power;
- repeated political marriages increased Morgue's prestige.

The draft compresses this into:

> “In adulthood, Camus would possess not only exceptional magical ability but also beauty and political skill that made her a formidable presence in high society.”

It retains the political marriages but drops the source's explicit seduction/beauty-tactic mechanism and the mature-body/political-power description.

**Classification:** softened / generalized source explicitness.  
**Required Phase-4 action:** restore the omitted future-adult meaning accurately and non-euphemistically without importing later wiki biography.

Other harsh material in Chapter 22 is retained, including Vikir's overwhelming killing pressure, physically seizing Camus's arm, claiming it as his under the property analogy, and drawing the blade.

## Chapter 24 — FAIL: nonsexual clothing/body-state detail softened

The source's accident sequence is nonsexual but more explicit about the children's clothing state than the draft:

- Camus's outer clothing burns away and she is left in underwear;
- she fears the humiliation of being seen that way by the crowd;
- a prior punishment memory explicitly involves being stripped and put outside;
- Vikir gives Camus his outer clothing and is therefore left in underwear;
- the narration explicitly discusses children running around naked as something Vikir does not regard with adult shame;
- when the smoke clears, the source describes Vikir standing exposed/bare-bodied and bleeding while Camus is covered by his Baskerville clothing.

The draft intentionally neutralizes this into phrases such as:

> “children being caught half-dressed”

and later:

> “Vikir stood nearby in what remained of his training clothes”

That changes concrete, nonsexual source information into vaguer wording.

**Classification:** softened / generalized bodily-clothing detail.  
**Required Phase-4 action:** restore the concrete clothing state in neutral, nonsexual prose while preserving the embarrassment/rescue context and avoiding sexualization.

The later chapter material is otherwise unsanitized: it preserves the territorial doctrine of killing hostile monsters/tribes, hunting fleeing survivors, preventing regrouping/revenge, and Hugo's line that Morgue blood will make the rubies redder.
