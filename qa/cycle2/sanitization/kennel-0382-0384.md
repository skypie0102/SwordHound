The draft reduces this to isolated nouns—**“Blood. Flesh. Organs. The water darkened.”**—and loses the source's explosive body-destruction force and immediacy.

The later Black Sun / Starvation Drought destruction, seaweed pulverization, venom delivery, crawling, rising temperature, moisture loss, death-marking black spots, and BDISSEM shriveling toward an old-woman form are otherwise retained.
