## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 422 — PASS

The draft retains the extreme drought, burned vegetation, wildfire, dried lakes, mass animal deaths, famine conditions, tavern alcohol, civil-war rumors, assassination/political crisis context, monster threat, and public fear surrounding the Night Walkers. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 423 — FAIL

The draft preserves the girl's bloodied torn clothing, her parents temporarily shutting the shelter door, her fingernails tearing as she claws at it, her pleading promises, the parents reopening the door, and blood spraying when the gnolls attack.

However, when Tudor intervenes, the Chinese says the gnoll biting the father's leg has its head **burst like a watermelon**. The draft reduces this to the gnoll simply **“lost its head.”** That removes the source's explicit bursting/crushing image.

### Chapter 424 — FAIL

The opening Poison Gnoll skull burst and poisonous brain matter are retained. But as the fight escalates, the Chinese explicitly contrasts Tudor's earlier clean head/heart wounds with later corpses showing **burst internal organs**. The draft generalizes this to **“a ruin of shattered bodies,”** removing the explicit organ damage.

Tudor's missing-arm pain, assassination/coup history, severed arm during escape, suicidal last-stand impulse, Bianca's intervention, corpse-meat desperation, and hardening battlefield outlook are otherwise retained.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 423:** restore the source-supported head-bursting “like a watermelon” force.
- **Chapter 424:** restore the source-explicit burst-internal-organ battlefield imagery.
