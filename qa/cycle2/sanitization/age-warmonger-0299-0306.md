## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 299 — PASS

Winston's mortality speech retains the source's concrete death examples—fatal bathroom fall, food-related illness, falling object, and mana rampage—and its “die at any time” force. The assassination interruption also retains skin tearing, soft tissue rupturing, dark-red blood from Winston's mouth, heart penetration, and blood bursting from his chest/back.

### Chapter 300 — PASS

The draft preserves Sadi's heel-driven confirmation strike on Winston's already ruptured heart, the fatal result, hostile faculty condemnation, violent whip amplification, the conditioned criminals' adoration/fear/obsession, requests for whipping/stepping, deliberate mana-rampage self-detonation, and auditorium collapse. No material unsupported sanitization was found.

### Chapter 301 — FAIL

Several source-explicit gore details are materially generalized:

- the opening says numerous criminals are **ground into meat paste** and Osiris stands in a downpour of **blood and flesh**; the draft says criminals “disappeared” under the attack and retains only rain of blood;
- the criminal struck by Tudor and Sancho dies when **his head and abdomen burst apart**; the draft reduces this to “the attacker died instantly”;
- Camus's finishing spikes explicitly enter from the criminals' **lower body/groin and pierce through to the top of the head**, lifting them into the air; the draft reduces this to black spikes merely bursting upward and killing them.

The naked, mutilated Humbert decoy, sexual-predator/arsonist/murderer/kidnapper/child-predator list, corpse smoke, screaming face-fruit, and flesh/juice tasting are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 302 — FAIL

The Fashion Master remains a skin-harvesting killer and his threat to skin the mother/daughter alive is retained, but the source also jokes that the two victims might barely provide enough material for the **upper body of the coat because Sadi/the Queen needs very generous chest room**. That source-present body-directed taunt is omitted.

More materially, after the pair escapes, the Chinese says Night Hound's fangs **pierce tough skin and tear internal organs** while killing the pursuing criminals. The draft reduces this to “Then killed three more criminals chasing them,” removing the explicit organ damage.

The later autograph scene keeps the upper-chest exposure, blood from the pen scratching skin, Sadi's threat to kill prior autograph recipients, and the spit request.

### Chapter 303 — FAIL

The draft preserves Sadi's abuse/discrimination history, revenge motive, coagulated-blood aura, being thrown aside like refuse, Winston's stabbing/slashing, and the moral-philosophy confrontation.

But when Amdusias overwhelms Sadi, the Chinese says she is thrown into the wreckage and **spits/coughs blood and fragments of internal organs**. The draft retains only **“Coughed blood.”** The internal-organ gore is omitted.

### Chapter 304 — PASS

The draft preserves the execution/denial scripture including the prostitute's accusation, Nabokov's dementia-impaired state, Winston's childhood charity disaster and parents crushed to death, theft/violence/ingratitude around the food distribution, the partner's betrayal at sea, his inability to resist being thrown overboard, his prior buried-under-fence survival, the heart-piercing betrayal by Sadi, and Dolores's fear of becoming a burden.

### Chapter 305 — FAIL

Dolores confronting Humbert, the missing hands/explosive restraints, trauma breakthrough, broad love crossing class/status/age/race/gender, spiritual awakening, and the eight-fang/Ninth-Fang progression are retained.

After the full-force Eighth Fang, however, the Chinese says Vikir is so spent that **whenever he opens his mouth, hot blood gushes out**. The draft reduces this to the much milder **“Coughed blood.”**

### Chapter 306 — FAIL

The draft strongly preserves Nabokov breaking Amdusias's foreleg, the three declarations of love / Quo Vadis story, her mace, Winston's “Die, old hag!” attack, broken clavicle/skull strike, Banshee's student-bracelet clue, Child Hunting, and all students being swallowed into the Hell Tree.

But after Nabokov's skull strike, the Chinese says **blood and fragments of internal organs spray from Winston's lips**. The draft reduces this to **“Blood spilled from his mouth.”** The internal-organ material and spray force are omitted.

- **302:** restore the source's coat/chest-room taunt and Night Hound's explicit internal-organ tearing.
- **303:** restore Sadi's expelled internal-organ fragments with the source-supported blood.
- **305:** restore the hot-blood-gushing condition after Vikir's all-out attack.
