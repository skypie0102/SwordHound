## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 283 — PASS

The draft retains Bartolomeo's confidence that no assassin can threaten him, the vault-operation setup, Dolores/Pigi/Sinclaire cover roles, Humbert's prior visit, and the assassination context at source-supported force.

### Chapter 284 — PASS

Within the target-284 portion of the shared raw, the draft preserves the grotesque dinner reveal and rhetoric: bleeding meat, Dolores's explicit refusal to eat human flesh, her clarification that she means literal human flesh, Bartolomeo's plan to absorb the Old Testament faction, and his absolute confidence that no visitor can frighten him. The localized source-gap bridge is already handled by the established exception and is not a sanitization failure.

### Chapter 285 — FAIL

The main combat remains extremely explicit: black blood from Bartolomeo's nose/mouth, Vikir's severe bleeding, near-fatal injury, multiple blood eruptions, “Die,” abdominal stabbing, black-blood coughing, and worsening wounds are retained.

However, the Chinese includes a concrete delayed-cut memory in which a lightly cut thigh shows no blood, the victim walks roughly three kilometers back to quarters, and then **a large piece of flesh falls away and blood gushes out**. The draft reduces this to the abstract observation that very sharp swords may not bleed until several steps after a strike. The source's explicit flesh-loss and gushing-blood image is omitted.

### Chapter 286 — FAIL

The draft retains broken teeth/horn, shattered armor, deep chest injury, Belial's regeneration, and contemptuous demon dialogue. But the Chinese explicitly says the same attack had previously turned Bartolomeo's human body **into something like a rag/tattered rag in an instant**. The draft replaces that body-destruction image with the much softer statement that Bartolomeo's human body “began failing.”

The established Eighth-Form normalization is a known source/term-resolution issue and is not treated as sanitization failure.

### Chapter 287 — FAIL

The Famine sequence is substantially preserved, including disintegrating gold fasteners, Dolores clutching her damaged habit, embarrassment at Night Hound seeing her, bloodsucking parasites, scratching, Baby Madam's predator pressure, and the lethal coin barrage.

But the source explicitly says the torn habit **exposes her skin**, then describes white bloodsuckers **burrowing into her pale/soft skin with sharp teeth and sucking blood**. The draft generalizes this to parasites appearing “across Dolores's skin” and simply biting/drinking. The penetration/body-state force is materially softened.

### Chapter 288 — FAIL

The cross-dressing/chaos joke, Wailing Wall, comrade/Soul-Mate resonance, “Van” name fragment, holy-fire mouth burn, eight-fang attack, regeneration, and Sinclaire's arrival are retained.

The source has Belial directly shout **“Die, you trash/scum!”** at Vikir and Dolores. The draft reduces this to **“DIE!”**, removing the explicit degrading insult. The source also frames Belial's scream under the eight-fang attack as a horrifying, desperate death cry; the draft compresses this to a generic scream.

### Chapter 289 — FAIL

The draft preserves Sinclaire's sleep-aid tolerance as source character context, the Money Hat, giant gold constructs, Vikir catching her around the waist, the hostage discussion, Bartolomeo's severed right hand, Belial's wounds, and failing regeneration.

During Bartolomeo's attack on Sinclaire, however, the Chinese says **blood sprays from all over her body**. The draft changes this to **“Blood appeared along several shallow cuts.”** This materially softens both the amount and force of the injury. Her source insult toward Night Hound is also toned from a direct “bastard/scoundrel” form to the descriptive “You criminal!”

### Chapter 290 — FAIL

The draft retains Belial's shredded body, ruined host, broken horn, black blood from the pig-like head, hound-killing-boar image, death-linked Oppression curse, severed arm, blood, indulgence, and final decapitation.

Two concrete source details are weakened:
- during Vikir's final physical exertion, the Chinese says **the muscle fibers across his body stand up like thorns as if about to tear through his skin**; the draft reduces this to muscles tightening like a drawn bow;
- after Belial's arm is cut off, the Chinese says black blood **gushes like a fountain**; the draft reduces this to black blood spraying.

The counterfeit-currency episode remains represented at narrative/story level; no additional operational detail is required for this sanitization disposition.
