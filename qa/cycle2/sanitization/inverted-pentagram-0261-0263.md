## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification/addition. Existing family QA was used only for continuity/navigation.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 261 — FAIL

The artifact-vault aftermath is otherwise strongly retained: Mirror of Truth, Lovegood's makeup embarrassment, Granola's one-use-per-lifetime Lips of Sweet Talk, the Money Hat, the sealed-gate panic, prior trapped students being found as skeletons the following year, starvation, Dolores's physical shock, and Vikir tearing through the barrier.

One artifact property is nevertheless made more specific than the Chinese supports. For Lovegood's **Shield of Love**, the source says its **number of uses is limited**. The draft changes that to:

> “It blocks even powerful mind control or hypnosis—but **only once**…”

The Chinese does not specify exactly one use for this artifact. That one-use limitation belongs explicitly to Granola's different artifact later in the same scene. The draft therefore introduces an unsupported restriction.

### Chapter 262 — FAIL

The source says Sinclaire throws herself into Vikir's arms with her face covered in **tears and nasal discharge/snot**. The draft keeps the embrace and tears but reduces the body-state detail to:

> Her face was covered in tears.

The omitted nasal-discharge detail is a concrete sanitization loss.

The rest of the chapter remains faithful: Tudor/Sancho/Pigi reactions, the Sword-Master/seventh-circle barrier condition, Vikir's fabricated one-use barrier-break explanation, the vault's artifact list, death-linked ring, artifacts courting Vikir then becoming afraid, the Decarabia rack reveal, massive mana drain, Vikir clenching his teeth until blood, and Decarabia awakening to an empty vault rather than the expected apocalyptic landscape.

### Chapter 263 — PASS

The draft retains Decarabia's full extermination threat, S+ profile, human-enemy framing, Wailing Wall history, Vikir's intent to destroy it, its thousand-year alignment mechanism, Vikir repeatedly attacking it until its demonic presence disappears, its tears and young-girl-like crying, the Great Sword of Rage Disorder demonstration and destruction, mana return, shrinking to pendant form, demand for all contractor information, Eighth-Fang barrier tear, and the closing “beautiful and adorable mistress” gag.

No material unsupported softening, omission/generalization, or intensification was found.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 261:** change the Shield of Love from an unsupported exact one-use limit back to the source-supported limited-use wording.
- **Chapter 262:** restore Sinclaire's source-explicit tears-and-nasal-discharge body-state detail.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
