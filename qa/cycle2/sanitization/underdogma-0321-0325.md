## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. The rebuilt drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Prior completeness QA remains coverage/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 321 — PASS

The draft retains the dead/black-white world-fragment ecology, blood-red Hell Tree sap, starved organs, monsterization risk from overdrinking, Dogma's low-birth/weak-body/commoner grievance framing, humiliation under privileged students, and the reveal of the triplets chained, battered, and repeatedly beaten. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 322 — FAIL

The draft preserves the triplets' slave-like humiliation, Dogma kicking them, the twenty-plus students spitting into their sap, Highbro drinking it, and the survival-over-pride argument.

However, the source then gives several deliberately extreme historical examples of people enduring humiliation to survive or gain opportunity. The draft generalizes this list:

- source: someone sold himself to an **elderly male patron** to finance a volunteer army; draft reduces this to “sold themselves to wealthy patrons”;
- source: someone crawled beneath a **vagrant's crotch** to deceive an enemy; draft generalizes this to “beneath another man's legs”;
- source: someone **cooked his own son** to satisfy his ruler; omitted;
- source: a physician **ate an enemy's feces** to monitor health while waiting for an assassination opportunity; omitted.

Because the whole point of the passage is the extremity and degradation of those examples, the compression materially sanitizes the source.

### Chapter 323 — FAIL

The draft preserves Vikir breaking the triplets' chains, accusing Dogma of imitating the strong, Dogma's envy logic, the Commoner Faction's solidarity, demon-alignment history, Level-peeking glasses, Beetleman encounter, and Vikir's warning to people with reasons to live.

But Dogma's source threat is singular: if Vikir refuses to cooperate, they will have to **cut off his hand**. The draft changes this to:

> “we'll cut off your **hands**.”

That adds a second hand and therefore intensifies the threatened mutilation beyond the Chinese source.

### Chapter 324 — PASS

The draft retains the 50+ Beetlemen disabled through joint locks, Dogma's bloodshot frenzy, compulsive sap drinking despite his swelling abdomen, crushing a follower's skull with bone/brain/blood falling into the sap, continued drinking, mutation through the bloodstream and bone marrow, biting through students' upper bodies, chewing flesh and candies, and the completed Majin transformation. No material Phase-1 sanitization loss was found.

### Chapter 325 — FAIL

The draft retains Dogma's D-grade grievance, Dolores's umbrella intervention, his anger and resentment, the A+ Majin combat, limb-severing/drowning strategy, throat tearing and blood spray, Level-candy exchange, and final Magic Resistance gain.

Two source-present areas are sanitized:

1. In Dogma's outburst at Dolores, the Chinese explicitly says she is **beautiful and has a good figure/body**, then imagines her later meeting a man who is **handsome, tall, and has a good figure/build**. The draft keeps “beautiful” but drops Dolores's good-figure/body remark and changes the man's body descriptor to generic **“strong.”**
2. The Conversion Bug gag is again rendered in abstract mechanics. The source explicitly says Baby Madam eats 3,000 candies and **excretes thirty candies**, Vikir looks at the colors coming from **her butt**, and on the final attempt **her butt trembles** before the candy drops. The draft changes this to candies simply “emerging,” the parasite “processing” them, and “POP,” removing the bodily-function framing.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **323:** change the unsupported plural-hand mutilation threat back to the source-supported singular hand.
- **325:** restore the source's body-attractiveness wording and the Conversion Bug's butt/excretion framing at source-supported force.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
