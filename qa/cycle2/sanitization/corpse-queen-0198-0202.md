## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 198 — FAIL

The zombie/body-decay material is retained with strong fidelity: missing lower jaw, purple hanging tongue, gas-swollen body, and rotting flesh clotted into greasy masses all remain. However:

- the Central Clocktower newspaper report again omits the Chinese source's explicit reference to the attack's **death toll**;
- the source says finding and **killing** the copycat is more important than anything else, while the draft changes this to finding the attacker and killing them **“if necessary,”** weakening an explicit lethal intention.

### Chapter 199 — FAIL

The blood, flesh, shattered-bone, undead-child-remains, ghoul, and threat material is retained. But the source specifically identifies the masked contractor as female from her **height plus chest and buttocks/hip proportions**. The draft generalizes this to **“Balanced. Trained. And—clearly female,”** removing the source's anatomical basis for the observation.

### Chapter 200 — PASS

The draft retains the source's mountains-of-corpses / sea-of-blood history, direct death threats and insults, Dead Pool spikes piercing Vikir's body, regeneration of flesh/bone/blood, burned and gnawed bones covered with ash and fractures, lack of a peaceful death, and the dry-fleshed high undead. No unsupported softening, omission/generalization, or intensification was found under the Phase-1 gate.

### Chapter 201 — FAIL

The draft preserves Ah'Heman as a ruined corpse, his hatred and resentment, torn bodies, flesh/bone recombination, and the grotesque Flesh-Patch Giant. But it softens/omits two source-explicit corpse-degradation beats:

- Chinese says Ah'Heman's lips have rotted away, exposing both his **teeth and rotting gums**; the draft keeps the missing lips/teeth but drops the rotting gums.
- Chinese recalls other named victims becoming **living-dead puppets and toys of demons**, explicitly characterizing corpse collection and processing into puppets as a demonic specialty. That dehumanizing corpse-puppet description is omitted from the draft.

### Chapter 202 — FAIL

The source opens with Vikir's mouth expelling **thick blood clots and fragments of internal organs**, followed by broken bones, torn muscle, damaged organs, severed bodily structures, and extensive wounds. The draft keeps the severe injury and regeneration sequence but generalizes the opening to **“Thick blood. Fragments from inside his body.”** It removes both the mouth-expulsion detail and the explicit internal-organ identification.

The later blood trail, execution/guillotine imagery, attempted killing of the helpless Corpse Queen, injuries, Geronto intervention, and Sadi pursuit are otherwise retained.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 198:** restore the source's death-toll reference and the unqualified lethal intent toward the copycat.
- **Chapter 199:** restore the source's anatomical chest/hip-proportion basis for identifying the masked figure as female.
- **Chapter 201:** restore the rotting-gum detail and the source's living-dead-puppet / demons'-toys characterization.
- **Chapter 202:** restore the mouth-expelled thick blood clots and internal-organ fragments at source-supported force.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

## Family result

**FAIL** because Chapters 198, 199, 201, and 202 require Phase-4 remediation.
