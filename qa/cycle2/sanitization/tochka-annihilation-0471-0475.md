## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 471 — PASS

The draft preserves Tochka's exhausted water state, death-before-dishonor framing, reanimated great-house ancestors, skin-and-bone Baskerville dead, corpse processing into undead soldiers, and the violence-motivating desecration without material sanitization.

### Chapter 472 — FAIL

The source says that **every swing of Beelzebub sends more than a dozen heads flying into the sky**. The draft reduces this to **“Every swing ... sent more heads into the air,”** losing the source-explicit scale. The surrounding Fourth/Sixth Form destruction and blood-smell battlefield material are retained.

### Chapter 473 — FAIL

The Chinese explicitly compares Vikir splitting the head of a giant Poison Human to **cutting open a watermelon** while he talks with Barrymore. The draft omits this action/image entirely, moving from Barrymore's past directly into the conversation about Hugo.

### Chapter 474 — FAIL

When Hugo's matching Eighth Form finishes Juskin, the source describes the body exploding apart under a **rain of flesh and blood**. The draft lists **“Blood. Flesh. Bone. All scattered.”** but removes the raining flesh-and-blood image and its visceral force.

### Chapter 475 — FAIL

The source states that after both Flauros's arms are severed, Vikir speaks while **tearing/ripping Flauros's heart**. The draft changes this to Vikir merely **driving Beelzebub toward the exposed chest**. The later decapitation sequence—visible heart behind ribs, black blood, torn fur, snapped muscle fibers, broken bone, ruptured vessels, severed head—is otherwise retained strongly.
