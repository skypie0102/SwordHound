## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 465 — PASS

The draft retains Kirko's clearly female transformed body, Asmodeus swollen from consumed blood, Flubber absorbing Red Death, Vikir's original survival instruction, Asmodeus's need for a blood-bearing symbiotic host, Kirko's deep-sea transformation, her days-long breath-holding ability, and her demand that Vikir take responsibility for saving her.

### Chapter 466 — FAIL

The Chinese describes Boston Terrier's battlefield attack with **waves of blood surging over the ground and blood raining from the sky**. The draft retains **“Blood waves rolled across the ground”** but drops the explicit blood-rain image.

The surrounding gore—severed limbs, skull holes, corpse piles, bodies split in half, blood-soaked paths, and the Counts' violent advance—is otherwise retained.

### Chapter 467 — FAIL

The source gives a specific physical/emotional beat during Dolores's purification effort: Vikir, who has never relied on others, **rests his head on her shoulder** while drawing support from her. The draft generalizes this to the scarred hound merely **“leaning on her,”** removing the source-specified contact point and romantic/emotional force.

Poisoning, vomiting, painful writhing, Poison Human conversion risk, Dolores's physical strain, and the Red Death flood are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 468 — FAIL

During Vikir's clash with the Reaper, the Chinese explicitly says Vikir's palm strike produces the dull sound of the Reaper's **sternum/breastbone breaking**. The draft renders this only as **“Bone broke.”** That generalizes a concrete anatomical injury.

The Hell Tree seed barrage, Pigi shielding Vikir with his body, blood-covered state, poison fog, and lethal pursuit remain faithful.

### Chapter 469 — FAIL

The source states that Hell Tree seeds spread through Pigi's body like buckshot, embed deeply, and **tear his internal organs**, after which he **swallows blood clots rising into his mouth**. The draft reduces this to seeds passing **“Through flesh. Through organs.”** and Pigi swallowing **“a mouthful of blood.”** Both the organ-tearing injury and clot detail are materially generalized.

The chapter otherwise retains severe blood loss, black-blood coughing, apparent death, the Reaper's blood-spraying wounds, Flauros's true Pigi objective, and Vikir's lethal response.

### Chapter 470 — PASS

The draft retains Flauros's torn left arm/chest, emergency heart displacement, involuntary fear and trembling, bloodshot eyes, plan to kill every survivor at Tochka, endless poison production, Granola's memories of deaths/poison experiments, drug-and-abuse-created Poison Humans, and the one-use yes/no artifact decision without material sanitization.

- **467:** restore Vikir specifically resting his head on Dolores's shoulder.
- **468:** restore the explicit sternum/breastbone break.
- **469:** restore the seeds tearing Pigi's internal organs and the blood-clot detail.
