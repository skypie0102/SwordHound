## Chapter 40 — FAIL

The manuscript preserves the blood-soaked bedroom, decapitated assassin, assassination attempts by poison/acid/sniper/arson/carriage, the assassin's rank, Beelzebub ability context, and the stated need to root out the attackers.

One violent action is softened:

- Chinese: the assassin catches Vikir unaware at close range and **stabs him lightly in the chest** with the dagger.
- Manuscript: the dagger merely **“touched his chest.”**

The later faint scratch is the *result* of Vikir's defenses; it does not erase the fact that the attacker actually stabbed him. Reducing the attack itself to “touched” weakens the source action.

**Disposition:** FAIL — violent action euphemized.
