## Chapter 35 — FAIL

The chapter preserves the slave-auction setting, abduction/drugging/abuse context, dehumanizing treatment of captives, the caged tribeswoman's condition, and the overall exploitative nature of the auction.

However, two English choices intensify the audience reaction beyond the Chinese wording:

1. A noblewoman merely looks at / reacts to the naked tribesman in the source; the manuscript adds that she stares **greedily**.
2. At the end, the source describes the expressions of the noblewomen and some middle-aged men as gradually relaxing/softening when the boy is displayed; the manuscript upgrades this to **open fascination**.

These are unsupported intensifications. Cycle 2 requires fidelity in both directions: no sanitizing and no added salacious emphasis.

**Disposition:** FAIL — unsupported intensification.

## Chapter 36 — FAIL

The manuscript preserves the auction, bidding, coercive abuse, breaking of restraints, decapitations, blood rain, Beelzebub, 5th Fang, hostage sequence, self-incision to retrieve the horn, and the Pit Bull Knights' arrival.

Ordinary source material is nevertheless softened in two places:

1. Vikir's crude body-directed command to Baron Gambino is reduced to the neutral **“Turn around.”**
2. After the tearing attack, the Chinese explicitly states that onlookers witness a man being **torn apart alive**; the manuscript retains blood spray and screams but drops that direct description.

These are ordinary sanitization defects independent of the safety-limited exploitative setup.

**Disposition:** FAIL — softened/generalized source language.

## Chapter 37 — FAIL

The manuscript faithfully preserves the execution order, decapitated noblewoman, severed wrists/ankles, crawling victims, planned executions, abuse/whip marks on the captive tribeswoman, and continuing slaughter.

One explicit source detail is generalized:

- the fleeing middle-aged man's **internal organs splatter out on the spot** in the Chinese;
- the manuscript reduces this to being **“cut open on the spot.”**

That loses concrete gore specificity present in the source.

**Disposition:** FAIL — explicit source detail generalized.
