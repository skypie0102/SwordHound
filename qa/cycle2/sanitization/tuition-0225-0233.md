## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. The rebuilt drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. The existing completeness-rebuild family QA is used as coverage/navigation evidence, not as proof of sanitization fidelity.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 225 — FAIL

The rebuilt chapter restores the post-festival gossip, Dolores's newspaper correction, the Ms. Ouroboros setup, Cerberus-hide sewing, and the Tochka starvation memory. However, the source states that Ms. Ouroboros's **eyewitnesses all died**. The draft first reframes this as **“none still capable of giving useful testimony”** and later as **“No witnesses,”** removing the source-explicit death condition. The source's warning that no one can predict how much blood she may cause is also softened into a generic potential-damage statement.

### Chapter 226 — PASS

The tuition-season class divide, dropout risk, Vikir's partial scholarship, Banshee's recommendation, operational-security reasoning around money trails, Sinclaire's multiple jobs/volunteer work, Vikir's assassination expenses and redirection of illicit wealth to orphanages, guild-job structure, and Tudor's loan offer are retained at source-supported force.

### Chapter 227 — PASS

The draft preserves Sherpa's systemic exploitation, MiniPin's protection of guides and porters from being used as expendable shields, his harsh training maxim, family guilt, past handling of hungry thieves, the skull-shattering danger of Vikir's warning punch, the pure-physical-strength reveal, and the knockout strike.

### Chapter 228 — PASS

The repeated home-intrusion intimidation sequence remains faithful: MiniPin's swollen jaw, family left unharmed, wife sleeping deeply, “DIE!” attack, bare-handed stop of Graduator aura, breaking swords/helmets, repeated knockouts, family evacuation, fear of night, persistent stalking/beating embarrassment, and the crumpled-request deduction are preserved without material sanitization.

### Chapter 229 — FAIL

The source explicitly describes the den kill as follows: upper-level Gnolls are **fried alive in oil**, middle-level Gnolls **suffocate to death from smoke**, lower survivors are unable to escape their **suffocation fate**, and remaining survivors are **smothered alive** at blocked exits. The draft reduces this to “upper-level Gnolls were burned,” “middle levels filled with smoke,” falling oxygen, rising heat, and jammed passages. The deaths remain inferable, but several source-explicit death mechanisms and the “alive” force are materially softened.

The later trampling and intraspecies fighting are retained.

### Chapter 230 — FAIL

The source says the burning animals create a **nauseating** odor, while the draft removes that reaction. More materially, after Baby Madam bites the Poison Gnoll, the Chinese states that the monster **sprays internal contents from its mouth and anus** before dying. The draft reduces this to **“Fluid escaped from both ends of its body.”** This removes the explicit anatomy and bodily-expulsion force.

The throat punctures, violent blood spray, foam at the mouth, fatal Poison Gnoll danger, corpse processing, and hidden-gold discovery are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 231 — FAIL

The source explains Ms. Ouroboros's lack of reliable identification because all witnesses either **died or went mad**. The draft renders the second condition as witnesses who **“lost the ability to give coherent testimony.”** That is materially softer than the source-explicit madness outcome.

The poisoned barbed whip, shredding aura sphere, left-side reaction weakness, Baby Madam's web/mist, committed MiniPin strike, Anubis fire, distress signal, and Imperial-unit interruption remain faithful.

### Chapter 232 — FAIL

The source opening says many corpses damaged by boiling oil or entanglement have **rotted**. The draft changes this to skin/meat/organs being merely **damaged**, removing the explicit decomposition state.

The source also says bones are commonly processed into **medicine or ritual items**. The draft adds **“Tools.”** That use is not supported by the Chinese source and constitutes unsupported addition/intensification of the material-use list.

The corpse grading, dissection/taxidermy market, food/feed/fertilizer uses, bounty, MiniPin's family/financial history, and scholarship discussion are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 233 — PASS

The draft preserves the Empire-top-ten auction scale, noble/power-family blocs, seduction and intimidation recruitment attempts, coordinated boycott, Vikir burning half the hides and then half again, the threat to burn the rest, reset to four times the opening price, four-year tuition payoff, anonymous surplus donation, and orphanage-volunteer condition.
