## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 425 — PASS

The draft retains the Crying Castle execution legend, refugee suffering from famine/drought/fire/monsters, injured refugees, Dolores's source-present beauty/dignity, Tudor's missing arm as a reminder of the brutal surface reality, Dolores's concern for his suffering, and the reunion embrace. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 426 — FAIL

When the group realizes Donquixote's home defenses may be thinner, the Chinese explicitly proposes using the opening to **assassinate the core figure(s)**. The draft reduces this to **“We hit the center.”** Dolores's subsequent intent to hunt Passamonte / Fourth Corpse Chimeries remains, but the preceding explicit assassination force is still softened.

### Chapter 427 — FAIL

The source says Pedro should already have had **all four limbs cut off and been imprisoned underground**. The draft generalizes this to **“Pedro should have been crippled. Imprisoned. Buried in a dungeon.”** The specific four-limb amputation punishment is removed.

The lethal heart shot, regeneration, Tudor's blood-covered near-fatal chest injury, flattened nose, split lips, blood, and broken teeth are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 428 — FAIL

During Sinclaire and Bianca's reconciliation, the source explicitly says **both their tears and nasal discharge/snot are flowing**. The draft reduces this to **“Both girls were crying. Neither looked dignified.”** The nonsexual bodily detail is sanitized away.

Sinclaire's blood-at-the-mouth strain, tears, betrayal guilt, and the friendship reconciliation are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 429 — FAIL

Two concrete source details are generalized:

- Dolores's hammer impact makes Pedro's eyeballs feel as if they will pop out, and the Chinese immediately clarifies that **they actually do pop out**. The draft says only that his eyes **“bulged outward.”**
- the group explicitly **cuts off Pedro's hands and feet/limbs** before twenty-four sanctified stakes are driven into him. The draft reduces this to **“Arms. Legs. Mobility. All destroyed.”**

Pedro's face flattening, combined suppression, twenty-four stakes, interrogation, Tudor's death sentence, and explicit decapitation with black blood are otherwise retained.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **427:** restore the source-specific four-limb amputation punishment.
- **428:** restore the tears-plus-nasal-discharge detail.
- **429:** restore the actual eye-protrusion result and explicit limb severing.
