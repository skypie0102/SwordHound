## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 377 — FAIL

The draft preserves Sakkuth's plague/filth framing, infected blood/saliva danger, the feces-pit newspaper, and the mortality of weak Level-One prisoners. However, the Chinese explicitly compares those frail prisoners—most of whom die within a year—to **short-lived bugs/insects**. The draft stops at “Many died within one year,” omitting the source's dehumanizing simile.

### Chapter 379 — PASS

### Chapter 380 — FAIL

Garam's fatal injuries are materially generalized. The Chinese says **all the bones in his body are crushed into powder**, with fragments driven into his internal organs, and that the major organs responsible for metabolism have **ruptured** under internal pressure. The draft changes this to **“Bones crushed into fragments… Vital organs failing.”** That softens both the pulverization and organ-rupture force.

The draft otherwise retains Garam's destroyed eyes, shattered teeth, torn tongue, unavoidable death within minutes, nerves burning with extreme pain, final words, corpse dehydration, and flesh/bone/organs collapsing to dust/ash.

### Chapter 381 — PASS

The disguise sequence retains the bloodstained/torn guard uniform, deliberate facial burn scars, Garam identity materials, rank-based degradation, hidden name tag, Vikir's willingness to consider killing Kirko if necessary, and his decision to spare her because of Garam's last words and her future usefulness. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.
