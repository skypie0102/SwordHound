## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Existing family QA remains continuity/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 403 — PASS

The draft retains the deteriorating food supply, likely prisoner starvation, skeptical guards describing newly arrived prisoners as having gone insane during the Iron-Maiden descent, the approaching Demon War, Nouvelle Vague's future mass-death history, the Level-Nine assassin rumor, and Kirko's awkward post-Black-Tongue attachment/embarrassment. No material unsupported sanitization or intensification was found.

### Chapter 404 — FAIL

The source has Black Tongue address Garam with the explicit scatological nickname **“shit-eating dog.”** The draft sanitizes this to **“Rotten Dog.”** This removes the source's crude bodily/profane force.

The rest of the jailbreak setup remains strong: BDISSEM's death/withdrawal disabling the prison restraints, Vikir's planned prison-wide uprising, the bloody-storm framing, Kirko's survival relocation, and the Black Tongue reveal are preserved.

### Chapter 405 — FAIL

The repeated source-explicit **“shit-eating dog”** insult is again sanitized to **“Rotten Dog.”**

Separately, when Vikir cuts apart Black Tongue's leech-like muscle fibers, the Chinese says **blood, flesh, and internal organs** scatter through the air. The draft reduces this to **“Blood and tissue,”** generalizing away the internal-organ gore.

Black Tongue's arbitrary experimentation on prisoners and guards, his sadistic continuation after research completion, the destruction of Kirko's family, doppelganger-leech eggs, oil/body-heat leeches, and corridor inferno are otherwise retained.

### Chapter 406 — PASS

The draft preserves Black Tongue starving the living parasite, its refusal to die, drying it out and grinding off meat powder to form Asmodeus, the blade feeding on blood and venom, the reciprocal chest/thigh impalement, Vikir's entire ear being torn free with internal structures damaged, severe pain and balance loss, Black Tongue's predatory pressure, and Vikir's deliberate corridor-cutting setup. No material sanitization or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 407 — FAIL

During the mass arrival, the Chinese describes some prisoners as having been reduced by the descent to **a pot of stew made from flesh mush and bloody water**. The draft generalizes this to **“masses of blood and flesh.”** The source's deliberately dehumanizing, liquefied-corpse image is materially softened.

Black Tongue's pressure death, Asmodeus consuming his blood after his lungs rupture, the sea-serpent form, Black Sun shattering it into nine fragments, corpse handling, whipping of raving prisoners, and the Level-Nine female-prisoner arrival remain otherwise faithful.

### Chapter 408 — FAIL

Two source-explicit coercive/bodily beats are omitted:

- Vikir actually **pushes Daylily seeds into BDISSEM's mouth**, causing her to struggle in pain before she submits; the draft only moves a seed toward her and has her agree before insertion.
- after Vikir's threatening laugh, BDISSEM is so frightened that her **crotch turns yellow and her pants and even upper clothing become wet**. The draft reduces this to **“She lost control of herself from fear.”**

The source's Daylily threat, moisture drain, forced mouth opening, death threat, prison-wide restraint release, and retention of BDISSEM for a second task are otherwise preserved.
