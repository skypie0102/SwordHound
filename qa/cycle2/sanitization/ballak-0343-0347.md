## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity/navigation evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 343 — FAIL

The accelerated-time ecology, Daylily danger, dying mayflies, rapid wound recovery, ambush, chest-directed spear, throat restraint, four-arrow catch, and the female attacker's willingness to risk her life are retained.

Two source-explicit details are softened:

- the man and woman are described in Chinese as **almost naked**, covering their bodies with animal skins and leaves; the draft changes this to **“almost no conventional clothing”**;
- after Vikir twists the spear, the source explicitly confirms that the man's **wrist is broken**; the draft says only that his **“wrist gave out.”**

### Chapter 344 — FAIL

The future-history tragedy is strongly retained: Tudor shields Bianca and the other survivors with his own body, dies, Bianca survives holding his burned body, her unspoken love, grief, rain, and Vikir's resolve to kill Amdusias all remain.

However, after seven biological years of accelerated time, the source explicitly says **Bianca's body had changed greatly**. The draft reduces this to **“Bianca looked older too,”** generalizing away the source's body-development statement.

### Chapter 345 — PASS

The forest hazards, poisonous insects, bone-marrow mosquitoes, thorned vines, survival/assassination skills, Ballak camouflage, muscular tattooed bodies covered in animal skins/leaves, Red Death history, Scale of Forced Equality mechanics, candy extraction/reconsumption, and rapid bodily growth are retained at source-supported force.

### Chapter 346 — PASS

The chapter preserves Vikir's rapidly growing stats/body, arm and wrist/elbow disabling attacks with audible cracking, terror of the Ballak warriors, lethal-capable aura slashes, the hunting-chief threat level, and the food/salt danger context. The source's broken-limb force is sufficiently represented by the crack/arm-gave-way sequence and is not treated as sanitized.

### Chapter 347 — FAIL

The draft preserves Aheul's prior sexual harassment by Bourgeois merchants, drugged earrings, Amdusias's attack, Akwilla's death, kill-or-be-killed missions, Ballak's refusal to murder the students, and the apparent mass decapitation ending with blood mist.

But the Chinese says that, before dying, Akwilla left Amdusias with a **potentially fatal / mortal wound**. The draft weakens this to simply **“wounded Amdusias severely.”** That materially reduces the source's stated severity.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **343:** restore the source's “almost naked” state and explicit broken-wrist result.
- **344:** restore the source-supported statement that Bianca's body had changed greatly after the accelerated aging.
- **347:** restore the mortal/potentially fatal force of the wound Akwilla inflicted on Amdusias.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.

## Family result

**FAIL — Chapters 343, 344, and 347 require Phase-4 remediation.**
