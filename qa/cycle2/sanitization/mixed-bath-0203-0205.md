## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 203 — FAIL

The draft retains Vikir's severe blood loss, the guard kicking dog-form Vikir, the killers' earlier forehead scars, the explicit threat to kill the six bullies and their families, the current animal abuse, Vikir's thought of killing them, and the image of six severed heads.

Two source-explicit cruelty beats are nevertheless softened:

- the Chinese says the six habitually **humiliated and tormented** juniors, peers, and seniors they considered weak; the draft compresses this to generic **“bullied”**;
- one student proposes **burning the dog alive**; the draft reduces this to **“Burn it.”**

### Chapter 204 — FAIL

The animal-abuse details are strongly retained: kicking, spitting, cutting with broken glass, cigarette burns, drunkenness, and the internal thought that it would have been better to kill the dog before Dolores saw it.

However, the source also includes degrading/violent consequence language that the draft removes:

- Dolores characterizes such unfit elites as **pests** who will harm more people as they rise; the draft keeps the harm rationale but drops the dehumanizing label;
- during the students' begging, one explicitly cries that **his father will beat him to death** if he is expelled. The draft generalizes the pleas and omits that violent line.

### Chapter 205 — FAIL

The source explicitly states that Vikir feels no lust; the draft correctly retains that clarification. It also retains the awkward shared-bath setup, Dolores washing him, her invitation for Choco to wash her, the paw kiss, Vikir licking her cheek, Dolores's romantic interest in the Night Hound, and the recovery/sword-theory material.

But several source-present non-explicit physical/body-state beats are sanitized away:

- Dolores's soft fingers moving over Vikir's body while washing him;
- rubbing his belly and using her nose to wipe foam from his nose;
- Dolores **repeatedly/enthusiastically kissing** Choco after he licks her cheek, reduced to a hug;
- her lips touching his nose again while holding him in bed;
- the explicit statement that Dolores is **naked after the bath while lying under the bedding**, reduced to simply drying off and going to bed.

These are source-present details and should not be removed merely because the scene is awkward or suggestive.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 203:** restore the source's humiliation/torment force and explicit “burn it alive” threat.
- **Chapter 204:** restore the dehumanizing “pests” characterization and the student's explicit fear that his father will beat him to death.
- **Chapter 205:** restore the omitted non-explicit physical-affection/body-state details at source-supported force while preserving the source's explicit no-lust framing and avoiding added eroticization.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
