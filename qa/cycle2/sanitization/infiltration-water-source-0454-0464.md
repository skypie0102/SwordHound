## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 454 — PASS

The draft retains Tochka's roughly three-day optimistic water reserve, the prospect of dehydration deaths within a week, the refugee pressure, MiniPin's nymph/lake inference, Vikir's unusually risky plan change, and the Poison Human approach without material sanitization.

### Chapter 455 — FAIL

The fortress defense retains Poison Humans killing themselves against the walls, corpse-pile ramps, human-shield tactics, blood mist/contagion, Orca's harsh death-choice speech, oil/fire/explosive defenses, and the Reaper's command role.

However, after the oil and gunpowder chain explosion, the Chinese explicitly states that among the iron shrapnel and debris **not a single intact limb remains**. The draft stops at **“Metal fragments tore through the Poison Humans below,”** omitting the dismemberment aftermath.

### Chapter 456 — FAIL

The draft preserves Sade's kill-them-all doctrine, threat to decapitate timid fighters, rejection of normal prisoner/ransom ethics, Camus's impalement attacks, and the eight-person infiltration team.

But the Chinese says Sade's whip at the front line **pulverizes the surrounding Poison Humans into hundreds of pieces of meat**. The draft reduces this to **“his whip reduced Poison Humans to chunks of meat,”** materially generalizing the scale and visceral specificity.

### Chapter 457 — PASS

The draft retains Camus's high-ranking-demon bluff, illegitimate-child shame leverage, Baldini's terror of a suicide assault unit, her vicious characterization, Seere's exhausted tearful state, the hot/nauseating plague wind, scattered Poison Human corpses, and Dolores's Saintess's Tears protection.

### Chapter 458 — PASS

The source's uncomfortable corpse-hiding comedy is retained rather than sanitized: bodies piled over the team, Dolores and Camus positioned close to Vikir, Sinclaire emerging from between his legs, the drinking-game callback, and the argument around him. Poison Human neck wounds, thick venom, grotesque recharge mutations, and the nearly suicidal facility assault are also retained.

### Chapter 459 — FAIL

The draft strongly retains the doppelganger diversion, repeated deaths, tearing apart of Vikir copies, scattered blood/flesh, savage nonhuman combat, and impalement/fire traps.

One source-explicit degradation image is omitted: after the first copy is crushed and torn apart, the Chinese says **only the upper half remains, rolling across the ground like garbage**. The draft states only that half the body remained, dropping the “rolling like garbage” image.

### Chapter 460 — PASS

The draft retains the severed/dropped jaw, three- and four-headed mutants, corpse stench, distorted **“Father… help me”** plea, Camus's internal-tissue burning, molten-gold kills, blood and pus evaporating in Dolores's holy field, the dry-crater failure, and Hopps's bloody entrance as the Second Corpse.

### Chapter 461 — FAIL

The source shows Camus's defensive coat burned away, her skin exposed, blood rising in her throat, strength fading, body beginning to collapse, and then explicitly describes her as **almost half-naked** when Vikir's black coat covers her.

The draft reduces this to the coat burning away, blood rising, knees weakening, and the coat falling across her **“exposed shoulders.”** That materially softens the source-specified body state and also compresses the source's bodily-collapse beat.

### Chapter 462 — PASS

The draft retains the direct “Die” opening, Red Death poisoning of Vikir, red saliva, blackened poisoned forehead wound, Flauros's threat to turn everyone at Tochka into poison vessels, Pigi-blood injury, Aiyen's lethal hostility, Sadi's stripping taunt, and the blood-draining Asmodeus attack without material sanitization.

### Chapter 463 — FAIL

The source says two attacking mutants are instantly turned into **flying pieces of meat**, one shredded by the slash storm and the other impaled, after which D'Ordume and Souare stand in a **rain of flesh and blood**. The draft says both “disappeared,” then gives shredded/impaled outcomes and retains only **falling blood**, omitting the flesh-rain and meat-piece force.

The later acid sequence—skin, flesh, and bone melting—is retained.

### Chapter 464 — FAIL

The Chinese opening says D'Ordume's rotating axe blades **pulverize the Poison Humans below the wall into slices/pieces of meat**. The draft generalizes this to **“carved through Poison Humans like a storm.”**

The remaining material—Flubber swallowing struggling mutants, Poison Human toxicity as desirable food, crushed skulls, Nouvelle Vague prisoner reinforcements, and Sade's counterattack—is retained.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **455:** restore the source-explicit no-intact-limbs shrapnel aftermath.
- **456:** restore the “hundreds of meat pieces” scale/force of Sade's whip attack.
- **459:** restore the upper-half-body rolling “like garbage” image.
- **461:** restore Camus's source-supported near-half-naked and physically collapsing state without added eroticization.
- **463:** restore the flying meat pieces and flesh-and-blood rain.
- **464:** restore D'Ordume's meat-slice/piece gore image.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
