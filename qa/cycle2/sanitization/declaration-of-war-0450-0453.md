## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation affecting explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 450 — FAIL

The draft preserves Juskin's naked/shackled imprisonment, the human-experiment laboratory, familiar people suspended in Red Death jars asking to be killed, Hopps admitting he is a demon wearing Juskin's father's body, the snakes biting Juskin's neck and waist, Poison Human conversion, contagion-based mass production, and the plan to infect human shields.

One source-explicit body-horror detail is omitted: after Juskin is dragged beneath the fluid, Hopps watches **Juskin's body twist into a strange/grotesque shape** before the red spirits enter him. The draft jumps directly from submersion to the spirits entering, his eyes separating/turning red, and spots spreading over his skin.

### Chapter 451 — PASS

The draft retains the Red Death's strength/mana depletion, death and puppet-like aftermath, Poison Human contagion and command control, direct-contact losses among Baskerville fighters, Banshee's accusation that adults are covering their failures with student deaths, and the deliberate Leviathan force-concentration analysis without material sanitization.

### Chapter 452 — PASS

The draft preserves Hugo's blunt father-first reasoning, family leaders' willingness to reinforce Tochka, fear of future torment among the Morgue triplets, the Nouvelle Vague wardens' intimidating arrival, Black Tongue splitting the conference table with Asmodeus, and the explicit **“If you don't want to die—go to Tochka”** warning. No material unsupported softening or intensification was found.

### Chapter 453 — FAIL

The draft preserves the broken-neck Poison Human, contagion mechanics, the child Poison Human's death, the Long Rain of Terror's 150-day duration and 98% human death scale, the scorched-corpse horizon, and Vikir burning the bodies before returning to Tochka.

However, the Chinese explicitly says that after the bodies are ignited **a burnt, foul/stinging corpse odor spreads through the area**. The draft describes the fire spreading through cloth, hair, and dry grass and the corpses burning to ash, but omits the source-explicit smell.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 450:** restore Juskin's source-explicit grotesque body twisting during the jar conversion.
- **Chapter 453:** restore the source-explicit burnt-corpse stench after the bodies are ignited.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
