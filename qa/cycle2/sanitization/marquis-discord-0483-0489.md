## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 483 — PASS

The draft retains the palace being empty because everyone is dead, Andras's objectifying praise of Camus's face/figure and repeated proposals, the death/coffin taunts, Vikir's demon-killing resolve, the original-timeline deal, and the final burning-away-of-human-flesh reveal.

### Chapter 484 — FAIL

Andras's livestock argument is broadly retained, including forced birth/death, human suffering, children dying in alleys/orphanages, demon livestock living past one hundred, free breeding, and slaughter at one hundred.

However, the Chinese gives the argument concrete mortality statistics: **Imperial infant mortality above 80%** and an **average human lifespan only in the thirties**. The draft replaces this with vague lines such as **“Most children do not live long,” “Infants die,”** and **“Orphans die,”** materially softening the source's numerical death scale.

### Chapter 485 — FAIL

The chapter strongly preserves the dead comrades/children, real weapons piercing Vikir, blood, beheading/self-death history, and Ninth Form breakthrough.

But when Andras's darkness-adapted eyes are burned by the sudden light, the source says **pungent smoke gushes/sprays from his eyelids**. The draft reduces this to **“His eyelids burned. Smoke rose.”** The body-specific injury/smoke force is materially softened.

### Chapter 486 — PASS

The draft retains Andras coughing blood, nine fangs tearing across the life/death threshold, crushing/grinding/pulverizing attack verbs, the body-shattering torrent, broken demon sword, cracked body with red magma beneath, broken wings, and eyes shriveled black.

### Chapter 487 — PASS

The source's old-timeline devastation remains: severed head on Baskerville's gate, rivers/blood, corpses, 99% human death, Vikir's execution, human-blood mud in the eastern desert, plague-rotted southern sea, burning north, screams, and heroes dying like discarded bodies.

### Chapter 488 — FAIL

The draft retains the skin-burning gazes, insects-through-organs sensation, ordinary-person blood-death risk, the sixty-two stronger demons, Baal banner, and Decarabia's terror. However, the source explicitly says the huge shadow behind the portal gives off a **suffocating foul stench**. The draft omits that sensory degradation detail entirely.

### Chapter 489 — PASS

The draft retains the severed displayed head, black blood, Pigi's prior death, Vikir handling his corpse, non-decaying body, intensifying death smell distinct from rot, awakening, and the abnormal shared Andras identity.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **484:** restore the source's explicit >80% infant mortality and average-lifespan-in-the-thirties figures.
- **485:** restore the source's pungent smoke erupting from Andras's burned eyelids.
- **488:** restore the suffocating foul stench emanating from Baal's distant presence.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
