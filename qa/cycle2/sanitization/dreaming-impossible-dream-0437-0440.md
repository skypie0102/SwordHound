## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Drafts were checked directly for unsupported softening, euphemization, omission/generalization, mistranslation that changes explicit force, and unsupported intensification. Historical family QA remains continuity evidence only.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 437 — PASS

The draft retains Passamonte's flesh-and-blood swamp / bone-mountain visions, Chimeries's black skeletal-horse body, S+ information window, confidence/recklessness authority, the frog bursting from overconfidence comparison, the neck bite and torn black flesh, dark foam, blood tears, and the full-cavalry threat.

No material sanitization loss or unsupported intensification was found.

### Chapter 438 — FAIL

Several source-explicit details are softened or omitted:

- Camus is described not only as taller and less baby-faced, but as having a **slender figure with mature curves**; the draft removes that body-description beat.
- Camus jokes that, had she left the iron rods straight, the cavalry could have become **horses and knights skewered like sausages and flattened like pancakes**. The draft generalizes this to the cavalry being “butchered,” losing the source's grotesque/comic specificity.
- Orca's insult toward Sade explicitly calls him an **old and lecherous/perverted escaped convict**; the draft reduces this to **“filthy old escapee.”**

The rest of the sniper-ambush setup, Camus's physical affection toward Vikir, and the House Usher threat remain substantially faithful.

### Chapter 439 — FAIL

The draft retains Sade's blood-spraying whip attacks, Chimeries being battered through walls, impaled by iron spikes, ankles/wrists severed, heart exposed, Pigi's blood applied to Gungnir, and Tudor's final heart strike.

One concrete gore image is softened: the Chinese says Orca's club turns approaching assassins into **“meat patties” with one blow**. The draft generalizes this to the club simply **“crush[ing] anyone who approached too closely.”**

### Chapter 440 — PASS

The draft retains the civil-war death scale of thousands to tens of thousands per day, near-destruction of Donquixote's cavalry, Chimeries-skin trophy, Sade/Orca debt framing, Vikir's unusually sharp reaction around his old hatred, the alliance map, and the immediate move against House Usher. No material sanitization discrepancy was found.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **438:** restore Camus's source-supported mature-curves descriptor, the sausage/pancake cavalry image, and Orca's full old/lecherous escaped-convict insult force.
- **439:** restore the source's explicit one-blow “meat patty” gore image for Orca's club attacks.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
