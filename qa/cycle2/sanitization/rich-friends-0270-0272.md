## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 270 — FAIL

The draft preserves the cult massacre context, Bourgeois money-laundering plan, “fat lazy pigs” self-condemnation, explicit violence-as-answer logic, planned assassination of Bourgeois core members, and assassin skill/funding discussion.

However, when Vikir asks what qualities an assassin needs, the Chinese explicitly frames Dolores as someone who **has never killed anyone**. The draft changes that to:

> Dolores—who had never lived as an assassin—

This removes the source's direct fact about her lack of killing experience and replaces it with the broader, less violent fact that she has not lived as an assassin.

### Chapter 271 — PASS

The draft retains Banshee's contempt, Cindywendy's smoking, disguised appearance, exaggerated “older sister” intimacy, physical cheek-pinching and reddening, Vikir's lost Ballak concern, anonymous support for pre-regression comrades/benefactors, military funds, the direct confirmation that House Bourgeois's head is a demon, and Vikir's refusal to treat money as an end in itself.

No material unsupported softening, omission/generalization, or intensification was found.

### Chapter 272 — FAIL

The Academy/Winston thread retains the life-threatening mana-rampage history, unexplained major scar on Orwell, Sadi/Nouvelle Vague remarks, and political tension. The Oracle setup also retains Dolores's intention to remove the demon from Bourgeois and keep Pigi/Sinclaire out of the actual demon fight.

But the source explicitly describes Oracle's real-money fund as a **sharp blade that will sever the head of the demon hidden inside House Bourgeois**. The draft changes this to:

> a blade aimed at the demon hidden inside House Bourgeois.

That materially softens the explicit decapitation image.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 272:** restore the source's explicit blade/sever-the-demon's-head image.

No manuscript change is made during Phase 1 discovery.
