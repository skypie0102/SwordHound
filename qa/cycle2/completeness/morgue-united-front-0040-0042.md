## Review standard

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 40 — FAIL

The draft otherwise preserves the blood-soaked bedroom, decapitated assassin, recent poison/acid/sniper/arson/carriage assassination attempts, full Beelzebub ability window, replacement of Troll Superspeed Regeneration with Infernal Buffalo **Tough Life**, assassin's Sword-Graduator rank, Barrymore's arrival, Hugo's praise, popularity rankings, Colosseo admission rules, triplet recommendation, territorial-inspection assignment, ruby-mine strategy, and the Morgue joint-expedition reveal.

One source action is incomplete. The Chinese says the close-range assassin catches Vikir off guard and **lightly stabs him in the chest with the dagger**; only afterward do Styx protection and Tough Life reduce the result to a faint scratch. The draft changes the attack itself to the dagger merely **“touched his chest.”**

The resulting scratch is present, but the actual stabbing action is missing/softened. This is therefore a completeness defect as well as the already-recorded Phase-1 sanitization defect.

### Chapter 41 — PASS

The complete transition from Underdog to the frontier is present: Chihuahua farewell, Staffordshire escort, Boston Terrier recruitment interest, Morgue forces disguised as miners, Light/Dark Faction numbers, hidden Baskerville elites, territorial-inspection cover, and the plan to counter raiding tribes.

The warning display is fully retained: burnt-meat smell, iron spikes made from trace soil iron, skeletons and charred bodies, humans and monsters impaled together, bodies burned beyond bone, wind scattering black powder, and a charcoal-like remnant falling to ash.

The arrival and characterization of Highsis/Middlesis/Lowsis, their ages, Three Flowers / Three Plagues reputation, elemental assignments, tenant-law exchange, provocation, water/plant/earth barrier, Staffordshire's assessment, and Highbro/Middlebro/Lowbro stepping forward while naming Vikir the face of Baskerville are all present.

### Chapter 42 — FAIL

The six-way confrontation, Vikir's surprise at the triplets protecting him, their gratitude over his Academy recommendation, old-life use as dirty-task hounds, Three Flowers versus Baskerville triplets combat, water/earth/plant synergy, First Fang counterattack, Vikir's cynical wish that both sides die, Staffordshire exchange, Camus's overwhelming fire/iron intervention, scorched triplets, wounded/crawling Morgue sisters, and Camus's seven-year return are all present.

However, the draft introduces an unsupported continuity change in Vikir's recollection of why the triplets became more obedient. It says:

> “Did losing a few fingers really make them this obedient?”

The earlier Chinese continuity establishes that **Lowbro's right index finger** was severed and later treated/recovered; Chapter 42's shorthand `手指断了` refers back to that event. The draft changes the single-finger injury into **“a few fingers,”** inventing a plural injury that did not occur.

Because Phase 2 explicitly checks continuity-bearing descriptors and invented detail, this is a completeness/accuracy FAIL even though it is not a harshness/sanitization defect.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **Chapter 40:** restore the source-supported close-range chest stabbing while retaining the faint-scratch result caused by Vikir's defenses.
- **Chapter 42:** correct the retrospective finger injury to the source/continuity-supported single right index finger rather than “a few fingers.”

No manuscript change is made during Phase 2 discovery.
