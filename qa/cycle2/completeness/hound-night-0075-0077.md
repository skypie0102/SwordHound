## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 75 — FAIL

The draft preserves the Saint Mecca oil-drum infiltration, two-hour breath-hold statement, Hound of the Night alias, accepted four-day route resolution, jungle monster encounters, bone-sucking-mosquito delay, Pomeranian's illness, secret Underdog return, Chihuahua's three fainting episodes, Pomeranian's care and funding, Baskerville-Morgue records, Cindywendy/Judy lead, jungle-trade monopoly proposal, and the transition toward Quovadis healing.

Several ordinary details are nevertheless incomplete:

- during flood travel, the Chinese explicitly says Vikir speeds up by stepping on floating **corpses or logs**; the draft has only **“logs and debris”**;
- the source says Chihuahua still remembered the absent Vikir so strongly that he sometimes became **red-eyed while drinking**; this small but explicit loyalty/emotional detail is absent;
- when Vikir predicts Cindywendy will approach Judy, the source gives a concrete precedent: Cindywendy had previously approached an impoverished local baron, **Gambino**, and become his investment adviser. The draft gives only general reasoning about Cindywendy's habits and omits that named example.

The contradictory raw travel-time statement is not treated as a defect because the accepted family mapping already resolves it against the itemized route and aligned witness as four days.

### Chapter 76 — FAIL

The hybrid opening/context is present: Saint Mecca's population/social order, strict civic piety, slum exclusion, checkpoints, midnight bowing, and Vikir's refusal to bow are all represented. The well incident is also preserved at a non-operational level, along with the Leviathan marker, Quovadis/Leviathan hostility, urgency before civilian deaths, Quovadis residence, Dolores's status, her refusal to privilege wealth, Vikir's battlefield memory, “lost lamb” answer, and the door opening.

Ordinary source content is still missing or compressed:

- the children are terrified by Vikir's killing intent and blood-red gaze so severely that they **collapse and wet themselves**; the draft has them merely freeze, scream, and run;
- the source explicitly identifies Dolores as a **second-year** Academy student and a direct-line young house head/heir figure in addition to being Saintess and student-council vice president; the draft retains the latter roles but omits the second-year and young-house-head detail;
- three source petitioners give concrete, individually named/status-heavy pleas and offers—family connections, Bourgeois-linked commercial titles plus prior Baskerville service, and specific land/villa/carriage compensation. The draft collapses these into three generic summaries, losing named entities and concrete dialogue content.

The operational pathogen details remain intentionally safety-limited and are not included in the remediation target.

### Chapter 77 — FAIL

The containment-hall design, Mozgus appearance, plague suspicion, book weapon, Mid-to-High Sword Graduator estimate, page-blade attack, Ballak footwork, Beelzebub, roughly 600-kilogram Kilogram Hammer, six Fangs, Incinerate, burning hand/scripture, holy-water extinguishing, book-screen feint, shattered plate and chain, three-statue collision, bleeding aftermath, many-against-one standoff, and Dolores's final appearance are all retained.

Mozgus's source backstory and combat assessment are materially compressed:

- his divine power is described as illuminating **a thousand li**; the draft generalizes this to illuminating battlefields;
- he is explicitly characterized as uncompromising toward evil and **even crueler than demons for the sake of justice**; this is omitted;
- the source says he **fought a thousand demons alone and died heroically**; the draft reduces this to having died fighting demons;
- Vikir remembers learning torture from Mozgus and then using those techniques while **torturing young masters of the Seven Great Families** in the dungeon; the draft reduces this to “a few interrogation methods” with no application memory;
- the source says current Mozgus is at roughly **half** the strength Vikir remembers from before regression, but that Quovadis home-ground healing makes the present matchup **nearly even**; the draft keeps only a qualitative “weaker” / “fight would not be simple” summary;
- the holy book is explicitly described as weighing **dozens of kilograms**; the draft calls it massive but drops the quantitative detail.
