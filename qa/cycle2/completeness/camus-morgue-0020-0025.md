Completeness is independent from Phase-1 sanitization. A source-present beat that is absent or materially collapsed is recorded here even when the same chapter already has a sanitization failure.

### Chapter 22 — FAIL

The source contains a distinct future-adult Camus characterization block that is not fully represented in the draft.

The Chinese explicitly states that adult Camus:

- wins over the men of high society through her beauty;
- is skilled at deliberately using beauty/seduction as a political tactic;
- has a mature, notably curvaceous figure whose appeal even proud royalty cannot readily resist;
- becomes a personification of political power through that combination;
- repeatedly uses political marriages to raise Morgue's prestige.

The draft reduces this to **“beauty and political skill that made her a formidable presence in high society”** followed by the political-marriage sentence. The source-present seduction-tactic, mature-figure, royal-resistance, and explicit beauty-as-power details are absent rather than merely translated differently.

The chapter's present-day eight-year-old material remains otherwise complete: mine-ownership logic, map argument, paper-wall analogy, Vikir claiming the crossed arm, Camus's unfamiliar embarrassment at close proximity, and the short-blade cliffhanger.

### Chapter 24 — FAIL

The main accident and political aftermath are present, but several concrete source beats from the fire/clothing accident are absent or generalized.

The Chinese explicitly includes, in neutral nonsexual context:

- Camus already having **tears and nasal discharge** on her face before fearing the additional humiliation;
- after Vikir gives Camus his outer garment, Vikir himself standing there explicitly in **underclothes**;
- once the smoke clears, the narration describing Vikir as **bare-bodied/naked** while bleeding, which prompts the spectators' comments about his sturdy, trained physique.

The draft retains Camus's burned clothing, underclothes, fear of exposure, prior punishment memory, Vikir giving her his cloak, his minor bleeding injury, and the crowd's physique comments, but changes Vikir to having **“what remained of his training clothes”** and omits the source's tears-plus-nasal-discharge detail.

The later ruby-mine strategy, Baskerville extermination doctrine, plan to redirect displaced monsters/barbarians toward Morgue, Hugo's satisfaction, Adolf's betrothal proposal, the eight-versus-twenty age gap, Osiris's existing engagement, Camus's preference for same-age/younger prospects, her gaze toward Vikir, and Adolf's private warning are complete.
