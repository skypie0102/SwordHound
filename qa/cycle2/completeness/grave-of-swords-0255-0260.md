## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 255 — FAIL

Ballak's disappearance from trade/contact, Aiyen/Akwilla confidence, Wraith Tree metaphysics and mass-death germination, Snake/Seere future ownership, corpse-army and soul-fruit use, Camus-versus-Snake scale, Yuuni salt desert, monster mummies, Basilisk corpse, old Baskerville knight, Fourth-Fang kill evidence, Pit Bull uniform, Vikir's memorial badge, and Hugo's sealed letter are retained.

The Basilisk information window remains materially softened. The Chinese states that **no blade or magic can kill** the creature. The draft changes this to “Neither **ordinary** blades nor magic could **easily** kill it,” weakening the source's absolute profile claim.

### Chapter 256 — FAIL

Hugo's “Brother C” letter, succession inference, Seven Counts history, six responding Counts, Cane Corso identification, salt-storm lethality, Vikir's Styx/Beelzebub endurance, red-black tower, superior spatial distortion, and Grave-of-Swords inscription are retained.

The crow death remains incomplete. The source states that after the salt storm snaps its neck and wings and mummifies it in the air, the falling corpse's **entire body's bones shatter**, while explicitly clarifying that the bird had already **died from rapid dehydration before impact**. The draft omits both the full-body bone-shattering aftermath and the dehydration cause of death.

### Chapter 257 — PASS

The Cradle/Grave maxim, blade-stair pressure, Sword-Master entry requirement, Cane Corso's physical description and status, Hugo's admission, missing demon legions, shredded demonic remains, Vikir's Eighth-Fang motive, Wraith Tree wartime growth, sacrifice-fed expansion, skull fruit, blood-colored juice, screams, undead restoration, Cane Corso's curiosity, and Vikir's challenge are all retained without material completeness defect.

### Chapter 258 — PASS

The Fourth-Fang exchange, Beelzebub recognition, Cain/Abel reference, Picaresque dog-form evasion, groin attack, Anubis five-trajectory shot, Baby Madam restraint, Seventh Fang, Lurking Ambush recognition, armor completely stopping Sword-Master aura, neck seizure, torn palm landing, Sword-Master tier explanation, “last page” clue, tower-master reference, and Nine-Fang reveal are all retained.

### Chapter 259 — FAIL

The Ninth-Fang clash, Vikir's defeat, death-edge desperation, Eighth-Fang awakening, near-bisection, Gargoyle regeneration, Beelzebub slot window, Cane Corso's sword-book history, Wraith Tree handoff, Sixth/Seventh/Eighth progression, Ninth-Fang death threshold, corpse-like hands, and Death Knight reveal are retained.

However, the source deliberately maps the **nine fangs** to **nine destructive actions**:

1. pierce,
2. seize,
3. tear,
4. cut,
5. sever,
6. cut off / truncate,
7. crush,
8. grind,
9. pulverize.

The draft reduces that enumeration to **eight** actions—“pierced, caught, tore, cut, severed, crushed, ground, pulverized”—collapsing the separate sixth cutting/severing action. Because the numbered action list mirrors the nine-fang structure itself, this is a material completeness loss even though the overall destructive force remains.

Because Chapter 259 passed Phase 1, this is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 260 — FAIL

The Death Knight information window, Cane Corso's power comparison, Ninth-Fang death requirement, Tenth-Fang death-and-awakening requirement, inability to rest, original Baskerville ancestor's Grave/Cradle relationship, life-as-equation philosophy, disappearing tower, two Wraith Tree saplings, Eighth-Fang advancement, and return route are retained.

Two source fidelity defects remain:

- the Chinese explicitly says Vikir **had never truly loved anyone in his life** before describing his emotional progression; the draft generalizes this to spending one life “rejecting emotion”;
- Cane Corso's Ninth-Fang concept list rejects ordinary understanding, empathy/resonance, acceptance, belief, common sense, and causality. The draft adds unsupported **“Probability.”**

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **255:** restore the Basilisk window's absolute “no blade or magic can kill it” force.
- **256:** restore the crow's full-body bone-shattering aftermath and rapid-dehydration cause of death.
- **259:** restore the full nine-action enumeration so all nine fangs retain distinct source actions.
- **260:** restore the direct “never truly loved anyone” characterization and remove unsupported “Probability.”

No manuscript text is changed during Phase 2 discovery.
