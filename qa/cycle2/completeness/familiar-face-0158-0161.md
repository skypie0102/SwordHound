## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 158 — FAIL

The draft preserves Ephebo's restored bones/flesh/organs, grotesque anatomy, Graduator level, corridor destruction, bent reinforcement, burst water pipes, black blood, chain restraint, hammer launch, Dolores's release spell, decapitation, head-pinning/regeneration precaution, Demon King-class Quilt reveal, holy suppression of regeneration, and mask artifact/face reveal.

The draft changes that purpose to ending the enemy **“in the most efficient way possible.”** That replaces a distinct source statement about pain/cruelty with efficiency and therefore leaves the chapter incomplete.

### Chapter 159 — FAIL

The draft preserves the rapidly dissolving face, oily residue/rotting smell, Mozgus resemblance and twin resolution, closed-door-training danger pattern, Ninth Corpse inference, Camus concern, the mask's roughly one-quarter Beelzebub-level density, Dolores's faith crisis, H-shaped dormitories, rot/grave atmosphere, Martin Luther message, intercepted pigeon, and Pedo/Hebe entrance.

But the source narrator explicitly describes Dolores's lowered, shaken expression as a **beautiful and sorrowful sight that would make any man who saw it feel heartache**. That narrator-level descriptive/affective beat is absent.

### Chapter 160 — FAIL

- Dolores explicitly realizes she is seeing a **young man's naked body at close range** through the destroyed clothing and blushes from that awareness; the draft reduces this to exposed skin/muscle and a generic healer-body comparison.

### Chapter 161 — FAIL

The magic-circle/fire/iron-spike combat, fifth/sixth-circle assessment, furnace conditions, foot/heel penetration, molten-iron rain, Geronto's detonation, reconstructed corpse evidence, Camus suspicion, height difference, childhood clothing-loss incident, identifying mole, Morgue inference, grave-robbing clue, four-mask power scale, and Quilt entrance are retained.
