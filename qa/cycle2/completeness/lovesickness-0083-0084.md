### Chapter 84 — FAIL

Ordinary completeness losses remain:

- the source characterizes **Rokoko** as a shamanic tribe **famous for cannibalistic customs**; the draft merely says Rokoko survivors arrived;
- Ahun's repeated calling of Vikir's name is explicitly compared to a devout believer invoking a god, filled with trust, affection, and joy; the draft retains the chanting but drops that reverent comparison and emotional specification;

The draft reframes this primarily as gratitude becoming a lasting alliance, softening the source's more controlling strategic intent.
