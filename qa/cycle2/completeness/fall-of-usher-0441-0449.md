- Vikir's cleared escape route includes several monsters **beheaded with blood dripping from the severed remains**; the draft reduces this to dead monsters.

The draft compresses this to “mixed among the ridiculous mountain of fan gifts,” losing the concrete note/list beat.

### Chapter 445 — FAIL

Madeline's pursuit, demon-shell deterioration, dried Lakedog basin, disaster/Gate-of-Destruction inference, eight-gate formation knowledge, repeated failed escape routes, black-blood writing, and flood reveal are retained.

Completeness defects:

- Madeline explicitly fantasizes about **having many children and consuming/enjoying children made from her own blood and flesh**, with a drooling reaction; the draft reduces this to “Children. Fresh blood. Fresh flesh”;

### Chapter 446 — FAIL

The floodgate release, modified eight-gate/eight-trigram trap, Sade's additions, Vikir's Ballak water-control experience, Masyaf destruction decision after the black flare, Bianca's grief, surviving demonized Usher force, Isolde confrontation, self-destruction warning, drought-limited washout, demon-army claim, and Daylily ambush are retained.

Because Chapter 446 passed Phase 1, this is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.
