## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 483 — FAIL

The Imperial-Palace construction scale, empty/dead palace, First Crown Prince reveal, Camus objectification/proposals, Vikir's false-identity history, coffin/death taunts, demon reveal, Kraken/Baby-Madam discussion, Vikir's regression origin, missing-brother question, Imperial foresight, original-timeline mirror, restoration offer, immediate refusal, Andras identification, and burning-away of human flesh are retained.

One source-present rhetorical characterization is replaced rather than translated. The Chinese says the Crown Prince's bizarre, reality-disconnected lines continue **like the illogical symptoms commonly shown by a person with schizophrenia**. The draft removes that comparison and substitutes **“A performance continuing after the stage itself had burned down.”** The general sense of unreality remains, but the source's explicit comparison is lost.

Because Chapter 483 passed Phase 1, this is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 484 — FAIL

Andras's true form, refusal to fight at first, Vikir's rejection of resurrection, Eighth Form attack, half-truth/half-lie temptation, chicken/domestication analogy, human hardship list, forced-birth/death argument, seven-desire framing, demon-livestock proposal, “God” comparison, brother objective, Emperor offer, human upward-drive rebuttal, Asmodeus/Kirko advantage, and Andras's contract-compulsion threat are retained.

Several source-specific details are compressed:

- Imperial **infant mortality is above 80%**;
- average human lifespan is only **in the thirties**;
- orphanage volunteer service has become a **required course in noble schools**;
- Andras's livestock analogy specifically compares slaughter categories to **No. 7 / No. 8 chickens**, whereas the draft generalizes this to livestock classified by age and growth.

The first two were already Phase-1 failures; the latter two are additional completeness losses.

### Chapter 485 — FAIL

The dead-comrade accusations, dead children, Nymphet confrontation, weaponized tongues/words, real hidden-weapon injuries, Andras's contract temptation, Vikir's thorn-road refusal, prior beheading and self-death, Eighth-Form collapse, near-death Ninth-Fang emergence, Cane-Corso teaching, and full Ninth-Form realization are retained.

Source-present bodily details remain softened/generalized:

- when the sudden light burns Andras's darkness-adapted eyes, **pungent smoke gushes from his eyelids**; the draft reduces this to “His eyelids burned. Smoke rose”;
- the old comrades explicitly shed **bloody tears** during their accusations; the draft gives blood running down faces and later generic crying, losing that exact image;
- Vikir's resumed voice is compared specifically to a groan produced by **intestines rupturing**; the draft broadens this to “organs tearing apart.”
