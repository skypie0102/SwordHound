## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 279 — FAIL

Damien's succession history, Bourgeois heir test, **Esse, Non Videri**, Juliet/Romeo relationship, Damien's contempt for Romeo, refusal of medical aid, forced breakup, storm-night confrontation, pursuit, carriage crash, Juliet's later unresponsive living state, failed medical/divine remedies, Damien's regret, blood tears, and Wraith Tree solution are retained.

- the public-story sequence explicitly says the overturned carriage left **both Romeo and Juliet dead on the spot** before Damien corrects the rumor and reveals Juliet actually survived. The draft gives the crash and then the correction, but omits that explicit public-story death statement.

### Chapter 280 — FAIL

The bargain, Damien's willingness to surrender everything, Vikir's communication-not-cure limitation, “death is escape” rebuke, one-month-later mansion visit, Juliet's condition, Pomeranian's soul diagnosis, living-spirit explanation, Wraith Tree manifestation, Juliet/Romeo summoning, reconciliation, Romeo's time limit, and wedding/funeral convergence are retained.

- the Wraith Tree is explicitly identified as an ancient artifact Vikir found in the **Grave of Spears**, left by the legendary black magician Morgue Tzersi; the draft keeps the Tzersi association but omits the Grave-of-Spears provenance.

### Chapter 281 — FAIL

The Gate of Death, Juliet's choice to follow Romeo, Damien's frantic objection, thirty-minute deadline, rapid wedding, Pomeranian's role, kiss, Damien deliberately ending Juliet's weakened bodily life with a pillow, Juliet/Romeo crossing, the reopened-gate farewell, Damien's tears/mucus/saliva breakdown, Vikir's reflection on learning what he does not understand, and the bargain for the head of House Bourgeois are retained.

However, Vikir's officiant line is substantively rewritten. The Chinese says, in substance:

> remember that **my existence is for you, not your existence for me**; may you continue to live well.

The draft replaces that with:

> “You exist for each other by choice—not as possessions.”

“Choice” and “possessions” are not present in the source, while the original asymmetrical existence-for-the-other wording is removed. This is unsupported replacement text under the completeness/fidelity gate.

This is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 282 — FAIL

This is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.
