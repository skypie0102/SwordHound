## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 139 — FAIL

The Venomphion discovery sequence is substantially complete: the present-era knowledge gap, hidden abdominal stinger, postmortem trigger, Banshee's near-hit, later danger reassessment, Sinclaire's behavioral taxonomy, Bianca's strength standard, and transition into the orphanage confrontation are retained.

The orphanage material is not complete. The source explicitly states Doxeller's “premium” child-trafficking criteria—noble birth, pale/fine appearance, literacy/education—and then has him further incriminate himself through dehumanizing blood/flesh language. The draft compresses this to children he considered “high quality” and cuts the exchange short.

The source also states that Vikir is about to swing Beelzebub so Doxeller's **head separates from his body and rolls away**. The draft raises the weapon but omits that intended outcome.

### Chapter 140 — FAIL

The Quovadis-technique clue, Ephebo name reveal, Upper/High Graduator level, Seven-Fang structure, Vikir's Hugo/Seth reflection, Doxeller's failsafe explosion, ledger recovery, Dolores's arrival, and mask recovery are retained.

Material source actions/details are nevertheless missing or generalized:

- Vikir says he will **cut off Ephebo's hands and feet**, since only the mouth is needed for interrogation; the draft changes this to taking away the ability to run.
- The Seven-Fang sequence explicitly enlarges wounds, severs/crushes the body to prevent regeneration, and can reduce Ephebo to a rag-like state; the draft abstracts these results.
- Vikir kicks Ephebo onto a protruding iron support, bends it into a hook, and leaves the body **impaled**; the draft reduces this to restraint against damaged steel.
- Vikir plans to obtain information by **dissecting Ephebo's body piece by piece**; the draft keeps only generalized forensic examination.
- Doxeller digs through rubble until his fingers are **bloody**; the draft omits the bleeding.
- Ephebo's escape leaves **flesh and internal-organ fragments** on the metal; the draft reduces these to unspecified traces.
- The source explicitly says children were sold to demons **as food**; the draft says only that the wealth likely came from trafficking.
- The recovered child victims are dozens of bodies reduced to **skin and bone**, and guards cite **splattered blood and flesh** as evidence of the attacker's strength; the draft substantially generalizes both descriptions.

These are completeness losses, not merely stylistic differences.

- **140:** restore the omitted action, injury, interrogation, escape-remnant, trafficking-consequence, and crime-scene details at source-supported force.
