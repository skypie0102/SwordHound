## Review standard

Chinese source is the semantic authority. Each chapter was directly checked for complete source coverage, exact rule/mechanical wording, ordinary narration, physical detail, repeated dialogue, internal thought, and unsupported additions. Phase-1 findings remain independent.

## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 155 — FAIL

The draft preserves Dolores's panic/temporary inability to speak, Vikir's two motives for taking blame, the laundry-room alibi, Night Hound transformation, detailed volunteer-work infiltration routes, exact dormitory age rings, loosened window, and safety-notice arrival.

But the source repeatedly describes the alleged incident specifically as Vikir **urinating on the Saintess's belly**. The draft generalizes this to wetting himself while lying over/on her and to having “done it on the Saintess.” This loses a repeated source-specific bodily detail.

### Chapter 156 — FAIL

All fourteen rules are substantially present, including the all-sexual-activity prohibition, hanging-child anomaly, paired patrols, 35-meter lake depth, restroom pairing, wall-moaning response, and the Rule 4 / Rule 14 contradiction. Peri's HELP ME fragment, looping corridor, and Dolores at the emergency stairwell are also retained.

However, Rule 4 contains an unsupported instruction in the draft. The Chinese says to **go to the emergency-exit stairs, crouch against/by the wall, close the eyes, and cover the ears**. The draft changes this to **“Crouch in a corner with your forehead against the wall.”** Dolores later happens to be found with her forehead on the wall, but that later action does not make it part of the written rule. The draft therefore imports later scene-specific behavior into the notice and fails the completeness/no-invention gate.

### Chapter 157 — FAIL

The draft preserves Dolores's explicit age of seventeen, her attempt to clear Vikir's name, the girls' disbelief, her pursuit of the Night Hound, the Quovadis/Saintess's Tears questions, forehead-to-chest plea, Red Death comradeship interpretation, Rule 6 breathing, and Ephebo arrival.

But several source-present reactions are compressed:

- a girl says Vikir's calmness despite his soaked pants creates a strange contrast that makes her **heart flutter**; the draft reduces this to “almost impressive”;
- another girl's exaggerated maternal reaction includes wanting to **change his diaper and breastfeed him**; the draft reduces this to “change his diaper or feed him”;
- Dolores's description of the Night Hound as both deeply suspicious and **heart-stirring** is generalized to his being “impossible for her to ignore.”

These are distinct source beats, not merely style choices.

## Required Phase-4 remediation

- **155:** restore the repeated source-specific Saintess-belly urination accusation at source-supported force.
- **156:** remove the unsupported forehead-to-wall instruction from Rule 4 and return the notice to the Chinese wording.
- **157:** restore the omitted heart-flutter, source-specific maternal-comedy wording, and heart-stirring Night Hound reaction without expanding beyond the source.
