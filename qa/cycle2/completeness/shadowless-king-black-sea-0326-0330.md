Because Chapter 326 passed Phase 1, this is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 329 — FAIL

The dissolving oil-paper bag, hidden Daylily seeds, rapid water absorption, air-sac buoyancy, salt-water growth limitation, ship lurch, falling cargo, Shadowless King blocking the ascent, seed implantation into jellyfish tissue, forced ascent, pressure-change ruptures, surface breakout, ship movement order, Starvation-Drought tentacle cuts, and Blaze setup are retained.

Two source-present details are lost or weakened:

- the Chinese explicitly notes that the **oil-paper bag was an item obtained from a random box** and had turned out to be useful; the draft omits that provenance/aside;
- the Shadowless King's killing intent is stated to make the Beetlemen flying the ship **faint instantly**. The draft weakens this to several Beetlemen “nearly” stopping their flight.

Because Chapter 329 passed Phase 1, this is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 330 — FAIL

The triplets' Sword-Master inference, four-years-earlier Madam Eight-Legs memory, Commoner-Faction fear of death/crippling retaliation, thigh-deep footing on the jellyfish bell, Daylily field-knot lattice, tentacle entanglement, extraction line, gas-sac chain reaction, inability to dive, Bianca's pre-regression fire-arrow precedent, achievement messages, and fairy reaction are retained.

Three source mismatches remain:

- the Chinese explicitly says the triplets pull the line extending from **Baby Madam's butt**; the draft neutralizes this to “Baby Madam's silk”;
- the Shadowless King, trapped at the surface in poison and fire, can only be **slowly roasted/cooked**; the draft reduces this to “It could only burn”;
- the Chinese says official records place the Empire's Sword Masters at **no more than ten**. The draft narrows this to **“fewer than a handful publicly acknowledged,”** introducing a materially different numerical claim.

The first two overlap the existing Phase-1 sanitization failure; the Sword-Master count is an additional Phase-2 completeness/fidelity defect within the same chapter.
