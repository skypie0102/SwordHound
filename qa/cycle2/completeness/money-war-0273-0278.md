## Chapter dispositions

### Chapter 273 — FAIL

The Oracle recruitment discussion, Dolores/Vikir corridor encounter, Temisquira fan gifts, implied appearance compliment, Vikir declining the club, three-week profit reveal, ten-thousand-recipient probability-filter scheme, roughly thirty surviving believers, collection of investment money, Cindywendy's third successful trade, clean-profit rationale, and Dolores's growing Night-Hound curiosity are retained.

However, after the Stock D split, the draft adds:

> Stocks. Gold. Beans. Wheat. Bronze. Orichalcum. Real estate. Bonds.

Those categories are not present in Chapter 273's Chinese source. They belong to Dolores's retrospective report in **Chapter 274**, where the draft also retains them. Chapter 273 therefore imports and duplicates later source material, creating a cross-chapter placement/completeness defect.

This is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 274 — PASS

The forbidden girls' dormitory roof, Dolores's Academy-insider suspicion, female-mage/Night-Hound-room clue, Mirror of Truth hesitation and refusal, Oracle progress report, the gold/grain/mineral/real-estate/bond prediction categories, Cindywendy trade profit, legal-but-underhanded framing, Pigi/Sinclaire protection plan, sleeping medicine, insider-turning strategy, old economic-report method, Bartolomeo/Damien succession press war, Damien's political exile/hostage role, Dolores's prior Bourgeois assignment, and the plan to spend the investment fortune on one extravagant party are all retained without material omission, collapse, duplication, or unsupported invention.

### Chapter 275 — FAIL

Oracle's financial-world notoriety, cash purchase of the banquet hall, **10%** tip, immediate renovation, roughly one hundred principals / more than three thousand total attendees, Sinan family arrival, Puss in Boots guide, **23 km** remaining distance, **16 km vs. 21 km** silk-curtain comparison, red-cloak dress code, luxury replacement cloak, cocktail/restroom joke, elaborate restroom decor, and restroom reveal are retained.

But Saltsail's fortune origin loses a concrete source figure. The Chinese says he amassed enormous wealth by transporting **60,000 tons of salt** throughout the Empire. The draft generalizes this to **“tens of thousands of tons of salt.”** Under the full completeness gate, that exact quantitative detail must be retained.

This is a new completeness-only remediation chapter.

### Chapter 276 — FAIL

The fifty-plus replacement cloaks, diamond “ice,” extreme main-hall excess, 150-plus cooks, liquor pool and seven bridges, live premium fish, dancers/fireworks, deer-called-a-horse scene, Pigi/Sinclaire cover assumptions, Vikir's end-times decadence memory, borrowed party money, false-identity debt insulation, and Damien identification are retained.

- many rich people approaching the end times are said to have effectively **gone mad**, pursuing luxuries outside ordinary humanity; the draft reduces this to increasingly extreme extravagance.

### Chapter 277 — FAIL

Damien's Imperial/private dual role, negligible family influence, survival-by-promotion inference, Imperial-hostage value, Vikir's servant disguise, money-worship conversation, cheap sympathy toward Juliet, Damien's terrace withdrawal, money-as-limited-tool philosophy, dead-daughter provocation, killing intent, and planned permanent injury to the disguised Vikir are retained.

### Chapter 278 — FAIL

Juliet's public poison-death story, Bourgeois's bought military advantages, Bahamut/Baskerville sword lineage, old restricted technique, imperfect fusion, Damien's Fourth Form, weapon break, Night Hound combat reputation, Hugo's money-versus-power lecture, face-first takedown, Damien's refusal to beg, Vikir's statement that he did not come to kill him, and Damien's death wish are retained.

- Night Hound's danger is stated to rival Ms. Ouroboros **or even exceed her**; the draft only says he is a threat often mentioned beside her.
