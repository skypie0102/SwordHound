## Important decisions and corrections

1. Preserved the legal escalation exactly as structured by the source: Article 1 Section 1 = **100,000,000 gold**; Section 2 adds **900,000,000** for **1 billion total**; Section 3 adds **9,000,000,000** for **10 billion total**.
2. The Chinese translation layer later writes `十亿` (1 billion) at Judy's payout despite the immediately preceding clauses totaling `一百亿` (10 billion). Chinese Ch. 31 repeats the same reduced unit once, but its later budget line and the independently aligned English witness preserve the intended **10-billion** payoff. The draft therefore uses **10 billion** and records the Chinese unit-conversion inconsistency here rather than silently treating the law as broken.
3. Preserved Judy as a **girl**, her limp, malnutrition, wilted-flower basket, sick mother, and dead father context. Recovered-English gender drift is rejected.
4. Preserved the crowd's fear that accepting the offer could be interpreted as insolence and lead to execution.
5. Preserved Judy's very modest private hope: enough money to buy hot meat porridge for her mother.
6. Preserved the near-riot when the crowd believes Vikir is reaching for his sword.
7. Preserved the reveal that Vikir reaches past the sword to the money pouch and actually pays the promised reward.
8. The chapter's governing point remains explicit: **“This is the law. It will be obeyed.”** The scene is about establishing credible enforcement, not charity alone.
