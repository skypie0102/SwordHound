1. Preserved the public expectation that the seven heirs will be humiliated and released after their families send lavish gifts—then the reversal when **seven severed, salted heads** appear in the central square.
2. Preserved the cumulative legal punishments before execution: full-face tattooing, repeated wrist/arm cutting applied retroactively to **72 violations**, eye removal, death for human trafficking, and postmortem exile of the bodies beyond the city wall.
3. Kept the statutes as atomic information windows and matched them to the established Chapter 29 legal terminology.
4. Preserved the criminal charge set: illegal entertainment establishments, lobbying, human trafficking, drug consumption/distribution, tax evasion, violence, sexual assault, kidnapping/imprisonment, coercive drugging, forced prostitution, bribery, extortion, intimidation, hired violence, and murder.
5. Preserved Chihuahua's conflicted reaction: satisfaction at seeing abusive local heirs humbled, combined with fear of economic/political retaliation from their families.
6. The Chinese translation layer briefly says **Hugo** will not remain Deputy Magistrate long. Immediate continuity, office assignment, and the aligned witness make the intended referent **Vikir**. The draft corrects the referent rather than creating an impossible Hugo office assignment.
7. Preserved Vikir's ruthless strategic logic: he expects to leave Underdog City relatively soon, likely for Colosseo Academy or active deployment, and therefore prefers a drastic short-term purge even if his successor inherits side effects.
8. Preserved the explicit line that evidence can be found—and if it cannot be found, **manufactured**. No moral gloss is added.
9. Preserved the seven heirs' decision to remain silent to protect their families and avoid wider collective punishment.
10. Preserved the arrival of the City Hall torturer with **more than one hundred custom knives/tools**, his thirty years of experience, and his horror at Vikir's designs.
11. Preserved Vikir's memory that Age-of-Destruction torture methods broke even demonic prisoners and his disturbing nostalgia at getting to use those methods again.
12. Chapter 35 is a new family: the underground **slave auction / Freak Show** begins, so no auction content is pulled backward into Chapter 34.
