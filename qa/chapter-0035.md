## Important decisions and corrections

1. The Chinese heading `拍卖场之花 (1)` is inconsistent with the numbered continuation: Chinese Ch. 36 is `奴隶拍卖 (2)`, Ch. 37 is `奴隶拍卖会 (3)`, and recovered English 35–37 are *Slave Auction (1)–(3)*. The production title is therefore **Slave Auction (1)** while the Chinese heading anomaly is documented rather than silently ignored.
2. Preserved the auction as an **unreported illegal slave market** containing displaced tribespeople, kidnapped commoners, distant nobles, and other people who should not legally be traded.
3. **Freak Show** is retained from the Chinese source's embedded English phrase. No dedicated current Fandom entry was found, so it is not claimed as independently canonical.
4. Chihuahua's cover identity is **Chihuahua Montblanc**, using forged/imitated Montblanc credentials. This continues his Chapter 31 calligraphy/imitation skill without adding explanation absent from the source.
5. Preserved the auction's treatment of objects, monsters, animals, and enslaved people as interchangeable merchandise.
6. The monster window is kept atomic as **Infernal Buffalo ‘Murcielago’**, Danger Rating A, 3 m, Le Rouge et Le Noir Mountain 2nd Ridge, Oil Realm, nineteen hearts. The exact `Murcielago` spelling is supported by Chinese transliteration plus the independently aligned English witness; no dedicated current Fandom monster-name entry was located.
7. Preserved the carcass sale at **250,000,000 gold** and the detail that all nineteen hearts had to be destroyed.
8. Preserved the diverse slave victims and the bidders becoming more excited as the victims cry and despair.
9. Preserved the unnamed tribal girl as approximately seventeen, with black-and-silver hair, pointed ears, ash on her face, a spiked collar, torn hides, scars, and an unknown language. **Do not name her yet**, even though later Fandom material identifies her; the Chinese has not revealed the name in this chapter.
10. Preserved the descending failed bids of **5m → 3m → 2m → 1m gold**, the auctioneer's suggestion that she could be used sexually or as a gladiator, the statement that she could be valued merely as meat, and her biting off a slaver's finger.
11. The chapter ends exactly with the unveiling of the shackled fifteen-year-old boy as the auction's finest lot; his identity is reserved for Chapter 36.
